Round 2 · The Science

🔬 Round 2 — The three tests & reading a reading

20-SEC BRIEF

Physical (temperature, how cloudy) · Chemical (pH, oxygen) · Biological (which creatures live there). A reading only means something when you know what's good and what's bad.

Which test is it? “We count the mayfly larvae.”

Oxygen reads 3 mg/L (low). Good or bad for fish?

💡 Say the consequence: “low oxygen → fish can't survive → poor health.”

pH is 5 upstream, 7 downstream of a works. What does that suggest?

🌟 Link it to the human-impact story from L1c — pollution changes the reading.
Round 3 · The Bugs

🐛 Round 3 — Quadrats, bugs & biodiversity

20-SEC BRIEF

A quadrat is a square we drop to sample fairly. More different creatures = higher biodiversity = usually a healthier habitat. Some bugs are “clean-water only” — finding them is a great sign.

Challenge — which quadrat suggests the healthier habitat?

Common slip: more bugs = healthier. It's more variety, not just more bodies.
🌟 Higher: why do we take several quadrats, not just one? (reliable, representative results)
