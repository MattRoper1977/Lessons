Our footprints — and our care.

People change a grassland two ways. Tap each card.

Harm
👣

The worn path

Walking the same line squashes the soil and kills the plants — that's how bare paths form. Litter and dog mess harm it too.

👆 Tap for did-you-know
Did you know? Even one walk off the path through a meadow can leave a mark that lasts for weeks. That's why we stick to the paths.
Care
🌾

How it's looked after

The Wildlife Trust cuts or grazes the grassland at the right time, and leaves some areas wild — so lots of different species survive.

👆 Tap for did-you-know
Did you know? A meadow cut too often — or never — loses its variety. It needs the right care. Look after it, and it fills with orchids, insects and birds.

The sampling toolkit

QuadratA square frame (often 50cm). Put it down, count what's inside.
TransectA line across a habitat — sample along it to see how it changes.
Random samplingPlacing samples by chance so results are fair, not biased.
ID keyA spotter sheet to name what you find (daisy, clover, plantain…).
BiodiversityThe variety of different living things in a place.
SpeciesOne particular type of living thing, e.g. a daisy.

Key facts

Where: the grassland at Portrack Marsh — part of a protected SSSI mosaic (ponds, reedbeds, grassland, scrub).
Why sample: you can't count a whole field — small fair samples give a picture of the whole habitat.
Human impact: trampling and litter harm a meadow; the right care (cutting/grazing at the right time) keeps it full of life.

Looking after yourself outdoors

Being in green space can help us feel calmer and less stressed. A simple way to steady yourself anywhere green — the 5-4-3-2-1:

👀 5 things you can see · 👂 4 you can hear · ✋ 3 you can feel · 👃 2 you can smell · 🌬️ 1 slow breath (in for 4, out for 6).

Memory hooks

Quadrat: "A square you share with the daisies." 🔲

Sample, don't count all: "Small squares, done fair, tell you what's there."

Biodiversity: "More kinds = more life." 🦋
