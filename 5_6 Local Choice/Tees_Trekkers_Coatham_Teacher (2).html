I DoLO2⏱ 5 min

🧭 Take a bearing off the map

The leader's method

1. Lay the compass edge from where you are to the next control.

2. Turn the dial so its lines point to grid north (straight up the map).

3. Read the bearing at the index line.

Bearings: N = 000° · E = 090° · S = 180° · W = 270°. The diagonals (NE/SE/SW/NW) are the trip-ups.
Spot the Mistake⏱ 3 min

🔍 Two bearing errors — find them

"Facing North, East is on my left."

"The bearing for South is 090°."

✅ Facing North, East is on the RIGHT (West is on the left).

✅ South is 180°. 090° is East.

✍️ Describe a route, then follow it

A leader describes a route using at least two types of terminology, then follows the bearings control to control — that's orienteering.

Two types is the minimum (LO4)

"Head south-east (direction) for 900 m (distance) to the Open Dunes (landmark)."

Other types: grid reference, time, contour features.

You describe the next leg

From the Open Dunes to the SSSI — give me two types of terminology.

"Head south-east for about 1 km to Coatham Sands SSSI." Direction + distance + landmark = three types.
