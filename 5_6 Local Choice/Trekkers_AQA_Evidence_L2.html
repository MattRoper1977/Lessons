Starter · 5 min

What does GOOD reflection sound like?

Compare these

A: "It was fine."

B: "It was good but my feet hurt."

C: "The dune section was harder than I expected — next time I'd wear thicker socks and pace myself more on the soft sand."

Which is the strongest reflection? Why?

What makes C strong

  • Names the specific bit (dune section)
  • Says what was hard (the sand)
  • Gives a fix for next time (thicker socks, pace)
Reflection = NAMING + LEARNING. Bad: "It was OK." Good: "X happened, next time I will Y."
I Do · 6 min

Self-assessment — being honest is the skill

The 5-point Trekker scale

  • 5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ — I could teach this to a new Trekker.
  • 4 ⭐⭐⭐⭐ — I did this well on most walks.
  • 3 ⭐⭐⭐ — I did this. Some walks better than others.
  • 2 ⭐⭐ — I tried. I need more practice.
  • 1 ⭐ — I struggled. I'd want help next time.
Honest 3s are worth more than dishonest 5s. AQA rewards real reflection — not bragging.
We Do · 6 min

Self-assess one outcome — together

Pick O3 Hazards. Class shares ratings. Staff probes the reasoning.

Vote out loud — O3 Hazards across all 5 walks

How would YOU rate yourself, 1–5, on Hazards across the unit?

Then justify with:

  • "I gave myself a ___ because…"
  • "My best walk for hazards was ___ because…"
  • "To push to a 5, I'd need to ___…"
Aspire: If you said 5, what would prove it? Be ready to defend the score.
I Do · 5 min

WWW · EBI · Next time — the 3-step reflection

Three lines, every walk

  • WWW (What Went Well) — name a specific success.
  • EBI (Even Better If) — name a specific tweak.
  • Next time — a concrete change for the next walk.
✅ Strong example — Eston Hills:
WWW: I kept pace with the group on the climb.
EBI: I forgot a snack and got tired near the top.
Next time: I'll pack a banana & refill my water before we leave school.

Independent · 15 min

YOUR portfolio finish

Pen on. Sheet out. Use your printed reflection pack.

Foundation — your job

  1. For EACH of the 6 outcomes, circle a face: 😊 / 😐 / ☹.
  2. "My best walk was ____ because ____" (one line).
  3. Tick 3 reflection prompts and finish each sentence.
  4. Sign your sheet. Staff sign too.
Reflection word bank: proud · enjoyed · tired · cold · learned · would change · favourite · hardest.

Middle — your job

  1. Rate yourself 1–5 on EACH of the 6 outcomes (be honest).
  2. Write a WWW + EBI + Next time line for EACH of the 5 walks.
  3. Name ONE skill you want to use on the next walking trip.
  4. Sign and submit.
Sentence frame: "On the ___ walk, what went well was ___. Even better if I had ___. Next time I will ___."

Higher — your job

  1. Rate yourself 1–5 per outcome — and justify each rating with a sentence.
  2. Full WWW / EBI / Next time across all 5 walks.
  3. Write a "Skills transfer" paragraph — 3 walking skills I'd take into adult life.
  4. Identify one outcome to push to a 5 next time, and HOW.
  5. Submit; be ready to share one reflection aloud.
Aspire: Higher pupils may be selected to model their reflection for the AQA moderator.
