"Darlings, gather round. We have a problem."

A successful launch doesn't happen because one person nailed it. It happens because every person on the team knew their job, trusted each other, and didn't fall apart when things went sideways.

Post-Trip Reflection · Session 1

The Challenge & Your Team Roles

The Setup — Round 1 Rules

  • Move "stock" from the Stockroom (start zone) to the Shop Floor (end zone).
  • You have 2 minutes. Most items delivered wins.
  • The Runner is blindfolded. Only the Voice may speak to them.
  • Only one item can be carried at a time.
  • Only the Runner crosses the Stockroom line. Everyone else stays put.

Not wearing the blindfold? Totally fine.

Closing your eyes works exactly the same way. We're on trust here, not on stage. Do what feels okay for you.

Session 2 · 40 minutes

Return to Set

Debrief — Pull Round 1 Apart

3 minutes in your teams. Then 2 minutes whole-class share. Be honest — this is how the Final Round gets won.

Question 1

What worked?

One specific thing your team did well. Not "teamwork" — be precise.

Question 2

What fell apart?

One moment the system broke. Why did it break?

Question 3

What will you change?

One concrete change for the Final. Say it out loud to your team.

Push it further: A West End show rehearses the same scene for weeks before opening night. Why bother? What do they get out of repetition that one round can't give you?

Post-Trip Reflection · Session 2

Final Round — The Rules Change

New Rule

Silent Service

The doors are open. The guests are here. You cannot break character.

Not one word. Claps, nods, taps on the shoulder, pre-agreed signals only. Speak — and that delivery doesn't count.

3 minutes to refine

  • Replace your verbal calls with physical signals.
  • Agree a "stop — reset" signal for when it goes wrong.
  • Re-brief your Runner. Their trust in you is everything now.
