Knowledge Organiser — Risk & Sampling

Risk row — 4 ingredients

  1. HAZARD — the actual thing (e.g. slipping on wet rocks).
  2. WHO — who is at risk (pupils, staff, public).
  3. RISK LEVEL — Likelihood × Consequence = Low / Medium / High.
  4. CONTROL — what you'll DO to make it safer (specific actions, not "be careful").

Stem: "____ could happen to ____. Risk: ____. We will ____."

3 Sampling strategies

StrategyHowBest when
RandomPick spots by chance (hat / random number).You want to remove "I-like-this-spot" bias.
SystematicSame gap each time (every 100m / 200m).⭐ Showing change over distance — best for our aim.
StratifiedSplit into zones, then random within each zone.The river has very different sections (moor / farm / urban).

Common river hazards: slipping on wet rocks · cold water (hypothermia) · sudden current after rain · weather (sun / rain) · dropping kit · access road traffic · lost phone signal.

Common controls: wellies · buddy on bank · stay 1m back unless measuring · keep depth below knee · check weather forecast · carry a spare phone/whistle · agreed meeting time · brief everyone before setting off.
