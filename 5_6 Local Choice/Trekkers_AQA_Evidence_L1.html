I Do · 6 min

The 6 AQA Walking Outcomes

Listen first. Don't write yet. These 6 things are what AQA asks every walker to show.

  1. O1 — Equipment. Show that you wore/brought the right clothing & kit for walking.
  2. O2 — Plan a route. Show that you helped plan a walk using a map.
  3. O3 — Hazards. Show that you identified hazards along the route.
  4. O4 — Compass. Show that you used a compass to find direction.
  5. O5 — Complete a walk. Show that you safely completed a walk of a sensible distance.
  6. O6 — Reflect. Show that you can think back on the walk — what went well, what you'd change.
Memory hook: EPHCR — Equipment · Plan · Hazards · Compass · Complete · Reflect. Six words. Six outcomes. You did them all.

I Do · 5 min

How to write a good evidence statement

Three things every statement needs — W·W·W

  • Where — Which walk? (e.g. "On the South Gare → Majuba walk…")
  • When — Roughly when? (e.g. "…on Tues 16 June…")
  • What — What did YOU specifically do that proves the outcome?
✅ Strong example (O4 Compass):
"On the Rec with Compasses walk, I used a compass to find that the gate at the rec was North-East of where we started. I wrote the bearing on my sheet."
❌ Weak example: "I used a compass." — Where? When? What did you DO with it?
We Do · 6 min

Write one together — O3 Hazards

Pick a walk where you spotted hazards. Build the sentence as a class using Where · When · What.

Build the statement

Where: _________________________________________________

When: _________________________________________________

What I did: ____________________________________________

What hazards I spotted: __________________________________

Try: "On the South Gare → Majuba walk on Tues 16 June, I spotted 3 hazards — cyclists on the promenade, loose sand in the dunes, and a road crossing into Majuba car park. I called them out so the group could see them."
Aspire: Higher tier — add the action you took for each hazard (called out / waited / stepped aside).
Independent · 15 min

YOUR evidence — for all 5 walks

Pen on. Sheet out. Use your printed pack. Aim for 1 strong evidence statement per outcome.

Foundation — your job

  1. For each of the 6 outcomes, TICK which walks gave you evidence.
  2. For each outcome, write 1 word or 1 short phrase as your proof.
  3. Sign your sheet. Get staff to sign too.
Scaffold word bank: coat · boots · map · hazards · compass · bearing · north · finished · enjoyed · would change.

Middle — your job

  1. Tick all the walks that gave you evidence on each outcome.
  2. For EACH of the 6 outcomes, write 1 full sentence using Where · When · What.
  3. Star your strongest evidence walk (the one you'd point AQA to).
  4. Sign your sheet.
Sentence frame: "On the ___ walk on ___, I ___, which shows I can ___."

Higher — your job

  1. Tick the grid, then write 2 sentences per outcome — the evidence + the SKILL it shows.
  2. Add a 1-line reflection per outcome (what got harder/easier across the walks).
  3. Identify your "showcase walk" (the one that hits the most outcomes).
  4. Be ready to explain ONE outcome aloud to the class next lesson.
Aspire: Higher pupils may be selected to model their evidence sheet for the AQA moderator.
Exit Ticket · 3 min

Quick check

  1. Name 2 of the 6 AQA outcomes.
  2. What 3 things does every evidence statement need? (W · W · W)
  3. Which walk is your showcase walk (hits the most outcomes)?
  4. Have you signed your evidence sheet? ☐
  5. Has staff signed it? ☐
