👋 TA Focus

Planning session for the South Gare → Majuba walk on 16th June. Year 10 build on prior work (compass use at Eston Rec, Eston Hills, their own planned-and-walked route). Paired: one pupil plans & describes the route, the other rehearses following it, taking bearings and recording observations. Ends with a printable certificate + planning evidence; LO1/LO6 are completed on the walk itself.

  • Pairs: sit them in twos at sign-in. The planner's bearings become the follower's answers — built-in peer checking. A solo pupil can play both roles.
  • Plan (LO1/2/4): the dial "take a bearing" is the core skill — turn the bezel to grid north, read it. "Show me" helps without blocking.
  • Checks (LO5): tie answers to the real reserve — fragile marram dunes, ground-nesting birds, the incoming tide on an open beach.
  • Follow (LO6): the Street View button opens the real coast — let them actually see South Gare and Majuba; it lands well with reluctant pupils.
  • Motivation: the walk is real and dated — 16th June. Frame today as "get your plan right and you lead it on the day." They've earned this through Eston Rec and Eston Hills; this is the step up to a coastal walk. Rank rewards completion + accuracy, not speed.
  • Access: read-aloud, bigger text, calm mode in ⚙️.
