3. Recall — L17

How do you measure velocity?

Mark 10 m, drop a float, time it, repeat 3 times, average. Velocity = distance ÷ time.

3 things to do with an anomaly

In order. Tap each card.

🔍

1. Spot it

2× or 3× the others

Look at the pattern. The 1.20 next to four 0.4s is obvious.

📝

2. Highlight + justify

never delete it

Why? Deep hole · misread · stick hit soft patch.

Deleting = data tampering.

🧮

3. Average without it

divide by N–1

(0.42 + 0.45 + 0.43 + 0.41) ÷ 4 = 0.43 m

✓ Honest scientists win more credit than "perfect" data scientists.

Knowledge Organiser — Fieldwork Day

Calculating averages

VariableCalculationUnits
Avg depthSum of depth readings ÷ number of readings.m
VelocityAvg time = (t₁+t₂+t₃) ÷ 3, then 10 m ÷ avg time.m/s
Avg pebble sizeSum of 10 long-axis values ÷ 10.cm
Cross-sectionWidth × avg depth.m²

Anomaly handling — 3 steps

  1. Spot it — value 2× or 3× the others.
  2. Highlight + justify — circle it, write WHY (deep hole? misread? eddy?).
  3. Average without it — divide by N-1 not N. Never delete it.

Conclusion stem

"Our data shows that as the river flows downstream, ___, ___ and ___ increased while ___ decreased. This supports / does not support our hypothesis because ___."

Sense check: upstream = shallow, slow, big chunky pebbles. Downstream = deep, fast, small smooth pebbles. If your numbers don't match that pattern, check your maths — or you have a real anomaly.
