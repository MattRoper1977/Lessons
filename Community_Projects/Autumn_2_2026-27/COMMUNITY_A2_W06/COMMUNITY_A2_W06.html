Share the Difference and Partner Handover Proposal

Decision checkpoint: A team wants to share their stall results and write a partner proposal. One pupil wants to say "Everyone in town loved our stall" and message an outside group directly. What is best?

  • Option A: Share the real tally (5 Keep, 1 Change) and small-group limit, and give the partner draft to an adult to approve.
  • Option B: Say "Everyone in town loved it" so the project sounds bigger.
  • Option C: Send the outside message straight away without asking an adult.

Pick an option, then check or reveal.

Option A is right. It shares true proof with an honest limit and keeps outside contact inside adult safety rules.

Partner turn: Tell your partner one true fact to share in Part 1 and one fair give-and-take step for Part 2.
