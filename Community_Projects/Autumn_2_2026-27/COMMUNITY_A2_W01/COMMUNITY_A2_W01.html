P5 · Our team plan1 / 2

We plan a full day next term (date to be confirmed) at the open-air museum. Point, speak, draw, write or use a scribe.

Pick your route:

Supported

  1. Read or hear one research card. Point to what you want to see. It still needs a venue check.
  2. Choose a question: What was this used for? How is it different now? Tell or show your own idea.
  3. Choose a job. Put two codes by a shared job if needed. Ask for help with any unfilled job.

Point, speak, draw, write or use a scribe.

Independent

  1. Read a research card or an adult-approved extract. Choose what your team wants to see and give a reason.
  2. Write or say a then-and-now question. Mark one idea the source supports and one thing to check at the venue.
  3. Agree the four jobs. Pair up to share a job or combine manageable roles. Keep each person’s own part clear.

Point, speak, draw, write or use a scribe.

Aspire

  1. Compare two research themes. Choose the stronger enquiry for a small tabletop exhibit and explain why.
  2. Write an evidence question and a then-and-now comparison. Flag any claim the source cannot support.
  3. Plan a compact shared route and doubled jobs. Explain how every pupil can contribute without separate walks.

Point, speak, draw, write or use a scribe.

Staff · Same task and finish on every route. Teacher leads; three TAs support hands-on. No learner is questioned publicly without choosing to share.

P6 · The trip cost challenge1 / 2

PRACTICE ALLOWANCES ONLY. Ten pupils and four adults. No quote or payment. Use the cost cards. Point, speak, draw, write or use a scribe.

Pick your route:

Supported

  1. Use the common total £364. Choose a practice transport card with an adult. Add its return cost.
  2. Find 10% by dividing that total by 10. Add this extra amount. Use a calculator or ask for one step at a time.
  3. Divide the plan total by 10 pupil places. Say or point to your cost and a reason for the team choice.

Point, speak, draw, write or use a scribe.

Independent

  1. Check the common subtotal on the cost card. Compare at least two return transport allowances.
  2. Add transport to £364, then add 10% contingency. Divide the final planning total by 10 pupil places.
  3. Check a partner’s sum. Give a reason for the choice and name an owner check still needed.

Point, speak, draw, write or use a scribe.

Aspire

  1. Compare all three return transport allowances. Check the £364 common cost and 10% contingency.
  2. Calculate cost per pupil place using 10. Compare cost per traveller using 14 and explain why the answers differ.
  3. Test a no-purchase budget by removing the £50 reserve before contingency. Explain a useful trade-off and an unconfirmed cost.

Point, speak, draw, write or use a scribe.

Staff · Teacher coordinates the three team cost tasks; one TA runs the decision station (station D) in short slots, other TAs support the shared task. Follow the owner run-sheet for the exact station arrangement.

P6 · The trip cost challenge2 / 2

Common cost: pupil entry £150 + adult entry £80 + lunch/drink £84 + pupil shop reserve £50 = £364.

Return transport: existing minibus £150; hired minibus £450; hired coach £650. Owner must check capacity, access, drivers and all costs.

My check

£364 + transport £________ = £________.

10% contingency £________. Planning total £________.

Divide by 10 pupil places = £________ each. This is a cost allocation, not a charge to families.

Shop: up to £5 per pupil. Buying nothing is valid. Spend £________; money left £________. Adult shopping is outside this model.

Finish

Give one checked cost and one team budget line: “We choose ________ because ________. Total £________, or £________ per pupil place. We still need to check ________.” Show, say or share quietly. Keep your own calculation even if you visit station D.

Staff · Model totals (practice only): transport £150 / £450 / £650 gives £514 / £814 / £1014 before contingency; +10% = £51.40 / £81.40 / £101.40; planning totals £565.40 / £895.40 / £1115.40; per pupil place (÷10) £56.54 / £89.54 / £111.54 — a model allocation, not a charge.

Practice cost model (not real quotes)

  • Pupil entry 10 × £15 = £150; adult entry 4 × £20 = £80; lunch and drink 14 × £6 = £84; optional pupil shop reserve 10 × £5 = £50 (adult shopping excluded). Common cost £364.
  • Return transport: £150 existing minibus, £450 hired minibus with driver, £650 hired coach with driver.
  • Totals before contingency £514 / £814 / £1014; +10% £51.40 / £81.40 / £101.40; planning totals £565.40 / £895.40 / £1115.40.
  • Per pupil place (all adult costs allocated across ten pupils): £56.54 / £89.54 / £111.54 — a model allocation, not a charge. The worksheets also compare cost per traveller across fourteen people.
  • Buying nothing means actual shop spending can be zero and unused reserve remains unspent. The hired driver’s cost is in the transport allowance; the model does not add that driver’s entry or lunch. An adult driving an existing vehicle cannot supervise pupils while driving.
  • Verify real admission, concessions, access, capacity, return quote and food arrangements separately. No booking, payment, publication or external contact is performed.

Feedback and evidence

Codes VF · WS · I · NS · E · R · // · ?. At check-out, record E against each pupil’s target on the evidence app. Keep the learner’s idea, adult reply, one next-step prompt and response in the same colour, on the same page, or usual mode before recording R; record E only for actual banking. A group photograph alone does not prove every pupil’s contribution.
