Decision checkpoint: At our practice stall, buyers cannot see which tray is gluten-free because the sign is flat behind the tally box. Which choice is best?

Jobs Behind the Project and Fixing a Problem

Do this in your book first.

  1. 1. Open your book or task sheet. Write or point to your Part 1 job card.
  2. 2. Name two skills for that job. Link one skill to a task you did.
  3. 3. After the reset, write or point to your Part 2 problem, cause and safe fix.

Pick your route (Supported, Independent, Aspire, Choice)

Supported

Do this in your book first.

  • Part 1: Pick one job card. Point to two skills it needs and one task you did.
  • Part 2: Point to the problem on the card. Pick one safe fix from two choices.

Check: I will know I have done this when I match one job to two skills and pick one safe fix.

Point, speak, sign, draw, write or choose. You may pass.

Independent

Do this in your book first.

  • Part 1: Pick one job role and write two skills it needs. Write how you used one skill.
  • Part 2: Write the problem and its cause. Compare two safe options and pick one fix.

Check: I will know I have done this when I record one job, two skills, my own task, and one safe fix.

Point, speak, sign, draw, write or choose. You may pass.

Aspire

Do this in your book first.

  • Part 1: Compare two job roles and their skills. Link both to real tasks from our project.
  • Part 2: Separate facts from guesses. Compare two options across safety and access, and justify your fix.

Check: I will know I have done this when I compare two jobs and justify one safe fix with a clear check.

Point, speak, sign, draw, write or choose. You may pass.

Choice

Do this in your book first.

  • Part 1: Pick how to show it: point, speak, sign, draw, write or choose. Match a job and two skills.
  • Part 2: Pick how to show it: point, speak, sign, draw, write or choose. Show two options and one safe fix.

Check: I will know I have done this when I show my job link and safe fix in my chosen way.

Point, speak, sign, draw, write or choose. You may pass.
