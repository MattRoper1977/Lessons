Frame the past and get walk-ready: Team quiz starter

Team quiz starter

Whole-class board quiz — not printed on paper.

Team Question 1: Which kit item makes our group easier to see during a walk rehearsal?

Answer: A. Hi-vis vest — bright reflective bands make the group easy to see.

Team Question 2: Who stays beside you throughout an indoor rehearsal or walk?

Answer: B. Your buddy and adult — staying in buddy pairs near the adult keeps the group safe.

Team Question 3: After asking an adult first, which photo frame choice protects privacy?

Answer: B. Hands holding our frame on the view — keep faces and names out of every photo.

Team Question 4: If a queue hides a flat sign, what safe fix should your team test?

Answer: C. Raise the sign safely and check from the viewer's spot — rehearsal proves visibility for that tested spot only.

What I do now
1. Huddle quietly with your team for each board question.2. Agree on A, B or C together — nothing to write on paper.3. Check the revealed answer before the next question.
Done whenAll four whole-class team quiz questions answered on the board.
