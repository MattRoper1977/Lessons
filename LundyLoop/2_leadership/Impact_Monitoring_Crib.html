How monitoring works here

One rich vignette per pathway per term beats a spreadsheet of tallies. The vignette is written after a naturally occurring loop is noticed — never staged. Combined with the pulse log overleaf, leadership gets a truthful picture of quality and workload with almost zero added burden.

Rhythm: Term 1 — baseline pulse + first vignettes. Term 2 — repeat, compare against Term 1 wording. Term 3 — decide, with evidence, whether the pilot becomes policy. The comparison is qualitative and honest: better, worse or the same, in whose words?

Reading the pulse honestly: if "workload" scores fall while "responding" scores rise, the loop is relocating burden rather than removing it — pause and fix before scaling. That result is a finding, not a failure.
Termly leadership question: across the vignettes and the pulse, can we name one pupil per pathway whose provision genuinely changed because of something they did or said? If yes, Influence is real. If not, we have a coaching focus.
