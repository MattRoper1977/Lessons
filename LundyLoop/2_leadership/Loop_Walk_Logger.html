Loop Walk Logger

One noticed loop per visit, logged in under a minute. Everything stays on this device — nothing is uploaded anywhere. Pupil initials only; no staff names, ever: this is a coaching tool, not a tally.

Before you start — what this tool holds.
  • What is captured: a date, a pathway, a subject/episode note with pupil initials only, four short observation notes, a strength, a coaching next step and a follow-up date.
  • Why: to coach practice. It is not a performance record, not a tally, and there are no staff names in it.
  • Where it stays: in this browser, on this device, in local storage. Nothing is sent anywhere — there is no server, no account and no sync. Another device shows an empty sheet.
  • Who can see it: anyone who can open this browser profile on this machine. Use an approved, school-owned device.
  • Retention: entries are useful for the current term. Clear them at the end of term; the termly pattern is what carries forward, not the rows. Review: end of each term.
  • Deletion: Clear all on this device, below the list. It is immediate and cannot be undone.
  • Export: Export CSV creates an ordinary file in your Downloads folder. Once exported it leaves this tool's protection — store it where pupil information belongs, and delete it when it has served its purpose.
