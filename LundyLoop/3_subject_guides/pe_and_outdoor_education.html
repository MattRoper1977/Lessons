PE and Outdoor Education — Lundy Loop Subject Guide

Whole-school implementation companion · 2026/27

Dominant dialect: Practical and physical. Use the dialect of the learning episode; hybrid lessons may move between families.

SPACESafe, regulated timing; private correction where public feedback may dysregulate; clear physical boundaries.
VOICECorrected movement, rep, route choice, bearing, teamwork action or tactical decision.
AUDIENCEAdult watches the re-attempt and responds to what actually happened.
INFLUENCENext cue, level of support, route, tactic, equipment or challenge changes.
AreaPractice translation
TechniqueOne cue at a time; avoid overload.
SafetyImmediate intervention; response demonstrated safely.
Tactics/teamworkPupil explains or demonstrates a changed decision.
EvidenceRoutine coaching is not photographed. Capture only meaningful qualification/PfA progress.
Worked loop
“Next rep, plant your non-kicking foot beside the ball.” The pupil retries; the adult observes and progresses to target placement.
Avoid marking theatre
Do not create written worksheets solely to prove live coaching occurred.
Subject QA prompt: What was the student’s response, how was it genuinely received, and what did it change next?
Codes used naturally: VF WS I NS+ // ? R E. R is a concept first and a written code only where a natural record exists.
