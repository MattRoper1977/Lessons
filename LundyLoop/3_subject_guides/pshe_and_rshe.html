Worked loop
A pupil adds a trusted adult and an appropriate service to advice for a fictional character. The teacher receives the reasoning and tests emergency help in the next scenario.
Avoid marking theatre
Never photograph, code, investigate or turn a disclosure into assessment evidence.
Subject QA prompt: What was the student’s response, how was it genuinely received, and what did it change next?
Codes used naturally: VF WS I NS+ // ? R E. R is a concept first and a written code only where a natural record exists.
