TA Card — The Lundy Loop Which room are you in, and how a loop closes there · September 2026

Made by Matt
Settle the room first — SPACE is your first job in every room. Regulate before you educate. A pupil who is not settled cannot use their voice yet; settling them is the loop starting, not a delay to it.

The loop: Space → Voice → Audience → Influence. The pupil speaks, is genuinely heard, and it changes something. Your part differs by room — read the room you are in below.

BUILD

Closes on adult receipt — ring + R.

You are the audience. Receive the pupil's voice, and mark R when a response has genuinely come back. Out loud, as a class, is enough here.

An R without a received response is a false record.

GROW · LAUNCH

Closes when the pupil writes their line.

You are audience, never verifier. Add no initial, no receipt mark, no sign-off; store nothing. The close lives on the pupil's pen, not on your signature.

If there is no R box, nothing is missing.

"Not yet = Voice present, Audience pending" is a real answer on every pathway. "Nothing yet" is a printed answer, and an empty row is a finished row — never fill one in for a pupil.

The trap — receipt by the back door

Do not supply an R, an initial, or any "seen by" mark in a GROW or LAUNCH room, however incomplete it feels without one. That mark is a false record: it moves the close off the pupil's pen and onto your signature. Leave it alone — it is already closed.

Missed day-close (absence, early finish, trip)

The end-of-day re-read strengthens a close; writing the line is what made it. A day whose close never happened is a closed day. Never "catch it up tomorrow" — there is no backlog.

Assessed lessons (Week 7)

Read the Conditions Card before handing anything out — the Card decides, not the slide. Extra time, breaks, a reader or a scribe are never withheld at any tier.
