The Loop in 25 Minutes

Twilight INSET running order · facilitator copy · designed for the September launch anchor

Resources: this sheet · R_Gate_Calibration_Game.html on the screen · five_minute_briefing.html printed as the takeaway · one real exercise book with an NS+ → response → R chain in it (borrow one from a LAUNCH pupil).
Session promise, said out loud at the start: "Nothing in the next 25 minutes adds marking. Most of it removes marking. The one thing it protects is the R-gate."

Running order

TimeSegmentFacilitator notes
0–3The book, not the slideOpen the real book. Show one chain: dated NS+, purple response, R. "This already exists. Tonight is about naming why it works and agreeing one rule." No theory yet.
3–8The four wordsSpace, Voice, Audience, Influence — one sentence each, pointed at the chain in the book. Land the reframe: feedback isn't finished when the adult speaks; it's finished when the response is received and changes something.
8–18Calibration gameRun the game on the screen, whole room voting by hand before each reveal. Let disagreements breathe — the argument between "not yet" and "no" IS the training. Do at least the first eight scenarios; skip ahead to the firewall one if time is tight.
18–22The one ruleAgree it as a room: R only after genuine Audience. Everything else in the pack is guidance; this is the non-negotiable. Take questions on codes using the strip on the briefing sheet.
22–25One lesson tomorrowEach person names (to a partner, not the room) one lesson tomorrow and what Space, Voice, Audience and Influence will look like in it. Hand out the five-minute briefing on the way out. Done.

Anticipated pushback, honest answers

They'll say…You can say…
"This is more marking with extra steps."It's less. Written feedback becomes strategic, not routine; VF doesn't need recording; the only new discipline is not writing R until it's true.
"My subject doesn't work in books."Correct — that's why there are ten subject guides. A watched re-attempt in PE is a complete loop with zero writing. Your dialect is in your guide.
"What about pupils who won't respond?"Triage first: can't-yet, won't-now, or can't-this-way. Each has a different move, and none of them is "mark harder". See the SEND overlay.
"Is this for Ofsted?"Deliberately not — nothing here is made for inspection, and under the renewed framework that's precisely what makes it strong evidence.
Facilitator's own R-gate: the session has worked only if staff can state the one rule unprompted a week later. Ask three people, casually, next Friday. That's your Audience.
