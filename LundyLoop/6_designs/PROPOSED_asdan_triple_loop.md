## 0 · The design question

The 79 ASDAN lessons (BUILD 31 · GROW 18 · LAUNCH 30 — derive from the tree, never quote)
already produce real products, printed evidence surfaces, witness statements and learner
confirmation. What they lack is not another form; it is **three processes kept visibly
distinct**:

1. **The learning loop** — clarify → elicit → interpret → respond → revisit. The adult's read.
2. **The Lundy participation loop** — Space → Voice → Audience → Influence around a genuinely
   open matter. The pupil's route in.
3. **The portfolio loop** — the six controlled evidence states, defined once at
   `quality/toolkits/CLAIMS_REGISTER.md` ("The six evidence states", Pass TK-1):
   draft → curriculum-aligned → candidate evidence → centre-verified → submitted → certified.
   **A repository fact, verified at HEAD — the pack's citation of it was TRUE.**

A photograph, a pupil response and an assessor judgement are not interchangeable. Each loop has
its own question, its own close and its own owner; the design makes the seams explicit so no
loop borrows another's authority.

## 1 · What holds regardless of this design (the estate's account, restated not amended)

- **Closure is pathway-relative and whole-school** (74c33ae, live): BUILD closes when an adult
  genuinely receives it; GROW and LAUNCH close when the pupil writes the line — the adult is
  audience, not verifier, and no initial is expected.
- **R-A09:** no second copy of any closure mark, ever. A branch or a state is expressed as the
  next step or the evidence action — never a code beside a pupil's name.
- **One next step per pupil per lesson**; the day-close act is the return visit (B2 Amendment 2).
- **The LAUNCH warrant wording is Matt's**, specimen-gated (B3; OPEN_ITEMS #25).

## 3 · What the design adds per lesson (evaluation summary — nothing is authored into lessons)

The pack's per-lesson architecture (its 79-row matrix): name the precise thing to elicit, the
observable evidence, the likely branches, the response per branch, one genuinely open matter,
the audience and their power, the report-back route, and whether the artefact is lesson work,
curriculum-aligned, or offerable as candidate evidence. Evaluated as **sound and well-aligned**;
it becomes real only through the specimen route (three overlays under `ASDAN_Lundy/specimens/`,
PROPOSED, chained in `HANDOVER.md` behind LL-J's delivery).

## 4 · Anti-design — how this rots, and the control

| the rot | the control |
|---|---|
| the three loops collapse into one evidence routine | each loop's close named separately; the seams table in `ASDAN_Lundy/TRIPLE_LOOP_MODEL.html` |
| the six branches ship as a rival TA card | they do not ship as a card; the two additions route through WT–DS (queue 18) |
| a branch or state becomes a recorded code | R-A09; branches are next steps, states live on evidence items only |
| evidence gaps start driving next steps | the boundary rule: create a later authentic opportunity, never manufacture evidence |
| the matrix becomes a compliance tracker | it stays an inputs-side planning artefact; `data/` ships only in a full-pack situation, SHA-verified, and did not ship here |
