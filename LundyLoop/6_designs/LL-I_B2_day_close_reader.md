## 0 · The question, and the answer

**Who reads the pupil's written closure line, and when** — no initial, signatory, receipt, nothing stored/copied/counted/listed?

> **The pupil does, at the end of the day — reading their own line against the day's work and the next step the adult already gave.** The adult is audience, never verifier; nothing is recorded that was not already on the sheet.

**What closes the loop — the sentence the whole design rests on:**

> **The day-close read is not a second close — it is where the pupil reads, and the close stays on the pen.**

The loop closes when the pupil **writes** the line (GROW's own theory: *"writing it is what closes it"*). The day-close reading is a **strengthening**, and the reader F2 was looking for. Keeping the close on the pen, not the slot, is the point: if the re-read were the close, closure would move to a slot the pupil does not control — the failure this design exists to avoid.

---

## 1 · Why the pupil, not an adult — from measurement

- **B1.4 / R-H07:** GROW closure is *pupil-writes* by design; there is no adult R and none is wanted. "0 of 8" was BUILD-blindness (a gate keying on adult receipt), not a gap.
- **B2.0 F2:** the line has adult responders to the *work* (Yellow-Box/DIRT, the Feedback Sheet's *"my next step"*, the ASDAN Assessor Witness) but **no reader of the line itself.** An adult reader as *verifier* would rebuild BUILD's received-R on the wrong pathway — the species change R-A09 forbids and R-H08 warns TAs are primed to attempt.
- So the reader is **the pupil**, the adult stays **audience**. A **connection**, not authoring: the pupil already writes the line.

## 2 · The day-close flow — collate · read · close-out — on surfaces that already exist

Inside the existing Tutor-Time **check-out → evidence → exit** moment (B1.1). It adds a pupil action, not an artefact or a store.

**Collate** *(pupil, ~2 min)* — today's line(s), Feedback Sheet(s), work/photo into the pupil-held **clear A4 day sleeve** (LL-G steal-list): breaks nothing if lost, pupils are told so, an empty day is data. No collection, no new folder.

**Read together** *(pupil; adult is audience)* — the pupil reads, side by side:
1. their own written line — *"What I said, and what it changed"*;
2. the **next step the adult already gave** on the Feedback Sheet — *"Pupil response — my next step"* (present, not invented);
3. the **work, or its photo** (work not faces unless consent is filed).

**The question has no failing answer (amendment 1).** Not *"do these three agree?"* — for these pupils *"did your line match what you did?"* is a compliance check in a reflection's clothes, and the estate forbids that shape (a wrong prediction is never failure; *"nothing yet"* is a printed answer). Instead: **"what do these three show together?"** — or **"which of these would you show someone?"** No answer a pupil gives can be the wrong one, and **disagreement between the three is information about the day, never about the pupil.**

**Close-out** *(pupil finalises; adult returns to the next step)* — the pupil decides what goes into their evidence (ASDAN portfolio / AQA UAS), **with** the adult not **for** them. **The adult's day-close act is the return visit (amendment 2):** they go back to the next step they *already gave* at Independent Work and ask **"what happened to it?"** — they do **not** issue a new next step here. That closes LL-G's loop (the return visit was the only delivery gain LL-G ever claimed) instead of opening a second unread one at day level — the F2 defect, a layer up.

**Per pathway:** BUILD unchanged (ring + R already closes at lesson level; the written-line read is not imposed on it). GROW is the gap this fills. LAUNCH the same, feeding UAS; its step-up (B3) lives in the *line's demand*, not this mechanism.

---

## 3 · The slot is optional by design (amendment 3) — because closure is not

SEMH provision: absence, early finishes, trips, a day that ends badly — the slot will be missed, often. The design says so, **to pupils**:

> **The re-reading is a strengthening, not a requirement. The line is closed by writing it. A day whose close never happened is a closed day.**

Not *"catch it up tomorrow"* — that accumulates, and a backlog of unread lines becomes a portfolio someone audits (§6). Optional is not a concession: it *follows from* GROW's theory of closure. Require the re-read and closure silently moves from the pupil's pen to the slot.

---

## 4 · What is deliberately NOT built

- No adult R / initial / receipt / "seen by" on GROW or LAUNCH (R-A09, R-H08).
- No store, column, total, percentage, tally or list of lines or marks. **Reading is not recording.**
- No retyping — the carry is the pupil moving the sheet into the sleeve, not copying a mark (*"the first spreadsheet arriving politely"*).
- The Loop Mark ring stays single-copy on the BUILD sheet, never transcribed.
- Photos: work not faces unless consent filed.
- Assurance already lives in the per-lesson ASDAN/UAS evidence — the slot must not duplicate or aggregate it.

## 6 · Anti-design — how this rots, and the control

| the rot | the control |
|---|---|
| **The line becomes a tally** | nothing collected; the sleeve is pupil-held; the Loop-Walk check asks a **pupil** *"what does your line mean?"*, never a count (R-A09) |
| **The adult finishes the sentences** | the line is the pupil's own hand *or their words scribed verbatim* — never composed; adult is audience |
| **The read becomes a checklist done *to* the pupil** | no grid, no ticks; the pupil reads three things and decides; the question has no failing answer (§2) |
| **The next step multiplies** | the adult *returns* to the existing next step, issues none at the day-close (amendment 2) |
| **The backlog becomes an audited portfolio** | the slot is optional and the line is already closed by writing; a missed close is a closed day (amendment 3); the sleeve breaks nothing if lost |
| **The re-read becomes a receipt by the back door** | no signature/initial/receipt; the adult leaves no mark, only tomorrow's returned question |
| **A TA supplies the missing R** (R-H08) | the calibration-game pathway section: *no adult initial is expected on GROW/LAUNCH* |
