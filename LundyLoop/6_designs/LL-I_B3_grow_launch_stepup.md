## 0 · The finding B3 exists for

**B1.3:** the GROW and LAUNCH written closure line is **byte-identical** — *"Closing the loop: this one ends on paper… writing it is what closes it"* + *"What I said, and what it changed"* — FK grade 1.4 / reading age ~7.3 on **both**. ## 1 · Two caveats that decide the *axis*

- **The inversion.** The closure line (~7.3) reads *easier* than the BUILD strip (~10.4): the pathway currently steps **down** in loop-language demand at the seam where everything else steps up. The step-up must be **designed on purpose**.
- **The glyph caveat.** The formulas cannot see the BUILD strip's six-glyph ring-one format, so its effective demand is *lower* than 10.4 suggests. **Consequence:** do **not** chase the step-up by raising the sentence-level reading age. **Reading age is the wrong axis.** The step-up belongs in the **cognitive move the line demands**, not decoding difficulty.

## 2 · The step-up is a *dimension* — RATIFIED: evidence/warrant

- **GROW line — contribution + effect.** *"What I said, and what it changed"* — Voice + Influence. Right for GROW.
- **LAUNCH — contribution + effect + the WARRANT (*how the pupil knows it changed*).** Ratified, for three re-observable reasons:
  1. **It is the unclaimed AQA move.** LAUNCH's own assessed verdict form is already a warrant structure — *"Together the sources are ___ useful because each covers the other's bar"* — so the step-up **teaches the move the destination assesses**, honouring the standing rule that we assess on what the sequence teaches. Audience and mechanism would each add a dimension the paperwork never asks for.
  2. **It raises thinking, not decoding** — the only direction the glyph caveat permits (the pupil must now *defend*, not just report).
  3. **It reverses the inversion on purpose** — loop-language demand now steps up where everything else does, and the step is **cognitive rather than lexical**.

**Unchosen candidates, recorded not deleted** (a choice that names its alternatives can be revisited):
- **Audience** (*for whom* it changed) — **not chosen:** partly present in GROW's account work already, and the UAS paperwork does not ask for it.
- **Mechanism** (*why* it changed) — **not chosen:** it is the GROW causal-account move, so it does not *step up* from GROW; and, like audience, the destination never assesses it as the closure move.
