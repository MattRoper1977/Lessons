## 0 · The design, in one paragraph

Fifteen minutes at the end of the day, **two lanes with one boundary**. The **optional
day-close lane** is ratified B2 restated: the pupil reads their existing written line beside
their work and the next step the adult already gave; nothing is copied, signed or stored, and
its question — *"What do these show together?"* — is a ratified B2 Amendment 1 variant with no
failing answer. The **portfolio-curation lane** moves **one existing item**: the pupil says
what it shows, only exact words and missing metadata are added, and the item lands no further
than *candidate evidence queued for authorised review*. Four end states are displayed as equal:
**filed/queued · awaiting assessor check · returned for one authentic addition · no upload
today.**

## 2 · The B2 reconciliation, on paper

| B2 clause (ratified) | this design |
|---|---|
| Amendment 1 — the read has no failing answer | the read lane asks *"What do these show together?"* — a ratified variant; disagreement is information about the day, never the pupil |
| Amendment 2 — the adult returns to the step already given | no lane issues a next step; the handoff names an existing owner, never a new step |
| Amendment 3 — the slot is optional; a missed close is a closed day | "No upload today" is an equal outcome; the queue is session-only; nothing accumulates |
| the close stays on the pen | the read lane records nothing; the curation lane touches evidence items, never closure lines |

**The day-close lane is INPUT to LL-J, not run by this pass** (ruling §3.3) — LL-J owns the
day-close half, and the lane's material is attached to the LL-J queue entry in `HANDOVER.md`.

## 3 · "Returned for one authentic addition" — why it is KEPT here and was refused there

The same-day science ruling (LL-S1, Gate 6 of `quality/LUNDY_SCIENCE_ACCEPTANCE_GATES.md`)
refused *"returned for…"* as an end-state of a **reflective read** — a verdict handed to a
pupil. This design keeps *"Return to subject — needs a real later performance, audience,
revision or criterion element"* because it is a different object: an **evidence-state verdict
about the ITEM** in the curation lane, one of four outcomes presented as equally legitimate,
matching the estate's own claims-accuracy position (create a later authentic opportunity rather
than manufacture evidence). B2 Amendment 1 governs the day-close READ, which is a separate lane
recording nothing — **verified in the shipped Studio: the read lane presents no outcomes at
all.** The two rulings are reconciled on paper in the REGISTER records of this pass, not left
divergent by silence.

## 4 · Anti-design — how this rots, and the control

| the rot | the control |
|---|---|
| the optional read becomes a required close | closure is defined once, whole-school (74c33ae); the read lane records nothing; Gate 1 of `quality/LUNDY_ASDAN_ACCEPTANCE_GATES.md` |
| the queue becomes a backlog | session-only, cleared at close; "no upload today" is an equal outcome; Gate 5 |
| the studio starts creating evidence | the curation lane may only place what already exists; "never upload" list in the Studio; the tutor cannot create, invent or decide sufficiency |
| a receipt mark sneaks in | the Studio's own linter warns on `initial|seen by|adult receipt` for GROW/LAUNCH; the estate's Gate 3 |
| the four outcomes become a score | no counts, no percentages, no per-pupil rows; R-A09 |
