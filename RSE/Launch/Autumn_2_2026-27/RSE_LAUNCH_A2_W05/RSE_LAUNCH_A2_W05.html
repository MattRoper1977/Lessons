Arrival · 4 min

Invite a private regulation check. Green · Ready, Amber · Help, Red · Pause. Offer a quiet start, usual aid or pass. Use the cue and accept the pupil’s usual communication mode.

Starter · 2 min

Starter question 1 revisits last week. Take one minute for recall and reveal. What could you do if pressed to take an unknown substance? Cue: Pause · refuse · trusted adult. Expected: Refuse or move away if safe. Tell a trusted adult. Do not take an unknown substance. Then ask: Does agreeing to a photo mean agreeing to post it? Expected: No. Ask about this specific use. Respect a no or a changed answer. Use the made-up task. Begin in your book.

Teaching script 2

I do · 5 min

Watch, choose and check. Ask before sharing a photo. Respect a no or changed answer. Pause and seek help if concerned. Adult example: a person agreed to a photo but not a post. The reply is: I will not share it. We can use another image. Keep the first try. Ask if a step is unclear. Use your usual aid.

We do · 5 min

One person says no to posting a group photo. What now? Ask for a choice and a reason before revealing. C. Do not post the photo. A smaller group still counts as sharing. Try a new case: someone first agrees, then says no before posting. Stop the post and respect the new answer.

You do · 17 min

Keep first work and any change separate. Read or scribe exact pupil words as needed. Open your book. Use Concern, Action and Reason. Choose a paper case. Keep private details out. Add a respectful reply and a route to help. Supported route. Use cards A and B with adult reading help. Choose a safe action and a reason. Independent route. Teaching script 1

Arrival · 4 min

Invite a private regulation check. Green · Ready, Amber · Help, Red · Pause. Offer a quiet start, usual aid or pass. Use the cue and accept the pupil’s usual communication mode.

Starter · 2 min

Starter question 1 revisits last week. Take one minute for recall and reveal. What could you do if pressed to take an unknown substance? Cue: Pause · refuse · trusted adult. Expected: Refuse or move away if safe. Tell a trusted adult. Do not take an unknown substance. Then ask: Does agreeing to a photo mean agreeing to post it? Expected: No. Ask about this specific use. Respect a no or a changed answer. Use the made-up task. Begin in your book.

Made by Matt

Teaching script 2

I do · 5 min

Watch, choose and check. Ask before sharing a photo. Respect a no or changed answer. Pause and seek help if concerned. Adult example: a person agreed to a photo but not a post. The reply is: I will not share it. We can use another image. Keep the first try. Ask if a step is unclear. Use your usual aid.

We do · 5 min

One person says no to posting a group photo. What now? Ask for a choice and a reason before revealing. C. Do not post the photo. A smaller group still counts as sharing. Try a new case: someone first agrees, then says no before posting. Stop the post and respect the new answer.

Made by Matt

Teaching script 3

You do · 17 min

Keep first work and any change separate. Read or scribe exact pupil words as needed. Open your book. Use Concern, Action and Reason. Choose a paper case. Keep private details out. Add a respectful reply and a route to help. Supported route. Use cards A and B with adult reading help. Choose a safe action and a reason. Independent route. For three cards, give a concern and action. Explain a reason and a route to help. Aspire route. Explain why a small group still shares information. Adapt the advice when someone changes their answer. Choice access. Agree a route and response mode. Point, sign or direct a scribe.

Teaching script 4

Review · 4 min

Check one part. Respond to one helpful next step. Optional. Respond to feedback with an adult. You may pass. Mark R only after the pupil has responded to feedback with an adult. Keep the first response and any change separate. Edit in the same colour, on the same page or usual mode.

Exit · 3 min

Ask the two route checks. What helped you most today, or what would you change next time? Point, speak, sign, draw, write or choose. You may pass. Accept the pupil’s own example or a pass. Record their own words and actual support. At check-out, record E for … against the pupil's target.
