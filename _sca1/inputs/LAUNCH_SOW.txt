A21	IGCSE: primary route plus an optional stretch
A22	For most Launch learners the primary science route remains the laddered, fully accessible pathway (White Rose Science → Edexcel Entry Level Certificate 8939 → Edexcel GCSE Biology 1BI0 / Combined Science), which gives strong SEND access arrangements, multiple entry points and termly AQA UAS accreditation. ⏎  ⏎ In addition, the COMPLETE Edexcel International GCSE Sciences suite – Biology (4BI1), Chemistry (4CH1) and Physics (4PH1) – is offered as an OPTIONAL route for a small number of students who have the mathematical fluency, secure attendance and prior attainment to access its extra demand. Eligible students are entered for IGCSE Biology 4BI1 in place of GCSE Biology 1BI0, with IGCSE Chemistry and Physics taught as the separate-science stretch, giving a full three-science IGCSE offer. The option sits alongside the GCSE ladder rather than replacing it, so no learner is placed on a cliff edge: eligible students can aim higher while everyone keeps the accessible ladder and its banked, termly progress. Eligibility is confirmed by the science lead with the SENCo before a student is entered. ⏎  ⏎ The laddered English and maths routes are unchanged: English – Functional Skills (E1 to L2) → AQA Step Up to English → AQA GCSE English Language/Literature; Maths – White Rose → Entry Level / Functional Skills → Edexcel GCSE Mathematics.
