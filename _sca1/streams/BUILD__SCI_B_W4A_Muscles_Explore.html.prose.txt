✅ Success criteria
Know that muscles pull rather than push.
Describe one muscle contracting while its partner relaxes.
Use a model to explain bending and straightening.

STEP
Use a cardboard arm with two bands/strings. Pull the top band: the forearm bends.
STEP
Name the modelled change: biceps contracts/shortens; triceps relaxes.
STEP
Pull the lower band to straighten: the jobs swap. Neither band ever pushes.
