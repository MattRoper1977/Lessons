✔ What this model helps us see
It shows that friction acts against the direction of motion, and that grip comes from the pair of surfaces touching, not from one of them alone.
✘ What this model does not show
It leaves out the size of the force, the heat it produces, and the tiny roughness that is doing the actual work.
