Osmosis: Water Across a Membrane
Annotated scenarios + precise definition + one explanation/re-attempt.
W5L2 explores the core practical and percentage change.

Osmosis Core Practical
Results table + percentage-change calculations + explanation of gain/loss.
W5L3 graphs/interprets and evaluates the practical.

Analyse & Evaluate Osmosis Data
Graph interpretation + zero-crossing estimate + method improvement.
W6L1 introduces active transport and contrasts direction/energy.

Active Transport
Comparison table + classified examples + one causal explanation.
W6L2 explores root-hair/gut applications and uptake data.

Active Transport in Root Hairs & Gut
Annotated cell + graph interpretation + causal explanation.
W6L3 independently compares all three processes.
I can apply active transport to root hair and gut cells and interpret evidence linking uptake to respiration.
Compare Diffusion, Osmosis & Active Transport
