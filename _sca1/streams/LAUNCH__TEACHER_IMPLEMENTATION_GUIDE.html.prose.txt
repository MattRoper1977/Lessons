LAUNCH GCSE Biology v3 · Teacher Implementation Guide
Three lessons a week: Introduce → Explore → Do.
Introduce
Build precise GCSE knowledge from observation and prediction. Vocabulary and definitions arrive after the visual/evidence has created a reason for them.
Explore
Drive variables, practicals, models, data and calculations. Pupils test the idea and explain patterns. The activity is never the endpoint.
Do
Independent GCSE transfer: calculations, graph interpretation, evaluation, practical evidence or exam responses. Feedback must cause a visible learner action.
LAUNCH Lundy
SPACE → VOICE → AUDIENCE → INFLUENCE runs repeatedly. The learner edit/re-attempt/response stays on the existing work; the adult genuinely receives it; the next instruction, scaffold or retrieval plan changes.
Support staff
Wait → self-prompt → visual cue → general verbal → specific verbal → model → direct support. Reader/scribe and maths scaffolds are access arrangements, not a reduction in Biology demand. Step back as independence grows.
Assessment tone
No public grades or comparison.
Model answers/checklists show answer shape and scientific precision rather than generating grades.
A wrong first attempt followed by an understood correction is evidence of learning.
Stretch means deeper application/evaluation, not simply more questions.
Pupils joining mid-year:
every arrival slide carries a read-it-here route.
Autumn 1 W1–W2 were baseline weeks.
No science was taught in them, so the W3 lesson opens from what pupils already know rather than from a previous lesson. The baseline itself is delivered separately and is not in this repository.
