## Method and limits
- Cells come from explicit path patterns per pathway, subject and week (scratch script; patterns extend the G6 `cells.py` to W3–W7 and add the W7 folders `*_[AWARD]/Autumn2_W7_2026-27/`, `Science/*/Autumn2_W7_2026-27/`, `LAUNCH_[AWARD]/W13-W14_2026-27/`, `Art/Build/CREATIVE_ARTS_A2_W07/`, and `voc_launch_a2_wNN_`).
- "LIVE" = every .html, .pdf and LESSON_SOURCE.json of the cell on main returned 200 from https://madebymatt.uk/Lessons/ (URL-encoded, no-cache), with all files fetched rather than sampled. Phrase counts come from raw HTML/JSON and `pdftotext`, with whitespace collapsed. .docx/.pptx were not scanned.
- PR coverage = the PR's changed-file list matched against the same patterns, plus the alternative Humanities folders used by #756, #763 and #767. PR "draft"/mergeable/check states were read from GitHub at about 11:05 UTC.
- Not checked: catalogue (`resources.json`) entries per cell, and page rendering. A file served 200 is not proof that it displays correctly.
- Timetable = the repository's measured 2026-27 timetable. Its owner-decision slots (Behaviour Intervention vs D&T/practical) are not given rows.
