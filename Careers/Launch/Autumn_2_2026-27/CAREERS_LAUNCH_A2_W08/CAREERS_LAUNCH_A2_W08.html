I do · 6 min· 1/2

Show what I know: Autumn 2 check

If you missed a lesson

Use just the quick cards you need before the check. An adult can read and model the short try.

Week 1 · Show a true skill

A CV uses clear, truthful examples. Give a task and your own action rather than a vague claim about being good at everything. Try: Try one true action sentence.

Week 2 · Match the application

Read what a practice role asks for. Choose an example that fits the task and keep your own part clear. Try: Match sorting to a label-check action.

Week 3 · Check the draft

A digital copy may show private details in the background. Check the audience and cover, crop or remove private information. Try: Point to a detail that needs covering.

Week 4 · Answer and improve

Listen to the interview question, ask for it again if needed and give a true example. Keep the first answer and a change after feedback. Try: Try a clear example aloud or in your mode.

Week 5 · Ask a useful question

Use a clear enquiry question and listen or read for the reply. An unanswered point stays still to check; do not fill it with a guess. Try: Try a question about training.

Week 6 · Keep work findable

An index points to actual work. Label the version and status; keep a missing item as a gap. Try: Choose a truthful status label.

Week 7 · Check the whole record

Use real work, your own responses and actual help. A neat folder does not prove every skill; choose a specific next learning action. Try: Show one work clue and one next action.

Worked model

  1. Read the item and find what it asks.
  2. Use your own action or the made-up work clue.
  3. Keep the help, gap and next action clear.

Adult example: a work note says three labels were checked with a prompt. The claim can say I checked labels with help.

  • Use a quick card for a missed lesson before the check.
  • Keep an adult model separate from your own work.
  • One clear action is more useful than a vague boast.
