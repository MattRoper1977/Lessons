We do · 5 min

Choose and explain a step

Team game

Build it from a clue

Use paper cards for: Greet and ask / Note the actual reply / Mark what is still unknown.

Join one of four bands. Work in pairs in your band. One partner looks away from the model, if agreed. The other gives one clue at a time. Order or draw the cards. Then compare with the model and swap roles.

If agreed, hold the step cards and stand in order. A seated row works too. The teacher moves from team to team to check. You may point, sign or direct an adult. A quiet turn counts.

Earn 1 point for each step in the right place. Earn 1 more for a clear clue or a checked fix. Every team can score. Help each other. No points are taken for a retry.

This is a paper team game. Keep the task below and all adult checks.

Arrival · 4 min

Invite a private regulation check. Green · Ready, Amber · Help, Red · Pause. Offer a quiet start, usual aid or pass. Use the cue and accept the pupil’s usual communication mode.

Starter · 2 min

Starter question 1 revisits last week. Take one minute for recall and reveal. How did feedback help your practice interview last week? Cue: Example · feedback · retry. Expected: Use one specific suggestion to improve the answer, then try it again. Then ask: The reply gives no hours. Can you write hours as a fact? Expected: No. Mark hours to check. Ask a clear follow-up question. Use the made-up task. Begin in your book.

Teaching script 2

I do · 5 min

Watch, choose and check. Greet and ask one clear question. Listen or read, then note the reply. Mark unknown details to check. Adult example: Hello. What training is given for the stockroom role? Reply: A task demonstration is given at the start. Note: a demonstration is given. Hours: to check. Keep the first try. Ask if a step is unclear. Use your usual aid.

We do · 5 min

The reply gives training but no hours. What next? Ask for a choice and a reason before revealing. B. Keep hours as unknown. Ask: Could you tell me the hours for this role? Pair rehearsal: ask about hours. The adult replies: these are not confirmed. Write hours to check and ask how to find out.
