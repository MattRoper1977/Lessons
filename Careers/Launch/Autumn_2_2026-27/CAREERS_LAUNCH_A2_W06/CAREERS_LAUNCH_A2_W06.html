We do · 5 min

Choose and explain a step

Team game

Build it from a clue

Use paper cards for: Find the actual work / Label version and own part / Check claim and keep gaps.

Join one of four bands. Work in pairs in your band. One partner looks away from the model, if agreed. The other gives one clue at a time. Order or draw the cards. Then compare with the model and swap roles.

If agreed, hold the step cards and stand in order. A seated row works too. The teacher moves from team to team to check. You may point, sign or direct an adult. A quiet turn counts.

Earn 1 point for each step in the right place. Earn 1 more for a clear clue or a checked fix. Every team can score. Help each other. No points are taken for a retry.

Guidance

This is a practice portfolio. No application is sent. Use learner-owned work and keep adult models separate. Do not invent an interview, past action, independent skill or missing item. Cover private contact details on practice copies and keep originals securely with staff. Use neutral version labels rather than calendar labels on pupil pages. A neat index is not proof of work or readiness for employment.

Formative practice. Record actual actions, support and gaps. Accept the pupil’s own example or a pass. Record their own words and actual support.

Preparation and safety

Prepare the learner’s actual work records with privacy checks, blank index, books, labelled adult models and usual aids. Examples may include a skills record, practice application, interview record and enquiry draft; keep unavailable items as gaps. No live account, personal contact detail or external submission is needed.

Starter · 3 min

Starter question 1 revisits last week. Take one minute for recall and reveal. Last lesson, the reply did not give the hours. What did we note? Cue: Still to check · ask clearly. Expected: We marked the hours as still to check and asked a clear follow-up question. We did not invent the hours. Then ask: An index lists an application, but the page is missing. Is the work complete? Expected: No. The index is a guide to work, not the work itself. Mark the gap and plan to find or make the missing item. Start in the book. Use the reference cards.
