Week 1 · A strength and a task.

A skill is something a person can do. Link a strength to a task it could help.

Week 2 · Jobs and tasks.

A stock helper can sort paper stock. Match the role to a task and give a clue.

Week 3 · Two steps.

Week 4 · Ask when unsure.

Pause. Ask about the missing detail. Hear the reply and show the meaning before acting.

Agree a role, do that part and pass it on clearly. A sorter can ask the counter to count.

Use your own actual work to support a skill. A model made by another person is not your work.
