Lesson overviewBUILD · Week 1

What is a career? My journey so far

How is a career bigger than one job?

  • How is a career bigger than one job?
  • I can explain what a job and a career are and say one thing I might like to do in the future.
  • Use your own safe examples or the fictional case. Share only what you choose.

Learning intention

I can explain what a job and a career are and say one thing I might like to do in the future.

Success criteria

  • I can sort jobs and other experiences.
  • I can explain that a career develops over time.
  • I can name a possibility or something I want to find out.

Lundy Loop · your voice matters

SPACE

Choose a calm place and a supported way to take part.

VOICE

Say, point, draw, write or pass. A fictional example is welcome.

AUDIENCE

The adult listens and checks that your meaning is understood.

INFLUENCE

At the exit, agree one useful next step or change to your support.

I Do · learn and modelBUILD · Week 1

A journey has many parts

  • A job is one particular work role.
  • A career can include learning, different jobs, breaks and changes.
  • An idea for the future is a possibility, not a promise.
Your response

Does a career have to stay the same?

Reveal the model
What a good answer can look likeNo. New experience, learning and circumstances can change a career.
