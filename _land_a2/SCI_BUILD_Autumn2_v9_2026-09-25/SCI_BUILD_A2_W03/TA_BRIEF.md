Arrival (5 min): Point to a safe rock. Staff: Offer a safe kit choice and short first–then cue.

Starter (3 min): Think back. Say, sign or point. Staff: Ask three low-stakes prior questions; answers in note.

I Do (5 min): Watch two safe tests. Staff: Pre-teach words; show the full model; ask the show-me check.

We Do (7 min): Test two safe rocks. Staff: Hand over the missing step; offer F or H route. Reset once.

We Do 2 (5 min): All of us show one choice. Staff: Take all pupil responses at once; use decision rule and parallel hinge.

Independent (8 min): Show each test. Record what you saw. Staff: Choose one adult for supported pupils, one for group; swap across the week.

Review (4 min): Check one result. Hear a next step. Staff: Give VF and one NS(+); invite one self-edit. Reset once.

Exit (3 min): Tell an adult what you saw. Staff: Hear feedback and next step, choose next start, then one-minute reset.

Planned adaptation — cognition learning: Give a three-step visual card for test rock hardness and water; remove its last cue after a correct try.

Planned adaptation — communication interaction: Point to the real kit before a short spoken, signed or AAC response; wait for a turn.

Planned adaptation — semh: Offer a private first–then cue and planned pause before the hinge; re-enter with one step.

Planned adaptation — sensory physical: Use a stable tray, large cards or an accessible input; no forced touch.

Planned adaptation — eal: Rehearse a full M or H idea in any language before writing; keep the science challenge.

Planned adaptation — eef strategy: cognition learning: scaffolding; communication interaction: explicit instruction; semh: cognitive/metacognitive strategies; sensory physical: technology; eal: flexible grouping

Staff: confirm the planned adaptations actually used and record any changes below.

If behind — next lesson: Autumn 2 W04: Identify everyday uses of different rocks (links D&T / Construction).

## Individual target opportunities

Cognition & learning: We do: State the observed result at rock B, rather than guess it. Record the pupil’s own sequencing, attention or independence target, including the prompt level needed.

Communication & interaction: Check: respond to “The tray under a new rock C is wet before the water test. What should the class do first?” in the pupil’s agreed mode. Evidence an individual communication/turn-taking target without requiring speech.

SEMH: Arrival: Point to a safe rock. Use the agreed predictable start or pause/re-entry plan; evidence the pupil’s own regulation or engagement target privately.

Sensory/physical: Independent: Show each test. Record what you saw. Offer the agreed accessible input, enlarged material or adapted equipment; record the pupil’s target-related access/independence, not a diagnosis.

EAL: Model/shared rehearsal: use hardness, absorb, water, test with the actual example; allow first-language planning and oral rehearsal before the independent response. Record the agreed language target; EAL is not SEN.

Capture E against the pupil's own IEDP target on the school's evidence app; the IEDP carries their EHCP-linked and Boxall targets, so one record serves every plan. Follow the pupil's IBSP where it differs from these routines.
