Arrival (5 min): Pick a safe model or card to look at. Staff: Offer a safe kit choice and short first–then cue.

Starter (3 min): Think back. Say, sign or point. Staff: Ask three low-stakes prior questions; answers in note.

I Do (5 min): Watch one fact with one clue. Staff: Pre-teach words; show the full model; ask the show-me check.

We Do (7 min): Match a real model to a fact. Staff: Hand over the missing step; offer F or H route. Reset once.

We Do 2 (5 min): Choose one answer. All of us show it. Staff: Take all pupil responses at once; use decision rule and parallel hinge.

Independent (8 min): Show work you did about body, food or rock. Staff: Choose one adult for supported pupils, one for group; swap across the week.

Review (4 min): Hear one next step. Fix a part if you can. Staff: Give VF and one NS(+); invite one self-edit. Reset once.

Exit (3 min): Tell an adult what you know and how. Staff: Hear feedback and next step, choose next start, then one-minute reset.
