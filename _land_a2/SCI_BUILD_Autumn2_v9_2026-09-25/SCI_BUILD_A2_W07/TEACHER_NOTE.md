## Faded example, tiered task and stretch

I do full: “I point to the bone model. It shows a skeleton. The bones help hold the body up.” We do part-done: “I point to a safe rock. I felt a rough side. My fact is ____.” **Removed step:** Use the real observation to state a fact about the rock. Independent: no filled step. **Next fade:** Show only an object; pupil chooses the question, evidence and fact.

We do F: Point to one model. What can it show? M: Match one fact to the model or action that supports it. H: Which fact needs a new check rather than a card? Independent F: Show one fact about a body or rock. M: Show two facts. Tell how you found one. H: Pick your best real evidence. Why does it fit? **Go deeper:** Pick a claim you cannot show. What could you check? **Named harder stretch — Evidence judge:** Pick the best real result. Why does it fit?

## Hinge and decision

**Question:** A class claims a clean plastic lid floats. Which evidence could check the claim?

| Option | Correct? | Diagnosis |
|---|---|---|
| A photo of a different lid. | no | A different item is not direct evidence for this lid. |
| A guess from the lid colour. | no | Colour alone does not test floating. |
| A card about another rock. | no | Evidence about another item misses the claim. |
| A safe water test of that lid. | yes | Selects a direct safe observation of the claimed behaviour. |

**Rule:** About 3 in 4 or more right → move on; otherwise re-model I point to the bone model. It shows a skeleton. The bones help hold the body up. Ask the parallel hinge. No scores or answers saved. **Parallel hinge:** A pupil claims a toy rolls farther on a smooth mat. What evidence could check it? Compare measured rolls from the same start on two mats.

**Misconceptions:**

| Misconception | Spot | Correct |
|---|---|---|
| A picture is proof of a rock hardness result. | Offers a food or generic rock picture as a test result. | Use an actual safe sample or genuine recorded result from a completed test. |
| A skeleton model and a rock sample answer the same question. | Uses one item for every claim. | Match each question to a relevant model or observation. |


**Feedback codes:** VF · WS · I · NS(+) · E · R · // · ? At We do 2, record WS or I and actual prompt level; at Review, VF and specific NS(+); at Exit, one self-edit // or read back ? R means a response to the next step heard by an adult in the next lesson, never auto-awarded. E: evidence captured on the school's evidence app only if an observable curriculum action occurred, never while distressed. The page stores no response. Pathways: high support with VF/WS; Build: mix of WS/I; Grow: mostly I and self-edit; Launch: independent self-assessment.

## Adults, regulation and prompt ladder

Wait and count to 5. → Point to the object or model. → Ask: What will you check next? → Model one step and give the next turn back. → Finish one example together, then let the pupil try. No physical prompt. Stage scripts: I do “Watch my first step. What do you see?”; We do “What step is missing? Show it with the kit.”; Independent “What can you do first on your own?”. At Independent, one adult works with supported pupils, the other holds the group; choose roles at the start and swap across the week. If only one adult attends, use the actual staffing plan. At Exit, hand off the deepest prompt level and what was independent.

**Cold-call / invite bank, answers:**
- F: Point to a bone model. → The bone model or card; What is a skeleton? → Bones that help hold a body up.
- M: What can show a fact about a rock? → A safe rock sample or real result from a completed test; What does evidence mean? → What you saw or did to support a claim.
- H: Which claim needs a fresh check? → A claim about a result no one has observed or recorded; Why cannot a card prove hardness? → It labels an idea but is not a test on that particular sample.

**Regulation:** Agree a quiet pause and supervised place. Give one short cue. Re-enter at the last completed step. No evidence while distressed.

## Prepared model handling · practical safety

**Kit:** Clean adult-checked model or rock, matching fact cards, tray

**Method:** Choose one checked model or sample and match an observed fact to it.

**Pupil Risk:** A loose or rough model part could scratch or fall.

**Pupil Safe:** Use checked blunt models on a tray. Keep small parts together and wash hands after shared kit.
