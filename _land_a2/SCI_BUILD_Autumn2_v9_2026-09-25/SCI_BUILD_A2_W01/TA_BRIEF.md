Arrival (5 min): Look at the kit. Point to one thing you know. Staff: Offer one safe, clean sheet and one clean tub; use first–then and a visual cue.

Starter (3 min): Think back. Say it, sign it or point. Staff: Ask all three retrieval questions orally; source cells and answers are in this note.

I Do (5 min): Watch me sort one clean card. Staff: Pre-teach the four words with the kit; model the full sort; then ask the show-me check.

We Do (7 min): Sort the clean cards with us. Staff: Use the part-done model, then the live card sort; offer F and H routes without changing the goal.

We Do 2 (5 min): Show one choice at the same time. Staff: Use symbol or point by default. Apply the 3-in-4 rule and a parallel hinge if needed; include one quiet-minute reset.

Independent (8 min): Choose a clean item. Show what you can do. Staff: One adult supports pupils and the other holds the group; swap over the week. Hands-on item or card is valid.

Review (4 min): Show one step. Hear one next step. Try it. Staff: Give VF and a specific NS(+); let the pupil edit (//). Include one quiet-minute reset.

Exit (3 min): Show how you know. Tell your teacher. Staff: Hear the pupil by speech, sign, point, drawing or writing; use the response to choose the next starter. Include one quiet-minute reset.

Planned adaptation — cognition learning: Give a three-card visual sequence and one action at a time; retain the full goal.

Planned adaptation — communication interaction: Model point/sign/speech choices, pause for an answer, and use the same three labelled cards.

Planned adaptation — semh: Offer a private first–then cue and a planned pause before the hinge; re-enter with one choice.

Planned adaptation — sensory physical: Use large clean card or a tray that stays in place; point or use an accessible switch/keyboard rather than grasping.

Planned adaptation — eal: Pair for spoken rehearsal in any language, then use the same visual words and M/H challenge; do not move the pupil to F for language alone.

Planned adaptation — eef strategy: cognition learning: scaffolding; communication interaction: explicit instruction; semh: cognitive/metacognitive strategies; sensory physical: technology; eal: flexible grouping

Staff: confirm the planned adaptations actually used and record any changes below.

If behind — must teach core: Choose a clean item. Show what you can do. Preserve the individual evidence required above.

If behind — shorten or merge: After a secure hinge, shorten repeated oral rehearsal. If needed, merge the exit reflection into the Arrival of Autumn 2 W02. Keep the core evidence and any safety teaching; record the change and coverage impact.

If behind — next lesson: Autumn 2 W02: Compare and group rocks by their appearance and simple properties.

If behind — dependency: Pass on today’s checked understanding and unresolved hinge errors for the next starter. Do not replace incomplete individual work with a group product.

SEMH: Arrival: Look at the kit. Point to one thing you know. Use the agreed predictable start or pause/re-entry plan; evidence the pupil’s own regulation or engagement target privately.
