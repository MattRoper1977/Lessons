Researching a genetic condition

I am learning to use a named source to present careful facts about a genetic condition.

Words to use

WordMeaningExampleMy language
inheritedpassed to a child through genesThe card says cystic fibrosis is inherited. 
varianta changed version of a geneThe card describes a changed CFTR variant. 
carriera person with one changed copy in this recessive exampleA carrier usually does not have cystic fibrosis. 
sourcewhere a claim comes fromThe NHS page is the source for this card. 

Model

The NHS card says cystic fibrosis is inherited. Changed CFTR copies come from both parents. One changed copy usually means a carrier; two usually cause the condition. Name the NHS card with each claim.

Offline source card

NHS card: Cystic fibrosis is inherited. It can affect breathing and digestion. Changed CFTR copies are inherited from both parents; one changed copy usually makes a carrier. Treatments can ease symptoms. The genetic cause is inherited; infection precautions are separate. This card predicts nothing about a person.

Source: NHS, Cystic fibrosis; NHS Genomics Education Programme, Cystic fibrosis. Checked 24 September 2026.
