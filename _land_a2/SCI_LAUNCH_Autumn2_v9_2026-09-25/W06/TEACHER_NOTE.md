## AfL hinge and misconceptions

**Question at We do 2:** A fictional family-source card says altered gene copies passed from parents to their child. Which claim does that evidence support?

| Option | Correct? | Diagnosis |
|---|---|---|
| A. The altered copies spread through a shared cough. | no | Confuses an inherited CFTR variant with an infection spread by a cough. |
| B. The altered copies arose from the child’s wish. | no | Attributes inherited gene copies to a child’s wish instead of parental transmission. |
| C. The altered copies were inherited from the parents. | yes | Uses the new case accurately. |
| D. The altered copies travelled through drinking water. | no | Confuses inherited gene copies with an environmental exposure through water. |

**Decision:** About 3 in 4 or more right → move on. Otherwise re-model an anonymous one-copy/two-copy card and the named NHS line, then ask the parallel hinge. No automatic mark or saved response. **Parallel hinge:** Another fictional card says altered gene copies were passed through eggs or sperm. Does this support inheritance or person-to-person infection? Answer: inheritance.

| Misconception | How to spot | Correction |
|---|---|---|
| The genetic cause of cystic fibrosis can spread by contact. | Pupil chooses A, treating the altered copies as contagious. | Use the NHS inheritance line: CFTR changes are inherited. Separately, people with CF may need infection-control advice; do not conflate the genetic condition with infections. |
| A carrier with one changed copy must have cystic fibrosis. | Pupil chooses C correctly but says one changed copy always means the condition. | Contrast the carrier and two-copy rows in the NHS source card; use “usually” and no real-person claim. |

## Feedback, EHCP broad areas and regulation

**What I record and where:** note hinge response split and the named misconception on the staff quick-record grid; record actual code and prompt level in feedback. E: evidence captured on the school's evidence app. Never gather evidence during distress or store pupil data in HTML.

**Regulation:** Check in before the hinge, top rung and Independent. At the agreed pause signal give space and a single return step. Collect no evidence while a pupil is distressed. Re-enter at the last completed step, with one small choice and private correction.

## Sources, checks and limits

Every scientific and diagram label claim is listed in `LESSON_SOURCE.json` with a named official source and status. False distractors are deliberately incorrect and diagnosed. Read source claims in context; no medical claim about an individual. The workbook says “online”; the accepted lesson uses a curated offline NHS source card to meet the zero-external-load rule. No pupil research on their own phone and no individual diagnosis. The genetic cause is inherited; people with CF may separately need infection-control advice, as the NHS page explains.
