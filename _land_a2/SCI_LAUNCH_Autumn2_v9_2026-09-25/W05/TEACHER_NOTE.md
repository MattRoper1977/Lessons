## AfL hinge and misconceptions

**Question at We do 2:** New cross: Aa × Aa. What is the chance of aa for each offspring?

| Option | Correct? | Diagnosis |
|---|---|---|
| A. 1 in 4 for each offspring. | yes | Uses the new case accurately. |
| B. 1 in 2 for each offspring. | no | Counts the two heterozygous squares rather than the one aa square. |
| C. 3 in 4 for each offspring. | no | Counts all outcomes except aa rather than the recessive genotype. |
| D. Certain for every single offspring. | no | Treats one possible aa square as a guaranteed outcome. |

**Decision:** About 3 in 4 or more right → move on. Otherwise re-model the Aa × aa top and side headings with two colours, pair one allele in each square, then ask the parallel hinge. No automatic mark or saved response. **Parallel hinge:** Third cross: AA × aa. What is the chance of Aa for each offspring? Answer: certain; every possible pair contains one A and one a. This does not set an exact family size.

| Misconception | How to spot | Correction |
|---|---|---|
| A possible aa outcome is certain for every offspring. | Pupil chooses D, making the aa outcome certain. | Each offspring has one chance in four in the model; it is not guaranteed. |
| The two Aa cells in a cross count as aa. | Pupil chooses B, counting Aa squares as aa. | Read the two allele symbols in each square; only one square is aa. |
