LAUNCH Science Autumn 2 W5

A simple genetic cross

I am learning to calculate genetic probability with a Punnett square and evaluate its limits.

Words to use

WordMeaningExampleMy language
allelea version of a geneA and a represent two alleles in our invented model. 
genotypethe pair of alleles shown by lettersAa is one possible genotype in this model. 
dominantan allele shown by one copy in this simple modelA is dominant in our invented flower model. 
recessivean allele shown when two copies are present in this modelaa shows the recessive version in our model. 
Punnett squarea grid that shows possible allele pairsThe Punnett square for Aa × aa has four cells. 

Model

Place A/a atop the grid for one parent; place a/a at its side for the other. Pair one allele from each edge: Aa, aa, Aa, aa. Two of four boxes are aa: a 1 in 2 chance per offspring, not an exact family count.

How I know

  • I can pair one allele from each parent.
  • I can find the aa chance in four squares.
  • I can evaluate why probability does not guarantee an exact count.
