LAUNCH Science Autumn 2 W3

Stem-cell decisions

I am learning to weigh a possible benefit and risk of stem-cell research using evidence.

Words to use

WordMeaningExampleMy language
stem cella cell that can divide and form specialised cell typesStem cells are being studied for possible medical uses. 
benefita possible helpful resultA possible benefit would be replacing damaged cells. 
riska possible harmful result or uncertain effectA study must consider a risk to volunteers. 
evidenceinformation that tests or supports a claimThe trial needs evidence of both benefit and harm. 

Model

A fictional proposal may replace damaged cells. A possible harm is unknown. A careful study must measure both benefit and risk; possible use is not proven treatment.

How I know

  • I can name a possible benefit and risk.
  • I can ask for evidence that tests a claim.
  • I can justify further research without claiming a cure.

You can plan in any language. Speak before you write: Speaking: “I would recommend … because …; I would also check …”
