Science · LAUNCH · Autumn 2

STAFF · Unit rationale · 24 September 2026

What we teach and why

Topic 2, Cells & control; Topic 3, Genetics (intro). Enquiry: How can we use observations and evidence to explain topic 2, cells & control; topic 3, genetics? Science develops explanations of the natural world, safe investigation, measurement and critical use of evidence, helping pupils make informed decisions about health, materials and the environment.

Weekly outcomes

W01: Describe mitosis & the cell cycle (animation). W02: Explain growth & stem cells. W03: Stem-cell ethics discussion (links RE/PSHE). W04: Describe DNA, genes & chromosomes. W05: Complete a genetic cross (digital Punnett square). W06: Research a genetic condition online; present. W07: Topics 2 to 3 assessment (Dec).

Why it is right for our pupils

For pupils ready for formal qualifications with planned therapeutic support, access arrangements and reasonable adjustments. The policy’s indicative reading profile is 11+ and access to GCSE/Functional Skills reading. Regulation, relational check-ins, executive-function support and exam stamina remain part of teaching. Predictable routine, an early achievable task, response-mode choice, no cold calling and a quiet alternative support re-engagement.

Qualification and entry

Core: Pearson Edexcel GCSE Biology 1BI0 / Combined Science 1SC0 Foundation. The SoW also lists optional IGCSE Biology 4BI1, Chemistry 4CH1 and Physics 4PH1 for eligible pupils under a separate programme. Biology 1BI0 Foundation is the named core route; Biology versus Combined Science entry, full specification coverage, tier and series: staff to confirm. Optional IGCSE entry is not implied. Wider pathway: GCSE; Functional Skills Level 1–Level 2; BTEC; ASDAN CoPE. The policy identifies these as pathway routes; the subject SoW determines which route this teaching actually feeds. Exact individual entry/unit is for staff to confirm.

Why this route

Recognised qualifications and explicit application of knowledge connect sustained subject learning to suitable further study, training and employment.

Next step

Source evaluation, quantitative reasoning, self-advocacy and sustained independent work prepare pupils for college, apprenticeships, work experience or a supported internship, as appropriate. Gatsby 4: connect today’s subject skills with relevant work and further-study roles.

Evidence of impact

F: model-building checks, quick recall tests. S: Topics 2 to 3 assessment (Dec). Books and staff observation capture the actual individual contribution, support and response to feedback. Feed attendance/engagement, behaviour, qualifications and destinations measures as categories; no figures or impact claims are supplied.

The progression chain

Re-engage: predictable access and early success. Attendance and engagement: build and review participation. Meaningful qualifications: map actual evidence to confirmed criteria. Skills for adulthood: practise safe choices, communication and independence. Positive destinations: connect learning to an appropriate next step. Reduce NEET: sustain participation in education, employment or training through reviewed transition support.
