Arrival (5 min): Look at this week’s seedling and skin-repair model. Point to one detail you notice. Staff: Offer a private, predictable start. Do not require previous knowledge.

Starter (3 min): Recall three ideas from earlier lessons. Show one answer at a time. Staff: All pupils respond; reveal the answers only after attempts. Revisit success criteria.

I Do (5 min): Watch the full model. Point to the step that answers today’s question. Staff: Model one complete example; check every pupil’s response to the show-me question.

We Do (7 min): Complete the missing step in the part-done model. Speak, then match the cards. Staff: Wait five seconds, gesture, then cue if needed. Support the route without supplying the missing answer.

We Do 2 (5 min): Choose one hinge answer. Show it at the same time as the group. Staff: Collect simultaneous responses, apply the decision rule and parallel hinge in the staff note.

Independent (8 min): A skin model shows division. What gives a new cell its job? Staff: At lesson start choose one adult with supported pupils and one holding the group; swap during the week. Record independence and prompt level.

Review (4 min): Tell a partner what you changed after feedback. Mark a real edit // and read it back when asked ?. Staff: Give a precise VF or NS(+) only when it actually happens. Invite a true self-edit and read-back.

Exit (3 min): Show what you know with a model and explain one reason. Staff: Hear the answer privately in a valid mode and use it to change the next example or prompt.
