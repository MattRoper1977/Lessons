LAUNCH Science Autumn 2 W2

Growth and stem cells

I am learning to explain how cells divide and then take on different jobs for growth and repair.

Words to use

WordMeaningExampleMy language
differentiationa cell becoming specialised for a particular jobA developing skin cell differentiates to do a job in skin. 
stem cella cell that can divide and form specialised cell typesSome adult stem cells make replacement blood cells. 
meristema plant region whose cells divide to make new cellsA root tip contains a meristem. 
growthan increase in size, including by making more cellsA shoot grows as its cells divide and elongate. 

Model

In skin, mitosis adds cells; some stem-cell descendants differentiate for a tissue job. At a shoot tip, meristem cells divide, then elongate and differentiate.

How I know

  • I can distinguish making more cells from cells taking on a specialised job.
  • I can compare animal growth with plant growth.
  • I can identify a role for an animal stem cell or a plant meristem.
