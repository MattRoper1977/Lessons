## AfL hinge and misconceptions

**Question at We do 2:** Which paired statement is scientifically accurate?

| Option | Correct? | Diagnosis |
|---|---|---|
| A. Mitosis halves chromosome number; a gene is the whole genome. | no | Confuses mitosis with meiosis and gene with genome. |
| B. Mitosis removes DNA; an allele is a separate cell. | no | Treats DNA as used up and allele as a cell. |
| C. Mitosis makes one unsplit cell; DNA is only in proteins. | no | Misses cell splitting and confuses DNA with protein. |
| D. Mitosis gives matching body cells; a gene is a section of DNA. | yes | Secure cell-cycle and gene distinction. |

**Decision:** About 3 in 4 or more right → begin the check. Otherwise re-model a two-colour copy/separate/split strip and a short gene mark on a DNA string, then ask the parallel hinge. Do not pre-answer the three check items. **Parallel hinge:** What shows a gene on our string model? A. A marked section of the DNA string (expected). B. The whole organism. C. A cell outside DNA. D. An empty grid square.

| Misconception | How to spot | Correction |
|---|---|---|
| Mitosis halves chromosomes. | Pupil chooses A, the halving claim. | Use a separate two-colour strip to show copying and matching sets; do not coach the assessment response. |
| A gene is the whole genome. | Pupil chooses A, calling a gene the whole genome. | Show a short mark on a separate DNA string, then leave the check item unsupported. |
