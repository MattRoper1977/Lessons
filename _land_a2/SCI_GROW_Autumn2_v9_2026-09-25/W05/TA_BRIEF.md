Arrival (5 min): Match each question to a kit card: toy and surface, globe and torch, or warming chain. Staff: Offer a predictable seat or standing spot and the first visual cue. No public test.

Starter (3 min): Think back. Say, point or write one answer for each question. Staff: Ask three low-stakes retrieval questions; prior coverage must be checked.

I Do (5 min): I choose a toy for a motion question. The toy went less far on rough paper in this run. I must repeat before I claim why. Staff: Model the full reasoning step, pre-teach four words and ask the show-me question.

We Do (7 min): Torch and ball are chosen for day and night. Add the missing clue. Say why it fits. Staff: Show the part-done example, then allow paired talk before an answer. One-minute reset inside this stage.

We Do 2 (5 min): A toy travels less far on rough paper in this example. Choose the best idea to test. Staff: All pupils answer the four-option hinge; use the decision rule, not a score.

Independent (8 min): Choose two stations. Give a claim and matching clue for each. Name one limit. Staff: One adult supports agreed pupils, the other holds the group; swap across the week. One-minute reset inside this stage.

Review (4 min): Does your clue fit? Fix one clue or add a limit. Staff: Give a specific verbal comment (VF) and invite one self-edit (//) or read-back (?).

Exit (3 min): Name a model, claim and clue. Say what changed after feedback. Staff: Hear the answer and name how it changes the next example or support. One-minute reset and handoff.

## Individual target opportunities

Cognition & learning: We do: Name the observation of the marked place moving away from the light. Record the pupil’s own sequencing, attention or independence target, including the prompt level needed.

Communication & interaction: Check: respond to “A model trolley stops sooner on a mat than on a smooth board. Which idea is relevant to test?” in the pupil’s agreed mode. Evidence an individual communication/turn-taking target without requiring speech.

SEMH: Arrival: Match each question to a kit card: toy and surface, globe and torch, or warming chain. Use the agreed predictable start or pause/re-entry plan; evidence the pupil’s own regulation or engagement target privately.

Sensory/physical: Independent: Choose two stations. Give a claim and matching clue for each. Name one limit. Offer the agreed accessible input, enlarged material or adapted equipment; record the pupil’s target-related access/independence, not a diagnosis.

EAL: Model/shared rehearsal: use force, rotation, evidence, climate with the actual example; allow first-language planning and oral rehearsal before the independent response. Record the agreed language target; EAL is not SEN.

Capture E against the pupil's own IEDP target on the school's evidence app; the IEDP carries their EHCP-linked and Boxall targets, so one record serves every plan. Follow the pupil's IBSP where it differs from these routines.
