Arrival (5 min): Sort these: weather today; climate over many years. Point to the long-term idea. Staff: Offer a predictable seat or standing spot and the first visual cue. No public test.

Starter (3 min): Think back. Say, point or write one answer for each question. Staff: Ask three low-stakes retrieval questions; prior coverage must be checked.

I Do (5 min): I order the chain: burn fuel → more carbon dioxide → more heat stays → warming. Cutting emissions acts near the start. A flood defence helps with an impact. Staff: Model the full reasoning step, pre-teach four words and ask the show-me question.

We Do (7 min): The burn fuel and impact cards are in place. Sort the missing gas and warming cards. Explain the link. Staff: Show the part-done example, then allow paired talk before an answer. One-minute reset inside this stage.

We Do 2 (5 min): Choose what follows burning more fossil fuel in our warming chain. Everyone answers together. Staff: All pupils answer the four-option hinge; use the decision rule, not a score.

Independent (8 min): Build a cause-to-impact chain. Compare an emissions cut with help for an impact. Give one reason for each. Staff: One adult supports agreed pupils, the other holds the group; swap across the week. One-minute reset inside this stage.

Review (4 min): Hear feedback. Move one chain card and say why. Staff: Give a specific verbal comment (VF) and invite one self-edit (//) or read-back (?).

Exit (3 min): Name a cause, an effect and an action. Which link does your action change? Staff: Hear the answer and name how it changes the next example or support. One-minute reset and handoff.

Planned adaptation — eal: Teach gas, cause and impact with arrows. Plan in any language; use M/H frames and keep the comparison challenge.

## Individual target opportunities

Communication & interaction: Check: respond to “A bus depot burns more diesel. Which immediate link belongs in its emissions chain?” in the pupil’s agreed mode. Evidence an individual communication/turn-taking target without requiring speech.

SEMH: Arrival: Sort these: weather today; climate over many years. Point to the long-term idea. Use the agreed predictable start or pause/re-entry plan; evidence the pupil’s own regulation or engagement target privately.

Sensory/physical: Independent: Build a cause-to-impact chain. Compare an emissions cut with help for an impact. Give one reason for each. Offer the agreed accessible input, enlarged material or adapted equipment; record the pupil’s target-related access/independence, not a diagnosis.
