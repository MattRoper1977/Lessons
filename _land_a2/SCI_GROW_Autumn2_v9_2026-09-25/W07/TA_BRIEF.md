Arrival (5 min): Choose a model used this term. Point to one question it could help answer. Staff: Offer a predictable seat or standing spot and the first visual cue. No public test.

Starter (3 min): Think back. Say, point or write one answer for each question. Staff: Ask three low-stakes retrieval questions; prior coverage must be checked.

I Do (5 min): I choose a question, then a matching model and clue. For day and night, the torch lights one side of a turning ball. I explain the place turning into shade. This is practice, not help during formal evidence. Staff: Model the full reasoning step, pre-teach four words and ask the show-me question.

We Do (7 min): In practice, the question and torch-and-ball model are supplied. Complete the missing clue: what happens to the marked place as the ball turns? Staff: Show the part-done example, then allow paired talk before an answer. One-minute reset inside this stage.

We Do 2 (5 min): Show everyone which model best helps explain day and night. Staff: All pupils answer the four-option hinge; use the decision rule, not a score.

Independent (8 min): Choose two science questions from forces, space or climate. Show an answer with a model and a matching clue. Use only your agreed support if this is formal evidence. Staff: One adult supports agreed pupils, the other holds the group; swap across the week. One-minute reset inside this stage.

Review (4 min): In practice, hear one comment on claim and clue. Self-edit one mismatch. If this is formal evidence, receive and act on feedback after collection under the agreed conditions. Staff: Give a specific verbal comment (VF) and invite one self-edit (//) or read-back (?).

Exit (3 min): Tell the adult what you learned, which clue you used, your feedback and one next step. They use your answer to plan the next model or support. Staff: Hear the answer and name how it changes the next example or support. One-minute reset and handoff.
