Arrival (5 min): Choose the body that makes its own light: Sun, Earth or Moon. Staff: Offer a predictable seat or standing spot and the first visual cue. No public test.

Starter (3 min): Think back. Say, point or write one answer for each question. Staff: Ask three low-stakes retrieval questions; prior coverage must be checked.

I Do (5 min): I read card B: Earth goes round the Sun. I point to card B as my source. A claim without a matching source is a guess. Staff: Model the full reasoning step, pre-teach four words and ask the show-me question.

We Do (7 min): Card A is matched to Sun is a star. Card B has a fact but no label. Find the missing source label for Earth goes round the Sun. Staff: Show the part-done example, then allow paired talk before an answer. One-minute reset inside this stage.

We Do 2 (5 min): Choose the claim that our class fact cards support. Show one answer at the same time. Staff: All pupils answer the four-option hinge; use the decision rule, not a score.

Independent (8 min): Share two facts about the Solar System. Name the class card that supports each fact. Correct one claim if its card does not match. Staff: One adult supports agreed pupils, the other holds the group; swap across the week. One-minute reset inside this stage.

Review (4 min): Hear which card backs your fact. Change a source label or fact if needed, then say what changed. Staff: Give a specific verbal comment (VF) and invite one self-edit (//) or read-back (?).

Exit (3 min): Say one checked fact and point to the matching class card. Say which source label you fixed. Staff: Hear the answer and name how it changes the next example or support. One-minute reset and handoff.
