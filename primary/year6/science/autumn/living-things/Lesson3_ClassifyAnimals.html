I do · 2 4 min

Model good vs poor key questions, then watch a key build.

Fair questions only

A key asks yes/no questions to split animals, step by step.

Model the ruleA key question must be closed (yes/no) and observable (a feature you can see). "Does it have six legs?" ✓   "Is it cute?" ✗ (that's an opinion).

Watch for: classifying by where an animal lives or how it moves — use body features instead.

TA Focus · Classify animals
Traps to watch

Pupils classify by habitat ("lives in water") or movement ("it swims"). Steer them to body features — legs, wings, scales, feathers.

Tier steer
  • 🌱 Spot a yes/no question vs an opinion.
  • 🌿 Use a key correctly, one branch at a time.
  • 🌳 Build a multi-step key and justify the order.
Always

Every key question = closed (yes/no) + observable.
Cold Call · Question bank
  • What is a classification key?
  • Give a good yes/no question.
  • Why is "Is it cute?" a bad question?
  • What does "observable" mean?
  • What is the first split for animals?
  • Turn "Is it big?" into a fair question.
