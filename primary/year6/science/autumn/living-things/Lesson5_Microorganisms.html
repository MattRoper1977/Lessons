I do · 2 4 min

Explain the mouldy-bread investigation and bust the myths.

The mouldy bread test

Mould is a fungus — it spreads and grows over time.

Model the mythsNot all bacteria are harmful — many help us. And you can't see a single microbe with your eyes; you need a microscope. Seal damp and dry bread in bags and watch the mould over days.

Safety: keep the bags sealed shut — never open them once the bread is inside.
