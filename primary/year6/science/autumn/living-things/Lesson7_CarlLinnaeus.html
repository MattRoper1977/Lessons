I do · 2 4 min

Show the tree of life, then the limits of his model.

A brilliant start — but not perfect

Linnaeus sorted life into nested groups, from big to small.

Model the limitsLinnaeus didn't have all our tools. He didn't know about microorganisms, and he didn't separate reptiles as their own group. As science learned more, the system was improved.

Big idea: a model can be useful and still have limits. Science keeps improving its models.

Discuss together
Then: no microscopes → no microorganisms. Now: microscopes → we classify bacteria, viruses and fungi too.
