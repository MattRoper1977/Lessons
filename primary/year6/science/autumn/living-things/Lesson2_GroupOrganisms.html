Whales come up to breathe air — that makes them mammals, not fish!

Model the mythsNot every sea animal is a fish — a whale is a mammal (it breathes air). And not every plant has flowers.

🌸 Flowering plants

Make flowers, fruit and seeds. E.g. rose, daisy, apple tree.

🌿 Non-flowering

No flowers — make spores instead. E.g. fern, moss.

Invertebrate groups to know: insects (6 legs), arachnids (8 legs), molluscs (soft body).

TA Focus · Group organisms
Traps to watch

“All sea animals are fish” (whale = mammal, breathes air). “All plants have flowers” (ferns, moss don't). Soft body ≠ invertebrate automatically — it's the backbone that decides.

Tier steer
  • 🌱 One rule at a time (legs / no legs; backbone / none).
  • 🌿 Group + one feature as evidence.
  • 🌳 Separate two vertebrate groups by feature; justify the tricky ones.
Always

Backbone is the deciding feature — not size, shape or where it lives.
Cold Call · Question bank
  • What is a vertebrate?
  • Name the five vertebrate groups.
  • One feature of a bird?
  • Why is a whale not a fish?
  • Name a non-flowering plant.
  • How many legs has an insect? An arachnid?
