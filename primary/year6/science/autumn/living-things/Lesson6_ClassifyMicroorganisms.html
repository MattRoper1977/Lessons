Three shapes to know

Microbes have shapes too — that's how we classify them.

Model the key questionsGood yes/no questions for microbes: "Is it spherical?" · "Does it have a tail?" · "Is it made of long, thin tubes?" Build a whole-class question bank.

Watch for: "microbes can't be classified" — they can, by shape and features, just like animals and plants.
