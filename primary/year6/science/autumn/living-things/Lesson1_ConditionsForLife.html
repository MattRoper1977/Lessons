Say thisAn organism is a living thing — an animal or a plant. Every organism can do seven jobs to stay alive. We remember them as MRS GREN.

I do · 2 4 min

Model the misconception, then what living things need.

The trickiest idea: plants move too

Plants grow and bend towards the light — that's movement. Plants are alive!

Model the trapA car moves and “drinks” fuel — but it can't grow or reproduce, so it is not alive. A fallen leaf was alive, but isn't a living thing now.

🐾 Animals need

  • Food · water · oxygen
  • Shelter · space

🌿 Plants need

  • Water · sunlight · air
  • Space & nutrients

Big difference: a plant makes its own food from sunlight.

TA Focus · Conditions for life
The trap to watch

Pupils equate “moves” with “alive”. The car and flame are where they slip.

Tier steer
  • 🌱 Keep it to alive / not alive; supply the MRS GREN word.
  • 🌿 Push for a “because…” with one sign of life.
  • 🌳 Ask them to justify the tricky cases (flame, seed, leaf).
Always

Insist on the word “because” before any answer counts.
