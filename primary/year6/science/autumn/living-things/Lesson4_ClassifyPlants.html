I do · 2 4 min

Bust the tree myths, then watch the seasons.

Deciduous, evergreen, coniferous

Deciduous trees drop their leaves in autumn; evergreens keep theirs all year.

Model the wordsDeciduous = loses its leaves in autumn (oak). Evergreen = keeps leaves all year (holly). Coniferous = evergreen tree that grows cones, not flowers (pine).

Watch for: judging by looks alone — a mushroom has a "stem" but it's a fungus, not a plant.

TA Focus · Classify plants
Traps to watch

"Anything with a stem is a plant" — a mushroom is a fungus. "Coniferous" is new — link it to cones + evergreen (pine).

Tier steer
  • 🌱 Loses leaves vs keeps leaves.
  • 🌿 Name the type + a feature.
  • 🌳 Untangle evergreen vs coniferous (holly vs pine).
Always

Classify by features (flowers, cones, leaves), not by first impressions.
Cold Call · Question bank
  • Flowering or non-flowering — how do you tell?
  • What is a coniferous tree?
  • Deciduous means…?
  • Name a non-flowering plant.
  • Is a mushroom a plant?
  • What do conifers make instead of flowers?
