TA Focus · Comparing sources
Aim for balance: renewables don't run out and make no gases, but are weather-dependent. For the sorter, ask 'is this a plus, a limit, or a myth?' Two myths: renewables have 'no downsides' and are 'always dearer' (they're now often cheaper).
