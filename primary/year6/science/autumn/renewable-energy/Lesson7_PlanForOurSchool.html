TA Focus · Proposal & reasons
The move: claim + evidence + reason. Push pupils past 'it looks cool' to reasons from the block ('it cuts greenhouse gases', 'sunlight is free'). For the sorter, ask 'is this a strong reason, a weak one, or nothing to do with it?'
Cold Call · Question bank
  • What are you proposing for our school?
  • What makes a reason strong?
  • Give evidence from this block.
  • Why is 'it looks cool' a weak reason?
  • How does your plan help the planet?
