We do · 1 6 min

Build a pros/cons list for solar together.

Good points and drawbacks

👍 no greenhouse gases · free from the Sun · low running cost  |  👎 less power at night · needs space for panels

Fair view
Solar is clean and renewable, but it isn't perfect — it needs daylight, and a lot of panels for a lot of power.
Real scientists weigh both sides.

Play the animation. Bust: solar doesn't work in the UK / on cloudy days.

Sunlight becomes electricity

Light hits the panel and flows as electricity along the wire to power a home.

Model the ideaSolar panels use light, so they still work on cloudy days — just less. The UK gets plenty of daylight, which is why you see panels on so many roofs here.

Watch for: "solar panels don't work in the UK" and "solar makes greenhouse gases".
