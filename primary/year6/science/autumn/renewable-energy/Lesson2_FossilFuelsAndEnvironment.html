Starter 5 min

Recall Year 5: greenhouse gases, greenhouse effect, climate change.

Warming the Earth

Burning fossil fuels releases greenhouse gases. These trap heat from the Sun, causing global warming — the gradual rise in the Earth's temperature — and climate change.

I do · 2 4 min

Play the animation. Bust: the greenhouse effect is entirely bad / it's just "hotter".

Heat gets trapped

The Sun's heat comes in; extra greenhouse gases trap more of it, warming the Earth.

Model the ideaThe greenhouse effect is natural — it keeps Earth warm enough to live on. The problem is the extra gases from burning fossil fuels, which trap too much heat.

Watch for: "the greenhouse effect is always bad" and "climate change just means hotter".

TA Focus · Greenhouse gases
Hold the chain: burn fuels → release gases → trap heat → warming → climate change. For the sorter, ask 'does this put gases in the air, or keep them out?' Two myths: the greenhouse effect is 'all bad' (it's natural; extra gases are the issue) and climate change is 'just hotter'.
