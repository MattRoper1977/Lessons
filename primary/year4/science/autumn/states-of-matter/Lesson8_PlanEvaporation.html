Planning the evaporation test

We put the same amount of water in shallow dishes in three places at different temperatures — a fridge, the classroom, and on a radiator — and check over hours to see where it evaporates fastest.

Our evaporation test

Same water in three places — we change only the temperature and keep the water and dish the same.

Model the ideaOnly the temperature of the place changes. Using the same amount of water and the same dishes keeps it fair, so any difference must be down to temperature.

Watch for: thinking evaporation won't happen below 100°C — it happens at everyday temperatures too.

TA Focus · Planning evaporation
Same water, same dishes, three places (fridge/classroom/radiator); change ONLY the temperature; measure water left over hours (not seconds). Bust 'evaporation won't happen below 100°C'. Push for a reasoned prediction: hottest place → fastest.
