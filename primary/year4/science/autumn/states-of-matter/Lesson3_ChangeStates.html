Starter 5 min

Introduce the four changes of state and their names.

Changing state

Melting (solid→liquid) and freezing (liquid→solid) happen with temperature. Evaporation (liquid→gas) and condensation (gas→liquid) do too. These changes are reversible — you can go back.

I do · 2 4 min

Play the animation. Bust: once melted it can't go back / boiling is the only evaporation.

Melting and freezing go both ways

Heat melts ice to water; cooling freezes it back. It's the same water either way.

Model the ideaMelted chocolate or a melted ice cube can become solid again if it cools — the change is reversible. And water can evaporate slowly at everyday temperatures, not only when it boils at 100°C.

Watch for: "once it melts it can't turn back" and "evaporation only happens at 100°C".

TA Focus · Changes of state
Two questions unlock it: 'hotter or colder?' and 'which state to which?' Heating → melt/evaporate; cooling → freeze/condense. Bust two myths: melted things can't go back (they can — reversible) and evaporation needs boiling (it happens slowly at room temperature too).
