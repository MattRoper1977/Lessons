Tricky materials

Sand pours, but it is a solid — it is made of many tiny solid grains. Honey and treacle are liquids that flow slowly. Some materials, like toothpaste, are hard to sort at all.

I do · 2 4 min

Play the animation. Bust: anything that pours is a liquid.

Sand looks like a liquid…

Sand pours like a liquid, but it lands as a pile of separate solid grains — water spreads into one puddle.

Model the ideaPouring does not make something a liquid. Zoom in on sand and you see thousands of tiny solid pieces. Zoom in on water and it is one connected liquid.

Watch for: "if it pours, it's a liquid" and "only liquids can be poured".
