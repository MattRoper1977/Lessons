Planning a fair test

In a fair test we change one thing, measure what happens, and keep everything else the same. Our question: How does the temperature of the water affect the time it takes for ice to melt?

Our fair test

Same ice cube in warm and cold water — we change only the temperature and keep everything else the same.

Model the ideaOnly one thing changes — the water temperature. If we also changed the ice size or the amount of water, we wouldn't know which change caused the difference. That's why we keep them the same.

Watch for: wanting to change more than one thing at once, which makes the test unfair.

TA Focus · Planning a fair test
Keep the language simple: change ONE thing, measure what happens, keep the rest the SAME (no 'variables' jargon yet). We change water temperature, measure time to melt, keep the ice/water/cup the same. Bust the urge to change two things at once.
Cold Call · Question bank
  • What is a fair test?
  • What will we change?
  • What will we measure?
