Animals with no spine

An invertebrate has no spine. Many have a hard exoskeleton on the outside. Insects have six legs, three body parts and antennae; spiders have eight legs.

I do · 2 4 min

Play the animation. Bust: all minibeasts are insects / invertebrates have bones inside.

Inside vs outside

A vertebrate has its skeleton (spine) inside; an invertebrate has no spine — often a hard shell outside.

Model the ideaNot every minibeast is an insect — a spider has eight legs, so it's not. And invertebrates have no bones inside; a beetle's hard shell is an exoskeleton on the outside.

Watch for: "all bugs are insects" and "invertebrates have a skeleton inside".

TA Focus · Spine or no spine
The first cut is simple: spine = vertebrate, no spine = invertebrate. Then group invertebrates by legs — insect (6, three body parts), spider (8), soft-bodied (worms/slugs). Bust 'all minibeasts are insects' and 'invertebrates have bones inside' (exoskeleton is outside).
