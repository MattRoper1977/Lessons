Animals with a spine

An animal with a spine is a vertebrate. Vertebrates are sorted into five groups: mammals, birds, fish, amphibians and reptiles — each with its own features.

I do · 2 4 min

Play the animation. Bust: a whale is a fish because it lives in water.

Look past where it lives

Every one of these has a spine — but their features (fur, feathers, gills…) put them in different groups.

Model the ideaA whale lives in water but breathes air, has no gills and feeds its young milk — so it's a mammal, not a fish. Where an animal lives doesn't decide its group; its features do.

Watch for: "if it lives in water it's a fish" and thinking bats are birds.

TA Focus · Vertebrate groups
Anchor on features, not habitat: mammals (fur/milk), birds (feathers), fish (gills/scales), amphibians (moist skin), reptiles (dry scales) — all have a spine. For the sorter, ask 'gills or milk?' The big myth: a whale/dolphin is a fish because it lives in water — it's a mammal.
