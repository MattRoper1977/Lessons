Trace the Moon's orbit and its light together.

Orbit and light

Reason it out
The Moon orbits Earth (not the Sun). Its glow is sunlight bouncing off its surface — like a mirror.
"Earth's gravity keeps the Moon in orbit, just as the Sun's gravity keeps Earth in orbit."

Reflected light

Bust the mythsThe Moon does not make its own light — it reflects the Sun's. It can be seen in the day as well as at night. And Earth is not the only planet with a moon — many planets have their own.
