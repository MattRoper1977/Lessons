We do · 1 6 min

Use a globe and a lamp to show day and night.

Model it together

Lamp and globe
The lamp = the Sun (it stays still). Spin the globe: the lit side = day, the dark side = night.
"As I turn the globe, our country moves out of the light — daytime becomes night."
I do · 2 4 min

Play the animation. Bust the "Sun disappears" myth.

Turning into night

As your spot on Earth turns away from the Sun, day becomes night. The Sun never moves.

Bust the mythsThe Sun does not disappear at night — your side of Earth has simply turned away from it. And the Sun does not move across the sky — Earth rotates.

Watch for: "the Sun goes to sleep / moves away at night." It is Earth turning.

We do · 2 5 min

Explain time differences together.

Different times, different places

Reason it out
Only half of Earth faces the Sun at once. As it spins, different countries take turns in the light.
That is why it can be morning in one country and night in another.

Traps to watch

Myths: the Sun disappears at night, and the Sun moves across the sky. Both are the same error — it is Earth rotating that makes the Sun seem to rise and set.

Tier steer

  • 🌿 Explain what causes night and day.
  • 🌳 Explain time differences between countries.
Always

Earth turns; the Sun stays still. Facing the Sun = day.
