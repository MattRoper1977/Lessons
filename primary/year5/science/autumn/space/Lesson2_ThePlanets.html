Spot the pattern
Where are all the rocky ones? Where are all the gas giants?
"The rocky planets are all near the Sun. The gas giants are all far from the Sun."
Answer the starterMercury is closest, but Venus is the hottest — its thick atmosphere traps the heat. Closest does not automatically mean hottest.
I do · 2 4 min

Show the scale picture. Let the size land before you say the number.

How big is the Sun, really?

You could line up about 109 Earths across the face of the Sun.

Bust the mythEarth feels enormous because we live on it. It is not. The Sun is far larger than every planet put together — and it is only an average-sized star.

Watch for: pupils who think the Sun is small because it looks small. It looks small because it is 150 million km away.

We do · 2 5 min

True or false, hands up, and always ask "how do you know?"

True or false?

Three big ones
1. Earth is bigger than the Sun.    2. All the planets are rocky.    3. Only Earth has a moon.
All three are false. Make them prove it: the Sun is about 109 Earths wide · the outer four are gas giants · Uranus has 27 moons.
Push it
If Jupiter is the biggest planet, why is it not a star?
Because it does not make its own light — it still orbits the Sun.

TA Focus · The Planets
Traps to watch

Three myths run this lesson: Earth is bigger than the Sun (it is about 1/109th the width), all planets are rocky (only the inner four), and only Earth has a moon (Uranus has 27; Mercury and Venus have none).

Tier steer
  • 🌱 Order the first four with the mnemonic said aloud — do not ask for all eight from memory.
  • 🌿 Order all eight, then sort rocky / gas giant / not a planet.
  • 🌳 Explain the near-rocky, far-gas pattern and why the Sun and Moon are neither.
Always

Rocky = solid ground, inner four · gas giant = no surface, outer four · the Sun is a star, the Moon orbits a planet.

If a pupil stalls

Go back to the mnemonic. Say it with them, then let them write only the letters first — M V E M J S U N — and fill the names in after.
