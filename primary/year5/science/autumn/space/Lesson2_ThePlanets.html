We do · 1 6 min

Name each object as a star, a planet or something else.

Star, planet or…?

☀️ Sun   🌍 Earth   🌙 the Moon   🪐 Jupiter   ☄️ a comet

Reason it out
Does it give out its own light? → star. Does it orbit the Sun? → planet. Does it orbit a planet? → moon.
"The Sun makes its own light, so it is a star. Earth orbits the Sun, so it is a planet."
I do · 2 4 min

Play the animation. Bust two myths as you watch.

One star, eight planets

The Sun sits at the centre; the planets orbit around it. Pluto is a dwarf planet.

Bust the mythThere is only one star in our Solar System — the Sun. And Pluto is not a planet: it was reclassified as a dwarf planet because it is too small.

Watch for: pupils thinking every dot of light in the sky is a planet, or that Pluto is still a planet.

TA Focus · The Solar System
Traps to watch

Space is new, so vocabulary is fragile. Two big myths: there is more than one star (no — only the Sun), and Pluto is a planet (no — a dwarf planet).

Tier steer
  • 🌱 Sort star / planet / other with the word bank.
  • 🌿 Give a reason (orbits the Sun / makes its own light).
  • 🌳 State the rule that separates a planet from a moon.
Always

Star = makes light · planet = orbits the Sun · moon = orbits a planet.
