TA Focus · Modelling
Traps to watch

Pupils treat a model as reality: believing planets really are that size or that close together. Keep returning to scale — sizes and distances are not real in most models.

Tier steer
  • 🌱 Name one advantage of a model.
  • 🌿 Give an advantage and a disadvantage.
  • 🌳 Explain why nothing in a classroom can be truly to scale.
