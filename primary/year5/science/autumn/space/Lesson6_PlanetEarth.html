Bust the mythsThe Sun does not rotate around the Earth — Earth rotates. The Sun is the largest object in the Solar System (not Earth), and so far Earth is the only planet known to support life.

TA Focus · Planet Earth
Traps to watch

Myths: the Sun rotates around Earth (no — Earth rotates), Earth is the biggest object (no — the Sun is), and other planets support life (currently only Earth is known to). It only looks like the Sun moves.

Tier steer
  • 🌱 Earth spins on its axis once every 24 hours.
  • 🌿 Explain "rotate on its axis".
  • 🌳 Explain why the Sun appears to move.
Always

Earth moves; the Sun stays still. Axis = Pole to Pole.
