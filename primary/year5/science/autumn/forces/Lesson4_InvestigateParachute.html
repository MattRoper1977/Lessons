I do · 2 4 min

Play the animation. Bust: it's the wind slowing it, not air resistance.

Big canopy, slow fall

From the same height, the big parachute falls more slowly — more air resistance.

Model the ruleThe bigger canopy meets more air, so air resistance slows it — this is the force we're testing, not a random gust. Keep the test indoors or shielded so wind doesn't spoil it.

Watch for: "it's the wind slowing it, not air resistance", and stopwatch slips (forgetting to reset).

TA Focus · Running the test
Stopwatch skill first: start on release, stop on landing, reset to zero. Keep drops from the same height. For the sorter, ask 'does this make it more trustworthy, or spoil it?' The myth is 'it's the wind, not air resistance'.
Cold Call · Question bank
  • Why drop from the same height?
  • Why time in parts of a second?
  • How many sizes should we test?
  • Why repeat each size and average?
  • What force are we actually testing?
