We do · 1 6 min

Decide together: does friction help or hinder here?

Helpful or in the way?

🛞 car tyres   🚲 bike brakes   👟 shoe soles   📦 pushing a heavy box   ⚙️ machine parts wearing out

Two columns
Tyres, brakes, soles → friction helps (grip & stopping).   Pushing a heavy box, parts wearing → friction gets in the way.
Friction is not "good" or "bad" — it depends on the job.
I do · 2 4 min

Play the animation. Bust: friction only happens when moving / only on rough surfaces.

Which way does friction push?

As the box moves right, friction pushes left — always against the motion.

Model the ruleFriction always acts against the direction of movement. It happens even between smooth surfaces like glass — and it is there even when an object is not moving yet.

Watch for: "friction only happens when things move" and "friction only happens on rough surfaces".

We do · 2 5 min

TA Focus · Friction
Keep two ideas front and centre: friction needs contact, and it always opposes motion. For the sorter, ask 'does friction help or get in the way here?' The two myths (only when moving / only on rough) are the ones to gently challenge.
