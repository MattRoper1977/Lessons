Plan the water test

Change: shape of the object  |  Measure: time to reach the bottom  |  Keep same: same clay mass, same cylinder, same water

Variables again
Independent = shape · Dependent = time to sink · Controlled = mass of clay, liquid, cylinder, start point.
Use the same mass of modelling clay so only the shape changes.
I do · 2 4 min

Play the animation. Show streamlined vs blunt sinking.

Streamlined sinks faster

The streamlined shape slips down through the water; the blunt shape is held back.

Model the ruleThe streamlined shape has less water resistance, so it reaches the bottom first. The blunt shape pushes against more water and sinks slowly.

Keep the clay the same mass for each shape — otherwise the test isn't fair.
