Four FoodWise lessons matching the uploaded D&T Weeks 1-4 and the current GROW D&T sequence. Weeks 5-7 in that scheme are textiles and are outside this Food pack.

CONTENTS
- index.html: lesson menu and download links.
- HTML/: original lesson slideshows with keyboard navigation and answer reveals.
- PowerPoints/: editable teaching decks with teacher notes and sources.
- Slide_PDFs/: printable slide exports.
- Documents/Pupil_Booklet.docx and .pdf: pupil activities and writing space.
- Documents/Resource_Cards.docx and .pdf: lesson sources, scenario cards and practical resources.
- Documents/Teacher_Guide.docx and .pdf: 40-minute plans, preparation, support, answers, assessment and references.
