# N6-F §F3.4 — The LAUNCH PEQ level: two sources, and what each one costs

**For Matt and Cheryl. Nothing has been edited for this. It is a report.**

The order that commissioned this asked us to record a contradiction between the
SoW Pathway Ladder and the merged estate anchor, on the premise that the ladder
"gives LAUNCH **PEQ Level 1 Award** with no Entry 3 floor". Measured against the
operative workbook, **that premise is wrong in its second half**, and the real
disagreement is narrower and more interesting. Both are set out below.

### The premise, corrected

**D13 does carry an Entry 3 statement.** It ends `(E3–L1 only in 2026/27.)`, and
the Qualification Map says the qualification itself spans "Entry 3 and Level 1".
So the claim that the ladder gives LAUNCH Level 1 "with no Entry 3 floor" does
not survive reading the cell to the end.

What D13 lacks is the *phrase* C13 uses. GROW's cell says `Floor: Entry 3 units.`
in as many words; LAUNCH's does not. Every other row in the ladder that has a
floor states it with the word "Floor:". So LAUNCH is the one PEQ cell where the
Entry 3 bound is expressed as a range note rather than as a floor. That is a
drafting inconsistency, and it is almost certainly how the "no Entry 3 floor"
reading arose.

## What actually disagrees

Not "Level 1 versus Entry 3 floor" — both sources allow both levels. The
disagreement is about **which level LAUNCH is aimed at**:

| | names the LAUNCH target as | treats the other level as |
|---|---|---|
| SoW workbook (D13, F15) | **Level 1** Award / Extended Award / Certificate | Entry 3, via the range bound `E3–L1` |
| merged estate (×137) | **Entry 3** | Level 1, as *stretch* |

They are exact inversions of one another. One says L1 with E3 permitted below;
the other says E3 with L1 reached for above.

---

## The arithmetic consequence of each reading

PEQ is portfolio-assessed, Pass/Fail, six units per level, 1 credit ≈ 10 hours.
An Award is the smallest of the three shapes; Extended Award and Certificate
require progressively more credit.

**If the workbook is right — LAUNCH is a Level 1 cohort.**
Every LAUNCH pupil is entered for a Level 1 Award as the baseline, and the
Extended Award and Certificate are live targets. On Matt's cohort figures that
puts **two to four pupils at their working level and the rest above it.** The
majority would be carrying a portfolio at a level they are not working at, and
the Entry 3 evidence they *can* produce would sit outside the entered
qualification. Expect a large gap between work done and credit awarded, and an
E3 fallback that has to be arranged late for most of the group.

**If the estate is right — LAUNCH is an Entry 3 cohort with stretch.**
Everyone is entered at Entry 3, which matches where the cohort is working, and
the two to four pupils at Level 1 are entered — or stretched — at L1 unit by
unit. Almost everyone certificates. The cost is on the other side: the LAUNCH
lane then **certificates at the same level GROW does**, because C13 puts GROW at
"PEQ Level 1 Award. Floor: Entry 3 units." A ladder whose top two rungs award
the same thing is not a ladder, and that is visible to anyone reading the
Pathway Ladder sheet.

**The bind.** The workbook's reading breaks the cohort. The estate's reading
breaks the ladder. Neither is free, and the choice is not ours.
