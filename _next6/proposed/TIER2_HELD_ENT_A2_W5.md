## Why it is Tier 2 and not Tier 1

The SoW row is `'GROW Weekly - Autumn'!C155 — "Try an eco or sport challenge."`
The lesson delivers a controlled fair test that serves the enterprise project;
participation in the challenge is present but subordinated to it.

**This is not claim-accuracy.** The shipped criterion is not false — it is
narrower than the SoW row. The proposed wording *adds participation as an
assessed criterion*, which changes what a pupil is asked to do and how the week
is evidenced. The estate precedent permits a Tier 2 edit only where it corrects
a false claim; this corrects nothing false.

The auditor that derived the row reached the same conclusion unprompted and
wrote **"HOLD — do not apply; this changes SC meaning."**
