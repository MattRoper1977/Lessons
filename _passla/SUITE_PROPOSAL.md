## 2 · Proposed suite shape — strands × weeks, slots, half-term

**Established estate pattern (measured):** both BUILD (`Hub: BUILD ASDAN · Autumn 1`) and
GROW (`Scheme … · Aut 1`) built the **Autumn 1 half-term** as their lesson suite — BUILD =
5 strands × 6–7 weeks (31), GROW = 3 strands × 6 weeks (18). LAUNCH mirrors this:
**build the Autumn 1 (HT1) module for each of the five ASDAN strands.**

The SoW's Autumn HT1 is a **7-week** grid (Aut1·W1–W7). Two shapes are on the table:

- **Recommended — 6-week modules (5 × 6 = 30 lessons).** Mirrors GROW's 6-week module
  pattern exactly. For the PEQ strand this is *pedagogically clean*: W1 = PEQ intro/choose
  level, **W2–W6 = a complete Communication-skills (ComSk1) unit arc** (know → know → plan →
  use/evidence → review/sign-off). Week 7 (Team-working intro) opens the *next* module and
  is deferred.
- **Alternative — 7-week modules (5 × 7 = 35 lessons).** Matches the SoW's Autumn 7/7 grid
  and BUILD's Careers precedent (7 weeks). Adds each strand's W7 "outcome/portfolio-evidence"
  week. More faithful to the grid; 5 extra files; the PEQ W7 (Team-working intro) sits
  awkwardly as a module tail.

### 4.2 · What the BUILT Autumn-1 suite alone banks (do not overclaim)

The **Phase-2 built suite is Autumn 1 only.** Being precise about the difference between the
*year's ceiling* and the *built module's banking* (the exact trap that produced GROW's
mislabels):

- The built PEQ Autumn-1 module (W1–W6) fully evidences **ComSk1 (Communication, 3 cr)** and
  *begins* Team working. It does **not** on its own reach Award/Extended Award/Certificate —
  those need Spring + Summer modules built and banked later.
- The four short-course strands' Autumn-1 modules bank their **ASDAN short course + AQA UAS**
  and *contribute* skill evidence (teamwork, communication, decision-making) that the PEQ
  strand's "use" arc can draw on — but they do not bank a PEQ unit by themselves.
- **Print rule:** the built suite may state the **year target** ("works toward PEQ L1 Award /
  Extended Award / Certificate") at hub/START_HERE level, but a per-lesson `Banks:` strip may
  only claim what that lesson evidences — i.e. **ComSk1 by code on the PEQ module**, and the
  **short course + UAS** on the other four. Nothing else is printed as banked.

### 4.3 · The ≥10-hour cross-week window

Each PEQ unit needs its plan *used over ≥10 cumulative hours* (a cross-week window, not one
lesson) **and** at least one assessed activity meeting its minimum (ComSk1: presentation ≥3
min OR text ≥250 words; group ≥3). At 40-min periods the 10h accrues across the strand's
5–6 weeks **plus** the cross-curricular contexts the SoW names ("a single challenge can bank
PEQ credit and cover another curriculum area"). The suite must **surface** this window (GROW
never did — "10 hours" appeared 0×). `UNVERIFIED-AGAINST-SPEC`.
