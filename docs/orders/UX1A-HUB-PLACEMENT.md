## §1 The standing rule that governs all of it

**1.1 Nothing lands without its catalogue row in the same arc.** PR #163 put 208
files live that nothing linked to; the only public route was a direct URL.
**Deployment and discoverability are one job, not two.**

**1.2 Granularity is per unit or per pack, never per lesson.** Twelve entries,
not 192.

**1.3 A surface may be omitted deliberately — and the omission is written down
with its reason**, so a later pass reads a decision rather than a gap.

---

## §2 Tutor time and assemblies — no card, two routes in

**2.1 No fifth subject card.** The content is tutor time, not lessons. The estate
already keys 17 of the 23 originals under `Tutor_Time/`, and the deck census
reads **196 "tutor" against 33 "assembly"**. Audience, cadence and navigation
need all differ from lesson planning.

**2.2 It lives on the Teach side** and is reachable from there as its primary
route.

**2.3 Browse route.** One row beneath the four subject cards, **visually quieter
than a card, no chips, one line**. Label it for what people search rather than
what the folder is called — *"Tutor time, assemblies and PSHE sessions"*, or the
closest wording the estate already uses. It links to **the collection**, not to
individual sessions.

**2.4 Search route, and this is the one that matters.** Every session is present
in the catalogue, the site search index **and** the sitemap.

> Someone who types "KCSIE" or "bystanders" into the Lessons hub search and gets
> nothing has been told the content does not exist.

Prove it after landing by searching **three session titles and one topic word**,
and report what each returns.

**2.5 NOTHING SURFACES until LF1-M's restoration completes.** Seventeen re-cuts
are currently missing verbatim safeguarding lines. A discoverability pass that
exposes them before restoration **makes the defect easier to reach, not harder**.
(Safeguarding wording is never edited by this estate's tooling — flag only, to
Matt. This ruling gates *exposure*, it does not authorise a text change.)

**2.6 The successor-pointer pages at the retired routes stay** as ruled in Part A.
Old URLs keep working and point forward.

## §3 Computing — obvious now, promotable later

**3.1 Not a fifth card yet.** Two units on a card of its own reads as thin beside
Science, and the standing constraint is not to overload the hub.

**3.2 Card face.** Add a **third CTA to the Lifeskills card — "Explore
Computing"** — beside *Explore Life Skills* and *Explore FoodWise*. This is
existing grammar: the card already carries two CTAs, so it adds prominence
without adding a pattern.

**3.3 Subject page.** Computing gets its own page on the shared `?subject=`
template with **the same treatment Science gets** — unit accordion, format chips,
Open/Download verbs. Two units today: the live **GROW ICT Autumn 1** unit and
**GROW Computing** (Scratch, AQA UAS 71638).

**3.4 Label.** The card face, CTA and subject page say **"Computing"**. The route
stays `ICT/` and **no live URL is renamed** — GC1's rule governs paths, and a
display label is not a path. **Record the mapping** so nobody later "fixes" the
inconsistency by renaming the tree.

**3.5 Award visibility.** An **"AQA UNIT AWARD" chip** on the card face,
mirroring ARTS AWARD on the Art card. Same grammar, same weight.

**3.6 Promotion trigger — written down so it is never re-argued.**

> Computing graduates to its own subject card when it carries **three or more
> units**, **or** its **first certificated award cohort completes** — whichever
> comes first.

At that point it takes the standard four-card treatment and the Lifeskills CTA
becomes a pointer. The SoW already names six continuation units — **122058**
scoring, **119195** variables, **112907** webpage, **122060** sound and effects,
**121956** micro:bit, and the Python bridge — so **expect this to fire**.

**3.7 Computing appears only after #493 and its admission cycle land.** A CTA to
a unit that is not yet served is worse than no CTA.

## §4 The cross-cutting award collection — later, with its own trigger

**4.1** AQA Unit Award units already exist in more than one subject — Computing,
the Tees Trekkers walking unit, ASDAN work. That argues for a **Unit Awards
collection** rather than a chip per card.

**4.2 It is NOT built now.** The hub ships **All Subjects only, no tabs** — that
ruling stands. The §3.5 chip carries the visibility until then.

**4.3 Trigger:** when the hub ships **Collections**, a **Unit Awards** collection
is the first one, assembled **from the award field in the record** rather than
hand-listed.
