One migration-and-identity enquiry, rooted in the Tees Valley, taught at three levels of challenge. Every lesson runs the Lundy Loop — Space, Voice, Audience, Influence — and every capture reaches for the next pathway up. Pick a pathway, open its Scheme of Work, and launch any week.

The Build → Grow → Launch pathway

Progress Schools groups learning into three ability-and-confidence pathways. The same enquiry runs through all three — the difference is the level of historical reasoning expected, and how much independence the evidence asks for.

BUILD · ExploreFoundational. Place, notice, take first evidence. Confidence before complexity. Light calm studio.
GROW · DefensibleMiddle. Rank causes, qualify judgements, explain with evidence. Week 7 assessed account.
LAUNCH · GCSE BridgeMost rigorous. Source utility, causal courtroom, sustained thesis. Week 7 assessed source assessment.

The Lundy Loop in every lesson

Four zones, one loop — identical across the whole Teesside programme (art and humanities). Colour marks the zone; the loop always closes, even on a hard day, with the week’s stamped ticket.
