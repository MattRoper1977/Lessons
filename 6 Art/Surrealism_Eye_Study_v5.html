I Do

🔲 Gridding turns a scary photo into easy little boxes

Watch me. One big eye is hard. Twenty small boxes are easy — you only ever copy what is in one box.
0/5 steps
1. Draw a tonal scale in the corner (your shadow menu).
2. Grid your whole page with light, even squares.
3. Work box by box — outline only, no shading yet.
4. Happy with the outline? Gently rub the gridlines out.
5. Now add tone — light layers first, darks last (2B → 6B).

Pressing hard for the grid leaves grid-ghosts forever. Press lightly so it rubs away clean.

Knowledge Organiser – Surrealism: Realistic Eye Study (1 of 2)

WordWhat it means
ToneHow light or dark something is. Tone is what makes a drawing look 3D, not flat.
GriddingSplitting a photo into squares so you copy one small box at a time — accurate, never a blank page.
HighlightThe brightest spot in the eye. Leave the paper white — never shade it.
IrisThe coloured ring. In greyscale it is soft mid-grey, with fine lines running from the centre.
PupilThe black centre — your darkest dark (press hardest, 6B).
ProportionThe size of each part compared with the others. Gridding keeps proportion right.
BlendingSmoothing tone so there are no hard edges — light layers built up slowly.

Key distinctions: a real eye is mostly three tones — the white highlight, the grey iris and the black pupil. Build them light first; you can always go darker, but you cannot un-press a heavy line.

🎵 Memory hook: “white dot · grey ring · black hole” = highlight · iris · pupil. Get those three and the eye looks alive.
⚠️ Common mistakes: shading the white highlight by accident · pressing too hard too early · forgetting to rub the grid out · making the pupil grey instead of black.

🧠 Self-check routine: squint at your drawing → can you see a clear white, a clear grey and a clear black? If two tones blur together, push the darks darker.

Scaffolding Sheet – Foundation

Your 5 steps

1) Tape your gridded sheet next to your blank grid.

2) Copy the outline one box at a time.

3) Find the iris circle and the round pupil.

4) Press hardest for the black pupil — leave the white dot blank.

5) Add soft grey to the iris in light layers.

Only three tones

White highlight · grey iris · black pupil. Pencils: 2B for grey, 6B for the darkest dark.
