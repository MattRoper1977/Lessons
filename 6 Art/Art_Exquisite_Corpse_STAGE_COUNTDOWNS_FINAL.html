I Do · 9–12 min

The golden rule: hide it, but connect it

Draw BIG

Fill only your third. Bold shapes beat tiny detail.

Cross the fold

Finish with two short lines that poke into the next section.

Fold backwards

Hide everything except those two little connection lines.

Pass left

No peeking, clues or explanations.

Two lines over the fold = the next artist can connect.

Only use this if the physical fold demonstration has not landed. The lesson timing assumes I do not play it.

We Do · 12–14 min

Spot the stronger start

A

A tiny head floating in the middle, no lines crossing the fold.

B

A large head filling the space, with two neck lines crossing the fold.

It is bold, uses the space and gives the next artist a clear joining point.

Independent · 34–38 min

Become the creature's art director

Choose one finished creature and make it feel complete.

Name it

Combine an adjective and an object: The Floating Teapot.

Give it a world

Add a habitat that is as unexpected as the creature.

Unify it

Repeat one pattern or colour across all three sections.

Art idea: repeating a visual feature creates unity — it helps separate parts feel like one artwork.
