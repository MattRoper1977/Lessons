BUILD Animation Framework

Every asset is layered. Every movement means one thing. Drop build-anim.css, bio-svg.js and build-anim.js into any lesson and the whole library is available.

1 · Motion vocabulary — what each movement means

Pupils learn these without being taught them. Keep them consistent across every lesson in the unit.

2 · A teaching stage — teacher-controlled, one click at a time

Press ▶ Next. Narrate the line the bar prints for you. Nothing appears until you choose.

The text that confirms the idea can live outside the picture and still follow the same clicks — give it data-ba-step="n":

backbone — a line of small bones down the middle of the back.
vertebrate — an animal that HAS a backbone.
<div class="ba-stage" id="demo-fish" data-ba-asset="fish" data-ba-script="@teach"></div>
<p data-ba-step="5" data-ba-for="demo-fish">backbone — a line of small bones…</p>

3 · A prediction panel — pupils commit before the reveal

Tap an animal. Three dots appear. Take the vote. Then press Next and the answer builds itself.

<div class="ba-predict" data-ba-animals="bird:Robin, worm:Earthworm, frog:Frog"></div>

4 · Writing your own script

<div class="ba-stage" data-ba-asset="crab" data-ba-script="
  show body,shell,legs           :: A crab.
  glow shell + hi shell          :: The hard bit is on the OUTSIDE.
  label shell                    :: Hard case outside.
  show nobone + cross            :: Down the middle — nothing.
  label kind                     :: No backbone → invertebrate.
"></div>

One line = one click. verb targets, joined with + when several things happen together, then :: and the sentence you say out loud.

5 · Poseable assets — when the change IS the concept

Some subjects are about the change of state, not the parts. pose bend sets data-pose on the stage and the asset's own CSS does the rest — one click, everything moves together.

css: '.ba-stage[data-pose="bend"] [data-part="forearm"]{transform:rotate(-72deg)}'

<div class="ba-stage" data-ba-asset="arm" data-ba-script="
  pose bend     :: I pull the top one. The arm comes up.
  pose straight :: Now the bottom one. The jobs swap.
"></div>
