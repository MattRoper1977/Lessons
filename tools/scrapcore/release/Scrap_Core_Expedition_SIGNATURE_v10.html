MADE BY MATT · OUTPOST BRAVO PILOT BRIEFING

Scrap Core SIGNATURE is a systems-driven expedition roguelite. Every tunnel alters the biome, rotating event fronts change the rules mid-run, and every extraction decision shapes the outpost economy.

Expedition loop

  • Push into four deterministic strata, mine valuables and return through the surface shaft.
  • Loose silt collapses, sludge spreads, and lava flows through tunnels you create.
  • Use sonar to reveal rare deposits, unstable masses, field-cache signals and the projected paths of the Slag-Wyrm and Void Singularity.
  • Defeat environmental titans with controlled collapses, volatile chain reactions, phase dashes and terrain engineering—not conventional gunfire.

Surface strategy

  • Sell a haul for cash, stockpile named ores for recipes, or refine it into alloy.
  • Socket one Drill, Thermal, Utility and Avionics artifact. Complete four-part sets activate six named build synergies.
  • Accept contracts for secured rewards. Expedition grades are optional in System settings; Command Clearance XP remains available without them. Autosave captures active runs, biome events, caches, cellular materials and titan state.
