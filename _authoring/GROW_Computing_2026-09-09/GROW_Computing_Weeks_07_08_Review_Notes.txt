REVIEW OF THE SUPPLIED COMPUTING WEEKS 7–8

The foundation was useful: a consistent 40-minute GROW sequence, three support
routes, pupil voice, a separate teacher model, and a sensible final pair of
lessons for adding complexity and testing. Those strengths have been retained.

CHANGES MADE
1. Rebuilt both eight-slide PowerPoints with editable text and code diagrams,
   a worked test and repair, fuller route instructions and teacher notes.
2. Made the moving Hazard the fully taught Week 7 task. The partial collectible
   alternative is now an extension requiring complete instructions and agreed time.
3. Replaced the assumption that slower is always fairer with a comparison using
   test results. A slower hazard can occupy a crossing for longer.
4. Corrected the HTML checks: all four arrows are tested; Hazard contact and
   avoidance are checked; Finish counts a new response; restart checks a new run.
   Shared results keep a history, and the no-contact location is off the hazard path.
5. Repaired six unintended Scratch block-parent links. The intentional classroom
   bug-hunt faults remain, with the correct explanation and separate working model.
6. Added a complete five-check pupil record to every Week 8 route. An all-pass
   result is accepted; pupils can improve instructions or agree a next step.
7. Added the six-outcome final evidence check and clarified the distinction between
   the local lesson activities and the official AQA summary-sheet requirements.
8. Aligned the planning, corrected references to missing picture/object cards,
   and removed a nearly empty overflow page from the Week 8 Main booklet.

The standalone Week 7 upload duplicated Week 7 in the combined upload. Both
revised ZIPs now use the same Week 7 files. The Week 7-only hub links only to
files included in that ZIP. Earlier course materials were used as references.

The Read Me states the verification completed and its limits.
