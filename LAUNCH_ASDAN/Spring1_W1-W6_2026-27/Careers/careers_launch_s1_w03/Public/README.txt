LAUNCH Careers Spring 1 Week 3
Experiencing work through a placement or simulation
Year A. Spring 1 Week 3, 18–22 January 2027. Friday 22 January 2027, P1. One forty-minute formative lesson for a pair. Six-week term 4 January–12 February; Week 6 assessing. Actual activity date/mode recorded separately; no clock time supplied.

Exact weekly outcome: Experiencing work: placement/simulation.

B90 Spr1·W3 and C90 exact outcome retained. Inherited framework/resource context from row 88 generic. Row 90 gives no minute sequence: authored 5/8/10/12/5 lesson and twelve-minute classroom shift. Same business/order/roles as Week 1. Four task stages and timed list implement owner brief. School-arranged real placement replaces simulation and uses the neutral reflection sheet unchanged; actual placement tasks/timings arranged by school. Supported/Standard/Stretch retained.

Success checks
Take part in the agreed work task through an accessible role. Keep a truthful progress record and handover. Reflect briefly on what went well and what was hard/unclear, self-assess five Week 1 expectations and choose a next step.

Same invented workplace
An invented small business prepares simple paper packs for customers. It has a supervisor, a preparer and a checker. It has no name, real employer, location or contact details. This is classroom simulation only.

Prepare
Two large ready-cut cards and two strips per pair, plus spares; written order, task cards, timed list, tier packs, vocabulary, books or blank typed reflection and usual aids. Reset/reuse paper for round two; do not report reused materials as commercial output. Adult may take the partner role. Confirm private help and pause/return.

Placement alternative
A real placement arranged by the school replaces the classroom simulation. Use the same reflection sheet unchanged. School confirms tasks, supervision, access and actual timing separately; the twelve-minute classroom list is not a real-placement schedule. Record actual date, mode, task and evidence. If activity has not happened, mark pending and do not invent experience.

Arrival and I Do
0–5: retrieve one Week 1 expectation and Week 2 keep-clear/report response. Confirm simulation or school-arranged placement mode. 5–9: explain set up, make/check, record and hand over. 9–13: model one checked pack and one still needing a check; record both honestly. Model handover: “One is ready; the other needs checking. Please check it before calling the order complete.” This is an invented example, not learner evidence.

We Do
13–19: order the four cards and rehearse a role agreement and handover with the written order visible. Read the relative shift clock: 0–2, 2–6, 6–9, 9–12. Preview the five self-rating labels; do not prefill judgements. 19–23: hinge B, report partial progress rather than call unfinished work complete. Parallel A, ask about an unclear next step rather than guess. Re-model and recheck privately.

You Do
23–25 set up: order, materials, access and roles. 25–29 make/check: two brief rounds with reset and swap, so each can try preparer and checker. 29–32 record: actual role and progress, known counts, unfinished checks, help and a brief evidence clue. 32–35 hand over: each learner gives a short update to adult/partner. Reduce repeats or quantity for access; record what happened, never manufacture completion. Keep a stable work area at the transition.

Review and exit
35–38: each gives one short what-went-well statement and one hard/not-tried/unclear statement, then rates the five expectations. Pointing or a scribed selection is valid. “Nothing was hard today” is valid; do not invent a difficulty. 38–40: privately check one evidence clue, set one NS and keep the reply/next check time; save individual record. Missing evidence stays visible.

Supported
Keep four numbered task cards, a short checklist, model and sentence starts. Read one step at a time; use usual aid, pointing, voice, typing or exact scribe. Adult can partner. Make your own brief log/reflection and five honest self-ratings.

Standard
Use the task cards and timed list to agree roles, make/check, record actual progress and hand over. Give one example of what went well and what was hard or unclear. Rate all five expectations and choose a next step. Keep normal aids.

Stretch
Compare the plan with actual progress and explain an adjustment that helped. Give a precise handover of unfinished checks and evaluate a limit of this short shift. Use the same individual reflection and five expectations; extra writing optional.

Model check and fade
After the model ask whether both packs were checked. Key: only one. The other must be handed over as needing a check. Keep task/order/model in view; fade answer cues only. A normal reading/communication aid is access, not automatically a content prompt.

Timed list and roles
The shift list is a twelve-minute classroom guide: 2 + 4 + 3 + 3. Pause or reduce repetitions when needed. If a task is unfinished, record and hand it over honestly. Do not race, remove a normal aid, or penalise a support need. An adult can partner; no forced peer performance. Role swap is planned, not fabricated if not attempted.

Adaptations and prompt ladder
Cognition and Learning: numbered cards, short checklist, one step and reduced quantity. Communication and Interaction: rehearsal, wait time, written order, usual aid and exact scribe/readback. SEMH: predictable routine, private review, pass and pause/return. Sensory and Physical: clear accessible table, seated roles and large print. EAL: plan in any language. 1 Point to place/instruction; 2 ask a short question; 3 rephrase; 4 scaffold/choice; 5 model first step only then return task.

Private help route
Trusted adult or school help route. Point to HELP, write or ask privately. If one adult is busy, use the route to another. Pass, pause and supported return available. No personal employment, family or accident account requested.

Reflection sheet in both modes
Evidence Sheet pages 1–3 use neutral activity fields and the same five expectations. Use unchanged for simulation or school-arranged real placement; actual task/date can differ from the lesson. Page 2 blank typed work/reflection log; page 3 blank five-expectation review; page 4 staff record. Do not add an employer/place name. Book/usual aid default.

Evidence and judgement
Keep actual task/role/progress and handover, a short what-went-well and hard/unclear reflection, five self-ratings, one actual example and next step/reply. Ratings exactly as Week 1: I did this; I did this with a prompt; I need another try; Not seen today. Normal access distinct from supplied thinking. Missing role or opportunity is Not seen today. Judge reflection from evidence, not personality, pace or a real employment history.

Feedback and portfolio
VF, WS, I, NS, //, ?, E and R generic codes. E actual banked work. R after Voice, Audience, NS and reply in the same colour, on the same page or usual mode. One NS might add the unfinished check to a handover. Retain first try and response. Offer to Communication portfolio; staff confirm any accredited mapping. A simulation does not prove real placement attendance or workplace competence.

Safe delivery
Ready-cut paper only. No tools, machines, money, food, live customers, personal data or deliveries. Stop/keep clear/report if a real concern appears; follow actual school procedures. No staged hazard or cleanup activity. Real placement arrangements and approved tasks belong to the school; these cards do not replace induction or supervision.

Scope
Only Week 3 authored. W4 following instructions/teamwork, W5 customer service, W6 reflection/assessing follow one per return. Same invented business. Further GROW Careers paused. Listen calmly to concerns, avoid probing or secrecy promises and follow usual procedures; keep concerns outside shared evidence.
