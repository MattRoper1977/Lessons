Success checks
Make an honest four-part reflection: what I did, what I learned, what I would do differently, and which expectations I met. Link it to actual work and keep a gap visible. Rate five Week 1 expectations and agree a next step. Staff review six outcomes separately using actual evidence.

Same invented workplace
An invented small business prepares simple paper packs for customers. It has a supervisor, a preparer and a checker. It has no name, real employer, location or contact details. This is classroom simulation only.

Prepare
Actual half-term books or saved work, portfolio list, four reflection cards, tier pack, vocabulary, Evidence Sheet and usual aids. Keep missing items visible; do not recreate a completed experience. Confirm private help, typing/exact scribe and supported follow-up route. No tools or new customer simulation required.

Arrival and I Do
0–5: find actual work or note missing items; confirm activity mode, actual date and usual access. 5–9: introduce the four reflection parts and five Week 1 expectations. 9–13: model invented work with one pack checked and the second still needing a check. Separate an observed action from a next-step intention. CfU: were both checked? Key no, only one. An intended request for help is not an observed help request.

We Do
13–19: match the invented claims to the stated evidence, keeping the unchecked pack visible. Rehearse four frames with a model or neutral statement, then locate each learner’s own work. 19–23: main hinge B uses one actual check, an honest gap and next step rather than an unsupported claim that all work was perfect. Parallel A uses actual work/date/mode or records a gap rather than copying the model as personal experience. Re-model and recheck privately.

You Do
23–35: each learner makes their own four-part reflection from actual work. About three minutes per part is a guide within the twelve-minute window; redistribute time and reduce writing for access. Keep actual task/role, date, mode and work reference, with a specific clue. If no activity was completed, mark not yet completed and agree a supported follow-up; a rehearsal or model cannot become a completed placement record.

Review and exit
35–38: each rates the five Week 1 expectations and gives an actual clue where available. Missing opportunity stays Not seen today. 38–40: privately agree one NS, help and review time, keep the learner’s reply and save work. Staff retain the six-outcome grid with actual evidence references, support, gaps and follow-up. It is separate from the learner’s own ratings.

Supported
Choose one actual piece of work. Use the four reflection prompts, word bank, model and sentence starts. Plan in your usual way, use an aid or exact scribe, then check the words are your own. Keep gaps visible, rate the five expectations and choose one next step.

Standard
Use actual half-term work or a real placement record if one exists. Complete the four reflection parts with specific examples. Rate the five expectations against your own evidence, explain a gap and agree one next step and help.

Stretch
Compare two actual pieces of evidence or points in your work. Explain what changed, what helped and a limit of your evidence. Use this to justify one future adjustment. Complete the same four reflection parts and five self-ratings; extra writing optional.

Model check and fade
Only one pack was checked in the invented example. Instructions and quality has one stated check; help-seeking is a proposed next step, not a performed action. Keep actual evidence, frames and normal aids visible. Fade answer cues only; a normal aid is access. Do not require a perfect story, full output or invented weakness.

Reflection and access
Use the actual half-term workplace activity: the invented classroom simulation, or a real placement if one happened. Record actual activity date, task, role, mode and work reference. No employer, person or place names. Do not invent a placement or completed task. If no activity was completed, mark that and agree a supported follow-up; do not use a model as your own evidence.

Adaptations and prompt ladder
Cognition and Learning: one actual item, four short prompts, visible example and reduced writing. Communication and Interaction: rehearsal, wait time, usual aid and exact scribe/readback. SEMH: predictable private review, pass and pause/return; no personality grade. Sensory and Physical: accessible table, large print and seated response. EAL: plan in any language. 1 Point to place/instruction; 2 ask a short question; 3 rephrase; 4 scaffold/choice; 5 model first step only then return task.

Private help route
Trusted adult or school help route. Point to HELP, write or ask privately. If one adult is busy, use the route to another. Pass, pause and supported return available. No personal customer, employment or family account requested.

Evidence sheet
Page 1 gives neutral four-part guidance and five expectations. Page 2 is a blank Type instead reflection record with actual date/mode/task/work reference. Page 3 has blank learner self-ratings and next step/help/reply. Page 4 is teacher only: six exact weekly outcomes, blank tick/comment cells and four blank closing fields. No paper-order or fixed-shift condition on the reflection fields.

Evidence and judgement
Keep the four reflection parts, an actual evidence clue, a visible gap and next step/reply. Learner ratings remain: I did this; I did this with a prompt; I need another try; Not seen today. Staff never prefill these. Teacher only: six exact weekly outcomes, each with an initially blank tick cell and comment cell. Tick only when actual evidence supports the outcome. Comment with a work reference, support used and any gap or follow-up. An unticked outcome is not proof of failure. Keep learner self-assessment separate; never prefill the learner ratings. Judge links between claims, work and future adjustment; no personality, reading-fluency, pace or attendance inference.

Feedback and portfolio
VF, WS, I, NS, //, ?, E and R generic codes. E actual banked work. R after Voice, Audience, NS and reply in the same colour, on the same page or usual mode. One NS might add an evidence clue or make a next step clearer. Retain first try and reply. Offer to Communication portfolio; staff confirm accredited mapping. Classroom work cannot prove real placement attendance or whole-workplace competence.

Safe delivery
Review actual evidence calmly; no new practical or confrontation task. Keep real employer/person/place names and private concerns out of shared work. Listen calmly if a concern is shared, avoid probing or secrecy promises and use the school’s usual route. Offer private help, pass/pause/return and a supported follow-up for missing work.
