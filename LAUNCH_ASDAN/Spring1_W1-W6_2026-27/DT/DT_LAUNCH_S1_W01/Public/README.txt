THE BRIEF AND EVIDENCE
Plan one meal for one person within an invented £2.00 ingredient-use budget. Keep the completed pupil plan and a one-line justification of balance. In the book, record dish, ingredient amounts, prices, total and money left. The pupil owns the choices and explanation; exact confirmed scribing is available. Practical approval can remain pending while the plan meets today's learning outcome. This week does not provide food preparation or tasting evidence.

TIMING
Arrival 5; Starter 3; I do 5; We do 7; We do 2 5; Independent 8; Review 4; Exit 3. Total forty minutes. Brief resets sit inside Arrival, Independent and Review. Models, extra slides, source cards, tier tasks and safety review fit within these stages.

MEAL AND COST MODEL
A cold bean and vegetable wholemeal wrap, fruit and plain yoghurt. The six listed ingredient-use costs total £1.40, leaving 60p. Replacing the 30p apple with a 35p pear gives £1.45 and 55p left. All prices are invented, charge only the amount used, and do not represent the price of buying whole packs. The yoghurt is one 60 g amount shared between the filling and side, so it is charged once. Water is available without charge in the classroom model. No spending is required.

BALANCED EATING
Use the original balanced plate recap and group table. The two largest groups in a balanced pattern are vegetables and fruit, and starchy foods. Include protein and dairy or a suitable alternative; oils and spreads are used in small amounts when needed. Balance builds across the day or week. The listed amounts are planning examples, not personal dietary targets. Avoid calorie counting, weight talk and home-finance disclosure.

READY FOR NEXT WEEK
The selected model dish has three steps: prepare the washed produce with agreed tools; mash ready-to-eat cooked beans and mix the filling; fill and fold the wrap and serve the sides. Use the Dish_for_Week_2 and Kitchen_Safety_and_Hygiene documents. The recipe includes an adult readiness page. An adult opens tins and removes sharp lids. Actual ingredient labels, allergies, dietary needs, access, tools, supervision and chilled storage must be checked before food is issued. No dry beans, heat or raw animal food are needed for this model. Tasting is optional. Suitable substitutions require the same checks; no food is described as allergen-free.

TIERS AND ACCESS
Use Supported, Standard or Stretch, as named in the current brief. Choose one pack. The core evidence remains the pupil's plan and reason. Books come first. Pupil pages prompt work in the book; they are not compulsory write-on worksheets. Plan in any language. Offer reading aloud, a calculator, pointing, speech, signing or exact pupil-directed scribing. Read words back for correction. Keep personal dietary information private and offer a calm pause.

MATERIALS FREE ROUTE
Read the brief and invented prices aloud. Pupils speak, sign or direct their choices and total. Rehearse the three steps with empty hands. An adult records confirmed words when a recording method is available. Keep transcription pending until it exists. This demonstrates planning or simulation, not practical food-handling competence.
