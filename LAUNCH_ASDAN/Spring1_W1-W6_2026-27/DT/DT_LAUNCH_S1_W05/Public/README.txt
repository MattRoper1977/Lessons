THIS WEEK
Retrieve the actual Week 4 design. Measure from the ruler zero, mark and recheck two 100mm by 120mm pieces and 10mm seam lines on the left, right and bottom. Default cut-size check is within 2mm; seam-line check within 2mm. These guide improvement rather than grades. The running-stitch card demonstrates aligning, threading or using a prepared needle, starting between layers, small even passes about 5mm apart through both layers, gentle flat thread tension and securing on the back. Take a small stitch through both layers, leave a loop before pulling tight, pass the needle through the loop and pull snug; repeat once. An adult selects and supervises actual tools. Count needles, stop for a missing or broken needle and do not release work until all parts are accounted for.

BOOK FIRST AND ACCESS
Pupil pages are prompts for the book, not compulsory write-on worksheets. Plan or rehearse in any language. Do not require personal disclosure, public performance or food tasting. Use actual pupil choices and comments rather than copied model evidence.

Any adhesive route requires actual label/fabric approval and full cure before testing; no guessed time, heat or solvents are required. Use the pupil's recorded approved design and keep an unresolved practical pending.
