THE PRACTICAL
Use the actual checked Week 1 plan. The model dish is a cold bean and vegetable wholemeal wrap, fruit and plain yoghurt. Three steps: prepare the washed produce using agreed tools or prepared vegetables; mash cooked ready-to-eat beans and mix the filling; fill and fold the wrap and serve the sides. No heat or raw animal food is needed. The model quantities are one wrap, 80 g drained beans, 60 g carrot, 40 g cabbage, 60 g yoghurt and one small apple. Split the single yoghurt portion into about 20 g in the filling and 40 g alongside. Follow the actual approved plan, needs and substitutions. Tasting is optional.

TIMING
Arrival 5; Starter 3; I do 5; We do 7; We do 2 5; Independent 8; Review 4; Exit 3. Total forty minutes. Pictures, safety hinge and tier choices fit within these stages. The independent stage still has the agreed supervision. Calm pauses use tools down and food protected. Start clearing progressively and stop safely if the dish is unfinished.

BEFORE DURING AND AFTER
Before: use current school records and actual ingredient labels; give GO only after the adult checks. Wash and dry hands, clean the station, confirm tools and keep chilled food stored until needed.
During: supervise agreed tools, maintain clean utensils and protect food from contamination and allergen cross-contact. Stop for changed ingredients, spills, cuts or uncertainty. Keep chilled food out for the shortest preparation time.
After: the adult agrees serving, storage or disposal. Keep chilled food at 0–5°C, follow use-by and storage instructions, and plan same-day eating or agreed safe chilled transport. Transfer unused beans out of an opened tin into a clean covered food container. Refrigerate leftovers promptly; normally use within 48 hours or freeze if suitable, following labels. Clean and clear, put waste away and wash hands.
An adult opens tins and removes sharp lids. A non-specialist cover adult uses washed prepared vegetables and fork assembly unless approved and competent to supervise sharp tools. Staff follow current school health and outbreak restrictions.
