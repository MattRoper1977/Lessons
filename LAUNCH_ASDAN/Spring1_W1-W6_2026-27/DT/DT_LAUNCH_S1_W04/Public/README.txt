THIS WEEK
Design a small reusable open-top pouch for three paper prompt cards, each 60mm by 80mm. Compare generic felt, woven cotton and knit using actual sample tests or clearly labelled model information. The original fabric photo shows appearance only; it does not prove stretch, firmness, edge behaviour or pupil testing. Compare hand running stitch, approved fabric adhesive bonding and optional generic fastenings. A fastening closes an opening and does not replace joined sides and bottom. Default plan: two 100mm by 120mm pieces,10mm seam lines on left, right and bottom, top open, hand running stitch. Record the pupil’s own choice and any approved adaptation.

TIMING AND TIERS
Arrival 5; Starter 3; I do 5; We do 7; We do 2 5; Independent 8; Review 4; Exit 3. Total 40 minutes. Demonstrations, supporting cards and tier tasks fit inside those stages. Select Supported, Standard or Stretch, as specified in the strand brief. Independent is a timetable stage and does not mean unsupervised sharp tools.

BOOK FIRST AND ACCESS
Pupil pages are prompts for the book, not compulsory write-on worksheets. Plan or rehearse in any language. Accept pointing, speech, signing, confirmed scribing or pupil-directed adult handling where appropriate. Keep actual adult support visible. Offer a calm pause with tools down and materials protected. Do not require personal disclosure, public performance or food tasting. Use actual pupil choices and comments rather than copied model evidence.

MATERIALS FREE AND SAFETY
Each cover plan and pupil pack supplies a materials-free route. Picture-based choices, paper mock-ups and empty-hand rehearsal are clearly recorded as planning or simulation. They do not prove an unperformed textile or food practical skill. For textiles, staff approve and supervise actual scissors and needles, count all sharps and stop for missing or broken parts. Any adhesive route requires actual label/fabric approval and full cure before testing; no guessed time, heat or solvents are required. Use the pupil's recorded approved design and keep an unresolved practical pending.
