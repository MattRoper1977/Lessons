Online safety & media literacy (Safer Internet Day).
Thursday 21 January 2027 P4
Before the lesson, quietly agree the familiar pause signal and supervised place/route with the learner. A pupil may show a pause card or use the usual aid without a public explanation. A staff member acknowledges and supports the agreed route; never send a learner off alone or remove supervision. Offer a neutral task and a supported return, or arrange a later private catch-up. Pass/step-out is not failure and does not require personal disclosure.
