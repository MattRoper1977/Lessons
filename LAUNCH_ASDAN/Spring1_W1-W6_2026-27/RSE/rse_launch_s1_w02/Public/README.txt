Managing stress & change (Children's MH Week).
Thursday 14 January 2027 · P4
Before the lesson, quietly agree the familiar pause signal and supervised place/route with the learner. A pupil may show a pause card or use the usual aid without a public explanation. A staff member acknowledges and supports the agreed route; never send a learner off alone or remove supervision. Offer a neutral task and a supported return, or arrange a later private catch-up. Pass/step-out is not failure and does not require personal disclosure.
Any disclosure goes to the safeguarding lead through school procedure. Listen calmly, reassure, do not probe, investigate or promise secrecy. Follow urgent school procedures where needed. Keep actual concerns and identifying details out of the lesson pack, book tasks and shared learning evidence; use the school's separate safeguarding record. If a learner cannot identify a trusted adult, support them privately and do not press. Private plans are not collected, photographed, displayed or compared for assessment. Scribing the personal plan is only by agreement; the learner keeps it.
