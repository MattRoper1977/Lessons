The Model — Knowing Myself for Work

Key Idea: Skills, interests and strengths, each backed by an example.

👀 Look: skills, interests and strengths build a profile you can match to work.

Step 0 of 3

Skills — what you can do.

A skill is something you can do and improve: using tools, talking to customers, working carefully. Name it, then back it with an example.

Interests — what pulls you in.

Interests are the tasks you look up from and time has gone. They point toward work you will stick at.

Strengths — proven, with evidence.

A strength is a skill you can prove with a real example: ‘I kept full attendance’, ‘I fixed the display’. Evidence is what an employer believes.

Proving It — The Evidence Loop

Key Idea: An example an employer would believe beats an adjective.
Step 0 of 3

Documentarian

photo your completed skills-and-strengths profile.

Annotate

write ‘my evidence for ___ is ___’.

Witness

staff sign your profile as careers evidence.

For the moderator, not just the folder

A tick says it happened. A witness statement says what you did. Before the photo is filed, the adult writes one sentence naming the thing they actually saw you do — and rings whether you did it Supported, Standard or Stretch.

That ring is the part that counts. It is the difference between “the class did this” and “this candidate did it, at this level, with this much help.” The witness sheet prints with every pack.

Scaffolding – Supported

Access & sensory notes (staff)
· The supported task today is: Sort skill and interest cards into MINE and NOT YET, then pick one strength with an adult.
· Fine motor: offer the chunky-grip pen, and accept a card sort or a spoken answer scribed by an adult — the judgement is the evidence, not the handwriting.
· Sensory: offer the write-it-first option before any share-aloud; nobody is put on the spot cold.
· Cognitive load: a completed small piece beats a half-done large one at moderation. Cut the target, never the thinking.
· Ring the level honestly on the witness sheet. Supported with the work finished is a stronger portfolio entry than Standard with it abandoned.
