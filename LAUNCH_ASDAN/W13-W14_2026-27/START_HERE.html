Truth and authority boundary

Use genuine school-held records only. Never backdate, stage an event, reconstruct a quotation, invent a result or convert adult wording into learner voice. A missing item remains missing; an uncertain permission or status remains awaiting an authorised check.

These pages teach and may help organise potential evidence. They do not add a unit, level, criterion or qualification claim. Registration, assessment, verification and claims remain with authorised centre roles. Nothing is uploaded or stored by these offline pages.

Response and support

Pointing, sorting, writing, typing, signing and exact-word adult scribing can preserve access. Record adult preparation, prompting, scribing and filing separately from learner decisions and meaning. The Supported, Standard and Stretch routes keep the same truth boundary while changing independence, comparison and transfer demand.
