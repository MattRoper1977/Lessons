Team game

Evidence relay

In teams, pass a paper pointer round cards A, B and C below. One partner names a claim. The next points to data that can check it. The next says one limit. Swap roles. The teacher walks round to hear each team. Stay seated and pass or point if you wish.

Points. Each team earns 1 point for naming Card C and a timing it records, and 1 more for the three-session limit. Every team can score and retry.

Starter · 3 min.

Starter question 1 revisits last week. Take one minute for recall and reveal. Why do we use heading styles in a document? Cue: Heading · find facts. Expected: Heading styles label each section so a reader can find facts quickly. Then ask: Which source gives checkable evidence: a sales slogan or a trial record? Expected: The trial record, because it gives checkable timings from three sessions. Use made-up source cards. Plan in your book before typing.
