Words | 2 minutes

Words for checking sources

source: Where a claim or fact comes from.

claim: Something a source says is true.

evidence: Details that help us check a claim.

purpose: Why the source was made.

FICTIONAL TEACHING EXAMPLE

Evidence helps us check it.

Teaching notes

Model source and evidence with M, then give processing time. Use the word page or point to a printed definition; no oral rehearsal is required.
