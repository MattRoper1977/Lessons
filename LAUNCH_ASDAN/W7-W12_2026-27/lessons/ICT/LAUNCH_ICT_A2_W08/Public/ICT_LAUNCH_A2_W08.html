ICT portfolio audit checklist.

  • 1. Safe files: Check clear filenames in the unit folder and fictional data only.
  • 2. Checked source: Confirm Source A (Paper 12, Card 10, Fasteners 6).
  • 3. Formulas: Verify =B4*C4, =B5*C5, =B6*C6 and =SUM(D4:D6) (28) in D4:D7.
  • 4. Chart: Verify three item bars (12, 10, 6) from 0 without the whole total (28).
  • 5. Note & Slide: Verify structured paragraph, slide title first and chart description.
  • 6. Compare & Sign off: Compare v1 (28) and v2 (32) and record exact support used.

Use fictional kit data only. Keep all earlier saved versions intact during your audit.

Use these headings in your book.

  • Safe file and privacy check.
  • Source A and formula check.
  • Three-item chart check.
  • Structured note and slide check.
  • Version 1 and version 2 comparison.
  • Support record and one improvement.
