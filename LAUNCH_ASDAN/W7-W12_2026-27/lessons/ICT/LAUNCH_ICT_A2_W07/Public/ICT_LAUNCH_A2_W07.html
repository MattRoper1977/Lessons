We do · 8 min

Choose and explain a step.

Team game

Pass the update

In teams, pass a paper pointer along cards A, C, D and E below. Each partner checks one part: source, row totals, chart or slide. Show the next partner the value to carry forward. The teacher walks round to check that each part agrees. Swap roles. Stay seated or direct a partner if you wish.

Points. Each team earns 1 point for matching row totals 12, 10 and 6 with total 28, and 1 more for a three-item chart from zero plus a title-first slide with a description. Every team can score and retry.
