Teaching notes

Initially paper exceeds card by 10 points: 20 minus 10. After the change the gap is 2: 12 minus 10. Starting a bar scale above zero exaggerates lengths. The chart does not show preferences, reasons for the kit, real prices or pack counts. Keep the fair scale and B4=12.
