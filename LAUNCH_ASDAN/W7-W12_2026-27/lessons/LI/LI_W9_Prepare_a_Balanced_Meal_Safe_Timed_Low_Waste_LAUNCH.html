LAUNCH · ASDAN · W9 · LI

Prepare a Balanced Meal: Safe, Timed & Low-Waste

Combine balance, timing, safe preparation and waste reduction in a real adult-life meal task while keeping dietary assumptions and competence claims bounded.

🎯 Learning objective: I can justify a simple balanced meal for an agreed fictional or personal brief, sequence preparation safely, complete authorised actions, respond to one timing or resource constraint and evaluate waste and support.
✅ Success criteria
  • I can locate the evidence and conditions relevant to this task.
  • I can compare an alternative and explain a limitation or trade-off.
  • I can revise, transfer or define an owned next action after feedback.

TA Brief · Prepare a Balanced Meal: Safe, Timed & Low-Waste

Priority now

Keep safety controls firm while using least-prompt-first for sequence, timing and evaluation; do not complete skilled actions merely to produce a polished meal.

Move learning forward

If secure, change serving time or one resource and ask for a justified recovery; if not yet, anchor the learner at the next safe checkpoint and return ownership of the sequence.
