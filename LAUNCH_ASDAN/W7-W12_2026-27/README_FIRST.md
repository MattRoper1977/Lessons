# LAUNCH ASDAN — Weeks 7–12 (2026–27)

Open START_HERE.html.

This pack contains 30 interactive, self-contained 40-minute HTML lessons:

- Pack Week 7 = Autumn 1 Week 7
- Pack Weeks 8–12 = Autumn 2 Weeks 1–5
- Five strands each week: PEQ, Careers, Independent Living, Vocational and Community

This route is screen-only. Printable pupil work, portfolio evidence and ASDAN assessment records come from the existing lessons.

Here, “existing lessons” means printable teaching and learner-work materials only. Official assessment records must use freshly downloaded current unit booklets and centre-authorised forms.

The continuation pages are offline-first and make no external requests. They do not store or upload learner information.

Important: the lessons support teaching and authentic evidence generation. They do not automatically satisfy, assess, sign off or award an ASDAN unit or qualification. Use current centre-authorised ASDAN materials, assessment planning, registration, assessor records, IQA/EQA processes and safeguarding controls.

Autumn 2 Weeks 6–7 remain deliberately outside this pack for the later portfolio/outcome/IQA block.
