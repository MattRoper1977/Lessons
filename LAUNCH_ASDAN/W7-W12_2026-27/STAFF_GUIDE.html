PEQ Team Working spine

The screen pages teach and create authentic evidence opportunities. They do not award a unit or qualification.

  • A team/group has at least three members.
  • The team collaboratively decides goals, roles, responsibilities, tasks and ground rules.
  • At Level 1, the plan contains at least two SMART team goals.
  • Any mapped plan must be used for at least 10 hours of genuine activity; scheduled lesson time does not automatically prove this.
  • The review should include at least two positive outcomes, difficulty management, something done differently, at least two development areas and future use.

Week 12 is an interim review / prepare-for-assessor-review point. It must not be presented as automatic sign-off. Continue authentic activity into the later W13–W14 evidence/IQA block whenever the minimum or evidence sufficiency has not been met.

Controlled cross-strand evidence

Careers, food preparation, hospitality and the community project can provide authentic teamwork contexts only when the centre’s current assessment plan maps them, the learner genuinely follows the agreed team plan, actual duration is logged and the learner’s individual contribution is traceable. Work used for another framework does not automatically become PEQ evidence.

Assessment and authenticity boundary

  • Initial assessment selects the learner’s appropriate level and qualification size before registration. LAUNCH branding does not establish PEQ level.
  • Use the current ASDAN PEQ specification, current unit booklet, centre assessment plan, assessor records and IQA/EQA processes.
  • Registration must occur before assessment.
  • Evidence must be valid, current, sufficient, authentic and traceable by criterion and evidence location.
  • Record reasonable adjustments/support separately. They may preserve access but must not supply the assessed response.
  • The learner must not present another person’s or generative AI output as their own assessed work.
  • Interactive lesson choices are rehearsal and are not qualification evidence by themselves.

SEMH delivery

  • Stable space, familiar adult and clear time boundary.
  • Least Prompt First: wait → self-prompt → visual/source cue → general verbal → specific verbal → model → direct support.
  • No forced public performance or public grading.
  • Access remains while cognitive demand increases through evidence, comparison, limitation, trade-off and transfer.
  • Use fictional/non-sensitive personal data for CVs, applications, digital footprint, money and contact details.
  • Local risk assessment, food hygiene, allergen controls, supervision and safeguarding always override the screen.
This route is screen-only. Printable pupil work, portfolio evidence and ASDAN assessment records come from the existing lessons. Here, “existing lessons” means printable teaching and learner-work materials only. Official assessment records must use freshly downloaded current unit booklets and centre-authorised forms. No pupil print pack has been generated.
