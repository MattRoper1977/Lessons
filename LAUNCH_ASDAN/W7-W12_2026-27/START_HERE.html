Continuation point

This pack continues the supplied Autumn 1 estate chronologically. Week 7 closes the five existing W1–W6 strands honestly; Weeks 8–12 deliver Autumn 2 Weeks 1–5. Autumn 2 Weeks 6–7 remain for the later evidence, portfolio and IQA block.

This route is screen-only. Printable pupil work, portfolio evidence and ASDAN assessment records come from the existing lessons. Here, “existing lessons” means printable teaching and learner-work materials only. Official assessment records must use freshly downloaded current unit booklets and centre-authorised forms. These continuation pages store and upload nothing; qualification decisions remain with the centre’s current authorised ASDAN systems.
