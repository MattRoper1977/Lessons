SHARED DESIGN
The default stand uses suitable thin card and paper tape. Body blank 120 mm wide by 260 mm long; folds at 80, 160 and 240 mm from the same short edge, leaving a 20 mm joining tab inside the first face at the top seam. Keep the ends open. The triangular body base is 120 mm wide by 80 mm deep. Retaining channel 100 mm long by 30 mm wide, with two long folds at 10 and 20 mm from the same long edge. Tape the back flange to the lower front slope, shelf out, front stop up toward the card. The channel projects beyond the body. Overall table-area target is 160 mm wide by 120 mm deep. After adult clearance, test one actual 80 mm by 60 mm paper card for 30 seconds on a level table with hands away. Check removal and safe handling. Do not load with a phone or other heavy object. Written ruler measurements govern; diagrams are not to scale or cutting templates. Cut-size and fold-position targets within 2 mm guide improvement rather than grades. Approved adaptations use their own matching dimensions and plan.

SAFETY
Hand tools only. The taught default needs approved scissors, a ruler, fingers for folds, suitable card and paper tape. Adults approve the actual material, grip, work area, tool condition and supervision. Keep the helping hand clear of the cutting path, turn the card, close scissors and put them down for pauses. If card is hard to cut or fold, stop for approved thinner or prepared material. No craft knife, sharp scoring blade, saw, drill, power tool, hot glue operation or solvent is required. Hot glue is compared as a heated adhesive only. PVA is an optional design choice only after actual label, material and adult approval, with full specified cure before a joined test; no cure time is invented. Return and account for tools, clear offcuts safely and stop for damaged or missing tools. Safety content is non-graphic.
