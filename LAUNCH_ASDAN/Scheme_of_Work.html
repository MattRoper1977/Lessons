LAUNCH ASDAN · Scheme of Work

Autumn 1 · 2026–27 · five ASDAN strands

The design in one paragraph. Five ASDAN strands run across Autumn 1 on the v5 studio chassis: 3-tier arrival and exit, I-Do → We-Do → Independent (15:00, Supported/Standard/Stretch), Lundy artefact slide, evidence loop (photo · annotation · witness), full print pack off the title slide. Personal Effectiveness completes Communication skills (ComSk1) this half-term and works toward a PEQ L1 Award / Extended Award / Certificate across the year (Entry 3 primary, Level 1 stretch; the Stretch tier is written to the Level 1 evidence standard, so moving a flying pupil up is a registration decision, not a re-teach — no Level 2 is registered or claimed here). The other four strands bank ASDAN short courses + AQA UAS.

PEQ Entry 3 (Level 1 stretch)

Works toward PEQ Entry 3 (Award / Extended Award / Certificate; Entry 3 primary, Level 1 stretch, in 2026/27). Autumn 1 completes Communication skills (ComSkE3 · ComSk1 (stretch)). Before-teaching checklist: PEQ cohort registration with ASDAN (evidence counts only after registration; IQA before any EQA sampling) · Trips & Visits booking for any off-site community or vocational work · display space claimed for the strand boards. No L2 registration in 2026/27.
Accreditation facts for staff (spec v1.2). Level 1 sizes: Award 4 credits · Extended Award 9 · Certificate 14 (max 3 adjacent-level credits — in practice only one adjacent unit can ever count). All six L1 units = 15 credits, so a full year homes an L1 Certificate. Partial achievement still certificates at unit level. The 10-hour plan-use window applies to every skill except Communication. RPL reaches back three years and can include ASDAN Short Course achievement. Assessment records need assessor and learner signatures; IQA before EQA; the first-year EQA sampling visit must be booked during this first delivery year (coordinator action). Unit and level attribution is the centre coordinator’s decision — nothing is promised to a learner.
