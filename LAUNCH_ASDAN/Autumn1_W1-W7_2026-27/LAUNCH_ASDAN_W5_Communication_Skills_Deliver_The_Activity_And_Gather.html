Deliver It, Log It, Check the Tools

LAUNCH · ASDAN · Week 5. ASDAN cross-strand. Deliver It, Log It, Check the Tools.

Objective: deliver the communication activity you planned and record the evidence as it happens, file one piece of personal admin correctly, and complete the pre-use check on the tools you will need next week.

Success evidence: the activity delivered with the answers written on the sheet, one admin item filed with the date, and a signed tool check.

Contact happens only with the adult named on your plan. No personal contact details are written down.

40 minutes. Offline.

Arrival · retrieve

Evidence is recorded live or not at all

Last week you wrote the plan. This week you run it, and the difference is the recording.

Evidence written afterwards is a memory of the activity, and memories tidy themselves up.

Written during it, the answers are the answers, including the ones you did not expect.

So the sheet is on the desk while you speak, and the pen is in your hand.

That feels awkward and it is the correct way to do it.

Insist on the sheet being on the desk before any call. A pupil who writes afterwards has produced a story, not a record.

You need your plan from last week, the evidence sheet, one admin item and the tool check card.

Read your plan from last week before you touch anything else. It tells you what you agreed to ask.

If the plan and today disagree, the plan wins; changing it now is a decision to write down.

3 minStarter · predict

Starter · create the need

Two accounts of the same call

Account A: I rang them and they said they need tins mostly.

Account B: asked what three items they run short of in December; answer was tinned tomatoes, long-life milk and nappies; recorded at 10.14 with the coordinator's job title, not their name.

Both happened. Only one can be handed to an assessor.

The second names what was asked, what came back, and when.

It also shows the pupil kept a name out of the record on purpose.

  • 1 · sheet out before you start.
  • 2 · write the answer as it is given.
  • 3 · write the time.
  • 4 · keep names out.

Read account B again and notice what is not in it: no name, and no opinion about the call.

Both of those are deliberate. A record with an opinion in it is a story.

7 minI Do · model

I Do · model

Running it with the pen in my hand

I put the plan and the evidence sheet side by side before I do anything.

I read my written question once, to myself, then make the contact with the adult beside me.

I ask exactly what I wrote. Not a longer version of it.

As they answer, I write. I do not wait for them to finish and then summarise.

If they say something I did not expect, that goes down too, in their words.

At the end I write the time and the job title, never the person's name.

Then I read the sheet back and stop. Tidying it later is how records get invented.

Model writing while the other person is still speaking. Pupils will not do it unless they see it.

I read the question once to myself and then do not look at it again while I speak.

Reading it aloud from the page makes it sound like a form, and people answer forms shortly.

We Do · everyone commits

Sort the records

Six record cards are on your table. Sort them into usable evidence and not usable.

Answer written at the time. Answer remembered that evening. Answer with the person's full name on it.

Answer with the time and job title. Answer summarised into one word. Answer with a failed attempt logged.

Then match each unusable one to the rule it breaks.

When your table is done, hold up the one you found hardest to place.

Two of the six are borderline. Decide anyway, and be ready to say which rule you leaned on.

I Do 2 · connect

Admin and tools are the same habit

Personal admin means the piece of paper that has to be somewhere findable by a date.

A bus pass form, a consent slip, an appointment card.

The skill is not filling it in. It is putting it where you will find it, and writing the date.

The tool check is the same habit pointed at equipment: look before you need it, not when you need it.

So today you check next week's tools, not this week's.

Anything that fails goes on the list now, with a week to sort it.

The tracker is a single sheet with dates on it. Anything filed with no date on the tracker is lost.

Filing is not putting something somewhere safe. It is putting it somewhere findable.

5 minWe Do 2 · rehearse

We Do 2 · lab

Check the tools in pairs

Work in twos. One runs the tool check aloud, the other holds the card.

Handle, moving parts, guard, and the space it will be used in.

Say each part before you look at it.

The person with the card ticks only what they heard.

Then swap and check a second tool.

Say the part name before you look at it. Looking first and naming afterwards is a report, not a check.

9 minIndependent · evidence

Independent · evidence

Sheet, admin, and the tool card

Work on your own. Finish your evidence sheet with the time on it.

Check it for names. If a name is there, cross it out and write the job title.

Then file your admin item where it belongs and write the date on the tracker.

Then finish and sign your tool check card, listing anything that failed.

If you finish early, write who you would tell about the failed item.

  • Supported · complete the evidence sheet with support, and sign the tool card.
  • Standard · complete the sheet with the time and no names, file the admin item with its date, and sign the tool card.
  • Stretch · as Standard, and name who you would tell about a failed item and by when.

Write the time as a real clock time. About ten is not a time anyone can check.

If your contact said something you did not expect, that line is the most valuable one on the sheet.

3 minExit · hand forward

Exit · Audience and Influence

Read the answer to an adult

Read what your contact actually said to the adult at your table.

They read it back and ask what time it was recorded.

If the time is missing, add it now rather than estimating it later.

Then choose what happens next: file it, follow up next week, or ask a second question.

Write that choice on the sheet with today's date before you leave.

The evidence sheet is clipped to last week's plan and both go in the unit folder.
