Pick My Level, Prove One Routine

Set out before pupils arrive. Everything is from the approved PEQ pack. Per table: 3 job cards, 3 self care cards, 3 packing up cards. Per pupil: 1 record card, 1 routine strip, 1 word bank strip, and 3 level cards (Entry 3, Level 1, Not sure). Keep 6 spare routine strips on your desk. Photo A (ready bench) and Photo B (not ready bench) are printed A3 cards from the pack; prop them on the board ledge, no screen needed. Put the class sheet on your desk: one printed list of every pupil name, with a column for the level chosen and a column for the next action. After the exit, photograph every finished record card. Then file the record cards and the class sheet in the PEQ folder.

ASDAN cross-strand. Autumn 1. Week 1 of 7.

ASDAN PEQ. PEQ is short for Personal Effectiveness Qualification. From now on we say PEQ.

Today's objective

  • I will choose my PEQ level for today, Entry 3 or Level 1.
  • I will write one routine that keeps me organised.
  • I will name one vocational skill that a real workplace uses.
A record card with four boxes. Box 1: the card I chose. Box 2: my three routine steps, one on each row. Box 3: the workplace skill. Box 4: my level and one reason. Anything I am not sure about is circled.

Every card, photograph and example today comes from the approved school pack. Nobody is asked about home, family, health, money or anything private.

7 minI Do · model

I Do · model

The PEQ has six units. Today is unit one.

Four words

  • Routine: steps you do in the same order every time.
  • Organised: my things are ready before I need them.
  • Vocational skill: a skill a workplace pays a person to use.
  • Level: how much help you choose for one task.

Entry 3 means the steps are given to me. Level 1 means I plan the steps myself.

A level is a choice for one task on one day. You can choose again next week. It is not a label on you.

Watch me do one, with the Cafe job card from the pack.

The card says set up six tables before opening.

I read both level cards before I start. I say: I choose Level 1 because I want to plan the steps myself.

I say: my routine is wipe, then lay, then check.

I write those three steps on my strip, one on each line.

I say: the workplace skill on this card is being prepared.

At the end I check. I did plan the steps myself. That is my reason for Box 4.

Now watch what I do when I am stuck. On the Reception card is a word I do not know. I do not guess. I write not sure, circle it, and ask an adult.

7 minI Do 2 · test

I Do 2 · connect

Same steps, two names

A routine you already use in school has a name in a workplace.

In school you get your kit ready before a lesson. In a workplace that is called preparation.

In school you put the tools back. In a garden team that is called safe tidy away.

A self care card, modelled the same way

Watch me do one more. The washing hands card, from the self care pack. These cards show school only.

I say: the card says wash your hands before food tech.

I say: my routine is sleeves up, then soap and water, then dry my hands.

I write those three steps on my strip.

I say: the workplace skill on this card is food safety.

Nobody is asked about home, family, health, money or anything private. The card is the evidence, not your own life.

There is a third card type. The packing up card. Its steps are check the timetable, put the right book in the bag, put the bag on the hook. That routine keeps me organised.

All three card types work the same way in your record card.

5 minWe Do 2 · rehearse

We Do 2 · lab

Table lab. Each table has three job cards, three self care cards and three packing up cards.

Every pupil has their own routine strip. Spare strips are on my desk.

Choose a row. You can change row at any time. The row is not you.

  • ◆ Choose one card: a job card, a self care card or a packing up card. Match it to a routine strip. Say the match out loud.
  • ▲ Write the three steps for your card on your own strip, in order. Then name the workplace skill.
  • ★ Write the three steps, name the workplace skill, then write one line saying what proof shows you did it.

If a word is hard, circle it and write not sure. That is a correct move.

Read the three rows aloud before anyone chooses. If a table stalls, hand them one matched pair.

9 minIndependent · evidence

Independent · evidence

Fill in your record card

Now you do the work. You write, point or place cards. An adult will read each box to you.

  • Box 1: the card you chose. A job card, a self care card or a packing up card.
  • Box 2: your three routine steps, one on each row, in the right order.
  • Box 3: the workplace skill name for those steps.
  • Box 4: your level and one reason. Entry 3, or Level 1, or Not sure and an adult helps me choose.

Boxes 1 to 4 are the lesson. Work at your own pace, in any order.

Circle anything you are not sure about and write not sure beside it. That works in any box.

You may point instead of writing. An adult writes your exact words.

Every box points at something real. A card, a photograph, or something that happened in this room.

Circulate with the word bank strips. Scribe exact words, not tidier ones. No photographs yet.

3 minExit · hand forward

Exit · Audience and Influence

You say one thing. An adult writes it down and reads it back to you in your own words.

You say yes, or you say change it. Changing it is the point.

Pointing at your record card counts as speaking.

Finish this sentence: my level for next week is ... because ...

Then one real next action is chosen and written on your card. Keep the same level next week. Or move from Entry 3 to Level 1. Or do it next week without a routine strip.

If you chose Not sure, an adult picks one action with you.

The class sheet is one list of every name, level and next action from today. Your teacher gets it. It goes in the PEQ folder. It sets which cards are on your table next week.

Read back word for word, even a three word answer. If a pupil corrects you, rewrite it in front of them.
