Resources & Tools List — LAUNCH ASDAN

Made by Matt · Autumn 1 · 2026–27 · five ASDAN strands · one shopping-and-setup sheet

One standalone kit sheet for the LAUNCH ASDAN term. Each block lists the resources the strand’s six lessons actually use, drawn from the lesson tasks themselves. The shared evidence kit (tablet, witness slips, print packs, display boards) covers all five strands.

PEQ Level 1 (E3 floor · L2 stretch)

Six-skill self-assessment cards + PEQ word cards (communication, listening, planning, review) · the Effectiveness Board display (headlines only, never rankings) · SMART communication-plan sheets + audience-question cards · feedback (WWW/EBI) slips · sentence-starter strips + L1 command-word mats.

Careers & Experiencing Work · Employability

Strengths / interests / sector cards + local-job photo cards · the Careers Board display · CV and job-application templates · prepared employer-question sheets · pathway and labour-market info cards · SMART careers-target sheets.

Living Independently

Routine / organisation cards + planner templates · home-safety checklist + hazard cards · care-symbol cards + sorting trays · cleaning-product label cards (with gloves as PPE) · appointment / document record templates · Eatwell Guide mat or poster + food picture cards (allergy list checked first — every food session) + practice money and practice price labels (never family finances) · the Independence Board.

Vocational Skills (Hospitality & Gardening)

PPE — goggles, gloves, aprons, sturdy footwear · hand-tool and kitchen/garden-tool sets with a sign-out tray (count out, count back) · instruction / step cards · team-role cards · task-standard checklists · the Vocational Board display.

Community Project & Enterprise

Community photo / survey kit + camera or tablet · needs-map display + evidence pins · project-plan sheets (step · owner · date) + role cards · partner-approach templates · practice money and price labels for the enterprise (project money only) · the Community Board (need → plan → deliver → promote).

Shared evidence kit (all strands)

One tablet for the Documentarian role · printed witness-statement slips (Assessor §4 + Learner §5) · the full print pack off each title slide · display space for the term-long Lundy artefact boards (headlines only, never rankings) · a photo-printing route for annotated portfolio evidence.

Standing checks: photos show work, not faces, unless consent is filed · the allergy list is checked before any food session · money content is practice money only, never family finances · tools are counted out and back every session · off-site community or vocational work runs under the Trips & Visits and health-and-safety policies.

PEQ credit-route framing

  • Award: all credits are at the level of the qualification.
  • Extended Award and Certificate: no more than 3 credits may come from units at the level above or below.
  • The smallest unit is 2 credits, so no more than one adjacent-level unit can contribute.
  • Totals: Entry 3 and Level 1 — 4 / 9 / 14; Level 2 — 4 / 9 / 15; Level 3 — 6 / 11 / 18.
  • Level 3 is for learners aged 16+ and is not a route for this cohort.
  • Barred combinations apply across all six skill families; where the same family is held at two levels, only the highest-level unit counts.
