## How to tell them apart in the catalogue

- v3 science: title ends `(40-min route)`, `family` = `Science Teesside`, path contains `v3_40min/`.
- v3 BUILD estate: title ends `(v3 route)`, `family` = `BUILD Estate v3`, path starts `BUILD_Estate_v3/`.
- Everything else is the live default.

## What the v3 routes are, and are not

They are a lighter 40-minute chassis: Arrival → Starter → I Do → We Do → I Do 2 → We Do 2 →
Independent → Exit, with a two-page print pack. The live suites are a heavier chassis with a
17-section print pack, and they carry the estate's closure sentinels.

**They are not a complete BUILD week.** English, Maths and PSHE were deliberately not rebuilt —
there was no authoritative existing sequence to upgrade. Autumn 2 does not exist by design.

**The v3 BUILD_ASDAN route produces no ASDAN assessment records.** Portfolio evidence for
accreditation comes from the existing `BUILD_ASDAN/` lessons, which carry the Assessor Witness
Statement and its learner-confirmation line in 49 witness surfaces. Retiring the live 31 would
strip that line from 31 of the 49.

**The v3 LAUNCH science route has no external clip.** The live `SCI_L_W5_L2_OsmosisCP.html`
carries the Oak osmosis core-practical link that Matt personally cleared — the only
externally-verified clip in the science estate. Retiring the live fifteen discards it.
