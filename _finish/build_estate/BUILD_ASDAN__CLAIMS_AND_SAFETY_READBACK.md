## Claims
The current SOW itself says exact ASDAN module/challenge codes, registration and relevant approvals must be confirmed locally before teaching/evidence counts. The v3 HTML therefore presents module mappings as current-repo mappings, not certification.

## BUILD Lundy
Space = co-regulation/key adult/visual structure.
Voice = point, sign, demonstrate, verbal response, annotation/re-attempt or authorised short clip.
Audience = adult genuinely receives the response before R/tagging.
Influence = next try, scaffold, activity or regulation choice changes.

## FoodWise
Checked 10 Aug 2026 against current official NHS/FSA material:
- NHS Eatwell Guide: balance is an overall dietary pattern and does not have to be achieved at every single meal.
- FSA food-hygiene material continues to centre cleaning, cooking, chilling and avoiding cross-contamination.
- Effective handwashing matters; gloves are not a substitute for the approved hygiene routine.
Local allergy lists, food-preparation risk assessment and school procedure remain authoritative.
The v3 lessons avoid pupil-body/weight/calorie judgement and use fictional examples.

## Careers
Provider entry requirements, eligibility, vacancies, salaries and route details are time-sensitive and must be checked from current approved careers/provider information. The lessons do not promise eligibility or outcomes.

## Community Project
Do not invent a partner, partner testimony, site approval or off-site visit. Trips/Visits and safeguarding procedures govern off-site activity and real partner contact.

## Evidence
Decision → observable action/product → learner meaning/review.
A photo alone is not treated as understanding.
No lesson creates an upload quota or stores pupil data.
