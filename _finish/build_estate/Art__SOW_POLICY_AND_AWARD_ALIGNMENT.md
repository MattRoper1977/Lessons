## Feedback-policy alignment
The uploaded Feedback Policy describes immediate/verbal feedback as dominant in practical/vocational/BUILD settings.
BUILD:
- SPACE: co-regulation, key adult, visual schedule, sensory tools, movement and explicit permission to refuse.
- VOICE: point, sign, repeat, demonstrate, choice board or short clip.
- AUDIENCE: adult genuinely receives the response; R without engagement is not Audience.
- INFLUENCE: the next try, regulation choice and learning episode are calibrated from the response.

The v3 pages implement those rules staff-side without storing codes or pupil data.

## Evidence model
decision/choice → observable studio product/action → pupil explanation/review.

## Award guardrail
The source SOW describes supported Trinity Arts Award Explore and AQA UAS 'Creating artwork' preparation.
A trained Arts Award adviser must audit the complete personal arts log and current requirements before any award claim.
The HTML lessons do not self-certify the award.

## Reference imagery
The source SOW requires locally approved, credited Teesside reference images and verified practitioner/organisation sources before teaching.
The W2 v3 page therefore permits a teacher to load a local approved image into browser memory only; it does not ship unverified artist imagery or biography.
