About

Made by Matt is one teacher's lessons, packs, slides and games, made in his own time and put online so other teachers can use them.

Who makes it

One person. Everything here is written, designed and checked by one teacher, outside working hours. There is no team and no company behind it. That is why some corners are still rough and why things arrive in batches: a lesson gets written, taught, fixed and then published.

How you may use it

Free to use and print in class. Teachers may use and print these resources in their own classrooms. Please do not republish, sell or host copies without permission.

Not for resale or re-hosting. Link to a page here rather than copying it to another site, so that fixes reach everyone.

Working offline

Downloaded packs work offline. Every lesson page is a single file, and the slides, worksheets and teacher notes in a pack are ordinary files. Once they are on your machine they open without a connection, so a dropped network in a classroom does not stop a lesson.

What this site is not

This is an independent resource and is not endorsed by any school, exam board or awarding body. Where a lesson follows a published specification or a widely used teaching approach, the specification belongs to its owner; the lesson content, wording, layout and activities here are Made by Matt's own.

It is not a school, and it cannot take safeguarding reports. If you or a pupil need help, the Report a concern page lists who can.
