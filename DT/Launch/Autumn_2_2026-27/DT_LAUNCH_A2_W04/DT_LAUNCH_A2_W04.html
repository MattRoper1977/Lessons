Guidance

Staff approve and supervise each setup. No coins, heavy masses, small choking-risk items, body-weight tests or deliberate collapse. Hands stay clear while the loaded model is observed; never support it from below. Stop at three blocks or sooner for slipping, splitting or sharp bending. The adult removes the load. Record a slight bend as an observation; sharp bending ends the test. If the setup is unsuitable, record not tested and why. A supplied model must be identified as adult-made; testing it does not prove prior building. No result is prefilled. A proposed improvement is not claimed effective without a later fair retest.

Formative practice. Record actual actions, support and gaps.

Accept the pupil’s own example or a pass. Record their own words and actual support.

Preparation and safety

Inspect the saved channel models. Prepare equal-height stable supports on a low tray with a 10 cm gap and three identical large light foam or plastic blocks. Staff trial the setup and choose blocks that stack safely. Prepare blank results tables and a separate adult model.

Staff approve and supervise each setup. No coins, heavy masses, small choking-risk items, body-weight tests or deliberate collapse. A supplied model must be identified as adult-made; testing it does not prove prior building. No result is prefilled. A proposed improvement is not claimed effective without a later fair retest.

Guidance

Staff approve and supervise each setup. No coins, heavy masses, small choking-risk items, body-weight tests or deliberate collapse. Hands stay clear while the loaded model is observed; never support it from below. Stop at three blocks or sooner for slipping, splitting or sharp bending. The adult removes the load. Record a slight bend as an observation; sharp bending ends the test. If the setup is unsuitable, record not tested and why. A supplied model must be identified as adult-made; testing it does not prove prior building. No result is prefilled. A proposed improvement is not claimed effective without a later fair retest.

Formative practice. Record actual actions, support and gaps.

Accept the pupil’s own example or a pass. Record their own words and actual support.

Starter · 2 min

Starter question 1 revisits last week. Take one minute for recall and reveal. How did we check our channel model in the last lesson? Cue: Flat middle · raised sides · fit. Expected: We checked the flat base, two raised sides and centred fit across the supports. Then ask: Where should each light block go for the check? Expected: At the centre of the model, in the same place each time. Start in your book. Use the reference cards.

Teaching script 2

I do · 5 min

Test and improve a card bridge. Ask the adult to approve the setup. Place one light block at the centre. Wait five seconds. Look and record. Keep the 10 cm gap, block type, centre position and five-second wait the same. Try two, then three blocks only if stable. Stop at three blocks, or sooner if it slips, splits or bends sharply. Three blocks is the test cap, not the maximum strength. A proposed change is not proven until a fair retest.

We do · 5 min

The bridge starts to slip. What next? Ask for a choice and a reason before revealing. B. Stop and keep hands clear. The adult removes the blocks. Record the slip. Do not add more. On an empty setup, show the stop signal. Explain why holding the bridge from below would change the test.
