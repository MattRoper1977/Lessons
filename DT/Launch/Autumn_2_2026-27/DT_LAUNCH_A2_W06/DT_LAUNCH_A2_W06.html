Teaching script 1

Arrival · 3 min

Invite a private regulation check. Green · Ready, Amber · Help, Red · Pause. Offer a quiet start, usual aid or pass. Use the cue and accept the pupil’s usual communication mode.

Starter · 3 min

Starter question 1 revisits last week. Take one minute for recall and reveal. How did we record a missing process photo in the last lesson? Cue: Real record · honest gap. Expected: We labelled the missing photo and used a real plan, model or test note. We did not pretend a new photo showed the old step. Then ask: A model photo looks neat. Does that prove its strength? Expected: No. The photo shows its shape. A real test record can show what happened in the test. Start in the book. Use the reference cards.

Teaching script 2

I do · 5 min

Show my model and explain a change. Show the model and say what it was for. Tell one step you did and the help used. Use a real result to propose a change. Adult example only: a card bridge spans a gap. Its test note says the centre bent. A folded edge might help the centre. A fair retest would check the idea. A proposed change is not a proven result. Hear the real audience question. Reply or ask for it again. You may say: I need to check.

We do · 5 min

We do decision checkpoint. All routes: Which claim fits a proposed change? Choices: A. It will hold any load. B. It may help. A fair retest can check. C. A neat model needs no test. Take each learner’s choice and clue before revealing. B. A change may help. Use a result to explain it, then a fair retest to check. Do not claim any load is safe. Hinge check for all routes. The test note says the model slipped. Can I say it passed every test? Give a reason. No. Report the slip and the stop. If the link is unclear, model a result-to-change sentence and ask again before the book task. Record actual help and adapt before independent work.

Guidance

No new load test is required. Display a stable model with adult approval and keep a clear space. Use work-only images under local permissions; no public upload or personal account. Do not require a group performance or recording. A prepared script is preparation until the learner has shared it with a real audience and responded to a real question. Never invent a result, earlier action, audience or reply. Record help and keep unfinished parts open.

Formative practice. Record actual actions, support and gaps. Accept the pupil’s own example or a pass. Record their own words and actual support.

Teaching script 1

Arrival · 3 min

Invite a private regulation check. Green · Ready, Amber · Help, Red · Pause. Offer a quiet start, usual aid or pass. Use the cue and accept the pupil’s usual communication mode.

Starter · 3 min

Starter question 1 revisits last week. Take one minute for recall and reveal. How did we record a missing process photo in the last lesson? Cue: Real record · honest gap. Expected: We labelled the missing photo and used a real plan, model or test note. We did not pretend a new photo showed the old step. Then ask: A model photo looks neat. Does that prove its strength? Expected: No. The photo shows its shape. A real test record can show what happened in the test. Start in the book. Use the reference cards.

Made by Matt

Teaching script 2

I do · 5 min

Show my model and explain a change. Show the model and say what it was for. Tell one step you did and the help used. Use a real result to propose a change. Adult example only: a card bridge spans a gap. Its test note says the centre bent. A folded edge might help the centre. A fair retest would check the idea. A proposed change is not a proven result. Hear the real audience question. Reply or ask for it again. You may say: I need to check.

We do · 5 min

We do decision checkpoint. All routes: Which claim fits a proposed change? Choices: A. It will hold any load. B. It may help. A fair retest can check. C. A neat model needs no test. Take each learner’s choice and clue before revealing. B. A change may help. Use a result to explain it, then a fair retest to check. Do not claim any load is safe. Hinge check for all routes. The test note says the model slipped. Can I say it passed every test? Give a reason. No. Report the slip and the stop. If the link is unclear, model a result-to-change sentence and ask again before the book task. Record actual help and adapt before independent work.

Made by Matt

Teaching script 3

You do · 17 min

Within the task stage, allow four minutes for book planning, seven for individual sharing and six for the real question, reply and capture. Use short private turns. Supported work can use one frame at a time. Keep the original words, actual question, reply, one specific feedback prompt and later change separate. A not-tested record or missing item remains a gap. In your book, plan a short account of your model. Include its purpose, your step, help and a real result. Link a proposed change to that result. Share with a chosen audience. Keep their real question and your reply. Supported Use a frame to show your own model or work record. Give a real result and one change with help. Independent Explain purpose, your step, help, result and change. Respond to a real audience question. Aspire Explain what a fair retest would keep the same. Use your result when you answer the audience question. Choose how Choose a tier and your usual response mode. Use a quiet adult audience, a note, sign or aid.

Teaching script 4

Review · 4 min

Check one part. Use feedback to make one change. Optional. Respond to feedback with an adult. You may pass. Mark R only after the pupil has responded to feedback and the adult has engaged with the response. Keep the first response and any change separate. Edit in the same colour, on the same page or usual mode.

Exit · 3 min

Ask the two route checks. What helped you most today, or what would you change next time? Point, speak, sign, draw, write or choose. You may pass. Accept the pupil’s own example or a pass. Record their own words and actual support. At check-out, record E for … against the pupil's target.
