We do

Check the flat blank

  • Check each end against the plan. Both pairs of marks must read 2 cm and 6 cm from the star.
  • Check that each dashed line joins matching marks. The middle panel is 4 cm wide.
  • If a mark differs, check ruler zero. Correct the mark and say what changed.

You do · Supported · Independent · Aspire · Choice

Start in your book

  1. In your book, sketch and label the 20 cm by 8 cm plan. Show the start edge and two fold lines.
  2. After the adult’s safety check, mark the staff-cut blank and check both ends.
  3. Keep your blank flat. Record a check and any support used beside your plan.
CardRead the card
ADraw the plan. Add 20 cm, 8 cm and the two fold lines.
BCheck the flat card is 20 cm by 8 cm. Choose one long start edge.
CPut ruler zero at the start edge. Mark 2 cm and 6 cm at one short end.
DAt the other short end, mark 2 cm and 6 cm from that same long edge.
EJoin the two 2 cm marks. Then join the two 6 cm marks. Use dash lines.
FCheck the plan and marks. Store the card flat for the build.
