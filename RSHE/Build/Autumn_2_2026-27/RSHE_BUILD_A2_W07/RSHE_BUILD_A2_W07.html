Guidance.

Keep all first responses before modelling or feedback. Starter question 1 recalls the previous topic for one minute; the rest of Starter contains the initial checks. Do not show task keys, Word help meanings or worked answers until the first record is secured. Read exact task words and scribe without correction. Record reading, aid and exact scribing as access support. Reduced choices, answer cues, supplied reasons, models and step prompts are content support. Record each prompt and its timing. Keep later replies or retries separate; do not relabel them as independent first performance. Mark missing, unobserved and omitted checks honestly. No total score, qualification result or completion is inferred. Staff keep source dates and private work references in the private audit; pupil pages contain no dates. A pause or omitted term is not a wrong answer. If a real concern arises, listen and follow safeguarding privately; do not use it as lesson evidence. Do not promise secrecy.
