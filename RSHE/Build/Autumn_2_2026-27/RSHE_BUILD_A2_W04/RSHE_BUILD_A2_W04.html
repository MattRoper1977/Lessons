Guidance.

Use fictional third-person stories only. Never invite disclosure, personal comparisons, real borrowing or touching. No photographs, body checking, removing clothes or personal role-play. A clear freely given yes can use speech, sign, pointing or an aid; eye contact is not required. Silence or no clear reply is not agreement. Stop for a changed mind. Do not demand private reasons or repeat requests to wear someone down. Respect a pass or pause without asking why. If a real concern arises, use the safeguarding procedure privately. Keep that record separate. This lesson teaches everyday boundaries, not legal rules about sexual consent.
