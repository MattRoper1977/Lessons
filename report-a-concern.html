Report a concern

If something is wrong, or you are worried about yourself or someone else, you do not have to sort it out on your own. Here is who can help. You can talk to any of them, and you will not be in trouble for asking.

If you are in danger right now

Call 999. Ask for the police or an ambulance. If you cannot speak, stay on the line and press 55 when you are asked to.

Talk to a trusted adult at school

Every school has a designated safeguarding lead. That is the person whose job it is to help when a pupil is worried, unsafe or being hurt. Any adult at school can take you to them. You can start by telling the adult you trust most. You do not need to have the right words.

Childline

Call 0800 1111. It is free, it does not show on a phone bill, and it is open all day and all night. You can talk about anything, big or small, and you do not have to give your name.

If something has happened online

If someone online has made you feel uncomfortable, asked you for pictures, tried to meet you, or talked to you in a sexual way, you can report it to CEOP. They are part of the police and they deal with this every day.

Report to CEOP

If a nude picture of you has been shared

If you are under 18 and a nude or nearly-nude picture or video of you has been shared online, or you are worried it might be, Report Remove can help get it taken down. It is run by Childline with the Internet Watch Foundation. You stay in control and it is confidential.

Use Report Remove

If you need someone to listen

Call the Samaritans on 116 123. It is free, open all day and all night, and for anyone who is struggling. You do not have to be in a crisis to ring.

About this site

This site is an independent teaching resource made by one teacher. It is not a school, and it cannot take reports or act on them. Nothing you type into a lesson on this site is sent anywhere. If you need help, please use one of the people or services above.
