How to use Evidence Binder

  1. ⚙ Setup — one binder per cohort; tick PEQ levels and the real units load pre-filled. Paste your class list.
  2. 🗓 Plan — cohort details, pathway time-budget check, planned activities, and the printable ASDAN planning record for pre-delivery IQA.
  3. 📷 Capture — photograph work or add witness / oral / learning-log records, tag criteria and pupils. Every item gets a permanent EV number.
  4. 🔎 Review — the assessor records a decision per criterion. Tagging suggests; only decisions achieve. The inbox queues what's next.
  5. ✅ Coverage — pupils × criteria at a glance with decision status; the qualification tracker counts confirmed credits and flags barred combinations.
  6. 📄 Portfolios — print-ready PDF per pupil: cover, decisions, evidence pages.
  7. 💾 Back up each binder weekly — everything lives only in this browser until you do.
Safeguarding note: photos stay on this device — nothing is uploaded anywhere. Treat the device and backups with the same care as a paper portfolio.
