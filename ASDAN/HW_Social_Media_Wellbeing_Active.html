Social Media & Emotional Wellbeing

Main question: What is one way social media helps wellbeing, and one way it might harm it?

Learning objective:
  • To explore how social media can affect young people's emotional wellbeing in positive and negative ways.
Success criteria — I will…
  • vote on real scenarios and give one reason out loud
  • help build our class team of helps vs harms
  • write my verdict — just two sentences

Navigation: Arrow keys / Spacebar  |  40 minutes  |  Skills: Use of English · Discussion

I Do — A Balanced View⏱ 4 min · listen, then tap to reveal

We're not deciding "good" or "bad" — we're looking at balance.

❌ Misconception: "Social media is always bad for young people."
✅ Truth: it depends on how it's used, what you see, and how you feel afterwards. (Tap the myth to bin it.)

Can HELP wellbeing ✅

🤝 Keeps people connectedGroup chats, calls, staying close to family and friends far away.
🎓 Helps people learnRevision pages, tutorials, new skills — free and instant.
🛟 Gives support networksFinding people who get it — hobbies, fandoms, communities.
🎨 Creativity & self-expressionMaking, sharing, editing — being seen for what you create.

Can HARM wellbeing ⚠️

🪞 Comparison anxietyEveryone's highlight reel vs your behind-the-scenes.
💬 CyberbullyingUnkind messages that follow you home in your pocket.
😴 Sleep loss"One more scroll" at 2 a.m. — the brain pays for it tomorrow.
⏰ Pressure to be onlineStreaks, replies, FOMO — feeling you can't switch off.

Key vocab — tap a chip to flip it:

WellbeingHow someone feels in their mind and body
Comparison anxietyFeeling bad comparing your life to others online
CyberbullyingBullying using phones, games, apps or social media
Support networkPeople or groups who help you

You Do — My Verdict⏱ 7 min · the ONLY writing today

The pundit's verdict 🎙️ — your slip, your call.

Use the team boards we just built. Pick your pathway. Two sentences done well beats a page done badly.

🟢 Build: finish the sentences using the team boards.
Social media can help because .
Social media can harm because .
🟠 Grow: full sentences, then rank.
One positive effect is because .
One negative effect is because .
The bigger effect is .
🔵 Launch: add a judgement.
The most serious impact is because .
This is more serious than because .
💡 Evidence tip: one clear reason beats three vague ones. "…because it affects sleep, which affects mood" scores; "…because it's bad" doesn't.
Try: "…because it keeps people connected" · "…because comparing yourself to edited posts lowers confidence" · "…because losing sleep changes your mood the next day".
🌱 Aspire: finished early? Flip your verdict — argue the opposite side out loud to a partner. Can you make it convincing?
Exit Ticket — Full-Time Whistle⏱ 5 min

Answer the main question on your slip. 📣

Main question: What is one way social media helps wellbeing, and one way it might harm it?
Two sentences. Frames if you need them:
Social media can help wellbeing because .
It can harm wellbeing because .
Stretch: which impact do you think is most serious? Why?

Model answer (revealed after slips are in):

Social media can help wellbeing because it connects people to friends and support networks. It can harm wellbeing because staying up late scrolling causes sleep loss, which lowers mood the next day.
🌱 One helpful habit to try this week: take a break from scrolling · follow positive accounts · talk to a trusted adult if something feels wrong.
