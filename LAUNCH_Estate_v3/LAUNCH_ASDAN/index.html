🧠 PEQ Entry 3 (Level 1 stretch)

Current LAUNCH hub says Autumn 1 completes Communication skills (ComSkE3 · ComSk1 (stretch)) and works toward a PEQ Entry 3 Award/Extended Award/Certificate across the year. In 2026/27 Entry 3 is the working level and Level 1 is the stretch tier for the 2–4 pupils above it; no Level 2 is registered or claimed. Authorised assessor/coordinator controls all claims.

W1 · Intro & Choosing My LevelProtect the integrity of level choice while helping pupils understand what evidence makes the conversation fair and transparent.W2 · What Makes Communication EffectiveMake communication effectiveness observable so pupils can plan and self-check rather than wait for general adult feedback.W3 · Active Listening & Giving FeedbackReplace vague ‘good listening’ expectations with inclusive, observable functions.W4 · Plan a Communication ActivitySupport independent project planning while keeping consent, risk and delivery authority explicit.W5 · Deliver the Activity & Gather EvidenceAllow pupils to rehearse adaptations and evidence decisions without generating a false performance record.W6 · Review Progress & Sign Off the UnitTeach transparent portfolio curation while drawing a bright line around assessment authority.

🧭 Careers & Experiencing Work

Current hub maps this strand to an ASDAN short-course / AQA UAS evidence route. Live pathway, employer, entry requirement and opportunity information must be checked from current approved sources; no outcome is promised.

W1 · Know Myself for WorkDevelop a profile pupils can actually use to compare opportunities independently.W2 · The World of Work & Its SectorsAvoid static job lists by showing cross-sector roles and transferable skills.W3 · Labour-Market Information & PathwaysBuild independent source-checking and reduce reliance on remembered or generic career claims.W4 · Meeting an EmployerGive pupils a portable routine for employer visits, talks or interviews and make next-step transfer explicit.W5 · Matching My Profile to OpportunitiesTeach pupils to hold fit and uncertainty together and generate their own next enquiry.W6 · Setting SMART Careers TargetsEnd the sequence with an achievable enquiry or preparation action pupils can carry forward independently.

🏠 Living Independently

Current hub maps this strand to an ASDAN short-course / AQA UAS evidence route. Use fictional/non-sensitive examples where possible; practical tasks use local safety/supervision and never require private household/medical data.

W1 · Self-Care Routines & OrganisationSupport routine independence through task-specific scaffolding while preserving dignity and privacy.W2 · Home Safety & Hazard AwarenessTurn safety into a repeatable scan-and-report process rather than a fear-based list.W3 · Laundry & Household MaintenanceBuild a visual procedure pupils can transfer to a real appliance only after local demonstration.W4 · Keeping a Clean, Safe Living SpacePrevent overload and unsafe improvisation by externalising the task boundary and end state.W5 · Personal Admin & ResponsibilitiesBuild functional admin literacy while enforcing the ASDAN data boundary.W6 · Plan & Shop for a Balanced MealIntegrate food, money and organisational skills into a single real-world planning sequence.

🧰 Vocational Skills

Current hub maps this strand to Hospitality/Gardening ASDAN short-course + AQA UAS evidence. The HTML is never a risk assessment or tool authorisation; competent/local adult and current controls remain authoritative.

W1 · Introduction to Vocational Skills & WorkplacesBuild a cross-workplace skill language before pupils encounter specific tasks.W2 · Health, Safety & Hygiene at WorkMake the hierarchy and ownership of workplace controls visible while avoiding generic ‘be careful’ messages.W3 · Following Instructions & RoutinesTeach a self-checking routine that reduces passive waiting and unsafe guessing.W4 · Teamwork in a Vocational SettingMake workplace coordination visible and allow pupils to rehearse disruption before the real task.W5 · Tools, Equipment & Safe UseSupport safety reasoning while maintaining a non-negotiable boundary between digital rehearsal and physical authorisation.W6 · Complete a Supported Vocational TaskHelp pupils use support deliberately and explain their own contribution without being pushed toward false independence.

🌍 Community Project & Enterprise

Current hub maps this strand to ASDAN short-course + AQA UAS. Real partners, participants, publications and impact claims require verified approval/evidence; do not invent contact, consent or outcomes.

W1 · Identify a Community NeedRaise the quality of enquiry before pupils commit to a social-action or enterprise response.W2 · Plan a Social-Action or Enterprise ProjectMake causal reasoning and feasibility visible before pupils invest in branding or activity details.W3 · Agree Roles, Aims & TimelineGive pupils enough project visibility to locate and own their next step without constant adult direction.W4 · Partner with a Local Group or CharityBuild professional partnership reasoning while enforcing contact and data boundaries.W5 · Begin Delivering the ProjectLet pupils rehearse operational decisions and recovery before working with real participants or partners.W6 · Promote Our ProjectMove promotion from visual decoration to audience-centred, evidence-led communication.
