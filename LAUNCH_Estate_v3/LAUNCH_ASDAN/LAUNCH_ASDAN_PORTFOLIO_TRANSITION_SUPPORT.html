Support page only. It is not counted as an invented seventh taught lesson in each six-lesson strand.

Evidence audit

For each strand, distinguish genuine complete evidence · genuine evidence needing learner evaluation/support attribution · evidence genuinely missing · assessor/coordinator/code check still needed.

Independent review

Use one learner-led review: what changed, evidence, support used, limitation/trade-off and one next action. Do not backfill missing experiences with staged photos or invented witness statements.

Transition

Carry forward one current careers/PfA/vocational/community target or one communication skill into the next half-term. Where an external adult owns the action, tell the learner who, what and when they will hear back.

Claims

Final PEQ/ASDAN/UAS/short-course decisions and registration stay with the authorised assessor/coordinator/centre process. L2 wording in the 2026/27 PEQ hub is stretch language only, never an L2 registration.
