Verification, shared value, adult contact gate and partner voice

🎯 Learning objective: I can use verification, shared value, adult contact gate and partner voice to make a substantiated decision, evaluate limitations/trade-offs and transfer the learning to a real or unfamiliar context.
✅ Success criteria
  • I can identify the evidence/conditions relevant to verification, shared value, adult contact gate and partner voice.
  • I can compare at least one alternative/counterpoint and qualify my decision.
  • I can use feedback to revise, transfer or define a next action without adult takeover.

Purpose

Build professional partnership reasoning while enforcing contact and data boundaries.

Claim boundary

Current hub maps this strand to ASDAN short-course + AQA UAS. Real partners, participants, publications and impact claims require verified approval/evidence; do not invent contact, consent or outcomes.

Care / safety / data

External contact/publication/partner/participant activity stays behind authorised adult, consent, safeguarding and data-protection gates. Impact remains ‘not yet known’ until evidence exists.

Evidence

Prepare the partner brief and approved contact. Record actual priorities, boundaries, decisions and next action exactly when received.

Interactive rehearsal is never assessment evidence by itself.

Wider-curriculum capture

Use the school’s authorised evidence system. Launch policy expects selective weekly wider-curriculum capture, not a staged photo from every page.
