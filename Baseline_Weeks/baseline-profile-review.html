Pupil voice

Ask rather than infer:

  • What helps you get started?
  • What makes work harder?
  • How do you prefer to show what you know?
  • What would you like adults to understand about learning with you?
  • What are you interested in or looking forward to?

Do not convert this page into a diagnosis or automatic placement decision.

Suggested staff discussion

Space: What conditions allowed the learner to respond?

Voice: Which response mode showed their thinking most clearly?

Audience: What did an adult genuinely learn from the response?

Influence: What will change in teaching, support or the next task because of it?
