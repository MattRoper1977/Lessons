The Repair Café

On Saturday morning, the old library hall was busier than usual. Volunteers had turned it into a repair café. Instead of throwing broken objects away, people could bring them in and learn how to fix them.

Mina arrived with a pair of headphones that only worked on one side. She expected someone else to repair them, but a volunteer called Jay showed her how to check the cable carefully. They found a loose connection near the plug. Mina watched the first repair, then completed the second step herself.

Across the room, another group repaired a bicycle light and stitched a torn jacket. By lunchtime, the hall was full of objects that had been given another chance. Mina left with working headphones and a new idea: fixing something could be more satisfying than replacing it.
