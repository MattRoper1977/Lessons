## The one that is stale by its own slot

`V07_Viral_Claims_AI_Democratic_Disagreement`, batch **07**, slot **2 Nov 2026**:

> *"Votes at 16 is already available in all UK elections" — as checked 9 September 2026 the
> national reform is a Bill; check current official eligibility before teaching.*

> *Representation of the People Bill: as checked 9 September 2026, Lords first reading
> 3 September and second reading 14 September.*

A second reading on **14 September** and a teaching slot on **2 Nov** are seven weeks apart.
The claim will be out of date on the day it is taught. **It is not changed here**: the deck
already tells the teacher to check current official eligibility before teaching, and an
honestly labelled dated claim is not a defect — a silently updated one is a provenance break.

`S09_Young_Carers_Wellbeing` carries its own recheck date in the same spirit: its listed
media review falls on **30 October**, before its 2 Nov slot.
