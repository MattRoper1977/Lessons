## D45 — the GROW Computing collectible is backlog, not a gap

> **GROW Computing Week 7 — collectible route (Star shows at the flag, hides on
> contact with Player) is unauthored. Candidate extension pack, needs its own
> booklet steps, teacher-guide row, reference block and `.sb3` model before any
> surface names it. AQA 71638 outcome 5 does not require it.**

Nothing is built for it in this order. It is **not a STOP, not a defect, and not
carried forward as residue on GC1** — GC1 closes without it.

### How this arrived

The revision made the moving Hazard the fully taught Week 7 task. D40 then asked
for the hub, unit index and subject-page copy to give the collectible **equal
billing**, derived from the pack's own text.

Measured across all 35 files of the revision — lesson, booklets, step-by-step,
challenge, teacher guide, planning, `Start_Here`, `Read Me`: the collectible
appears in **exactly one sentence**, in `GROW_Computing_Weeks_07_08_Review_Notes.txt`:

> "2. Made the moving Hazard the fully taught Week 7 task. The partial collectible
> alternative is now an extension requiring complete instructions and agreed time."

That file is an authoring record which **D37 routes to `_authoring`, never to a
surface**, and the sentence itself says the route is not yet authored. Equal
billing was therefore a requirement to describe teaching material that does not
exist, and writing it would have breached derive-degrade-never-invent, which is
the stronger rule. **The stop was correct; the rule was wrong** (D44).

### What the copy says instead

What is authored — the moving Hazard — and nothing else. No mention of a Star, of
collecting, or of an alternative route, on any surface.

This does not weaken the AQA position. Outcome 5 reads *"add something to make the
game more complex, eg items to collect on the way round, a door which can only be
passed when all items are collected"*. The examples are illustrative, not
required, and a moving obstacle with a contact consequence satisfies the outcome
on its own terms.

**One constraint survives:** no surface may state or imply that a moving obstacle
is the *only* way to meet outcome 5. Measured across the revision: **0 surfaces**
do. No listed conflict.

The live case is already covered without a copy change. The teacher guide's
pupil-voice loop adopts, adapts or defers a pupil's own proposal with a reason —
so a pupil who asks to collect something is answered by the chassis as it stands.
