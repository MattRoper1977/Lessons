GROW ASDAN · WEEKS 7–8

Consolidation, Portfolio Audit & Next Action

The current GROW ASDAN hub explicitly sets Weeks 7–8 as consolidation and portfolio audit. This is therefore one support studio, not invented extra module lessons.

5 min

Arrival · portfolio triage

Choose PEQ, Community Project or Enterprise. Sort evidence: genuine and complete · genuine but learner meaning/support record needs strengthening · genuinely missing · claim/code/approval needs coordinator check.

5 min

I Do · honest evidence check

Model the difference between a rehearsal screenshot and real evidence. The v3 interaction rehearses thinking; qualification evidence comes from the real independent task, project, pitch, calculation, learner review or authorised witness/process.

5 min

We Do · Audience

Adult receives one learner response fully and names back the actual progress/change before any R/tag/witness note.

20 min

Independent · strengthen only what is authentic

  • Annotate genuine work with learner meaning.
  • Complete an authentic reflection/re-attempt that can validly happen now.
  • Record support/witness information only where the current centre/programme process requires it.
  • Leave missing experience as missing and schedule the real opportunity.
  • Community Project delivery remains Autumn 2 where the current hub says it does.
5 min

Exit · Influence

Choose one real next action: authorised assessor/coordinator check · specific authentic evidence still needed · Autumn 2 project action · next PEQ goal · enterprise keep/change/test decision. Tell the learner what the adult-owned action is and when they will hear back.
