  • I can qualify the verdict using evidence and uncertainty.

🔍 Enquiry

How can criteria, perspective and timescale produce a defensible ‘how big a deal’ judgement?

‘Significant’ is not a fact printed on a person or event. What does a historian have to decide before using the word?
