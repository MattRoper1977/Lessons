Plan & Rehearse the Skill Share

Bronze Part D · Skill Share (Plan)

🎯 Learning objective: I can break an arts skill into teachable steps, plan access/checks and rehearse how I will know the learner has understood.
✅ Success criteria
  • I can choose a skill I genuinely understand.
  • I can break it into a manageable sequence.
  • I can plan a check/adaptation for another learner.
