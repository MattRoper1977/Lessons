Staff · accreditation status
  • Regulated PEQ evidence: name the PEQ unit and criterion references, collect authentic evidence, make an assessor decision, and complete internal quality assurance.
  • Separate activity: ASDAN Short Course and AQA UAS activity is not regulated PEQ evidence by itself.
  • No automatic conversion: a Short Course certificate does not automatically become PEQ credit.
  • Prior learning: any claim must follow ASDAN’s RPL procedure and can count only when achieved within 3 years of the qualification quality-assurance date.
