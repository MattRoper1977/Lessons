Clean Hands, One Snack, One Photo

BUILD · ASDAN · Week 6. ASDAN cross-strand. Clean Hands, One Snack, One Photo.

Objective: make one snack and one drink from the approved snack card, use the four hygiene steps in order, record the work with a photo and a comment, and take the first job on the community project card.

Success evidence: one snack made in step order, one photo, one comment that names a step, and one project job written down.

Every snack comes from the approved snack card. You do not talk about food at home. You do not talk about anyone's body, size or looks. You follow the card in front of you.

40 minutes. Food station and desks.

Arrival · retrieve

Hygiene is an order, not a look

Hygiene is not about how a snack looks.

A snack can look fine and still be unsafe.

Hygiene is the order you do things in.

You wash first. You clean the space next.

Then you make the food. Then you clear up.

Four steps. Always the same four steps.

Say the four step names once and leave them on the board all lesson. Pupils will need the words in their comment later.

Last week you wrote the first message to our local partner.

Today that partner gets a real job from you.

Two words matter today. Hygiene. Evidence.

Hygiene is how you work. Evidence is proof you worked.

One without the other is not enough here.

You need three things. Your snack card. Your photo sheet. A pen.

Starter · create the need

Two trays, one problem

Here are two photos of the same snack.

Both trays hold four crackers and some cheese.

One was made with washed hands on a wiped board.

One was made on a board nobody cleaned.

Look at both photos. Try to pick the safe one.

You cannot. The two trays look the same.

Germs are too small to show up in a photo.

So looking at the plate proves nothing at all.

The only proof is the order you worked in.

  • 1 · you cannot see germs.
  • 2 · so you cannot check food by looking at it.
  • 3 · so you check the steps instead.

That is why we record the steps, not only the plate.

I Do · model

I Do · model

Four steps, in order, out loud

Watch me make the snack on my card.

My card says crackers, cheese and a cup of squash.

Step one is wash. I use soap for twenty seconds.

I dry my hands on a paper towel, not on my top.

Step two is clean the space. I wipe the board and the table.

I get my plate, my cup and my knife out now.

Everything I need is on the table before I start.

Step three is make. I follow the card order.

I cut the cheese on the board. I lay it on the crackers.

I pour the squash. I do not fill the cup to the top.

Step four is clear up. I do it while the snack waits.

Board washed. Knife washed. Table wiped. Bin used.

Model one card only. If a pupil asks for a second recipe, point them back to the card on their own desk.

Watch what my hands do between the steps.

I touched the bin lid. So I washed my hands again.

That is the rule. Hands to bin, hands to sink.

If you drop food, it goes in the bin. It never goes back on the plate.

Sort the cards, then match them

You have six hygiene cards on your table.

Sort the six cards into two groups.

Group one is before you touch food.

Group two is after you touch food.

Then match each card to one of the four steps.

Hold up the card when I call its step.

Hold it up even if you are not sure.

A card I can see beats a card you kept down.

One card fits two steps. Say which one you chose.

I Do 2 · connect

I Do 2 · connect

A photo plus a comment

Now I record what I did.

A photo on its own is weak evidence.

My photo shows a plate. It does not show the wash.

So I write a comment under the photo.

The comment names the step and says what my hands did.

I wrote this. I washed my hands and wiped the board before I cut the cheese.

That one line turns a plate into evidence.

There are two more rules for the photo.

Photograph your hands and your board. Keep faces out.

No faces go in the ASDAN folder.

Take the photo while the step is happening.

A clean board at the end is not proof you cleaned it first.

  • 1 · take the photo during the step.
  • 2 · keep faces out of the frame.
  • 3 · name the step in your comment.
  • 4 · say what your hands did.

Independent · evidence

Your turn at the station

You get one turn at the food station.

Take your snack card to the station with you.

Do the four steps in order. Do not skip step two.

Ask the adult for your photo when your hands are working.

Then write the comment under the photo on your sheet.

Write your project job line while you wait for your turn.

  • Supported · do the four steps with an adult beside you, ask for your photo, and say your comment for the adult to write.
  • Standard · do the four steps on your own, take one photo, and write a comment that names a step.
  • Stretch · as Standard, and write what you need and who you ask before your community project job can start.

Do not start until your hands are dry.

If you touch your face or your phone, wash again.

That is not a punishment. Your hands picked something up.

If you finish early, check a friend's comment for a step word.
