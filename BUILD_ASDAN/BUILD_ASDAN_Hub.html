BUILD ASDAN — the full term · Studio Decks

Every ASDAN slot, one connected system. Reluctant writers build portfolio evidence through making, doing and photographing — not paperwork.

What this banks, stated honestly. Most BUILD slots bank an ASDAN Short Course — a non-regulated programme recorded through a portfolio and internal moderation, carrying no grade and no level. Where a pupil is registered for ASDAN PEQ, that is a regulated qualification with a named unit, code and credit. The centre is registered for both; what remains is per-pupil registration, assessment and quality assurance. Evidence status uses the controlled states in the claims register — nothing is certified until the awarding organisation says so.

Shared blueprint (every pack): a title illustration + warm-up → a different settle activity each week (wordsearch, match-up, odd-one-out, sequence, sort, team quiz) → ▣ MODEL (I do / we do) → ◆ INDEPENDENT (you do — Standard or Supported) → the Lundy loop onto a term-long class artefact → Evidence & review (Documentarian photos, annotated visuals, witness-statement shorthand → ASDAN tracker + evidence status). Zones are icon+label+colour (never colour alone), Calm Mode + print built in, no pupil names.

Staff · PEQ credit-route rules (spec v1.2, Oct 2025)
  • Award: all credits are at the level of the qualification.
  • Extended Award and Certificate: a maximum of 3 credits may come from units at the level above or below.
  • Adjacent-level arithmetic: the smallest unit is 2 credits, so at most one adjacent-level unit can contribute.
  • Totals: Entry 3 and Level 1 — 4 / 9 / 14; Level 2 — 4 / 9 / 15; Level 3 — 6 / 11 / 18. Level 3 is 16+ and is not for this cohort.
  • Barred combinations: this applies across all 6 skill families; where the same family is held at 2 levels, only the highest-level unit counts.

Staff accreditation note: if the full qualification credit total is not reached, the PEQ units the learner has achieved can still be certified (spec v1.2 §5.1).

Staff · accreditation status
  • Regulated PEQ evidence: name the PEQ unit and criterion references, collect authentic evidence, make an assessor decision, and complete internal quality assurance.
  • Separate activity: ASDAN Short Course and AQA UAS activity is not regulated PEQ evidence by itself.
  • No automatic conversion: a Short Course certificate does not automatically become PEQ credit.
  • Prior learning: any claim must follow ASDAN’s RPL procedure and can count only when achieved within 3 years of the qualification quality-assurance date.
