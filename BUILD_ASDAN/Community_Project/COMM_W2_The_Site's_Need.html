The Site's Need

What the design must meet

Arrival · 10 min

Settle & recap

1What does a green space need?
2Big or small — what fits the site?
3Thumb check: thinking about others' needs — okay?
4Point to a feature a site might need.

Vocabulary wordsearch — find the words (no crosswords):

  • SITE
  • NEED
  • DESIGN
  • FIT
  • USER
  • REQUIRE
  • MEASURE
  • PLAN
MODEL zone · I do / we do

Instruction · 15 min

Watch, then try together

  • Finding out what the site and its users need.
  • Turning a need into a design requirement.
  • Checking our idea fits the space.
INDEPENDENT zone · you do

Independent task · 20 min

Your task

Standard: List the site's needs from the photos, then agree two requirements our design must meet.
Supported: Match site-need cards to our asset with an adult and pick one 'must-have'.

Evidence & review · 10 min

Prove it — the launch-level loop

  • DOCUMENTARIAN: photo the needs list.
  • ANNOTATE: 'our design must ___'.
  • WITNESS: staff tick 'identified a site need', sign.

Predicted grade: review after evidence is filed.
