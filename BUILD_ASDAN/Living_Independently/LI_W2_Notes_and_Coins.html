The Model — Notes & Coins

Key Idea: Every coin has a value — know them.

👀 Look: four different coins — one whole pound. Same value, different shapes.

Step 0 of 3

Recognising notes and coins by value.

Every UK coin and note has a fixed value — 1p to £2, £5 to £50. Recognising them at a glance is the base skill everything else stacks on.

Making a given amount two different ways.

Any amount can be built several ways: £1 is two 50ps, five 20ps, ten 10ps. Seeing the swaps is what makes change-giving make sense.

Counting a small total carefully.

Careful counting means one pile, one pass, biggest coins first. Rushing is how totals go wrong — slow is smooth, smooth is fast.

Scaffolding – Supported

Access & sensory notes (staff)
· The supported task today is: Match the coins to their value cards, then build one amount using the marked coin tray.
· Fine motor: offer the chunky-grip pen, and accept a card sort or a spoken answer scribed by an adult — the judgement is the evidence, not the handwriting.
· Sensory: Money is the sensitive part here, not noise. Every example stays practice money, and nobody is ever asked what their family has or spends.
· Cognitive load: a completed small piece beats a half-done large one at moderation. Cut the target, never the thinking.
· Ring the level honestly on the witness sheet. Supported with the work finished is a stronger portfolio entry than Standard with it abandoned.
