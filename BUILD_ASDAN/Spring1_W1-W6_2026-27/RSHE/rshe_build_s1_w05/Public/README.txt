Identify who helps me through change.
Wednesday 3 February 2027 · P1 · Year A.
If a pupil cannot identify a trusted adult or support in any area, support them privately and do not press them. Offer a familiar school role and help make contact through the school route; accept any blank space, a blank card or pass. Any disclosure goes to the safeguarding lead through school procedure. Listen calmly, reassure, do not probe or promise secrecy. Follow urgent school procedures where needed. Do not write disclosure details in this pack, the private card, a book task or shared learning evidence. Do not read private cards aloud, collect or photograph them for assessment. Staff may support completion only with pupil agreement; the pupil keeps the card.
