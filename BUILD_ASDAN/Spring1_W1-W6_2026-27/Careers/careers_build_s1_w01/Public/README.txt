BUILD Careers Spring 1 Week 1
Careers can change
Year A. Spring 1 Week 1, 4–8 January 2027. Tuesday 5 January 2027, P3. One 40-minute formative lesson for up to five learners. Spring 1 runs 4 January–12 February, six weeks; Week 6 is assessing. Source Week 5 retains its mixed evidence role. No clock time supplied.

Weekly outcome: Students understand that career journeys are not always straight and that people may change jobs or retrain.
Exact lesson outcome: I can put a simple career story in order and identify where someone adapted to change.
Assessment evidence: Career-story sequence / resilience note.
Source: BUILD Careers physical row 16 / Spring 1 row 1. Exact E16 weekly outcome, F16 focus, G16 lesson outcome and J16 evidence retained. H16 sequence and I16 timing retained. Review is private rather than a compulsory whole-class response. K16 reduced choices, pictures, model and usual access are built in; all sources are in the pack, so no website navigation is needed. Generic unnamed invented histories replace any real-world example. One coping strategy is recorded for each learner. Source MCQ/choice becomes the two-card route; Aspire is an optional extension. No named exam or organisation wording carried over.

40 minutes: 5 arrival/retrieval + 8 I Do + 10 We Do + 12 You Do + 5 review/exit. Slides 7 and 8 share 23–35 minutes. Do not add their times. Help slide 11 is available throughout; staff slide 12 is hidden. Processing time and brief pauses sit inside the total.

Supported is the default. Pre-teach vocabulary, read one caption at a time and retain the model and word bank. Whole-set and two-card choices both use story C. Independent uses all four cards with usual access; Aspire explains the skill link and another coping step. Visual symbols, captions and card letters carry meaning without colour-only coding. No cold calling, public questioning or compulsory personal story.

All stories, people and work histories are invented and declared. Pictures show work/support types; they are not portraits of the same person. Model A: J K L M, boxes to plants; K change, L new learning. Guided B: R P S Q, food to boxes; P changed shifts, S new learning. Individual C: V T W U, plants to bikes; T job ends, W new learning. Printed B and C are mixed. Each learner chooses one coping strategy: seek help, make a small plan, or agree a pause then plan. Accept other safe relevant ideas. Change is not failure. No promise of a quick job or guaranteed retraining outcome. No real employer, provider, place, product or platform is named.

Book, pointing or placing cards are valid. Reusable print cards are references. Either choice can use the blank editable Type instead area on Evidence Sheet page 2. Use the usual save routine with lesson code and work reference, then show an adult privately. Aid, reader and exact scribe are equally valid; read back and allow correction. Plan in any language. No internet, login or phone needed.

Private help: trusted adult or the school's help route. Point to HELP, write a request or ask for a private talk. Confirm an available adult and pause/return arrangement. If one adult is busy, seek another through the route. No questions about family job loss or finances. If a concern arises, listen calmly, avoid probing or secrecy promises and follow usual procedures. Keep concerns outside public work.

Judge sequence and adaptation separately; record one coping strategy. Keep actual first response, access and answer prompts, one NS and reply. Fixed cards or supplied order are recorded as support. A pass is unobserved. E means actual work banked; R follows Voice, Audience, NS and response. Add to Careers Portfolio and offer to Communication portfolio. Staff confirm any accredited mapping; do not infer future employment success or personal resilience. Main hinge B; parallel A; model check L.
