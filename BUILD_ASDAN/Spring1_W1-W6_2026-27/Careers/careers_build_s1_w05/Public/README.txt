What this lesson teaches
Prepare and ask a useful question, listen to the answer and make a tentative private reflection. FE means further education; ITP means independent training provider. These are categories, not names. Progression means a possible next step. Yes, maybe and no are all valid preferences; no place or final decision is promised.

Success checks
Ask a prepared question using the usual communication mode. Keep the actual answer or an honest unknown. Record yes/maybe/no and identify one feature heard; a reason may remain private. Judge the question and record, never the preference.

Prepare
Confirm school-arranged encounter, category, access and private question turns. If no visitor, select script A or B for staff role-play. Books, question cards, record and usual aids ready. Keep provider/person/place names out of shared work. No internet, login or phone. Confirm trusted adult and pause/return.

Arrival and I Do
0–5: recall a Week 4 fact to check; supply the idea if absent. 5–9: read exact outcome and explain provider categories, question, answer and progression. 9–13: model asking the support question to invented A, hearing word card/break/step help, recording the answer and choosing maybe while another fact remains unknown. The model is not a required learner answer.

We Do
13–19: use planning Card 1. Choose ready question or build one about course content, entry, support, travel or progression. Rehearse privately with a partner or adult; no cold calling. 19–23: hinge on an unanswered travel question; model an honest note and next check. Keep each learner’s prepared question.

You Do
23–35: use private or quiet turns with the arranged visitor, or staff playing invented A/B. Learner asks by voice, aid, typed message or chosen card voiced by an adult. Respond to that question, then allow a clarification. Others rehearse or record while waiting. Keep actual question and answer; read back for confirmation. Record mode and actual date. Do not prefill a visitor answer from the script.

Review and exit
35–38: record yes/maybe/no, a feature heard and an optional reason in private; mark unanswered facts honestly. Set one NS and retain reply. 38–40: show prepared question and reflection, then bank the record. If the real encounter is later, keep its date/answer/reflection pending; any practice has its own role-play label.

Supported
Choose between two ready questions. Keep the picture, question card, model and word bank. Rehearse privately. Use speech, sign, aid, typing or an adult voicing the learner’s selected question. Exact scribe reads back. Record help supplied.

Independent
Choose a ready question or build one from a topic. Ask through the usual communication mode, record the answer and yes/maybe/no reflection. Keep normal reading and communication aids.

Aspire
Ask a relevant follow-up or identify a fact still to check. Link the reflection to one answer feature. No extra public presentation or personal disclosure required.

Model check and fade
A mentions word cards and short breaks. Which question fits? A what help could I ask for; B what is the journey time? Key A. Point to the answer clue if unsure. Keep the question bank and usual aids; fade only answer cues.

Hinge and parallel check
Main: travel details are not known. A invent a journey time; B write not known yet and ask for help to check. Key B. Re-model fact versus guess. Parallel: an entry answer is unclear. A ask for it again and note what is still unknown; B write any grade. Key A. Recheck privately; pass is unobserved.

Private help route
Use a trusted adult or the school’s help route. Point to HELP, write or ask for a private talk. Pass, pause and supported return are available. If one adult is busy, seek another through the route. No real grades, home address, travel history or family finances requested.

Typed and scribed routes
Either question choice uses Evidence Sheet page 2, Type instead, with blank fields. Save through usual routine with lesson code/work reference; show adult privately. Exact scribe records words/selections, reads back and permits correction. Record usual access separately from supplied content.

Evidence and judgement
Exact source evidence: Provider encounter record and suitability reflection. Keep actual date, mode, provider category only, prepared question, asking mode, actual answer, yes/maybe/no, a feature heard, optional reason, unknowns, access, prompts, first response, one NS and reply. A chosen question voiced by an adult can be aided asking; log it. A question supplied without learner selection is not independent preparation. Preference is never graded. Keep partial evidence; pass unobserved.

Misconceptions and feedback
A provider category is not one named organisation. A training option does not always include a paid job. A promising answer does not guarantee a place. Maybe is a valid reflection; an unknown stays unknown. Practice scripts do not describe actual provision.

Encounter and record boundaries
School arranges the real encounter and checks its required duration, timing and entitlement records separately. This 40-minute lesson and staff role-play do not establish completion of a real provider encounter. If the visit happens elsewhere, use this lesson to prepare and complete the record at reflection. Never claim a visitor attended when absent. Use a separate dated record for each mode; leave future answers pending. No names in this pack or shared records.

Where this leads
This helps me ask about learning or training and decide what I need to find out next.
