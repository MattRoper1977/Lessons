Entry requirements and my gaps
Year A. Spring 1 Week 4, 25–29 January 2027. Tuesday 26 January 2027, P3. One 40-minute formative lesson for up to five learners. Spring 1 runs 4 January–12 February, six weeks; Week 6 assessing. Source Week 5 retains its mixed evidence role. No clock time supplied.

Weekly outcome: Students compare their current strengths/qualifications with a realistic course, training or job requirement.
Exact lesson outcome: I can identify one requirement I already meet and one thing I may need to work on.
Assessment evidence: Requirement-match checklist and one improvement target.
Source: BUILD Careers physical row 19 / Spring 1 row 4. Exact E19 weekly outcome, F19 focus, G19 lesson outcome and J19 evidence retained. H19 sequence and I19 timing retained. Teacher models requirement matching, then learners use an invented job card and a three-state checklist. Source RAG structure is expressed by marks and words: S Shown here, ? Check, N Next step, without colour-only meaning or deficit labels. A short classroom skill task provides possible current evidence without personal records. Support and one improvement target keep routes open. Review is private; K19 websites become prepared cards. Usual aids, reduced choices and model retained; Aspire optional.

40 minutes: 5 arrival/retrieval + 8 I Do + 10 We Do + 12 You Do + 5 review/exit. Slides 7 and 8 share the 23–35-minute task window; do not add their times. Help slide 11 is available throughout; staff slide 12 is hidden. Processing and brief pauses included.

Requirements on the three invented job cards cover attendance, a generic qualification type/level, a skill and an attitude. They are practice details, not real recruitment rules. No employer, provider, exam board, qualification title or place is named. No real vacancy or eligibility claimed. No personal attendance or qualification records requested. Actual requirements and support must be checked before real decisions.

Three states adapt the source RAG checklist: S Shown here means a relevant clue from the task; ? Check means missing information; N Next step means an agreed practice or information focus. Marks and words work without colour. An unknown item may become a next-step target while remaining unknown. A gap is something to check or practise, never a label for a person or a closed route.

Model A food service helper: the invented person asked for help with a word, so attitude is Shown here. They have not tried the three-step plan, so skill is Next step. Attendance and qualification are Check. Target: next lesson, use a word list with a trusted adult to put three order cards in plan order and check all three. Model check A; main hinge B; parallel A. Re-model unknown versus evidence and recheck privately if unclear.

Two task choices: B stockroom, order bag / box / tray; C garden, order fill pot / add seed / water. These are word-order tasks only. Do not do the real job or use tools, food or gardening materials. Keep the plan and usual aid. Correct learner-produced order may show the card's classroom skill; log supplied answers. A given answer is not independent evidence. Attendance and qualification remain Check; attitude is Shown here only if relevant asking for help is observed, otherwise Check. Do not infer records from class participation.

Supported is default: one requirement at a time, two states or next steps, full job card, picture, plan, model, words and frames. Independent keeps usual access. Aspire distinguishes unknown from unable and makes the target more specific. Choose a relevant thing to practise or find out with help, a time and a check. Practice after one success is valid; do not invent a weakness. If a skill is not yet shown, retain partial evidence and arrange another supported check.

Book and reusable cards are default. Pointing, usual aid, reader, typing or exact scribe are valid. Evidence Sheet page 2 Type instead has blank fields for either card, actual task order, four states and clues, shown need, next step and target. Save through usual routine with lesson code/work reference; show adult privately. Scribe exact words, read back and permit correction. Normal access is separate from answer help. Plan in any language. No public questioning, internet, login or phone needed.

Judge evidenced strength and next-step identification separately. Keep actual work, target/help/time/check, access, prompts, first response, NS and reply. Shown here is limited classroom evidence, not proof of real job eligibility. Pass is unobserved; partial evidence retained. E actual banked work; R after Voice, Audience, NS and reply. Add checklist and target to Careers Portfolio; offer to Communication portfolio. Staff confirm any accredited mapping.

Private help: trusted adult or school's help route. Point to HELP, write or ask for a private talk. Confirm an available adult and supported pause/return. If one adult is busy, seek another through the route. No real grades, attendance, family work or finance disclosure. Listen calmly, avoid probing or secrecy promises and follow usual procedures; keep concerns outside public evidence.
