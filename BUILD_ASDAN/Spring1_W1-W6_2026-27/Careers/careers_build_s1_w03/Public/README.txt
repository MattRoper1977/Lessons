Apprenticeships — earn and learn
Year A. Spring 1 Week 3, 18–22 January 2027. Tuesday 19 January 2027, P3. One 40-minute formative lesson for up to five learners. Spring 1 runs 4 January–12 February, six weeks; Week 6 assessing. Source Week 5 retains its mixed evidence role. No clock time supplied.

Weekly outcome: Students understand the basic features of an apprenticeship.
Exact lesson outcome: I can explain that an apprenticeship combines paid work with training and identify one thing I would need to apply.
Assessment evidence: Apprenticeship fact card.
Source: BUILD Careers physical row 18 / Spring 1 row 3. Exact E18 weekly outcome, F18 focus, G18 lesson outcome and J18 evidence retained. H18 sequence and I18 timing retained. Prepared invented profiles replace real vacancies and websites. Learners label pay, training, employer and entry needs, check myths and connect a chosen profile to its sector. Review is private. K18 reduced choices, visual model, sentence starts and usual access retained. Aspire is optional. All example entry and application details are practice information, not universal requirements or real eligibility judgements.

40 minutes: 5 arrival/retrieval + 8 I Do + 10 We Do + 12 You Do + 5 review/exit. Slides 7 and 8 share the 23–35-minute task window; do not add their times. Help slide 11 is available throughout; staff slide 12 is hidden. Processing and brief pauses are within the total.

An apprenticeship combines paid work with training. An employer provides the job; a trainer helps learning. They can work together. All profiles are invented for practice. A bike repair, B woodwork, C food making. No real vacancy, pay rate, entry grade, age rule or current opening is claimed. No named employer, provider, scheme, website or place. The practice entry and application details are not universal rules. Staff check actual requirements and support through the usual advice route before a real application. The lesson is reading, matching and explaining; no tools, food handling or practical trade work.

Model A using pay, training, employer, entry need and To apply fields. Compare A reading a safety note with B measuring a length. Myth/fact keys: Fact, Myth, Myth. Model check A; main hinge B; parallel A. Re-model paid work plus training and recheck privately if unclear. A pass is unobserved.

Two task choices: B woodwork or C food making, with the same fact-card checks. Connect the chosen profile to its sector. Supported is default: one field at a time with two labels or two details; keep full profile, pictures, word bank, frames and model. Independent retains usual access. Aspire compares one need with A and suggests a question before a real application. No learner must state a future plan. A neutral choice for a made-up person is valid.

Reusable cards and books are the default. Pointing, aid, reader, typing or exact scribe are valid. Evidence Sheet page 2 Type instead has blank fields for either profile, a source clue and one next step/reply. Save through the usual routine with lesson code and work reference; show an adult privately. Scribe exact words/selections, read back and permit correction. Record normal access separately from supplied answers. Plan in any language. Pictures, captions and labels carry meaning without colour-only codes. No public questioning, internet, login or phone needed.

Judge the two outcome parts separately: explanation of paid work plus training; one relevant need from Entry need or To apply on the selected profile. Accept equivalent words or aided selections. Matching labels alone is insufficient to show the explanation. Pay amount, employer type or job title is not an application need. Do not infer actual eligibility or practical skill. Keep partial evidence, actual support, first response, one NS and reply. E is actual banked work; R follows Voice, Audience, NS and response. Add fact card to Careers Portfolio and offer to Communication portfolio. Staff confirm any accredited mapping.

Private help: trusted adult or the school's help route. Point to HELP, write a request or ask for a private talk. Confirm available support and pause/return. If one adult is busy, seek another through the route. No personal grades, family work history, finances or future-plan disclosure. Listen calmly, avoid probing or secrecy promises, follow normal procedures and keep concerns outside public evidence.
