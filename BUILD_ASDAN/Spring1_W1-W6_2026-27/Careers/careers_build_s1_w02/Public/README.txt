What can I do after school?
Year A. Spring 1 Week 2, 11–15 January 2027. Tuesday 12 January 2027, P3. One 40-minute formative lesson for up to five learners. Spring 1 runs 4 January–12 February, six weeks; Week 6 assessing. Source Week 5 retains its mixed evidence role. No clock time supplied.

Weekly outcome: Students recognise the main post-16 routes available.
Exact lesson outcome: I can name at least three next-step options: college/sixth form, apprenticeship/training and employment with training/support.

40 minutes: 5 arrival/retrieval + 8 I Do + 10 We Do + 12 You Do + 5 review/exit. Slides 7 and 8 share 23–35 minutes; do not add their times. Help slide 11 is available throughout; staff slide 12 is hidden. Processing and brief pauses are within the total.

Name three option groups: 1 college/sixth form; 2 apprenticeship/training; 3 employment with training/support. These are broad introductory groups; actual routes may overlap. A paid apprenticeship has planned learning. A training course may not include a paid job. FE means further education after school. An independent training provider is a generic type teaching job skills, not a separate fourth group. No named college, provider, employer or place. No real availability, pay amounts, fees, entry grades or eligibility claim. Staff check age, entry and ongoing learning requirements through the usual advice route before a real decision.

Supported is the default. Whole grid or one row at a time with two labels. Both name all three groups and match B and E. Keep icons, exact labels, word bank, frames and A model. Independent keeps usual access. Aspire explains C/D differences and a question for F. Pointing, book work, aid, reader, typing or exact scribe are valid. Pictures and words carry meaning without colour-only coding. No public questioning.

All examples are invented. A college course and B sixth form are group 1, learn. C paid apprenticeship is group 2, both. D training course without a paid job is group 2, learn. E paid job with training/help is group 3, both. F says paid job only, so the information sort is earn; its learning/support details are unknown. Ask what training/help comes with it. F is not a complete route recommendation. Do not infer that no support exists. Main hinge B; parallel A; model check A.

Book and reusable print references are the default. Either route can use Evidence Sheet page 2 Type instead: blank three option fields, B/E matches, clue, optional choice and NS/reply. Use the usual save routine with lesson code and work reference; share privately. Exact scribe reads back and allows correction. Usual access is recorded separately from answer help. Plan in any language. No internet, login or phone needed.

Private choice check is optional: one group to find out more about, not sure, pass or an invented person's choice. No public reason, personal history or family plan required. No choice is marked right or wrong and it is not a final destination decision.

Private help: trusted adult or the school's help route. Point to HELP, write a request or ask for a private talk. Confirm an available adult and supported pause/return. If one adult is busy, use the route to another. Listen calmly, avoid probing or secrecy promises, follow usual procedures and keep concerns outside public work.

Keep actual post-16 options grid. Judge three-group naming separately from example matching/sorting. Do not count college and sixth form as two distinct groups while omitting another. Accept accessible equivalent wording that keeps the distinction and retains training/help with employment. Record actual access, supplied labels, first response, one NS and reply.
