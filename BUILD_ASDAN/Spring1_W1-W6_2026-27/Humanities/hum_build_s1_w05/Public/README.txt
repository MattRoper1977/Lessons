Public edition · BUILD Humanities · Spring 1 Week 5 · Year A
Thursday 4 February 2027, P3, forty minutes, three to four pupils.
Outcome: Ask a question about the past.
Theme: Resilience & Change.
Use Shared_Resources and assets from this bundle. White/Pink sheets are equivalent. Keep word bank, sentence starters and visible model at every task. Exact scribing and usual aids are available. No public questioning, cold calling or required reading aloud. The bag and box stories are invented and reused from Week3. Pupil chooses a stem and subject/detail, builds a question, and asks privately to an adult. Show words, use an aid or direct a scribe if preferred. Record the pupil's choices, actual asking mode, reply and help separately. An unknown answer is valid. Do not invent absent people, places, dates or reasons. Keep the question with a picture clue in a book. Actual responses and help remain blank until teaching. School files stay in store. No publication or external contact performed.
