Thursday 28 January 2027, P3, forty minutes, three to four pupils.
Outcome: Talk about how my local area has changed.
Theme: Resilience & Change.
Use Shared_Resources and assets from this bundle. White/Pink sheets are equivalent. Keep word bank, sentence starter and visible model at every task. Exact scribing and usual aids are available. No public questioning or required reading aloud. The high street, park and bus-stop scenes and their changes are invented. Use one paired example to communicate a then/now change privately. Record invented model or pupil-stated known example; do not treat model material as verified personal local history. No names, addresses, directions or personal disclosure requested. Actual responses and help remain blank until teaching. School files stay in store. No publication or external contact performed.
