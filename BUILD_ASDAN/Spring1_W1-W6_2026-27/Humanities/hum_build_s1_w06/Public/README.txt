Outcome: Record 'then & now' with photos/captions.
Theme: Resilience & Change. Assessing week.

Draw or use the supplied pictures in place of photos. Give each panel one caption that fits. Short selected phrases and confirmed exact scribing are valid. No real people, places or events as subjects, no personal photos or disclosure. Use the six-outcome teacher grid once per pupil, with dated evidence and actual support. Do not infer all six outcomes from one piece. Missing evidence is not failure; use U not seen. Actual responses and judgements remain blank until teaching. School files stay in store.
