BUILD ASDAN — Scheme of Work (Autumn 1)

One connected system across all six ASDAN slots · SEMH alternative provision

Shared blueprint (every lesson — Studio Deck v2): a slide deck with its own illustration: title & settle → warm-up quick-four → a spark statement → ▣ MODEL zone (I do / we do) → a different settle activity each week (W1 wordsearch · W2 match-up · W3 odd-one-out · W4 sequence · W5 sort · W6 team quiz — all click-only, no timers, no scores) → ◆ INDEPENDENT zone (Standard/Supported) → the Lundy loop onto a term-long class artefact (Strengths Wall · Money-Smart Board · Balanced-Plate Gallery · Project Board · Challenge Wall) → Evidence & review (Documentarian photos, annotated visuals, witness-statement shorthand → ASDAN tracker + evidence status). Zones are icon + label + colour (colourblind-safe); Calm Mode, reduced-motion and print built in; no pupil names.

Launch-level evidence engine: every lesson banks portfolio evidence as it happens — a rotating Documentarian photographs the work (Lundy Voice), photos are annotated not written up, and pre-printed witness-statement shorthand lets staff tick the exact ASDAN competency, sign and file. Each lesson names the ASDAN module/challenge it banks.

Connected: every lesson has prev/next nav; each slot has a hub; one master BUILD ASDAN Hub links all six slots. Careers has a taught W6 (post-16 routes); Week 8 is consolidation & portfolio completion.

Slot 1 · Careers

Careers & world of work · strengths to jobs (banks ASDAN Living Independently M8 / AQA UAS)

  • W1 My Strengths — Strengths → work. Lundy Say one strength you would want written about you. Banks: ASDAN LI M8 / AQA UAS.
  • W2 Jobs in My Community — Who does what locally. Lundy Say one local job you would want to know more about. Banks: ASDAN LI M8 / AQA UAS.
  • W3 Skills Employers Want — Skills → examples of mine. Lundy Say the skill you are growing, not the one you have mastered. Banks: ASDAN LI M8 / AQA UAS.
  • W4 Routines & Reliability — Getting there, on time. Lundy Say one thing that would make your morning easier. Banks: ASDAN LI M8 / AQA UAS.
  • W5 Applying Myself — A simple application / profile. Lundy Read one line of your profile out loud — one line, not the page. Banks: ASDAN LI M8 / AQA UAS.
  • W6 What Happens After Year 11 — Four post-16 routes. Lundy Say the route you are leaning towards — leaning is enough, and “not sure yet” goes on the wall like any other answer. Banks: ASDAN LI M8 / AQA UAS.
  • W7 My Career Profile — Poster & portfolio. Lundy Say the one thing you would want a stranger to notice about your poster. Banks: ASDAN LI M8 / AQA UAS.

Slot 3 · Living Independently

ASDAN Living Independently · M1 Earning & Spending Money (Aut1)

  • W1 Where Money Comes From — Income vs spending. Lundy Say one thing you'd like to save up for. Banks: ASDAN LI M1 challenge 1.
  • W2 Notes & Coins — Making amounts. Lundy Show a partner an amount and have them name it. Banks: ASDAN LI M1 challenge 2.
  • W3 Needs vs Wants — Sort and justify. Lundy Decide one 'want' you could wait for — why? Banks: ASDAN LI M1 challenge 3.
  • W4 Everyday Prices — Estimate & compare. Lundy Say one shop where you'd look for value. Banks: ASDAN LI M1 challenge 4 (links Numeracy).
  • W5 A Simple Budget — Plan the money. Lundy Show your budget to a partner and explain one choice. Banks: ASDAN LI M1 (budget challenge).
  • W6 Shopping & Change — A practical money task. Lundy Name one real place you'll practise this — report back. Banks: ASDAN LI M1 (practical) — module complete.
