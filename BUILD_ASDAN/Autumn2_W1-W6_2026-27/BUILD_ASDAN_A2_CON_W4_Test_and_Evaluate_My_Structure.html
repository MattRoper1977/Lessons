SEE4 min

I Do · model one decision

Model aloud: one safe decision, its reason and the check that follows.
Choose one purpose-linked test and predict a checkable result without calling it guaranteed.
Keep the setup, surface, position and teacher-set model load the same for each attempt.
Record what actually happened, then compare it with the brief and prediction.
Do not reveal the pupil’s whole answer. The model demonstrates a process that can transfer.

DO16 min

Independent · act, explain and evidence

16-minute independent mission

DO: Complete one approved test cycle: purpose, prediction, fixed method, actual result, brief comparison and one keep/change decision. Repeat once only if safe and useful, then give the supervising adult a truthful evaluation.

Choose the lightest route that preserves the learning entitlement. Routes can change during the lesson.
◆ Supported

Choose one of two tests; follow picture steps; mark worked/partly/not yet and select one keep/change card.

▲ Standard

Plan and run the approved test, record two observations and explain one evidence-led improvement.

★ Stretch

Compare two trials or models under controlled conditions and justify whether the evidence is sufficient for the claimed improvement.

Equipment / setup
  • completed model
  • original brief/plan
  • test-choice cards
  • flat test mat or fixed gap
  • teacher-approved counters/templates
  • result scale
  • evaluation sheet
  • eye protection if local assessment requires
Safety and privacy

Staff set test limits and stop conditions. No body-weight, throwing, high drop, sharp failure or uncontrolled destructive test; clear the area and handle damaged models only as directed.
