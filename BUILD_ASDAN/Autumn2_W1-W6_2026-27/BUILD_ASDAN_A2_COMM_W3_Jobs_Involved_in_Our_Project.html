SEE4 min

I Do · model one decision

Model aloud: one safe decision, its reason and the check that follows.
Start with an observable project task, such as checking stock, making a component or preparing a message.
Match the task and skill to one or more generic job families, allowing multiple justified answers.
Create a current-information question and identify an approved source or careers adult; do not invent an answer or contact.
Do not reveal the pupil’s whole answer. The model demonstrates a process that can transfer.

DO16 min

Independent · act, explain and evidence

16-minute independent mission

DO: Create a three-card careers map from the live project. Each card shows one project task, transferable skill, plausible generic job family, pupil reason and one current question. Identify an approved source route, but leave the answer blank until genuinely checked.

Choose the lightest route that preserves the learning entitlement. Routes can change during the lesson.
◆ Supported

Match two project-task cards to two job-family cards; select a because clue and choose one prepared question.

▲ Standard

Complete three evidence-led job links and write/say one current-information question.

★ Stretch

Show how one project skill transfers to two job families, identify a stereotype risk and explain what a current source must verify.

Equipment / setup
  • real project task cards
  • transferable-skill cards
  • generic job-family cards
  • job-link template
  • approved careers materials where available
  • question frames
  • communication aid
Safety and privacy

Do not require personal career or family-employment disclosure. No salary, vacancy, eligibility, provider route or outcome is promised; time-sensitive information must come from a current approved source.
