Claims and evidence guardrail

This lesson is a teaching and evidence-generation resource. It does not itself award or certify an ASDAN Personal Effectiveness Qualification, unit, criterion or credit. This is not self-certification. If the centre uses pupil-generated work as potential evidence, an authorised assessor must consider authentic, valid, current and sufficient evidence through the centre’s approved assessment and quality-assurance process. Verify the current module or challenge code, registration, assessment criteria, centre approval and certification status locally.

The HTML stores no pupil response. A photo alone is not evidence of understanding. Adult support is recorded separately from pupil authorship. Evidence status is not a grade. Supported / Standard / Stretch are movable routes, not identities. There is no upload quota or evidence debt.
