Independent · act, explain and evidence

16-minute independent mission

DO: Prepare one contribution with action, evidence, limitation, next step and one feedback question. Deliver it only if the approved audience is genuinely available; otherwise label the work PREPARATION/REHEARSAL, schedule a genuine approved share and make no delivery or impact claim. Record the actual response/status, including not received yet.

Choose the lightest route that preserves the learning entitlement. Routes can change during the lesson.
◆ Supported

Choose one artefact and one true caption; point, play audio or use exact-word scribing; select the feedback question from two options.

▲ Standard

Prepare the four-part message and feedback question; deliver only to a confirmed audience, then record the actual response/status and next step. Otherwise label rehearsal and schedule the genuine share.

★ Stretch

Compare intended and observed impact, explain a limitation and propose an evidence-led continuation.

Equipment / setup
  • truth-checked project evidence panels
  • approved display or presentation materials
  • communication aids/audio if consented
  • feedback question cards
  • response record
  • privacy checklist
Safety and privacy

Use only confirmed approved audiences, images and messages. An unavailable audience means preparation/rehearsal plus a scheduled genuine share—not a delivery or impact claim. No pupil must perform publicly or discuss bullying experience. Do not imply partner endorsement, whole-community impact, Duke sign-off or certification without real authorised evidence.

VOICE → AUDIENCE → INFLUENCE4 min

Exit · receive and act on the response

Voice

VOICE: Share one project fact you are comfortable representing.

Audience

AUDIENCE: State the exact response received, including no response yet.

Next action

NEXT: Choose one audience suggestion to use, question or decline with a reason.

VOICE

The pupil chooses the contribution, communication mode and sharing boundary; opting out of public performance does not remove participation.

AUDIENCE

A named approved school audience is invited to receive the contribution and one question; actual status is recorded as response received, no response yet or audience unavailable/rehearsal.

INFLUENCE

A real received suggestion or pupil review changes the next version/action. If no audience response arrives, the adult reports that status and confirms the scheduled genuine share or closes the route without inventing influence.

Adult close

Name back what was heard or seen. State what will change, continue, be checked locally or be reported back—and when, where the action belongs to an adult.
