1 · D&T without a workshop — the pop-up bench

You don't need a workshop for this build. The whole D&T unit runs from a toolbox + ordinary tables: a bench hook and a clamp turn any sturdy table into a cutting station; sawing and sanding happen outdoors or by an open window; tools live in a sign-in/sign-out tray (count out, count back — it's also behaviour gold); everything packs into one lidded crate at the end. One portable folding workbench is a nice upgrade, not a requirement.

3 · Materials + the pallet safety rule

✔MaterialQtyNotes
☐Reclaimed pallets3–4HT-stamped only — see below

Pallet acceptance check (do this before anything else) — the stamp is one check of several, never sufficient on its own. Accept a pallet only if every line below passes. Reject on any single failure, even when it is HT-stamped.
  • Stamp: HT (heat-treated) present. Never use MB-stamped pallets (methyl bromide, a toxic chemical treatment). No stamp = don't use it.
  • Provenance: you know where it came from and what it carried. Unknown history = reject — the stamp records how it was treated, not what was later spilled on it.
  • Contamination: no chemical, oil or food staining, and no unusual smell.
  • Coatings: no paint, stain or varnish — these cannot be sanded or cut safely by pupils.
  • Condition: no rot, splitting, delamination or crushed blocks.
  • Metal: swept both faces and both edges with a magnet for nails and broken-off shanks.

Dust is a risk-assessed control, not a mask. Wood dust is a hazardous substance. Work through the hierarchy in order, and record the named control for each dusty task:
  1. Prevent — choose the method that makes least dust (hand tools, pre-cut stock, damp wiping rather than dry sweeping).
  2. Extract — on-tool extraction or local exhaust ventilation where the task requires it.
  3. RPE — suitable, correctly selected and fitted, and only after the first two. A generic dust mask is not RPE selection, and moving outdoors is not a control on its own.

Named stop condition: if visible dust is being raised that extraction is not capturing, work stops and an adult is told. Young people need particular risk assessment, instruction and supervision. PENDING-LOCAL-APPROVAL (H&S / COSHH / technician). Review: [date].
