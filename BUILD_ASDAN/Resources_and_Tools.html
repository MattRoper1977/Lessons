Resources & Tools List — BUILD Programme

Made by Matt · Autumn 1 · D&T Community Upcycling + all ASDAN slots + Tutor Time · one shopping-and-setup sheet

1 · D&T without a workshop — the pop-up bench

You don't need a workshop for this build. The whole D&T unit runs from a toolbox + ordinary tables: a bench hook and a clamp turn any sturdy table into a cutting station; sawing and sanding happen outdoors or by an open window; tools live in a sign-in/sign-out tray (count out, count back — it's also behaviour gold); everything packs into one lidded crate at the end. One portable folding workbench is a nice upgrade, not a requirement.

3 · Materials + the pallet safety rule

✔MaterialQtyNotes
☐Reclaimed pallets3–4HT-stamped only — see below
☐Spare timber battens (new)4–6Backup for short cuts — supports "adapt, don't waste"
☐Optional: pre-cut wildlife-box panel kit1–2Supported-tier route to a guaranteed win
Pallet stamp check (do this before anything else): only use pallets stamped HT (heat-treated). Never use MB-stamped pallets (methyl bromide, a toxic chemical treatment) or painted/stained/oily pallets — they cannot be sanded or cut safely by pupils. No stamp = don't use it. This one check is the difference between reclaimed and risky.

5 · ASDAN slot resources (per slot, whole module)

Slot 1 · Careers

Strength picture cards + local-job photo cards · display space for the Strengths Wall (stays up all term) · one-page profile templates + sentence-starters · career-poster paper/pens · tablet for the Evidence Captain.

Slot 3 · Living Independently

Play money (coins + notes) · two labelled tins/hoops (IN / OUT, NEED / WANT) · everyday-item picture cards with price labels · simple till + receipts for the W6 shopping role-play · Money-Smart Board display space.

Slot 4 · FoodWise

Eatwell Guide floor mat or poster · food picture cards + a small set of real foods for sorting (allergy list checked first — every session) · aprons, chopping boards, safety knives · snack ingredients (W5) · hygiene checklist cards · Balanced-Plate Gallery display space.

Slot 5 · Community Project

Visual menu of asset options + vote tokens · site photos of the green space · role badges (incl. Documentarian) · Project Board display (partner-visible) · partner contact details · Trips/Visits paperwork for the install.

Slot 6 · Junior/Young Duke + Enterprise

Challenge cards/booklet + pledge stickers · Challenge Wall display · materials for the chosen enterprise (decided W5) · camera/tablet for challenge evidence.

Tutor Time (BV · BF · KCSIE)

Whiteboard + the decks — that's it. Printed evidence trackers (or the online/spreadsheet versions) · space for the charter line, repair script and Trusted Hand displays.

6 · Whole-programme kit (shared)

One tablet (Documentarian / Evidence Captain photos) · printed witness-statement slips · the evidence trackers (paper / online / spreadsheet) · five display areas for the term-long Lundy artefacts · a photo-printing route for annotated portfolio evidence.
