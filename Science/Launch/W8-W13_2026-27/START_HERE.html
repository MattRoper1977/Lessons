LAUNCH GCSE Biology · six-week lesson pack

18 complete 40-minute Edexcel GCSE Biology 1BI0 Foundation lessons for Weeks 8–13: one Introduce, one Explore and one Do lesson each week.

Enzyme Action: Preparing the pH Investigation

Outcome: Edexcel GCSE Biology 1BI0 Foundation · Paper 1 · Topic 1 · enzyme action, pH and Core Practical 1.10 preparation.

Equipment: Planning sheet, apparatus cards, pH-buffer labels, results-table template and current centre method/risk assessment supplied by the teacher. No reagent handling is required in this Introduce lesson.

Amylase and pH: Continuous-Sampling Investigation

Outcome: Edexcel GCSE Biology 1BI0 Foundation · Paper 1 · Topic 1 · specification point 1.10 investigation of pH and enzyme activity.

Equipment: Only the apparatus and prepared solutions listed in the centre's current Pearson-aligned CP 1.10 method: typically labelled amylase, starch and buffer solutions, iodine spotting tile setup, clean transfer equipment, reaction vessels, timer and authorised temperature-control equipment.

Cell Cycle: Copy, Check, Divide

Equipment: Stage cards, chromosome-copy counters, prepared diagram or micrograph set, sequencing grid and command-word frame.

Week 9 · Explore

Mitosis Sequence: Chromosome Evidence

Outcome: Edexcel GCSE Biology 1BI0 Foundation · Paper 1 · Topic 2 · specification point 2.1 · mitotic sequence and chromosome evidence.

Equipment: Teacher-approved micrographs or prepared slides, microscope if authorised, stage-evidence key, tally grid, calculator and command-word frame.

Week 9 · Do

Mitosis: Identical Daughter Cells

Equipment: Chromosome counters, before/after table, calculator, fictional tissue dataset, application cards and GCSE command-word frame.

Week 10 · Introduce

Growth and Differentiation: Building an Organism

Stem Cells and Meristems: Potential Compared

Equipment: Source-and-outcome cards, supplied fictional research table, calculator, comparison matrix and enlarged branching model.

Week 12 · Introduce

Genetic Information: Cell to Gene

Outcome: Describe DNA, genes and chromosomes.

Equipment: Nested hierarchy cards, chromosome/DNA ribbon model, labels, arrow strip and pencil.

Open W12L1 · Introduce

Week 12 · Explore

DNA Structure: Pair, Twist, Critique

Outcome: Describe DNA as a polymer of nucleotides arranged as two strands coiled into a double helix, with complementary base pairs joined by weak hydrogen bonds.

Equipment: Large nucleotide/base cards, reusable backbone strips, clips or hook-and-loop tabs, sequence sheet and pencil.

Open W12L2 · Explore

Week 12 · Do

Fruit DNA: Extract, Observe, Evaluate

Outcome: Explain how DNA can be extracted from fruit; connect the observation to genetic information.

Equipment: Adult-prepared fruit, salt-detergent solution, sealable bag, filter/mesh, clear tube, cold ethanol controlled by an adult, tray and observation sheet.

Open W12L3 · Do

Week 13 · Introduce

Inheritance Language: Allele to Phenotype

Outcome: Explain monohybrid inheritance using genetic diagrams and Punnett squares.

Equipment: Large P/p allele tiles, genotype cards, phenotype cards, vocabulary mat and pencil.

Week 13 · Explore

Punnett Squares: Construct the Prediction

Outcome: Explain monohybrid inheritance using genetic diagrams and Punnett squares.

Equipment: P/p allele tiles, 2 × 2 grid sheets, calculator, genotype/phenotype key and pencil.

Open W13L2 · Explore

Week 13 · Do

Inheritance Data: Predict, Calculate, Evaluate

Outcome: Calculate and analyse outcomes using probabilities, ratios and percentages from monohybrid crosses.

Equipment: Blank Punnett grids, simulated repeated-batch dataset, calculator, formula strip, graph paper or comparison table and pencil.
