## Shared design

- exact LAUNCH stage labels and 40-minute pacing
- Supported, Standard and Stretch routes
- physical, offline digital and no-equipment parity
- model help and honest model limit
- unmarked prediction, hinge feedback and retry
- genuine adult Audience followed by one visible next teaching move
- no network request, account, upload, analytics or persistent pupil state
- no device media permission; a familiar adult read-aloud route is documented in every lesson
