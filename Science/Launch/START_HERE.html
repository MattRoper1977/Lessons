Five-week sequence

Week 3

Discover: Cells Under the Microscope

Outcome: I can describe how a light microscope magnifies cells, and why resolution matters as well as magnification.

Week 3

Use: Calculating Magnification

Outcome: I can calculate magnification, image size and actual size, and convert between mm and µm.

Open W3L2

Week 3

Master: Magnification Exam Questions

Outcome: I can answer exam-style magnification questions independently, showing full working and the correct units.

Week 4

Discover: Diffusion

Outcome: I can explain diffusion and the factors that affect its rate.

Open W4L1

Week 4

Use: Diffusion in the Lungs — Testing Breath

Outcome: I can apply diffusion to gas exchange in the alveoli, and use the limewater test as evidence that the body makes carbon dioxide.

Open W4L2

Week 4

Master: Explaining Diffusion in the Exam

Outcome: I can answer exam-style diffusion questions independently, matching my answer to the command word.

Open W4L3

Week 5

Discover: Osmosis

Outcome: I can define osmosis and explain how it differs from diffusion.

Open W5L1

Week 5

Use: Osmosis Core Practical

Outcome: I can carry out the osmosis core practical and calculate the percentage change in mass.

Open W5L2

Week 5

Master: Analysing the Osmosis Practical

Outcome: I can analyse osmosis results with a graph and evaluate the method independently.

Open W5L3

Week 6

Discover: Active Transport

Outcome: I can explain active transport and distinguish it from diffusion and osmosis.

Open W6L1

Week 6

Use: Active Transport in Root Hairs and the Gut

Outcome: I can apply active transport to root hair and gut cells, and use data to link it to respiration.

Open W6L2

Week 6

Master: Comparing the Three Processes

Outcome: I can answer 'compare' exam questions across the three transport processes independently.
