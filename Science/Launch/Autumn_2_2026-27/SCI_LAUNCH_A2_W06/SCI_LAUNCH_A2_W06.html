Verbatim SoW objective: Research a genetic condition online; present.

Arrival · 5 min

Look at this week’s NHS source card. Point to one detail you notice.

Offline source card

NHS card: Cystic fibrosis is inherited. It can affect breathing and digestion. Changed CFTR copies are inherited from both parents; one changed copy usually makes a carrier. Treatments can ease symptoms. The genetic cause is inherited; infection precautions are separate. This card predicts nothing about a person.

Source: NHS, Cystic fibrosis; NHS Genomics Education Programme, Cystic fibrosis. Checked 24 September 2026.

Offer a private, predictable start. Do not require previous knowledge.

I do · 5 min

Watch the full model. Point to the step that answers today’s question.

Full model: The NHS card says cystic fibrosis is inherited. Changed CFTR copies come from both parents. One changed copy usually means a carrier; two usually cause the condition. Name the NHS card with each claim.

Show me: Show me: is the genetic cause of cystic fibrosis inherited or passed by brief contact?

Answer: Inherited. This says nothing about infection precautions a person with CF may need.

Model one complete example; check every pupil’s response to the show-me question.

We do · 7 min

Complete the missing step in the part-done model. Speak, then match the cards.

One-minute reset: use the agreed move, then return to one step.

Part-done model: The NHS card says cystic fibrosis is ___. It involves the ___ gene. One changed copy usually means a ___. Source: ___.

Speak first: The card names …; this sentence supports …; this line is about …

F · support: Point to the card’s condition, inheritance and care lines before speaking.

M · explain: Explain which card sentence supports inheritance and which sentence describes care.

H · stretch: Use the card to reject the claim that every carrier has symptoms.

Match each short statement to a source-card section.

Ready to match.

Wait five seconds, gesture, then cue if needed. Support the route without supplying the missing answer.

Choose one answer

Independent · 8 min

Present three sourced facts from the offline NHS card, then correct an unsupported claim.

One-minute reset: use the agreed move, then return to one step.

Talk before write: My source is …; it supports …; it does not tell us …

You can plan in any language.

F: State the condition and one fact with the NHS source named. The card says …; the source is …

M: Explain CFTR inheritance and separate it from infection. The CFTR change is inherited because …; infection advice is separate because …

H: Revise an unsupported claim with the NHS card. The claim is not supported by …; I would revise it to …

Go deeper: Why does one changed copy in this example not justify saying a carrier has the condition?

Early-finisher stretch — Source claim audit: Repair a draft saying a CFTR change spreads by touch and outcomes are fixed; source both repairs.

Show it your way: point, sign, speak, draw, type, write, use a scribe or a staff-captured photo.
