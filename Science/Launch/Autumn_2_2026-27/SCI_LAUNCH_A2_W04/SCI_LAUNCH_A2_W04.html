I do · 5 min

Watch the full model. Point to the step that answers today’s question.

Words

WordMeaningExample
DNAthe molecule that stores genetic instructionsDNA contains the instructions for making proteins.
genea section of DNA that codes for a specific proteinThe coloured section of the model stands for one gene.
chromosomea long DNA molecule packaged with proteins in a cellA chromosome contains many DNA sections, including genes.
genomethe entire DNA of an organismThe genome includes all of the organism’s chromosomes and other DNA.

Full model: Mark a short section of a DNA cord as a gene. Coil the cord as one chromosome; place it inside a border for the genome. Name the section, molecule, package and complete DNA set.

Show me: Show me: is a gene a section of DNA, or is all DNA inside one gene?

Answer: A gene is a section of DNA.

Model one complete example; check every pupil’s response to the show-me question.

We do · 7 min

Complete the missing step in the part-done model. Speak, then match the cards.

One-minute reset: use the agreed move, then return to one step.

Part-done model: DNA cord → a short marked ________ codes for a protein → chromosome package → the genome contains all DNA.

Speak first: Speaking: “A gene is part of …; a chromosome contains …”

F · support: Use the full cord model; place the gene label on the short marked section.

M · explain: Explain how the short DNA section relates to one gene and the larger package.

H · stretch: Explain why “one chromosome = one gene” misrepresents scale and role.

Select where each label belongs in the string model and explain one mismatch.

One-minute reset: use the agreed move, then return to one step.

Talk before write: Speaking: “Move this label because …”

Go deeper: What distinctions disappear if the whole genome is called one gene?
