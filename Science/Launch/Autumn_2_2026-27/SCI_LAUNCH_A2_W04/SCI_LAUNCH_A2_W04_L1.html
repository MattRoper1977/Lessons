Bench · coil 2 m without cutting

The cord never breaks. The gene never leaves. It just gets hidden.

Kit (per group of 2–3): 2 m of wool or string, a small pot with a lid, one strip of coloured tape, a ruler.

  1. Wrap the tape round one short stretch of the wool: that is your gene.
  2. Coil all 2 m into the pot without cutting it. Close the lid.
  3. Can you see the gene now? Open, pull the wool out, and find the tape.
  4. Scale check: real DNA packs about 2 m into a nucleus of about 6 µm. Measure your pot. How much too big is the model?
