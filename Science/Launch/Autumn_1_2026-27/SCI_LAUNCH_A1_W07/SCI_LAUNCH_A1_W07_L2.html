Stage 3 · Starter

Starter · 4-beat model and retrieval

Whiteboard 4-beat sequence

Beat 1: Beat 1 · Circle the command word FIRST: it tells you the shape of the answer the mark scheme will reward.
Beat 2: Beat 2 · DESCRIBE = What happens / data pattern (no "because" needed); EXPLAIN = Why it happens ("because..." + biological mechanism).
Beat 3: Beat 3 · CALCULATE = Unit check + working + number; COMPARE = Similarities ("Both...") + Differences ("...whereas...").
Beat 4: Beat 4 · Glitch-buster: "A true Biology fact earns marks whatever the command word says" is FALSE — a Describe fact scores 0 explanation marks on an Explain question!

Ready: step 0 of 4

Class fingers or thumbs vote

An exam question asks: "Explain why the potato cylinder gained mass in pure water. [3 marks]" A student writes: "The potato cylinder went up from 2.0 g to 2.2 g." Why does this true statement score 0 marks?

Hold up 1, 2, 3 or 4 fingers (or point), then tap to check.

Retrieval 1: Why is confusing "Describe" and "Explain" the single most common reason students drop marks on GCSE Biology graphs?

Clue

Describe vs Explain trap.

Answer: On a "Describe the graph" question, students waste time explaining why instead of quoting the trend and numbers; on an "Explain the graph" question, they just state that the line goes up without saying why ("because...").

Retrieval 2: What is the difference between a 1-mark "State" question and a 3-mark "Explain" question?

Clue

Match length to tariff + command.

Answer: A 1-mark "State" question wants a single short fact or name; a 3-mark "Explain" question wants a linked 3-step causal chain using "because" and "so".

Retrieval 3: How do you spot a command-word mismatch when checking your own work?

Clue

Self-check signature words.

Answer: Read your answer back and check for the signature words: numbers/trend for Describe, "because/so" for Explain, working + unit for Calculate, and "Both... whereas..." for Compare.

Step 1: Context: A potato cylinder starts at 2.00 g and finishes at 2.22 g after soaking in pure water (0.0 mol/dm³ sucrose).

  1. Step 1: Context: A potato cylinder starts at 2.00 g and finishes at 2.22 g after soaking in pure water (0.0 mol/dm³ sucrose).
  2. Step 2: Shape 1 — DESCRIBE what happened to the potato cylinder [1 mark]: "The potato cylinder gained 0.22 g in mass (increased from 2.00 g to 2.22 g)." (What only — no reason needed).
  3. Step 3: Shape 2 — EXPLAIN why the potato cylinder gained mass [3 marks]: "It gained mass because pure water is more dilute (higher water concentration) than the potato cells (1), so water moved into the cells by osmosis (1) across the partially permeable cell membrane (1)."
  4. Step 4: Shape 3 — CALCULATE % change [2 marks]: "(2.22 − 2.00) ÷ 2.00 × 100 = +11.0%." · Shape 4 — COMPARE pure water vs 0.8 mol/dm³ sucrose [2 marks]: "Both changed mass by osmosis, whereas the cylinder in pure water gained mass (+11.0%) while the cylinder in 0.8 mol/dm³ lost mass."

Worked example 1

Worked example 1 (Explain WAGOLL): "Explain why the potato cylinder gains mass in pure water. [3 marks]" -> The water concentration is higher outside the cells (in pure water) than inside the potato cytoplasm (1), so there is a net movement of water into the cells by osmosis (1) through the partially permeable cell membranes (1).

Stage 7 · We do 2

We do 2 · method order and data table

Order the scientific method or reasoning steps

  1. Choose the matching answer shape (What/trend for Describe; "because..." cause-and-effect for Explain; working + number for Calculate; "Both... whereas..." for Compare).
  2. Read the question stem and circle the command word FIRST (State, Describe, Explain, Calculate, or Compare).
  3. Audit each draft answer against the 3 bins: ● MATCHES command word, ■ DOES NOT MATCH (wrong shape), or ▲ OVER-ANSWERED.
  4. Rewrite any mismatched answer by adding the missing causal link ("because...") or the missing second side ("whereas...").

Use move up and Move down to place the steps in order, then check.

STATE / NAME [1]One short fact, name, or term1–5 words; no long paragraph"Mitochondria." / "Osmosis."
DESCRIBE [2]Say WHAT happens or quote the data pattern"As X rises, Y rises from... to..." (no why)"As sucrose rises from 0.0 to 1.0 mol/dm³, mass change falls from +11.2% to −17.1%."
EXPLAIN [2–3]Say WHY or HOW (link cause to effect)"...because... so..." + key Biology terms"It gains mass because water enters by osmosis down its gradient across the membrane."
