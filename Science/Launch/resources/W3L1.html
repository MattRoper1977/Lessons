We Do · Lens detective

Choose the first safe step. Then predict what changes at higher power.

  1. Start at ×4 objective.
  2. Find and centre the specimen.
  3. Focus, then increase power.

Discuss, point, write or dictate your first reason. Use one example first; extend only if there is time.

Reveal shared reasoning after an attempt

With a ×10 eyepiece: ×4 gives ×40; ×40 gives ×400. The field of view shrinks. A larger image is not automatically clearer.

This page keeps your writing while it stays open. It does not save or send it.

Model explanation and diagram

Magnification is how many times larger an image is. Resolution is whether close points can be seen separately. Enlarging a blur does not recover detail.

Watch for: Do not add the lens powers. At high power use fine focus only; follow the microscope’s approved procedure.

Give a smaller support cue

Total magnification = eyepiece × objective. A bigger image is not always a clearer image. Describe what you can see before you explain it.
