Teacher implementation guide

A concise teaching and access guide for all twelve GROW Science lessons.

Before teaching

  1. Open the lesson locally and check the title slide, equipment and safety notes.
  2. Prepare the physical route and its offline digital/no-equipment equivalent.
  3. Identify the familiar adult who can genuinely receive pupil Voice at the exit.
  4. Decide whether a selected print tier is needed; printing is always an explicit action.

Read-aloud and accessibility

Device speech is intentionally omitted: no media permission is requested, and the deck remains dependable offline. A familiar adult may read the exact current task while the text stays visible. Word help adds a plain-language bridge while preserving the scientific term. Calm view stops automatic local-film motion; Teacher Freeze pauses lesson motion. Large text increases the interface text.

Truthful Lundy closure

Space is offered by the equivalent participation routes. Voice changes state only after a pupil commits a response. Audience changes only after a familiar adult genuinely receives it. Influence changes only after an adult selects and enacts one next teaching move. Reload begins a fresh session; no static completion claim survives.

Lesson list

  • W8A · Day and Night: Sky Shift — I can explain that Earth's rotation causes day and night and makes the Sun appear to move across our sky.
  • W8B · Day and Night: 24-Hour Control Room — I can use a rotating-Earth model to collect and explain evidence across one complete day-night cycle.
  • W9A · Spherical Bodies: Shape Evidence — I can use observations from different viewpoints to describe the Sun, Earth and Moon as approximately spherical bodies.
  • W9B · Spherical Bodies: World Shape Lab — I can collect fair comparison observations and combine them with real space evidence to describe the Sun, Earth and Moon as approximately spherical.
  • W10A · Solar System Research: Evidence Desk — I can research one Solar System focus using suitable sources, accurate notes and a clear source record.
  • W10B · Solar System Presentation: Mission Briefing — I can present researched Solar System findings clearly, credit sources and respond to an audience question with a change or a clear reason plus one transfer step.
  • W11A · Climate Chain: From Gas to Warming — I can explain how extra greenhouse gases raise Earth's average temperature and connect warming to wider climate effects.
  • W11B · Climate Action: Plan, Measure, Improve — I can explain how an action reduces climate impact, distinguish reducing causes from coping with effects, and plan evidence to check whether it worked.
  • W12A · Science Connections: Evidence Control — I can connect a scientific question to the right idea and use evidence to explain my answer.
  • W12B · Answer Lab: Say It, Support It, Improve It — I can give a scientific answer with evidence and reasoning, then improve it after audience response.
  • W13A · Rover Rescue: Plan a Fair Test — I can plan a fair investigation of how surface type affects a rover's travel distance.
  • W13B · Rover Evidence Lab — I can carry out a comparative fair test, use repeated distance results for a conclusion and improve the method.
