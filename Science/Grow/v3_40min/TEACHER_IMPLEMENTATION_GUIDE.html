Alternative 40-minute route for GROW Science Aut 1 W3–W7. The existing route remains available in the Lessons catalogue.

Teacher implementation guide

How to run the v3 lessons as a coherent GROW Science teaching system.

The non-negotiable 40-minute grammar

Explore: 4 Arrival + 4 Starter + 5 I Do + 5 We Do + 4 I Do 2 + 4 We Do 2 + 10 Independent + 4 Exit = 40 minutes.

Do: 3 Arrival + 3 Starter + 4 I Do + 4 We Do + 3 I Do 2 + 4 We Do 2 + 15 Independent + 4 Exit = 40 minutes.

What makes v3 distinctive

Visual hooks are local and teacher-controlled; official Oak media is optional. Every graphical or interactive element serves a prediction, misconception, model, decision or explanation. Hands-on work is used whenever it makes the invisible Science visible or creates better evidence. Support routes change modality and scaffolding without lowering the learning goal.

Feedback as participation

Run SPACE → VOICE → AUDIENCE → INFLUENCE repeatedly. A pupil answer is not the end of a check: it changes the next question, scaffold, explanation or task. Use the staff Live Loop button to decide the learning move; nothing is stored.

Evidence rule

Prefer a three-part trace: pupil decision/prediction → observable action/product/result → pupil explanation/evaluation/next step. A photo by itself is not enough. The separate end-of-day window supports lean same-day evidence handling where the school policy requires it.

Pupils joining mid-year: every arrival slide carries a read-it-here route.

Autumn 1 W1–W2 were baseline weeks. No science was taught in them, so the W3 lesson opens from what pupils already know rather than from a previous lesson. The baseline itself is delivered separately and is not in this repository.
