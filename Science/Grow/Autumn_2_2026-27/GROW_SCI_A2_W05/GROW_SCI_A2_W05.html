Verbatim SoW objective: Consolidate forces, space and climate; answer scientific questions.

Arrival · 5 min

Match each question to a kit card: toy and surface, globe and torch, or warming chain.

Offer a predictable seat or standing spot and the first visual cue. No public test.

Starter · 3 min

Think back. Say, point or write one answer for each question.

  1. Give one link in the warming chain.
  2. Name one fact our Solar System cards support.
  3. How can friction affect a moving toy?

Today I will check:

  • I can match a question to a model.
  • I can give a claim with a matching clue.
  • I can say one thing the model cannot show.

Ask three low-stakes retrieval questions; prior coverage must be checked.

I do · 5 min

I choose a toy for a motion question. The toy went less far on rough paper in this run. I must repeat before I claim why.

Words: force · rotation · evidence · climate

WordIt meansExample
forcea push or pull on an objectFriction is a force that can slow a toy.
rotationa turn around an axisEarth’s rotation brings day and night.
evidencean observation or result that supports a claimThe shorter run is evidence for my answer.
climateweather patterns over many yearsA hot day alone does not show a climate trend.

Watch: Toy motion question → choose toy and surface → measure distance → test friction as one cause.

Show me: If my question is about day and night, should I point to the toy or torch-and-ball?

Model the full reasoning step, pre-teach four words and ask the show-me question.
