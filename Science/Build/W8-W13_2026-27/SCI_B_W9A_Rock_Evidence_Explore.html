  • I can state and apply one grouping rule.

WHAT THIS MODEL HELPS US SEE

Coded macro views slow down naming and make the observation-to-rule sequence visible.

WHAT THIS MODEL DOES NOT SHOW

Appearance alone cannot prove an unknown rock's identity or hidden properties. Lighting, scale, weathering and natural variation can change how a specimen looks.

Step 1

Keep the code and hide the name. Place one specimen on a white tray and observe it from more than one angle.

Step 2

Select two or three useful features: grains, crystals, bands, sheet-like planes, holes, colour or surface appearance.

Step 3

Record only what is visible. ‘It is granite’ is a name claim; ‘several crystals are visible’ is an observation.

Word help keeps the scientific term visible and adds a plain-language bridge.

Apply the same meaning to every specimen. Another clear rule may create a different valid grouping.
