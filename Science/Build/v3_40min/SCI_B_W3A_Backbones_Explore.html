Backbones and No Backbones

🎯 Learning objective: I can sort animals into those with a backbone and those without.
✅ Success criteria
  • Find the backbone on a skeleton diagram.
  • Name examples with and without a backbone.
  • Explain one way an invertebrate can be supported without an internal backbone.
