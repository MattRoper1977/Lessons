Verbatim SoW objective: Test rocks for hardness and whether they let water through.

Arrival · 5 min

Point to a safe rock.

Offer a safe kit choice and short first–then cue.

Ask three low-stakes prior questions; answers in note.

Try it with kit

Guess first: Will drops pass through?

Kit: Two safe rocks · Adult-held blunt tool · Dropper and tray

  1. Guess first.
  2. Adult tests a mark on each rock.
  3. Add three drops to A. Check the edge and below.
  4. Do the same on B. Record the route seen.

Risk: Tools or rough rocks may hurt skin; spills may be slippery.

Safe: Let staff use the tool. Keep drops in the tray. Do not taste; wash hands after.

Check against the school risk assessment and CLEAPSS guidance. Adult handles tool, inspects edges and controls water; no dust or tasting.

Hand over the missing step; offer F or H route. Reset once.
