## Lesson architecture
Every A/B lesson is exactly 40 teaching minutes:
Arrival → Starter → I Do → We Do → I Do 2 → We Do 2 → Independent evidence → Exit.

## BUILD Lundy implementation
SPACE: predictable low-load entry, co-regulation, familiar adult, safe modality.
VOICE: point, sign, verbal response, demonstration, re-attempt, edit or authorised short clip.
AUDIENCE: adult genuinely receives the response.
INFLUENCE: next attempt/instruction/regulation/scaffold changes because of the response.

BUILD closure is genuine adult Audience. This test bundle does not impose the GROW-style pupil-written closure line.

## Codes and capture
VF / WS / I / NS+ / E / R / // / ? appear only in the staff-side policy reference. The lesson does not automatically add codes, store responses or upload evidence. If local practice uses R, it follows genuine Audience.

## Body and food safeguarding
Body and food concepts stay on models/diagrams/fictional examples. No pupil body, weight, personal diet, calorie, restriction, or good/bad-food framing is used.

## Media
Each lesson contains a local visual hook and works offline. Oak resources are optional click-to-open teacher media.
