## Scope
This test bundle upgrades the current repository’s W3–W7 LAUNCH estate: 15 files, three per week.

## Weekly rhythm
1. Introduce
2. Explore
3. Do / independent evidence

Each lesson is exactly 40 teaching minutes and uses the company grammar:
Arrival → Starter → I Do → We Do → I Do 2 → We Do 2 → Independent → Exit.

## LAUNCH scientific observation rule
Scientific labels/conclusions do not lead the experience. Learners observe, predict, measure, test, calculate or reason from evidence before the explanation/reveal.

## Feedback / Lundy
SPACE: stable seating/adult, clear deadlines, no public grading, processing time and access arrangements.
VOICE: in-lesson edit, structured verbal response, calculation, graph/data reasoning, practical demonstration or re-attempt.
AUDIENCE: adult genuinely reads/listens/watches and engages with the scientific response.
INFLUENCE: next instruction, question, scaffold, retrieval plan or later mock preparation changes.

The learner response remains on the existing work. No adult signature/countersignature is introduced. If local practice uses R, it follows genuine Audience and is never generated by these files.

## Evidence
Learner decision/prediction/set-up → observable scientific work → learner explanation/evaluation/edit.
Core lesson files store no pupil data and upload nothing.

## Autumn 1 W1–W2 are baseline weeks

No science was taught in W1 or W2; they are baseline assessment weeks. This pack originally
described a taught W2 (microscope core practical) and retrieved it at W3L1. That claim has been
removed from the lesson, the hub and the manifest, and the W3L1 arrival now opens from what pupils
already know.

**Nothing here states what the baseline covered.** The baseline runs on PythonAnywhere and is not
in this repository, so no lesson cites it as prior learning and no baseline topic is named. A
separate public `Baseline_Weeks/` pack exists in this repo; it is a *different instrument* from the
PythonAnywhere baseline and is not what any given pupil sat.
