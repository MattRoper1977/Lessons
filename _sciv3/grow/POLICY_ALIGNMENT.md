## What v3 implements

The 40-minute teaching grammar is unchanged: Arrival → Starter → I Do → We Do → I Do 2 → We Do 2 → Independent evidence → Exit. Lundy is threaded through the live checks rather than bolted on at the end. Visual hooks, simulations and practical tasks always terminate in pupil thinking that an adult can genuinely receive.

The uploaded feedback policy includes organisation-wide codes (VF, WS, I, NS+, E, R, //, ?) and lean Earwig capture. The current repository Science guidance contains stricter controls around additional marks/records. This test bundle therefore **does not automatically write codes, store responses, create an upload queue or upload evidence**. It exposes the policy codes staff-side as a reference only; local operational instructions decide how they are applied.

## Media rule

External media is optional and teacher-clicked. Every lesson has a local visual hook and works without network access.
