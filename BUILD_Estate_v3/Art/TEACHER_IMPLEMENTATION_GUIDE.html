BUILD Art · Teesside Studio Suite v3

Teacher implementation guide

The company grammar

Every 40-minute lesson uses: Arrival → Starter → I Do → We Do → I Do 2 → We Do 2 → Independent studio evidence → Exit/Influence. The screen frames the decision; the real art-making remains the centre of the lesson.

Art is not a quiz subject here

The v3 interactions are studio probes, models and decision tools. They do not award XP or public scores. A correct click never substitutes for an observed mark, material test, brief, critique, artwork, curation decision or reflection.

Support staff

Least prompt first: wait → self-prompt → gesture/point → visual cue → general verbal → specific verbal → model → direct/physical support only where appropriate to the pupil plan and local practice. Then fade support. Record adult support separately from pupil authorship.

Lundy + live feedback

SPACE (steel) establishes conditions. VOICE (oxide) is the pupil’s choice, mark, demonstration, word or re-attempt. AUDIENCE (river) means an adult genuinely receives it. INFLUENCE (foundry) changes the next try, support, material, example, display or studio plan.

Evidence

Use the triad: pupil decision → observable studio work/action → pupil explanation/review. A photo alone usually shows that something existed, not what the pupil understood or chose.

Consent and privacy

  • Personal disclosure/place knowledge is optional.
  • Public display is optional; W7 makes privacy a curatorial decision.
  • Do not capture faces or identifiable work publicly without the required consent.
  • Safeguarding disclosures leave the evidence workflow.

Qualification claims

The suite is written to prepare/support the Arts Award Explore evidence journey described by the SOW. It does not award or certify anything itself. Adviser/centre audit remains essential.
