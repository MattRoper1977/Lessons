BUILD ASDAN · WEEK 8

Portfolio Consolidation & Evidence Studio

40 minutes · faithful to the current SOW: consolidation/portfolio completion, not invented new module content.

5 min

Arrival · evidence triage

Choose one slot. Put the real task/product beside the evidence requirement. Mark: evidence present · needs learner meaning · needs adult support record · genuinely missing.

5 min

I Do · model an honest evidence check

Model the difference between “a photo exists” and “this shows the learner’s decision/action plus their explanation”. Do not backfill a challenge by inventing a quote, staged photograph or adult-written pupil response.

5 min

We Do · Audience check

Adult genuinely receives one learner response and names what was heard/seen before any R/tag or evidence-status change.

20 min

Independent · strengthen what is genuinely strengthenable

  • Annotate an existing genuine photo/product with the learner’s meaning.
  • Complete an outstanding reflection/re-attempt that the learner can authentically do now.
  • Add adult support/witness information only where the current programme/centre process requires it.
  • Leave genuinely missing practical experience as missing; schedule it rather than simulate it.
5 min

Exit · Influence

Choose one real next action: portfolio ready for local adviser/assessor check · specific evidence still needed · registration/code/approval to verify · next-term action. Tell the learner what will happen and when where the action sits with an adult.

Claims guardrail

The SOW itself says to confirm exact ASDAN module/challenge codes, registrations and approvals before teaching/evidence claims. This studio does not self-certify a module, UAS or award.
