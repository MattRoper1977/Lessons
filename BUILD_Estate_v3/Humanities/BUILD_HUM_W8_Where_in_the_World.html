Source gate

Every genuine source: what · who · when · where from · rights/use. An unattributed item is not ready. Any adapted/constructed item must be explicitly distinguished from an untouched historical source under the centre’s approved wording.

Accessible source pack

Enlarge, chunk, transcribe/audio or provide another colour-safe/tactile route as needed. If format blocks the pupil, do not misread that as a historical misconception.

Private material

A pupil’s family photo/letter/document remains private material on loan: do not turn it into a classroom source, retain or display it without consent.
