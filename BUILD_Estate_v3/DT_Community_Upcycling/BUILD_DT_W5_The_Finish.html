STATUS

Prompt card — not a risk assessment. Approved risk assessment: [local ref]. Local owner: [name/role]. Review before live use: [date].

Current safety gate

No safety/first-aid wording is treated as sufficient unless it has named source, checked date, review date, owner and local approval where required.

Wood dust

HSE guidance checked 10 Aug 2026: control exposure at source/extraction; suitable RPE may be additional where required. Do not use an open window/door or a generic dust mask as the control plan. Local COSHH/RA decides the actual controls. Review before/at 1 Sep 2026.

Reclaimed timber

The repo safety gate says an HT stamp is only one check and is not sufficient by itself. Material acceptance is staff/technician/local-approval territory, not a pupil decision.

Missing sharp/tool

Stop work and tell staff; staff manage tool accounting and movement under the centre procedure. Do not use collective detention as a safety control.
