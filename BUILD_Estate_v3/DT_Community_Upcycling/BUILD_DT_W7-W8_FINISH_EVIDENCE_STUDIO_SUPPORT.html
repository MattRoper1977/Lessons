Weeks 7–8 · Finish, strengthen evidence & hand forward

Support page based on the repository Weekly Plan. This is deliberately not two invented new curriculum lessons.

What the plan authorises

Finish/overflow, strengthen evidence, complete the ASDAN Vocational/D&T portfolio work and agree the first Autumn-2 construction step.

Suggested 40-minute studio grammar

Arrival evidence check → product/brief check → teacher model of one evidence gap → pupil finish/evidence work → adult receives learner explanation → one next construction step.

Evidence priority

Prefer a real design/making decision plus product/process evidence plus pupil explanation. Do not create staged photographs simply to fill a portfolio.

Safety

Prompt card — not a risk assessment. Any practical finish/rework uses the existing approved local RA/COSHH/tool controls. Do not turn “overflow week” into permission for unplanned processes.

Qualification guardrail

The current weekly plan explicitly describes evidence status rather than a grade for the Short Course. Verify current ASDAN requirements/registration before any portfolio claim.
