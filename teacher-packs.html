Teacher packs

Every lesson on the site stays free to view and print in class. The packs are for teachers who want the whole week ready to go, offline, in one download.

What a week bundle contains

Fifteen files, all for the same week, all in one download:

  • The lesson page. The same single file you can open on the site, saved for offline use.
  • The slides. Ready to show, in the order the lesson runs.
  • The pupil resources booklet. Everything a pupil is handed during the week, in one print run.
  • Three printed tier packs. Supported, Independent and Aspire, each with a Choice extension inside for pupils who finish Aspire.
  • A TA brief. One page on who to sit with, what to say and when to step back.
  • A teacher and cover plan, twice. The lesson minute by minute, written so a cover teacher can run it cold: once as an editable document and once as a PDF.
  • A knowledge organiser. The key words, facts and ideas for the week on one page.
  • A staff answer sheet. Model answers for every tier.
  • An editable pack. The printed packs in a form you can change for your own class.
  • A word page. The week's vocabulary with plain definitions.
  • A source record. Where every fact, figure and source in the week came from.
  • A checksum list. So you can confirm every file arrived whole and unchanged.

Every bundle has passed the same checks: a 40-minute twelve-stage lesson, feedback-loop codes on every route, a pupil-voice question, 7:1 contrast throughout and tagged PDFs.
