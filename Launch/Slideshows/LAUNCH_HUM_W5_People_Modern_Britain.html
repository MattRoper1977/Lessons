I Do

I Do: 'How Far' Applied to People

Key Idea: The stem 'how far did individuals shape modern Britain' demands a weighed answer: agency evidenced, structures acknowledged, verdict in degrees.

📖 The Weighing Frame

Agency side — Choices and changes, evidenced per person.

Structure side — Wars, policies, economies that opened the doors.

Degree verdict — 'Substantially, within limits set by…' — never all-or-nothing.

🧠 Watch me think

Weigh the nurse: agency — she chose to come, stayed through hostility, trained others; ward records and testimony evidence it. Structures — the NHS Act created the posts; recruitment campaigns opened the route. Verdict in degrees: individuals like her substantially shaped the NHS's reality — within frames national policy built. Both sides present, degree declared.

I Do

I Do: The Representative-Person Problem

Key Idea: One life can illustrate a pattern but never prove it alone — LAUNCH answers pair the person with the pattern's numbers.

👀 Look: one life illustrates. Numbers prove. The answer needs the join between them.

📖 Person + Pattern

The life — Vivid, evidenced, singular.

The pattern — Recruitment figures, union rolls, employment data.

The join — 'One of thousands: the records show…'

🧠 Watch me think

My nurse is one woman. The claim is about 'individuals' plural. So I join her to the pattern: NHS recruitment brought tens of thousands from the Caribbean and beyond; staffing returns show wards that ran on their work. Her story gives the pattern a face; the pattern gives her story its scale. Neither alone answers the stem — together they do.

The weighing frame

SideWhat goes here
AgencyChoices and changes, evidenced per person — what they did.
StructureWars, policies, economies that opened the door — what made it possible.
The join“One of thousands: the records show…” — person tied to pattern.
Degree verdict“Substantially, within limits set by…” — never all-or-nothing.

Degree words

barely · partly · substantially — pick one, then add the limit clause. An answer with no limit clause has not weighed anything.

Watch for the trap. The stem says individuals, plural. A single vivid life answers a different question unless you join it to the pattern’s numbers.
