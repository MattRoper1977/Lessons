I Do

I Do: Interrogating an Unfamiliar Extract

Key Idea: Unfamiliar maps yield to method: orient, key-check, grid the essentials, read relief — THEN touch the problem.

👀 Look: contours are not decoration. Read the relief first, and the route argues itself.

📖 First-Contact Method

Orient — North, scale, the settlement pattern's shape.

Grid the essentials — Six-figure the problem's fixed points.

Relief read — Contours before commitments — the land votes first.

🧠 Watch me think

Watch first contact, no shortcuts: north confirmed; 1:25 000 so 4 cm = 1 km; settlements string the valley floor. Cycle route endpoints gridded: 645 210 to 668 224. Now relief: contours crowd the direct line — a 90-metre climb. The pretty straight route just lost to the valley path. The land voted; I listened; NOW I plan.

I Do

I Do: Map Evidence in an Argued Answer

Key Idea: The route recommendation is an essay in miniature: a thesis, map-cited proofs, a counter-route steel-manned, a verdict. It is LAUNCH's whole term on one sheet.

📖 The Miniature Essay

Thesis — 'The valley route serves best.'

Map-cited proofs — Grid refs, measured distances, contour counts.

Counter-route + verdict — The direct line's best case — beaten on the evidence.

🧠 Watch me think

Full circle, watch: 'The valley route (645 210 → via 652 216 → 668 224) runs 4.2 km on ground never exceeding a 10 m climb, passing the school at 655 218. The direct line saves 1.1 km — its best case — but buys a 90-metre ascent crossing the A-road unbridged at 660 219. On safety and gradient, the valley route wins.' Thesis, cited proofs, counter turned, verdict landed. That's the term, in coordinates.

📘 Reference Zone — keep this beside your work

First-contact method — before you touch the problem

StepWhat you do
1. OrientNorth confirmed. Scale read. The settlement pattern’s shape noted.
2. Key-checkRead the key for anything unfamiliar before you need it.
3. Grid the essentialsSix-figure the problem’s fixed points and write them down.
4. Read reliefContours. Crowded lines mean climb. The land votes here.

Scale — 1:25 000

4 cm on the map = 1 km on the ground. Measure with the edge of a page, mark it, then lay it on the scale bar.

The recommendation is an essay in miniature

Thesis — the route named. Proofs ×3 — a grid reference or a measurement on every one. Counter-route — its best case, stated fairly. Verdict — the deciding factor, in degree words.

Cite like this: “the valley route (645 210 → 652 216 → 668 224) runs 4.2 km on ground never exceeding a 10 m climb”. A claim with no grid reference is not map evidence.
