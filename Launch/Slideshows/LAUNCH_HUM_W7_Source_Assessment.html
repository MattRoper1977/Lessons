I Do

I Do: The Utility Answer's Architecture

Key Idea: The assessed utility answer runs a trained route. Own the enquiry, then take Source A (NOP, yield, bar), then Source B the same way. Finish with the set verdict — and give it a thesis of its own.

👀 Look: under pressure you do not need inspiration. You need the next station, and it is always known.

📖 The Route

Own the enquiry — Restate it — utility has no meaning without it.

Per source — NOP fast, yield declared, bar stated, discount applied.

Set verdict — 'Together the sources are ___ useful because each covers the other's bar.'

🧠 Watch me think

Watch the model's skeleton only — you flesh yours alone today: 'For an enquiry into migrant living conditions: Source A (account book) yields material daily life, bars feelings — moment discount minor. Source B (complaint letter) yields grievance and voice, bars typicality — purpose discount applied. TOGETHER: substantially useful; A gives the floorboards, B gives the temperature.' Route run. Now it's yours.

⭐ Assessed Conditions Card — staff and pupil copy

THE RULE: SUPPORT ACCESS, NEVER CONTENT. A pupil may be helped to get at the task — reading, scribing, timing, seating, breaks. A pupil may not be helped to decide what to say. If in doubt, do not.

This work is completed under supervised conditions: unaided, except for agreed access arrangements. It is then submitted for assessment.

Everyone has the enquiry and both sources. The table below is about scaffolds only. Access arrangements are separate, are never tiered, and appear beneath it.

TierScaffolds allowedScaffolds not allowed
🤝 SupportedThe opening and close frames; the checklist sheet; lined paperNew content; model sentences; anyone deciding which source is useful, or how useful
👤 StandardThe checklist sheet; lined paperFrames, model paragraphs, word banks of any kind, prompting on what to argue
🚀 StretchThe checklist sheet; lined paperAny content help at all, including model moves

The judgement is the assessment. Deciding how useful a source is, and saying so in your own words, is the thing being assessed. Everything that helps a pupil reach the page is access. Anything that supplies the judgement is not.

Access is never withheld, at any tier. Extra time, a break, a scribe, a reader, a change of seat or room are access arrangements, not scaffolds. A pupil at Stretch has the same right to them as a pupil at Supported. A reader may read the enquiry and the sources aloud; a reader may not explain them, summarise them, or say which one matters more. A scribe writes the pupil’s words and does not improve them. Where an arrangement is not written down, provide it and record it rather than withhold it.

🛑 STUCK MID-ANSWER? DO NOT STOP — MOVE.

Leave a gap, mark it with a star, and go on to the next source. An answer with one starred gap scores; an answer abandoned at the gap does not. Come back to the star when the rest is down. This is a strategy, not a concession.
