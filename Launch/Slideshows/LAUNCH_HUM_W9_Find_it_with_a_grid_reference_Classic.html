Find it with a grid reference

Autumn 2 · Week 1 · 40 minutes

Learning intention

I can use a map key and grid references to give a precise, checkable location.

Success criteria

  • Read three symbol meanings from a key.
  • Give four- and six-figure references.
  • Explain a reference’s precision and one map limit.

Use Next to begin. Ask for word help or your usual support when needed.

Teacher response guidance

  • A parking: 4020; 403204. B information: 4121; 414216. C camp site: 4222; 426223.
  • D parking: 5030; 506303. E information: 5131; 512317. F camp site: 5232; 524325.
  • Four figures here identify a 1 km square. Six figures identify a 100 m square. Neither supplies live opening/access conditions.

Arrival · Read the map, not the picture

Check north points upward on this map, the key explains symbols and easting is read first. Do not infer that north is always at the top of every map.

Starter · A square or a point?

Ask what near leaves uncertain. Expected: a grid reference narrows the location so another reader can find the same square.

I do · Along first, then up

Four figures identify a 1 km square here; six identify a 100 m square. Our local fictional grids omit the two-letter national block, so they are not real British National Grid positions.

We do · Give a checkable location

Check A parking 4020/403204, B information 4121/414216 and C camp site 4222/426223. Diagnose swapped coordinates separately from a wrong tenths count.

I do · Keep both coordinate parts

Explain that the two-letter block normally identifies a 100 km square; do not invent letters for this fictional map. Avoid “exact location”: the reference has finite precision.

We do · Test the symbol claim

Expected: the symbol shows parking at a location. It does not establish charges, available spaces or current opening; require the claim to stay with the key.

Independent · New map, same method

Provide a fresh grid, not a renamed copy of the model coordinates. Exact reading is available; do not point out the answer’s lower-left corner unless recording that prompt.

Exit · Read one location back

Receive one correct independent six-figure reference and the easting explanation. If a pupil reverses parts, have them trace east first and then north on Map B.
