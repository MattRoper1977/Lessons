I Do

I Do: Nature, Origin, Purpose

Key Idea: NOP is the archivist's triage: WHAT the source is, WHERE and WHEN it comes from, WHY it exists. Each of those answers changes what the source can prove.

📖 The Triage

Nature — The type and its habits — registers record, posters persuade.

Origin — Author, date, place — proximity and stake.

Purpose — The job it was made to do — the great predictor of selection.

🧠 Watch me think

Triage the poster: Nature — advertisement; exaggeration is its dialect. Origin — the works office, boom years; they NEEDED men. Purpose — to pull labour north. Now the yield: weak evidence for actual wages, superb evidence for the labour shortage and what recruiters thought would tempt a man. NOP didn't dismiss the poster — it re-aimed it.

I Do

I Do: Utility Is Relative to the Enquiry

Key Idea: No source is useful in the void. The LAUNCH move: state the enquiry, run NOP, then declare what the source is genuinely useful FOR — and not for.

📖 The Utility Verdict

For the enquiry… — Utility judged against a named question.

Genuinely useful for — The yield NOP actually licenses.

Not for — The tempting misuse, explicitly barred.

🧠 Watch me think

The landlady's account book, for an enquiry into migrant living conditions: nature (tracking, unglamorous, honest by function), origin (inside the lodging house, weekly), purpose (money, not posterity — no reason to pose). Verdict: genuinely useful for rents, diets, overcrowding and arrears; NOT for how lodgers FELT — the book records shillings, not sentiments. That last clause is where LAUNCH answers live.
