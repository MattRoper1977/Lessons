LEARNING OBJECTIVE

LO: To improve one part of my artwork after listening to feedback and record what I changed.

✓ I can give feedback that is kind, specific and actionable.
✓ I can receive feedback and choose one piece to apply.
✓ I can log the change: before, after, and why I chose it.
Success criteria: By the end you will have compared two versions of a piece, practised the feedback stems on a demo, exchanged feedback on developing portraits, applied ONE chosen piece, and logged the before/after with your reasoning — refinement evidence for Unit 1 Part A.

STARTER · talk task

Spot the improvement

Two versions of the same piece, side by side. With your partner: find every change — then the harder question: which change did the MOST work, and how can you tell?

Sentence stems:
“They changed…”
“The change doing the most work is…”
“I can tell because now the piece…”

Stuck? Cover half of each version and compare the halves. Small changes hide; their EFFECTS don’t — hunt the effect, then trace it back.

I DO · teacher explains

Feedback is a craft with rules

🤝 The stems

“One thing that works…” + “one thing to try…” — in that order, every time. The stems make honesty safe in both directions.

🎯 Specific & actionable

“Try a finer brush on the eyes” can be done TODAY. “Make it better” cannot. Feedback is measured by what the receiver can DO with it.

🧭 The receiver chooses

You’ll hear several suggestions; you apply ONE — the one that serves YOUR intention. Filtering feedback is an artist’s skill, not rudeness.

📷 Log the change

Before photo, the change, after photo — plus WHY you chose that piece of feedback over the others. The why is the Silver line.

Misconception: “Taking feedback means doing everything you’re told.” Taking feedback means HEARING everything and choosing what serves your intention. Your piece, your call — the journal just wants the reasoning behind the choice.
WE DO · together on the board

Sort the feedback — works, or needs work?

Kind + specific + actionable = works. Vague, unkind, or undoable-today = needs work. Test: could the receiver act on it in ten minutes without guessing?

HANDS-ON · the main event

Exchange and refine 🔄

Ground rule (60 seconds): stems only, work not person, and the maker’s choice is final — feedback is a gift, not an instruction.

Station 1 · FEEDBACK CIRCLE

Demo piece first — practise the stems together. Then exchange on real portraits: giving is expected, receiving-aloud is opt-in; written or TA-relayed feedback counts the same.

Station 2 · APPLY ONE

Choose the one piece of feedback that serves your intention. BEFORE photo — then refine.

Station 3 · LOG THE CHANGE

Before/after into the journal + the reasoning: “I chose this feedback because… and rejected ___ because…” Verbal-first counts.

GCSE Stretch: the technique-vocabulary upgrade: name the process in your feedback and your log — blend, bleed, layer, contrast, focal point.
