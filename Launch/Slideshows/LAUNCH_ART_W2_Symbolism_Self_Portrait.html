STARTER · talk task

The game with no wrong answers

If you were an OBJECT, what object? An ANIMAL? A WEATHER? With your partner, answer at least one — and then the real question: what does your answer KNOW about you that a mirror doesn’t?

Sentence stems:
“I’d be a ___ because…”
“A mirror shows my face; my answer shows my…”
“The weather one is hardest because…”

Stuck? Pick fast and interrogate after — the speed answer is usually the honest one. And “I’d rather not say why” is a complete, respected answer.

I DO · teacher explains

Symbols do the talking; media set the voice

🔣 The game is a tool

Object/animal/weather answers ARE symbols — Kahlo’s hummingbirds and thorns are the same move, painted. Your answers seed today’s portrait.

🎨 Media chosen on purpose

Collage fragments? Ink that bleeds? Digital layers that ghost? At Silver you choose media FOR a reason — and the reason goes in the journal.

📷 Develop in stages

Stage photos as you build: each one a decision made visible. Development, not perfection, is what Part A assesses.

📓 Reflect = why

“I used torn edges” is a diary. “I used torn edges because nothing about that year felt clean-cut” is a REFLECTION. Silver pays for the because.

Misconception: “Reflection just means describing what you did.” Description is the diary; reflection adds the WHY — the choice, the alternative you rejected, the effect you wanted. One because per stage turns a diary into a Silver journal.
WE DO · together on the board

What could each symbol stand for? Sort them

These are “coulds”, not answers — symbols are personal. Sort by the most common reading: strength holds firm, change transforms, calm settles.

HANDS-ON · the main event

Develop the portrait 🪞

Ground rule (60 seconds): symbols are personal property — nobody decodes anyone’s work uninvited, and “I’d rather not say” ends any question, no follow-up.

Station 1 · THE GAME

Object, animal, weather — choose your three (or one, done deeply). One line each on why, verbal or written. These seed the portrait.

Station 2 · DEVELOP

Build the surreal self-portrait in your chosen media — your symbols working, your face optional. Stage photos at every real decision.

Station 3 · REFLECT

Journal: each stage photo gets its because — “I chose ___ because ___”. Oral rehearsal first is a route; the BHM artist-influence line joins here.

GCSE Stretch: media experiments: the same effect tried three ways, annotated — which won, and why. That page IS GCSE development.
