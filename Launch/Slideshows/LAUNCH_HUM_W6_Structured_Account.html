I Do

I Do: The Thesis That Controls

Key Idea: A controlling thesis is arguable, precise and present everywhere: stated up front, carried by topic sentences, re-anchored at every paragraph's close.

👀 Look: “control” is physical. Every paragraph is clipped to the thesis, or it is not in the essay.

📖 Control Mechanics

Arguable & precise — 'Migration made modern Teesside — decisively, though within structural limits.'

Topic-sentence carry — Each paragraph opens by advancing, not announcing.

Re-anchor — Paragraphs close by cashing their evidence back into the claim.

🧠 Watch me think

Watch a topic sentence carry weight: NOT 'The census shows many migrants' (announcement) but 'The scale of arrival — 90,000 by 1901 — is the thesis's first proof' (advance). The evidence serves the claim by name. Then the re-anchor: '…which is why the town's very existence argues my case.' The spine never disappears under the flesh.

I Do

I Do: Sustaining Through the Counter and Close

Key Idea: Sustained argument survives its hardest test: the steel-manned counter. Then it lands a close that cashes the whole account back into the thesis, developed.

📖 The Last Movements

Steel-manned counter — 'The strongest objection holds that…' — full strength, then answered.

The turn — 'Yet this concedes precisely what it must deny…'

Developed close — Thesis echoed one register higher — with the evidence now behind it.

🧠 Watch me think

Model the turn: counter at full strength — 'geology, not migration, made Teesside; no ore, no town.' The turn — 'yet ore made nothing for centuries until hands arrived; the objection names a condition and calls it a cause.' Week 2's courtroom, weaponised inside an essay. Then the close: 'Decisively, then — with the ore as stage and the people as the play.' Spine intact to the final word.

MoveWhat it doesTest
