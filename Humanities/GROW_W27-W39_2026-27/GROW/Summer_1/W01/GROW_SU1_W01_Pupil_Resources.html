Our place in the wider world

Pupil resources | GROW Humanities Summer 1 Week 1

Locate my place the UK and the wider world on maps.

Use this labelled reference for the activities. Give your own response in the blank spaces. Point, speak, draw, type or write.

The world view locates our area approximately. Your teacher supplies a checked local map and UK atlas page for the closer views.

Evidence cards

Read with a partner or adult then use a named detail

A Our locality

Middlesbrough is on the River Tees, in Teesside in north-east England. These labels identify our locality within the UK. A local map shows more nearby detail than this world map.

Source: Locality facts from the task brief; teacher-checked local map

Limit: The world marker is approximate. It cannot show street positions.

B The UK and its neighbours

The UK is in Europe. The Atlantic Ocean lies to the west and the North Sea to the east. Use the UK marker and the named water labels.

Source: Natural Earth outlines and named geographical labels

Limit: Country outlines and labels are simplified at this scale.

C Choosing a map scale

Fictional task: a visitor needs to find our school entrance. A detailed local map is useful. A world map shows how the UK relates to continents and oceans, but cannot show the entrance.

Source: Authored classroom comparison of map purposes

Limit: Choose a map for the question. A world view does not show a safe walking route.

D A map comparison routine

Locate Teesside, then the UK, then Europe on the world view. Compare a teacher-selected local map, UK atlas page and world page. Name one detail that appears or disappears as the area shown changes.

Source: Teacher-selected atlas and local map; supplied world view

Limit: No map shows every feature. The local source must be checked before teaching.
