Evidence cards

Read the source and limitation before using a detail

A Question and roles

Agree: What is the land used for along our route? Choose an observer, tally recorder and location checker. The adult supervises the approved grounds route. Swap a role after a count.

Source: Authored teaching example

Limit: This example supports practice. Check your own place separately.

B One clear counting rule

Count each visible feature once, using its main use. Keep a tally in the matching category. Mark uncertain uses with a question mark and check with the adult. A count of features does not measure land area.

Source: Authored teaching example

Limit: This example supports practice. Check your own place separately.
