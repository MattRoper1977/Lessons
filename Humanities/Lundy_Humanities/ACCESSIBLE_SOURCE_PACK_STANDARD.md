# Accessible source pack standard

## Same historical goal, different access route

Every source pack should offer, as needed:

- original image/facsimile where rights allow;
- high-contrast crop or magnified version;
- accurate transcript;
- accessible-language gloss that is clearly labelled as support, not the source;
- read-aloud/audio route;
- vocabulary and context preview;
- numbered lines or details for evidence citation;
- tactile/physical/large-format map alternative;
- colour-independent map symbols;
- private/general route for identity-sensitive content.

## Preserve the historical work

Access may change decoding, size, presentation, motor demand or response mode. It must not:

- supply the inference;
- identify the “most useful” source;
- write the causal link;
- choose the significance criterion;
- complete the thesis;
- improve a scribed answer.

## Source integrity

A transcript or adaptation records what changed. Accessible formatting does not erase provenance, rights or limitation.

## Assessment

For assessed Week 7, only centre-approved access arrangements and permitted surfaces are used. This standard does not authorise a new scaffold.
