# Week 7 protection and aftercare

## Protected files

- `Grow/Slideshows/GROW_HUM_W7_Write_The_Account.html`
- `Launch/Slideshows/LAUNCH_HUM_W7_Source_Assessment.html`

## Before assessment

- confirm enquiry, permitted materials and timings;
- confirm each pupil's access arrangements;
- separate access from content scaffold;
- ensure source pack and provenance are approved;
- ensure staff know scribe/reader boundaries.

## During assessment

- support access, regulation, timing, seating and breaks;
- do not supply thesis, evidence selection, source judgement, counterargument or conclusion;
- record arrangements through the existing centre process;
- stop and follow safeguarding procedure if required.

## After pens-down

Permitted Lundy participation concerns:

- conditions;
- environment;
- accessibility;
- instructions;
- future assessment design.

It does not include:

- improving the assessed answer;
- adding a missing claim/evidence link;
- tutor captioning;
- a post-hoc audience invented as evidence;
- uploading before teacher authorisation.

## Marking and next step

The authorised teacher/assessor reviews the work and gives one next step. The optional day-close returns to that step; it does not create another.

## Anthology

After marking and consent, use a representative range rather than only “best” answers.
