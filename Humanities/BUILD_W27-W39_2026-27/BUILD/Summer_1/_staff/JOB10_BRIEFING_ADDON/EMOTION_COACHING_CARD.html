Emotion coaching

Adult regulates first. Keep words short; use the visual cue.

  1. 1Notice. See a change in pace, posture, voice or participation. Settle your own voice and pace first.
  2. 2Name plainly. Say what you see. Use a body signal if an emotion label does not land.
  3. 3Validate privately. Acknowledge that this feels hard, calmly and without an audience.
  4. 4Neutral boundary. State one short safety or task limit; offer the agreed pause or a choice.
  5. 5Solve when regulated. Return with one small step. Talk it through later, even next day.
  6. 6Regulate, then process. At a calm check-in, rehearse the signal, choice and repair.

What it is not
Not letting it go · not talking them out of it · not a long chat mid-meltdown.

Space, not closeness
Use the pupil’s agreed supervised place and return cue. Do not insist on proximity.
