Fact-checked source set

Links are retained for teacher checking; lesson files make no external request. Faith-community accounts are named perspectives, not universal representations.

  • OS: A beginner’s guide to using grid references — The lesson’s River Tees grid is a constructed rehearsal map, not an OS map.
  • PDP: Teesport — An operator promotes its service; the page does not describe every social, cultural or environmental effect of connection.
  • ONS: How life has changed in Middlesbrough: Census 2021 — Broad categories cannot explain an individual’s identity, belief, route or reason for moving; the religion question is voluntary.
  • MBC: Local population diversity — Country of birth is not the same as nationality, ethnicity, faith, identity or reason for migration.
  • BAPS-LDN: Diwali & Hindu New Year Annakut Celebrations 2023 — One institution’s account of one year cannot represent all Hindu, Jain, Sikh or other Diwali-related practices.
  • MRJ: Chanukah — This is one Jewish movement’s account; spelling, emphasis and practice vary within and between Jewish communities.
  • JML: Reform Hanukah Lighting — One photograph and catalogue account cannot show every participant, meaning, setting or Jewish practice; the museum explicitly notes variation.
  • WJC: Jews around the world celebrate Hanukkah — An older organisational news story selects a few examples and cannot represent current population, every location or every local practice.
  • CofE: Christmas — An Anglican source does not represent every Christian denomination, culture or Christmas practice.
  • WCC: Christmas messages across the world bring light and hope — Selected messages are examples, not a survey of all Christian communities.

Safeguarding and respectful enquiry

  • Personal/family migration, faith and festival disclosure stays optional; historical, local, general or fictional/composite routes are equally valued.
  • No learner represents a whole community. Use ‘some’, ‘this source’, ‘one example’ and ‘practice varies’.
  • Public sharing requires consent and a representative range—not ‘best work’ ranking.
  • Any sensitive disclosure follows the school’s safeguarding process; it is not printed into a lesson evidence queue.
