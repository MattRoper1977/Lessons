Resource use and carbon dioxide

Learning goal

Explain resource use and climate change using online data.

How can we make a justified decision about sustainability?

I can interpret resource and climate evidence, evaluate responses, compare options against criteria and justify a practical decision.

40 minutes, with flexible pauses. Point, speak, draw, type or write. A fictional example is welcome.

A The measured record

NOAA GML annual mean CO2 file; 2026 release

Limit: One observatory record is not the global average. The selected years omit changes between them; values can be revised.

B Explaining the cause

NASA explains that burning fossil fuels releases CO2 and that greenhouse gases slow heat loss to space. This connects resource use for energy with climate change. NASA made this public science explanation; page updated 13 July 2026, read 21 September 2026.

NASA Causes; public explanation, updated 2026

Limit: This explanation supports a mechanism. The selected CO2 table is not a temperature record.

C A weaker claim

AUTHORED EXAMPLE created for this lesson on 21 September 2026: an unattributed post says CO2 figures prove every place warmed by the same amount. Its imagined purpose is to persuade, but it supplies no author, measurement method or evidence.

Authored source-critique example; not a real post

D What the unit measures

NOAA states that CO2 is expressed as a mole fraction in dry air, abbreviated ppm. Read ppm as parts per million. The producer is NOAA GML; the file dated 5 September 2026 makes monitoring data available for scientific and public use.

NOAA GML file header; accessed 21 September 2026

Limit: Concentration is evidence about a cause of warming, not a reading of temperature. Keep those quantities separate.

Teacher card

Resource use and carbon dioxide / LAUNCH_SU1_W01

Outcome: Explain resource use and climate change using online data.

Evidence to retain: F: source annotation, evidence selection and questioning.

Forty minute route

Arrival 4 min; starter 3; model 6; shared task 7; check 2; independent 12; review 3; exit 3. Pause time is flexible.

Preparation

Choose one independent route. Print the evidence and shared cards, required visual or data sheet, route worksheet and exit. The organiser is one separate A4 page. Keep answers with staff.

Prepare the cards, organiser and one task route. Model the four steps before independent work. Use the supplied source record and distinguish real evidence from authored scenarios. The teacher supplies the school’s copy of No One Is Too Small by Greta Thunberg; no extract or quotation is included. Use the six NOAA rows and dry-air ppm units. Explain that the final selected year is the latest complete year in the downloaded file, 2025. Do not infer global temperature from one observatory.

Access and regulation

Pre-teach terminology and model evidence use. Chunk sources and data; provide an initial frame, then reduce it. Maintain normal access arrangements. Extend by weighing limitations, alternative explanations and evidence quality. Adults support access and evidence selection without supplying answers. Use labelled examples and a reduced data set before the full task.

Offer a calm start, an agreed pause and a private response option. Accept speech, pointing, typing or writing. Invite reasoned disagreement without personal or family disclosure.

Use sensitive content warnings where relevant. Do not ask for family details or personal experiences.

Assessment

Record the learner’s own reasoning and support used. Keep the conclusion individual and use the exit to plan one next step.

UAS: no approved unit code is supplied. Match the learner’s evidence to the centre-selected unit and exact criteria before submission. This pack makes no automatic award claim.

Resource use and carbon dioxide

Learning goal: Explain resource use and climate change using online data.

Words to use

WordMeaning
provenanceWho made a source, when and why
concentrationAmount of a substance within a mixture
resourceSomething people use to meet a need
limitationA boundary on what evidence can establish

Remember

Check the producer, date, purpose and units before using a value.

Keep a measured concentration separate from a temperature claim.

How to show your learning

Audit the selected years, provenance and the distinction between concentration and temperature.

Use the cards and organiser. Choose your own evidence and explanation.

Evidence matters

Fictional cases are teaching examples. Historical, geographical and religious claims use the named sources. A model video demonstrates a method.

You can respond by speaking, pointing, drawing, typing or writing. A fictional personal example is welcome.

A The measured record

NOAA Global Monitoring Laboratory publishes the Mauna Loa annual mean CO2 series to monitor atmospheric change. File created 5 September 2026; accessed 21 September 2026. The selected values run from 338.76 ppm in 1980 to 427.35 ppm in 2025.

NOAA GML annual mean CO2 file; 2026 release

Limit: One observatory record is not the global average. The selected years omit changes between them; values can be revised.

B Explaining the cause

NASA explains that burning fossil fuels releases CO2 and that greenhouse gases slow heat loss to space. This connects resource use for energy with climate change. NASA made this public science explanation; page updated 13 July 2026, read 21 September 2026.

NASA Causes; public explanation, updated 2026

Limit: This explanation supports a mechanism. The selected CO2 table is not a temperature record.

C A weaker claim

AUTHORED EXAMPLE created for this lesson on 21 September 2026: an unattributed post says CO2 figures prove every place warmed by the same amount. Its imagined purpose is to persuade, but it supplies no author, measurement method or evidence.

Authored source-critique example; not a real post

Limit: A confident claim is not a measurement. Do not use this invented post as evidence about climate.

D What the unit measures

NOAA states that CO2 is expressed as a mole fraction in dry air, abbreviated ppm. Read ppm as parts per million. The producer is NOAA GML; the file dated 5 September 2026 makes monitoring data available for scientific and public use.

NOAA GML file header; accessed 21 September 2026

Limit: Concentration is evidence about a cause of warming, not a reading of temperature. Keep those quantities separate.

Shared investigation

Work with a partner or adult, or use your own table. One person suggests; the other checks the evidence. Swap after one turn. Passing is allowed.
