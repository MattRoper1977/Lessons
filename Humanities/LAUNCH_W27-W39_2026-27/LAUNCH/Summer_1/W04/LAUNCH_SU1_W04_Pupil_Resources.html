Evidence cards

Check the source and limit before using a detail

A Earth Day and our audience

Earth Day is observed on 22 April. Our product is a classroom digital message for school users. Name one feasible action and its intended effect. Keep it on the school-approved classroom device; do not create accounts or publish it online.

Source: EARTHDAY.ORG History; authored classroom brief

Limit: The date does not prove that a campaign action has worked.

B A supported climate link

NASA explains that burning fossil fuels releases CO2, which contributes to warming. A campaign may explain that link with a citation. It must not turn a small proposed action into a promise to stop climate change.

Source: NASA Causes; authored campaign application

Limit: This source does not calculate a CO2 saving for a school paper tray or a particular campaign.

C Weak drafts to repair

AUTHORED EXAMPLES, not facts: This will stop climate change. Our tray has already saved a tonne of CO2. Both go beyond the supplied evidence. Repair the claim, name the audience and action, and state what you will measure.

Source: Authored weak-draft examples, 21 September 2026

Limit: These are deliberately unsupported claims for editing, not estimates to repeat.

D An honest action message

AUTHORED PLAN: invite school users to choose clean spare paper for suitable drafts, with teacher permission. Count new sheets issued for the same task before and during a short trial. Explain the intended effect as reducing new-sheet use, then check it.

Source: Authored campaign and measurement plan, 21 September 2026

Limit: No guaranteed result or carbon saving is supplied. Include no pupil names, faces or personal details.
