Judging climate responses

Knowledge organiser | LAUNCH Humanities

Evaluate responses to climate change.

Words to use

WordMeaning
mitigationReducing greenhouse-gas emissions or increasing their removal
adaptationAdjusting to actual or expected climate impacts
impactAn effect of a change
evaluationA judgement using evidence and limits

Method to remember

Distinguish cause, impact and response.

Identify mitigation, adaptation or both.

State a benefit and a limitation for each approach.

Justify a provisional choice using evidence.

My explanation

I choose ____. Evidence from ____ shows ____. This matters because ____. A limit is ____.

Remember

Mitigation targets causes; adaptation adjusts to impacts. Explain a benefit and a limitation. Source: NASA, Mitigation and Adaptation.
