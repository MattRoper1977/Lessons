# LAUNCH_SU1_W06 · code map

Objective: Complete a sustainability decision report.

- **VF** — After the I-do hinge or at Review, the adult gives verbal feedback about the actual response to “I can defend or revise a sustainability decision using evidence, a criterion and a limitation”.
- **WS** — In We-do, record actual scaffolded work on the ladder and the prompt level; access aids alone are identified separately.
- **I** — At Independent application (slide-7), mark only a pupil’s own independent content decision or explanation.
- **NS+** — At Review / Exit, name one specific next step from the hinge, work or exit evidence; leave blank if none given.
- **//** — At Review, mark the point where the pupil actually self-edits a response against feedback or the criterion.
- **?** — At Exit, press “Read it back (?)” and hear, see or receive the pupil’s read-back by their agreed mode.
- **R** — At the next lesson Arrival, receive the pupil’s response to the NS+ and engage with it; close the loop then, not when NS+ is first given.
- **E** — At Independent / Exit, capture only observed Job 2 evidence if it occurs: communication & interaction: Defends a provisional decision with a source and limitation; cognition & learning: Links a criterion score to a specific evidence note; SEMH: Uses the agreed review routine to respond to a challenge to the decision. Use the existing evidence record.

Codes show possible walk-visible moments, not claims that they happened. Do not assign a code merely because its line appears here.
