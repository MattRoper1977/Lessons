Complete a sustainability decision report.

Words to use

WordMeaning
criterionA stated feature used to compare options
trade-offA choice involving benefits and disadvantages
limitationSomething the evidence cannot establish
decisionA choice justified by evidence and criteria

Method to remember

Score both options using the same explicit criteria.

Give evidence for each judgement.

Write preference, trade-offs, limits and next action.

Check accuracy and record support used.

My explanation

I choose ____. Evidence from ____ shows ____. This matters because ____. A limit is ____.

Remember

Give evidence for scores, a trade-off, a limitation and a next action. Record support. An adult can record the learner’s own words.
