Sources and checks

Sources

Each lesson carries its own sources and source notes in its lesson page and staff files. Nothing was added to them for this pack.

External pages can change or be filtered by a school network. Check any linked page on the school network before teaching.

Checks made for this pack

Every lesson page and pack file was matched by SHA-256 to the reviewed delivery and its manifest. Each lesson page carries one correction only, the week number and the term added to its lesson data; removing those added bytes recreates the reviewed page exactly. Every text layer was scanned for names before landing.

Live browser layout, phone and touch behaviour, printing and link access were not checked in this preparation.
