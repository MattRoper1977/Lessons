Pilgrimage and sacred places

Original captioned teaching model made for this pack. It demonstrates the supplied reasoning; it is not historical footage, survivor testimony or a documentary. The full transcript and factual source credits are included.

Question: Give one reason pilgrimage may matter to a believer.

Evidence: B Why Hajj matters: Practices during Hajj can express equality and shared commitment before God. Pilgrims have different personal experiences; clothing alone does not tell us what an individual believes.

Think aloud: For example: obedience to God, feeling equal and united, or prayer for healing.

Check: Do all Christians go on pilgrimage? No. Many never do.
