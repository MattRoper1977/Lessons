Question: Which principle asks you to imagine being the other person?

Evidence: A A Christian teaching: “Do to others as you would have them do to you.” Often called the Golden Rule. B A Humanist principle: Think about the consequences for everyone affected, and use empathy: how would it feel to be them?

Think aloud: Both: the Golden Rule and Humanist empathy.
