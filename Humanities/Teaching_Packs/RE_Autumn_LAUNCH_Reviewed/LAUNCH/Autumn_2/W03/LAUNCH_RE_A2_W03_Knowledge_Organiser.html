Rites of passage and community

Learning goal: Describe a rite of passage and explain how it can connect a person to a community.

Words to use

WordMeaning
rite of passageA ceremony that marks a new stage of life
ceremonyA formal event with set words and actions
commitmentA promise to stay loyal to something
Bar or Bat MitzvahA Jewish ceremony marking religious adulthood

Remember

Define “rite of passage”. A ceremony that marks a new stage of life.

Are rites of passage only religious? No. There are non-religious ceremonies too.

How to show your learning

Choose one rite of passage.

At a ___, ___. This means ___. It shows belonging because ___.

Evidence matters

Fictional cases are teaching examples. Historical, geographical and religious claims use the named sources. A model video demonstrates a method.

This is learning about religion and worldviews, not worship. Nobody is asked to share a personal belief, family detail or loss.
