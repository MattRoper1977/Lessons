Comparing responses to a life question

Original captioned teaching model made for this pack. It demonstrates the supplied reasoning; it is not historical footage, survivor testimony or a documentary. The full transcript and factual source credits are included.

Question: Give the strongest similarity between the two responses.

Evidence: B A Muslim response: Authored fictional Muslim profile: the speaker connects care for others with teachings about wishing others well and with zakat. They explain giving as a responsibility to God and to people in need. C A Humanist response: Authored fictional Humanist profile: the speaker uses empathy and considers consequences for people. They want to reduce suffering and help others, without grounding that choice in a belief in God.

Think aloud: Both support helping others and reducing harm, although they explain their reasons differently.

Check: Can two sources stand for two whole worldviews? No. They are two individuals.
