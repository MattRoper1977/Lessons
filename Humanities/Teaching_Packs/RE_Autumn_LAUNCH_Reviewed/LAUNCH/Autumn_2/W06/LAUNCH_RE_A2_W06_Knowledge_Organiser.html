A structured “compare” response

Learning goal: Produce a structured comparison using evidence from two worldviews.

Words to use

WordMeaning
compareTo show similarities and differences
describeTo say what something is like
howeverA word that introduces a difference
viewpointWhat a person or worldview thinks about a question

Remember

Which words show that an answer is comparing? Both, however, whereas.

Are two descriptions side by side a comparison? No. A comparison links them.

How to show your learning

Answer one Autumn 2 comparison question.

Both ___. However, ___ whereas ___, because ___.

Evidence matters

Fictional cases are teaching examples. Historical, geographical and religious claims use the named sources. A model video demonstrates a method.

This is learning about religion and worldviews, not worship. Nobody is asked to share a personal belief, family detail or loss.
