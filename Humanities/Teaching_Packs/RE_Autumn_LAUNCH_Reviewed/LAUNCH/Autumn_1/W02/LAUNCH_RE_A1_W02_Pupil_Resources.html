Evidence cards

Read, point or listen. Keep the source label with any detail you use.

A Christianity: key beliefs

Christians believe in one God, understood as Trinity. They believe Jesus is the Son of God, and that his teaching and example show how to live. Most believe in life after death.

Source: Authored RE summary

Limit: Christians differ on many details.

B Humanism: key beliefs

Humanists do not believe in a god. They hold that this life is the only one we have, that reason and evidence are the best guides, and that people can live good lives without religion.

Source: Authored summary of Humanists UK’s description of Humanism

Limit: Humanists differ on many details.

C Belief or practice?

A belief is held to be true: “God exists.” A practice is done: “going to church”. Compare belief with belief.

Source: Authored classroom resource

Limit: Some statements mix both.

D Shared ground

Both worldviews value kindness, honesty and responsibility for others. They differ on God and on life after death.

Source: Authored classroom resource

Limit: Sharing values does not make two worldviews the same.

Supported independent task

Complete this route only. Identify a key belief in two worldviews and explain one similarity or difference.

1. Use the two-source set with symbols.

2. Choose one belief from each worldview.

3. Say whether they are the same or different.

Support: Use the glossary and one chunk of evidence. Plan the claim and supporting detail before explaining. Complete the organiser one space at a time. Both ___. However, Christians believe ___, whereas Humanists believe ___.

An adult may read or scribe your exact response. They should not choose the answer.

Standard independent task

Complete this route only. Identify a key belief in two worldviews and explain one similarity or difference.

1. Choose one similarity or one difference.

2. Explain it using the belief cards.

3. Use “some” or “many”, not “all”.

Support: Both ___. However, Christians believe ___, whereas Humanists believe ___.

Check the required evidence and revise one detail.

Stretch independent task

Complete this route only. Identify a key belief in two worldviews and explain one similarity or difference.

1. Explain one similarity and one difference.

2. Quote or paraphrase a card as evidence.

3. Explain one consequence of the difference for daily life.

Support: Choose the evidence independently. Use the frame only if it helps.

Explain why two worldviews can share a value but ground it in different beliefs.
