Key beliefs across two worldviews

Original captioned teaching model made for this pack. It demonstrates the supplied reasoning; it is not historical footage, survivor testimony or a documentary. The full transcript and factual source credits are included.

Question: Name one belief from each worldview.

Evidence: A Christianity: key beliefs: Christians believe in one God, understood as Trinity. They believe Jesus is the Son of God, and that his teaching and example show how to live. Most believe in life after death. B Humanism: key beliefs: Humanists do not believe in a god. They hold that this life is the only one we have, that reason and evidence are the best guides, and that people can live good lives without religion.

Think aloud: For example: Christians believe in God as Trinity; Humanists believe this is our only life.

Check: Do all Christians believe exactly the same things? No. Use “some”, “many” or “most”.
