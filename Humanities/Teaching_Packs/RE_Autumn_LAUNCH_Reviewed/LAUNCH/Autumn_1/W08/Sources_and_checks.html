Evidence, belief and choices

RE & World Views · LAUNCH · Autumn 1 · Week 8

This return lesson teaches the key idea from the earlier lesson, then revisits six earlier topics through a short individual check. Source profiles are fictional; nobody is asked for personal beliefs, family details or loss.

Teaching sources

  • Belief, identity and belonging in adult life — Explain one way a belief or worldview can influence a person's identity or choices.
  • Key beliefs across two worldviews — Identify a key belief in two worldviews and explain one similarity or difference.
  • Sources of authority and meaning — Recognise a source of authority or meaning and explain how it may guide a believer or non- religious person.
  • Festivals, shared values and the Day of Peace — Explain how a festival or shared value can bring people together and identify one value linked to peace.
  • A structured “explain” response — Write or give a structured explanation using a point, evidence/example and explanation.
  • Remembrance and peace across beliefs — Compare how two worldviews may respond to remembrance or peace.
  • Belief and identity assessment — Use evidence from the half-term to explain how beliefs and worldviews can influence identity, values or choices.

Sources named in the teaching material

  • Humanists UK explanation of Humanism
  • Royal British Legion The poppy
  • United Nations International Day of Peace

Teacher-authored examples, manual model, captioned video and transcript are available offline. These sources were supplied with the teaching materials; external pages can change.
