Festivals, shared values and the Day of Peace

Learning goal: Explain how a festival or shared value can bring people together and identify one value linked to peace.

Words to use

WordMeaning
shared valueSomething people of different worldviews agree is important
peaceWhen people are safe and conflicts are settled without violence
symbolSomething that stands for an idea
communityPeople linked by a place, belief or activity

Remember

Name one value shared across two worldviews. For example, peace, welcome or dialogue.

Is the Day of Peace only for religious people? No. It is for everyone.

How to show your learning

Create a “Peace in Practice” response.

The value of ___ can lead to ___. This builds peace because ___.

Evidence matters

Fictional cases are teaching examples. Historical, geographical and religious claims use the named sources. A model video demonstrates a method.

This is learning about religion and worldviews, not worship. Nobody is asked to share a personal belief, family detail or loss.
