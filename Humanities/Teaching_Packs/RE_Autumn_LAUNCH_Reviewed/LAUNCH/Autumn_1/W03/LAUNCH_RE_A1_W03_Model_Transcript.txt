Sources of authority and meaning

Original captioned teaching model made for this pack. It demonstrates the supplied reasoning; it is not historical footage, survivor testimony or a documentary. The full transcript and factual source credits are included.

Question: Define “authority” in RE.

Evidence: C Authority is not “being in charge”: A boss can give orders. An authority in RE is a source a person chooses to trust for guidance.

Think aloud: A source people trust to guide what they believe or do.

Check: Is authority in RE the same as being in charge? No. It is a trusted source of guidance.
