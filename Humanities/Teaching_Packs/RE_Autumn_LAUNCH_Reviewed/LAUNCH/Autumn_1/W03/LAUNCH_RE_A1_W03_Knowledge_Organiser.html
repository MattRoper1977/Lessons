Sources of authority and meaning

Learning goal: Recognise a source of authority or meaning and explain how it may guide a believer or non- religious person.

Words to use

WordMeaning
authorityA source people trust to guide what they believe or do
sacred textWriting that is holy to a religion, such as the Bible or the Qur’an
conscienceA person’s inner sense of right and wrong
traditionBeliefs and practices passed down over time

Remember

Define “authority” in RE. A source people trust to guide what they believe or do.

Is authority in RE the same as being in charge? No. It is a trusted source of guidance.

How to show your learning

Choose one scenario.

___ may guide them because ___.

Evidence matters

Fictional cases are teaching examples. Historical, geographical and religious claims use the named sources. A model video demonstrates a method.

This is learning about religion and worldviews, not worship. Nobody is asked to share a personal belief, family detail or loss.
