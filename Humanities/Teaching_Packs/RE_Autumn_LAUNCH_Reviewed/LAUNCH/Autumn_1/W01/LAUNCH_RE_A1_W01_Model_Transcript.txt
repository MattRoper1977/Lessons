Belief, identity and belonging in adult life

Original captioned teaching model made for this pack. It demonstrates the supplied reasoning; it is not historical footage, survivor testimony or a documentary. The full transcript and factual source credits are included.

Question: How does Daniel’s belief influence his choice of work?

Evidence: A Daniel: Fictional profile: Daniel is 24, Christian and a care worker. He says, “I believe every person is made in the image of God, so everyone deserves dignity.” He chose care work over a better-paid job.

Think aloud: He believes everyone deserves dignity, so he chose care work.

Check: Does a belief fully determine a person’s identity? No. It is one influence among several, and views can change.
