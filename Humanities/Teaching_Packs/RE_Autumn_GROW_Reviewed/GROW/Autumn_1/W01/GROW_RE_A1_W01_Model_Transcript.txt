Beliefs and worldviews in our community

Original captioned teaching model made for this pack. It demonstrates the supplied reasoning; it is not historical footage, survivor testimony or a documentary. The full transcript and factual source credits are included.

Question: Name two different worldviews found in Britain.

Evidence: A Hannah: Fictional profile: Hannah is 14 and Christian. She goes to church with her gran and prays before exams. She says her faith helps her stay calm. C Ellie: Fictional profile: Ellie is 14 and non-religious. She calls herself a Humanist. She thinks people should use reason and kindness to decide what is right.

Think aloud: Any two, for example Christianity and Humanism.

Check: Does a non-religious person have beliefs and values? Yes. Everyone has a worldview, religious or not.
