How beliefs shape identity

Learning goal: Describe one way beliefs can shape a person's identity.

Words to use

WordMeaning
identityWho a person is and how they see themselves
influenceTo have an effect on something
valueSomething a person thinks is important in how to live
choiceA decision between options

Remember

What value does Imran’s belief lead to? Generosity: caring for those in need.

Is belief the only thing that shapes identity? No. Family, friends, place and culture also shape identity.
