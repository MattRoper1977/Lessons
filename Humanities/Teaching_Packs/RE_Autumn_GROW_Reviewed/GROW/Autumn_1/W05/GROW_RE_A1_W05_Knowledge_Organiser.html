Christmas and Christian belief

Learning goal: Explain how one Christmas tradition links to a Christian belief.

Words to use

WordMeaning
incarnationThe Christian belief that God became human in Jesus
NativityThe story of the birth of Jesus
traditionSomething people do again and again at a special time
beliefSomething a person holds to be true

Remember

Name one belief connected to Christmas. The incarnation: God became human in Jesus.

Is every Christmas tradition a Christian belief? No. Some traditions are cultural.

How to show your learning

Choose one Christmas tradition.

This links to ___ because ___.

Evidence matters

Fictional cases are teaching examples. Historical, geographical and religious claims use the named sources. A model video demonstrates a method.

This is learning about religion and worldviews, not worship. Nobody is asked to share a personal belief, family detail or loss.
