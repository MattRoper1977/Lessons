Shared values and what we remember

RE & World Views · GROW · Autumn 1 · Week 8

This return lesson teaches the key idea from the earlier lesson, then revisits six earlier topics through a short individual check. Source profiles are fictional; nobody is asked for personal beliefs, family details or loss.

Teaching sources

  • Beliefs and worldviews in our community — Identify different beliefs and worldviews in our community.
  • How beliefs shape identity — Describe one way beliefs can shape a person's identity.
  • Diwali: festival, story and meaning — Explain one meaning connected to Diwali.
  • Hanukkah and the theme of light — Describe how light is used at Hanukkah and explain one meaning it can represent.
  • Christmas and Christian belief — Explain how one Christmas tradition links to a Christian belief.
  • Comparing festivals of light — Compare two festivals of light respectfully using one similarity and one difference.
  • Remembrance and shared values — Explain one shared value connected to remembrance and respect.
