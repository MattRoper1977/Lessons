Comparing festivals of light

Learning goal: Compare two festivals of light respectfully using one similarity and one difference.

Words to use

WordMeaning
compareTo look for what is the same and what is different
similarityA way that two things are the same
differenceA way that two things are not the same
respectfulShowing care for other people’s beliefs

Remember

Give one similarity between two festivals of light. Both use light as a symbol, or both bring people together.

Do we compare festivals to decide which is best? No. We compare to understand.

How to show your learning

Choose two festivals.

Both ___ and ___ ___. However, ___.

Evidence matters

Fictional cases are teaching examples. Historical, geographical and religious claims use the named sources. A model video demonstrates a method.

This is learning about religion and worldviews, not worship. Nobody is asked to share a personal belief, family detail or loss.
