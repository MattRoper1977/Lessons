Belonging across faiths

Learning goal: Describe what belonging can mean in two faith or worldview communities.

Words to use

WordMeaning
belongingFeeling part of a group and welcome in it
faith communityPeople who share a religion and meet together
practiceSomething people do as part of their beliefs
supportHelp given to someone

Remember

Give one way a faith community can support belonging. For example, sharing a meal, praying together or serving others.

Does everyone in a faith community feel belonging in the same way? No. Belonging varies between individuals.

How to show your learning

Complete the map for both profiles using the four lenses.

In ___ community, belonging can mean ___.

Evidence matters

Fictional cases are teaching examples. Historical, geographical and religious claims use the named sources. A model video demonstrates a method.

This is learning about religion and worldviews, not worship. Nobody is asked to share a personal belief, family detail or loss.
