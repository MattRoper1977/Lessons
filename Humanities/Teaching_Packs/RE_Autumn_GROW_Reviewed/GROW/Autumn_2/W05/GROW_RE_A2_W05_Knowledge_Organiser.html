Questions for a faith or belief visitor

Learning goal: Ask or answer a respectful question and record one thing learned from a faith or belief visitor.

Words to use

WordMeaning
lived experienceWhat life is really like for a person
respectful questionA question that is curious and kind
open questionA question that needs more than yes or no
boundaryA limit that keeps people comfortable and safe

Remember

Give one respectful question stem. For example: “What does ___ mean to you?”

Does one visitor speak for everyone who shares their worldview? No. They speak from their own lived experience.

How to show your learning

Ask or write one respectful question.

I learned that ___. One question I still have is ___.

Evidence matters

Fictional cases are teaching examples. Historical, geographical and religious claims use the named sources. A model video demonstrates a method.

This is learning about religion and worldviews, not worship. Nobody is asked to share a personal belief, family detail or loss.
