Place of worship enquiry

Original captioned teaching model made for this pack. It demonstrates the supplied reasoning; it is not historical footage, survivor testimony or a documentary. The full transcript and factual source credits are included.

Question: Name one feature of a mosque and its purpose.

Evidence: B Mihrab and minbar: The mihrab is a niche showing the direction of Makkah, which Muslims face to pray. The minbar is a raised platform where the imam gives the Friday talk.

Think aloud: For example, the mihrab shows the direction of Makkah for prayer.

Check: Is a place of worship used only for worship? No. Many also serve the community.
