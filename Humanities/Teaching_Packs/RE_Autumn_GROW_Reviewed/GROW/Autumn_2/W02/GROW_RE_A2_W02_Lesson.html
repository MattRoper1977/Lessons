GROW · RE & World Views · Autumn 2 · Week 2

Place of worship enquiry

Learning goal

Identify key features of a place of worship and explain one purpose.

What does it mean to belong to a community of belief?

I can describe belonging, explain a feature of a place of worship, use RE vocabulary and give a reasoned viewpoint.

40 minutes, with flexible pauses. Point, speak, draw, type or write. A fictional example is welcome. Nobody is asked to share a personal belief.

Editable Word packPrintable pupil resources PDF

This is learning about religion and worldviews, not worship. Nobody is asked to share a personal belief, family detail or loss.

Word and evidence bank

A Prayer hall

Many mosque prayer halls have open carpeted space for rows of worshippers. Chairs or other adjustments may support people who cannot comfortably stand, bow or kneel.

Authored RE summary

Limit: Mosques differ in size and design.

B Mihrab and minbar

The mihrab is a niche showing the direction of Makkah, which Muslims face to pray. The minbar is a raised platform where the imam gives the Friday talk.

Authored RE summary

Limit: Mosques differ in size and design.

C Washing area

Before prayer many Muslims wash in a set way, called wudu. Mosques provide a washing area. Shoes are left at the door.

Authored RE summary

Limit: Belief and practice vary between individuals and communities.

D Community use

Many mosques also run classes, food banks and community meals. A place of worship serves a community as well as worship.

Authored RE summary

Limit: Not every mosque offers the same activities.

Teacher card

Place of worship enquiry / GROW_RE_A2_W02

Outcome: Identify key features of a place of worship and explain one purpose.

Evidence to retain: F: labelled visitor guide with one feature and its purpose.

Forty minute route

Arrival 4 min; starter 3; model 6; shared task 7; check 2; independent 12; review 3; exit 3. Pause time is flexible.

Preparation

Choose one independent route. Print the evidence and shared cards, required visual or data sheet, route worksheet and exit. The organiser is one separate A4 page. Keep answers with staff.

Pause virtual tour frequently; explicitly teach respectful behaviour; support navigation and feature-purpose links; avoid unnecessary detail.

Access and regulation

Virtual visit option; image-led task; labels before sentences; reduced feature set; oral explanation; enlarged screenshots if needed.

Offer a predictable start, private response and agreed pause. No cold calling or forced personal disclosure. Rejoin at one clearly named next step. Keep teen-appropriate examples and the same learning goal across response modes.

This is learning about religion and worldviews, not worship. Nobody is asked to share a personal belief, family detail or loss.
