Belief and identity assessment

Learning goal: Show understanding of beliefs, festivals, belonging and respect using evidence from the term.

Words to use

WordMeaning
evidenceAn example or fact that supports an answer
sourceSomething that gives information, such as an image or a profile
explainTo say how or why, using “because”
next stepOne thing to improve next time

Remember

What makes evidence strong? It fits the question, it is accurate and you can explain it.

Is a model answer the only correct answer? No. A different answer with good evidence is just as strong.

How to show your learning

Answer each question with a point.

I think ___. The source shows ___. This means ___ because ___.

Evidence matters

Fictional cases are teaching examples. Historical, geographical and religious claims use the named sources. A model video demonstrates a method.

This is learning about religion and worldviews, not worship. Nobody is asked to share a personal belief, family detail or loss.
