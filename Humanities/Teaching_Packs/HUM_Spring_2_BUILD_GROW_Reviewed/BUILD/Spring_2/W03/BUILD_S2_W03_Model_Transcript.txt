Matching care to a need

Original captioned teaching model made for this pack. It demonstrates the supplied reasoning; it is not historical footage, survivor testimony or a documentary. The full transcript and factual source credits are included.

Question: What could help someone find resources?

Evidence: C A caring action: Fictional case: a new student cannot find the reading resources. A clear shelf label and a choice of formats could help. Ask what is useful before deciding.

Think aloud: A clear shelf label, an accessible list or a guided introduction.

Check: Why ask what helps before acting? It avoids assuming the person’s needs or wishes.
