Evidence cards

Read, point or listen. Keep the source label with any detail you use.

A The Good Samaritan

Easy-read retelling of the Christian parable in Luke 10: A traveller needs help. Two people pass by. A Samaritan stops, cares for him and arranges more help. The story asks what it means to be a neighbour.

Source: Authored retelling of Luke 10:25–37

Limit: A religious story teaches an idea; this retelling is not a quotation or a historical record.

B A fair turn

Fictional case: three pupils share one tablet. Jo needs text read aloud. The group can agree turns and switch on speech support. Equal dignity does not always mean identical support.

Source: Authored classroom case

Limit: This example does not describe every person or place.

C A caring action

Fictional case: a new student cannot find the reading resources. A clear shelf label and a choice of formats could help. Ask what is useful before deciding.

Source: Authored classroom case

Limit: This example does not describe every person or place.

M Malala Yousafzai

Malala campaigns for children’s education, especially girls’ education. She shared the 2014 Nobel Peace Prize with Kailash Satyarthi.

Source: Nobel Prize, Malala Yousafzai facts

Limit: An award recognises work; it does not prove that unequal access to education has ended.
