A person who challenged unfairness

Original captioned teaching model made for this pack. It demonstrates the supplied reasoning; it is not historical footage, survivor testimony or a documentary. The full transcript and factual source credits are included.

Question: What does Malala campaign for?

Evidence: M Malala Yousafzai: Malala campaigns for children’s education, especially girls’ education. She shared the 2014 Nobel Peace Prize with Kailash Satyarthi.

Think aloud: Children’s education, especially girls’ education.

Check: What does the 2014 prize show and not show? It recognises her work; it does not prove all education barriers ended.
