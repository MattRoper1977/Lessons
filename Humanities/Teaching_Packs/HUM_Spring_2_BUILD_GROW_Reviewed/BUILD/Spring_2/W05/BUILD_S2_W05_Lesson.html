Our class caring action

Learning goal

Take part in a class caring action.

What do stories and actions teach us about fairness?

I can identify fair and unfair choices, explain a caring action and recognise someone who challenged unfairness.

40 minutes, with flexible pauses. Point, speak, draw, type or write. A fictional example is welcome.

Teacher notes and answers

Teacher card

Our class caring action / BUILD_S2_W05

Outcome: Take part in a class caring action.

Evidence to retain: F: participation observation and recorded reflection.

Forty minute route

Arrival 4 min; starter 3; model 6; shared task 7; check 2; independent 12; review 3; exit 3. Pause time is flexible.

Preparation

Choose one independent route. Print the evidence and shared cards, required visual or data sheet, route worksheet and exit. The organiser is one separate A4 page. Keep answers with staff.

Use fictional situations. Do not ask learners to disclose neglect, conflict or personal experiences of unfairness.

Access and regulation

Use one-step instructions, symbols and a model first. Offer talk, point, draw, type or write. Reduce the number of choices; extend by asking for a reason or a second example. Adults prompt the first response then fade support.

Offer a predictable start, private response and agreed pause. No cold calling or forced personal disclosure. Rejoin at one clearly named next step. Keep teen-appropriate examples and the same learning goal across response modes.

Use sensitive content warnings where relevant. Do not ask for family details or personal experiences.

Assessment

Use questioning and the exit response to decide the next step. Record the learner’s individual contribution and the access support used.

UAS: no approved unit code is supplied. Match the learner’s evidence to the centre-selected unit and exact criteria before submission. This pack makes no automatic award claim.

Teaching and evidence record

Supplied scheme of work teaching sequence: Agree an achievable action and roles. Carry it out; record a contribution and discuss its effect.

Look for

F: participation observation and recorded reflection.

Accept accurate equivalents, including speech or exact scribing. Check the concept and use of evidence rather than length or handwriting. Record access support separately from content prompting.

BUILD: a concrete example with a reason or demonstrated action. GROW: explain or compare with a relevant detail. LAUNCH: connect evidence and reasoning; use a limitation when the outcome requires it. Keep RE responses non-GCSE.
