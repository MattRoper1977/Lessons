Worldviews and responses to change

Original captioned teaching model made for this pack. It demonstrates the supplied reasoning; it is not historical footage, survivor testimony or a documentary. The full transcript and factual source credits are included.

Question: Can different stated values lead to caring actions?

Evidence: D Community accounts: Fictional account 1: a faith group explains caring for creation as its reason for planting shade trees. Fictional account 2: a non-religious residents’ group gives human wellbeing as its reason for a cool-room project.

Think aloud: Yes. The accounts give different motivations for actions intended to reduce heat harm.

Check: Do these fictional accounts describe all faith or non-religious groups? No. They are authored examples, not representative findings.
