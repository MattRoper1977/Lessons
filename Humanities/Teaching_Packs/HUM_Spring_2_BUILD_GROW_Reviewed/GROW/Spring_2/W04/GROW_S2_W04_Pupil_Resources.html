Evidence cards

Read, point or listen. Keep the source label with any detail you use.

A Zones and events

Broad temperature zones relate to latitude. Near the equator is generally warmer; high latitudes are generally colder. Local climate also depends on altitude and other factors. Earthquakes relate mainly to plate boundaries, not climate zones.

Source: Authored map explanation

Limit: Broad bands simplify climate; they are not a detailed climate classification.

B Human influence

Burning fossil fuels adds carbon dioxide. Greenhouse gases slow heat loss to space. Human activity is the main cause of recent global warming.

Source: NASA Causes of climate change

Limit: One local hot or cold day does not establish the global trend.

C Two responses

Reducing fossil-fuel use tackles a cause of warming. Shade, water access and cooler buildings can reduce harm during heat. These responses do different jobs.

Source: Authored examples based on climate mechanisms

Limit: Their suitability depends on the place and the needs of people there.

D Community accounts

Fictional account 1: a faith group explains caring for creation as its reason for planting shade trees. Fictional account 2: a non-religious residents’ group gives human wellbeing as its reason for a cool-room project.

Source: Two authored teaching accounts with explicitly stated values

Limit: These are not reports of real organisations and do not represent every religious or non-religious person.
