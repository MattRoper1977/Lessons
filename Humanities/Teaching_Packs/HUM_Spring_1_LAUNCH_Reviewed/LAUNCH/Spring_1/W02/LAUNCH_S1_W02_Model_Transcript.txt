Understanding the Holocaust respectfully

Original captioned teaching model made for this pack. It demonstrates the supplied reasoning; it is not historical footage, survivor testimony or a documentary. The full transcript and factual source credits are included.

Question: Who perpetrated the Holocaust and who was its central target?

Evidence: B The Holocaust: The Holocaust was the systematic persecution and murder of six million European Jews by Nazi Germany and its allies and collaborators. Persecution developed from 1933; the genocide took place during the Second World War. Nazi policies also persecuted and murdered other groups.

Think aloud: Nazi Germany, its allies and collaborators murdered six million European Jews.

Check: Was the Holocaust simply another word for all wartime deaths? No. It refers to the Nazi-led genocide of European Jews; other victim groups also require accurate recognition.
