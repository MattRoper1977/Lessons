Evaluating causes and consequences

Original captioned teaching model made for this pack. It demonstrates the supplied reasoning; it is not historical footage, survivor testimony or a documentary. The full transcript and factual source credits are included.

Question: Did war automatically cause the Holocaust?

Evidence: A Europe in 1939: Nazi Germany’s expansion and invasion of Poland on 1 September 1939 led Britain and France to declare war on 3 September. Longer-term conditions and leaders’ choices help explain the conflict. B The Holocaust: The Holocaust was the systematic persecution and murder of six million European Jews by Nazi Germany and its allies and collaborators. Persecution developed from 1933; the genocide took place during the Second World War. Nazi policies also persecuted and murdered other groups.

Think aloud: No. Nazi antisemitism, deliberate decisions and policies were central; war and occupation expanded opportunities for persecution and murder.

Check: What should an evaluation add to a list of events? Supported links, weighed explanations and a conclusion.
