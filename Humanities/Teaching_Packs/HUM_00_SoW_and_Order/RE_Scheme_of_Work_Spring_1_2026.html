Spring 1 · BUILD · GROW · LAUNCH · 18 weekly outcomes · 1 × 40-minute lesson per week · Arrival → Starter → I Do → We Do → You Do → Exit Ticket

How this term works

No cold calling. A pupil may pass. Reflection is optional and never worship.

BUILD · Spring 1

Enquiry: How can a story show care? How can we choose a fair act? How can sorry help put things right?
Success criteria: Success today: I can… show one caring act and say how it helps. Success today: I can… choose a fair act and give a reason. Success today: I can… link sorry to one act that puts things right.

Week 1 · How can a story show care? F
This week's outcome
To be able to… find care in a story.
Activities
Arrival: In your book: Give four short replies or marks. Starter: Which person in the first picture might need help? I Do: In your book: Keep one useful word or model step. We Do: Show one caring act in the class story. You Do: Choose the adapted road story or the made-up club story. Listen to it again. Exit Ticket: In your book: Give two short replies, then show your confidence.
Key vocabulary
care, help, story
Resources required
Slides; Pupil resources; Knowledge organiser; Word page; Staff answers
Evidence
F — book task, targeted questioning and 2-question exit check.
Student adaptations
You may point, speak, sign, use symbols, draw or write. An adult may write your words. You may pass.
Classroom support
No cold calling. A pupil may pass.
