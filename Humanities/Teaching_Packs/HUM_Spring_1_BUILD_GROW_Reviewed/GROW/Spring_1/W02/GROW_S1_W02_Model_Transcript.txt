Investigating a rights campaign

Original captioned teaching model made for this pack. It demonstrates the supplied reasoning; it is not historical footage, survivor testimony or a documentary. The full transcript and factual source credits are included.

Question: Was Parks’ action separate from all earlier organising?

Evidence: A Before December 1955: Rosa Parks had years of civil-rights organising experience, including work with the NAACP. Montgomery buses enforced racial segregation.

Think aloud: No. The source describes years of civil-rights work.

Check: What is a limitation of one later biography? It selects events and cannot represent every participant’s experience.
