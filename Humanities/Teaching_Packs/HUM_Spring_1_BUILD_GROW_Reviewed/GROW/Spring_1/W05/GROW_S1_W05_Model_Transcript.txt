Writing an account of change

Original captioned teaching model made for this pack. It demonstrates the supplied reasoning; it is not historical footage, survivor testimony or a documentary. The full transcript and factual source credits are included.

Question: Explain a link in a historical account.

Evidence: B 1 December 1955: Parks was arrested after refusing to give up her seat to a white man on a Montgomery bus. Her action helped trigger a sustained community boycott. C 1956 change: The boycott and legal action challenged segregation. A Supreme Court ruling and pressure on bus revenues contributed to the desegregation of Montgomery buses.

Think aloud: The arrest in 1955 helped prompt a sustained boycott. Collective organisation and legal action contributed to the 1956 change.

Check: What should the conclusion do? Answer the enquiry using the evidence already explained.
