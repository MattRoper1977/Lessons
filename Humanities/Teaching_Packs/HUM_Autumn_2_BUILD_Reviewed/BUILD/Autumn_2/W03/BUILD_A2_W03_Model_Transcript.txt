Objects and respectful enquiry

Original captioned teaching model made for this pack. It demonstrates the supplied reasoning; it is not historical footage, survivor testimony or a documentary. The full transcript and factual source credits are included.

Question: What should we do before handling a borrowed artefact?

Evidence: O Objects and meanings: A diya is a small lamp associated with Diwali. A hanukkiah is used at Hanukkah. Ask before touching borrowed objects; use an unlit replica or a drawing.

Think aloud: Ask permission and follow its owner’s handling guidance.

Check: Can shape alone establish a religious meaning? No. Use the source or an informed explanation as well.
