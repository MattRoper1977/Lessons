Fairness and rights

Original captioned teaching model made for this pack. It demonstrates the supplied reasoning; it is not historical footage, survivor testimony or a documentary. The full transcript and factual source credits are included.

Question: Could reading support make a shared task fairer?

Evidence: B A fair turn: Fictional case: three pupils share one tablet. Jo needs text read aloud. The group can agree turns and switch on speech support. Equal dignity does not always mean identical support.

Think aloud: Yes. It can help a pupil access the same activity.

Check: Does a fair choice need a reason? Yes. Link it to a need or a fair opportunity.
