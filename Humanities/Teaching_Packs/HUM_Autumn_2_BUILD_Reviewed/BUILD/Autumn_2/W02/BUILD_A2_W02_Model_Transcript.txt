Comparing celebrations

Original captioned teaching model made for this pack. It demonstrates the supplied reasoning; it is not historical footage, survivor testimony or a documentary. The full transcript and factual source credits are included.

Question: Name one shared feature.

Evidence: D Diwali: Many Hindus mark Diwali with lamps, worship and gatherings. Light can express good overcoming evil and knowledge overcoming ignorance. Practices and stories vary. H Hanukkah: Hanukkah is a Jewish festival lasting eight nights. Lights recall the Temple’s rededication and the traditional account of oil lasting eight days. A hanukkiah has eight festival lights and a helper light.

Think aloud: Both can use lights in celebration.

Check: Name one difference supported by the sources. Hanukkah lasts eight nights; its lights recall rededication and the oil tradition.
