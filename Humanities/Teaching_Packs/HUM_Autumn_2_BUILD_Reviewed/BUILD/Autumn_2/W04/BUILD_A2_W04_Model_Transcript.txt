The sequence of a celebration

Original captioned teaching model made for this pack. It demonstrates the supplied reasoning; it is not historical footage, survivor testimony or a documentary. The full transcript and factual source credits are included.

Question: What comes first in our family’s plan?

Evidence: S One family’s celebration plan: Fictional Diwali plan: prepare a welcoming space; arrange unlit lamps with an adult; gather for worship; share food and greetings. This is one sequence, not a rule for every family.

Think aloud: Prepare a welcoming space.

Check: Is the plan a rule for every Diwali celebration? No, it is a fictional example.
