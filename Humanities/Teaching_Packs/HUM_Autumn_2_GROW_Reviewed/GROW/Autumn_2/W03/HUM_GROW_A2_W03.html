Discover: Connections across the world

Autumn 2 · Week 3Title

Learning goal: I can use Schematic Locator W and a festival greeting source to locate two fictional communities and explain how they connect.

In this lesson

  • Read Schematic Locator W across continents, oceans and compass directions.
  • Trace a fictional festival greeting connection between Place A and Place B.
  • State clearly what teaching markers show and leave unknown.

Arrival

What you need to know
  • On a map grid, we read the easting along first, then the northing up.
  • A four-figure grid reference identifies a whole square.

  2. Northing.
  3. Lots of possible ways — for example a story, a message, a gift, or a coincidence.
Route B · If you missed the last lesson
What you need to know
  • A continent is a large land region; an ocean is a large body of salt water.
  • A connection is a link between people or places.
  1. What word means a large land region shown on a world map?
  2. What does the North ↑ arrow show on Locator W?
  3. Lead-in: How could two far-apart groups share the same picture?

  1. Continent.
  2. North (compass direction up on the map).
  3. Lots of possible ways — for example a story, a message, a gift, or a coincidence.
