Source-based assessment

Original captioned teaching model made for this pack. It demonstrates the supplied reasoning; it is not historical footage, survivor testimony or a documentary. The full transcript and factual source credits are included.

Question: Test usefulness with a practice question about plans.

Evidence: B 1947 to 1948 report: Eric Walrond reported varied experiences: work for some men, but unemployment also described in the north-west. C 8 August 1972 Cabinet record: British ministers discussed Idi Amin’s order that Asian British-passport holders leave Uganda within three months.

Think aloud: A request can show what someone intended. It cannot establish that the plan happened. I apply this reasoning independently to the assessment sources.

Check: What evidence must be retained? Individual source annotations, selected evidence and supported judgements, with access support recorded.
