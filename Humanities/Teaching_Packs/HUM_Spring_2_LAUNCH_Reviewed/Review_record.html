LAUNCH_S2_W01 · Causes of tectonic hazards

Outcome: Explain causes of tectonic hazards.

Assessment: F: explanation, map/data interpretation and two exit questions.

Shared activity: chain — Locate the Japan case and model subduction, sudden movement and seafloor displacement. Interpret the event’s magnitude and map.

Independent task: Build an evidence-based cause, effects and responses diagram. Interpret a map or graph using one accurate value. Make a case-study judgement distinguishing hazard from risk.
