Analysing hazard data for a report

Original captioned teaching model made for this pack. It demonstrates the supplied reasoning; it is not historical footage, survivor testimony or a documentary. The full transcript and factual source credits are included.

Question: Which units belong on the temperature axis?

Evidence: Selected station maxima on 19 July 2022. Station: Coningsby. Maximum °C: 40.3. Units: °C.

Think aloud: Degrees Celsius.

Check: Can selected station maxima establish the temperature of every street? No. Coverage, exposure and local conditions vary.
