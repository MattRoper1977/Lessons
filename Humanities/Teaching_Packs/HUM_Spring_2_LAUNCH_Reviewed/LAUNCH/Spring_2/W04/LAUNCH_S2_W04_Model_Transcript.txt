Extreme weather and unequal risk

Original captioned teaching model made for this pack. It demonstrates the supplied reasoning; it is not historical footage, survivor testimony or a documentary. The full transcript and factual source credits are included.

Question: What was recorded at Coningsby on 19 July 2022?

Evidence: C Heat and unequal risk: On 19 July 2022 the Met Office recorded 40.3°C at Coningsby, 40.2°C at St James’s Park and 39.6°C at Topcliffe. Outdoor work increases exposure; difficulty accessing cool spaces can increase vulnerability. Shade and accessible cool spaces can reduce harm.

Think aloud: 40.3 degrees Celsius in the Met Office case-study report.

Check: Why can the same heatwave create unequal risk? People have different exposure, buildings, resources and access to cool spaces.
