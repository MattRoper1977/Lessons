Hazard effects and management

Original captioned teaching model made for this pack. It demonstrates the supplied reasoning; it is not historical footage, survivor testimony or a documentary. The full transcript and factual source credits are included.

Question: Which response directly addresses tsunami exposure?

Evidence: B Effects and responses: Shaking and coastal flooding damaged places and disrupted people’s lives. Earthquake-resistant design addresses shaking; warnings, evacuation routes and higher ground address tsunami exposure.

Think aloud: Accessible routes and timely warnings that enable movement to higher ground.

Check: Does earthquake-resistant construction remove all tsunami risk? No. Shaking and coastal flooding require different measures.
