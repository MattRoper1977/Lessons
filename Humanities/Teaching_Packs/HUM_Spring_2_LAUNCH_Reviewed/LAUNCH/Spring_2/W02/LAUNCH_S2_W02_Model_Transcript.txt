Tracking live hazards

Original captioned teaching model made for this pack. It demonstrates the supplied reasoning; it is not historical footage, survivor testimony or a documentary. The full transcript and factual source credits are included.

Question: Why record the snapshot time?

Evidence: D Reading a hazard feed: A USGS event row can include UTC time, place, magnitude and depth in kilometres. A short feed is a dated snapshot. It cannot predict the next earthquake.

Think aloud: The feed changes, so the evidence must be traceable to a particular capture.

Check: Can this small sample predict the next earthquake? No. A short event list is not a prediction.
