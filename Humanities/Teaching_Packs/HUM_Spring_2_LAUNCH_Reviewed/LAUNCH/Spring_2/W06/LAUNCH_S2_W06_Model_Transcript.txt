A hazard case-study report

Original captioned teaching model made for this pack. It demonstrates the supplied reasoning; it is not historical footage, survivor testimony or a documentary. The full transcript and factual source credits are included.

Question: Plan an evidence-based hazard judgement.

Evidence: Read the assessment requirement: S: case-study outcome against unit criteria; retain best evidence.

Think aloud: I connect a stated risk to a response, use a location and data value, then explain a limitation. A list of effects alone does not justify the judgement.

Check: What must accompany the graph or map? A title, source, relevant date, labels or key, and an explanation of what it contributes.
