Similarities between beliefs

Original captioned teaching model made for this pack. It demonstrates the supplied reasoning; it is not historical footage, survivor testimony or a documentary. The full transcript and factual source credits are included.

Question: What value do Aisha and Tom share?

Evidence: A Aisha: Fictional profile: Aisha is 15 and Muslim. She gives part of her pocket money to charity. She says, “My faith teaches me to help people in need.” B Tom: Fictional profile: Tom is 16 and a Humanist. He does not follow a religion. He helps at a food bank. He says, “People should look after each other.”

Think aloud: Helping others.

Check: Do Aisha and Tom have the same beliefs about God? No. They have different beliefs but share a value.
