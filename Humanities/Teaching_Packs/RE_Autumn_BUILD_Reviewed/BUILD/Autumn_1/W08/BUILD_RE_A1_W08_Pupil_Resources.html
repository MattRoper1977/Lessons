Route card · answers go in your book

Independent (I)

Write your choices for all four pictures; add a reason for one.

Supported (S)

Point to or say your choices for each picture; an adult may scribe your exact choices.

An adult may read or scribe your exact response. They should not choose the answer.

Choice/MCQ (C)

Choose the letters or match the alternatives in your book. Record C beside the work.

  1. The watch card links an object to a relative. Choose why it matters: A its price; B a memory of the relative.
  2. Look at the Torah scroll. Choose its faith: A Jewish; B Christian. Choose careful handling: A use a pointer; B touch the words.
  3. A volunteer greets a visitor and shows a seat. Choose: A welcomed; B left out. Choose the peaceful action: A include; B exclude.
  4. One person gives to charity; another helps at a food bank. Choose the shared value: A helping others; B holding every belief in common.

Stretch

Choose all four. Explain two reasons and name a respectful action.

  1. Special things. Mina’s old watch belonged to her grandad. Why is it special? A: its high price. B: it reminds her of him.
  2. Special books and care. Look at the Torah scroll. Choose its faith: Jewish / Christian. Choose careful handling: use a pointer / touch the words.
  3. Welcome and peace. A volunteer greets Sam and shows him a seat at the gurdwara. Is he welcomed / left out? Which builds peace: include someone / exclude someone?
  4. Shared values. Aisha gives to charity; Tom helps at a food bank. What do they share? A: every belief. B: helping others.

Read one response back (?); improve one detail and mark //. Show your exit privately.

Special things, respect and belonging

Learning goal: Share or describe a 'special to me' object respectfully.

Words to use

WordMeaning
meaningWhat something stands for or why it matters
memorySomething you remember
shareTo tell or show something to others
respectTreating people and things with care

How I know

A person, a memory, a belief or a place.

No. A photo, drawing or object card is a complete response.

Model steps

  1. Read the question.
  2. Select a useful source detail.
  3. Explain what it shows.
  4. Check the link.

This is ___. It is special because ___.

Use fictional source cards. A quiet response is welcome.

Evidence cards

A Speaking frame

This is ___. It is special because ___. A short share lasts about 20 seconds.

Authored classroom resource

Limit: A frame is a support. Your own words are welcome.

B Object card: a key ring

Fictional case: Lee has a key ring from a seaside trip. It reminds Lee of a day out with an aunt.

Authored classroom case

Limit: This example does not describe every person, family or community.

C Object card: prayer beads

Some people use beads to help them pray or reflect. For them the beads link to belief.

Authored RE summary

Limit: Beads are used in different ways in different faiths. Some people use none.

D Respectful listening

Give the speaker time. Listen in a way that works for you; eye contact is optional. Choose a kind comment or respectful question. Sharing privately with an adult is welcome.

Authored classroom rules

Limit: Listening can look different for different people. Eye contact is not required.
