Special books and places

Original captioned teaching model made for this pack. It demonstrates the supplied reasoning; it is not historical footage, survivor testimony or a documentary. The full transcript and factual source credits are included.

Question: Where is a Torah scroll kept in a synagogue?

Evidence: C Torah and synagogue: The Torah is sacred to Jewish people. In a synagogue the Torah scroll is kept in a special cupboard called the Ark. Readers often use a pointer instead of touching the words.

Think aloud: In a special cupboard called the Ark.

Check: Is a mosque a book or a place? A place. Many Muslims pray together there.
