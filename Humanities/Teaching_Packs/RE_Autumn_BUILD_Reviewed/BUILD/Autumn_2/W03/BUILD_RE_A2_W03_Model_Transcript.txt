Retelling a festival story

Original captioned teaching model made for this pack. It demonstrates the supplied reasoning; it is not historical footage, survivor testimony or a documentary. The full transcript and factual source credits are included.

Question: What do people do to welcome Rama and Sita home?

Evidence: D Lamps light the way: Rama and Sita travel home. People light rows of lamps to welcome them. This is one reason lamps are lit at Diwali.

Think aloud: They light rows of lamps.

Check: Which event comes first in the story? Rama and Sita are sent away to the forest.
