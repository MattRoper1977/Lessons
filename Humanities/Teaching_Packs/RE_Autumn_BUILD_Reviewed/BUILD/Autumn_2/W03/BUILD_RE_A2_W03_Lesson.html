Opening

BUILD · RE & World Views · Autumn 2 · Week 3

Retelling a festival story

Learning goal

Retell a festival story in the correct order using symbols or pictures.

How do people use light and stories to show what matters?

I can explain a festival symbol, compare two celebrations, retell a story in order and give a reason for my view.

40 minutes, with flexible pauses. Point, speak, draw, type or write. A fictional example is welcome. Nobody is asked to share a personal belief.

This is learning about religion and worldviews, not worship. Nobody is asked to share a personal belief, family detail or loss.

Teacher brief

Teacher notes and answers

Teacher card

Retelling a festival story / BUILD_RE_A2_W03

Outcome: Retell a festival story in the correct order using symbols or pictures.

Evidence to retain: F: ordered sequence and retelling.

Forty minute route

Arrival 4 min; starter 3; model 6; shared task 7; check 2; independent 12; review 3; exit 3. Pause time is flexible.

Preparation

Choose one independent route. Print the evidence and shared cards, required visual or data sheet, route worksheet and exit. The organiser is one separate A4 page. Keep answers with staff.

Model slowly; repeat story; use consistent visuals; check sequence before asking for explanation; avoid overloading with names/details.

Access and regulation

Reduced 3–4 card sequence; numbered cards; oral retell; pre-written captions; scribe/pointing option.

Offer a predictable start, private response and agreed pause. No cold calling or forced personal disclosure. Rejoin at one clearly named next step. Keep teen-appropriate examples and the same learning goal across response modes.

This is learning about religion and worldviews, not worship. Nobody is asked to share a personal belief, family detail or loss.

Assessment

Use questioning and the exit response to decide the next step. Record the learner’s individual contribution and the access support used.

UAS: no approved unit code is supplied. Match the learner’s evidence to the centre-selected unit and exact criteria before submission. This pack makes no automatic award claim.

Arrival answers

1. Light. Both festivals use lamps or lights.

2. The order in which things happen

3. They light rows of lamps.

4. Rama and Sita are sent away to the forest.

Shared activity reasoning

1. Rama and Sita are sent away to the forest — 1. The story starts when they leave home.

2. Ravana takes Sita away — 2. This happens while they live in the forest.

3. Rama, helped by Hanuman, rescues Sita — 3. The rescue comes after Sita is taken.

4. People light lamps to welcome them home — 4. The lamps end the story.

Exit responses

What do people do to welcome Rama and Sita home? They light rows of lamps.

Which event comes first in the story? Rama and Sita are sent away to the forest.

Teaching and evidence record

Supplied scheme of work teaching sequence: Arrival: 4 retrieval questions. Starter: put three everyday events in order. I Do: retell the chosen festival story with large symbol cards. We Do: sequence the cards together. You Do: sequence 4–6 symbols/pictures and retell orally, by labels or short captions. Exit ticket: choose the first/last event.

Look for

F: ordered sequence and retelling.

Accept accurate equivalents, including speech or exact scribing. Check the concept and use of evidence rather than length or handwriting. Record access support separately from content prompting.

BUILD: a concrete example with a reason or demonstrated action. GROW: explain or compare with a relevant detail. LAUNCH: connect evidence and reasoning; use a limitation when the outcome requires it. Keep RE responses non-GCSE.
