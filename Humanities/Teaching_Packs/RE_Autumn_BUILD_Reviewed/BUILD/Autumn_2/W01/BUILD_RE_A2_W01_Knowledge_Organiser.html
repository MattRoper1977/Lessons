A festival of light: Diwali

Learning goal: Explore one festival of light and explain one meaning linked to it.

Words to use

WordMeaning
festivalA special time when people celebrate
DiwaliA festival of lights celebrated by many Hindus, Sikhs and Jains
diyaA small lamp, often made of clay
symbolSomething that stands for an idea

Remember

What can the light of a diya stand for? Good overcoming evil, and hope.

Does every family celebrate Diwali in the same way? No. Families celebrate in different ways.

How to show your learning

Choose one Diwali symbol.

Light can mean ___ because ___.

Evidence matters

Fictional cases are teaching examples. Historical, geographical and religious claims use the named sources. A model video demonstrates a method.

This is learning about religion and worldviews, not worship. Nobody is asked to share a personal belief, family detail or loss.
