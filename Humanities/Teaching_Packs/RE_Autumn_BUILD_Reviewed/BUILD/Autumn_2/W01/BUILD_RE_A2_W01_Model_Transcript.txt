A festival of light: Diwali

Original captioned teaching model made for this pack. It demonstrates the supplied reasoning; it is not historical footage, survivor testimony or a documentary. The full transcript and factual source credits are included.

Question: What can the light of a diya stand for?

Evidence: B The diya: A diya is a small lamp. Rows of diyas are lit at Diwali. For many people the light stands for good overcoming evil and for hope.

Think aloud: Good overcoming evil, and hope.

Check: Does every family celebrate Diwali in the same way? No. Families celebrate in different ways.
