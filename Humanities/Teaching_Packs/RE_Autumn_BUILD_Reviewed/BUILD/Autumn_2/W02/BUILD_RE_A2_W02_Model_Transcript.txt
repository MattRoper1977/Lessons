Comparing light in celebrations

Original captioned teaching model made for this pack. It demonstrates the supplied reasoning; it is not historical footage, survivor testimony or a documentary. The full transcript and factual source credits are included.

Question: Name one thing Diwali and Hanukkah both use.

Evidence: A Diwali: Many Hindus, Sikhs and Jains celebrate Diwali. Families light diya lamps. For many the light stands for good overcoming evil. B Hanukkah: Hanukkah is a Jewish festival. It lasts eight nights. Each night one more light is lit on the hanukkiah. It recalls the Temple being rededicated and oil that lasted eight days.

Think aloud: Light. Both festivals use lamps or lights.

Check: How many nights does Hanukkah last? Eight nights.
