Comparing light in celebrations

Learning goal: Identify one similarity between how light is used in two celebrations.

Words to use

WordMeaning
compareTo look for what is the same and what is different
HanukkahA Jewish festival of lights that lasts eight nights
hanukkiahA lamp with eight festival lights and one helper light
similarityA way that two things are the same

Remember

Name one thing Diwali and Hanukkah both use. Light. Both festivals use lamps or lights.

How many nights does Hanukkah last? Eight nights.

How to show your learning

Sort the feature cards into Diwali, Hanukkah or both.

Both ___. Diwali ___. Hanukkah ___.

Evidence matters

Fictional cases are teaching examples. Historical, geographical and religious claims use the named sources. A model video demonstrates a method.

This is learning about religion and worldviews, not worship. Nobody is asked to share a personal belief, family detail or loss.
