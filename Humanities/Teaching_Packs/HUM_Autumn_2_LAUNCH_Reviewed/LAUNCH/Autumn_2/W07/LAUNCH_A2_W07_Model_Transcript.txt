Writing up a fieldwork enquiry

Original captioned teaching model made for this pack. It demonstrates the supplied reasoning; it is not historical footage, survivor testimony or a documentary. The full transcript and factual source credits are included.

Question: Check a fieldwork conclusion against the evidence.

Evidence: Read the assessment requirement: S: enquiry process, map/data accuracy and conclusion.

Think aloud: My conclusion must use my actual counts and enquiry question. I state the sampled location and time so I do not generalise to a whole town.

Check: Why include a limitation? It shows the boundaries of what the method and sample can establish.
