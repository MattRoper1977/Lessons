CHEESE LAYER · ANIMATED MODEL
Not an art specialist? Say this: Say: “A studio artist controls their materials AND works safely. Pick one process — line, layering or texture. Practise controlling it. Notice where it goes wrong (the ‘glitch’) and how you repair it. Safe practice is part of the skill, not a rule bolted on.”
