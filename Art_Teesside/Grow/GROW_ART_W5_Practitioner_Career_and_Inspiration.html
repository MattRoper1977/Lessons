I Do

I Do: Studying a Practitioner Properly

Key Idea: Four lenses on any practitioner: start, route, practice, and the decisions at the forks — facts sourced, gaps admitted.

👀 Look: a career path, drawn honest — from first sketches to paid practice.

Step 0 of 4

The start, evidenced

Where/how they began: training or not, first tools, first paid work — from interviews/bios, sourced, not invented.

The route with forks

The zigzag mapped: the day job years, the pivot, the break. At each fork: what did they DECIDE? Decisions transfer; luck doesn't.

The practice today

A day in the work: materials, hours, collaborators, how work finds them. The unglamorous mechanics are the education.

Gaps admitted

'No source says why they left the studio job' — an admitted gap beats an invented fact, every time. Bronze research is honest research.

I Do

I Do: The Mirror Page

Key Idea: 'What this means for me': three honest transfers from their route to yours — a decision to copy, a pathway to investigate, a standard to adopt.
Step 0 of 3

A decision to copy

'They kept a day-skill alongside the art — I'll keep my digital skills warm.' Their fork, my policy.

A pathway to investigate

'The mural route ran through a local commission — who commissions walls HERE?' One named next step, findable from this room.

A standard to adopt

'They photograph every finished piece before it leaves — adopted, starting with Week 3's outcome.' Small, immediate, real. The mirror page turns admiration into equipment.
