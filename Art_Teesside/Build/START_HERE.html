Autumn 2 & Spring 1 · Bronze route

Two terms, one award. Autumn 2 opens Bronze Parts A, B and C; Spring 1 completes Part D. Every week carries a three-way diagnostic (wrong one way · wrong the other · right) and a before/after mount, so process and outcome sit side by side. No press, no rollers, no inks — rubbings, stencils and sponged acrylic only.
