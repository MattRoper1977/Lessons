I Do

I Do: Reflection That Counts

Key Idea: Explore reflection has three moves: the change (before/after), the evidence (point at it), the next step (specific and wanted).

👀 Look: share it, feel it, look back — the loop closes and the artist has grown.

Step 0 of 4

The change

'In Week 1 my lines were timid; by Week 6 my stipple held a focal point.' Before/after — not 'I got better'.

The evidence

I physically point: page 1 beside the finished piece. Reflection without pointing is just claiming.

The next step

'Next I want scratch-back on a bigger scale.' Specific, wanted, and it names a real technique — the sentence a Bronze year is built on.

Audience receipts in

Two visitor comments from the show get stapled beside the reflection — what I sent vs what they received, side by side.

Independent Work — Studio Time

Instructions: Work at your level. Evidence photos go in your Arts Award portfolio — your reflection sheet + audience receipts — the completed Explore portfolio check (Part D + final audit).
20:00

Supported

Task: Reflection sheet with the frame (change/point/next) + "I chose to share ___ because ___" + gap-hunt with a partner using the part cards.

Steps: Three frame sentences · folder open beside partner · sticky note per gap.

Standard

Task: Full reflection (three moves + receipts stapled) + "I chose to share ___ because ___" + complete four-part audit with every gap noted and one fixed TODAY.

Steps: Reflection written · audit against the tap-cards · the today-fix done, not promised.

Stretch

Task: Reflection + audit + "I chose to share ___ because ___" + the transfer paragraph: which studio habit you'll use outside art, evidenced from the term.

Steps: All Standard steps · the habit named · a term moment cited · where it transfers, specifically.
