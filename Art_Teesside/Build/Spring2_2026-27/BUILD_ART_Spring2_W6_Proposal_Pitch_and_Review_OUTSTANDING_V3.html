👩‍🎨 I Do · model one visible decision

Teacher model · decision, action, evidence

1 · Sentence 1 — WHAT: name the maquette and its strongest structural/visual decision.
2 · Sentence 2 — WHY THERE: connect the work to exact site evidence, not a generic ‘it looks good’ claim.
3 · Sentence 3 — NOTICE: state what people may notice, feel or question without claiming everyone will respond the same way.
Think aloud: “I am not hunting for a perfect sculpture. I am isolating one visible cause so I can explain my next move.”

2 · Visual knowledge organiser

Key terms

proposal
a visual and reasoned plan for work that is not yet installed
pitch
a concise explanation made for a real audience
feedback
a received response that can be accepted, tested or rejected with a reason
influence
a visible change caused by pupil voice and audience response

Artist-method focus

Use the three contextual lenses from Week 4 as precedents, not templates: name the method that influenced your proposal and show the different pupil-owned result.

Equipment / safety / maintenance

Maquette; selected site image/sketch; surface sample; A4/A3 proposal board; adhesive or clips; pens; feedback card; optional authorised recording device.

Display and recording are optional. Use spoken, mounted-board with witnessed walk-through, or written routes equally. Follow privacy/consent rules; no public upload is assumed.

Visual workflow

  1. Select representative frame, void, surface and site evidence.
  2. Write or rehearse: what it is; why this site; what people may notice.
  3. Pitch through the chosen equal-status route to a genuine audience.
  4. Receive one specific response; keep, test, change or reject it and show the consequence.
