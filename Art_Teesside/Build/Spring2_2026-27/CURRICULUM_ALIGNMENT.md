Six standalone 40-minute lessons. Repository originals were not changed.

| Week | Focus | Enquiry | Potential Arts Award alignment | Minimum evidence hand-off |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| W1 | Armature & Frame | What makes a structure stand up — and still let you change your mind? | Explore Part A · Take Part | Standing frame + baseline/revision sketch or authorised photo + weak point fixed + pupil reason. |

## Shared spine

**FRAME → VOID → SKIN → CONTEXT → SITE → PROPOSE & INFLUENCE**

## House-standard implementation

1. Arrival task: 2 × 2 recall ladder.
2. Visual knowledge organiser: terms, artist-method focus, safety and visual workflow.
3. Supported / Standard / Stretch practice as movable routes.
4. Diagnostic strip: wrong one way / wrong another way / useful model + fix.
5. Assessment: authorship versus support, same-award-part recovery, I Do / We Do / You Do anchor.

## Qualification guardrail

This lesson can support Trinity Arts Award Explore evidence. The page and finished object do not certify the award; a trained adviser must audit the pupil’s own decision, action and review in the full personal arts log.

The labels describe potential evidence alignment, not award completion. Source rights, adult/collaborator support and pupil authorship stay separate. No organisation endorsement, site access or qualification outcome is implied.
