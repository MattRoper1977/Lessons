CHEESE LAYER · VISUAL EXPLAINER
Not an art specialist? Say this: Say: “Today the studio is a surface lab. Pick an industrial surface — rust, brick, steel, water. Try a material or process that RECREATES, EXAGGERATES or TRANSFORMS it. The one that works becomes a tool you keep for your own piece.”

See the studio thinking

Think aloud: What changed? What stayed? Which decision had the strongest effect?
