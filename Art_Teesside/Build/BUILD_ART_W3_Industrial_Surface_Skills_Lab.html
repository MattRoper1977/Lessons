I Do

I Do: Three Techniques, Live

Key Idea: Watch the tool decisions, not just the results: pressure, speed, water, order.

👀 Look: industrial surfaces — rust, rivets, texture — become techniques in your hands.

Step 0 of 4

Dry-brush for corrugation

Almost no paint, dragged across — the brush skips and the ridges appear. Speed matters: slow drags smear.

Frottage for brick

Paper over real texture, soft pencil sideways. The wall draws itself — then I work INTO the rubbing.

Layered wash for river

Wet grey wash, dry fully, second darker wash stopping short — the stripe of first-layer that shows through IS the light on the water.

The label

Every strip gets technique + tool + one word verdict: 'works', 'flat', 'again with less water'.

I Do

I Do: Choosing to Recreate, Exaggerate or Transform

Key Idea: The same rust patch, three intentions: copy it faithfully, push it louder, or turn it into something new. Intention decides technique.
Step 0 of 3

Recreate

Faithful rust: stipple, true colours, patient. The test of control.

Exaggerate

Rust turned UP: oranges hotter, textures rougher, the patch grown huge. The test of nerve.

Transform

Rust as a map, a face, a coastline: the surface becomes a starting point. The test of imagination — and Week 4's brief will ask which you choose.
