## Three places I am contradicting the record

### 1. The fifth card is `island`, not `lift`

The card is right and your placement argument is untouched — a fallen island is caused at
cutting and seen at printing, which is exactly why it belongs in a printing fault table.
The **name** is the problem, twice over.

`lift` is step five of the chain, where it names the *correct* action. The sibling's `drag`
fix already uses it that way — "straight down, **lift straight up**". Calling the failure card
`lift` puts one word on both the success and the failure inside a single file, which is the
co-present-contradiction class this estate has spent the session removing.

The sibling calls it `island`. So does its own text: "NO BRIDGE. The **island** dropped out."

### 2. `slip` keeps its name; the record renamed it to `shift`

The physics you recorded is exactly right — the stencil moved between dabs. But the sibling's
card is `slip`, and the *old* GROW W2 key was also `slip`. Keeping it means the key survives
the re-physicsing unchanged and matches the sibling a teacher may hold beside it. `shift` buys
nothing and breaks two alignments.

Carry the sibling's fix verbatim — **"Tape it to the paper, not the desk"** — because it is the
only fault repair in the set that points back at a named chain step.

### 3. The cue under-specifies against the fault directly above it

Recorded: *"Squeeze until it stops dripping — that's enough paint."* That string appears
nowhere in the sibling; *dripping* has zero hits estate-wide. It is newly authored, and it
sets a lower bar than the sibling does: **a sponge that has stopped dripping is still wet
enough to `bleed`**, which is card 2.

The sibling says it twice, and both are better:

> Squeeze the sponge out **on scrap** before you touch the work.
> Squeeze the sponge out **before you start**.

Proposed cue, authored once in the word bank, combining the short form with the location that
makes it checkable:

> **Squeeze the sponge out on scrap before you start.**

Your auditory → visual-tactile decision survives intact — this is still something a pupil sees
and feels rather than hears, and the reason still stands: a pupil cannot hear a roller over a
busy room. It gains a place and a moment, which "stops dripping" lacks.
