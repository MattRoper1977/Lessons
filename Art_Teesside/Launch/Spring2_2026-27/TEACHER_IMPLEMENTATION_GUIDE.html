LAUNCH · Spring 2 Art · Teesworks & Reclaimed Spaces

Teacher implementation guide

Forty minutes means forty minutes: Arrival 3 · Starter 3 · I Do 4 · We Do 3 · I Do 2 3 · We Do 2 4 · Independent 16 · Exit/Influence 4. The overview is untimed.

Pathway grammar

Reading age 9–14 · optimise, qualify evidence and uncertainty.

Least-prompt ladder: wait → self-prompt → visual/source cue → general verbal → specific verbal → model → direct support

Response routes: write/edit · structured verbal · make/lead/demonstrate · compare/evaluate · plan/delegate · revise/adapt

Evidence triad: learner decision → authentic action → critical reasoning

Before each lesson

  1. Read the local risk assessment, equipment list, closed-kit route and evidence target.
  2. Prepare only authorised tools. Count controlled tools out and in. If one is missing, stop work and tell staff; staff manage movement and exits.
  3. Open the lesson locally and test projection, keyboard navigation, Calm mode, Static diagrams and print route.
  4. For W4, approve exact artist and organisation sources and rights before pupils arrive.
  5. Plan who genuinely receives exit Voice and what can change in response.

Lundy live loop

SPACE

Predictability, regulation, pass/return later, safe routes and stable sensory/motor access.

VOICE

A real choice, judgement, mark, demonstration, disagreement or revision.

AUDIENCE

A named adult actually reads, hears or watches the pupil response and names it back.

INFLUENCE

The next material, scaffold, question, setup, access route or decision visibly changes.

Qualification boundary

This lesson can support selected Trinity Arts Award Silver evidence only when authentic practice, experience or leadership exists beyond the interface. A trained adviser must audit the full portfolio; planning or a simulated pitch does not prove delivery or complete a Unit.

Bronze Part D scope and Silver 2A–B planning are particularly guarded: a classroom pitch does not prove authentic skill share or leadership delivery. Keep rights, consent, collaborator contribution and pupil authorship distinct.

Emergency basics

  • Hot-glue burn: cool under cool running water for at least 20 minutes, report/escalate, do not peel stuck material, do not use ice.
  • Wire/cutters: goggles where locally required, offcut down into tray, fold/tape/cap ends, carry wire coiled.
  • Mod Roc: cut strips before wetting, no dry sanding, bowl not sink, never wrap body parts, manage spills.
  • The card-slot route is always available and does not reduce the status of the artistic decision.
