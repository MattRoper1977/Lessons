LAUNCH Art Silver · Teesside Studio Suite Made by Matt

Upgraded build — the top tier, and the first to cover both Silver units in one term. Enhanced with the house system: deep-Teesside-tinted Lundy zones in the decks, plus per-week stamped tickets, a connecting spine (with the Unit 1→Unit 2 handover at Week 5) and a LAUNCH→GOLD ladder in the Evidence Pack — without changing a line of the authored Silver content. Follow the green “Feeds” line to see how the eight weeks connect.
