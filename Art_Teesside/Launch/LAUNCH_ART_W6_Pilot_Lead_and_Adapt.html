CHEESE LAYER · ANIMATED EXPLANATION
Not an art specialist? Say this: Say: “Pilot small before you go big. Try it, watch what actually happens, and ADAPT. Leadership is noticing what isn’t working and changing it — and sharing the roles, not doing everything yourself.”

See the relationship, not just the finished product

Expose planning, uncertainty and revision

Teacher models one complete chain: enquiry, option, risk, evidence, limitation, decision and consequence. The model includes one plausible error and shows when expert support is appropriate.
