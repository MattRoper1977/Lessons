My CV Builder

    Step 1 of 7 · Arrival · 1 minute

    Settle and check in

    What I do now

    1. Choose how you are starting.
    2. Think of one task you did in class, at home or at a club.
    3. Press Next.

    Done when: you have chosen how you are starting.

    How are you starting today? You can pass.

    Step 2 of 7 · Starter · Think

    What makes a skill claim stronger?

    What I do now

    1. Read the skill claim.
    2. Type one thing the learner could show a reader.
    3. Press Reveal answer.

    Done when: you can name a detail a reader could check.

    A learner says “I am careful.” What could they show a reader?

    Step 3 of 7 · We do · One shared choice

    Improve a weak CV line

    What I do now

    1. Read the three lines.
    2. Tap the line with a true action.
    3. Press Check, then say why it is stronger.

    Done when: you can say what makes the line stronger.

    Which line shows careful sorting?

    Step 5 of 7 · Review · Partner check

    Check and improve one line

    What I do now

    1. Share one skill line with a partner or an adult. Keep private details private.
    2. Check the action and one detail.
    3. Change the line in your CV if it can be stronger.

    Done when: your strongest line is ready.

    Step 6 of 7 · Exit and my voice

    What did you improve?

    What I do now

    1. Type the one line you improved, or the detail you added.
    2. Choose how confident you feel.
    3. Say what helped you today.

    Done when: you can explain one change.

    How confident do I feel about writing a CV line?

    Step 7 of 7 · Finish

    Print all my work

    What I do now

    1. Check the list below.
    2. Press Print all my work.
    3. Collect two pages: my lesson record and my CV.

    Done when: both pages are printed, or saved as a PDF.

      No printer here? In the print window, choose “Save as PDF” as the printer. You can print the PDF later, at school or at home.
