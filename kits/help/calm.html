Help: two minute reset

Six calm steps when things feel too much. Take your time.

Help level:

    Word help
    Still stuck?

    Still feeling overwhelmed? Stay in your seat, take one more slow breath, and show your help card so an adult can come quietly.

    Being stuck is normal. Try these three things first.

    1. 1Read the task again.Point to each word as you read.
    2. 2Look at your sheet.Find the box called What you need to know. Look at the picture too.
    3. 3Do the first small bit.Use the sentence starter or the first step.

    Still stuck? Put your hand up or show your help card. While you wait, do the next part that you can do.

    You can show what you know in different ways. Pick one.

    1. AWrite it.Use the sentence starter on your sheet.
    2. BDraw it and label it.Add three labels with key words.
    3. CSay it.Tell an adult your answer and they will write it down.

    Check with your teacher which way is right for this task.
