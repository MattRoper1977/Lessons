CHEESE LAYER · ANIMATED EXPLANATION
Not a history specialist? Say this: Say: “Weigh a source by PROVENANCE (who, when, why), CONTENT (what it says) and CONTEXT (what else was happening). A source can be reliable for one question and useless for another. ‘It’s strong evidence FOR ___, weak FOR ___.’ Utility depends on the question.”
