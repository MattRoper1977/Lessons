STARTER · talk task

Progress is easier to see in a line

Your work sits in date order on the table. With your partner: what changed between the first thing and the last? Technique? Confidence? Size of ideas? Progress hides day-to-day and shows in lines.

Sentence stems:
“Between W1 and now, my ___ changed…”
“The biggest jump was…”
“I couldn’t have made ___ in September because…”

Stuck? Compare your art-battle scrap with your identity piece. The distance between them is eight weeks of evidence — and it’s YOURS.

I DO · teacher explains

Sharing is a structure, not a spotlight

🗣️ The three stems

This is… / I did well… / Next I’ll… — piece, star, direction. Thirty seconds carries all three.

🛣️ Your route

Partner, TA, or camera: all count in full. The Arts Award needs your thinking WITNESSED, not performed.

🎯 A real next step

“Next I’ll…” only counts if you mean it — it seeds next term, so pick the step you actually want.

💬 The audience contract

Eyes on the work, wait to the end, one kind comment, one question if invited. Everyone signs by sitting down.

Misconception: “Presenting means performing to the whole class.” Presenting means your work is seen and your thinking is heard — by ONE person or a camera, fully counted. The route is your choice every single time.
WE DO · together on the board

Sort the moves — the SHARER’s or the AUDIENCE’s?

Holding, stems, pointing, next-step = sharer. Eyes, waiting, kind comment, invited question = audience.

HANDS-ON · the main event

Share it 🎤

Ground rule (60 seconds): the audience contract holds for every sharer identically — the quietest presenter gets the same eyes as the loudest.

Station 1 · LINE & CHOOSE

Work in date order; choose the piece that best shows your PROGRESS — not necessarily the prettiest. Say why to yourself first.

Station 2 · PRESENT

Three stems, your route: partner, TA or video. Pointing along the work while talking is presenting.

Station 3 · NEXT STEP

Log the step you mean. Highlight the piece you’ll build on — next term starts exactly there.

GCSE Stretch: invite one question, answer with a because — “I chose clay because…” is Bronze-level ownership.
