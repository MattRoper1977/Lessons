I Do

I Do: Opening Under Pressure

Key Idea: The assessed opening does three jobs in three sentences: restate the enquiry, state the thesis, signpost the route.

👀 Look: the assessor should know your answer before they reach your evidence.

📖 Three Sentences

Restate — The enquiry in your own words — proves you read it.

Thesis — Your framed verdict — the account's spine.

Route — 'First numbers, then labour, then institutions' — the map.

🧠 Watch me think

Watch the model opening land in 40 seconds: 'This account asks how far migration made Middlesbrough. I argue: decisively — the ore set the stage, but people turned it into a town. The case runs numbers, then labour, then the institutions newcomers built.' Three sentences. Everything after this is delivery.

I Do

I Do: The Close That Lands

Key Idea: The close re-lands the thesis with the evidence now behind it — developed, not repeated; no new material.

📖 Close Moves

Echo — The thesis returns — in stronger words.

Because-now — '…as the census, wage books and minutes have shown.'

Reach — One controlled sentence outward: what it means for Teesside now.

🧠 Watch me think

Model close, 30 seconds: 'So: decisively. The ore waited in the hills for eighty years — it was people, counted in the census, named in the wage books, organised in the minutes, who made a town of it. Teesside has been made by arrival ever since.' Echo, because-now, reach. Pens down.

⭐ Assessed Conditions Card — staff and pupil copy

THE RULE: SUPPORT ACCESS, NEVER CONTENT. A pupil may be helped to get at the task — reading, scribing, timing, seating, breaks. A pupil may not be helped to decide what to say. If in doubt, do not.

This work is completed under supervised conditions: unaided, except for agreed access arrangements. It is then submitted for assessment.

Everyone has the enquiry and their own plan. The table below is about scaffolds only. Access arrangements are separate, are never tiered, and appear beneath it.

TierScaffolds allowedScaffolds not allowed
🤝 SupportedThe opening and close frames; the checklist sheet; lined paperNew content; model sentences; anyone deciding the thesis or what the account argues
👤 StandardThe checklist sheet; lined paperFrames, model paragraphs, word banks of any kind, prompting on what to argue
🚀 StretchThe checklist sheet; lined paperAny content help at all, including model moves

The judgement is the assessment. Building the account and saying what it argues, in your own words, is the thing being assessed. Everything that helps a pupil reach the page is access. Anything that supplies the judgement is not.

Access is never withheld, at any tier. Extra time, a break, a scribe, a reader, a change of seat or room are access arrangements, not scaffolds. A pupil at Stretch has the same right to them as a pupil at Supported. A reader may read the task and their own plan aloud; a reader may not explain them, summarise them, or say which one matters more. A scribe writes the pupil’s words and does not improve them. Where an arrangement is not written down, provide it and record it rather than withhold it.

🛑 STUCK MID-ANSWER? DO NOT STOP — MOVE.

Leave a gap, mark it with a star, and go on to the next unit. An answer with one starred gap scores; an answer abandoned at the gap does not. Come back to the star when the rest is down. This is a strategy, not a concession.
