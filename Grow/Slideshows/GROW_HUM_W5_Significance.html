I Do

I Do: Criteria Plus Perspective

Key Idea: A defensible judgement names its criteria AND its standpoint: significant by which tests, for whom, over what span?

📖 The Full Frame

Criteria — Numbers · depth · duration · memory — declared up front.

Perspective — For whom: steel families, newcomers, the nation.

Timescale — Over what span: the decade, the century, so far.

🧠 Watch me think — the frame assembles

🛠️ Verdict 1 — “By depth, for Teesside’s steel families, over the decade since — the closure ranks first.”

🛠️ Verdict 2 — “By numbers and duration, for the town as a whole, over two centuries — 1850 keeps the crown.”

✅ Answer: two verdicts, zero contradiction. Same facts, different frames — and both frames are DECLARED. That is what makes them arguments rather than opinions.

I Do: The Defensible 'It Depends'

Key Idea: 'It depends' is strong exactly when the dependencies are named, weighed, and closed with a verdict anyway.

📖 Depends, Done Right

Name — 'It depends on criterion and standpoint…'

Weigh — '…for THIS enquiry, depth outweighs numbers because…'

Verdict — '…so, framed that way: the closure ranks first.'

🧠 Watch me think

Lazy: 'it depends, so who can say.' GROW: 'It depends on the test — and our enquiry asks about lived impact HERE, so depth is the right test, so the closure outranks 1850 for this question.' The dependency became the argument. That's the move your assessed piece will need in Week 7.

⚡ The GROW move: significance is not one number. A single total hides the standpoint that produced it. Score twice, then say which frame your enquiry asks for.

“It depends” done right — three steps, never two

1. NAME — “It depends on criterion and standpoint…”
2. WEIGH — “…for THIS enquiry, depth outweighs numbers because…”
3. VERDICT — “…so, framed that way: the closure ranks first.”

Stopping after step 1 is the lazy version. The dependency has to become the argument.

🛑 “IT DEPENDS” FEELS LIKE DODGING THE QUESTION.

It is — if you stop there. “It depends, so who can say.” scores nothing.

Name the dependency, then close it anyway: “our enquiry asks about lived impact HERE, so depth is the right test, so the closure outranks 1850 for this question.”

You still give a verdict. You just say what it is standing on.

❓ FAMOUS OR SIGNIFICANT? THEY ARE NOT THE SAME.

Something can be famous and change nothing. Something can change everything and be barely known.

Fame is only the memory test — one of four. The ironstone find is barely in national textbooks and it built a town of 90,000.
