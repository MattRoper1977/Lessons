STARTER · talk task

How does the atlas already know?

With your partner: you need to find Jamaica and you have 30 seconds. Flicking pages loses. What tools does the atlas give you to WIN — and how do they work?

Sentence stems:
“The index tells you…”
“The key tells you…”
“The grid square means…”

Stuck? Back of the atlas: the INDEX — name, page, grid square. On the map: the KEY — what every symbol means. Look-up beats flick-through every time.

I DO · teacher explains

The world in layers: continent → ocean → country

🌍 7 continents

Africa, Asia, Europe, North America, South America, Oceania, Antarctica — the biggest pieces of the jigsaw.

🌊 5 oceans

Atlantic, Pacific, Indian, Arctic, Southern. The Windrush crossed the Atlantic; the Romans crossed a sea so narrow you can swim it.

📖 Index & key

Index: name → page → grid square. Key: symbol → meaning. Two tools that make any map readable in seconds.

🚩 Your country

Today you locate one country our term connects you to — a Week 4 figure’s homeland, a heritage link, a team. Your link, your choice.

Misconception: “A country and a continent are the same thing.” A continent holds MANY countries. England sits inside Europe; Jamaica sits in the Caribbean, near the Americas. Get the layers right and every map makes sense.
WE DO · together on the board

Sort the names — CONTINENT, OCEAN or COUNTRY?

Seven huge lands = continents. Five giant waters = oceans. A land with its own flag INSIDE a continent = country. Which layer does the name live on?

HANDS-ON · the main event

Master the map 🌍

Ground rule (60 seconds): the atlas index is not cheating — it is the skill. Guess-and-flick is the only wrong method today.

Station 1 · LABEL THE SEVEN

Label all seven continents on the map with the named stickers — then the five oceans. Check a partner’s map: catch one slip kindly.

Station 2 · INDEX RACE

Use the atlas index to locate: Jamaica, Italy, and one country of your choice. Name → page → grid square — say the route as you go.

Station 3 · YOUR COUNTRY

Locate the country YOUR term connects you to — a Week 4 figure’s homeland, a heritage link if you choose to share one, or your team’s. Plant the flag; name its continent and nearest ocean.

GCSE Stretch: use the map KEY: find one physical feature in your country and name the symbol that revealed it.
