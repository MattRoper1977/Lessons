I Do

I Do: Planning the Assessed Account

Key Idea: An assessed account is architecture: an opening that states a thesis, ordered PEE units that build it, a counter-point handled, a close that lands the verdict.

📖 The Blueprint

Thesis opening — Your framed verdict, stated up front.

PEE units — Three, each with source evidence attached.

Counter + close — One objection answered; a close that re-lands the thesis, developed.

🧠 Watch me think

Watch me plan against 'How far did migration make Middlesbrough?': thesis — decisively, though iron set the stage. Unit 1: numbers (census evidence). Unit 2: labour (wage books). Unit 3: community institutions (chapel records). Counter: 'iron, not people?' — answered with Week 3's condition/trigger split. Close: re-land, one register higher.

I Do: Evidence Placement and Provenance Notes

Key Idea: A GROW plan doesn't just list evidence — it notes WHY each source deserves its slot, provenance included.

📖 Slotting Evidence

Fit — Which point does this source actually prove?

Provenance note — One bracket: (census — standardised, no sales motive).

Reserve — One spare source per unit, in case a claim needs reinforcement.

🧠 Watch me think

My census slot gets a bracket: '(official count — undercounts lodgers, still the best numbers we have).' See what that bracket buys? Next week, mid-account, that honesty becomes an evaluation sentence — pre-planned, not improvised under pressure.
