STARTER · talk task

Why do festivals of light make noise?

Diwali fireworks, carnival drums, bells at midnight. With your partner: festivals about LIGHT always bring SOUND. What does the sound add that the light alone can’t do?

Sentence stems:
“Light shows you… sound makes you…”
“Drums make a crowd feel…”
“Silence at a festival would feel…”

Stuck? Light is watched; sound is FELT — in your chest, in your feet. Festivals use sound to make everyone feel it together, at the same moment.

I DO · teacher explains

Sound has levers — learn them, then pull them

🎚️ The levers

Volume (loud/soft), tempo (fast/slow), rhythm (steady/jumpy). Three levers set the mood before a single melody exists.

🎉 Festivals engineer feeling

Carnival drums pull tempo UP for joy; temple bells pull volume DOWN for wonder. Festivals are mood-engineering at crowd scale.

🥁 Explore with reasons

Try each instrument, match sound to feeling — and add the Grow why: “the shaker feels nervous BECAUSE it’s fast and jumpy”.

📓 Closing the log

This page — sound, feeling, reason, clip — closes your Explore log across TWO art forms. Bronze loves breadth; you just banked it.

Misconception: “Music and art are separate subjects.” Same levers, different senses: contrast is volume, pattern is rhythm, pace is tempo. The Arts Award counts every art form — and artists who cross between them see more in both.
WE DO · together on the board

Sort the words — describing the SOUND, or naming the FEELING?

Ears-words describe the noise: booming, jingling, steady, jumpy. Inside-words name what it does to you: joyful, nervous, calm, wonder.

HANDS-ON · the main event

Build a mood 🎶

Ground rule (60 seconds): one sound at a time when listening, together when playing — ear defenders on the side, no comment needed to take them.

Station 1 · EXPLORE WITH REASONS

Each instrument: make the sound, name the levers, match the feeling — with a because. Your matches, your reasons.

Station 2 · RECORD

Record your favourite sound; one line on the feeling it engineers.

Station 3 · LAYER, PLAY & SET THE ORDER

Try two sounds together — drum under bells, shaker under song. What mood did the layer build? Play it to the group if you choose; opt-out stands. Then the group VOTES the running order for the showcase set — the order you choose is the order the school hears.

Bronze Stretch: name the commission: “my layered mood would suit ___ festival because…” — composer thinking.
