ORDER VB-RUN13 ruling H12-1: a demonym or adjectival form of a PLACES entry is
PLACE-class.

Derived, never typed. Every entry in PLACES was taken, the ordinary English
adjectival forms generated from it, and only the forms the live corpus or the
workbook actually contain were kept. 57 places in, 13 distinct forms out.

exactly one bulleted list and nothing else. Everything below the list is prose
without a leading dash, because a prose bullet in a register file registers its

## Measured and deliberately not registered

  "UK" occurs 40 times and is a form of a registered place, but it is an
  abbreviation, not a demonym, and the ruling names demonyms and adjectival forms.
  It is a candidate for PLACES itself, which is a separate ruling. Recorded so the
  omission reads as deliberate.

  "Street's" was produced by deriving from the last word of "Cannon Street". A
  multi-word place takes only its own possessive; deriving from a fragment turns a
  generic noun into a register entry, which would mask a real name. The derivation
  now takes the whole phrase.
