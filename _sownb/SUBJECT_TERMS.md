# Subject terms

Built 2026-09-02 under ORDER VB-RUN14 R2, the PLACES way: grep-derived from the
three SoW workbook cells, never typed. A capitalised pair that resolves to this
register, to HOUSE_LABELS or to PLACES never holds a lesson.

Sources read, and what each gave:

  the 897 workbook cells (verbatimOutcome and strand)
      48 distinct capitalised multi-word terms. 42 already resolve through
      HOUSE_LABELS or PLACES (programme names, award levels, events). One is a
      person (Mary Anning). One is a heading fragment (Apply Topics). Four are
      subject terms and are registered below.
  a spec keyword list (Edexcel 1BI0 / 1SC0 / IGCSE vocabulary)
      NONE EXISTS in this repository. Searched for 1BI0, 1SC0, IGCSE, vocab,
      keyword, glossary. The only hit, _sca1/tables/keywords_sow.csv, is a
      keyword-frequency audit of BUILD Science weeks 3-7 -- 58 lower-case words,
      mostly command verbs (explain, record, describe, evaluate) and generic
      nouns (water, group, plant, energy). That is not a subject vocabulary and
      registering it would make the case patch rewrite ordinary words. Nothing
      was invented to fill the gap.

House case, applied by the R2.3 patch mid-sentence only, never at a sentence
start, never in a heading, never on a NAMED-AFTER row:

- Solar System
- Social Enterprise

  Solar System      -> solar system
  Social Enterprise -> social enterprise

Subject NAMES, registered so the pair resolves, but they keep their capital
like a NAMED-AFTER term because they are the name of a subject, not a term in
one:

- Religious Education
- Religious Studies

NAMED-AFTER, registered and keeping the capital:

- Mary Anning

Measured and deliberately not registered:

  Apply Topics    a workbook column heading fragment, not a term.
  explain, record, describe, evaluate, water, group ...   the keyword audit's
                  verbs and generic nouns; see above.

What this register cannot do, said plainly. The g10 residue across the estate
(Fair Test, Stem Cells, Natural Selection, Spherical Bodies, The Sun, and the
rest) comes from deck text, not from the workbook cells, and the order names
the cells and a spec list as the only sources. So most of the residue stays
residue and is printed per deck in the readback, never resolved by hand. A
spec vocabulary list, when one is added to the repository, extends this file
by the same derivation.

The run-13 hygiene rule holds: exactly the bulleted lists above are register
entries, every word of every bullet is a token, and all prose is unbulleted.
