# Places

The place register for the g10 name gate. A capitalised pair whose tokens
include one of these words is a PLACE, never a person. Built 2026-09-02
(ORDER VB-RUN11F, NAMES) by `grep` of the live lesson corpus (pupil-facing
text, scripts, styles and SVG removed) and of every workbook cell on the new
spine, with the pair regex the gate itself uses. Only pairs that name a real
place are listed; phrases that merely contain a place-shaped word ("Middle
Pack", "Supported Point", "Geography Bridge") are not, and stay ROLE-LABEL or
UNRESOLVED on their own merits.

A place never holds a lesson: this list is estate-wide, not per deck, and a
deck is never cited here. Single-word entries are the towns and regions the
corpus names on their own; every word of every bullet becomes a place token.

## Not places (recorded so nobody re-adds them)

- "Bonfire Night" is an event, not a place; a deck that names it in a
  capitalised pair stays UNRESOLVED and needs its own wording, not this list.
- "Historic England", "Natural England", "Science Museum", "Jewish Museum",
  "River Trust" are organisations. They resolve through their place token
  ("England", "Museum" is not one) only where one is present; that is
  accepted, since none of them is a person.
