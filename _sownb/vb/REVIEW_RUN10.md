## 1 · BUILD Humanities W5 — “Same and different”

- **Trace:** `'BUILD Weekly - Autumn'!C50` — *Show respect for what makes us same/different (Black History Month).*
- **We-do type:** commit-and-reveal. Everyone picks same or different on a card, holds it face down, and the whole room turns over at once.
- **Reads at:** FK 2.94, inside the BUILD band 1.0–4.0.
- **Two diagrams:** choose → hold → show; and the three things you can say to somebody who chose the other card.
- **The one thing worth looking at:** the three sentence starters on the wall card. “What made you choose that?” · “That is a good reason.” · “Mine is different. Here is why.” That is the whole lesson in three lines, and a pupil can read one off the wall rather than invent it.

## 2 · GROW Humanities W5 — “Was it significant?”

- **Trace:** `'GROW Weekly - Autumn'!C50` — *Evaluate the significance of a person or event.*
- **We-do type:** spot-the-error. Three judgement sentences; two fail a test and one holds.
- **Reads at:** FK 6.44 — the highest of the sixteen, and inside the GROW band 3.0–7.0. Evaluative reasoning is genuinely harder language; it is recorded rather than smoothed.
- **The one thing worth looking at:** the three tests are *how many, how long, how different*, and fame is deliberately not one of them. The error the class hunts is a sentence that sounds confident and offers fame.
