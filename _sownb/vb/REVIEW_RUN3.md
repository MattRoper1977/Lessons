## BUILD Humanities W14 — VERIFIED, with the instrument corrected
This is the one that reds, and it is worth two minutes.
g18 called it thin: 1,616 pupil words against a floor of 1,638. But that floor is a single global number computed from the **GROW Science** pack and applied to every family, even though the tool's own description calls it "destination-relative". Measured against its own family, 1,616 is *above* five of its six live BUILD Humanities neighbours (1,517–1,671) and above the family's own p25 of 1,555.
So: not thin, not repaired, not reworded. The finding is against the instrument. g11 also showed red purely because it is wired to inherit g18's floor verdict; its own similarity score was 0.078 against a ceiling of 0.184.
