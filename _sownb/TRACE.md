## Thin-cell discipline

- `C143`, `C45`, `C170`, `C171` and `C44` do not themselves name an assessed criterion. Activities above make evidence usable without manufacturing one.
- LAUNCH Science uses no diagnosis, medical advice or personal disclosure. The research source is teacher-approved and local to delivery; the HTML opens no external runtime route.
- Community project events or records that do not exist remain marked missing. No partner action, consent, visit or outcome is simulated.
