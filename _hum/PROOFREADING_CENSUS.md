The safeguard, where present, is worded freely rather than to a fixed line — for

**R5 needs a ruling.** It says RE decks "keep their safeguard lines intact on every
deck ('not worship', 'nobody asked to share a belief')". Measured: the
belief safeguard exists on 13 of 22 and in varied wording; the "not worship" line
exists on **none**. Four decks mention worship only as descriptive content
("place of worship"). So there is nothing to keep intact on 9 decks, and nothing at
all to keep intact for "not worship". Keeping vs adding is your call.
