# PANEL COMPARISON — the science exemplar beside the transplant
ORDER HUM-T · stage B · STOP-T2

## The two are shaped differently

In science the loop is **two objects**: a status panel (`div.lundy`) and, as its
siblings in the same stage, the controls that drive it. The transplant packages the
status and the triad as **one** panel, so a stage cannot end up with the indicator
but no way to act on it — which is the state 607 Humanities stages were in.

## What it shows

* The triad now sits on a We Do stage, which science leaves bare. That is the
  increase ruling 1 authorises, not a copy of the exemplar.
* The response point quotes THIS stage's own task, in the deck's own words.
* On an RE deck it is phrased "what the source says", never a belief (ruling 4).
* The Exit influence line quotes the deck's OWN next-step sentence where it has one.
* No transplanted panel repeats the science exemplar's wording (row 37).
