Portfolio Studio ceiling

Normal maximum: tutor time may help place an item into the approved system or assessor queue while preserving its current state. It does not produce centre verification.

Approved language

  • “Candidate evidence queued against ___.”
  • “Evidence state reviewed; remaining outcome is ___.”
  • “Portfolio evidence captured; assessor review pending.”
  • “No candidate item today.”
Avoid: “qualification banked”, “UAS grade”, “unit certified”, “passed” or “signed off” — “unit certified”, “passed” and “signed off” only when the authorised process has genuinely reached that state; “qualification banked” and “UAS grade” never (AQA UAS carries no grade and no level; see quality/toolkits/CLAIMS_REGISTER.md).
