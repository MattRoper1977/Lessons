# Specimen acceptance

> **STATUS: PROPOSED — the specimen phase is not run by this pass and no trial is authorised yet.** It is chained in `HANDOVER.md` behind Pass LL-J’s delivery. *(Status line inserted by Pass LL-AS1, 2026-08-05; disclosed in the PR.)*

The three-specimen phase passes only when all three lessons meet the following.

## Learning

- staff identify precise evidence;
- at least one teaching/support response is observed;
- pupils receive a real reattempt, transfer or later opportunity;
- the evidence capture is not the end of the formative process.

## Lundy

- the matter is genuinely open;
- communication routes are accessible;
- Audience has power/responsibility;
- pupils receive report-back;
- no pupil is penalised for private/pass;
- GROW/LAUNCH receive no adult receipt mark.

## Portfolio

- criterion/product mapping is exact or marked not yet mapped;
- authorship/support is honest;
- no photo is used by default;
- tutor time does not create missing evidence;
- evidence state is not raised without authority;
- learner amendment/addition remains possible.

## Tutor time

- optional day-close remains optional;
- one-item maximum is workable;
- “no upload” is genuinely used when appropriate;
- temporary copies are cleared;
- no backlog is created.

## Safety and access

- no disclosure enters ordinary evidence;
- no unnecessary face/personal document;
- ordinary adjustments remain;
- staff workload is sustainable;
- pupils report that evidence still feels like their work.

## LAUNCH authoring gate

No pupil-facing warrant wording is authored into live lessons until Matt has read the specimen findings and explicitly approves the wording.
