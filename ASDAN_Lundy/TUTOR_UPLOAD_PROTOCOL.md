# Tutor upload protocol

> **STATUS: PROPOSED — not in use; run deferred behind the recorded two-part trigger** (`_close/OPEN_ITEMS.md` #24: the late-September three-week check recorded AND Pass LL-J delivered and read). Nothing here is authorised for use with pupils. *(Status line inserted by Pass LL-AS1, 2026-08-05; disclosed in the PR.)*

## Before the slot

- use an approved school device and approved portfolio/evidence system;
- receive the minimal subject handoff;
- know who owns the assessment decision;
- prepare private/pass/no-upload routes.

## During the slot

1. regulate first;
2. optional day-close remains optional and unrecorded;
3. maximum one existing item;
4. pupil chooses and explains;
5. staff may add only exact pupil words and supplied metadata;
6. check necessity, safety, permission, authorship, criterion and state;
7. place at current state or return.

## Upload/file naming

Use the centre's approved convention. A useful pattern is:

`approvedID_date_strand_lesson_item_currentState`

Do not put diagnoses, behaviour, family circumstances or safeguarding information in file names.

## After placement

- show the pupil where it is and what state it has;
- identify the next owner;
- clear temporary downloads, camera roll and clipboard copies;
- destroy the temporary queue under local procedure.

## Role boundary

A tutor may also be an assessor, but the actions must remain distinguishable. “I uploaded it” is not “I assessed it.”

## Errors

- Wrong pupil/item: stop, restrict access, follow local data incident process.
- Disclosure: stop ordinary workflow; use DSL procedure.
- Missing criterion: “not yet mapped”; return to subject.
- Missing authentic action/audience: plan a future opportunity; do not reconstruct.
- Duplicate: keep the strongest necessary item and remove the duplicate through approved process.
