# GROW_SCI_A2_W01 · Progress Schools staff card

**Scheme objective:** Explain day and night and the apparent movement of the Sun.

**Run it without a worksheet:** Display the poster and name the learning goal. Read one source chunk on the shared screen; learners find the three answers by speech, pointing, signing, symbol/AAC, drawing or writing. Use Read it back (?) on one sentence; let the pupil correct or extend an answer. Use the reading ruler or device text-to-speech when helpful. Staff device or projector only. An adult fades prompts as soon as the pupil can proceed.

**Four pupil-voice answers this topic enables:**
1. Learning: “I am learning to explain day and night with a rotating Earth.”
2. Feedback: “I was asked to check the source detail for: When does a place have day?”
3. Improvement: “I will add or correct the detail I found for: Why does the Sun seem to move?”
4. Success: “I can answer all three questions and use a source detail for the scenario.”

**Expected source anchors (not a script to give away):**
- What does Earth do? — Earth turns on its axis.
- When does a place have day? — A place has day when it faces the Sun.
- Why does the Sun seem to move? — The Sun seems to move because Earth turns.

**Accepted response routes:** verbal; demonstrated/pointed; signed; symbol/AAC; drawn; written. Record the route and prompt actually used in the existing record.

**Full feedback code set in this activity:**
- **VF:** Adult gives spoken feedback on one source-linked answer.
- **WS:** Pupil works with the adult's reading or choice support; record the support used.
- **I:** Pupil locates a source detail or answers without a content prompt.
- **NS+:** Ask for one specific next step: find the source line for “Why does the Sun seem to move?” and add its detail.
- **E:** E: evidence captured on {EVIDENCE_APP}
- **R:** Next lesson, receive and attend to the pupil's response to NS+ before marking the loop closed.
- **//:** Mark the exact point in the existing response where the pupil self-edits.
- **?:** Use Read it back; pupil reads, hears or indicates the highlighted sentence and checks their claim.

**Loop:** NS+ → pupil responds in their own mode → adult listens/looks and acts as Audience → R, normally revisited next lesson. A feedback mark alone is not R.

**Pathway expectation:** Pathways: high support, VF/WS · Build: mix of WS and I, can explain feedback · Grow: mostly independent, self-editing, NS and R · Launch: independent, self-assessing.

**Possible EHCP broad-area tag if an actual relevant behaviour is observed:** communication & interaction; cognition & learning. An observable example is locating a source line for “When does a place have day?” and communicating its meaning with the support level noted. This is not an invented outcome or proof of one.

**TA prompts, least first (stop and fade when the pupil acts):**
1. “Which line might help with the first question?”
2. “Where does the page use the word Sun?”
3. “Can you point to the sentence that could answer ‘Why does the Sun seem to move?’?”

**Stretch:** How would a person on the opposite side of the globe describe the same moment?

**Way in:** Point to the marked place and turn the globe slowly.

**Image brief:** A lamp representing the Sun and a globe with one marked place, with a rotation arrow. Add only a licensed or original image and the matching Widgit symbols if the poster is landed.
