# GROW_RE_A2_W04 · Progress Schools staff card

**Scheme objective:** Discuss a big question about identity respectfully and give a reason for a viewpoint.

**Run it without a worksheet:** Display the poster and name the learning goal. Read one source chunk on the shared screen; learners find the three answers by speech, pointing, signing, symbol/AAC, drawing or writing. Use Read it back (?) on one sentence; let the pupil correct or extend an answer. Use the reading ruler or device text-to-speech when helpful. Staff device (school tablet) or projector only. An adult fades prompts as soon as the pupil can proceed. In RE, report what the named religious and non-religious sources say; do not ask pupils what they believe.

**Four pupil-voice answers this topic enables:**
1. Learning: “I am learning to discuss a source-based question about identity respectfully.”
2. Feedback: “I was asked to check the source detail for: What does Humanists UK describe?”
3. Improvement: “I will add or correct the detail I found for: What makes a respectful answer?”
4. Success: “I can answer all three questions and use a source detail for the scenario.”

**Expected source anchors (not a script to give away):**
- What can shape a group identity? — A community can share practices or values.
- What does Humanists UK describe? — Humanists UK describes non-religious values and community.
- What makes a respectful answer? — A respectful answer says “the source says” and gives a reason.

**Accepted response routes:** verbal; demonstrated/pointed; signed; symbol/AAC; drawn; written. Record the route and prompt actually used in the existing record.

**Full feedback code set in this activity:**
- **VF:** Adult gives spoken feedback on one source-linked answer.
- **WS:** Pupil works with the adult's reading or choice support; record the support used.
- **I:** Pupil locates a source detail or answers without a content prompt.
- **NS+:** Ask for one specific next step: find the source line for “What makes a respectful answer?” and add its detail.
- **E:** E: evidence captured on Cypher
- **R:** Next lesson, receive and attend to the pupil's response to NS+ before marking the loop closed.
- **//:** Mark the exact point in the existing response where the pupil self-edits.
- **?:** Use Read it back; pupil reads, hears or indicates the highlighted sentence and checks their claim.

**Loop:** NS+ → pupil responds in their own mode → adult listens/looks and acts as Audience → R, normally revisited next lesson. A feedback mark alone is not R.

**Pathway expectation:** Pathways: high support, VF/WS · Build: mix of WS and I, can explain feedback · Grow: mostly independent, self-editing, NS and R · Launch: independent, self-assessing.

**Possible EHCP broad-area tag if an actual relevant behaviour is observed:** communication & interaction; cognition & learning. An observable example is locating a source line for “What does Humanists UK describe?” and communicating its meaning with the support level noted. This is not an invented outcome or proof of one.

**TA prompts, least first (stop and fade when the pupil acts):**
1. “Which line might help with the first question?”
2. “Where does the page use the word worldview?”
3. “Can you point to the sentence that could answer ‘What makes a respectful answer?’?”

**Stretch:** What does a source tell us about an organisation, and what can it not tell us about every member?

**Way in:** Pick one named source and complete “According to…”.

**Image brief:** Two named source cards with different ways of describing community and no personal-response boxes. Add only a licensed or original image and the matching Widgit symbols if the poster is landed.
