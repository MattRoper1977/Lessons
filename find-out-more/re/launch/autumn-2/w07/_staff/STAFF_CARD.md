# LAUNCH_RE_A2_W07 · Progress Schools staff card

**Scheme objective:** Use evidence to show understanding of beliefs, practices and their meanings across two worldviews.

**Run it without a worksheet:** Display the poster and name the learning goal. Read one source chunk on the shared screen; learners find the three answers by speech, pointing, signing, symbol/AAC, drawing or writing. Use Read it back (?) on one sentence; let the pupil correct or extend an answer. Use the reading ruler or device text-to-speech when helpful. Staff device (school tablet) or projector only. An adult fades prompts as soon as the pupil can proceed. Use this source reading before or after the formal check; permitted access arrangements govern assessed work. In RE, report what the named religious and non-religious sources say; do not ask pupils what they believe.

**Four pupil-voice answers this topic enables:**
1. Learning: “I am learning to show understanding of practices and meanings from sources.”
2. Feedback: “I was asked to check the source detail for: What is one non-religious ceremony?”
3. Improvement: “I will add or correct the detail I found for: What must a meaning claim include?”
4. Success: “I can answer all three questions and use a source detail for the scenario.”

**Expected source anchors (not a script to give away):**
- What is one named Christian practice? — A church service can include prayer.
- What is one non-religious ceremony? — Humanists UK describes non-religious naming ceremonies.
- What must a meaning claim include? — A meaning claim names its source.

**Accepted response routes:** verbal; demonstrated/pointed; signed; symbol/AAC; drawn; written. Record the route and prompt actually used in the existing record.

**Full feedback code set in this activity:**
- **VF:** Adult gives spoken feedback on one source-linked answer.
- **WS:** Pupil works with the adult's reading or choice support; record the support used.
- **I:** Pupil locates a source detail or answers without a content prompt.
- **NS+:** Ask for one specific next step: find the source line for “What must a meaning claim include?” and add its detail.
- **E:** E: evidence captured on Cypher
- **R:** Next lesson, receive and attend to the pupil's response to NS+ before marking the loop closed.
- **//:** Mark the exact point in the existing response where the pupil self-edits.
- **?:** Use Read it back; pupil reads, hears or indicates the highlighted sentence and checks their claim.

**Loop:** NS+ → pupil responds in their own mode → adult listens/looks and acts as Audience → R, normally revisited next lesson. A feedback mark alone is not R.

**Pathway expectation:** Pathways: high support, VF/WS · Build: mix of WS and I, can explain feedback · Grow: mostly independent, self-editing, NS and R · Launch: independent, self-assessing.

**Possible EHCP broad-area tag if an actual relevant behaviour is observed:** communication & interaction; cognition & learning. An observable example is locating a source line for “What is one non-religious ceremony?” and communicating its meaning with the support level noted. This is not an invented outcome or proof of one.

**TA prompts, least first (stop and fade when the pupil acts):**
1. “Which line might help with the first question?”
2. “Where does the page use the word meaning?”
3. “Can you point to the sentence that could answer ‘What must a meaning claim include?’?”

**Stretch:** What is the strongest one-sentence limit on a two-source comparison?

**Way in:** Name the practice, then look for its meaning in the matching source.

**Image brief:** A source pair with a practice label, meaning note and careful comparison arrow. Add only a licensed or original image and the matching Widgit symbols if the poster is landed.
