## What this does

Adds the **ASDAN-specific clauses only** — the decision procedure for accepting or refusing a
proposed ASDAN Lundy artefact where the science gates need an ASDAN reading.

## What this does not do

It authorises nothing (**R-SEMH01**); it grades nothing; it defines no closure (Gate 1 is the
whole-school account, 74c33ae); no repository change closes a centre action.

---

## Gate A1 — the six evidence states are used exactly, and tutor time has a ceiling

Evidence status uses `quality/toolkits/CLAIMS_REGISTER.md`'s six states **exactly** — no new
state, no synonym, no per-artefact variant. An artefact fails if it invents a state, displays
two states for one item, or lets any tutor-time surface move an item past **candidate evidence
queued for authorised review**. States 4–6 are centre and awarding-body actions; a commit, a
lesson ending or a studio session asserts none of them.

## Gate A2 — the two lanes never merge

Any ASDAN tutor-time artefact keeps the **day-close read** (records nothing, has no outcomes)
and the **portfolio curation** (one existing item, four equal end states) as separate lanes.
An artefact fails if the read lane acquires an outcome, a record or a required status — or if
the curation lane starts issuing next steps. *"Returned for one authentic addition"* is legal
**only** as a curation-lane verdict about an item (the §3.7 ruling); as a read-lane outcome it
is the failing answer Gate 6 forbids.

## Gate A3 — criterion honesty: "not yet mapped", never a guess

Where a unit, challenge or criterion mapping is unknown, the honest value is **"not yet
mapped"** — never a plausible code. The estate's precedent is OPEN_ITEMS #8 (the
descriptive-week mapping left STILL-UNDETERMINED rather than guessed). An artefact that
auto-fills, defaults or infers a criterion code fails.

## Gate A4 — authorship and support are recorded honestly, and never as a ladder of shame

Any authorship/support wording follows the estate's account: support is recorded so the next
adult knows where to start, a reasonable adjustment is access and never fades, and no code
ranks pupils. The vocabulary is **queued for the WT–DS reconciliation** (`HANDOVER.md` queue
18) — an ASDAN artefact that ships a second support vocabulary to staff before that
reconciliation fails, which is the R-H08 mis-training hazard arriving in a new domain.

## Gate A5 — nothing pupil-facing from a third-party pack before Matt has read it

Gate 7's ASDAN instance, stated because this pass executed it: `PUPIL_EVIDENCE_CARD.html` and
`PUPIL_PATHWAY_CARDS.html` were excluded from the branch entirely (evaluated-not-adopted,
OPEN_ITEMS; re-entry chained to the OPEN_ITEMS #24 trigger **plus Matt's read**). Any future
pack's pupil-facing artefact takes the same route.
