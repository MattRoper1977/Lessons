## ASDAN-specific: where a disclosure or a data hazard actually turns up

Named because these are the moments the general rule is hardest to apply, not new rules.

| moment | the risk | what happens |
|---|---|---|
| **The curation conversation** | a pupil explaining "what this shows" says something concerning | the studio task stops; the disclosure never enters the caption, the queue, the handoff or the portfolio; DSL route |
| **A witness statement or learner confirmation** | free text drifts from the product's authenticity into the pupil's circumstances | the statement records the work and the support, never the situation; anything concerning goes by the DSL route, not onto the sheet |
| **A performance or audience event (LAUNCH/PEQ)** | recordings capture other pupils, staff or a real audience without a filed consent | work not faces unless consent is filed; an audience member is never identified; the sheet or plan is used where it is stronger than a recording |
| **Real-world PfA artefacts** | bank, benefit, health, tenancy or identity documents used as "real" evidence | never uploaded, never photographed; a produced exemplar replaces the real document |
| **The temporary upload queue** | a session-only surface quietly becomes a store | the queue is cleared at session close; nothing in it survives the day; if it persists anywhere it has changed species and the artefact fails |
| **A tutor handoff note** | the handoff becomes a second record of the pupil rather than of the item | the handoff names the item, criterion, state and owner — never behaviour, circumstances or support needs |

## What no ASDAN evidence artefact may contain

Everything in the Science list (`quality/LUNDY_SCIENCE_DATA_FIREWALL.md` §"What no Science
evidence artefact may contain") applies verbatim — disclosures, diagnoses, full names where a
learner reference serves, unapproved captures, initials-treated-as-anonymous. ASDAN adds:

- a real personal document (financial, medical, identity, tenancy) or a legible image of one;
- an adult-composed reflection presented as the pupil's meaning — exact words or nothing;
- an invented audience, response or performance record.

## What this file does not decide

Deliberately out of scope, because deciding them here would assert what has not been settled:

- the approved platform, folder structure, learner-reference convention or filename pattern;
- retention and deletion schedules — proposed defaults live in `DATA_GOVERNANCE.md`,
  **PENDING-LOCAL-APPROVAL (DPO/SLT)**;
- which roles may capture, review, upload, correct or delete;
- photo/video permission rules and the device estate;
- **whether any upload workflow is authorised at all.** The Portfolio Studio's run is deferred
  behind the recorded two-part trigger (`_close/OPEN_ITEMS.md` #24); nothing here advances it.

## Standing rule

**A disclosure copied into an evidence export is a safeguarding failure wearing an evidence
costume** (`DATA_GOVERNANCE.md`). A portfolio that will one day leave the school — to an
assessor, an IQA sample, an awarding body — is the *easiest* export to forget, which is the
whole reason the ASDAN boundary is written as *leaves the workflow entirely* rather than *is
handled carefully*.
