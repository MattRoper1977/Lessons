# SEMH pedagogy house standard

> **Source: SEMH audit 2026-08-04, adapted.** Derived from audit document 07
> (`07_SEMH_PEDAGOGY_HOUSE_STANDARD.md`). Adapted to the estate's standing rulings,
> which the auditor could not see — every adaptation is marked **[estate]**.

## Purpose

A lesson is SEMH-aware when it preserves dignity, regulation and access **while
maintaining meaningful curriculum demand**. Calm design is not the same as low challenge.

This standard describes what is already true of the best lessons here. It is a
description to hold new work to, not a redesign brief. The estate's pedagogy was
judged strong by the 2026-08-04 audit and is **not** in scope for change.

## Twelve non-negotiables

### 1. Predictable entry
Show only what pupils need first: where they are, what they will do, how long the first
phase lasts, what success looks like and how to ask for help.

### 2. Multiple safe participation routes
Verbal, written, pointing/selecting, practical and private routes where appropriate.
"Pass" must be genuine and must not be followed by public pressure.
**[estate]** The standard null, where a gate needs one, is
*"I can't defend either yet — I need ___"*, not *"I'm not sure"* — it converts a null
into an observable pupil action. Where a writing line already carries its own null
(*"Pass is always allowed"*), do not author a competing one.

### 3. Explicit instruction
Model the thinking, not just the finished product. Concise I Do, guided We Do, planned
independent release.
**[estate]** The **I Do** convention is deliberate: no print counterpart, because it is
modelled live. Its absence from a print pack is not a defect.

### 4. Safe checking for understanding
Gather evidence from all pupils without requiring personal disclosure. Mini-whiteboards,
private response, selection, fictional scenarios. Cold calling is inappropriate when the
answer could reveal trauma, abuse, sexuality, family history or current risk.

### 5. One core route
The current task, success condition and next action should be immediately visible.
Optional depth, qualification notes, evidence administration and teacher controls should
not compete with the pupil route.

### 6. Authentic autonomy
Meaningful choices that change material, method, audience, sequence, role or solution.
Cosmetic choices alone do not create agency.

### 7. Least prompt first
Wait time → self-prompt → visual cue → general verbal → specific verbal → model →
direct support, in that order where safe. Record the highest prompt and plan to remove it.
**[estate]** Any record built for this is **paper/TA-side, never device storage, never
aggregated**. See `INDEPENDENT_WORK_RUBRIC.md` and the standing ruling that the
ring/mark is never counted or aggregated.

### 8. Regulation without avoidance
Allow movement, reduced sensory load, breaks, pass routes and alternative evidence. Do
not remove the central concept simply because support is needed.

### 9. Private attainment, public ambition
Task routes may be visible; fixed ability or predicted-grade identities should not be.
Pupils can move routes and reach for depth.
**[estate]** Tier names are **Supported / Standard / Stretch**. Route identity must be
conveyed by **icon AND word — never colour alone**.

### 10. Feedback must be used
Feedback should lead to a visible learner action: correct, improve, explain, choose,
test, redraft or apply.
**[estate]** The adult is a **witness and next-step-giver, never a signatory or a
receipt-mark**. A wrong prediction is never coded as failure.

### 11. Evidence thinking, not attendance
The evidence triad: (1) learner decision or prediction; (2) observable action, product or
result; (3) learner explanation, evaluation or next step.
**[estate]** Paper is primary — not a fallback. **No pupil words are stored on the
device.**

### 12. Truthful readiness
Every resource should be able to state which of these it is:
pedagogically drafted · fact/source verified · locally adapted · delivery ready ·
registration verified · assessment ready · print/offline/browser verified.
See `DELIVERY_READINESS_CHECKLIST.md`.
**[estate]** Browser and print states may only be claimed from a browser-capable
environment. This estate's agent containers cannot produce browser evidence; a skipped
gate is **UNVERIFIED**, never passed.

## Red flags

- adult completes the thinking while the learner completes the sheet;
- stretch means "write more";
- personal disclosure is necessary to access the task;
- a public tier becomes a permanent pupil identity;
- an interactive button supplies the answer before the pupil commits;
- a photograph is accepted as proof of understanding;
- accreditation, grade or legal claims have no source date;
- too many controls make navigation the main demand;
- "anonymous" safeguarding content is not reliably reviewed by staff;
- a technical pass is treated as proof that the lesson wording is correct.

**[estate] Two more, earned here:**

- **a surface duplicating a fact that could be derived from another surface.** Two
  authored copies of one truth will diverge. Where a display can be computed from a
  configured value, compute it.
- **a count in prose that no script re-derives.** Four such counts went stale in a
  single pass, every one correct when written.

## Lesson gate

Before teaching, a responsible adult should be able to answer:

1. What exactly must pupils learn?
2. What misconception or barrier is anticipated?
3. What does the model make visible?
4. What will every pupil think/do before any reveal?
5. How will support be faded?
6. What is the independent product or decision?
7. What makes Stretch cognitively harder?
8. What evidence will show authorship?
9. What personal/safeguarding risk is avoided?
10. What claim/source/qualification/readiness item remains open?

## What this standard does not do

It does not authorise redesigning lessons. It does not override `REGISTER.md`,
`HANDOVER.md` or `_close/OPEN_ITEMS.md`. Where it disagrees with those, **they win.**
