# Pathway challenge map — BUILD / GROW / LAUNCH

> **Source: SEMH audit 2026-08-04, adapted.** Companion to
> `INDEPENDENT_WORK_RUBRIC.md`. This map describes the cognitive demand each pathway is
> already designed to carry. The audit judged the estate's challenge progression strong;
> this map exists so new work matches it, not so existing work is rewritten.

## The demand ladder

| | BUILD | GROW | LAUNCH |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Core verb** | identify · select · sequence · demonstrate | explain · compare · apply · control | evaluate · synthesise · transfer · lead |
| **Evidence of thinking** | one relevant reason for a choice | a justification weighed against evidence | a limitation or trade-off named and handled |
| **Handling error** | recognise a mistake, make one correction | monitor and adjust mid-task | anticipate failure and redesign (pre-mortem) |
| **Authentic consequence** | a completed product | a product meeting a constraint | a piloted outcome adapted after real feedback |
| **A strong stretch** | transfer ONE idea, or explain why the choice works | introduce a constraint, counterexample, alternative interpretation or validity judgement | lead, delegate, pilot, adapt, and review impact |

## What does NOT raise challenge

More writing · more boxes · finishing faster · a harder label · a longer word list ·
an extra card · a second peer to ask.

The audit's phrasing, adopted: *"The weakest tiering simply adds more writing, more cards
or a harder label. That is nominal differentiation, not deeper learning."*

## Access is not scaffolding

**Reduced scaffolding targets the thinking, not access.** LAUNCH retains reading support,
navigation support, sensory regulation, scribing and separate-room arrangements while
content prompts are withdrawn. Removing an access arrangement in the name of challenge is
a defect, not a stretch.

This distinction is load-bearing in the assessed files, where the Conditions Card
guarantees specific access arrangements. A tier offer that withdraws one of those is not
authorised by the Card.

## Guardrail

Pathway demand is **not** a grade prediction, and a pathway name is **not** a pupil
identity. Pupils move between routes. Attainment judgement stays private; task demand is
public. See `SEMH_PEDAGOGY_STANDARD.md` §9.
