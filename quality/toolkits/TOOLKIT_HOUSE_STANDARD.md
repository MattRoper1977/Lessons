# Toolkit house standard — A to H

**STATUS: draft · OWNER: Matt · Pass TK-1, 2026-08-04**

Source: the 2026-08-04 SEMH Resource Toolkits audit, document 19, adapted. Corrections the auditor could not
have known are marked **[estate]**.

**Scope: the support/toolkit layer only** — hubs, schemes of work, planners, evidence packs, staff/QA tools,
SEND tools, safety cards, knowledge organisers, START_HEREs, primary hubs. **Lesson slideshows are out of
scope** and are governed by `quality/SEMH_PEDAGOGY_STANDARD.md`.

**The standard is a floor, not a template.** A toolkit meets it by being honest about eight things. It does not
meet it by looking like another toolkit. **[estate]** The audit is explicit that visual and pedagogical
uniformity would destroy the estate's strongest property; nothing in A–H may be read as licence to impose it.

---

## A · Identity and status

Every toolkit displays, in one strip:

- title and a plain-language purpose;
- intended users (pupil-facing / staff-facing / both — **and which**);
- owner;
- version;
- last reviewed · next review;
- readiness state, from the seven in `quality/DELIVERY_READINESS_CHECKLIST.md` — **not a new vocabulary**;
- awarding-body source and version where a qualification claim is made;
- supersedes / superseded-by, where either applies.

The strip's field list and its pilot are in `METADATA_SCHEMA.md`. **[estate]** Estate-wide rollout is a recorded
debt, not this pass — a metadata strip applied at scale to 500 files by a session that has not read them is a
worse artefact than no strip.

## B · Use-now card

- what it helps the user do;
- **what it does not do** — the field the audit found missing most often, and the one that prevents overclaim;
- preparation and materials;
- realistic use time;
- risks and the local approvals required before use;
- what data it captures, if any;
- expected output;
- available alternatives: easy-read, large-print, offline.

## C · SEMH pedagogy

**Defers entirely to `quality/SEMH_PEDAGOGY_STANDARD.md`.** **[estate]** That file is the estate's own, ruled by
Matt, and restating its twelve non-negotiables here would create a twin that diverges. The audit's C-section
adds nothing it does not already contain.

One clause belongs here because it is toolkit-specific: **a support tool must not manufacture an evidence
requirement the awarding body does not impose.** The estate has done this three times, most recently a
ten-hour window on Communication that the ASDAN spec does not contain (**R-K04**).

## D · Accessibility

**Defers to `ACCESSIBILITY_CONTRACT.md`** in this directory, which carries the measured baseline. Summary:
WCAG 2.2 AA for anything interactive; 24px minimum target and 44px preferred for frequent controls; reduced
motion honoured; meaning never carried by colour or symbol alone; print body text normally **11.5–12pt or
larger**, with a large-print route where the base cannot move; **no criterion may be handwriting-dependent**.

## E · Evidence and awards

- the exact criterion, outcome, code and version — never a friendly label standing in for a code;
- **programme versus qualification**, stated: a non-regulated ASDAN Short Course is not a qualification, and a
  PEQ unit is;
- candidate registration status and centre status;
- natural evidence before manufactured evidence;
- the decision / action / explanation triad, so a portfolio shows learning rather than attendance;
- the support or prompt record, kept separate from the outcome — see `quality/DESIGN_prompt_record.md`;
- assessor / IQA / adviser state;
- **no grade or certificate claim before the award exists.**

**[estate] Two hard prohibitions.** No hours threshold at any level, for any award. No Gold-facing Arts Award
progression claim — the adviser is trained to Silver.

## F · Safety and safeguarding

- a resource is never a substitute for local policy or an approved risk assessment;
- competence and supervision named;
- **every safety, medical or legal claim carries a source, a checked date and a review date** — see
  `SAFETY_CONTENT_GATE.md`;
- stop conditions and the emergency route;
- a safeguarding firewall beside free text — see `quality/SAFEGUARDING_CONTENT_GATE.md` and
  `DATA_GOVERNANCE.md`;
- no disclosure content in ordinary evidence.

## G · Data

**Defers to `DATA_GOVERNANCE.md`.** Summary: purpose and minimum data; approved device and location; retention
and deletion; export handling; no personal data by default; initials and pseudonyms still treated as
potentially identifying; no unapproved BYOD.

**[estate]** Two settled positions the standard must not reopen: class lists on school-owned machines are
accepted, and no storage key is renamed or migrated (**R-B01**, **R-B02**).

## H · Acceptance

Before a toolkit moves to `delivery-ready` or beyond:

- content owner review;
- accessibility checks against `ACCESSIBILITY_CONTRACT.md`;
- offline, link and print checks — **[estate]** print and offline checks cannot be claimed from an agent
  container and are **PRINT-UNVERIFIED** until run from a browser-capable environment;
- safeguarding and safety sign-off where either applies;
- awarding-body coordinator sign-off where a qualification claim appears;
- a short representative-user test;
- change log updated.

**[estate] A strong average never overrides a red gate**, and states 5 and 6 (`registration-verified`,
`assessment-ready`) are **centre actions** — no repository change closes them, and no commit may be presented as
having done so.

## What this standard deliberately does not require

Recorded so a later pass does not read silence as an omission and "fix" it.

- **No uniform visual template.** Preserving the estate's variety is a finding of the audit, not a concession.
- **No adult signature, initial or receipt mark on any pupil-owned closure surface.** GROW and LAUNCH close when
  the pupil writes the line. Adding a countersignature would change the artefact's species (**R-A09**).
- **No aggregation of closure marks** into a list, column, total or tracker. The absence is the control.
- **No daily collection register**, and no requirement that an adult read a closure line for it to count.
- **No hours gate**, at any level, for any award.
- **No second copy of anything derivable.** A count in prose goes stale silently; a count a script prints when
  it runs cannot (**R-G01**, **R-G03**, **R-G06**).
