## 1 · What PEQ is

- **Personal Effectiveness Qualifications** — ASDAN's suite that **replaces CoPE and AoPE**
  for new registrations, **operational from 1 Jan 2026**. [§2]
- Offered at **Award / Extended Award / Certificate**, at **Entry 3, Level 1, Level 2,
  Level 3**. **Portfolio-assessed, graded Pass/Fail.** [§2]
- It certifies **six core skills**. Vocational content (FoodWise, a community project,
  enterprise) is the *context* that generates the evidence — PEQ certifies the **skills**,
  not the vocational topic. [§2]

## 3 · Qualification sizes (the rules of combination)

- **Award** = 4 credits, all at level. [§2]
- **Extended Award** = 9 credits (min 6 at level, max 3 one level above/below). [§2]
- **Certificate** = 14 credits at E3/L1 (min 11 at level, max 3 adjacent); **L2
  Certificate = 15**. [§2] · The **six E3 units = exactly 14 = an E3 Certificate.** [Binder]
- **Barred combination:** the same skill at two levels cannot both count toward one
  qualification — **only the highest counts.** [§2]

## 4 · The unit spine — every unit has the same shape

**Know/understand the skill → create a plan (SMART targets) → USE the plan → identify/
describe/evaluate your own success.** [§2] Worked example, Communication at E3 [Binder]:

- E3.1–E3.3: **know** what communication / active listening / feedback are.
- E3.4: **create a plan** for a communication activity (purpose, audience, content,
  resources, ≥2 audience questions — "usually via a challenge for another PEQ unit").
- E3.5: **use the plan** — presentation **≥2 min** OR discussion **≥5 min** OR text **≥100
  words**; group work **≥3 people**.
- E3.6: **identify own success** (a positive outcome, use of active listening, an area to
  develop, where the skill will be used in future).

**Two instruments, both binding (not a conflict — resolved provisionally, confirm at
training):** the spec's *"the plan must be used over a minimum period of 10 hours"* is a
**cumulative cross-week window on plan use** (stated per-unit in the Additional assessment
requirements), so a unit is evidenced *across weeks, never inside one lesson*. The Binder's
**activity minimums** (presentation ≥2 min / discussion ≥5 min / text ≥100 words / group
≥3) govern **individual assessed activities**. Both apply to different things; neither
replaces the other. **Practical consequence for you:** a unit needs a plan used over ~10
cumulative hours *and* at least one assessed activity that meets its own minimum. Re-verify
the 10-hour wording against the committed spec.

## 5 · Command verbs set the level

- **Entry 3** = State / List / Identify / Give an example. **Level 1** = Outline / Describe
  / Give examples of a range. [§2]
- "Plural" = at least 2; "range" = at least 3 (unless stated). Units carry explicit
  minimum counts — e.g. ComSk1 presentation ≥3 min or ≥250 words; teams ≥3. [§2]

## 6 · What a portfolio must contain

- **Explicit evidence per criterion** (physical or digital portfolio). [§2] The **Evidence
  Binder** app is built to do exactly this — it picks "the next criterion that needs
  evidence" per pupil and tags each piece to criteria. [Binder]
- **Assessment records signed and dated by the assessor AND the learner** (and IQA where
  sampled), included in the portfolio. [§2] **⚠ Gap in your current printed records:** the
  lesson witness statements have an **assessor** signature but **no learner** signature —
  see FINDINGS §C.
- Learners must get the chance to **amend and resubmit**. [§2]
- Evidence types that count: observation, written work, oral questioning, witness
  statement, learner statement, presentation, research/learning log. [Binder]

## 7 · Assessment & quality assurance — the sequence that lets ASDAN award

- Centres may **devise their own activities** within ASDAN's guidance. [§2]
- **Learners must be registered before assessment counts;** names confirmed to ASDAN
  typically **≥4 weeks before sampling.** [§2]
- **IQA (internal quality assurance) must happen BEFORE EQA sampling — ASDAN cannot award
  without it.** [§2]
- **First-year centres MUST book an EQA sampling activity in year one**, once at least one
  unit is completed, assessed and IQA'd. [§2] ← **action item, this is you.**
- IQA staff should attend a **standardisation event at least every 3 years.** [§2]
- Assessors need **appropriate training but no formal assessing qualification.** [§2]

## 8 · Safeguarding (two units carry an explicit note)

**Decision making** and **Wellbeing in learning** carry safeguarding notes: if a learner
names a real situation or discloses a wellbeing issue, it routes through the centre's
safeguarding processes, **and learners must be told this in advance.** [§2] The Binder
carries a safeguarding note; confirm the Wellbeing/Decision lessons tell learners the
disclosure route (currently UNDETERMINED — needs the unit booklets).
