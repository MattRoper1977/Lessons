## 1 · What PEQ is

- **Personal Effectiveness Qualifications** — ASDAN's suite that **replaces CoPE and AoPE**
  for new registrations. The key-dates PDF confirms **CoPE/AoPE close to new registration
  on 31/12/2025** (England), so any Sept-2026 cohort **must** be on PEQ. [DATES §2]
- Offered at **Award / Extended Award / Certificate**, at **Entry 3, Level 1, Level 2,
  Level 3**. **Portfolio-assessed.** (The spec does not grade the units Pass/Fail as a
  scale — a unit is achieved when all its criteria are met to standard and EQA-sampled.)
  [SPEC §3, §8]
- It certifies **six core skills**. Vocational content (FoodWise, a community project,
  enterprise) is the *context* that generates the evidence — PEQ certifies the **skills**,
  not the vocational topic. [SPEC §2, §18]

## 3 · Qualification sizes (the rules of combination) [SPEC §3]

- **Award** = 4 credits, all at level (E3/L1/L2). **⟲ CORRECTED: L3 Award = 6 credits** —
  the "4" is E3/L1/L2 only. [SPEC §3]
- **Extended Award** = 9 credits (min 6 at level, max 3 one level above/below) — E3/L1/L2.
  (L3 Extended Award = 11, min 8.) [SPEC §3]
- **Certificate** = 14 credits at E3/L1 (min 11 at level, max 3 adjacent); **L2
  Certificate = 15** (min 12). (L3 Certificate = 18, min 15.) [SPEC §3] · The **six E3
  units = exactly 14 = an E3 Certificate.** [Binder / SPEC §2]
- **Barred combination:** the same skill at two levels cannot both count toward one
  qualification — **only the highest counts.** [SPEC §4]

## 4 · The unit spine — every unit has the same shape

**Know/understand the skill → create a plan (SMART targets) → USE the plan → identify/
describe/evaluate your own success.** [§2] Worked example, Communication at E3 [Binder]:

- E3.1–E3.3: **know** what communication / active listening / feedback are.
- E3.4: **create a plan** for a communication activity (purpose, audience, content,
  resources, ≥2 audience questions — "usually via a challenge for another PEQ unit").
- E3.5: **use the plan** — presentation **≥2 min** OR discussion **≥5 min** OR text **≥100
  words** (one activity only); group work **≥3 people**. [SPEC §15]
- E3.6: **identify own success** (a positive outcome, use of active listening, an area to
  develop, where the skill will be used in future). [SPEC §15]

**The "10-hour" window — ⟲ CORRECTED scope (now spec-verified).** *"The plan must be used
over a minimum period of 10 hours"* is a real per-unit line on the **"Use the plan"**
criterion — a **cumulative cross-week window**, so those units are evidenced *across weeks,
never inside one lesson*. **But it is on five of six skills only — Decision making,
Learning, Team working, Thinking, Wellbeing — and is NOT on Communication (ComSkE3 /
ComSk1).** Communication instead carries the **activity minimums** (presentation ≥2 min E3 /
≥3 min L1, discussion ≥5/≥8 min, text ≥100/≥250 words, group ≥3). Both bind, on different
units/instruments; neither replaces the other. **Practical consequence:** for Dec/Learn/
Team/Think/Wellbeing a unit needs a plan used over ~10 cumulative hours *and* its assessed
activities; for **Communication there is no 10-hour rule** — just the activity minimum.
[SPEC §15, §16 — verified unit by unit]

## 5 · Command verbs set the level [SPEC §14]

- **Entry 3** = State / List / Identify / Give an example. **Level 1** = Outline / Identify
  a range of / Give examples of a range of / **Describe** (the L1 "use/review" outcomes use
  *Describe*). [SPEC §14] The **v1.2 Oct 2025** change was precisely to L1/L2/L3 command
  verbs, "to clarify the distinction between levels" — so verb pitch is now settled.
- **"Explain" is not an L1 PEQ command verb** (it belongs to L2+). Teaching prompts that say
  "Explain" over-reach *relative to the criteria*, but as discussion prompts (not assessment
  criteria) they are not a breach — do not rewrite them; do not touch the deliberate Stretch-
  tier L2 reach. [RECONCILIATION E3]
- "Plural" = at least 2; "range" = at least 3 (unless stated). Units carry explicit
  minimum counts — e.g. ComSk1 presentation ≥3 min OR ≥250 words; teams ≥3. [SPEC §14, §15]

## 6 · What a portfolio must contain

- **Explicit evidence per criterion** (physical or digital portfolio). [§2] The **Evidence
  Binder** app is built to do exactly this — it picks "the next criterion that needs
  evidence" per pupil and tags each piece to criteria. [Binder]
- **Assessment records completed, signed and dated (physically or digitally) by the
  assessor, the learner, and — where sampled — the IQA**, included in the portfolio.
  [SPEC §8] **✓ Already in place:** the "5 · Learner confirmation" block (T2-4) was merged
  into **all 79** ASDAN witness surfaces on 2026-07-29 (REGISTER R-E20/R-E21), so the
  printed record now carries **both** the assessor and the learner signature. The blind
  audit's "no learner signature" gap is **closed**. [RECONCILIATION G2]
- Learners must get the chance to **amend and resubmit** — re-submitted evidence must be
  assessed. [SPEC §8]
- Assessment judgements are recorded **"for example within the unit assessment booklet"** —
  that (member-gated) booklet is the per-criterion record; witness statements are
  supplementary, and the Binder already gives per-criterion capture. [SPEC §8]
- Evidence types that count: observation, written work, oral questioning, witness
  statement, learner statement, presentation, research/learning log. [Binder]

## 7 · Assessment & quality assurance — the sequence that lets ASDAN award

- Centres may **devise their own activities** within ASDAN's guidance (Assessment Planning
  Guidance). [SPEC §8]
- **Learners must be registered before any assessment takes place** (coordinator's action,
  at cohort set-up); names/details/units confirmed to ASDAN **usually ≥4 weeks before the
  EQA sampling activity.** [SPEC §11]
- **IQA must happen BEFORE EQA sampling — ASDAN cannot award without it.** [SPEC §9]
- **First-year centres MUST book an EQA sampling activity in year one**, for a date once at
  least one unit is completed, assessed and IQA'd. [SPEC §10] ← **action item, this is you.**
- IQA staff should attend an ASDAN PEQ **standardisation event at least once every 3
  years.** [SPEC §10, §12]
- Assessors need **appropriate training** (incl. "Assessment for ASDAN Qualifications" +
  qualification training) **but no formal assessing qualification**; IQAs additionally need
  "IQA for ASDAN qualifications" training. [SPEC §12]

## 8 · Safeguarding (two units carry an explicit note)

**Decision making** and **Wellbeing in learning** carry safeguarding notes — and **only
those two** (both E3 and L1): if a learner names a real situation or discloses a wellbeing
issue, it routes through the centre's safeguarding processes, **and learners must be told
this in advance** ("communicated to learners as part of the session"). [SPEC §17]
**Communication (ComSk1) carries no safeguarding note** — so the LAUNCH Autumn ComSk1
module is correctly outside it; the note lands on the Spring/Summer Decision/Wellbeing
modules. The **exact required wording** (if prescribed) is in the member-gated unit booklets
— STILL-UNDETERMINED. [RECONCILIATION H1/H2]

## 10 · PH-3 addendum (2026-08-18)

- **Partial achievement certificates at unit level** [SPEC §5.1]: a learner who misses the
  qualification size is still certificated for each unit achieved.
- **Only one adjacent-level unit can ever count** toward an Extended Award or Certificate —
  the adjacent cap is 3 credits and the smallest unit is 2, so a second adjacent unit always
  busts the cap. (This is why "one level above or below" needs no further resolving.)
- **Sept-2025 booklet direction:** L1 command verbs got *easier* (ThSk1 1.1.3/1.1.4 →
  "Give examples of", 1.2.1 → "Identify"; only 1.7.1 rose to Describe); L2/L3 got harder.
  Never "top up" L1 demand.

### Two rules that are easy to get backwards

- **Never top Level 1 demand up.** The Sept-2025 booklets moved L1 command verbs *down*
  (ThSk1 1.1.3/1.1.4 Outline → "Give examples of"; 1.2.1 Outline → "Identify"). A Stretch
  stem may sit easier than Outline; it must never sit above Describe. [SPEC §19]
- **SMART is introduced but NOT assessed at E3 team working.** The SMART minimum sits only
  at TmWkSk1 (1.3.1a). No task may gate the E3 unit on producing SMART targets. [SPEC §15]

### Barred combinations decide where a pupil's credit lands

A learner's evidence for a skill banks at **one level only** — Entry 3 *or* Level 1, never
both; the higher counts (spec §6.5). Every PEQ witness statement now carries a
`Level this evidence banks at — tick ONE: ☐ Entry 3 ☐ Level 1` row beside the assessor
signature, so the decision is recorded per pupil, per session, on the paper that banks it.
