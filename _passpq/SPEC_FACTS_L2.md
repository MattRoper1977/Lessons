## 3 · Level 2 units in full (pp.52–66) — LOs, ACs, minima

### ComSk2 — Communication skills, 3 cr / 24 GLH (pp.52–54)

Learning outcomes 2.1–2.6: understand communication (2.1) · active listening (2.2) ·
feedback (2.3) · **create plans for different types of communication activities** (2.4) ·
use the plans (2.5) · evaluate own success (2.6).

Assessment criteria: 2.1.1 Describe communication incl. (a) ways (b) types (c) purposes ·
2.1.2 Describe components of effective communication · 2.1.3 Explain difficulties + impact
of poor communication · 2.2.1 Describe active listening · 2.2.2 Explain its importance ·
2.2.3 Describe its components · 2.3.1 Define feedback · 2.3.2 Explain why feedback matters
· 2.3.3 Describe ways feedback is demonstrated, with examples across ways/types/purposes ·
2.4.1 Create plans incl. (a) purpose (b) audience (c) type + reason for choice (d) content
outline (e) resources (f) potential audience questions and responses — **no review point
in the ComSk2 plan** · 2.5.1 Use the plans · 2.6.1 **Evaluate** personal progress incl.
(a) range of positive outcomes (b) use of active listening (c) how difficulties were
managed (d) use of **peer feedback** to inform future development (e) areas of further
development (f) future situations.

Additional assessment requirements (p53): 2.1.1a ≥**4** ways of communicating · 2.1.1b
≥**3** types · 2.1.1c ≥**3** purposes · 2.1.2 ≥**4** components · 2.1.3 ≥**4** difficulties
· **2.4.1 ≥2 plans covering 2 DIFFERENT ways of communicating** (can include a
presentation or a group discussion) · 2.4.1f ≥**4** questions, 2 per activity · **2.5.1
activity minima: presentation ≥4 min · discussion ≥10 min · text ≥500 words** · 2.6.1a
≥**3** positive outcomes · 2.6.1f ≥**2** areas of further development.

Guidance (p54): LO2.4/2.5 evidence expected via a challenge leading to other PEQ units
(e.g. CrTh, TmWk, DecMk); otherwise evidence of preparing incl. topic research. 2.6.1c —
a learner who had no difficulties can state this. **A group must have ≥3 members.**
**No 10-hour plan-use line on ComSk2** — the activity-time minima above stand instead.

### DecMkSk2 — Decision making skills, 2 cr / 16 GLH (pp.55–57)

LOs 2.1–2.6: understand decision making · tools and techniques · identifying situations ·
create a plan · use the plan · assess own success.

ACs: 2.1.1 Define decision making · 2.1.2 Describe a range of factors · **2.2.1 COMPARE
decision-making tools/techniques for a range of situations** · **2.3.1 Describe example
situations needing a decision in the short-, medium- and long-term** · 2.3.2 Explain
relevance + impact of poor decisions · 2.4.1 Create a plan incl. (a) SMART targets
(b) reasons (c) use of tools/techniques (d) potential difficulties (e) support routes
(f) resources **(g) a review point** · 2.5.1 Use the plan · 2.6.1 **Evaluate** progress
incl. (a) positive outcomes incl. targets met (b) use of tools/techniques (c) things done
differently (d) how difficulties were managed (e) areas of further development (f) future
situations.

Requirements (p56): 2.1.2 ≥**3** factors · 2.2.1 ≥**3** tools/techniques · 2.3.1 ≥**3**
situations · 2.4.1a ≥2 SMART targets · 2.4.1b ≥2 reasons, 1 per target · 2.4.1d ≥2
difficulties · **2.5.1 plan used over ≥10 hours** · 2.6.1a ≥2 positive outcomes · 2.6.1c
≥2 things done differently · 2.6.1e ≥2 areas of further development.

Guidance (p57): tools in the plan chosen from those in 2.2.1; learners may share a
situation but must decide independently. **Safeguarding "Please note" box (p57):**
situations must not negatively impact wellbeing; a potential safeguarding concern goes
through the centre's safeguarding processes, **and this is communicated to learners as
part of the session.**

### TmWkSk2 — Team working skills, 2 cr / 16 GLH (pp.60–61)

LOs 2.1–2.5: understand teamwork · features of effective teamwork · create a team plan ·

ACs: 2.1.1 Define teamwork · 2.1.2 Explain advantages AND challenges with own/real-world

Create a plan incl. (a) **a range of SMART team goals** (b) roles/responsibilities/tasks

Requirements (p61): 2.1.2 ≥**3** advantages AND ≥**3** challenges · 2.2.1 ≥**3** features

positive outcomes · 2.5.1c ≥2 things · 2.5.1d ≥2 areas of further development · 2.5.1e ≥2

### CrThSk2 — Critical thinking skills, 3 cr / 24 GLH (pp.62–63)

LOs 2.1–2.7: understand critical thinking · tools and techniques · assessing information ·

ACs: 2.1.1 Define critical thinking skills · 2.1.2 Explain why it matters · 2.1.3 Describe

**2.2.1 COMPARE a range of tools/techniques, reflecting on own preferences** · **2.3.1
Assess information for facts, opinions and assumptions** · **2.3.2 Assess information

### WellbLe2 — Wellbeing in learning, 3 cr / 24 GLH (pp.64–66)

LOs 2.1–2.7: understand learner wellbeing · how wellbeing impacts learning · techniques,
people and services that support it · why mistakes matter · create a plan · use the plan ·
assess own success.

ACs: **2.1.1 Describe learner wellbeing incl. one description each of (a) emotional
(b) social (c) physical (d) mental wellbeing** · 2.2.1 Describe a range of factors
affecting wellbeing · 2.2.2 Explain how they impact learning · 2.3.1 Describe a range of
ways learners can support their wellbeing · **2.3.2 COMPARE techniques, reflecting on
personal preferences** · 2.3.3 Describe a range of people who can support wellbeing ·
2.3.4 Describe a range of services · 2.4.1 Explain why mistakes are important for learning
· 2.4.2 Explain how mistakes can be positive for wellbeing, with examples · 2.5.1 Create a
plan incl. (a) SMART targets (b) reasons (c) support routes (d) resources **(e) a review

incl. targets met (b) changes to how they view their own learner wellbeing (c) how
difficulties during learning were managed (d) areas of further development (e) future
situations.

Requirements (p65): **2.1.1 one (1) description per category** · 2.2.1 ≥**3** factors ·
2.3.1 ≥**3** ways · 2.3.2 ≥**2** techniques · 2.3.3 ≥**3** people · 2.3.4 ≥**3** services ·
2.5.1a ≥2 SMART targets · 2.5.1b ≥2 reasons, 1 per target · **2.6.1 plan used over ≥10
hours** · 2.7.1a ≥2 positive outcomes · 2.7.1b ≥2 changes · 2.7.1d ≥2 areas of further
development. **Safeguarding "Please note" box (p66):** disclosures with a potential
safeguarding concern go through the centre's safeguarding processes, **communicated to
learners as part of the session.**

## 4 · The 10-hour windows and review points at L2 — precise scope

- **10-hour plan-use** attaches to the "Use the plan" AC on **five of six L2 units** —
  DecMkSk2 2.5.1 · LSk2 2.4.1 · TmWkSk2 2.4.1 · CrThSk2 2.6.1 · WellbLe2 2.6.1 — and
  **NOT ComSk2**, which carries the activity minima instead (≥4 min / ≥10 min / ≥500
  words). Same shape as E3/L1 (`SPEC_FACTS.md` §16).
- **A review point is a required plan element on the same five units** — DecMkSk2 2.4.1g ·
  LSk2 2.3.1f · TmWkSk2 2.3.1g · CrThSk2 2.5.1e · WellbLe2 2.5.1e — and **NOT on the
  ComSk2 plans** (2.4.1 a–f has no review point). E3 plans have **no** review points at
  all; L1 plans carry them (see the L1 unit pages, `SPEC_FACTS.md` §15). Resource
  templates must respect this per level.
