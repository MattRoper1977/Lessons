## 3 · Judgement calls, each recorded rather than absorbed

### J1 · §2b vs SPEC_FACTS — no conflict, so nothing was escalated
The master prompt's §2 rule is that a §2-vs-`SPEC_FACTS` conflict stops the item to
PROPOSED with both readings quoted. Both were read line-for-line first: unit codes, credits,
GLH, qualification sizes, the ComSk tier split, and the other five units' E3 minima all
**agree**. No item needed escalating. Two facts from `SPEC_FACTS` §19 governed the work and
are recorded because they are easy to get backwards: **L1 command verbs moved DOWN** in the
Sept-2025 booklets (never top L1 demand up), and **registration/approval is DONE** (never
frame it as pending).

### J2 · A-P68's TA line did NOT already name both floors
§3 E2 says the line "already names both floors — keep, verify it matches §2b exactly."
Verified, and it does not. Measured: both markup variants in both decks name **ComSk1's
floors only**. Keeping it as written would have left the staff brief telling an assessor
that Entry 3 pupils owe 4 components, 3 difficulties, 4 audience questions and a 3-minute
talk. It now names both floors and says which tier evidences which unit. **This is a
correction to the ruling's premise, not a departure from its intent** — the intent was that
staff can see both floors, which they now can.

### J3 · E5 has no deck target, and that is a measurement
§2c attaches the spec's safeguarding note to decks that **run** the DecMk situation choice
or the WellbLe improvement discussion. Measured across all 14 PEQ files: **0 occurrences of
either activity**, and 0 occurrences of "safeguard*" anywhere in the PEQ suites. Autumn 1
delivers Communication (LAUNCH) and a GROW mix on team working, thinking and learning. GROW
W1 runs a strengths-and-interests audit; W4 runs a personal routine strip. Neither is the
spec's box. Writing the line into a deck that does not run the activity would have applied
something outside §3 and put a safeguarding instruction beside an activity it does not
describe. **The requirement is carried into `COMPLIANCE_CHECKLIST.md` item 15 instead**,
flagged as becoming required the moment a DecMk or WellbLe deck is authored.

### J4 · Universal teaching surfaces state E3 first and name L1
E2's rule is per-tier, but several surfaces are not tier-scoped — a Big Idea, a Key Facts
list, a knowledge organiser. Left at the L1 figure they would contradict the Standard task
below them; moved to the E3 figure alone they would hide the demand the 2–4 stretch pupils
actually carry. They state the **E3 figure first and name the L1 one after**
("… — at Level 1, two of each"). That is the only reading that leaves no surface lying to
either group.

### J5 · Scope — what the census found but this pass did not touch
- **29 short-course decks still carry `Stretch (L2 standard)`** (Community Project 6,
  Enterprise 6, Community Enterprise 5, Living Independently 6, Vocational 6). §5 holds
  that family out of this pass, and PROP-1 F07 disclosed the same boundary. **0 PEQ decks
  carry it.**
- **The six GROW Community Project decks WERE swept**, but only for their PEQ banking claim
  (`ASDAN PEQ L1 cross-unit project work`, 48 occurrences). Their UAS community-evidence
  wording is the held family and is untouched.
- **BUILD is untouched**, as §5 requires. Pass PQ proved BUILD banks short courses and zero
  PEQ; nothing in BUILD names a PEQ level for this pass to re-anchor.
