## 1 · Credit definition and Guided Learning Hours (§5.1 p9; §9 p16)

- **One credit ≈ 10 hours of learning, *including assessment of learning*.** (§5.1, p9) —
  confirms PEQ_PRIMER §2 / CREDIT_PATHWAYS.
- **GLH** = time a learner is taught or supervised by the centre for the unit; **GLH
  include assessment** where the assessment is carried out directly by a centre assessor/
  tutor. (§9, p16)
- **TQT** = GLH + estimated independent (directed but unsupervised) study hours. (§9, p16)

**Rules-of-combination prose (§5.1, pp9–10):**
- Learners **may complete units at the level above or below** the qualification level, where
  available. The **minimum credits required at the level** of the qualification must be
  achieved for certification. (p9)
- Achievement of **more than the maximum credits at the level above** may result in a
  **different qualification outcome**; centres unsure of the outcome should contact ASDAN. (p9)
- If a learner does not achieve enough credits for the qualification, **certification is
  given for the units achieved.** (p9)

**Correction to prior docs (CREDIT_PATHWAYS/PEQ_PRIMER §3):** the blanket statement
"Award = 4 credits, all at level" is **true for E3/L1/L2 only**. **L3 Award = 6 credits
(min 6)**, L3 Extended Award = 11 (min 8), L3 Certificate = 18 (min 15). Within this
estate's scope (E3/L1) the "4-credit Award / 9-credit Extended Award / 14-credit
Certificate (min 11)" figures are all **VERIFIED**.

## 4 · Barred unit combinations (§6.5, p15)

Where a learner works towards units at more than one level, **only one unit from each skill
row can count toward a single qualification; the credit from the highest-level unit
counts.** (§6.5, p15) The six barred rows are the six skills across E3 → L1 → L2 → L3:
Communication (ComSkE3/ComSk1/ComSk2/ComSk3), Decision making (DecMkSkE3/DecMkSk1/
DecMkSk2/DecMkSk3), Learning (LSkE3/LSk1/LSk2/LSk3), Team working (TmWkSkE3/TmWkSk1/
TmWkSk2/TmWkSk3), Thinking→Critical thinking (ThSkE3/ThSk1/CrThSk2/CrThSk3), Wellbeing
(WellbLeE3/WellbLe1/WellbLe2/WellbLe3).

**Observed spec quirk (record, do not "fix"):** the §6.5 barred-combination table prints
the L2/L3 thinking cells as "(ThSk2)" and "(ThSk3)", whereas the authoritative unit tables
§6.3/§6.4 give the codes as **CrThSk2 / CrThSk3**. Treat **CrThSk2/CrThSk3** as the codes;
the barred table's "ThSk2/ThSk3" is shorthand. (§6.3–6.5, pp13–15)

## 5 · Recognition of Prior Learning (§7, p15)

- RPL eligible **only for units/learning achieved within the 3 years prior** to the
  qualification quality-assurance date. (§7, p15)
- Centres remain responsible for sufficient assessment + IQA on RPL claims.
- **Award of all units/qualifications depends on successful EQA sampling.** (§7, p15)

## 6 · Centre requirements (§8, p16)

- Centres must hold **ASDAN Qualifications membership approval**, and **where appropriate
  request approval for the Personal Effectiveness suite**, *before delivering*. (§8, p16)
- The centre is responsible for ensuring all delivery/assessment/IQA staff meet ASDAN's
  requirements, **including attending appropriate training** (see §15). (§8, p16)

## 7 · Delivery (§9, p16)

- Centres develop learners' knowledge/skills **before assessment**. (§9, p16)
- **Mixed-level groups are explicitly permitted:** the qualifications "can be delivered to a
  group of learners working towards **different levels and sizes** of qualification at the
  same time." (§9, p16)
- **Differentiation is required:** staff "must plan teaching, learning and assessment that
  is **effectively differentiated** for their learners." (§9, p16)

## 8 · Assessment (§10, p17)

- Unless a unit stipulates otherwise, **centres may devise their own assessment
  activities**, following ASDAN's guidance (Assessment Planning Guidance). (§10, p17)
- Evidence must be **valid, current and sufficient** and support a reliable decision.
- Learners produce a **portfolio** (physical or digital). **There must be explicit
  evidence, within the portfolio, to demonstrate achievement of all criteria at the
  required standard.** → **explicit evidence per criterion.** (§10, p17)
- Learners must be given **adequate opportunity to amend/add** evidence; re-submitted
  evidence must be assessed. (§10, p17)
- **Reasonable adjustments** apply (Ofqual definition); see ASDAN Reasonable Adjustments
  Policy. (§10, p17)
- **Records** of assessment planning and judgements **must be produced and retained** and
  made available for IQA/EQA with the associated evidence (Retention of Assessment
  Materials Policy). (§10, p17)
- **Signatures:** assessment records should be "completed, signed and dated (physically or
  digitally) by the **assessor, learner and (where sampled) the IQA**" and included in the
  portfolio. → confirms **assessor + learner** signature requirement; IQA where sampled.
  (§10, p17)
- Assessment judgements are recorded **"for example within the unit assessment booklet."**
  → the per-criterion record lives in the (member-gated) **unit assessment booklet**;
  witness statements are supplementary. (§10, p17)

## 9 · Internal quality assurance (§11, p18)

- Centres must carry out sufficient, effective IQA, **including IQA of assessment decisions
  prior to EQA sampling** and standardisation. (§11, p18)
- **ASDAN cannot award where IQA has not been carried out.** (§11, p18)
- IQA of an appropriate sample of assessed portfolio evidence must be done **before EQA
  sampling**. (§11, p18)
- IQA records (sampling + standardisation) must be produced, retained, and made available
  for EQA. (§11, p18)

## 10 · CASS & external quality assurance (§12, pp18–19)

- EQA (external moderation) reviews centre assessment + IQA **before awarding**; ASDAN may
  EQA-sample **at any point** during delivery. (§12, p18)
- **First-time centres MUST arrange an EQA sampling activity during the first year of
  delivery, for a date when at least one unit has been completed, assessed and IQA'd.**
  (§12, p19) ← the mandatory first-year EQA booking.
- EQA usually **remote**; ASDAN may do in-person/on-site. (§12, p19)
- Centres must **confirm the names and details of learners** expected to achieve the
  qualification by the date ASDAN sets; ASDAN then provides the sample. (§12, p19)
- **EQA feedback report** issued via the ASDAN website within **20 working days** of a
  successfully completed EQA activity. (§12, p19)
- **IQA staff should attend an ASDAN PEQ standardisation event at least once every 3
  years.** (§12, p19; restated §15.2, p20)

## 11 · Administration & certification (§14, p20)

- **Registration:** centres should create a cohort and **register learners as soon as
  possible after starting delivery and *before any assessment takes place*.** A **course
  coordinator must be assigned** at the point of setting up the cohort. (§14, p20) →
  registration is the coordinator's action, before assessment.
- **Names for awarding:** centres must confirm names/details/units to be submitted **by
  ASDAN's deadline, usually a minimum of 4 weeks before the scheduled EQA sampling
  activity.** (§14, p20)
- **Certificates** issued to centres within **20 working days** of successful award. (§14, p20)

## 12 · Assessor & IQA requirements (§15, p20)

- **Assessors** must engage in **appropriate training, including "Assessment for ASDAN
  Qualifications" and relevant qualification training**, and have relevant assessment
  knowledge/expertise. **No formal assessment qualification is required.** (§15.1, p20)
- **IQAs** must engage in **relevant qualification training + "Assessment for ASDAN
  Qualifications" + "Internal Quality Assurance for ASDAN qualifications" training, prior
  to any IQA activity**, and have relevant IQA knowledge/expertise. **No formal IQA
  qualification is required.** IQAs should attend a **Centre Standardisation event at least
  once every 3 years.** (§15.2, p20)

## 14 · Unit spine and command verbs (§17)

- **Common unit shape** (all units): *Know/understand the skill → create a plan (incl.
  SMART targets, where applicable) → USE the plan → describe/identify own success/progress*.
  (§17, per unit) L1 "Use the plan" outcomes and the "identify success" outcome use the
  verb **Describe** (e.g. ComSk1 1.6.1, DecMkSk1 1.6.1). (§17, pp38–50)
- **Command verbs by level (from the assessment criteria, §17):**
  - **Entry 3:** *State · List · Identify · Give an example of.* (E3 units, pp24–35)
  - **Level 1:** *Outline · Identify a range of · Give examples of a range of · Describe.*
    (L1 units, pp38–50)
  - **"Plural" = at least 2; "Range" = at least 3** (unless a criterion states otherwise).
    Stated on every unit's Additional assessment requirements page. (§17)

## 16 · The "10-hour" plan-use window — precise scope (§17)

**"The plan must be used over a minimum period of 10 hours"** is a per-unit line in the
Additional assessment requirements, attached to the **"Use the plan"** criterion. It is
present on **five of six skills at each level — Decision making, Learning, Team working,
Thinking, and Wellbeing — but NOT on Communication** (ComSkE3 / ComSk1), which instead
carries the presentation/discussion/text **activity-time minimums**. (§17, verified unit by
unit, pp24–50.) This corrects the blind audit's generalisation that the 10-hour line
"appears per-unit"; it is absent from Communication. See `RECONCILIATION.md` (flag D).

## 17 · Safeguarding disclosure (§17)

Two skills carry an explicit safeguarding note in their Additional assessment guidance:
**Decision making** (DecMkSkE3 p27, DecMkSk1 p41) and **Wellbeing in learning** (WellbLeE3
p35, WellbLe1 p50). Both state that where a learner identifies/discloses a potential
safeguarding concern it must be managed through the centre's safeguarding processes, and
**"this should be communicated to learners as part of the session"** — i.e. learners told
in advance. (§17)

## 18 · Cross-cutting assessment-guidance note (§17, ComSkE3 p25 / ComSk1 p39)

Evidence for the Communication **plan/use** outcomes (E3.4/E3.5; 1.4/1.5) is **expected to
be generated through a challenge that also leads to another PEQ unit** (e.g. Thinking, Team
working, Decision making). Where the activity is not tied to another PEQ unit, there must be
evidence of **preparing for communication, including research of the topic**. (§17, pp25/39)
This underwrites the GROW/LAUNCH cross-strand banking model.
