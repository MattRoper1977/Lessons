## Rules of combination (§2, to re-verify against spec)

- 1 credit ≈ 10 hours incl. assessment.
- **Award** = 4 credits, all at level.
- **Extended Award** = 9 credits (min 6 at level, max 3 from one level above/below).
- **Certificate** = 14 credits at E3/L1 (min 11 at level, max 3 adjacent); **L2
  Certificate = 15**.
- **Barred combination:** the same skill at two levels cannot both count toward one
  qualification — only the highest counts.

## BUILD (Entry 3) — reachable **only if PEQ E3 is claimed and banked**

As built, BUILD claims **no PEQ** (COVERAGE_BUILD §0), so the PEQ credit reachable today is
**zero**. *If* the six E3 units were claimed and banked from the existing vocational work:

| target | credits | reachable? |
|---|---|---|
| **Award (E3)** | 4 | Yes — any 2–3 units (e.g. ComSkE3 3 + DecMkSkE3 2 = 5) |
| **Extended Award (E3)** | 9 | Yes — e.g. ComSkE3 3 + WellbLeE3 3 + DecMkSkE3 2 + LSkE3 2 = 10 |
| **Certificate (E3)** | 14 | Yes — **all six E3 units = exactly 14** |

→ BUILD's vocational contexts can generate all six E3 units; the ceiling is the **E3
Certificate**. Nothing is banked as PEQ today — this is the unbanked opportunity.

## GROW (Level 1) — reachable, but capped by the Wellbeing gap

GROW's PEQ strand teaches five of six L1 units; **WellbLe1 (Wellbeing, 3 cr) is a GAP**
(COVERAGE_GROW §1). Assuming each taught unit is fully evidenced and banked (currently
PARTIAL — depends on the GCOMM/ENT project strands closing use/review):

| units banked | credits | highest target reachable |
|---|---|---|
| five taught units (Com 3 + Dec 2 + LSk 2 + TmWk 2 + ThSk 3) | **12** | **Extended Award (L1)** — 12 ≥ 9 ✓; **Certificate (14) NOT reached** |
| + WellbLe1 added (3) | **15** | **Certificate (L1)** — 15 ≥ 14 ✓ (min 11 at level ✓) |

→ **As built, GROW's realistic PEQ ceiling is an L1 Extended Award (9 cr).** The L1
**Certificate** (14 cr) is **not reachable without a sixth skill**. There are **two routes**
to it (below); either satisfies the barred-combination rule because Wellbeing would appear
at **only one level**.

### Route A · add WellbLe1 to GROW (same-level)
Teach and bank Wellbeing at **L1** → six L1 units = 15 cr → **L1 Certificate** (min 11 at
level ✓). Requires new teaching in the GROW strand (COVERAGE_GROW §1 GAP).

### Route B · adjacent-credit route (PROVISIONAL — spec-gated)
The five taught L1 units already give **12 credits at level (≥ the min 11 at level)**. The
Certificate allows **max 3 credits adjacent** (one level above/below). Bank the sixth skill,
**Wellbeing, at E3 (WellbLeE3, 3 cr) or L2** as the adjacent contribution → **12 + 3 = 15 ≥
14** → **L1 Certificate** with no new L1 Wellbeing teaching, if the E3 Wellbeing evidence
can be generated (e.g. from BUILD's FoodWise/wellbeing context). **Barred-combination
satisfied:** Wellbeing appears at one level only (E3), Communication/Decision/Learning/Team/
Thinking at L1 only. **Flag PROVISIONAL** — re-verify the "max 3 adjacent" and "min 11 at
level" thresholds against the committed spec before relying on this.

**Decision-relevant summary:** an L1 Certificate is reachable either by teaching Wellbeing at
L1 (Route A) or by banking Wellbeing at E3 within the adjacent allowance (Route B). Both go
to Cheryl as options alongside "Extended Award as-is" (QUESTIONS §Q2).

## Cross-tier pupils — the barred-combination trap

A pupil doing BUILD (E3) **and** GROW (L1) cannot count the same skill twice. If they
evidence ComSkE3 **and** ComSk1, **only ComSk1 (the higher) counts**. So a cross-tier
pupil's E3 work in a skill is superseded, not additive, once they achieve L1 in that skill.
Registration should target **one level per skill per pupil** — which the Evidence Binder
already enforces ("Each pupil works at one level… Entry 3 and Level 1 evidence never mix").
