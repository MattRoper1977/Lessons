# DATES_2026-27.md — dates a centre starting PEQ delivery in September 2026 must know

**Sources (both cited per line):**
- **[KD]** ASDAN *Qualifications 2026 — Key dates and performance information* (© ASDAN,
  "Updated ASDAN July 2026"). Not committed (copyright); facts extracted with page cites.
- **[SPEC]** the PEQ specification v1.2 Oct 2025 (see `SPEC_FACTS.md`), for the *operational*
  deadlines the key-dates PDF does not carry.

**What the key-dates PDF is — and is not.** [KD] is a **qualification-lifecycle** table
(review date · Ofqual registration-end date · certification-end date · performance points ·
Wales designation). It does **not** publish operational scheduling dates (registration
windows, EQA booking lead times, claim deadlines) — those are set per centre by ASDAN and
governed by the spec. Do not read a blank cell as "no deadline"; read it as "no *lifecycle
end* date set — the qualification continues." (KD p1 note; p4 col. headers)

---

## 1 · The PEQ suite is current and continuing — no near-term end date

- **PEQ suite review date: 31/08/2027.** [KD p4–5] Per the KD p1 note, because **no final
  registration-end or certification-end date is printed** against the PEQ rows, ASDAN
  intends to **continue offering PEQ beyond the review date**. A Sept-2026 start is well
  inside the live window. [KD p1, p4–5]
- **No registration-end / certification-end date** is set for any PEQ E3 / L1 / L2 unit or
  qualification (cells shown "-" / n/a). [KD p4–5]
- **Funding flag:** the E3 / L1 / L2 PEQ rows carry **"\*\*" = currently funded in England
  for ages 14–16 only**; L3 PEQ carries performance/UCAS points (Award 8, Ext. Award 8,
  Certificate 16). [KD p1 legend, p5] PEQ is funded in England and Northern Ireland. [SPEC §4 p9]
- Ofqual numbers for the units the centre has registered (for the coordinator): **E3
  Certificate 610/5902/X · L1 Certificate 610/5905/5 · L1 Extended Award 610/5906/7 · L1
  Award 610/5904/3** (full list in `SPEC_FACTS.md` §3). [KD p4–5]

## 2 · Why PEQ (not CoPE/AoPE) for a new Sept-2026 cohort — predecessor cut-off

- **CoPE (Certificate of Personal Effectiveness) and AoPE (Award of Personal
  Effectiveness): last registration 31/12/2025; last certification 31/12/2027** (England /
  Ofqual). [KD p2] So **CoPE/AoPE cannot take new registrations after 31 Dec 2025** — a
  Sept-2026 cohort **must** be registered on **PEQ**. (Wales predecessor dates differ:
  final registration 30/09/2026, certification 30/09/2028. [KD p2])
- This matches PEQ_PRIMER §1: PEQ replaces CoPE/AoPE for new registrations, operational
  from 1 Jan 2026. (The "1 Jan 2026" operational date is a PEQ_PRIMER/§2 claim; [KD]
  confirms the predecessor **registration-end of 31/12/2025**, which is the load-bearing
  fact for "new cohorts go on PEQ".)

## 3 · Operational deadlines for 2026–27 delivery — from the spec (per centre)

These are the dates that actually drive a first delivery year. They are **relative** (ASDAN
sets the absolute date per cohort), so they are listed as sequencing rules, not calendar
dates. [SPEC]

| When | What | Cite |
|---|---|---|
| **Done — 2026-08-21** | Cohort created, **course coordinator assigned**, **learners registered** and units entered. | [SPEC §14 p20] |
| **During the first year of delivery** | **First-time centres MUST book an EQA sampling activity**, for a date when **≥1 unit is completed, assessed and IQA'd**. | [SPEC §12 p19] |
| **Before that EQA sampling** | Complete **IQA** of an appropriate sample — ASDAN **cannot award without IQA**. | [SPEC §11 p18] |
| **≈4 weeks before the scheduled EQA sampling activity** | **Confirm learner names/details/units** to ASDAN (ASDAN's deadline is *usually a minimum of 4 weeks prior*). ASDAN then issues the sample. | [SPEC §14 p20; §12 p19] |
| **Within 20 working days** of a successful EQA activity | ASDAN releases the **EQA feedback report**. | [SPEC §12 p19] |
| **Within 20 working days** of successful award | ASDAN issues **certificates** to the centre. | [SPEC §14 p20] |
| **At least once every 3 years** | IQA staff attend an ASDAN PEQ **standardisation event**. | [SPEC §12 p19; §15.2 p20] |
| **Within the 3 years prior** to the QA date | Only learning achieved in this window is eligible for **RPL**. | [SPEC §7 p15] |

## 4 · Flagged as ambiguous — do not interpret, confirm with ASDAN

- **The exact EQA sampling date and the "≈4 weeks" names deadline are set by ASDAN per
  cohort**, not published in [KD]. The centre must obtain them from its ASDAN account /
  EQA coordinator. Do not assume a fixed calendar date. [SPEC §12/§14]
- **PEQ registration-end / certification-end dates are blank in [KD].** The KD p1 note reads
  a blank as "continues beyond review date," but the **31/08/2027 review** is the point at
  which ASDAN may set such dates. Confirm current status before planning a multi-year cohort
  that would certificate after Aug 2027. [KD p1, p4–5]
- **Funding (14–16 only, England)** for E3/L1/L2 PEQ — confirm current funding/eligibility
  and any 16–19 route with ASDAN when planning cohorts, as [KD p1] advises centres to check
  current funding details. [KD p1, p5]

## 5 · What is **not** in these PDFs (so nobody infers it)

- No PEQ-specific registration *window* open/close calendar dates. [KD carries lifecycle
  end-dates only.]
- No EQA scheduling calendar — remote by default, date set by ASDAN. [SPEC §12 p19]
- No claim/certification *submission* calendar — driven by the per-cohort EQA date + the
  ≈4-week names rule. [SPEC §14 p20]

Where any of the above is needed, it is an **ASDAN-account action for the coordinator**, not
a fact derivable from the committed instruments. Recorded in `COMPLIANCE_CHECKLIST.md`.
