# ASDAN_CHANGES_FOR_PLANNERS.md

**One row per lane x week x slot.** This is the input to the weekly-plan update:
what is timetabled, what deck sits there, what changed, and what paperwork it
generates.

Roles only. No pupil data, no pupil initials, no staff names, no reading ages.
**Safe to paste anywhere**, including into a session that holds real pupil records.

Generated from the school's own 2026-27 timetable, with the workbook cell cited on
every slot. Slots that bank their own subject, and free periods, are excluded from
the rows and counted in the summary, so the denominator stays visible.

## 0 - Read this before you plan anything

**The ASDAN suites cover weeks 1-7. The year is 38 weeks long.**

Every authored ASDAN deck in the estate sits in weeks 1-7
- one Autumn 1 strand per subject. From week 7 onward the slots are still
timetabled, still carryable, and still bank PEQ evidence, but **there is no deck**
for them. Those rows below read `NO-PLANNER-SURFACE (no deck authored)`.

That is not a defect in this table. It is the single largest thing the weekly-plan
job has to decide, and it would be invisible if the rows were quietly omitted.
