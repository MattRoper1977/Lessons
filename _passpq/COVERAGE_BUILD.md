## 0 · The finding that governs this whole matrix

**BUILD claims no PEQ.** No BUILD lesson names a PEQ unit, an Entry 3 level, or "Personal
Effectiveness" (CLAIMS §1: 0 occurrences of each across 31 lessons). Every BUILD banking
claim is an **ASDAN short course** (FoodWise, Living Independently) or **AQA Unit Award
Scheme** ("Personal challenge", enterprise, community evidence).

Consequence for this matrix: **no E3 PEQ criterion can be scored COVERED**, because
"covered" requires the criterion to be *claimed and evidenced as PEQ*, and none is. The
honest verdict for every E3 unit is **UNCLAIMED** — the vocational contexts plainly
*generate* skill evidence that *could* bank E3 units, but the provision banks it elsewhere.
GAP vs UNCLAIMED is the key distinction here: this is not missing teaching, it is
**unbanked teaching**. That is a registration/labelling decision (Cheryl), not a teaching
gap (§2: "vocational content is the context that generates the skill evidence").

Criterion-by-criterion COVERED/PARTIAL scoring is therefore **deferred** until two things
exist: (1) the committed spec, and (2) a decision to actually claim PEQ E3 in BUILD. What
follows is the per-unit evidence-generation map that decision would rest on.

## 2 · Spine, minimum-count, command-verb tests

- **The plan → use → review spine.** Only the **Community Project** (W1–W6) is an
  unambiguous plan→use→review arc (it plans a handover and delivers it, then reviews the
  benefit). FoodWise builds toward "plan a healthy meal" (W6) but the earlier weeks are
  discrete knowledge/skill challenges, not a single tracked plan. Living Independently,
  Careers and Duke are challenge-sequenced. **Verdict: the arc exists in one of five
  modules; the other four would be PARTIAL on the E3.4→E3.6 chain if PEQ were claimed.**
- **The "≥10 hours" rule — PROVISIONALLY-RESOLVED (spec-gate open, per close-out ruling).**
  Not a conflict: the spec's line is *"the plan must be used over a minimum period of 10
  hours"* — a **cumulative cross-week window on plan use**, stated per-unit in the Additional
  assessment requirements. The Binder's activity-minimums (presentation ≥2 min, text ≥100
  words, group ≥3) govern **individual assessed activities**. **Both bind; neither replaces
  the other.** The finding stands regardless: **BUILD surfaces neither** — 0 occurrences of
  "10 hours / minimum period", and its challenge-based weeks do not clock cumulative plan
  use. Re-verify the 10-hour wording against the committed spec PDF.
- **Command-verb pitch.** BUILD is pitched low (SEMH alternative provision; "Say / Sort /
  photo" tasks), which is **appropriate for E3** (State/List/Identify). Spot-check found no
  over-pitched "explain and evaluate" demands on the Standard tier. This is the one
  dimension where BUILD looks well-matched to E3 — the pitch is right; the *labelling* is
  the problem, not the level.
- **Evidence architecture.** Every BUILD lesson carries: KO, Documentarian **photo +
  annotation + witness tick**, an **Assessor Witness Statement** (candidate name / date /
  observable action / tier ringed / evidence attached / **assessor declaration +
  signature + date**), and a feedback sheet "kept with your ASDAN portfolio". This is
  strong. **Two deltas vs §2's assessment-record rule (both protected surfaces — reported,
  not edited):**
  1. **No learner signature/declaration.** §2 requires records signed and dated by
     **assessor AND learner**. The witness statement has assessor sign-off only. (0
     occurrences of "learner sign" estate-wide.) → FINDINGS §C, Tier-3/Tier-2.
  2. **Per-activity, not per-criterion.** The witness statement records "activity
     witnessed" and tier, but does not map to a named E3 criterion. §2 requires
     "explicit evidence per criterion". The **Evidence Binder** supplies per-criterion
     capture; the *printed lesson* record does not. → the Binder is the mechanism; confirm
     the workflow is Binder-first (QUESTIONS_FOR_CHERYL §Q6).
