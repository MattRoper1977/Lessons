# ASDAN media and video register

**Checked:** 5 August 2026. Recheck the exact page, captions, availability, safeguarding, adverts, comments, age suitability and school filtering before teaching.

## Source hierarchy

1. **ASDAN** for current qualification purpose, six core skills and portfolio/real-world context.
2. **UK public bodies**: National Careers Service, GOV.UK, Bank of England, MoneyHelper, Food Standards Agency, NHS and HSE.
3. **Established UK education and youth organisations**: Skills Builder Partnership, EEF, Young Enterprise, RHS and #iwill-related government material.

Prefer the official page containing the media rather than a search result or re-upload. Do not autoplay. Use captions. Open the exact segment before pupils arrive. A clip is optional: every lesson below has a no-video route.

## Clip routine

1. Ask the observation or prediction question before play.
2. Play only the shortest segment that contains the required evidence.
3. Freeze the image.
4. Pupils point, select, calculate, order or explain from the frame.
5. Replay once with a second question if useful.
6. Return immediately to the visual manipulation and real task.

Never use a video as evidence that a pupil completed an ASDAN task. It is a teaching source only.
