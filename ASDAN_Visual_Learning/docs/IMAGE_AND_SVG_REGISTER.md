# ASDAN image and SVG register

## Bundled original resources

The pack contains **85 original SVG teaching models**, one for every taught lesson. They use no external font, photograph, logo or third-party asset. Each file has a stable `viewBox`, `<title>`, `<desc>`, visible labels and a static reduced-motion state.

These are **models**, not evidence and not literal photographs. Where pupils need reality, pair the model with the actual approved object, packaging, room, tool, workplace image, local map, project artefact or official source.

## Rights and safeguarding checklist for any added image

Record the creator, title, date, organisation/collection, exact item page, licence or written permission, access date, permitted projection, permitted local saving, permitted adaptation and permitted public-web reuse.

Also confirm:

- no identifiable pupil or audience member without filed consent;
- no real personal financial, medical, identity or tenancy document;
- no visible address, account number, barcode, signature or personal reference;
- no unsafe technique shown without the current local control and adult framing;
- no product image used as an endorsement;
- alt text describes the teaching-relevant evidence rather than appearance alone;
- a collection landing page is not treated as an item-level reuse licence.

## External image types by topic

- **Careers:** official National Careers Service job-profile images or locally approved workplace photographs; pair each image with visible tasks, not stereotypes.
- **Money:** fictional notes, coins, receipts, price cards and budgets. Do not photograph a pupil’s real app, statement, bill or payslip.
- **Food:** official Eatwell Guide, FSA label examples, approved packaging and teacher-prepared ingredient/allergen cards.
- **Community/enterprise:** verified local place, partner and user-context images supplied by staff; never invent a partner or response.
- **Living independently:** staged fictional room, laundry, cleaning and admin examples; obscure all personal details.
- **Workshop/vocational:** HSE or manufacturer/school-approved safety images plus photographs of the actual local equipment taken without pupils.
- **Gardening:** RHS school-gardening images where page-level rights allow, or original photographs of the setting’s own plants and tools.
- **PEQ:** original diagrams of communication, teamwork, goal setting, decision making and reflection rather than generic stock photographs.
