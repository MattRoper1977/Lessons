Humanities — Lundy Loop Subject Guide

Whole-school implementation companion · 2026/27

Dominant dialect: Hybrid: book-based, discursive and fieldwork. Use the dialect of the learning episode; hybrid lessons may move between families.

SPACEAccessible sources, safe discussion structures, clear chronology/context and regulated conditions.
VOICEImproved explanation, source inference, corrected map skill, re-taken fieldwork action or reasoned judgement.
AUDIENCEAdult reads/hears the reasoning and checks use of evidence.
INFLUENCENext source, question, scaffold, fieldwork decision or interpretation changes.
AreaPractice translation
KnowledgeCorrection or elaboration linked to the taught substantive knowledge.
SourcesFeedback targets provenance, inference, utility or corroboration.
Extended responseOne or two next steps linked to argument and evidence.
FieldworkThe practical dialect applies during measuring, observing and recording.
Worked loop
“Add a second source and explain whether it supports the first.” The pupil compares them; the teacher then selects a conflicting source.
Avoid marking theatre
Do not treat opinion alone as a completed response where the curriculum requires evidence and reasoning.
Subject QA prompt: What was the student’s response, how was it genuinely received, and what did it change next?
Codes used naturally: VF WS I NS+ // ? R E. R is a concept first and a written code only where a natural record exists.
