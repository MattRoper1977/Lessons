MADE BY MATT - GROW SCIENCE
Autumn 1, Weeks 3-7 | Ten 40-minute lessons | Revision 6 September 2026

START WITH THE TEACHER GUIDANCE
Each lesson folder has editable teaching slides (PPTX), a slide PDF, pupil
materials in DOCX/PDF, and separate teacher guidance/answers in DOCX/PDF.
The W3B and W5B folders also include optional experiment recording XLSX files.
Read the lesson guidance, select an appropriate supported, standard or stretch
response route, and print only the pupil pages needed. The teacher answers are
kept in separately labelled files.

TEACHING ORDER
Week 3: A - Friction: friend and enemy; B - Test friction on different surfaces.
Week 4: A - Levers, pulleys and gears; B - Compare a lever's pivot positions.
Week 5: A - Planning a fair test; B - Run and judge a fair test.
Week 6: A - Earth and the planets; B - Explore an orbit model.
Week 7: A - The Moon and reflected light; B - Model the Moon's phases.
The current weekly source uses an A lesson followed by a B workshop. The
source Monday/Tuesday labels identify that order; timetable dates are not set.

USE OFFLINE
The Office/PDF materials are self-contained. Native editable diagrams,
discussion, paper sorting and safe demonstrations replace HTML controls and
video-dependent tasks. No publisher videos or images are republished here.
Each lesson gives accessible alternatives and equipment/safety guidance.
Use an observation/director role or the clearly labelled model-data route when
appropriate. Supplied teaching data are not pupil measurements. Keep raw
results, missing runs and separate retries distinct; the spreadsheet does not
replace the practical or justify inventing results.

ACCURATE COVERAGE
This pack covers the ten current taught lessons in Autumn 1 Weeks 3-7. It is
not labelled as a complete half term. Weeks 1-2 are baseline assessment in the
scheme; Standard/Specialist baseline packs have not been relabelled as GROW.
The calendar has an Autumn 1 Week 8 enrichment slot, but no approved GROW
Week 8 source was identified. That slot is not filled by these downloads.
Older accepted HTML teaching versions remain available on the website.

SOURCE AND FORMATS
SOURCE_MANIFEST.json maps each lesson to its exact source-file version and
lists file formats, revision dates, sizes and SHA-256 checksums.
PPTX text, diagrams and speaker notes are editable; DOCX text/tables are
editable. PDF text is searchable. The lesson deck/PDF layouts and every
document page have been rendered and visually reviewed using the available
rendering applications. Native Microsoft Office desktop/mobile applications
and classroom equipment have not been tested.

BRANDING
The supplied approved Made by Matt mark is included unchanged as a JPEG on
the pack guide. Accepted teaching content remains preserved.
