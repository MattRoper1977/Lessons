Slide 1

TA Brief

Current stage

Objective

Describe how moving an opaque card changes its shadow size when the source and screen stay fixed.

Preparation

  • Fix source and screen 100 cm apart. Mark card positions 20, 40 and 50 cm from the source.
  • Keep card and screen upright and parallel, with source aimed at card centre. Use a small LED to reduce blurred edges.
  • Pre-check space and visibility. Exact model numbers are not expected from every real torch.

Method precautions

  • Use a cool low-power LED below face height. No lasers, hot lamps or sunlight.
  • Secure the screen and stands; keep floor paths clear and lit.
  • Do not move the light source or screen during comparisons; an adult checks the setup.

Practical alternative

Use the constructed ideal-model data and diagrams. They describe a point-like source; real measurements can differ because source size and measurement edges matter.

Access and regulation

Supported: Choose one route: short choices, word bank and pointing or adult-scribed explanation; keep the same science goal.

Standard: Use the evidence provided to record a decision and explain the science in short sentences.

Stretch: Apply the same core idea to a changed setup, identify a limitation and justify a better decision.

Regulation: Preview the sequence and equipment. Offer seated observation, a quiet paper route, partner rehearsal and a pass. Never require public performance or a darkened room.

Teacher answers

Retrieval 1–3

1 blocks light transmission. 2 on the screen. 3 no; it is an area receiving less light.

We do W1–W2

W1 shadow height falls as source-card distance grows: 20 cm at distance 20, 10 at 40, 8 at 50. W2 40 cm from source; other two produce 20 cm and 8 cm shadows.

Hinge

First option correct, with source and screen fixed. The card keeps the same actual height.

S1–S5

S1 A, 20 cm from source. S2 8 cm. S3 larger. S4 any two: source position, screen position, card size/orientation, source used. S5 40 cm.

T1–T4

T1 20, 10, 8 cm. T2 e.g. moving from 20 to 50 cm reduces model shadow from 20 to 8 cm. T3 card 40 cm from source, same 4 cm card upright and same source/screen 100 cm apart. T4 a real source has size; edges may blur; measurement/positioning can differ.

X1–X4

X1 larger than the 40 cm-position shadow. X2 between 10 and 20 cm, exclusive. Optional ideal calculation gives about 13.3 cm but is not required. X3 only position changes. X4 no; source-to-screen distance also affects size, so the fixed-screen comparison no longer applies unchanged.

Exit E1–E3

E1 larger. E2 source or screen position, or same card height. E3 40 cm from source.

Optional practical record

Actual values vary. Label as measured observations and retain any discrepancy from the model; do not overwrite them with the ideal numbers.

R1–R2

Responses vary. Credit a science-related revision or relevant next question. A pass or private response is acceptable; preferences have no fixed right answer.
