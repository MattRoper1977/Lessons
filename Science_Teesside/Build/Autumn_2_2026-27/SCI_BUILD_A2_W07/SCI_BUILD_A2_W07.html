Verbatim SoW objective: Review and evidence learning on skeletons, nutrition & rocks (Entry Level Science / UAS).

Arrival · 5 min

Pick a safe model or card to look at.

Offer a safe kit choice and short first–then cue.

Starter · 3 min

Think back. Say, sign or point.

  1. What stays the same in a fair water test?
  2. Is a finished brick raw or made?
  3. Where do animals get food?

Today I will check:

  • I can show one fact.
  • I can show what I used to find it.
  • I can name one next step.

Ask three low-stakes prior questions; answers in note.

I do · 5 min

Watch one fact with one clue.

Words: skeleton · nutrition · rock · evidence

Watch: I point to the bone model. It shows a skeleton. The bones help hold the body up.

Show me: Which object can show a fact about a rock: a safe sample or a food picture?

Pre-teach words; show the full model; ask the show-me check.

We do · 7 min

Match a real model to a fact.

Our turn: I point to a safe rock. I felt a rough side. My fact is ____.

Talk, then sort.

  • Bone model
  • Food-group picture
  • Safe rough sample

Ready to sort.

F · help

Point to one model. What can it show?

M · say why

Match one fact to the model or action that supports it.

H · stretch

Which fact needs a new check rather than a card?

Hand over the missing step; offer F or H route. Reset once.

We do 2 · 5 min

Choose one answer. All of us show it.

What best backs a claim about a rock surface?

Choose a step.

All of us: A class claims a clean plastic lid floats. Which evidence could check the claim?

Say why.

Take one quiet minute to reset.

Take all pupil responses at once; use decision rule and parallel hinge.

Independent · 8 min

Show work you did about body, food or rock.

Say it first. You can plan in any language.

Point, sign, speak, draw, type, write, use a scribe or a staff-captured photo.

F

Show one fact about a body or rock.

Say: I show ____.

M

Show two facts. Tell how you found one.

Say: I know ____ as I saw ____.

H

Pick your best real evidence. Why does it fit?

Say: This is good evidence as ____.

Go deeper: Pick a claim you cannot show. What could you check?

Evidence judge: Pick the best real result. Why does it fit?

Choose one adult for supported pupils, one for group; swap across the week.

Try it with kit · prepared model

Kit: Clean adult-checked model or rock, matching fact cards, tray

  1. Choose one checked model or sample and match an observed fact to it.

Risk: A loose or rough model part could scratch or fall.

Safe: Use checked blunt models on a tray. Keep small parts together and wash hands after shared kit.

Check the school risk assessment and exact prepared kit. This is optional model rehearsal, not a claimed core practical.
