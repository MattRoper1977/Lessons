Skeletons and classification

Skull protects brain. Ribs protect heart and lungs. Bones support the body. Vertebrate: backbone. Invertebrate: no backbone.

A muscle pair

Biceps contracts. Triceps relaxes. To straighten: triceps contracts, biceps relaxes.

Nutrition and body jobs

Carbohydrate: energy. Protein: growth and repair. Calcium: bones and teeth. Foods contain several nutrients. A varied diet provides different nutrients. Sugar per 100 g is only one part of nutrition evidence.

Balance and evidence

Narrow 2/5. Wide 5/5. Same model height and mass. Five similar taps. Wider feet: upright 5/5; narrow: 2/5. Invented teaching data.

Food sources and transfer

GRASS → RABBIT → FOX. Grass makes food using light. Arrows show food and energy passing to the animal that eats it.

Connected explanation

Food provides energy. Muscles use energy to contract and pull bones at joints. Models help explain this, but simplify real bodies.

Model steps

  1. The biceps contracts and pulls on the forearm.
  2. The triceps relaxes while the elbow bends.
  3. For the reverse movement, the partner muscle pulls.

Skeletons and classification

Skull protects brain. Ribs protect heart and lungs. Bones support the body. Vertebrate: backbone. Invertebrate: no backbone.

A muscle pair

Biceps contracts. Triceps relaxes. To straighten: triceps contracts, biceps relaxes.

Nutrition and body jobs

Carbohydrate: energy. Protein: growth and repair. Calcium: bones and teeth. Foods contain several nutrients. A varied diet provides different nutrients. Sugar per 100 g is only one part of nutrition evidence.

Balance and evidence

Narrow 2/5. Wide 5/5. Same model height and mass. Five similar taps. Wider feet: upright 5/5; narrow: 2/5. Invented teaching data.

Food sources and transfer

GRASS → RABBIT → FOX. Grass makes food using light. Arrows show food and energy passing to the animal that eats it.

Connected explanation

Food provides energy. Muscles use energy to contract and pull bones at joints. Models help explain this, but simplify real bodies.

Model steps

  1. The biceps contracts and pulls on the forearm.
  2. The triceps relaxes while the elbow bends.
  3. For the reverse movement, the partner muscle pulls.
