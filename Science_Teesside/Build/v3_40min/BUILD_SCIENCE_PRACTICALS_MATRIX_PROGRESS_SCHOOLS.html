Practicals & Equipment Matrix · W3–W7

One page per half-term: what each investigation needs, how it is made safe, and the two routes that keep every pupil in the practical — a digital alternative and a no-equipment fallback. A pupil who cannot handle materials still directs the investigation.

Safety authority. No CLEAPSS approval is claimed for any activity on this page. Each activity requires a local, proportionate risk assessment before use; local COSHH/risk-assessment decisions determine the actual controls, and a headteacher signature alone is not sufficient. An adult does all cutting and piercing — pupils never cut card, punch holes or fit fasteners. W5–W7 food topics use printed fictional resources only: no tasting, no real food in the room, no allergy exposure, and no pupil is asked about their own meals.

The two access routes are part of the practical, not a concession. Every investigation runs three ways — hands-on, digital, or directed — and all three produce the same conceptual evidence. Choosing the directed route records how the pupil accessed the investigation; it says nothing about ability.
