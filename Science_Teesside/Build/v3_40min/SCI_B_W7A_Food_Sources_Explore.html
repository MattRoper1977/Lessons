Animals Cannot Make Their Own Food

🎯 Learning objective: I can explain that animals get food by eating, while plants can make their own food.
✅ Success criteria
  • State that animals cannot make their own food.
  • Match animals to plausible food sources.
  • Build a simple food-source path and explain it.
