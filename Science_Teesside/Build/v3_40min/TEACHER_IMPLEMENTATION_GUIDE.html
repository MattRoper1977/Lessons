BUILD Science v3 · Teacher Implementation Guide

How to keep the lessons exciting without allowing the activity to replace the learning.

1 · The non-negotiable lesson grammar

Arrival retrieves the actual previous lesson. Starter creates curiosity without requiring today’s knowledge. I Do shows the invisible Science. We Do requires a genuine response. A second I Do/We Do is used only when the concept needs another model. Independent work produces attributable evidence. Exit is received by an adult.

2 · BUILD closure

BUILD closes through genuine adult Audience: read, listen or watch the pupil response and name back what was understood. Influence is the calibrated next try. Do not add a compulsory pupil-written closing line or adult signature.

3 · Support staff

Least prompt first: wait → self-prompt → visual cue → general verbal → specific verbal → model → direct support. Move back left as soon as the pupil can continue. Fine-motor, reading, sensory or communication barriers change the route, not the Science goal.

4 · Body & food safeguard

  • Teach on models, diagrams, cards and fictional scenarios — not pupils’ bodies.
  • No calorie, weight, restriction or good/bad-food language.
  • No personal meal judgement or forced disclosure.
  • Food tasks are classification/function/design models, not diet plans.

5 · Evidence rule

Strong evidence has a pupil decision or response, observable Science, and pupil meaning. Photos can be useful, but a photo alone usually proves only that a product existed.

Pupils joining mid-year: every arrival slide carries a read-it-here route.
