7 · Physical twin

Metre-rule counterbalance · 5–8 minutes
  • Equipment: metre rule, stable pivot, two equal slotted masses or sealed mass bags, paperclip payload.
  • Keep feet clear; use small classroom masses only; teacher checks the pivot.
  1. Balance equal masses at equal distances.
  2. Add the payload to one side and observe the turn.
  3. Move or add the counter-mass until level.
  4. Record what changed and what did not.
