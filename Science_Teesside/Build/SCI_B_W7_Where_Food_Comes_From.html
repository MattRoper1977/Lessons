Animals Cannot Make Their Own Food

Aut1·W7 — Explain that animals cannot make their own food; they get it from what they eat.

White Rose Science Year 3Pearson Entry Level Science 8939 (E1→E3); AQA UAS science units

✅ Success Criteria

  • I can say that plants make their own food.
  • I can say that animals must eat to get theirs.
  • I can put a simple food chain in the right order.

👩‍🏫 I Do · watch me think

Watch me read a food chain

🧠 Watch — do not write yet. Everybody gets the arrow wrong the first time. Watch which way it points.
producer — a plant. It MAKES its own food.
consumer — an animal. It EATS to get its food.
👩‍🏫 I Do · the same chain, written backwards

Now watch me read a wrong one

🧠 Watch me think: Same three living things. Same two arrows. Somebody has just put them the wrong way round.
food chain — a picture of who eats what, in order.
energy — what passes along the chain when something is eaten.
🤝 We Do · say it before I click

Producer or consumer?

Tap one. Everybody decides. THEN I click. Can it make its own food, or does it have to eat?
🤝 We Do · take the plant away

Why these chains start with a plant

Do not tell me yet. Watch what is left when the grass goes. When you know why, hand on your head.
The plant makes its own food in this chain. So the energy has to come into the chain there.
👩‍🏫 I Do 2 · the evidence loop

Evidence loop — because, and so

The fox is a consumer because it cannot make its own food, so it has to eat.

Food-chain change investigation

  1. 4 min: Find Monday’s chain of three. Follow an arrow. Say who gets the food.
  2. 20 min: Build both chains with the paper labels. Make one arrow point the wrong way. Use the feeding clues to fix it. Cover the plant in one chain. Predict what could change for the next two living things. Say why.
  3. 2 min: Pens down if you are writing; pause and check. Check an arrow and one prediction with an adult or a partner. Use the feeding clue. The order of the names alone will not tell us why.
  4. 6 min: Put the chain back or change it. Say one thing that could happen. Say one thing the model cannot tell us. Save a labelled picture. Clear the table.

Access and equipment: Use printed names of living things, arrow strips and a paper cover. Do not collect or feed real plants or animals. You can tell an adult where to move each card.

Knowledge Organiser — Animals Cannot Make Their Own Food

BUILD · White Rose Science Year 3 · Week 7

Key wordWhat it means
food chainA picture of who eats what, in order.
producerA plant. It MAKES its own food.
consumerAn animal. It EATS to get food.
energyWhat passes along the chain when something is eaten.

Key facts

  • Plants make their own food using light. Animals cannot do this.
  • The food chains in this lesson start with a green plant.
  • The arrow in a food chain means 'is eaten by' and points the way the energy travels.
  • Grass → rabbit → fox. The grass made its own; the rabbit and the fox had to eat.

Success criteria

  • I can say that plants make their own food.
  • I can say that animals must eat to get theirs.
  • I can put a simple food chain in the right order.

Adult notes — access & sensory
