# BUILD Science Weeks 8–13 — curriculum alignment

This pack follows the operational BUILD planning calendar: the first half-term runs through Week 8, so Week 8 closes the body-science sequence before the rocks sequence starts in Week 9.

| Week | Explore | Do | Curriculum purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | Sugar Evidence: Read the Label | Body Science Checkpoint | Compare sugar values on the same basis, then preserve evidence across the body-science sequence. |
| 9 | Rock Evidence Lab: Look Before You Label | Build a Rock Sorting Key | Observe supplied rocks, group them from evidence and create a repeatable key without claiming identity from appearance. |
| 10 | Rock Hardness: Mark or Groove? | Hardness Evidence Lab | Use a safe relative scratch/mark test and distinguish hardness from strength or a formal Mohs value. |
| 11 | Permeability: Surface or Through? | Water Through Rock Lab | Distinguish surface wetting/absorption from passage through a prepared sample and record bounded evidence. |
| 12 | Rock Jobs: Which Property Matters? | Choose Rock for the Job | Connect observed properties to a stated use while keeping claims inside the classroom evidence. |
| 13 | Fair Test Planner: Change One Thing | Method Pilot: Test the Test | Plan and pilot a fair permeability comparison, revise the method and make a W14 readiness decision. |

Week 13 deliberately stops at the method pilot. The full supplied-sample run, final results and conclusion belong to Week 14 and are outside this six-week pack.
