Six-week sequence

Week 8 · Explore

Sugar Evidence: Read the Label

Outcome: Sugar investigation and Autumn 1 consolidation: skeletons, muscles and nutrition.

Equipment: Four fictional or photocopied nutrition-label cards on the same per-100 g basis; number line; results strip; pencil; optional clean empty packets as visual references.

Open W8A · Explore

Week 8 · Do

Body Science Checkpoint

Outcome: Sugar investigation and Autumn 1 consolidation: skeletons, muscles and nutrition.

Equipment: Skeleton or labelled diagram; vertebrate/invertebrate cards; safe card-and-split-pin arm model; nutrition/body-job cards; food-group mats; simple food-chain cards; tiered checkpoint sheet.

Open W8B · Do

Week 9 · Explore

Rock Evidence Lab: Look Before You Label

Outcome: Compare and group rocks by their appearance and simple properties.

Equipment: Six teacher-pre-screened coded educational rock specimens or macro cards; white trays; hand lenses; feature tags; two hoops or sorting mats; Rock Comparison Ticket.

Open W9A · Explore

Week 9 · Do

Build a Rock Sorting Key

Outcome: Compare and group rocks by their appearance and simple properties.

Equipment: Coded rock specimens or macro cards from W9A; yes/no mats; criterion cards for crystals, grains, bands/planes and holes; Mystery T; Sorting Key Case Card.

Open W9B · Do

Week 10 · Explore

Rock Hardness: Mark or Groove?

Outcome: Rocks: test hardness.

Equipment: Two teacher-pretested coded dry rock samples; stable trays; secured blunt scratch jig or teacher demonstration; fixed guide; wipes; hand lens; result card; eye protection where required by local assessment.

Open W10A · Explore

Week 10 · Do

Hardness Evidence Lab

Outcome: Rocks: test hardness.

Equipment: Four teacher-pretested coded dry samples; stable individual trays; secured blunt scratch jig or teacher demonstration; fixed guide; wipes; hand lens; four-row result table; eye protection where assessed.

Open W10B · Do

Week 11 · Explore

Permeability: Surface or Through?

Outcome: Test rocks for whether they let water through (permeability).

Equipment: Cross-section picture cards, water-route arrows, observation cards, three-zone evidence sheet and pencil or communication aid.

Open W11A · Explore

Week 11 · Do

Water Through Rock Lab

Outcome: Test rocks for whether they let water through (permeability).

Equipment: Staff-built sealed rigs; pre-inspected prepared sample pieces; stable trays; 5 mL needle-free pipettes or syringes; marked collectors; 120-second timer; wipes; result sheet.

Open W11B · Do

Week 12 · Explore

Rock Jobs: Which Property Matters?

Outcome: Identify everyday uses of different rocks (links D&T / Construction).

Equipment: Four supplied job briefs, W10 scratch and W11 water evidence icons, property-question grid and pencil or communication aid.

Open W12A · Explore

Week 12 · Do

Choose Rock for the Job

Outcome: Identify everyday uses of different rocks (links D&T / Construction).

Equipment: Fictional job briefs, candidate evidence matrix, decision sheet, audience question card and pencil or communication aid.

Open W12B · Do

Week 13 · Explore

Fair Test Planner: Change One Thing

Outcome: Plan a simple fair test on rocks; pilot the method ready for the full investigation.

Equipment: Control cards, diagram of staff-built sealed rig, method planner, blank final results table, pilot checklist and pencil or communication aid.

Open W13A · Explore

Week 13 · Do

Method Pilot: Test the Test

Outcome: Pilot and revise a simple fair-test method ready for the full rock investigation.

Equipment: One clearly labelled staff-prepared practice rig or fixed pilot log; stable tray; 5 mL needle-free tool; 120-second timer; collector; wipes; method V1/V2 sheet; readiness card.

Open W13B · Do
