Water Through Rock Lab

Sequence outcome: Test rocks for whether they let water through (permeability).

🎯 Learning objective: I can follow or direct a fixed water-passage method, record what reaches the collector and make a careful comparison of supplied samples.
✅ Success criteria
  • Follow the fixed volume, time and sealed-route instructions safely.
  • Record surface observations and water collected below in mL.
  • Compare the supplied samples under this method without claiming waterproof forever.

Make the first scientific idea visible

PERMEABILITYhow water passes throughthe ability of a material to let water travel through connected spaces
VOLUMEamount of liquidthe amount of liquid measured here in millilitres
VALIDthe method worked as stateda result produced without the named fault or safety stop

WHAT THIS MODEL HELPS US SEE

Check in order: seal held → 5 mL added → 120 s timed → collector read in mL → claim names supplied samples and method.

WHAT THIS MODEL DOES NOT SHOW

The lab uses a short classroom test on prepared samples. Natural variation, tiny cracks, sample thickness and sealing can affect results; the test is not a full building-performance check.

Step 1

Staff check the stable tray, pre-inspected matched pieces, sealed collar and clear collector. Pupils may operate only agreed safe steps or direct an adult.

Step 2

Add the fixed 5 mL with a needle-free tool, start the 120-second timer and observe top, underside and collector without moving the rig.

Step 3

Record the amount collected below in mL and note surface-only, absorbed or passed-through evidence. Record a fault instead of inventing a result.

Word help keeps the scientific term visible and adds a plain-language bridge.

👩‍🏫 I Do 2 · connect the second idea

Connect the model to the second idea

Connection: the model is useful when its visible pattern is translated into a scientific explanation.
Connection 1

Compare collected-water values only after checking that both runs were valid. A lower value means less passage was observed in the stated time, not proven permanent waterproofing.

Connection 2

Notice what the fixed method tried to hold steady: starting volume, time, collar area and prepared thickness. W13 will name and test these method controls directly.

Connection 3

End with one careful sentence: Under this method, more water passed through supplied Sample __. One result set is useful evidence, but samples and repeats can vary.

🛡 Safety / sensoryOnly staff build and approve rigs. No hammering, chipping, scratching, licking, tasting, smelling or dust blowing. Keep samples on trays, contain spills, stop for leaks/damage/flaking/dust and wash hands. A director or fixed-data route is full participation.
