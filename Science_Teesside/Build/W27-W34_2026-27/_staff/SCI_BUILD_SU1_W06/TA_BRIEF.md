# SCI_BUILD_SU1_W06 · TA deployment brief

**Objective:** Explain how water travels through a plant.

## EEF five-layer scaffolding key

**Self-scaffold → prompt → clue → model → correct.** Move down only when the pupil can't go on; move back up as soon as they can. Start by waiting, checking the pupil’s own attempt and using the existing visual, communication or reading aid. An access aid is not a content answer.

1. **Self-scaffold — watch for:** Orders the supplied water-transport stages. Let the pupil check the source, key, diagram or own work before speaking.
2. **Prompt — two open questions:** “Where does soil water enter?” “Why does an upward arrow follow the stem?” Pause after each; accept point, sign, AAC, speech or writing.
3. **Clue — two narrowed questions:** “Which part is next after the roots?” “Where do the arrows go from the stem?” Give one clue at a time and then step back.
4. **Model — one step only:** Show one first step: Begin at a soil-water arrow and the root card. Then the pupil tries the next matching move with the source in view.
5. **Correct — one direct fact and when:** If the pupil still cannot continue after that model, give only “Roots.” for “Where does soil water enter the plant?”. Then ask for a fresh application, record the level used and fade back toward self-scaffolding.
