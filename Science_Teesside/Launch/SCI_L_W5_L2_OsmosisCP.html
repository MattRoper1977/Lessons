I do 2

3 minutes

I do 2 · make the reasoning visible

  1. Worked example only: initial 5.00 g; final 4.50 g.
  2. Change = 4.50 − 5.00 = −0.50 g.
  3. Percentage = −0.50 ÷ 5.00 × 100 = −10%.
  4. Negative sign: mass loss, consistent with net water leaving.

Watch for this mistake

A negative percentage change means the experiment failed.

A negative change is a mass loss; it can be a valid result consistent with net water leaving the tissue.

Knowledge organiser

LAUNCH SCIENCE · KNOWLEDGE ORGANISER

Osmosis practical and data

Learning objective: I can identify a fair osmosis method and calculate percentage change in mass from identified measurements.

Success looks like

  • Name the changed, measured and controlled variables.
  • Calculate percentage change using initial mass.
  • Explain the sign using net water movement.

Keep these ideas

  • Change the sucrose concentration; measure mass before and after exposure.
  • Keep tissue source, dimensions, solution volume, temperature and exposure time comparable.
  • Blot consistently before weighing so surface liquid does not distort the comparison.

Schematic teaching model, not to scale. Use stated measurements, not screen size.

Word help

initial mass — Mass before immersion.

final mass — Mass after the stated immersion interval and consistent blotting.

percentage change — (Final − initial) ÷ initial × 100.

control variable — A condition kept the same for a fair comparison.

Worked example

Worked example only: initial 5.00 g; final 4.50 g.

Change = 4.50 − 5.00 = −0.50 g.
