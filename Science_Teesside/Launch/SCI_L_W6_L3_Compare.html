I do 2

3 minutes

I do 2 · make the reasoning visible

  1. Compare diffusion and active transport using direction.
  2. Diffusion has net movement down a gradient; active transport can move against it.
  3. Compare using energy: active transport uses cellular energy; diffusion does not require it to drive the net flow.
  4. Both involve movement of substances, but that similarity does not remove the differences.

Watch for this mistake

All three processes are the same because particles move.

They share movement, but differ in the substance specified, gradient, membrane requirement and cellular energy use.

LAUNCH SCIENCE · KNOWLEDGE ORGANISER

Compare the three transport processes

Learning objective: I can identify diffusion, osmosis and active transport from substance, gradient, membrane and energy evidence.

Success looks like

  • Choose a process from a fully stated case.
  • Compare the processes using the same features.
  • Explain why an arrow alone may be insufficient.

Keep these ideas

  • Diffusion: net movement down a concentration gradient.
  • Osmosis: net water movement through a partially permeable membrane, from more dilute to more concentrated solution.
  • Active transport: movement against a concentration gradient using cellular energy.

Schematic teaching model, not to scale. Use stated measurements, not screen size.

Word help

passive — Not driven by a supply of cellular energy.

net — Overall, after opposite movements are compared.

membrane — A boundary that controls movement into and out of a cell.

comparison — A linked statement about two or more things.

Worked example

Compare diffusion and active transport using direction.

Diffusion has net movement down a gradient; active transport can move against it.

Compare using energy: active transport uses cellular energy; diffusion does not require it to drive the net flow.

Both involve movement of substances, but that similarity does not remove the differences.

A useful correction
They share movement, but differ in the substance specified, gradient, membrane requirement and cellular energy use.
