I do · 5 min

Watch the full model. Point to the step that answers today’s question.

Words

WordMeaningExample
differentiationa cell becoming specialised for a particular jobA developing skin cell differentiates to do a job in skin.
stem cella cell that can divide and form specialised cell typesSome adult stem cells make replacement blood cells.
meristema plant region whose cells divide to make new cellsA root tip contains a meristem.
growthan increase in size, including by making more cellsA shoot grows as its cells divide and elongate.

Full model: In skin, mitosis adds cells; some stem-cell descendants differentiate for a tissue job. At a shoot tip, meristem cells divide, then elongate and differentiate.

Show me: Show me: does making more cells on its own mean those cells now have a specialised job?

Answer: No; cell differentiation gives specialised roles.

Model one complete example; check every pupil’s response to the show-me question.

Complete the missing step in the part-done model. Speak, then match the cards.

One-minute reset: use the agreed move, then return to one step.

Part-done model: Skin: mitosis adds cells → differentiation gives a job. Shoot: meristem adds cells → ________ lengthens cells → differentiation.

Speak first: Speaking: “First cells divide. Then some cells …”

F · support: Use two labelled branches (animal tissue and plant tip); pupils place the missing elongation card and point to the corresponding change.

M · explain: Explain which animal and plant steps both make more cells and which give new jobs.

H · stretch: Explain why a shoot can increase in length even after the division step has ended.

Independent · 8 min

One-minute reset: use the agreed move, then return to one step.

Talk before write: Speaking: “A plant also …, whereas an animal …”

You can plan in any language.

F: Place divide, elongate and differentiate cards on the skin/shoot branches; omit elongate on the skin branch. In the shoot, first cells …; then they …

M: Explain why mitosis alone does not give a new cell its specialised tissue job. More cells are made by …, but a job develops when …

H: Compare growth in animal tissue with a plant shoot, including an appropriate stem-cell region and what each process adds. Both organisms …; however, the plant also … because …

Go deeper: A tissue gains more cells but no new cell types. What does the model explain, and what is missing?
