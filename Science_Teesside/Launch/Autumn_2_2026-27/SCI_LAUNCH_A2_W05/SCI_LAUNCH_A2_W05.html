Full model: Place A/a atop the grid for one parent; place a/a at its side for the other. Pair one allele from each edge: Aa, aa, Aa, aa. Two of four boxes are aa: a 1 in 2 chance per offspring, not an exact family count.

Show me: Show me: which allele can the aa parent pass on?

Answer: Only a.

Model one complete example; check every pupil’s response to the show-me question.
