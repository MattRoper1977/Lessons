Words

cell cycle — the series of steps in which a cell grows and divides
The cell cycle includes interphase, mitosis and cytokinesis.
interphase — the stage in which the cell grows and copies its DNA
The cell copies its DNA in interphase.
mitosis — the stage in which copied chromosomes separate into two nuclei
Mitosis separates the chromosome sets.
chromosome — a structure of DNA that carries genes
Each new nucleus receives a matching set of chromosomes.
cytokinesis — the division of the cell after the nuclei form
Cytokinesis splits one cell into two.
prophase — the mitosis phase when chromosomes condense
In prophase, chromosomes become easier to see.
metaphase — the mitosis phase when chromosomes line up in the middle
In metaphase, chromosomes form a line.
anaphase — the mitosis phase when the two copies of each chromosome are pulled to opposite ends
In anaphase, the two copies of each chromosome are pulled to opposite ends.
telophase — the mitosis phase when two new nuclei form
In telophase, each set gains a nucleus.

Full model: Interphase: copy DNA. Mitosis: prophase (condense), metaphase (line up), anaphase (the two copies of each chromosome are pulled to opposite ends), telophase (two nuclei). Cytokinesis: split the cell.

Show me: is DNA copied before, during or after mitosis?

Answer: Before mitosis, in interphase.
