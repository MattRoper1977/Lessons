Knowledge organiser

LAUNCH · A2 W7 · Lesson 3 · Shared resource

Learning objective

Show current understanding of the taught Autumn 2 content, then choose a precise next learning step.

Key words

Describe — Say what happens or what the evidence shows.

Explain — Connect a statement to a scientific reason.

Calculate — Use values and show the working.

Evaluate — Weigh evidence and identify a limitation.

Evidence — A value, observation or source supporting a claim.

Independent event — An outcome that does not determine the next outcome.

Remember

• Copy DNA before mitosis; each daughter cell retains the chromosome set.

• Stem cell evidence needs benefits, risks and uncertainty.

• A gene is a section of DNA; DNA has complementary base pairs.

• A genetic cross predicts a chance for each fertilisation.

Success looks like

• Show your own understanding under agreed conditions.

• Keep original evidence distinct from later correction.

• Choose a precise next learning step after feedback.

TA Brief

Current stage

Current stage:

Preparation and safety

No biological samples are needed. Use cards, diagrams or an adult-directed model. Do not require cutting, standing, personal disclosure or physical contact.

Scientific care

This classroom assessment covers the Autumn 2 content taught in this sequence, not every item in Edexcel Topics 2 and 3. It is newly authored, not a past paper or an exam-board assessment. Record raw evidence and access arrangements; do not convert the score into an invented GCSE grade.

Responsive feedback

During We do, the lesson teacher is the named audience. Offer one optional request: clearer labels, fewer cards, reading help, adult-directed handling or more thinking time. Explain what can change, try it and check its effect. Passing or asking privately is valid. No compulsory form or written conversion of speech. Do not infer school marking codes from clicks, saved answers or a completed box.
