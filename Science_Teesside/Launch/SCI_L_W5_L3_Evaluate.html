I do 2

3 minutes

I do 2 · make the reasoning visible

  1. At 0.4 mol dm⁻³ the percentages are −3, −4 and −5.
  2. Mean = (−3 − 4 − 5) ÷ 3 = −4%.
  3. Between +4% at 0.2 and −4% at 0.4, zero is about 0.3 mol dm⁻³.
  4. This is an estimated zero-mass-change condition, not an exact measurement of cell sap.

Watch for this mistake

The zero crossing proves the exact concentration of every cell.

It is an estimate for this tissue, method and exposure, with measurement and biological uncertainty.

LAUNCH SCIENCE · KNOWLEDGE ORGANISER

Evaluating the osmosis evidence

Learning objective: I can plot osmosis data, estimate a zero-change concentration and evaluate the method.

Success looks like

  • Calculate means and plot labelled axes.
  • Estimate where the line crosses zero.
  • Link a method improvement to a specific limitation.

Keep these ideas

  • Plot concentration on the horizontal axis and percentage mass change on the vertical axis.
  • Preserve negative values; zero mass change is not zero concentration.
  • The zero crossing estimates the solution giving no net mass change under those conditions.

Schematic teaching model, not to scale. Use stated measurements, not screen size.

Word help

mean — The sum of values divided by the number of values.

anomaly — A result that does not fit the pattern of the other results.

interpolation — Estimating between measured values.

evaluation — A judgement supported by evidence and its limitations.

Worked example

At 0.4 mol dm⁻³ the percentages are −3, −4 and −5.

Mean = (−3 − 4 − 5) ÷ 3 = −4%.

Between +4% at 0.2 and −4% at 0.4, zero is about 0.3 mol dm⁻³.

This is an estimated zero-mass-change condition, not an exact measurement of cell sap.

A useful correction
It is an estimate for this tissue, method and exposure, with measurement and biological uncertainty.
