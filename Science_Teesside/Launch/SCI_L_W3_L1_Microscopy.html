LAUNCH SCIENCE · KNOWLEDGE ORGANISER

Cells under the microscope

Learning objective: I can use a microscope model to explain magnification, resolution and safe observation.

Success looks like

  • Identify the eyepiece, objective and stage.
  • Calculate total lens magnification.
  • Distinguish a larger image from more detail.

Keep these ideas

  • A light microscope uses light and lenses to form an enlarged image.
  • Total lens magnification = eyepiece magnification × objective magnification.
  • Start with the lowest-power objective and follow the equipment instructions.

Schematic teaching model, not to scale. Use stated measurements, not screen size.

Word help

eyepiece — The lens you look through.

objective — The lens close to the specimen.

magnification — How many times larger the image is than the object.

resolution — The ability to distinguish two close points as separate.

Worked example

Eyepiece = ×10; objective = ×20.

Total = 10 × 20 = ×200.

Changing to ×40 gives ×400 overall.

That calculation alone says nothing about the resolution.

A useful correction
Enlargement and resolving detail are different; an enlarged blur can remain a blur.
