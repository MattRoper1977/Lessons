Review

2 minutes

Review · use one piece of evidence

Choose one response already made. Compare it with the model or a useful comment from your adult. Change one part when that helps, or explain why it is already supported.

No separate reflection sheet is needed. An adult listens, reads or watches the response and uses it to choose a useful next example.

Use item-level responses to choose the next lesson or revisit. Do not chase a total score or require a separate reflection form.
