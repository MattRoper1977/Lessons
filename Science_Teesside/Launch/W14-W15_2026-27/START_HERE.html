Genetic conditions: research and present with care

Three complete 40-minute lessons turn workbook cell C44 — “Research a genetic condition online; present.” — into a private, evidence-led sequence that works without a runtime connection.

🧬 1BI0 Foundation core📍 C44 only⏱ 3 × 40 minutes🛡 no personal disclosure

Held local candidate: nothing in this pack is pushed, merged, deployed or catalogued. The three lessons and this front door are teaching and potential evidence only. Missing source evidence, qualification and learner voice must remain visible rather than being reconstructed.

Three-lesson sequence

W14L1 · Introduce · 40 minutes

Frame a safe research question

Bound the C44 question, plan two approved sources, separate biological explanation from personal or medical claims, and name the uncertainty that the final presentation must preserve.

Open W14L1 · Introduce

W14L2 · Explore · 40 minutes

Triangulate source evidence

Record publisher, date, purpose and locator; paraphrase relevant evidence; compare agreement and difference; then write a bounded finding without copying or overclaiming.

Open W14L2 · Explore

W14L3 · Do · 40 minutes

Present evidence with care

Build and deliver an accessible explanation, credit two approved sources, receive one audience question and record whether evidence supports a revision, hold or safe redirect.

Qualification and safeguarding boundary

Staff qualification / truth boundary

The core route is Edexcel GCSE Biology 1BI0 Foundation / Combined Science 1SC0 Foundation. Optional IGCSE 4BI1/4CH1/4PH1 depth is staff-only stretch for eligible pupils and never governs the class route. Legacy 4SS0 framing is not governing.

No diagnosis or medical advice. No personal or family information. Do not ask for symptoms, test results, treatment choices or family histories. Redirect personal concerns through the school's approved safeguarding or health-support route.

Open local source drawer

The HTML opens no live URL. Prepare teacher-approved local extracts and source cards before teaching. Each used idea needs a publisher, date or review date, purpose and local locator. If the approved set cannot answer a question, keep the gap visible and name the responsible next check.

Across all routes, change access before demand: pupils may point, sign, type, use a diagram, present privately or direct exact-word scribing. The scientific decision remains the learner's.
