LAUNCH SCIENCE · KNOWLEDGE ORGANISER

Command words: answer the question

Learning objective: I can adapt a science response to describe, explain, compare, calculate or evaluate.

Success looks like

  • Identify what a command word asks for.
  • Use the same evidence in different ways.
  • Give a supported judgement rather than an unsupported opinion.

Keep these ideas

  • Calculate asks for a numerical answer with relevant working and units where appropriate.
  • Describe reports a pattern; explain adds a scientific reason.
  • Compare must relate both things; follow any wording asking specifically for similarities and differences.

Schematic teaching model, not to scale. Use stated measurements, not screen size.

Word help

describe — Give an account of a pattern or event.

explain — Connect a statement with a reason.

compare — Relate similarities or differences between the named things.

evaluate — Weigh evidence and limitations to reach a supported judgement.

Worked example

Question: evaluate a diffusion comparison with one trial at each temperature.

Evidence: the higher-temperature trial reaches the same endpoint sooner.

Limitation: one trial at each setting does not show variation.

Judgement: the evidence is consistent with the explanation, but repeats are needed to check repeatability.
