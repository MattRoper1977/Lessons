Stem Cells and Meristems: Potential Compared

Sequence outcome: Edexcel GCSE Biology 1BI0 Foundation · Paper 1 · Topic 2 · specification point 2.8, embryonic and adult animal stem cells and plant meristems.

🎯 Learning objective: I can compare embryonic stem cells, adult tissue stem cells and plant meristem cells using evidence about self-renewal, differentiation range and function.
✅ Success criteria
  • Define a stem cell as an unspecialised cell that can divide and differentiate.
  • Compare the wider differentiation potential of embryonic stem cells with the more limited range of many adult tissue stem cells.
  • Explain how meristem cells support continued plant growth and can form specialised plant tissues.

WHAT THIS MODEL HELPS US SEE

COMPARE with at least one similarity and one difference. Use source → evidence → differentiation range → limitation so a headline does not outrun the dataset.

WHAT THIS MODEL DOES NOT SHOW

A branching diagram makes developmental choices look fixed and equal. Real differentiation depends on signals, gene activity and conditions; the model omits failure, cell death, timing and ethical questions reserved for the next sequence.

Step 1

Use a branching model: one route maintains a stem-cell population through self-renewal; another route produces cells that begin differentiation.

Step 2

Place embryonic stem cells on a broad animal-cell branch and adult tissue stem cells on a narrower tissue-related branch, while noting that exact potential depends on type and conditions.

Step 3

Place meristem cells on plant branches leading to roots, shoots and specialised plant tissues; do not merge animal potency labels directly with plant development.
