# LAUNCH GCSE Biology Weeks 8–13 — curriculum alignment

The pack continues the existing LAUNCH cadence of Introduce → Explore → Do and develops Pearson Edexcel GCSE Biology 1BI0 Foundation knowledge and scientific reasoning across six weeks.

| Week | Introduce | Explore | Do | Curriculum purpose |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | Enzyme Action: Preparing the pH Investigation | Amylase and pH: Continuous-Sampling Investigation | Amylase Rate, Graph and Topic 1 Consolidation | Prepare, carry out and evaluate the pH/amylase investigation using the centre-approved method, then consolidate Topic 1. |
| 9 | Cell Cycle: Copy, Check, Divide | Mitosis Sequence: Chromosome Evidence | Mitosis: Identical Daughter Cells | Distinguish interphase, mitosis and cytokinesis; use visible chromosome evidence; apply mitosis to growth, repair and asexual reproduction. |
| 10 | Growth and Differentiation: Building an Organism | Stem Cells and Meristems: Potential Compared | Growth and Stem-Cell Data: Evidence Before Claims | Connect division, growth and differentiation, compare stem-cell/meristem potential and evaluate simulated data cautiously. |
| 11 | Stem Cells: Evidence Before Judgement | Stem-Cell Evidence: Benefit, Risk, Uncertainty | Stem Cells: Structured DISCUSS | Separate evidence, uncertainty and ethics; calculate frequencies; produce and visibly edit a balanced DISCUSS response. |
| 12 | Genetic Information: Cell to Gene | DNA Structure: Pair, Twist, Critique | Fruit DNA: Extract, Observe, Evaluate | Relate chromosome, DNA, gene and genome; teach DNA polymer/double-helix/base-pair structure; explain and evaluate non-core fruit-DNA extraction. |
| 13 | Inheritance Language: Allele to Phenotype | Punnett Squares: Construct the Prediction | Inheritance Data: Predict, Calculate, Evaluate | Apply allele/genotype/phenotype language, construct monohybrid predictions and compare expected with observed probabilities, ratios and percentages. |

The next planned week can move into genetic-condition or pedigree research; it is intentionally outside this pack.
