Watch the model

A typical body cell has a nucleus containing chromosomes. These pictures are not drawn to scale.

Worked explanation · reveal after thinking

A cell-to-gene zoom is a schematic, not a literal scale model. Not every cell has a nucleus and some DNA is outside the nucleus. For this Foundation model use a typical nucleated body cell. The ladder is two-dimensional; real DNA is three-dimensional.

32-second silent captioned explainer · optional

No sound or autoplay. Use instead of part of the modelling time, not as an extra activity.

Full transcript

Locate the chromosome. A typical body cell has a nucleus containing chromosomes. These pictures are not drawn to scale.

Unpack the DNA. Each chromosome contains a long DNA molecule. A gene is a section of that DNA.

Pair the bases. A pairs with T and C pairs with G. Each nucleotide contains a sugar, phosphate and base.

Twist the ladder. Two DNA strands form a double helix. Weak hydrogen bonds hold complementary bases together.

Work through one example

Worked example

Explain why a gene is not a separate small container inside DNA.

Step 1

A chromosome includes a long DNA molecule.

Step 2

A gene is a particular section of that molecule.

Step 3

Mark a section on a drawn strand, rather than drawing a new box inside it.

Step 4

The genome is the whole collection of DNA, not one highlighted gene.

Worked explanation · reveal after thinking

A typical cell contains a nucleus; the nucleus contains chromosomes; a chromosome contains a long DNA molecule; a gene is a section of that DNA. The genome means all the organism’s DNA, not another small part.

LAUNCH · W12 · Lesson 1 · Shared resource

Learning objective

Relate nucleus, chromosome, DNA, gene and genome using an accurate nested model.

Key words

DNA — A polymer that carries genetic information.

Chromosome — A long DNA molecule packaged with proteins.

Gene — A section of DNA containing information for a protein.

Genome — All of an organism’s DNA.

Nucleotide — A sugar, a phosphate and one base.

Complementary — Bases pair in a specific way: A with T, C with G.

Remember

• In a typical animal or plant cell, chromosomes are inside the nucleus.

• A gene is a section of DNA, not a separate object inside DNA.

• DNA has two strands arranged as a double helix.

• A pairs with T; C pairs with G.

Success looks like

• Connect nucleus, chromosome, DNA and gene correctly.

• Distinguish a gene from the genome.

• Explain one limitation of the zoom model.
