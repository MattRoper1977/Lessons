How these lessons are ordered and badged · 25 CONFORMS, 120 NOT YET

Each week lists its newest lessons first: the lessons in that week’s newest folder, then that folder’s Classic. Older 40-minute copies sit in Earlier versions. CONFORMS means the lesson’s own bytes carry the exemplar chassis (the PASS B census); NOT YET means they do not yet. The badge changes as lessons are rebuilt in place, and it never changes a lesson’s position.
