W11A
Climate Chain: From Gas to Warming		Cause, energy and effect cards; arrow strip; pencil; optional calm digital card route.Use calm, solution-aware language. Order printed cards or direct an adult to move them. A scribed exact response is equivalent evidence.		One ordered causal chain, one explained effect and one honest model limitation.

Climate Action: Plan, Measure, Improve		Scenario cards; before/after planning mat; pencil; optional local data-strip route.Pupils do not touch sockets, plugs, boilers, thermostats or site controls. Staff approval comes before any action.Use the on-screen scenario and fictional before/after cards, then give the action, mechanism and measure by pointing, speaking, typing in a pupil document or exact adult scribing.
Use the paper mat, point to cards or dictate exact words. No equipment or live site action is needed.		A safe action-to-mechanism chain, baseline, repeated measure, comparable condition and one limitation.

W12B

W13A
Rover Rescue: Plan a Fair Test		Planning mat; low ramp; trolley or rover; smooth and fabric surfaces; metre rule or tape; start and finish markers.Teacher checks a stable low ramp and clear lane. Do not push the rover, chase it or place hands in its path.Use the same two-condition plan and six blank result rows with a local rover representation; no results are saved.
Plan with cards only or direct an adult to place every method card. No equipment handling is required.		Question, prediction, variable table, safe numbered method, two conditions, six result spaces and one model limitation.
W13B
Rover Evidence Lab		Stable low ramp; same rover; smooth and fabric surface conditions; ruler or tape; six-row table; lane markers.Teacher checks the ramp and clear lane. Release without pushing; keep hands, feet and faces away from the moving rover.Run the same reasoning route with the fixed smooth and fabric results: 48, 51, 49 versus 27, 29, 28 cm.
Direct an adult through the physical method or use printed fixed data. Observation and decision roles are complete participation.		Prediction, all six raw results, pattern statement, evidence-linked conclusion, one improvement and one model limitation.

Shared safeguards

  • No pupil is forced to handle equipment, speak publicly or be recorded.
  • Pointing, directing an adult, signing, drawing, typing and exact adult scribing can preserve the same conceptual evidence.
  • Retries are expected and never scored.
  • In-page state exists only in the current tab and clears on reload.
  • All links are local; no account, upload or live website is required.
