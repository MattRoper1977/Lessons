WHAT THIS MODEL HELPS US SEE

The fixed six-result set provides a complete parity route and models how raw data become a cautious conclusion.

WHAT THIS MODEL DOES NOT SHOW

A short classroom ramp tests one rover, two surfaces and one set of conditions; it cannot represent gravity, terrain or every real rover design.

Step 1

Check the safe lane, low ramp, marked start and same rover. Commit a prediction before releasing anything.

Step 2

Release without a push, measure from the same line and record immediately. Repeat three times before changing condition.

Step 3

Keep every valid result. If the method fails, preserve the first note, repair the setup and repeat the run.

Word help keeps the scientific term visible and adds a plain-language bridge.

👩‍🏫 I Do 2 · connect the second idea

Connect the model to the second idea

Connection: the model is useful when its visible pattern is translated into a scientific explanation.
Connection 1

New investigation evidence: smooth 48, 51, 49 cm; fabric 27, 29, 28 cm. Every smooth result is greater.

Connection 2

Typical distance is about 49 cm on smooth and 28 cm on fabric. The observed intervals 48–51 cm and 27–29 cm do not overlap; the numerical ranges are 3 cm and 2 cm.

Connection 3

Conclusion: the rover travelled farther on smooth surface because lower friction opposed its motion less. State that the classroom model is simplified.
