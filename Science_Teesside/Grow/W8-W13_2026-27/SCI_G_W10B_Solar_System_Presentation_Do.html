Solar System Presentation: Mission Briefing

Sequence outcome: Research and present findings about the Solar System using ICT.

🎯 Learning objective: I can present researched Solar System findings clearly, credit sources and respond to an audience question with a change or a clear reason plus one transfer step.
✅ Success criteria

  • Make source labels visible or say them aloud.
  • Receive an audience question and record a useful change, or explain why the content stays and set a transfer step.

WHAT THIS MODEL HELPS US SEE

Use: Our question was __. Our claim is __. Evidence from __ shows __. Your question made me change __ / confirmed __, so next I will __.

WHAT THIS MODEL DOES NOT SHOW

A short briefing cannot contain every fact. Attractive slides do not prove accuracy, and an audience question does not replace source checking.

Step 1

State the focused question, then give one answer claim in a complete sentence.

Step 2

Select two or three facts that directly support the claim. Show a source label beside each or say the source aloud.

Step 3

Use a diagram, paper panel, slide or object only when it helps the audience follow the Science.

Word help keeps the scientific term visible and adds a plain-language bridge.
