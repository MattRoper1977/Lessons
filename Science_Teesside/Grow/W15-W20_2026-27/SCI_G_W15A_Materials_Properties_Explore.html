Materials by property

Verbatim workbook outcome: Compare and group materials by properties (hardness, conductivity, solubility). · Trace: GROW Weekly - Spring!C28

Objective: Compare supplied materials by hardness, conductivity and solubility, using observations to justify each grouping.
Success evidence:
  • Compare supplied materials by hardness, conductivity and solubility, using observations to justify each grouping.
  • A material can belong to different groups for hardness, conductivity and solubility because each property asks a different question.
  • One classroom result supports a cautious grouping for those materials and conditions; it does not define every material everywhere.
