GROW SCIENCE · OPTIONAL END-OF-DAY WINDOW

Receive → capture → influence tomorrow

Use the available daily slot to make learning visible without turning evidence into a quota.

Source note: this window follows the uploaded May 2026 Feedback & Marking Policy’s live Lundy architecture and lean digital/Earwig capture approach. It does not itself store data or upload anything.
2 min

1 · SPACE

Regulate first. Give time, a trusted adult and a low-cognitive-load route. No public comparison. Refusal today is information, not punishment.

3 min

2 · VOICE

Ask one genuine question: “What did you find out, change your mind about, or need help with?”

Write/editshort margin edit
Sayverbal reply
Showdemonstration/model
Captureshort pupil voice clip where appropriate
2 min

3 · AUDIENCE

The adult reads, listens or watches — then names back the content: “You said / showed ____.”

Do not mistake recording for Audience. A clip that nobody listens to has not been received.

3 min

4 · INFLUENCE

Make the response change tomorrow: next instruction, question, scaffold, explanation or practical role.

Tomorrow say: “Last lesson your response showed ____. Today we are starting with ____.”

Completeness: a lesson counts as complete only when Influence names an observable change. “The adult listened” is not Influence.

2–3 min

5 · Lean evidence action

When the work has genuine evidence value, use the school’s authorised evidence/Earwig workflow: short photo, ~30-second clip or one-line context note; look/listen before tagging; same-day capture where the policy requires it. The lesson files never upload automatically.
