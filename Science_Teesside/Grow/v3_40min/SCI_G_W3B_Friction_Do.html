Friction: Test the Surfaces

Science mission
Surface Test Lab: can our evidence identify the grippiest surface?

Aut1·W3 — Explore friction and how it can be helpful or unhelpful.

🎯 Learning objective: I can compare friction on different surfaces and use observations as evidence.
✅ Success criteria
  • I can make a prediction before testing.
  • I can keep the object and release method consistent.
  • I can use observations or measurements to support a conclusion.

✏️ Your independent product / decision

Surface investigation: compare at least three safe surfaces using the same object and release method. Record distance travelled or another agreed consistent measure. Repeat where time allows.
