Verbatim SoW objective: Describe causes and effects of global warming and how to reduce our impact.

Arrival · 5 min

Sort these: weather today; climate over many years. Point to the long-term idea.

Offer a predictable seat or standing spot and the first visual cue. No public test.

Starter · 3 min

Think back. Say, point or write one answer for each question.

  1. Name one fact about the Solar System and its class card.
  2. Why does one place have night?
  3. Name one way friction can affect motion.

Today I will check:

  • I can put cause before effect.
  • I can say how gases add to warming.
  • I can compare a cut in emissions with help for an impact.

Ask three low-stakes retrieval questions; prior coverage must be checked.

I do · 5 min

I order the chain: burn fuel → more carbon dioxide → more heat stays → warming. Cutting emissions acts near the start. A flood defence helps with an impact.

Risk: Melted ice can leave a slippery spill.

Safe: Keep ice in trays. Wipe spills and use no heater.

Check against the school risk assessment and CLEAPSS guidance. Dry spills; use no heat source. This surface observation is not proof of the greenhouse effect.

Show the part-done example, then allow paired talk before an answer. One-minute reset inside this stage.

We do 2 · 5 min

Choose what follows burning more fossil fuel in our warming chain. Everyone answers together.

Which action helps with an impact rather than cutting emissions?

Choose a step.

All of us: A bus depot burns more diesel. Which immediate link belongs in its emissions chain?

Point. Tell a partner why.

Take one quiet minute to reset.

All pupils answer the four-option hinge; use the decision rule, not a score.

Independent · 8 min

Build a cause-to-impact chain. Compare an emissions cut with help for an impact. Give one reason for each.

Talk first. You can plan in any language.

Point, sign, speak, draw, type, write, use a scribe or show a staff-captured photo.
