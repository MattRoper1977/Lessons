# SCI_GROW_SU1_W05 · TA deployment brief

**Objective:** Investigate recovering substances from reversible changes.

## EEF five-layer scaffolding key

**Self-scaffold → prompt → clue → model → correct.** Move down only when the pupil can't go on; move back up as soon as they can. Start by waiting, checking the pupil’s own attempt and using the existing visual, communication or reading aid. An access aid is not a content answer.

1. **Self-scaffold — watch for:** Chooses an appropriate recovery method for the specified mixture. Let the pupil check the source, key, diagram or own work before speaking.
2. **Prompt — two open questions:** “Can filter paper trap sand?” “Why does dissolved salt need a different method?” Pause after each; accept point, sign, AAC, speech or writing.
3. **Clue — two narrowed questions:** “Are visible grains or a dissolved solid present?” “Which separation method fits that observation?” Give one clue at a time and then step back.
4. **Model — one step only:** Show one first step: Start with whether grains are visible and insoluble. Then the pupil tries the next matching move with the source in view.
5. **Correct — one direct fact and when:** If the pupil still cannot continue after that model, give only “Filtration.” for “How can insoluble sand be separated from water?”. Then ask for a fresh application, record the level used and fade back toward self-scaffolding.

**Regulation break:** Offer the pre-agreed help/pause signal without a public explanation. Follow the pupil’s existing plan for place, adult and length; retain supervision. On return, point to the last completed step, give a small re-entry choice and record access/support factually.
