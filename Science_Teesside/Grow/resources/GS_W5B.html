Apply it

Use the current workshop

Use your actual results, or label the provided sample-data route. Record a conclusion with two values, one uncertainty and one method improvement. Keep the original evidence.

Teacher model · compare with the pupil’s evidence

Sample model: B has a mean of 28 cm compared with A’s 49 cm, so the trolley rolled a shorter distance on B in this setup. The conclusion depends on a controlled release. A fixed release mark and no push would improve consistency. Practical conclusions must follow the actual retained measurements.

I Do · explain and model

Look, explain, check

Check the setup and agree tester, director, observer or recorder roles.

Take three no-push releases per surface when practicable, recording centimetres each time.

Compare the results, retain uncertainty and conclude only what the evidence supports.

Connect the ideas

A mean summarises repeated values: add them and divide by the number of trials.

A mean does not remove a faulty method or prove the result true.

Separate what the values show from what we assume about the setup.

Quick check

One run was pushed accidentally. Should we erase it so the results look tidy?

Check the explanation

No. Keep it, label the known method problem and repeat if possible. The record should show why the repeat was needed.

The same science without video

The wheel is turning and the brake pad presses against its rim. Predict what happens. Does that scenario supply any controlled stopping-distance measurements for our two-surface trolley test?

Teacher explanation for the video or still scenario

No. It shows a wheel slowing as the brake grips. It does not compare our two surfaces with controlled releases or provide our repeated measurements.

Use a short extract or the still scenario in place of part of the modelling time. A video or model is not evidence that pupils completed a practical.

For the adult

Preparation and a likely misconception

Use Monday’s approved plan, a secured low ramp, the same trolley, two surfaces, tape and padded stop. Run the hands-on work during shared and independent stages. If no suitable setup, use the clearly labelled sample data throughout. Keep a known pushed trial in the record, label it invalid for the intended comparison, and state which valid trials were used in any mean.

Watch for: Keep real measurements and supplied values in separate tables. Do not paste sample numbers into a practical record.

Exit question and model answer

What does your evidence support—and what can it not establish?

Reveal a worked explanation after an attempt

It can support a comparison for the tested setup. It cannot establish every surface pair, and sample values do not establish practical completion.
