3 · Tutor Time — British Values · Behaviour Focus · KCSIE

Values and safeguarding, built into the routine. Eight interactive whiteboard sessions covering the fundamental British Values, our Behaviour Focus code and restorative repair, and protective KCSIE sessions — each ending with a check-out (the emotional register) and a pupil-run evidence slot. Eight tagged evidence entries per group per half-term: a dated SMSC / British Values / safeguarding record with pupil voice captured at the point of teaching.
Statutory version gate. KCSIE 2025 applies to teaching until 31 August 2026; KCSIE 2026 applies from 1 September 2026. The new RSHE statutory guidance becomes mandatory on 1 September 2026. Sessions delivered across that boundary are re-checked against the edition in force on the day they are taught. Owner: RSHE lead / DSL. Review: 1 Sep 2026.

How it holds together

The Community Project slot carries the D&T build into the community (partner liaison → site need → handover); the Duke eco-challenge links back to the upcycling project; Careers evidence banks toward the same accreditation family; and tutor time's democracy session votes on the same real choices the projects create. One term, one connected story — every strand producing evidence as it goes.
