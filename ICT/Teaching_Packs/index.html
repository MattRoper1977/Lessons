Eight weeks · 40 minutes

GROW Computing

What this pack is: Programming with Scratch. Pupils build one maze game across eight weeks: the sprites, the arrow controls, a wall rule, a win and restart, one new feature, then testing and repair. HTML lessons on the Made by Matt classroom chassis, with editable PowerPoint decks, pupil booklets and teacher guides. (from the pack’s Read Me and eight-week plan)

Every week can be completed on paper, with no device. Each week has a paper route: printed block grids to write the scripts on, the maze at its real coordinates to move a counter across, predict and trace tables, and a completion record. The paper route evidences the same outcomes as the screen route. It is a route, not a worksheet.

Week 7 adds a moving Hazard that glides between two points, with the contact rule on the Player sprite. Week 8 tests five things — arrows, walls, finish, the new feature and restart — and repairs three faults without breaking a check that was passing.

AQA Unit Award Scheme 71638, Programming with Scratch (unit 6), Level One. The centre’s AQA summary sheet remains the official record; eight lessons alone do not establish that every pupil has completed the unit. Weeks 1–6 rechecked 9 September 2026, Weeks 7–8 rechecked 10 September 2026.
