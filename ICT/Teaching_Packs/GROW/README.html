GROW ICT

Autumn 1 · Weeks 1–7 · Everyday digital skills

Extract the ZIP fully, then open index.html. The lesson slideshow and its local resources work offline. External reference links need internet access.

Included

  • HTML lessons using the established Made by Matt classroom chassis and pathway colours.
  • Editable PowerPoint teaching decks and matching slide PDFs.
  • Pupil booklet, printable resources and separate teacher guide in Word and PDF.
  • Lesson objectives, 40-minute plans, model responses, access options and source notes.

Keep the teacher guide and teacher notes separate when distributing pupil files. The lessons do not capture or upload pupil responses.

Lesson sequence

  • Week 1: Log on and complete a real task
  • Week 2: Format a notice for its reader
  • Week 3: Save, organise and find your files
  • Week 4: Build slides that help an audience
  • Week 5: Enter, check and explain simple data
  • Week 6: Online risks and a safer digital footprint
  • Week 7: A digital identity project with safe choices

Teaching files

Pupil booklet · Resource cards · Teacher guide

Use the supplied starter files. Make writable copies before pupils begin. Week 2 deliberately contains poor formatting and two wording errors to correct. Week 5 has a blank workbook plus a separate teacher worked example.

The fictional dataset contains Drawing 7, Model making 9, Coding 3 and Gardening 5: total 24. The chart excludes the total row. The final project may use the supplied chart; record whether the learner constructed it or inserted the supplied version.

Adaptation and later website use

These are downloadable teaching files for review and use. Website route placement can be completed after the repository health check. This package does not run an installer or publish anything.

Editable lesson content is included in Authoring/Lesson_Content.json. The HTML files contain their own styles and scripts; keep any local resource folders beside them.
