### Autumn — GROW PATHWAY   Autumn Term 2026/27   Theme: Identity & Belonging

**English & Communication (FS Level 1 to 2; Entry 3 floor) · Aut 1 · Sep to Oct (7 wks)** — _Identity in fiction & life writing (analysis)_
- **LO:** Read and analyse a whole text about identity; infer meaning and support with evidence; compare viewpoints across texts; deliver a formal presentation; write accurate, well-structured prose (L1-2).
- **Vocab:** infer, analyse, viewpoint, compare, evidence, cohesion, register
- **Accred:** Pearson Functional Skills English Level 1 to Level 2 (reading, writing, SLC); Entry 3 / Step Up to English floor where needed; AQA UAS 'Reading & analysis', 'Spoken presentation'.
- **Assess:** F: baseline reading age & writing screen (Sep); analysis checks.  S: presentation + descriptive/narrative writing (Oct) + FS L1-2 evidence.
- **SEND/SMSC:** Vocabulary pre-teach, sentence frames, knowledge organisers, extended time; Entry 3 floor tasks for lowest learners. SMSC: Int'l Literacy Day, Black History Month.

**English & Communication (FS Level 1 to 2; Entry 3 floor) · Aut 2 · Nov to Dec (7 wks)** — _Non-fiction analysis & writing for purpose_
- **LO:** Distinguish fact, opinion and bias; compare purpose, audience and viewpoint across non-fiction; write a formal text (article/report/persuasive) using paragraphs, discourse markers and accuracy (L1-2).
- **Vocab:** fact, opinion, bias, audience, purpose, discourse marker, cohesion
- **Accred:** Pearson Functional Skills English Level 1 to Level 2 (reading & writing); Entry 3 floor where needed; AQA UAS 'Writing for purpose'.
- **Assess:** F: reading quizzes, writing checklists.  S: formal/persuasive text + reading assessment (Dec) + FS L1-2 evidence.
- **SEND/SMSC:** Writing frames, spell-check protocols, chunked texts; Entry 3 floor tasks. SMSC: Anti-Bullying Week, Human Rights Day.

**Maths & Numeracy (White Rose Year 5/6; FS L1-2, Entry 3 floor) · Aut 1 · Sep to Oct (7 wks)** — _Number, calculation & fractions (Y5/6)_
- **LO:** Read, write, order, compare and round numbers to 1,000,000 and decimals; use negative numbers; add, subtract, multiply and divide efficiently; find equivalent fractions and calculate with fractions.
- **Vocab:** place value, round, negative, factor, equivalent, fraction
- **Accred:** Pearson Functional Skills Maths L1-2 (Y5/6 spine); Entry 3 floor; AQA UAS 'Using number'.
- **Assess:** F: baseline + number/fraction checks. S: number & fractions assessment (Oct) + FS L1-2/UAS.
- **SEND/SMSC:** CPA, worked examples, reduced-density sheets; Entry 3 floor tasks; Yr3/4 gap-fill. PfA: financial capability.

**Maths & Numeracy (White Rose Year 5/6; FS L1-2, Entry 3 floor) · Aut 2 · Nov to Dec (7 wks)** — _Fractions & multiplication/division (Y5/6)_
- **LO:** Add, subtract and multiply fractions and find fractions of amounts; divide up to 4-digit numbers by 1- and 2-digit numbers; solve multi-step multiplication and division problems in context.
- **Vocab:** numerator, denominator, remainder, multi-step, written method
- **Accred:** Pearson Functional Skills Maths L1-2; Entry 3 floor; AQA UAS 'Using number', 'Fractions'.
- **Assess:** F: fraction & method checks. S: fractions & mult/div assessment (Dec) + FS L1-2/UAS.
- **SEND/SMSC:** Fraction walls, scaffolded steps; Entry 3 floor tasks; KS3 stretch. PfA: number for life.
