First 60 seconds

Let's get thinking

A trusted five is one person per finger — people you'd actually go to if something felt wrong. Building the list when everything's fine means it's there when it isn't.

💬 Round the room: Hold up a hand: five fingers, five people who'd listen.
Spark 1

What a trusted adult does

1. Listens and takes you seriously.

2. Helps — doesn't judge, doesn't overreact.

3. Keeps you safe, at school, home or beyond.

Spark 2

Filling the hand

1. School counts: tutor, TA, safeguarding team.

2. Home and family. Clubs and coaches.

3. Childline 0800 1111 — always there, day or night.

The Talk · you do

Supported: Build your hand with the role cards; an adult scribes.
