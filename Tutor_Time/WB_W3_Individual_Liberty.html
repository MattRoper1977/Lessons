First 60 seconds

Let's get thinking

Liberty means your choices belong to you — what you're into, what you believe, who you are. The clever bit is the boundary: your freedom ends where someone else's begins.

💬 Round the room: Name one choice you made today before 9am.
Spark 1

What individual liberty means

1. Your right to make your own safe choices.

2. Your right to be yourself — style, interests, opinions.

3. Choosing NOT to do something is a choice too.

Your liberty runs all the way up to the point where it starts landing on somebody else. That's the whole of it.
Spark 2

Liberty has edges

1. My choice can't remove someone else's safety.

2. My choice can't remove someone else's choice.

3. When choices clash, we talk — that's next week.

The Talk · you do

Everyone plays

Standard: List three choices you truly own — and one place a choice meets an edge.
Supported: Pick 'my choice' cards from the deck; place one on the 'edge' mat with an adult.
Two minutes, then voices: one line per pair — Cold Call decides who speaks first.
