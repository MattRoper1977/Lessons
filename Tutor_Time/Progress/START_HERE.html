Progress Schools · Tutor Time

British Values · Beliefs & Faiths · KCSIE

Eight tutor-time sessions for Autumn 1: the five fundamental British Values (democracy, rule of law, individual liberty, mutual respect, tolerance of different faiths & beliefs) plus three protective KCSIE sessions — each ending with a check-out and a pupil-run evidence slot.

The session engine: Settle → ▣ MODEL spark → ◆ INDEPENDENT talk (Standard/Supported) → Check-out (pass allowed) → Evidence slot: a rotating pupil Evidence Captain photographs the task and reads the caption frame; the TA files it in the school's evidence system, tags and ticks. By Week 3 pupils run it themselves. KCSIE sessions are protective and pattern-level; disclosures follow the school safeguarding procedure.
