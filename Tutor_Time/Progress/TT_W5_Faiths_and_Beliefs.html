Tutor Time · Week 5 of 8

Faiths & Beliefs — Different and Equal

Tolerance of different faiths and beliefs

Settle

Name one festival or celebration you know (any culture).
MODEL zone · the spark
  • People around Teesside hold many faiths and beliefs — and many hold none. All are equal here.
  • Tolerance means you don't have to agree — you DO have to treat people fairly.
  • Curious questions are respectful; mocking never is.
INDEPENDENT zone · the talk
Standard: Match the festival/belief cards to what they celebrate, then write one curious (kind) question you could ask.
Supported: Match festival pictures to their symbol cards with an adult and pick one you'd like to learn about.
Check-out: One thing you learned about someone else's world today?
Round the room, one line or one word each — passing is allowed. End on tomorrow's one-liner.
Evidence slot — pupil & TA run:
  1. Evidence Captain (rotating) takes ONE photo of today's task — work, not faces (unless consent is on file).
  2. Captain reads the caption frame aloud and the class completes it: “Today we explored beliefs different to our own: ___.”
  3. TA: file it in the school's evidence system, tag BF + tutor group, tick the tracker. Done.

By Week 3 the Captain runs steps 1–2 without prompting; the TA only uploads. That's the routine.
