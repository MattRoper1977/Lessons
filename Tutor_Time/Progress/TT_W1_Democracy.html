Tutor Time · Week 1 of 8

Democracy — My Voice Counts

Having a say, fairly

Settle

One word: how does it feel when someone actually listens to you?
MODEL zone · the spark
  • Democracy means everyone gets a fair say — like our class votes for the community asset.
  • A fair vote: everyone hears the options, everyone gets one voice, we accept the result.
  • Where you already use it: class votes, school council, choosing challenges.
INDEPENDENT zone · the talk
Standard: Run a 60-second class vote on a real choice for this week (snack, music, seating plan) — propose, vote, accept.
Supported: Point to your choice card and post your vote token; the result is read out and we accept it together.
Check-out: Where did YOUR voice count today?
Round the room, one line or one word each — passing is allowed. End on tomorrow's one-liner.
Evidence slot — pupil & TA run:
  1. Evidence Captain (rotating) takes ONE photo of today's task — work, not faces (unless consent is on file).
  2. Captain reads the caption frame aloud and the class completes it: “Today we used democracy: we voted on ___ and accepted the result.”
  3. TA: file it in the school's evidence system, tag BV + tutor group, tick the tracker. Done.

By Week 3 the Captain runs steps 1–2 without prompting; the TA only uploads. That's the routine.
