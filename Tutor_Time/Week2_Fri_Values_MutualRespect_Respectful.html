British Value · Mutual Respect

Respect is bigger than being polite — tap a card for the part people forget.

🤝

It's mutual

You give it and you're owed it — both ways, by everyone.

Respect isn't earned by being the loudest; everyone starts with it.
🌍

It includes difference

People you disagree with, or who live differently, are owed it too.

Respecting someone isn't the same as agreeing with them.
🪪

It includes consent

Someone's body, image and privacy are theirs to control.

Their photo is theirs — not yours to edit, fake or forward.

Why respect is the whole game online

Here's this week's big link: deepfakes and shared images attack the one thing respect protects — a person's right to their own image.

The harm

Faking or forwarding someone's image treats them as a thing to use, not a person to respect. The image may be fake — the hurt is real.

Your part

Doubt the image, back the person. Never make or share fakes — and if it happens to someone, support them and tell a trusted adult.

Where respect shows up

Tap each — most of it is the small, quiet stuff.

👂

Listening

Letting someone finish, even mid-disagreement.

You can disagree hard and still let them speak.
📱

What you don't post

Not joining the pile-on, not forwarding the image.

The most respectful move is often the share you didn't send.
🛡️

Backing people up

Standing with someone being treated unfairly.

Respect is loudest when you defend someone who isn't there.
