First 60 seconds

Let's get thinking

Tolerance doesn't mean agreeing with everyone. It means people can be different — different faiths, backgrounds, opinions — and still be equal. The skill is disagreeing well.

💬 Round the room: One word for how you like to be treated.
Spark 1

Mutual respect

1. Mutual = both ways: I give it AND I'm owed it.

2. Respect in 20 seconds: hear someone out, hold a door, use a name kindly.

3. Disagree with the idea — never attack the person.

Tolerance is putting up with someone. Respect is not having to put up with them at all — aim for the second one.
Spark 2

Tolerance of faiths & beliefs

1. Around Teesside people hold many faiths — and many hold none. All equal here.

2. You don't have to agree. You DO have to be fair.

3. Curious questions are respectful; mocking never is.
