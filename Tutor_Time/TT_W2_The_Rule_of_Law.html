The Rule of Law — Fair for Everyone

Why rules protect us

2 min

Settle

Thumb check: rules — do they mostly help or mostly annoy?
5 minMODEL zone · the spark
  • Laws and rules exist to keep things fair and safe for everyone — including you.
  • Same rules for everyone: staff follow the workshop rules too.
  • Fair consequence, fresh start — that's the deal.
5 minINDEPENDENT zone · the talk
Standard: Sort the rule cards: 'protects me', 'protects others', 'both' — then defend one 'both' card.
Supported: Match each rule picture to who it keeps safe, with a partner or an adult.
5 minCheck-out: Name one rule that protected you or someone else today.
Round the room, one line or one word each — passing is allowed. End on tomorrow's one-liner.
3 minEarwig evidence slot — pupil & TA run:
  1. Evidence Captain (rotating) takes ONE photo of today's task — work, not faces (unless consent is on file).
  2. Captain reads the caption frame aloud and the class completes it: “Today we found that the rule ___ protects ___.”
  3. TA: upload to Earwig, tag BV + tutor group, tick the tracker. Done.

By Week 3 the Captain runs steps 1–2 without prompting; the TA only uploads. That's the routine.
