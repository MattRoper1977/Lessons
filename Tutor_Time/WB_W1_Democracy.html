Democracy — My Voice Counts

Having a fair say

One word: how does it feel when someone really listens to you?

Spark 1

What democracy means

  • Everyone gets a fair say — one person, one voice.
  • We hear the options before we choose.
  • We accept the result, even when our side loses.

Spark 2

Where you already use it

  • Class votes — like choosing our community asset.
  • School council and pupil voice.
  • At home: deciding together what to watch or eat.

Everyone plays

◆ THE TALK · you do
Standard: With a partner: agree one thing in school that pupils should get a vote on, and why.
Supported: Point to the picture card of something you'd like a vote on. An adult scribes your why.
LUNDY LOOP · VOICE

The class decides — for real

The winning idea from our vote goes to staff this week. Your voice, actually counted.

Check-out · pass allowed

Where did YOUR voice count today?

Tap a word or say your own — one each round the room. Passing is always okay.

Evidence slot · pupil & TA run

Prove it

  1. Evidence Captain: ONE photo of today's task — work, not faces (unless consent is filed).
  2. Captain reads, class completes:
“Today we used democracy: we voted on ___ and the result was ___.”
  1. TA: file it in the school's evidence system, tag BV + tutor group, tick the tracker.

Launch extension: one pupil writes the 'why it was fair' line under the photo.
