Tutor Time — BV · BF · KCSIE (Autumn 1)

Eight short sessions · check-out · pupil-run evidence slot

The engine (every session): Settle → ▣ MODEL spark, three teach-points → ◆ INDEPENDENT talk task, Standard/Supported → Check-out (one line or word each, pass allowed — doubles as the emotional register for the day) → Evidence slot.

The evidence slot — pupils and the TA run it: a rotating Evidence Captain takes ONE photo of the task (work, not faces unless consent is filed) and reads the caption frame aloud for the class to complete; the TA files it in the school's evidence system, tags the strand (BV/BF/KCSIE) + tutor group, and ticks the tracker. By Week 3 the Captain runs the slot unprompted and the TA only files.

The eight weeks

  • W1 · BV · Democracy — My Voice Counts — a real 60-second class vote, proposed, taken and accepted.
  • W2 · BV · The Rule of Law — Fair for Everyone — rules sorted by who they protect; same rules for staff.
  • W3 · BV · Individual Liberty — My Choices — safe choices I own, and where a choice meets an edge.
  • W4 · BV · Mutual Respect — Everyday Respect — the 20-second respectful disagreement, performed.
  • W5 · BF · Faiths & Beliefs — Different and Equal — festivals and beliefs across Teesside; curious questions, never mockery.
  • W6 · KCSIE · Safe Sharing — once sent, it's out of your hands; pressure is the red flag; never too late, never your fault.
  • W7 · KCSIE · Spot the Strings — kindness with a catch: secrets, debts, "prove yourself" — telling a trusted adult is strength.
  • W8 · KCSIE · My Trusted Five — a named speak-up network on one hand, school + home + Childline 0800 1111.

Safeguarding: KCSIE sessions are protective and pattern-level. Any disclosure follows the school safeguarding procedure — never investigated in session. Content connects to the KCSIE assembly suite (same themes, deeper) and the British Values assemblies.

Evidence trail: eight tagged evidence entries per tutor group per half-term — an inspection-ready record of SMSC/BV/KCSIE coverage with pupil voice built in.
