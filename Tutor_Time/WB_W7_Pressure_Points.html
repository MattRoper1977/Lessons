First 60 seconds

Let's get thinking

Most friendship is real. But 'kindness' with strings attached isn't kindness — and pressure has patterns you can learn to spot, online and off. Today we learn the patterns.

💬 Round the room: Thumb check: have you ever felt pushed into something? (No details — just thumbs.)
Spark 1

Pressure online

1. Anything you send can be saved, copied, shared — you lose control of it.

2. Nobody has the right to pressure you to share anything.

3. Already happened? NEVER too late, NEVER your fault — a trusted adult can help.

Spark 2

Pressure offline

1. Most kindness is real. 'Kindness' with a debt attached isn't.

2. Warning signs: keep it secret, carry this, prove yourself, you owe me.

3. Feeling trapped is the signal to talk — telling is strength, and you're not in trouble.
