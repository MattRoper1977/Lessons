Pressure Points — Real Friends Don't Pressure

Spotting pressure, online and off

Thumb check: have you ever felt pushed into something? (No details — just thumbs.)

Spark 1

Pressure online

  • Anything you send can be saved, copied, shared — you lose control of it.
  • Nobody has the right to pressure you to share anything.
  • Already happened? NEVER too late, NEVER your fault — a trusted adult can help.

Spark 2

Pressure offline

  • Most kindness is real. 'Kindness' with a debt attached isn't.
  • Warning signs: keep it secret, carry this, prove yourself, you owe me.
  • Feeling trapped is the signal to talk — telling is strength, and you're not in trouble.

Everyone plays
