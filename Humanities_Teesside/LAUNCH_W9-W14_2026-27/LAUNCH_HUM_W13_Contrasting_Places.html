👩‍🏫 I Do 2 · counter / qualification
🧠 Strengthen—not lengthen—the reasoning.

Week 13 evidence pack · real-source core / constructed equal fallback

Core where approved and accessible: use a teacher-curated real contrasting-place pack or approved online resources. Record publisher, date, access date, definition, boundary/scale and rights/use. The fictional profiles below are the executable offline/equal-access fallback.

Riverside East context: fictional dense urban-industrial riverside district with a port/logistics legacy, compact housing and several route crossings. Dalehaven context: fictional rural market-centre area serving a dispersed hinterland with a larger land boundary and fewer scheduled services. Context may help form hypotheses; it does not prove cause or quality.

Matched methods: density = residents per km² inside each fictional administrative boundary; land share = same classroom land-cover classification; services = the same ten-service basket reachable from the mapped centre within 15 minutes at 4.8 km/h; bus departures = weekday scheduled departures 07:00–19:00 from stops within 400 m of the centre; employment = working residents by the same broad categories.
