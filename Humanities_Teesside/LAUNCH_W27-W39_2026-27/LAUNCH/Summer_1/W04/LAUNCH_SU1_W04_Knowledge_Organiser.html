An accurate Earth Day campaign

Knowledge organiser | LAUNCH Humanities

Create a digital sustainability campaign linked to Earth Day.

Words to use

WordMeaning
audienceThe people a message is intended for
claimA statement that needs support
evidenceInformation used to support a claim
campaignA planned message asking for a stated action

Method to remember

Read the two AUTHORED weak drafts.

Replace overclaims with a bounded, evidenced message.

Name the audience and feasible action.

Check accuracy and keep the product in the classroom.

My explanation

Remember

Earth Day is 22 April (EARTHDAY.ORG). Keep the product in the classroom. A proposed paper trial is not a measured carbon saving.
