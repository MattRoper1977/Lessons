Evidence cards

Check the source and limit before using a detail

A The measured record

NOAA Global Monitoring Laboratory publishes the Mauna Loa annual mean CO2 series to monitor atmospheric change. File created 5 September 2026; accessed 21 September 2026. The selected values run from 338.76 ppm in 1980 to 427.35 ppm in 2025.

Source: NOAA GML annual mean CO2 file; 2026 release

Limit: One observatory record is not the global average. The selected years omit changes between them; values can be revised.

B Explaining the cause

NASA explains that burning fossil fuels releases CO2 and that greenhouse gases slow heat loss to space. This connects resource use for energy with climate change. NASA made this public science explanation; page updated 13 July 2026, read 21 September 2026.

Source: NASA Causes; public explanation, updated 2026

Limit: This explanation supports a mechanism. The selected CO2 table is not a temperature record.

C A weaker claim

AUTHORED EXAMPLE created for this lesson on 21 September 2026: an unattributed post says CO2 figures prove every place warmed by the same amount. Its imagined purpose is to persuade, but it supplies no author, measurement method or evidence.

Source: Authored source-critique example; not a real post

Limit: A confident claim is not a measurement. Do not use this invented post as evidence about climate.

D What the unit measures

NOAA states that CO2 is expressed as a mole fraction in dry air, abbreviated ppm. Read ppm as parts per million. The producer is NOAA GML; the file dated 5 September 2026 makes monitoring data available for scientific and public use.

Source: NOAA GML file header; accessed 21 September 2026

Limit: Concentration is evidence about a cause of warming, not a reading of temperature. Keep those quantities separate.
