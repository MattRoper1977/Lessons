# LAUNCH_SU1_W05 · TA deployment brief

## EEF five-layer scaffolding key

**Self-scaffold → prompt → clue → model → correct.** Move down only when the pupil can't go on; move back up as soon as they can. Start by waiting, checking the pupil’s own attempt and using the existing visual, communication or reading aid. An access aid is not a content answer.

1. **Self-scaffold — watch for:** Calculates the before/after difference while retaining the practice-data label. Let the pupil check the source, key, diagram or own work before speaking.
2. **Prompt — two open questions:** “Are these figures practice or observations?” “What changed between before and after?” Pause after each; accept point, sign, AAC, speech or writing.
3. **Clue — two narrowed questions:** “What is the same measure before and after?” “Which condition or comparison would help test the claim?” Give one clue at a time and then step back.
4. **Model — one step only:** Show one first step: Label the figures 'practice' or 'observed'; calculate the change from the two numbers. Then the pupil tries the next matching move with the source in view.
5. **Correct — one direct fact and when:** If the pupil still cannot continue after that model, give only “After minus before with direction stated” for “What does before/after difference mean?”. Then ask for a fresh application, record the level used and fade back toward self-scaffolding.

**Objective:** Link learning to local action with Community Project links.

| Stage | Position and role |
|---|---|
| Arrival / Starter | Sit at the side of the group with sight of access needs; quietly support the pupil using the agreed communication or reading aid. Observe the first unprompted attempt. |
| We do / We do 2 | Move beside the pair checking the model or source; alternate turns, point to the evidence and fade cues. Avoid becoming the pupil's answer-giver. |
| Independent | Move to the pupil needing a scribe, reader or accessible pointing aid if assigned; record the pupil's own choice or wording, with prompt level. Teacher retains oversight of the group. |
| Exit | Step back within hearing; offer the existing access route and allow an independent response where possible. Tell the teacher what was actually seen and what support was used. |

**Prompt ladder (stop once the pupil acts):** 1. Are these figures practice or observations? 2. What changed between before and after? 3. What would you need before claiming the action caused it?

Use VF for actual verbal feedback, WS for actual supported work, I for independent work; NS+ needs a specific next step and R only after a pupil response has been received and considered.

**Regulation break:** Offer the pre-agreed help/pause signal without asking for a public explanation. Follow the pupil's existing plan for place, adult and length; retain supervision. On return, point to the last completed step, offer a small re-entry choice, and note access/support factually. Do not create a difficulty to elicit evidence.
