Source: uploaded Humanities_Scheme_of_Work_2026-27.html, section LAUNCH · Summer 1 · Resources climate and sustainability; opened and read locally on 21 September 2026. URL: this local transcription (#sow); no public source URL was supplied. Weekly outcomes, teaching and evidence are copied from the supplied scheme.

Unit

Resources climate and sustainability

Enquiry

How can we make a justified decision about sustainability?

Success criteria

I can interpret resource and climate evidence, evaluate responses, compare options against criteria and justify a practical decision.

Vocabulary

resource, sustainability, climate change, mitigation, adaptation, cause, effect, response, evidence, provenance, usefulness, decision, criteria

Resources

Climate and sustainability data; selected No One Is Too Small extracts by Thunberg; online data sets; curated sources; highlighters; prepared tables; spreadsheet or graph paper; planning frame; presentation device; glossary.

Access and challenge

Pre-teach terminology and model evidence use. Chunk sources and data; provide an initial frame, then reduce it. Maintain normal access arrangements. Extend by weighing limitations, alternative explanations and evidence quality. Adults support access and evidence selection without supplying answers. Use labelled examples and a reduced data set before the full task.
