Arrival choices

You can begin without previous learning. Word help: worship means showing devotion to God or gods.

Start with 1 and 2. Try 3 and 4 when ready. Reading and exact scribing are available.

1 What does worship mean?

Support: Use the reminder strip or talk it through with an adult.

2 Which meaning fits worship?

Support: Choose: Showing devotion to God or gods OR Communicating with God, aloud or silently.

3 Give one purpose of prayer.

Support: Use the model evidence anchor A, B or read one relevant card together.

4 Is reflection always addressed to a god?

Support: Give a reason when ready.

Stretch: use a precise example or explain which detail supports your answer.

Evidence cards

Read, point or listen. Keep the source label with any detail you use.

A Salah

Muslims offer salah five times a day facing Makkah, after washing (wudu). Purposes include obedience to God, gratitude and staying mindful of God through the day.

Source: Authored RE summary

Limit: Belief and practice vary between individuals and communities.

B Christian prayer

Christians pray alone and together. The Lord’s Prayer includes praise, asking for daily needs and asking for forgiveness.

Source: Authored RE summary

Limit: Belief and practice vary between individuals and communities.

C Non-religious reflection

Many non-religious people use quiet reflection, mindfulness or time in nature to think about their lives and values. It is not addressed to a god.

Source: Authored RE summary

Limit: Some non-religious people do none of these.

D Purposes

Praise · gratitude · guidance · connection · reflection · asking for help.

Source: Authored classroom resource

Limit: One practice can have several purposes.

Shared investigation

1. Match each practice to a possible purpose.

2. Compare purposes across two worldviews.

Work with a partner or adult, or use your own table. One person suggests; the other checks the evidence. Swap after one turn. Passing is allowed.

Supported independent task

Complete this route only. Describe one form of worship, prayer or reflection and explain its purpose.

1. Choose one practice from the image sequence.

2. Put the steps in order.

3. Choose its purpose from the word bank.

Support: Use the glossary and one chunk of evidence. Plan the claim and supporting detail before explaining. Complete the organiser one space at a time. In ___, people ___. The purpose is ___ because ___.

An adult may read or scribe your exact response. They should not choose the answer.

Standard independent task

Complete this route only. Describe one form of worship, prayer or reflection and explain its purpose.

1. Choose one practice.

2. Explain what happens.

3. Explain why it matters to the person.

Support: In ___, people ___. The purpose is ___ because ___.

Check the required evidence and revise one detail.

Stretch independent task

Complete this route only. Describe one form of worship, prayer or reflection and explain its purpose.

1. Explain one practice and two of its purposes.

2. Compare it with a practice from another worldview.

3. Note one way it varies between individuals.

Support: Choose the evidence independently. Use the frame only if it helps.

Explain how a religious prayer and a non-religious reflection are similar and different in purpose.
