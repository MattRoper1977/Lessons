Worship, prayer and practice

Original captioned teaching model made for this pack. It demonstrates the supplied reasoning; it is not historical footage, survivor testimony or a documentary. The full transcript and factual source credits are included.

Question: Give one purpose of prayer.

Evidence: A Salah: Muslims offer salah five times a day facing Makkah, after washing (wudu). Purposes include obedience to God, gratitude and staying mindful of God through the day. B Christian prayer: Christians pray alone and together. The Lord’s Prayer includes praise, asking for daily needs and asking for forgiveness.

Think aloud: For example: praise, gratitude, guidance or asking for forgiveness.

Check: Is reflection always addressed to a god? No. Non-religious reflection is not.
