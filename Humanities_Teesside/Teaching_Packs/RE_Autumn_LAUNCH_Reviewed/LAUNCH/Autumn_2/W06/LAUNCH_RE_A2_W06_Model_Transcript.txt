A structured “compare” response

Original captioned teaching model made for this pack. It demonstrates the supplied reasoning; it is not historical footage, survivor testimony or a documentary. The full transcript and factual source credits are included.

Question: Which words show that an answer is comparing?

Evidence: B The structure: Both… (similarity). However… / whereas… (difference). Because… (reason from evidence).

Think aloud: Both, however, whereas.

Check: Are two descriptions side by side a comparison? No. A comparison links them.
