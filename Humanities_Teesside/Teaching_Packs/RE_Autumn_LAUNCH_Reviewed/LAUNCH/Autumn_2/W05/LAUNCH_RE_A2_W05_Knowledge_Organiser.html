Comparing responses to a life question

Learning goal: Compare how two worldviews respond to one important life question.

Words to use

WordMeaning
life questionA question about meaning, purpose or how to live
responseHow a worldview answers a question
similarityA way that two things are the same
whereasA word that introduces a difference

Remember

Give the strongest similarity between the two responses. Both support helping others and reducing harm, although they explain their reasons differently.

Can two sources stand for two whole worldviews? No. They are two individuals.

How to show your learning

Complete the comparison frame.

Both ___. The Muslim source says ___, whereas the Humanist source says ___.

Evidence matters

Fictional cases are teaching examples. Historical, geographical and religious claims use the named sources. A model video demonstrates a method.

This is learning about religion and worldviews, not worship. Nobody is asked to share a personal belief, family detail or loss.
