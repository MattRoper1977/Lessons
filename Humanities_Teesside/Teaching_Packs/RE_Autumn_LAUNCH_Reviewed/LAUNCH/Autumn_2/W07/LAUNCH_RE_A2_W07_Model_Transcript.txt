Beliefs and practices assessment

Original captioned teaching model made for this pack. It demonstrates the supplied reasoning; it is not historical footage, survivor testimony or a documentary. The full transcript and factual source credits are included.

Question: What is the difference between a belief and a practice?

Evidence: B Belief, practice, meaning: Practice: what is done. Belief: what is held to be true. Meaning: why the practice matters to the person.

Think aloud: A belief is held to be true. A practice is something done.

Check: May a pupil use a reader or scribe in the assessment? Yes, if it is their agreed way of working.
