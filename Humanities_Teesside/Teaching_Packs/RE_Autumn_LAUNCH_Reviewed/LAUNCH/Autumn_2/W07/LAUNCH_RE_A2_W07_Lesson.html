Opening

LAUNCH · RE & World Views · Autumn 2 · Week 7

Beliefs and practices assessment

Learning goal

Use evidence to show understanding of beliefs, practices and their meanings across two worldviews.

How do practices express what people believe?

I can explain the purpose of a practice, compare two worldviews and give a structured comparison using evidence.

40 minutes, with flexible pauses. Point, speak, draw, type or write. A fictional example is welcome. Nobody is asked to share a personal belief.

Editable Word packPrintable pupil resources PDF

This is learning about religion and worldviews, not worship. Nobody is asked to share a personal belief, family detail or loss.

Beliefs and practices assessment / LAUNCH_RE_A2_W07

Outcome: Use evidence to show understanding of beliefs, practices and their meanings across two worldviews.

Evidence to retain: S: Autumn 2 assessment using the appropriate access route.

Forty minute route

Arrival 4 min; starter 3; model 6; shared task 7; check 2; independent 12; review 3; exit 3. Pause time is flexible.

Preparation

Choose one independent route. Print the evidence and shared cards, required visual or data sheet, route worksheet and exit. The organiser is one separate A4 page. Keep answers with staff.

Use agreed access arrangements; clarify task language without supplying answers; record support; retain evidence and note misconceptions for Spring planning.

Access and regulation

Reader/scribe/typing as normal way of working; chunked assessment; reduced visual clutter; extra processing time; accessible response formats.

Offer a predictable start, private response and agreed pause. No cold calling or forced personal disclosure. Rejoin at one clearly named next step. Keep teen-appropriate examples and the same learning goal across response modes.

This is learning about religion and worldviews, not worship. Nobody is asked to share a personal belief, family detail or loss.

Assessment

Use a different example for shared practice. During independent assessment, keep the agreed reader, scribe, glossary or response mode, but withdraw answer models and record prompts separately.

UAS: no approved unit code is supplied. Match the learner’s evidence to the centre-selected unit and exact criteria before submission. This pack makes no automatic award claim.
