Beliefs and practices assessment

Learning goal: Use evidence to show understanding of beliefs, practices and their meanings across two worldviews.

Words to use

WordMeaning
beliefSomething a person holds to be true
practiceSomething people do as part of their beliefs
meaningWhat something stands for or why it matters
relevantDirectly connected to the question

Remember

What is the difference between a belief and a practice? A belief is held to be true. A practice is something done.

May a pupil use a reader or scribe in the assessment? Yes, if it is their agreed way of working.

How to show your learning

Explain one practice and the belief it shows.

The practice of ___ shows the belief that ___. Both ___. However, ___.

Evidence matters

Fictional cases are teaching examples. Historical, geographical and religious claims use the named sources. A model video demonstrates a method.

This is learning about religion and worldviews, not worship. Nobody is asked to share a personal belief, family detail or loss.
