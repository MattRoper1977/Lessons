Remembrance and peace across beliefs

Original captioned teaching model made for this pack. It demonstrates the supplied reasoning; it is not historical footage, survivor testimony or a documentary. The full transcript and factual source credits are included.

Question: Give one similarity or difference between the two responses.

Evidence: B A Christian response: Fictional source: “At our service we pray for those who died and for peace. I believe they are in God’s care, which gives me hope.” C A Humanist response: Fictional source: “I don’t pray, but I stand in silence. People live on in what they did and in our memories. Remembering should make us work to prevent war.”

Think aloud: For example: both keep silence, but only the Christian source prays.

Check: Is classroom reflection the same as worship? No. It is quiet thinking.
