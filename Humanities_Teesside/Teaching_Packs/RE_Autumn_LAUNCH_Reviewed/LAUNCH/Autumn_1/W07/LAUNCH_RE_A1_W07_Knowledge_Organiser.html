Belief and identity assessment

Learning goal: Use evidence from the half-term to explain how beliefs and worldviews can influence identity, values or choices.

Words to use

WordMeaning
evidenceAn example or fact that supports the point
relevantDirectly connected to the question
authorityA source people trust to guide what they believe or do
next stepOne thing to improve next time

Remember

What makes evidence relevant? It directly answers the question being asked.

Must every pupil write the same length of answer? No. Use your agreed access route.

How to show your learning

Write your point.

Point: ___. For example, ___. This shows ___ because ___.

Evidence matters

Fictional cases are teaching examples. Historical, geographical and religious claims use the named sources. A model video demonstrates a method.

This is learning about religion and worldviews, not worship. Nobody is asked to share a personal belief, family detail or loss.
