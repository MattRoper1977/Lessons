LAUNCH · RE & World Views · Autumn 1 · Week 3

Sources of authority and meaning

Learning goal

Recognise a source of authority or meaning and explain how it may guide a believer or non- religious person.

How do beliefs and worldviews shape identity, values and choices?

I can explain an influence, compare two worldviews, identify a source of authority and give a structured explanation.

40 minutes, with flexible pauses. Point, speak, draw, type or write. A fictional example is welcome. Nobody is asked to share a personal belief.

Editable Word packPrintable pupil resources PDF

This is learning about religion and worldviews, not worship. Nobody is asked to share a personal belief, family detail or loss.

Authored RE summary

Limit: Belief and practice vary between individuals and communities.

Many non-religious people turn to reason, evidence, conscience and the experience of people they trust.

Starter: ‘Who or what can guide a difficult decision?’ I Do: model sources such as sacred text, religious leader, tradition, reason, conscience and lived experience.
