Festivals, shared values and the Day of Peace

Original captioned teaching model made for this pack. It demonstrates the supplied reasoning; it is not historical footage, survivor testimony or a documentary. The full transcript and factual source credits are included.

Question: Name one value shared across two worldviews.

Evidence: B A Christian example: Jesus taught “Blessed are the peacemakers”. Fictional case: a church runs a mediation group for neighbours in dispute. C A Muslim example: The greeting “As-salamu alaykum” means “peace be upon you”. Fictional case: a mosque hosts an open day so neighbours can meet.

Think aloud: For example, peace, welcome or dialogue.

Check: Is the Day of Peace only for religious people? No. It is for everyone.
