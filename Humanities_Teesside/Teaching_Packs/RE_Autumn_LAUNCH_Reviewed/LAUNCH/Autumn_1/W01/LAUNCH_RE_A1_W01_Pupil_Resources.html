Arrival choices

You can begin without previous learning. Word help: worldview means the way a person sees life and what matters.

Start with 1 and 2. Try 3 and 4 when ready. Reading and exact scribing are available.

1 What does worldview mean?

Support: Use the reminder strip or talk it through with an adult.

2 Which meaning fits worldview?

Support: Choose: The way a person sees life and what matters OR To have an effect on something.

3 How does Daniel’s belief influence his choice of work?

Support: Use the model evidence anchor A or read one relevant card together.

4 Does a belief fully determine a person’s identity?

Support: Give a reason when ready.

Stretch: use a precise example or explain which detail supports your answer.

Evidence cards

Read, point or listen. Keep the source label with any detail you use.

A Daniel

Fictional profile: Daniel is 24, Christian and a care worker. He says, “I believe every person is made in the image of God, so everyone deserves dignity.” He chose care work over a better-paid job.

Source: Authored profile

Limit: This is one person's view. Others who share the worldview may describe it differently.

B Priya

Fictional profile: Priya is 26 and a Humanist. She says, “This is the one life we have, so I want mine to be useful.” She volunteers as a mentor and gives blood.

Source: Authored profile

Limit: This is one person's view. Others who share the worldview may describe it differently.

C Belief → choice → identity

A belief can lead to a choice. Repeated choices become part of how a person sees themselves and how others see them.

Source: Authored classroom resource

Limit: Real lives are less tidy than a three-step model.

D Other influences

Family, money, health, friends and place also shape adult choices. Belief is one influence, and people can change their views.

Source: Authored classroom resource

Limit: Belief does not fully determine identity.

Shared investigation

1. Label each piece of evidence: belief, choice or identity.

2. Highlight the phrase that shows influence.

Work with a partner or adult, or use your own table. One person suggests; the other checks the evidence. Swap after one turn. Passing is allowed.

Move or match the cards

Cards to use
“I want my life to be useful.”
Chose care work over a better-paid job
“Every person is made in the image of God.”

Possible labels: Belief / Choice / Identity

Support: Use the glossary and one chunk of evidence. Plan the claim and supporting detail before explaining.

Further challenge: Evaluate how far belief, compared with other influences, explains one person’s choice.

Supported independent task

Complete this route only. Explain one way a belief or worldview can influence a person's identity or choices.

1. Choose one profile and find the belief sentence.

2. Select the choice it led to from two options.

3. Say or finish: A worldview can influence identity by ___.

Support: Use the glossary and one chunk of evidence. Plan the claim and supporting detail before explaining. Complete the organiser one space at a time. A worldview can influence identity by ___. For example, ___.

An adult may read or scribe your exact response. They should not choose the answer.

Standard independent task

Complete this route only. Explain one way a belief or worldview can influence a person's identity or choices.

1. Complete the identity-influence map for one fictional person.

2. Show belief, value, choice and identity.

3. Explain one link in a full sentence.

Support: A worldview can influence identity by ___. For example, ___.

Check the required evidence and revise one detail.

Stretch independent task

Complete this route only. Explain one way a belief or worldview can influence a person's identity or choices.

1. Complete the map for both profiles.

2. Explain one link and one competing influence.

3. Judge how strong the influence of belief is, with a reason.

Support: Choose the evidence independently. Use the frame only if it helps.

Evaluate how far belief, compared with other influences, explains one person’s choice.
