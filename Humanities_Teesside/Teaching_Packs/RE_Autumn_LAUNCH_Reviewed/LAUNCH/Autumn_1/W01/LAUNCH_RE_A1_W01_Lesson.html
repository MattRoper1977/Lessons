Opening

LAUNCH · RE & World Views · Autumn 1 · Week 1

Belief, identity and belonging in adult life

Learning goal

Explain one way a belief or worldview can influence a person's identity or choices.

How do beliefs and worldviews shape identity, values and choices?

I can explain an influence, compare two worldviews, identify a source of authority and give a structured explanation.

40 minutes, with flexible pauses. Point, speak, draw, type or write. A fictional example is welcome. Nobody is asked to share a personal belief.

Editable Word packPrintable pupil resources PDF

This is learning about religion and worldviews, not worship. Nobody is asked to share a personal belief, family detail or loss.
