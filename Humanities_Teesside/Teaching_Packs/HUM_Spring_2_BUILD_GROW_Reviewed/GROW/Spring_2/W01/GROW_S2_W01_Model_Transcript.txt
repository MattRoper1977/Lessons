Mapping climate zones and hazards

Original captioned teaching model made for this pack. It demonstrates the supplied reasoning; it is not historical footage, survivor testimony or a documentary. The full transcript and factual source credits are included.

Question: Are earthquakes caused by being in a warm climate zone?

Evidence: A Zones and events: Broad temperature zones relate to latitude. Near the equator is generally warmer; high latitudes are generally colder. Local climate also depends on altitude and other factors. Earthquakes relate mainly to plate boundaries, not climate zones.

Think aloud: No. They are mainly related to tectonic processes.

Check: What helps explain broad temperature patterns? Latitude, with other influences such as altitude.
