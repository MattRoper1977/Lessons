Climate change causes and effects

Original captioned teaching model made for this pack. It demonstrates the supplied reasoning; it is not historical footage, survivor testimony or a documentary. The full transcript and factual source credits are included.

Question: What happens when fossil fuels are burned?

Evidence: B Human influence: Burning fossil fuels adds carbon dioxide. Greenhouse gases slow heat loss to space. Human activity is the main cause of recent global warming.

Think aloud: Carbon dioxide is added to the atmosphere, strengthening the warming influence.

Check: How do emissions reduction and shade differ? The first tackles a cause; shade can reduce exposure to heat.
