A story about fairness

Original captioned teaching model made for this pack. It demonstrates the supplied reasoning; it is not historical footage, survivor testimony or a documentary. The full transcript and factual source credits are included.

Question: Who stops to help in the retelling?

Evidence: A The Good Samaritan: Easy-read retelling of the Christian parable in Luke 10: A traveller needs help. Two people pass by. A Samaritan stops, cares for him and arranges more help. The story asks what it means to be a neighbour.

Think aloud: The Samaritan.

Check: What is the effect of his choice? The traveller receives care and arrangements for further help.
