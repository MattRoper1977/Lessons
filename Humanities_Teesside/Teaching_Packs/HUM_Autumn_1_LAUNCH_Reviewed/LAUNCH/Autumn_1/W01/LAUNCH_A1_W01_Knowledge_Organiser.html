Migration and identity in modern Britain

Learning goal: Investigate migration and identity in modern Britain through sources.

Words to use

WordMeaning
migrationMoving to live in another place
causeA reason something happened
consequenceSomething that happened as a result
provenanceA source’s origin and context

Remember

What can the telegram establish about motives? It records officials’ report of employment hopes among some travellers.

Can it establish how every traveller understood their identity? No. It does not record all their personal views.

How to show your learning

Annotate A for provenance, content and likely purpose.
