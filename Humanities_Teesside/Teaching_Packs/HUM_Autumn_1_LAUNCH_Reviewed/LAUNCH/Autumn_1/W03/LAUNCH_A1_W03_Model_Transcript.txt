Evaluating a digital archive source

Original captioned teaching model made for this pack. It demonstrates the supplied reasoning; it is not historical footage, survivor testimony or a documentary. The full transcript and factual source credits are included.

Question: Distinguish a document from the website holding it.

Evidence: A 11 May 1948 telegram: The Acting Governor of Jamaica told the Colonial Secretary that men had booked passages hoping for employment in Britain.

Think aloud: The telegram was created by officials in 1948. The National Archives provides modern access. I record both separately on the source passport.

Check: What makes a usefulness judgement convincing? A precise enquiry, source detail, provenance and a relevant limit.
