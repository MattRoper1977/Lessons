An interactive twentieth-century timeline

Original captioned teaching model made for this pack. It demonstrates the supplied reasoning; it is not historical footage, survivor testimony or a documentary. The full transcript and factual source credits are included.

Question: What is the gap between 1948 and 1972?

Evidence: A 11 May 1948 telegram: The Acting Governor of Jamaica told the Colonial Secretary that men had booked passages hoping for employment in Britain. C 8 August 1972 Cabinet record: British ministers discussed Idi Amin’s order that Asian British-passport holders leave Uganda within three months.

Think aloud: 24 years.

Check: Why are linked explanations more useful than dates alone? They explain the changes and the evidence behind them.
