BUILD · RE & World Views · Autumn 2 · Week 7

Festival reflection and UAS evidence

Learning goal

Make a simple festival reflection page showing what I have learned.

How do people use light and stories to show what matters?

I can explain a festival symbol, compare two celebrations, retell a story in order and give a reason for my view.

40 minutes, with flexible pauses. Point, speak, draw, type or write. A fictional example is welcome. Nobody is asked to share a personal belief.

Teacher card

Festival reflection and UAS evidence / BUILD_RE_A2_W07

Outcome: Make a simple festival reflection page showing what I have learned.

Evidence to retain: S: one-page reflection against unit criteria; retain UAS evidence.

Forty minute route

Arrival 4 min; starter 3; model 6; shared task 7; check 2; independent 12; review 3; exit 3. Pause time is flexible.

Preparation

Choose one independent route. Print the evidence and shared cards, required visual or data sheet, route worksheet and exit. The organiser is one separate A4 page. Keep answers with staff.

Check each evidence element individually; authenticate understanding through brief verbal questioning; record reasonable adjustments; retain page as possible UAS evidence.

Access and regulation

Highly visual one-page template; cut-and-stick option; scribe/audio/witness note; enlarged print; minimal writing; use normal access arrangements.

Offer a predictable start, private response and agreed pause. No cold calling or forced personal disclosure. Rejoin at one clearly named next step. Keep teen-appropriate examples and the same learning goal across response modes.

This is learning about religion and worldviews, not worship. Nobody is asked to share a personal belief, family detail or loss.

Assessment

Use a different example for shared practice. During independent assessment, keep the agreed reader, scribe, glossary or response mode, but withdraw answer models and record prompts separately.

UAS: no approved unit code is supplied. Match the learner’s evidence to the centre-selected unit and exact criteria before submission. This pack makes no automatic award claim.

Arrival answers

1. Because.

2. Looking back at what you have learned

3. For example: a diya stands for hope and good overcoming evil.

4. It is your own work, it is accurate and you can explain it.

Shared activity reasoning

1. Diya — Hope and good overcoming evil. Week 1: the light of the diya.

2. Hanukkiah — Eight nights of light. Week 2: eight festival lights and a helper light.

3. Red poppy — Remembrance. Week 5: many people wear one in November.

Exit responses

Name one festival symbol and its meaning. For example: a diya stands for hope and good overcoming evil.

What makes evidence strong? It is your own work, it is accurate and you can explain it.

Teaching and evidence record

Supplied scheme of work teaching sequence: Arrival: 4-picture retrieval quiz. Starter: choose the correct symbol/meaning pair. I Do: model a completed reflection page. We Do: complete the first section together. You Do: create a one-page reflection including a festival symbol, one meaning, one respectful action and one thing learned. Exit ticket: choose your strongest piece of evidence.

Look for

S: one-page reflection against unit criteria; retain UAS evidence.

Accept accurate equivalents, including speech or exact scribing. Check the concept and use of evidence rather than length or handwriting. Record access support separately from content prompting.

BUILD: a concrete example with a reason or demonstrated action. GROW: explain or compare with a relevant detail. LAUNCH: connect evidence and reasoning; use a limitation when the outcome requires it. Keep RE responses non-GCSE.
