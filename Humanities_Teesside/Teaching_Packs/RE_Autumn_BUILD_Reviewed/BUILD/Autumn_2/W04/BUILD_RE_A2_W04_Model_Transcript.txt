Taking part respectfully

Original captioned teaching model made for this pack. It demonstrates the supplied reasoning; it is not historical footage, survivor testimony or a documentary. The full transcript and factual source credits are included.

Question: Is making a rangoli-style pattern in class worship?

Evidence: A Learning, not worship: In RE we learn about celebrations. Making a pattern or a card in class is learning. It is not worship, and nobody is asked to share a belief.

Think aloud: No. It is learning about a celebration.

Check: Which kind of light do we use in the classroom? Battery lights only. No real flame.
