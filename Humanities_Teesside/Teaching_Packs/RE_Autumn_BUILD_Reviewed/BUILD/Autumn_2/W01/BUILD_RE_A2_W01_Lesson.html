BUILD · RE & World Views · Autumn 2 · Week 1

A festival of light: Diwali

Learning goal

Explore one festival of light and explain one meaning linked to it.

How do people use light and stories to show what matters?

I can explain a festival symbol, compare two celebrations, retell a story in order and give a reason for my view.

40 minutes, with flexible pauses. Point, speak, draw, type or write. A fictional example is welcome. Nobody is asked to share a personal belief.

Editable Word packPrintable pupil resources PDF

This is learning about religion and worldviews, not worship. Nobody is asked to share a personal belief, family detail or loss.

Word and evidence bank

A Diwali

Diwali is a festival of lights. Many Hindus, Sikhs and Jains celebrate it in autumn. Families light lamps, share food and visit each other.

Authored summary; see Hindu American Foundation, Diwali

Limit: Families celebrate in different ways. The stories told also differ.

B The diya

A diya is a small lamp. Rows of diyas are lit at Diwali. For many people the light stands for good overcoming evil and for hope.

Authored summary; see Hindu American Foundation, Diwali

Limit: A symbol can mean different things to different people.

C Rangoli

Rangoli are bright patterns made on the floor near the door. Many families make them to welcome guests.

Authored RE summary

Limit: Not every family makes rangoli.

D Light as a symbol

Light helps people see in the dark. Many festivals use light to stand for hope, goodness or welcome.

Authored classroom resource

Limit: Light is used in many faiths. The meanings are not all the same.
