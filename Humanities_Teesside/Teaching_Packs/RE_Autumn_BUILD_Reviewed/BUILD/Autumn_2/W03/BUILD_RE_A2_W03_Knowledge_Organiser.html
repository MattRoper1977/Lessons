Retelling a festival story

Learning goal: Retell a festival story in the correct order using symbols or pictures.

Words to use

WordMeaning
sequenceThe order in which things happen
retellTo tell a story again in your own way
RamaA prince in a story many Hindus tell at Diwali
SitaRama’s wife in the story

Remember

What do people do to welcome Rama and Sita home? They light rows of lamps.

Which event comes first in the story? Rama and Sita are sent away to the forest.

How to show your learning

Put four story cards in order.

First ___. Next ___. Then ___. Last ___.

Evidence matters

Fictional cases are teaching examples. Historical, geographical and religious claims use the named sources. A model video demonstrates a method.

This is learning about religion and worldviews, not worship. Nobody is asked to share a personal belief, family detail or loss.
