Hope and remembrance

Original captioned teaching model made for this pack. It demonstrates the supplied reasoning; it is not historical footage, survivor testimony or a documentary. The full transcript and factual source credits are included.

Question: What does the red poppy stand for?

Evidence: A The poppy: In the UK many people wear a red poppy in November. The Royal British Legion describes it as a symbol of remembrance and of hope for a peaceful future.

Think aloud: Remembrance and hope for a peaceful future.

Check: Is silence the only way to remember? No. People remember in different ways.
