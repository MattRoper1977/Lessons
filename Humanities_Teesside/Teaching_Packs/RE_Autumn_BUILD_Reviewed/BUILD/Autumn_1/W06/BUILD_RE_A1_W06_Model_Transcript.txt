Belonging and the Day of Peace

Original captioned teaching model made for this pack. It demonstrates the supplied reasoning; it is not historical footage, survivor testimony or a documentary. The full transcript and factual source credits are included.

Question: When is the International Day of Peace?

Evidence: A The Day of Peace: The United Nations set up the International Day of Peace. It is held every year on 21 September. People around the world think about how to build peace.

Think aloud: Every year on 21 September.

Check: Is reflection the same as worship? No. Reflection is quiet thinking.
