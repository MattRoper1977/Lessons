Special things, respect and belonging

RE & World Views · BUILD · Autumn 1 · Week 8

This return lesson teaches the key idea from the earlier lesson, then revisits six earlier topics through a short individual check. Source profiles are fictional; nobody is asked for personal beliefs, family details or loss.

Teaching sources

  • Special things and why they matter — Talk about what is special to me and give one reason why.
  • Special books and places — Recognise one special book or place linked to a faith.
  • A story of belonging — Listen to a story of belonging and identify one example of belonging.
  • Respecting special objects — Show one respectful way to handle or respond to a special religious object.
  • Similarities between beliefs — Notice one similarity between two people's beliefs or values.
  • Belonging and the Day of Peace — Reflect quietly on belonging and choose one action that helps create peace.
  • Sharing something special — Share or describe a 'special to me' object respectfully.

Sources named in the teaching material

  • Humanists UK explanation of Humanism
  • Sikh Coalition introduction to Sikh beliefs and practice
  • United Nations International Day of Peace

Teacher-authored examples, manual model, captioned video and transcript are available offline. These sources were supplied with the teaching materials; external pages can change.
