Special things and why they matter

Original captioned teaching model made for this pack. It demonstrates the supplied reasoning; it is not historical footage, survivor testimony or a documentary. The full transcript and factual source credits are included.

Question: Why is the watch special to Mina?

Evidence: A Mina’s watch: Fictional case: Mina wears an old watch. It was her grandad’s. It does not cost much. It matters because it reminds her of him.

Think aloud: It was her grandad’s and it reminds her of him.

Check: Does an object have to cost a lot to be special? No. The meaning makes it special, not the price.
