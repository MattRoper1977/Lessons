BUILD · RE & World Views · Autumn 1 · Week 1

Special things and why they matter

Learning goal

Talk about what is special to me and give one reason why.

What makes something special, and how do we show respect?

I can say why something is special, name a special book or place, and show one respectful action.

40 minutes, with flexible pauses. Point, speak, draw, type or write. A fictional example is welcome. Nobody is asked to share a personal belief.

Editable Word packPrintable pupil resources PDF

This is learning about religion and worldviews, not worship. Nobody is asked to share a personal belief, family detail or loss.

Independent application

  2. Point to a reason card: person, memory, belief or place.

An adult may read or scribe your exact response. They should not choose the answer.

Standard task

  1. Choose, draw or describe one special object. It can be fictional.
  2. Write or say one reason it matters.
  3. Name the type of reason: person, memory, belief or place.

___ is special because ___.

Check the required evidence and revise one detail.

Stretch task

  1. Describe one special object and give a reason.
  2. Explain why the same object might not be special to someone else.
  3. Link your idea to how people of a faith treat sacred objects.

Choose the evidence independently. Use the frame only if it helps.

Teaching and evidence record

Supplied scheme of work teaching sequence: Arrival: 4 simple retrieval/misconception questions about special, sacred, belief and respect. Starter: choose which object might be special and why. I Do: model 'This is special because...' using an everyday object, then link to how religious people value special objects. We Do: match objects to possible meanings. You Do: choose, draw or describe one special object and give a reason. Exit ticket: complete 'Something can be special because...'.

Look for

F: one special object and one reason.

Accept accurate equivalents, including speech or exact scribing. Check the concept and use of evidence rather than length or handwriting. Record access support separately from content prompting.

BUILD: a concrete example with a reason or demonstrated action. GROW: explain or compare with a relevant detail. LAUNCH: connect evidence and reasoning; use a limitation when the outcome requires it. Keep RE responses non-GCSE.
