Respecting special objects

Original captioned teaching model made for this pack. It demonstrates the supplied reasoning; it is not historical footage, survivor testimony or a documentary. The full transcript and factual source credits are included.

Question: Why do readers often use a pointer with a Torah scroll?

Evidence: C The Torah pointer: A Torah scroll is handwritten. Readers often follow the words with a pointer called a yad so that fingers do not touch the scroll.

Think aloud: So that fingers do not touch the handwritten scroll.

Check: Do you have to touch an object to show respect for it? No. Looking carefully is a respectful choice.
