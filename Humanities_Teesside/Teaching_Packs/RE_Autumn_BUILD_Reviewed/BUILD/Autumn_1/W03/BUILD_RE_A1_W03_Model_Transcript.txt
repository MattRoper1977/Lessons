A story of belonging

Original captioned teaching model made for this pack. It demonstrates the supplied reasoning; it is not historical footage, survivor testimony or a documentary. The full transcript and factual source credits are included.

Question: What is one sign that Sam is welcome at the gurdwara?

Evidence: B The welcome: A volunteer smiles and says hello. She shows Sam where to sit. Nobody asks what Sam believes. Everyone is welcome.

Think aloud: A volunteer greets him and shows him where to sit.

Check: Did anyone ask Sam what he believes before welcoming him? No. In the story everyone is welcome.
