GROW_A2_W01 · Countries continents and oceans

Outcome: Locate countries continents and oceans using maps and atlases.

Assessment: F: map-skills check and independent location/pattern question.

Shared activity: map — Zoom from Teesside to the world. Locate the UK, India, Japan and their neighbouring oceans.

Independent task: Label the UK, India and Japan, their continents and two oceans. Use an atlas or the supplied map key. Answer independently: which country lies beside the Pacific Ocean?
