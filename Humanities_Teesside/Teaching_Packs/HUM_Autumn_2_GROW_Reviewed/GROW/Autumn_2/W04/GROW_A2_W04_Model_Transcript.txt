Festivals of light around the world

Original captioned teaching model made for this pack. It demonstrates the supplied reasoning; it is not historical footage, survivor testimony or a documentary. The full transcript and factual source credits are included.

Question: Does Diwali belong to only one country?

Evidence: D Diwali: Many Hindus mark Diwali with lamps, worship and gatherings. Light can express good overcoming evil and knowledge overcoming ignorance. Practices and stories vary.

Think aloud: No. Communities observe it in several countries.

Check: What supports an explanation of a festival’s meaning? A credited account from people or organisations explaining the observance.
