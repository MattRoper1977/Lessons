Connections across the world

Original captioned teaching model made for this pack. It demonstrates the supplied reasoning; it is not historical footage, survivor testimony or a documentary. The full transcript and factual source credits are included.

Question: Can communities connect without people travelling?

Evidence: C A community link: Fictional case: a Middlesbrough community group shares festival greetings with a partner group in Mumbai. The link can involve people and communication without a physical journey.

Think aloud: Yes, through messages or shared online activity.

Check: Does one example describe everyone in either place? No.
