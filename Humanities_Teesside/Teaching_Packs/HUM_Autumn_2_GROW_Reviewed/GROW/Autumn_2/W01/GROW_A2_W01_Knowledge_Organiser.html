Countries continents and oceans

Learning goal: Locate countries continents and oceans using maps and atlases.

Words to use

WordMeaning
continentA large land region
oceanA large body of salt water
locationWhere something is
connectionA link between places or people

Remember

Which of the three countries lies beside the Pacific? Japan.

What changes when a map shows a much larger area? It shows less local detail at the smaller scale.

How to show your learning

Label the UK, India and Japan, their continents and two oceans.

___ is in ___. It lies beside ___.

Evidence matters

Fictional cases are teaching examples. Historical, geographical and religious claims use the named sources. A model video demonstrates a method.

You can respond by speaking, pointing, drawing, typing or writing. A fictional personal example is welcome.
