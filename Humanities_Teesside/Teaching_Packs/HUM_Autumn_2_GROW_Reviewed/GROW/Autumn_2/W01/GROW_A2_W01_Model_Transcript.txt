Original captioned teaching model made for this pack. It demonstrates the supplied reasoning; it is not historical footage, survivor testimony or a documentary. The full transcript and factual source credits are included.

Question: Which of the three countries lies beside the Pacific?

Evidence: B India and Japan: India and Japan are in Asia. India borders the Indian Ocean; Japan is an island country beside the Pacific Ocean.

Think aloud: Japan.

Check: What changes when a map shows a much larger area? It shows less local detail at the smaller scale.
