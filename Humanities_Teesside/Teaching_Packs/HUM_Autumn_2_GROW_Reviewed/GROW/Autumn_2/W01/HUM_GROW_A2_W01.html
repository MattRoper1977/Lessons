Humanities · GROW · Autumn 2 · Week 1

Find places: countries, continents and oceans

Learning goal

I can use a map and its index to locate places.

Today I can

  • I can find the UK, India and Japan.
  • I can link a country to its continent and ocean.
  • I can say what a map can and cannot tell me.

You can point, speak, sign, use symbols, draw or write.

You may pass. Use our supplied examples; no personal details are needed.

You do

You do · choose one route

15 min · 22–37

Make a short map record.

I · S · C

Independent

Use the world locator, index and place cards.

1. Locate the UK, India and Japan. Record each marker letter.

2. Give each country's continent and one ocean beside it.

3. Describe Japan's direction from India using the north arrow.

4. The community card links a festival in the UK and India. Explain why a country marker cannot tell us every person's belief.

5. Choose W or S for a street crossing. Give one reason.

Frame if useful: '____ is in ____. It lies beside ____.'

You may point, speak, sign, use symbols, draw or write. You may pass.

Review and improve: Check one country-to-ocean match using both the map and its place card. Make one change or confirm it with a clue.

I · S · C

Supported

Use the world locator, index and place cards with an adult. Do one country at a time.

1. Find United Kingdom in the index. Match its marker. Choose Europe / Asia; Atlantic / Indian.

2. Find India in the index. Match its marker. Choose Europe / Asia; Indian / Pacific.

3. Find Japan in the index. Match its marker. Choose Asia / Europe; Pacific / Atlantic.

4. Show the link from the UK to India on the community card. Does the card say some people or every person marks the festival?

5. Choose a map for a crossing: W world locator / S street map. Give one clue.

Frame: '____ is at ____. It is in ____. Its ocean is ____.'

You may point, speak, sign, use symbols, draw or write. You may pass.

Review and improve: Check one match. Change it or show the words that support it.

I · S · C

Choice

Choose how to show your map record: direct an adult moving labels, use symbols, tell an adult, or make your own sketch.

1. Choose two countries from UK / India / Japan. Locate both using the index.

2. For each one, choose a continent: Europe / Asia. Choose its ocean: Atlantic / Indian / Pacific.

3. For one of your countries, show the card clue that supports the ocean match.

4. Choose the claim that fits the community card:

A: Some people in different countries share a festival.

B: Everyone in one country has the same belief.

5. Choose W or S for finding a street crossing. Give one map clue.

You may point, speak, sign, use symbols, draw or write. You may pass.

Review and improve: Check one label with the card; change it or show why it fits.

I · S · C

Aspire

Use the world locator, index and place cards.

1. Locate all three countries. Give each marker, continent and ocean.

2. Compare India and Japan: one shared location feature and one difference.

3. Explain why W helps with country-and-ocean questions but not with choosing a street crossing.

4. Evaluate this claim: 'A dot in India proves that everyone there celebrates Diwali.' Use the community card and say what the map cannot show.

You may point, speak, sign, use symbols, draw or write. You may pass.

Review and improve: Check that your comparison has both parts. Add a precise map or card clue if needed.

TA Brief

No cold calling; nobody is asked to read difficult text aloud.

Current stage

1. Prepare the complete locator, index, key and place cards. Country dots are approximate markers, not boundaries; do not turn them into claims about people's beliefs.

2. Give one task at a time and private thinking time. Offer point, speak, sign, symbols, draw or write; any pupil may pass. No cold calling or personal disclosures.

3. Use one Arrival tier, one Exit tier and one You do route. Keep adult answers hidden until response time; note reading access and content prompts separately.

4. During We do, let every pupil make a private map choice before the shared labels move. Check whether they use the index, key and source card to support the choice.

5. Keep the final two-minute review inside You do. File the pupil's own location evidence and actual support the same day under HUM-GROW-A2-W01; link only relevant agreed EHCP targets.

Shared preparation, access and safety

For EVERY task, pupils may point, speak, sign, use symbols, draw or write. Read prompts aloud; allow thinking time and a private response. No cold calling. Any pupil may pass. An adult can record the pupil's own gesture, choice or exact words; a pass needs no book entry. No board copying or typing is needed.

Choose one Arrival tier and one Exit tier. Four short Arrival responses and three short Exit responses are enough; do not require all tiers. Keep Answer (adult) lines hidden until after responses. Each You do route has its own text page; pupils complete one route.

This is map learning within belief, festivals and belonging. Discuss the supplied community example, not pupils' own beliefs, families or losses. A country does not have just one faith or one way to celebrate. Do not infer an individual's belief from a place marker. No religious participation is required.

Prepare the locator map, place cards and three ocean labels from the complete specification below. A printed atlas may be an optional check, but the lesson needs no external page, link or app.

Adult-only EHCP note: Outcome opportunities are cognition and learning (follow a search-and-check sequence), communication and interaction (explain a location with a clue), SEMH (ask for a pause or a private response), and sensory or physical access (direct marker placement using a chosen mode). Observe only the pupil's relevant agreed target. Regulation strategy: uncover one country at a time, then offer a short eyes-away pause before returning to its ocean.

Same-day evidence — HUM-GROW-A2-W01: The assigned adult records the pupil's exact response, response mode and actual prompt used, and files one chosen book item, optional photo or brief observation the same day under HUM-GROW-A2-W01. Include a relevant agreed outcome observation if appropriate. A photo alone does not establish understanding.
