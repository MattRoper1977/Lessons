Grid references and navigation

Original captioned teaching model made for this pack. It demonstrates the supplied reasoning; it is not historical footage, survivor testimony or a documentary. The full transcript and factual source credits are included.

Question: What is the park’s four-figure reference on the practice grid?

Evidence: Practice grid: read eastings across first, then northings up. Four figures identify a whole square. The park is in 1122.

Think aloud: 1122.

Check: What does a four-figure reference identify? A grid square, not an exact point.
