Question: Annotate one item for a festivals display.

Evidence: H Hanukkah: Hanukkah is a Jewish festival lasting eight nights. Lights recall the Temple’s rededication and the traditional account of oil lasting eight days. A hanukkiah has eight festival lights and a helper light.

Think aloud: My comparison shows that both celebrations use light. Their stories and meanings differ.

Check: Can card H prove identical practice in every household? No. The source explains a perspective and recognises variation.
