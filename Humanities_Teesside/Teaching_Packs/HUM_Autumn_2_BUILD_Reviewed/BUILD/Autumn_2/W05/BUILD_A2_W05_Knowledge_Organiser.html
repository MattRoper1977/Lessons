Peace and remembrance

Learning goal: Reflect on peace and remembrance.

Words to use

WordMeaning
remembranceTaking time to remember people and events
peaceLiving without conflict or violence
respectTreating people with dignity
symbolAn image or object that carries meaning

Remember

Why might people remember people affected by conflict? To recognise lives, loss and experiences and to learn from the past.

What makes a caption respectful and useful? Accurate words, a source and no invented personal account.

How to show your learning

Create a respectful symbol and caption about why people remember.

People remember ___. My ___ helps because ___.

Evidence matters

Fictional cases are teaching examples. Historical, geographical and religious claims use the named sources. A model video demonstrates a method.

You can respond by speaking, pointing, drawing, typing or writing. A fictional personal example is welcome.
