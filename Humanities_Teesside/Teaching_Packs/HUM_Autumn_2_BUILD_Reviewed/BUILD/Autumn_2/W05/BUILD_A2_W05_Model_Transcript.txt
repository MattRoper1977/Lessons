Peace and remembrance

Original captioned teaching model made for this pack. It demonstrates the supplied reasoning; it is not historical footage, survivor testimony or a documentary. The full transcript and factual source credits are included.

Question: Why might people remember people affected by conflict?

Evidence: A The red poppy: The Royal British Legion describes the red poppy as a symbol of remembrance and hope for a peaceful future. It also shows support for the Armed Forces community.

Think aloud: To recognise lives, loss and experiences and to learn from the past.

Check: What makes a caption respectful and useful? Accurate words, a source and no invented personal account.
