The sequence of a celebration

Learning goal: Sequence the events of a celebration.

Words to use

WordMeaning
festivalA special period of celebration or remembrance
beliefSomething a person accepts as true
traditionA practice passed on
respectCare for people and what matters to them

Remember

What comes first in our family’s plan? Prepare a welcoming space.

Is the plan a rule for every Diwali celebration? No, it is a fictional example.

How to show your learning

Order the four cards from the supplied family plan.

First ___. Next ___. Later ___. Finally ___.

Evidence matters

Fictional cases are teaching examples. Historical, geographical and religious claims use the named sources. A model video demonstrates a method.

You can respond by speaking, pointing, drawing, typing or writing. A fictional personal example is welcome.
