LAUNCH_S1_W01 · Explaining causes of conflict

Outcome: Explain the causes of a twentieth century conflict.

Assessment: F: book task, questioning and two exit questions.

Shared activity: chain — Distinguish longer-term factors, decisions and the immediate trigger in Europe in 1939. Use the source set to connect the factors.

Independent task: Explain supported causal links leading to war in Europe in 1939. Use at least two factors and the invasion of Poland. Avoid claiming that one date alone explains the conflict.

LAUNCH_S1_W02 · Understanding the Holocaust respectfully

Outcome: Study the Holocaust sensitively linked to Holocaust Memorial Day.

Assessment: F: accurate contextual response and exit check; monitor regulation.

Shared activity: source — Establish factual context and the meanings of antisemitism, persecution and genocide. Examine a calm remembrance example.

Independent task: Write an accurate contextual response explaining what the Holocaust was. Create a brief memorial statement grounded in a source. Explain one word or design choice without inventing testimony.

Changes: Worked model linked to named relevant evidence; Arrival retrieval and pathway-specific supports; Task organiser and paper interaction cards; Consistent media transcript and source labels

LAUNCH_S1_W03 · Evaluating survivor testimony

Outcome: Evaluate survivor testimony from a curated online archive.

Assessment: F: source annotations, evidence selection and evaluation.

Shared activity: source — Read or listen to the curated prewar-life testimony segment. Identify the speaker, remembered period and later recording context.

Independent task: Annotate the credited testimony with what it reveals about one person’s life. Select a precise detail and evaluate its usefulness for the enquiry. State a relevant limitation without dismissing personal testimony.

LAUNCH_S1_W04 · Why remembrance matters

Outcome: Explain the significance of remembrance.

Assessment: F: significance response and exit check.

Shared activity: rank — Compare remembrance choices using accuracy, recognition and evidence. Justify a choice without ranking the value of victims’ lives.

Independent task: Explain the significance of remembrance using contextual and personal evidence. Create a respectful reflection. Evaluate how a particular remembrance choice helps an audience understand.

LAUNCH_S1_W05 · Creating a digital memorial

Outcome: Create a respectful digital memorial.

Assessment: F: factual accuracy, respectful representation and explanation.

Shared activity: memorial — Check a sample for accurate facts, source credits and restrained design. Choose a factual focus and accessible layout.

Independent task: Create a short digital memorial recognising a life or historical context from the curated evidence. Include accurate facts and a source credit; use no invented quotation. Export the memorial and explain one design choice.
