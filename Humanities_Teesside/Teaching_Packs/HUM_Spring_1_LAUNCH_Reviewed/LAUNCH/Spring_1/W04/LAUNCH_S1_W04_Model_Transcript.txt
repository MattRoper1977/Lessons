Why remembrance matters

Original captioned teaching model made for this pack. It demonstrates the supplied reasoning; it is not historical footage, survivor testimony or a documentary. The full transcript and factual source credits are included.

Question: Explain a remembrance design choice.

Evidence: D Remembrance choices: An accurate memorial can name a person, recognise a life and credit evidence. A simple layout, readable text and a clear source can support respectful attention.

Think aloud: Naming Gerda Schild Haas and crediting her account recognises a particular life. A calm factual caption can help readers attend to her experience.

Check: Why credit a source in a reflection? It makes the factual basis visible and avoids invented claims.
