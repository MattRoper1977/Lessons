Explaining causes of conflict

Original captioned teaching model made for this pack. It demonstrates the supplied reasoning; it is not historical footage, survivor testimony or a documentary. The full transcript and factual source credits are included.

Question: What was the immediate trigger for British and French declarations?

Evidence: A Europe in 1939: Nazi Germany’s expansion and invasion of Poland on 1 September 1939 led Britain and France to declare war on 3 September. Longer-term conditions and leaders’ choices help explain the conflict.

Think aloud: Germany’s invasion of Poland on 1 September 1939.

Check: Does identifying a trigger explain every cause? No. Longer-term conditions and choices also require explanation.
