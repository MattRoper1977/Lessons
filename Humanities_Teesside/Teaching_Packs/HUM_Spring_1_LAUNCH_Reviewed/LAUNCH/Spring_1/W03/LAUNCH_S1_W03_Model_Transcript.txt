Evaluating survivor testimony

Original captioned teaching model made for this pack. It demonstrates the supplied reasoning; it is not historical footage, survivor testimony or a documentary. The full transcript and factual source credits are included.

Question: Can a survivor’s account reveal something a policy document may miss?

Evidence: C Gerda Haas remembers Ansbach: In her later oral history, Gerda Schild Haas recalls that religion mattered in her family. She describes Jewish affiliation appearing on forms and in community life in Ansbach before persecution.

Think aloud: Yes. It can reveal personal experience and remembered meaning.

Check: Does one testimony represent all Jewish lives? No. Evaluate its particular perspective and context.
