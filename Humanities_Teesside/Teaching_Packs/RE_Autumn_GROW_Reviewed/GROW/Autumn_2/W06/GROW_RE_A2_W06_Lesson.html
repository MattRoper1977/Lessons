Opening

GROW · RE & World Views · Autumn 2 · Week 6

Personal values and respect for others

Learning goal

Identify a value that matters to a person and explain how respecting different values supports community.

What does it mean to belong to a community of belief?

I can describe belonging, explain a feature of a place of worship, use RE vocabulary and give a reasoned viewpoint.

40 minutes, with flexible pauses. Point, speak, draw, type or write. A fictional example is welcome. Nobody is asked to share a personal belief.

This is learning about religion and worldviews, not worship. Nobody is asked to share a personal belief, family detail or loss.

Shared activity reasoning

1. “See you next week. Hope the meal is good.” — Respectful. It accepts a different priority.

2. “You’re letting us down for a dinner?” — Needs changing. It mocks the other person’s value.

3. “I’d have stayed, but I get why you’re going.” — Respectful. It disagrees and still shows respect.
