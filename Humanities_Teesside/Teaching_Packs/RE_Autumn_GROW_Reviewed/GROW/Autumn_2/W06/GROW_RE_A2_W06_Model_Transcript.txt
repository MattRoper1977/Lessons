Personal values and respect for others

Original captioned teaching model made for this pack. It demonstrates the supplied reasoning; it is not historical footage, survivor testimony or a documentary. The full transcript and factual source credits are included.

Question: Give one way to show respect when values differ.

Evidence: B A respectful response: Ben says, “See you next week. Hope the meal is good.” He respects a value he does not share.

Think aloud: For example, accept their choice without mocking it.

Check: Does respect mean you have to agree? No. Respect does not require agreement.
