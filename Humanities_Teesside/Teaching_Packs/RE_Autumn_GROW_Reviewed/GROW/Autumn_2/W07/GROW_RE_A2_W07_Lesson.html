Opening

GROW · RE & World Views · Autumn 2 · Week 7

Belief and identity assessment

Learning goal

Show understanding of beliefs, festivals, belonging and respect using evidence from the term.

What does it mean to belong to a community of belief?

I can describe belonging, explain a feature of a place of worship, use RE vocabulary and give a reasoned viewpoint.

40 minutes, with flexible pauses. Point, speak, draw, type or write. A fictional example is welcome. Nobody is asked to share a personal belief.

This is learning about religion and worldviews, not worship. Nobody is asked to share a personal belief, family detail or loss.

Belief and identity assessment / GROW_RE_A2_W07

Outcome: Show understanding of beliefs, festivals, belonging and respect using evidence from the term.

Evidence to retain: S: belief-and-identity assessment and UAS evidence task.

Forty minute route

Arrival 4 min; starter 3; model 6; shared task 7; check 2; independent 12; review 3; exit 3. Pause time is flexible.

Preparation

Choose one independent route. Print the evidence and shared cards, required visual or data sheet, route worksheet and exit. The organiser is one separate A4 page. Keep answers with staff.

Use normal access arrangements; clarify instructions without giving answers; record support used; retain evidence; note misconceptions for Spring planning.

Access and regulation

Reader/scribe/typing as normal way of working; chunked pages; reduced reading; enlarged text; extra processing time; accessible response formats.

Offer a predictable start, private response and agreed pause. No cold calling or forced personal disclosure. Rejoin at one clearly named next step. Keep teen-appropriate examples and the same learning goal across response modes.

This is learning about religion and worldviews, not worship. Nobody is asked to share a personal belief, family detail or loss.

Assessment

Use a different example for shared practice. During independent assessment, keep the agreed reader, scribe, glossary or response mode, but withdraw answer models and record prompts separately.

UAS: no approved unit code is supplied. Match the learner’s evidence to the centre-selected unit and exact criteria before submission. This pack makes no automatic award claim.

Teaching and evidence record

Supplied scheme of work teaching sequence: Arrival: 4-picture retrieval quiz. Starter: identify the strongest evidence for an answer. I Do: model how to use a source/image in a response. We Do: complete one supported assessment question together. You Do: complete the belief-and- identity assessment/UAS evidence task using the agreed access route. Exit ticket: one strength and one next step.

Look for

S: belief-and-identity assessment and UAS evidence task.

Accept accurate equivalents, including speech or exact scribing. Check the concept and use of evidence rather than length or handwriting. Record access support separately from content prompting.

BUILD: a concrete example with a reason or demonstrated action. GROW: explain or compare with a relevant detail. LAUNCH: connect evidence and reasoning; use a limitation when the outcome requires it. Keep RE responses non-GCSE.
