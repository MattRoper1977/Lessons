Questions for a faith or belief visitor

Original captioned teaching model made for this pack. It demonstrates the supplied reasoning; it is not historical footage, survivor testimony or a documentary. The full transcript and factual source credits are included.

Question: Give one respectful question stem.

Evidence: A Good questions: “What does a normal week look like for you?” “Which festival means most to you, and why?” Open, curious and about the person’s own experience.

Think aloud: For example: “What does ___ mean to you?”

Check: Does one visitor speak for everyone who shares their worldview? No. They speak from their own lived experience.
