Big question: do beliefs make us who we are?

Learning goal: Discuss a big question about identity respectfully and give a reason for a viewpoint.

Words to use

WordMeaning
viewpointWhat a person thinks about a question
reasonWhy you think something
evidenceAn example or fact that supports a reason
dependsWhen the answer changes with the situation

Remember

Give one respectful discussion phrase. For example: “I see your point, but…”.

Is “it depends” an acceptable answer? Yes, when it comes with a reason.

How to show your learning

State your viewpoint on the big question.

I think ___ because ___. For example, ___.

Evidence matters

Fictional cases are teaching examples. Historical, geographical and religious claims use the named sources. A model video demonstrates a method.

This is learning about religion and worldviews, not worship. Nobody is asked to share a personal belief, family detail or loss.
