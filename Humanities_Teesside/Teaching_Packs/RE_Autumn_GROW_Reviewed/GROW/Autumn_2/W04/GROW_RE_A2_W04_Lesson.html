Opening

GROW · RE & World Views · Autumn 2 · Week 4

Big question: do beliefs make us who we are?

Learning goal

Discuss a big question about identity respectfully and give a reason for a viewpoint.

What does it mean to belong to a community of belief?

I can describe belonging, explain a feature of a place of worship, use RE vocabulary and give a reasoned viewpoint.

40 minutes, with flexible pauses. Point, speak, draw, type or write. A fictional example is welcome. Nobody is asked to share a personal belief.

Preparation

Choose one independent route. Print the evidence and shared cards, required visual or data sheet, route worksheet and exit. The organiser is one separate A4 page. Keep answers with staff.

Access and regulation

No forced speaking; students may write/place cards; sentence stems; processing time; visual response scale; third-person option.

Shared activity reasoning

1. “I see your point, but family matters too.” — Respectful. It accepts the other view and adds a reason.

2. “That’s a stupid thing to say.” — Needs changing. It attacks the person and gives no reason.

3. “It depends. Can you give an example?” — Respectful. It asks for evidence.

Exit responses

Give one respectful discussion phrase. For example: “I see your point, but…”.

Teaching and evidence record

Supplied scheme of work teaching sequence: Arrival: 4 retrieval questions. Starter: choose a big question, e.g. ‘Do beliefs make us who we are?’ I Do: model agreeing/disagreeing respectfully with a reason. We Do: sort statements into agree / disagree / depends and justify one. You Do: give a supported response to the big question using evidence/example. Exit ticket: one respectful discussion phrase.

Look for

F: supported response to the big question.

Accept accurate equivalents, including speech or exact scribing. Check the concept and use of evidence rather than length or handwriting. Record access support separately from content prompting.

BUILD: a concrete example with a reason or demonstrated action. GROW: explain or compare with a relevant detail. LAUNCH: connect evidence and reasoning; use a limitation when the outcome requires it. Keep RE responses non-GCSE.
