Belonging across faiths

Original captioned teaching model made for this pack. It demonstrates the supplied reasoning; it is not historical footage, survivor testimony or a documentary. The full transcript and factual source credits are included.

Question: Give one way a faith community can support belonging.

Evidence: A Simran: Fictional profile: Simran is Sikh. At the gurdwara she helps serve langar, a free meal for anyone. She says serving others makes her feel part of something.

Think aloud: For example, sharing a meal, praying together or serving others.

Check: Does everyone in a faith community feel belonging in the same way? No. Belonging varies between individuals.
