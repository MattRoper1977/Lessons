Hanukkah and the theme of light

Original captioned teaching model made for this pack. It demonstrates the supplied reasoning; it is not historical footage, survivor testimony or a documentary. The full transcript and factual source credits are included.

Question: Give one meaning of light at Hanukkah.

Evidence: C The oil: They rededicated the Temple and lit its lamp. Tradition says there was only enough pure oil for one day, but it lasted eight. D Hanukkah today: Jewish families light one more light each night for eight nights. The lights recall the rededication and stand for faith and hope.

Think aloud: Remembering the rededication of the Temple, faith or hope.

Check: How many nights does Hanukkah last? Eight nights.
