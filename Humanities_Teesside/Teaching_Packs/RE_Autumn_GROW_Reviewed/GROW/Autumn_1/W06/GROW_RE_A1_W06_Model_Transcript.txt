Comparing festivals of light

Original captioned teaching model made for this pack. It demonstrates the supplied reasoning; it is not historical footage, survivor testimony or a documentary. The full transcript and factual source credits are included.

Question: Give one similarity between two festivals of light.

Evidence: A Diwali: Many Hindus, Sikhs and Jains. Diyas and rangoli. For many Hindus the light recalls Rama and Sita and good overcoming evil. B Hanukkah: Jewish. Eight nights. The menorah recalls the rededication of the Temple and the oil that lasted.

Think aloud: Both use light as a symbol, or both bring people together.

Check: Do we compare festivals to decide which is best? No. We compare to understand.
