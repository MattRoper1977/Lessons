GROW · RE & World Views · Autumn 1 · Week 6

Comparing festivals of light

Learning goal

Compare two festivals of light respectfully using one similarity and one difference.

How do beliefs and festivals show what matters to people?

I can name different worldviews, explain a festival symbol, compare two festivals and explain a shared value.

40 minutes, with flexible pauses. Point, speak, draw, type or write. A fictional example is welcome. Nobody is asked to share a personal belief.

Editable Word packPrintable pupil resources PDF

This is learning about religion and worldviews, not worship. Nobody is asked to share a personal belief, family detail or loss.

Teacher notes and answers

Teacher card

Comparing festivals of light / GROW_RE_A1_W06

Outcome: Compare two festivals of light respectfully using one similarity and one difference.

Evidence to retain: F: comparison with one similarity and one difference.

Forty minute route

Arrival 4 min; starter 3; model 6; shared task 7; check 2; independent 12; review 3; exit 3. Pause time is flexible.

Preparation

Choose one independent route. Print the evidence and shared cards, required visual or data sheet, route worksheet and exit. The organiser is one separate A4 page. Keep answers with staff.

Model comparison of ideas, not ranking religions; use ‘some/many'; adult checks one accurate fact for each festival before comparison.

Access and regulation

Compare only two festivals; colour-coded prompts; ‘poth/however' frame; oral-first response; scribe/typing; reduced writing target.

Offer a predictable start, private response and agreed pause. No cold calling or forced personal disclosure. Rejoin at one clearly named next step. Keep teen-appropriate examples and the same learning goal across response modes.

This is learning about religion and worldviews, not worship. Nobody is asked to share a personal belief, family detail or loss.

Assessment

Use questioning and the exit response to decide the next step. Record the learner’s individual contribution and the access support used.

UAS: no approved unit code is supplied. Match the learner’s evidence to the centre-selected unit and exact criteria before submission. This pack makes no automatic award claim.

Arrival answers

1. The incarnation: God became human in Jesus.

2. To look for what is the same and what is different

3. Both use light as a symbol, or both bring people together.

4. No. We compare to understand.

Shared activity reasoning

1. Light is used as a symbol — Similarity. All three cards mention light.

2. The story behind the light — Difference. Each festival recalls a different story.

3. Families and communities gather — Similarity. All three bring people together.

Exit responses

Give one similarity between two festivals of light. Both use light as a symbol, or both bring people together.

Do we compare festivals to decide which is best? No. We compare to understand.

Teaching and evidence record

Supplied scheme of work teaching sequence: Arrival: 4 retrieval questions from Diwali, Hanukkah and Christmas. Starter: identify each festival from an image. I Do: model a respectful comparison using ‘poth / however". We Do: sort similarities and differences in symbols, stories and meanings. You Do: compare two festivals using a scaffold. Exit ticket: one similarity or difference.

Look for

F: comparison with one similarity and one difference.

Accept accurate equivalents, including speech or exact scribing. Check the concept and use of evidence rather than length or handwriting. Record access support separately from content prompting.

BUILD: a concrete example with a reason or demonstrated action. GROW: explain or compare with a relevant detail. LAUNCH: connect evidence and reasoning; use a limitation when the outcome requires it. Keep RE responses non-GCSE.
