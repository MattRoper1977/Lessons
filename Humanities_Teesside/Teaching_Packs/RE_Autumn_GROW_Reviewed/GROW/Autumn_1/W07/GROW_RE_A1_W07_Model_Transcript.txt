Remembrance and shared values

Original captioned teaching model made for this pack. It demonstrates the supplied reasoning; it is not historical footage, survivor testimony or a documentary. The full transcript and factual source credits are included.

Question: Name one value that Ruth and Dan share.

Evidence: B A religious response: Fictional profile: Ruth is Christian. At her church service people pray for peace and read out names from the village memorial. C A non-religious response: Fictional profile: Dan is a Humanist. He stands in silence at the town memorial and thinks about how to prevent war.

Think aloud: Respect, gratitude or hope for peace.

Check: Do people have to be religious to take part in remembrance? No. People of all worldviews remember.
