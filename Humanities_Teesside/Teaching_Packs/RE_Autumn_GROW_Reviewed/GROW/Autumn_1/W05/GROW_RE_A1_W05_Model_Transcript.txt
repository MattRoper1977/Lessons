Christmas and Christian belief

Original captioned teaching model made for this pack. It demonstrates the supplied reasoning; it is not historical footage, survivor testimony or a documentary. The full transcript and factual source credits are included.

Question: Name one belief connected to Christmas.

Evidence: A The belief: Christians believe Jesus is the Son of God. The incarnation means God became human in Jesus. Christmas celebrates his birth.

Think aloud: The incarnation: God became human in Jesus.

Check: Is every Christmas tradition a Christian belief? No. Some traditions are cultural.
