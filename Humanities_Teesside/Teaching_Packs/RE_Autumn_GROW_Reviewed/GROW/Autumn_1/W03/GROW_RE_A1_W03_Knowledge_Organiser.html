Diwali: festival, story and meaning

Learning goal: Explain one meaning connected to Diwali.

Words to use

WordMeaning
DiwaliA festival of lights celebrated by many Hindus, Sikhs and Jains
diyaA small lamp, often made of clay
rangoliA bright pattern made on the floor to welcome guests
symbolSomething that stands for an idea

Remember

Name one thing light can represent at Diwali. Good overcoming evil, hope or welcome.

Is Diwali celebrated only by Hindus? No. Many Sikhs and Jains also celebrate at this time, with their own meanings.

How to show your learning

Choose one Diwali symbol or practice.

At Diwali, ___ is a symbol of ___ because ___.

Evidence matters

Fictional cases are teaching examples. Historical, geographical and religious claims use the named sources. A model video demonstrates a method.

This is learning about religion and worldviews, not worship. Nobody is asked to share a personal belief, family detail or loss.
