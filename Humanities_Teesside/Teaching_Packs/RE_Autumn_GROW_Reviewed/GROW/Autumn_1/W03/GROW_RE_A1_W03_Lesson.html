GROW · RE & World Views · Autumn 1 · Week 3

Diwali: festival, story and meaning

Learning goal

Explain one meaning connected to Diwali.

How do beliefs and festivals show what matters to people?

I can name different worldviews, explain a festival symbol, compare two festivals and explain a shared value.

40 minutes, with flexible pauses. Point, speak, draw, type or write. A fictional example is welcome. Nobody is asked to share a personal belief.

Editable Word packPrintable pupil resources PDF

This is learning about religion and worldviews, not worship. Nobody is asked to share a personal belief, family detail or loss.

Word and evidence bank

A Rama and Sita

Many Hindus tell the story of Rama and Sita at Diwali. After Rama rescues Sita from Ravana, people light lamps to guide them home. Light stands for good overcoming evil.

Authored retelling; see Hindu American Foundation, Diwali

Limit: Not everyone who celebrates Diwali tells this story.

B Different communities

Many Sikhs mark Bandi Chhor Divas at the same time, remembering Guru Hargobind’s release from prison. Many Jains remember Mahavira. One festival season holds several meanings.

Authored RE summary

Limit: These are short summaries of long traditions.

C Practices

Families clean and decorate homes, make rangoli, light diyas, share sweets and visit each other. Many Hindus also pray to Lakshmi.

Preparation

Choose one independent route. Print the evidence and shared cards, required visual or data sheet, route worksheet and exit. The organiser is one separate A4 page. Keep answers with staff.

Image-led input; reduced symbol set; read-aloud; matching before writing; sentence stems; draw/label option.

Teaching and evidence record

Supplied scheme of work teaching sequence: Arrival: 4 retrieval questions. Starter: mystery images — diya, rangoli, lights, sweets. I Do: introduce Diwali through a short story/source and explain that it is celebrated in different ways by Hindu, Sikh and Jain communities. We Do: match symbols/practices to meanings. You Do: create a ‘Diwali: symbol → meaning’ response. Exit ticket: one thing light can represent at Diwali.

Look for

F: one symbol → meaning response.

Accept accurate equivalents, including speech or exact scribing. Check the concept and use of evidence rather than length or handwriting. Record access support separately from content prompting.

BUILD: a concrete example with a reason or demonstrated action. GROW: explain or compare with a relevant detail. LAUNCH: connect evidence and reasoning; use a limitation when the outcome requires it. Keep RE responses non-GCSE.
