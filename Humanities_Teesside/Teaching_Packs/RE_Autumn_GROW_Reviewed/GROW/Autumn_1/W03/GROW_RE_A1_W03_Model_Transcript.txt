Diwali: festival, story and meaning

Original captioned teaching model made for this pack. It demonstrates the supplied reasoning; it is not historical footage, survivor testimony or a documentary. The full transcript and factual source credits are included.

Question: Name one thing light can represent at Diwali.

Evidence: A Rama and Sita: Many Hindus tell the story of Rama and Sita at Diwali. After Rama rescues Sita from Ravana, people light lamps to guide them home. Light stands for good overcoming evil.

Think aloud: Good overcoming evil, hope or welcome.

Check: Is Diwali celebrated only by Hindus? No. Many Sikhs and Jains also celebrate at this time, with their own meanings.
