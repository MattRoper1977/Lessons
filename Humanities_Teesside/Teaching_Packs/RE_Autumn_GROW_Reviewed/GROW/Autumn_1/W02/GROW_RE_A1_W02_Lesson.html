How beliefs shape identity

Learning goal

Describe one way beliefs can shape a person's identity.

How do beliefs and festivals show what matters to people?

I can name different worldviews, explain a festival symbol, compare two festivals and explain a shared value.

40 minutes, with flexible pauses. Point, speak, draw, type or write. A fictional example is welcome. Nobody is asked to share a personal belief.

Editable Word packPrintable pupil resources PDF

This is learning about religion and worldviews, not worship. Nobody is asked to share a personal belief, family detail or loss.

Arrival answers

1. Any two, for example Christianity and Humanism.

2. Who a person is and how they see themselves

3. Generosity: caring for those in need.

4. No. Family, friends, place and culture also shape identity.

Shared activity reasoning

1. “God asks people to care for those in need.” — Belief. It is something Imran holds to be true.

2. Generosity matters to him — Value. It is what he thinks is important.

3. Helps at the food bank each week — Choice. It is what he decides to do.

Exit responses

What value does Imran’s belief lead to? Generosity: caring for those in need.

Is belief the only thing that shapes identity? No. Family, friends, place and culture also shape identity.
