The supplied Humanities scheme also plans 39 Summer lesson slots. No matching created Summer lessons were in the reviewed packs. RE source planning covers Autumn only. Those periods are not represented as completed lessons.

Changes across the packs

  • Matched the stated outcome, teaching sequence and assessment evidence to the supplied scheme; corrected internal examples, sources and datasets where they did not serve those aims.
  • Linked worked models to relevant named evidence. Added retrieval prompts, supported/standard/stretch routes, independent organisers, printable shared-activity cards and teacher answers.
  • Kept varied shared work: matching, sequencing, decisions, maps, source investigation, ranking, graphs, presentations and portfolios. Added keyboard-operated checks and feedback to card activities.
  • Retained BUILD’s concrete and symbol-supported tasks, GROW’s explanation and comparison, and LAUNCH’s evidence and application. RE LAUNCH remains an adult-life pathway without GCSE marking bands.
  • Rebuilt offline captioned teaching models and editable slides. Every lesson has a one-page A4 knowledge organiser, pupil pack and teacher notes.

Checks and remaining limits

All 240 revised Word files were rendered; page-bound checks found no empty pages or text outside the page. PDF organisers were checked for one-page A4 output. Slide packages were validated and rendered. Representative and dense page layouts were visually reviewed. Eleven data workbooks were created; fieldwork blank/zero behaviour was tested.

Live browser layout, phone/touch behaviour and playback on a school device could not be verified here because the available browser blocked local files. Check those before teaching. Source restrictions are recorded separately. This is a resource review against the supplied pathway guidance, not an assessment of individual learners; adjust reading, communication and sensory support using their plans.
