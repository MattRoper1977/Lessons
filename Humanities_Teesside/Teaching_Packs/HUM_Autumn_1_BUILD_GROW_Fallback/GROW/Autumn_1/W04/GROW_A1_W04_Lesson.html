Diverse British history

Learning goal

Investigate diverse British history linked to Black History Month.

How has migration shaped identity in Britain?

I can sequence events, use a source, explain a reason for migration and describe a contribution or change.

40 minutes, with flexible pauses. Point, speak, draw, type or write. A fictional example is welcome.
