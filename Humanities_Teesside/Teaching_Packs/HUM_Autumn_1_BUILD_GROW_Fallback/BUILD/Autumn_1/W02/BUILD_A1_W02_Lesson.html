BUILD · Humanities · Autumn 1 · Week 2

People who help us

Learning goal

Identify people who help in the community.

Who helps us belong to a community?

I can identify important people and places, describe a change, and explain one way I belong.

40 minutes, with flexible pauses. Point, speak, draw, type or write. A fictional example is welcome.
