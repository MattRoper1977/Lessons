Opening

BUILD · Humanities · Autumn 1 · Week 1

People special to me

Learning goal

Talk about family and people special to me.

Who helps us belong to a community?

I can identify important people and places, describe a change, and explain one way I belong.

40 minutes, with flexible pauses. Point, speak, draw, type or write. A fictional example is welcome.

    BUILD_A1_W01 · SOW page 3 · F: people map and one reason the person matters.

    Model an inclusive people map. Together match people to caring actions; independently add a chosen person using symbols or a sentence. Allow a fictional example without personal disclosure.

    Subject answers

    Sam explained choices and showed a quiet space, helping Alex join in with support. No. A fictional person is a complete response.

    Shared task feedback

    1. Sam explains the choices → Care: Alex knows what can happen next.
    2. Sam refuses to explain → Needs changing: Alex still needs clear information.
    3. Alex chooses a quiet table → Choice: The choice can help Alex participate.
