BUILD · Humanities · Autumn 1 · Week 6

Our class community map

Learning goal

Contribute to a class community map.

Who helps us belong to a community?

I can identify important people and places, describe a change, and explain one way I belong.

40 minutes, with flexible pauses. Point, speak, draw, type or write. A fictional example is welcome.

    Independent application

    Choose one route. Complete the required outcome with the support you need.

    Supported task

    1. Add one place or helper to a class map, with your own labelled copy.
    2. Explain why the place or person matters.
    3. Answer independently: what lies north of the hall on the practice map?
    Begin with one step. Read the sources with an adult, reduce the visible choice set and use: My contribution is ___. It matters because ___. North of the hall is ___.

    Keep all required evidence; speech, pointing, drawing and exact scribing are available.

    Standard task

    1. Add one place or helper to a class map, with your own labelled copy.
    2. Explain why the place or person matters.
    3. Answer independently: what lies north of the hall on the practice map?
    My contribution is ___. It matters because ___. North of the hall is ___.

    Check your evidence before adding a final explanation.

    Stretch task

    1. Add one place or helper to a class map, with your own labelled copy.
    2. Explain why the place or person matters.
    3. Answer independently: what lies north of the hall on the practice map?
    Plan your answer using the source labels. Use the frame if useful.

    Check whether the key lets another person understand the map; make one improvement.
