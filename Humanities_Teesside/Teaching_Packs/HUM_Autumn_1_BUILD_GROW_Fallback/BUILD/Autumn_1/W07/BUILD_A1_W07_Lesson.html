BUILD · Humanities · Autumn 1 · Week 7

Groups we belong to

Learning goal

Share a group team or community I belong to.

Who helps us belong to a community?

I can identify important people and places, describe a change, and explain one way I belong.

40 minutes, with flexible pauses. Point, speak, draw, type or write. A fictional example is welcome.

    Independent

    Independent application

    Choose one route. Complete the required outcome with the support you need.

    Supported task

    1. Create a symbol or drawing of a real or fictional group.
    2. Explain its purpose and one way people can belong.
    3. Share live, privately, through a recording or exact adult scribing.
    Begin with one step. Read the sources with an adult, reduce the visible choice set and use: This group is ___. People ___. Someone can belong by ___.

    Keep all required evidence; speech, pointing, drawing and exact scribing are available.

    Standard task

    1. Create a symbol or drawing of a real or fictional group.
    2. Explain its purpose and one way people can belong.
    3. Share live, privately, through a recording or exact adult scribing.
    This group is ___. People ___. Someone can belong by ___.

    Check your evidence before adding a final explanation.

    Stretch task

    1. Create a symbol or drawing of a real or fictional group.
    2. Explain its purpose and one way people can belong.
    3. Share live, privately, through a recording or exact adult scribing.
    Plan your answer using the source labels. Use the frame if useful.
