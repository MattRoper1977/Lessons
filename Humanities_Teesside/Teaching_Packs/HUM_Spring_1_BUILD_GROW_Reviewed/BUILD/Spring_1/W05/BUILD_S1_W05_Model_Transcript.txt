A question about the past

Original captioned teaching model made for this pack. It demonstrates the supplied reasoning; it is not historical footage, survivor testimony or a documentary. The full transcript and factual source credits are included.

Question: What source could help date an old telephone?

Evidence: D Questions worth asking: Who used this object? When was this photo made? A dated catalogue entry can help with time; a user’s account can help with experience.

Think aloud: A dated manufacturer’s catalogue or museum record.

Check: Would looking at the object tell us every user’s experience? No. A user account could help with that question.
