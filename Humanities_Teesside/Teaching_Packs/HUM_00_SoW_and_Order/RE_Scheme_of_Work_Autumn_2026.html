RE & World Views · scheme of work

Autumn term 2026 · BUILD · GROW · LAUNCH · 42 weekly outcomes · 1 × 40-minute lesson per week · Arrival → Starter → I Do → We Do → You Do → Exit Ticket

How this term works

Each week has one interactive lesson, a captioned model video, an editable Word pack, editable slides, a pupil PDF, teacher notes and a knowledge organiser. F means formative assessment; S means summative. RE here is learning about religion and worldviews. No activity is worship, and no pupil is asked to share a personal belief, family detail or loss. The planners name no approved UAS unit code: match retained work to the exact centre-selected unit before submission.

BUILD · Autumn 1

Enquiry: What makes something special, and how do we show respect?
Success criteria: I can say why something is special, name a special book or place, and show one respectful action.

Week 1 · Special things, beliefs & belonging F
This week's outcome
Talk about what is special to me and give one reason why.
Activities
Arrival: 4 simple retrieval/misconception questions about special, sacred, belief and respect. Starter: choose which object might be special and why. I Do: model 'This is special because...' using an everyday object, then link to how religious people value special objects. We Do: match objects to possible meanings. You Do: choose, draw or describe one special object and give a reason. Exit ticket: complete 'Something can be special because...'.
Key vocabulary
special, sacred, belief, object, meaning, respect
Resources required
PPT; age-neutral object images; 'special to me' object cards; reason cards; optional real objects; short evidence sheet or exercise book.
Evidence
F: one special object and one reason.
Student adaptations
Visual choices; 2-option matching; sentence stem; drawing/verbal/scribed response; no requirement to share a real personal object; typing option.
Classroom support
Model first; no cold calling; accept pointing before speaking/writing; prompt with concrete choices; keep personal disclosure optional.
