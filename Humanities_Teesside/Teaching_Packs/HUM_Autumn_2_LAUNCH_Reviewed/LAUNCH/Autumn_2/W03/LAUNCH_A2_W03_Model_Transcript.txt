Settlement and urbanisation

Original captioned teaching model made for this pack. It demonstrates the supplied reasoning; it is not historical footage, survivor testimony or a documentary. The full transcript and factual source credits are included.

Question: Is adding buildings alone enough to prove national urbanisation?

Evidence: B Reasons places grow: Work, transport and services can attract people and businesses. A growing town may need more homes and infrastructure. Population growth can also occur within existing settlements.

Think aloud: No. Urbanisation concerns the share of people living in urban places.

Check: What further evidence would show population change? Comparable population data from different dates.
