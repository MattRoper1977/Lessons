Causes of tectonic hazards

Original captioned teaching model made for this pack. It demonstrates the supplied reasoning; it is not historical footage, survivor testimony or a documentary. The full transcript and factual source credits are included.

Question: What caused the 2011 tsunami?

Evidence: A Japan 11 March 2011: A magnitude 9.1 earthquake occurred off north-east Honshu, Japan. Sudden movement on the subduction boundary displaced the seafloor and produced a tsunami.

Think aloud: Sudden seafloor displacement during the earthquake moved the water above it.

Check: Is magnitude the same as risk? No. Exposure and vulnerability also influence potential harm.
