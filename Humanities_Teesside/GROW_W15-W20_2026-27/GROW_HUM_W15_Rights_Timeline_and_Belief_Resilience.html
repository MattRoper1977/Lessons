Rights Timeline and Belief Resilience

Verbatim workbook outcome: Place key protest/rights events on a timeline (chronology). · Explore how beliefs help people cope with change and hardship. · Trace: 'GROW Spring'!C40 · 'GROW Spring'!C52

Objective: Place supplied rights and protest events in chronological order and explore how beliefs can support resilience in hardship without requiring personal disclosure.
Success evidence:
