# BUILD_SU1_W01 · TA deployment brief

## EEF five-layer scaffolding key

**Self-scaffold → prompt → clue → model → correct.** Move down only when the pupil can't go on; move back up as soon as they can. Start by waiting, checking the pupil’s own attempt and using the existing visual, communication or reading aid. An access aid is not a content answer.

1. **Self-scaffold — watch for:** Uses the key to locate the school without a content prompt. Let the pupil check the source, key, diagram or own work before speaking.
2. **Prompt — two open questions:** “What part of the plan are you looking at?” “Where could you check the symbol?” Pause after each; accept point, sign, AAC, speech or writing.
3. **Clue — two narrowed questions:** “Which picture and letter match in the key?” “Where is that symbol on the checked plan?” Give one clue at a time and then step back.
4. **Model — one step only:** Show one first step: Start at the school symbol; match one picture in the key to one named place. Then the pupil tries the next matching move with the source in view.
5. **Correct — one direct fact and when:** If the pupil still cannot continue after that model, give only “School” for “On the Townside plan, what does S mark?”. Then ask for a fresh application, record the level used and fade back toward self-scaffolding.

**Objective:** Name and locate my school and local features.

| Stage | Position and role |
|---|---|
| Arrival / Starter | Sit at the side of the group with sight of access needs; quietly support the pupil using the agreed communication or reading aid. Observe the first unprompted attempt. |
| We do / We do 2 | Move beside the pair checking the model or source; alternate turns, point to the evidence and fade cues. Avoid becoming the pupil's answer-giver. |
| Independent | Move to the pupil needing a scribe, reader or accessible pointing aid if assigned; record the pupil's own choice or wording, with prompt level. Teacher retains oversight of the group. |
| Exit | Step back within hearing; offer the existing access route and allow an independent response where possible. Tell the teacher what was actually seen and what support was used. |

**Regulation break:** Offer the pre-agreed help/pause signal without asking for a public explanation. Follow the pupil's existing plan for place, adult and length; retain supervision. On return, point to the last completed step, offer a small re-entry choice, and note access/support factually. Do not create a difficulty to elicit evidence.
