Townside picture plan

Practise with Townside. Use the teacher’s checked school plan for the real-school task.

local: Near the place we are talking about.

feature: Something in a place, such as a park or road.

map: A picture that shows where places are.

key: A guide to the symbols on a map.

Arrival: Supported

Use the Townside picture plan. Point, speak, draw or write.

1. Point to the school picture.

Use the roof and S symbol.

2. Which picture shows a park?

Choose the tree or the bus.

3. Point to the place beside the school on its right.

Use your finger to move one square.

4. Find B in the key. What place does it mark?

Point to the bus picture or say its name.

Arrival: Standard

Use the Townside picture plan. Point, speak, draw or write.

1. Where is the school on the plan?

Use top, bottom, left or right.

2. What does L in the key mean?

Find the same letter on the plan.

3. Which place is to the right of the library?

Keep the plan the same way up.

4. Which place is directly below the school?

Move down one square.

Arrival: Stretch

Use the Townside picture plan. Point, speak, draw or write.

1. Locate the school without using a place name.

Describe its position on the plan.

2. Why is a key useful to a visitor?

Link a symbol to its meaning.

3. From the school, move two squares right. Where are you?

Check each square with your finger.

4. Is the library nearer to the school than the park on this plan?

Count the equal squares along the top row.

Clue hunt and visitor note

Use the Townside plan. Match each clue to a place. Point to the picture that helped you.

1. I want to borrow a book.

2. I need a place to wait for a bus.

3. I want an open green space.

4. I have an appointment with a nurse.

Repair the visitor note

“The library is to the left of the school.”

Check the plan. Change one word.

Show what helped you decide.

Our school: Supported

Use the plan your teacher supplies.

  1. Choose the school label from two labels on your teacher’s plan.
  2. Point to the school. Name it or show its label.
  3. Find one local feature, then try a second with less help.

An adult reads one step. Use a counter to mark your choice. Point or say: The school is here.

Use the plan your teacher supplies.

  1. Name and mark the school on your teacher’s plan.
  2. Circle two local features. Name each one.
  3. Tell or write where one feature is in relation to the school.

Use the plan your teacher supplies.

  1. Locate and label the school and three local features.
  2. Choose one feature that a visitor might need.
  3. Describe a short route from the school to that feature.
  4. Check each turn. Explain how the key helped.
