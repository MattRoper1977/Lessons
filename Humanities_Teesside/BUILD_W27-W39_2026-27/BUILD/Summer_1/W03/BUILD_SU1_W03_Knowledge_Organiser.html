Comparing two places

Learning goal: Compare my place with a contrasting place.

Words to use

WordMeaning
compareLook at two things to find what is the same and different.
similaritySomething that is the same or alike.
differenceSomething that is not the same.
evidenceWhat we can point to or observe to support an idea.

A method to use

  1. Choose one feature to compare.
  2. Look at that feature in both places.
  3. Record a similarity or difference.
  4. Point to evidence and explain.

Point, speak, draw, type or write. Use the key and check the evidence.

Practice plans are invented. Use your teacher’s checked local sources for the final task.
