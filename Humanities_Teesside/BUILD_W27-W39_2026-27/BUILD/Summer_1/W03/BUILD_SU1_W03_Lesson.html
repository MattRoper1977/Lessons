Opening

BUILD · Humanities · Summer 1 · Week 3

Comparing two places

Learning goal

Compare my place with a contrasting place.

What is the same and different about two places?

I can observe two places, record a comparison and use evidence to explain a difference.

40 minutes, with flexible pauses. Point, speak, draw, type or write.

Independent

Independent application

Use the checked local and contrasting photo/map sets.

Choose one route and one response mode. Keep the same learning goal.

Supported task

  1. Choose one feature on the local source set with your teacher.
  2. Point to or draw what each place shows in the two columns.
  3. Say one difference using both sources.

Adult reads the labels. Use: My place shows ___. The other place shows ___.

Try a second comparison with less prompting.

Standard task

  1. Use the local and contrasting source sets.
  2. Complete two rows of the comparison grid.
  3. Explain one important difference using evidence from both places.

Use: In source ___ I can see ___. In source ___ I can see ___. This matters because ___.

Add one similarity, or state something the sources cannot tell you.

Stretch task

  1. Choose three features to compare in the two source sets.
  2. Record accurate observations from both.
  3. Explain which contrast could matter most for one activity.
  4. State one limit of the evidence.

Choose an activity such as playing outdoors or reaching a shop. Say “could” when making an inference.

Suggest a photo, map or observation that would help check your explanation.

Teacher card

Staff only | BUILD_SU1_W03

Comparing two places

Outcome: Compare my place with a contrasting place.

Assessment: F: observation and recorded comparison.

Teaching: Use two photo/map sets; build a comparison grid and explain one important contrast with evidence.

Preparation

Townside and Coastside are invented practice places. Before teaching, add two labelled local photos or a checked local plan and a contrasting place photo/map set to the editable evidence sheet. Do not present the practice plans as the learner’s actual place. Compare the same feature in both sets. A missing symbol is not proof that a feature is absent from a real place.

40 minute route

Opening 0; Arrival 4; Starter 3; I do 6; We do 7; Check 2; Independent 12; Review 3; Exit 3.

Access and feedback

One instruction at a time. Point, speak, draw, type or write. Use the supported route for a smaller choice set. Fade content prompts before the independent check.

Use neutral language about places and avoid asking about home addresses or personal circumstances. Offer a quiet response, a partner rehearsal or a pause.

Retain the learner’s observation and comparison. Record the source labels and support used. Accept pointing, speech, drawing or writing with evidence from both places.

Worked model

  1. Question: What is different about the green and open spaces shown?
  2. Evidence: Townside shows a park with a tree symbol. Coastside shows a beach beside the sea.
  3. Explain: The plans show different kinds of open space: a park and a beach.
  4. Check: Point to both symbols and keys. These plans do not tell us which place is better.

Arrival answers

Supported

  1. H marks the Townside shop.
  2. The shop is on the lower left.
  3. Coastside.
  4. A similarity: both show a shop.

Standard

  1. Accept school, library, park, bus stop, crossing, shop, court, clinic or flats.
  2. Accept harbour, beach, sea, shop, café or homes.
  3. Both show shops or homes.
  4. Coastside: the beach label and sand beside the sea.

Stretch

  1. Both show homes: Townside flats and Coastside homes.
  2. Townside shows a park; Coastside shows a beach.
  3. No. There are no noise measurements.
  4. No. It may be outside the selected area or omitted.

Shared activity answers

  1. A shop is shown. Both plans. Townside H and the Coastside shop symbol both show shops.
  2. A park is shown. Townside only. Townside P marks a park; no park symbol is shown on Coastside.
  3. A beach is shown. Coastside only. The Coastside beach label marks sand beside the sea.
  4. Homes are shown. Both plans. Townside F shows flats. Coastside shows homes.
  5. A harbour is shown. Coastside only. The harbour is labelled on Coastside; none is shown on Townside.

Check answer

No. The plan shows selected features only. We can say no school is shown, but we need more evidence to say there is no school.

Independent and exit answers

Answers depend on the checked local source set. Retain the learner’s observation and comparison. Record the source labels and support used. Accept pointing, speech, drawing or writing with evidence from both places.

Week 3: require observations from both places, an accurate comparison and evidence. Accept not shown or cannot tell where appropriate.
