Paper fieldwork tally

Real observations | Take this page, a pencil and a clipboard

Question: How many benches, bins and trees are in our two agreed areas?

Stay with the supervising adult. Follow the agreed route inside the grounds. Observe from the path. Make one mark for each object. Count each object only once. Return to class to total and check. No device is needed outdoors; do not photograph people.
