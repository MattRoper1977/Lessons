Independent application

Use the paper tally sheet outdoors. Leave devices indoors. If fieldwork cannot happen, use the labelled practice tally and mark your work PRACTICE.

Choose one route and one response mode. Keep the same learning goal.

Supported task

  1. Use the agreed paper route with the adult.
  2. Count one feature type in area A. Make one mark per object.
  3. Point to or say your total and one check when back inside.

Choose benches or bins. Adult reads the row; you make the marks. Use your usual communication support.

Try the same method in area B with less prompting.

Standard task

  1. Observe the agreed feature types in A and B on the supervised route.
  2. Keep a separate tally for each feature and area.
  3. Return, total each row and explain one check yourself.

Observer points; recorder marks. Swap roles so everyone has their own recording evidence.

Combine one feature’s totals across the two non-overlapping areas.

Stretch task

  1. Use the same supervised route and clearly defined boundaries.
  2. Record each feature once, then check a row independently.
  3. Combine the totals for one feature and explain your method.
  4. Name a limitation of today’s observations.

Say: We counted ___ in area ___. We avoided repeats by ___.

Suggest how to make a repeat observation more consistent.

Teacher card

Staff only | BUILD_SU1_W04

Fieldwork in our grounds

Outcome: Carry out simple fieldwork in the grounds.

Assessment: F: method, safe participation and accurate recording.

Teaching: Agree an enquiry and roles; model tallying. Collect observations safely and record results clearly for later analysis.

Preparation

Before teaching, an adult checks an accessible route: classroom to study area A, then area B, then classroom. All stops stay inside the school grounds, away from roads, vehicle movements, water and out-of-bounds areas. Mark two non-overlapping counting areas and boundaries on the route sheet. Keep the group supervised under school arrangements. Count fixed features only; take no photos of people and collect no personal details. If conditions or staffing prevent fieldwork, use the supplied PRACTICE tally indoors and record that fieldwork participation is not evidenced.

40 minute route

Opening 0; Arrival 4; Starter 3; I do 6; We do 7; Check 2; Independent 12; Review 3; Exit 3.

Access and feedback

Give one instruction at a time. Offer pointing, speech, drawing, typing or writing. Use a reduced choice set if needed. Keep communication support and fade content prompts before the individual check.

Offer a predictable route preview, a quiet adult-supported role, a seated accessible observation point if appropriate, and an agreed return-to-class option. Use the indoor practice route if needed.

Keep the paper tally and the learner’s own method check. Note safe participation and the support used. The indoor fallback can evidence tallying and checking, but not participation in fieldwork. Revisit the practical outcome when possible.
