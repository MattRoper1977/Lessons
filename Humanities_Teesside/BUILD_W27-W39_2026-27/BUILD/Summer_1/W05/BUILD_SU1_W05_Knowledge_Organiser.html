Continents and oceans

Learning goal: Talk about the wider world using continents and oceans.

Words to use

WordMeaning
continentOne of the seven large land regions in this lesson.
oceanOne of the five named parts of the connected global ocean.
world mapA flat picture showing places across Earth.
globeA round model of Earth.

A method to use

  1. Find land and ocean on the map.
  2. Choose a continent name and find its region.
  3. Choose an ocean name and find its water area.
  4. Point, name and check both choices.

Point, speak, draw, type or write. Check your evidence.

Use the pupil world map and label bank. Point, place a card, speak, draw or write.
