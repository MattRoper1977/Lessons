Evidence cards

Read the source and limitation before using a detail

D Question and method

An enquiry needs a clear question and a method. State where observations happened, what you counted and how you avoided duplicates. Name the roles and safe route used.

Source: Authored study checklist

Limit: If you use the supplied tally, label the work as practice rather than real fieldwork.

H Mapped observations

Use the checked class plan. Put observation point labels beside the matching counts. Check that the table, graph and conclusion use the same units and totals.

Source: Authored accuracy checklist

Limit: A concept picture cannot replace a checked plan of real observations.

O Comparison and conclusion

Use the same criterion for both places. Give a conclusion that answers your enquiry and includes data. Explain one thing the evidence cannot establish.

Source: Authored evidence routine

Limit: A source gap needs further evidence rather than a guess.

S Evidence selection

Select the enquiry, method, mapped observations, comparison and conclusion. Add dates, support used and one accuracy check. An adult may record your own spoken or pointed response.

Source: Authored evidence-bank checklist

Limit: The centre checks the selected evidence against its chosen UAS requirements. No unit code is assigned here.
