Opening

GROW · Humanities · Summer 1 · Week 1

Our place in the wider world

Learning goal

Locate my place the UK and the wider world on maps.

How does our locality compare with other places?

I can locate places, use maps and grid references, collect observations, distinguish physical and human features and support a comparison with evidence.

40 minutes, with flexible pauses. Point, speak, draw, type or write. A fictional example is welcome.

Teacher notes and answers

Teacher card

Our place in the wider world / GROW_SU1_W01

Outcome: Locate my place the UK and the wider world on maps.

Evidence to retain: F: map-skills check and independent location/pattern question.

Forty minute route

Arrival 4 min; starter 3; model 6; shared task 7; check 2; independent 12; review 3; exit 3. Pause time is flexible.

Preparation

Choose one independent route. Print the evidence and shared cards, required visual or data sheet, route worksheet and exit. The organiser is one separate A4 page. Keep answers with staff.

Prepare a checked map of the school’s locality in Teesside and atlas pages showing the UK and world. Keep the same locality in each view. Use the supplied world map for the wider context; do not treat its crowded UK-area labels as precise local positions. No home-address searches. If an atlas is unavailable, the teacher may use preselected printed map extracts.

Access and regulation

Chunk sources and tasks; pre-teach vocabulary with images. Offer sentence stems, organisers, oral rehearsal and typing. Start with one modelled example; extend through a second source, comparison or supported judgement. Check the learner can begin within two minutes, then fade prompts. Begin with a simplified map or data set, then add a stretch layer. Use clear fieldwork roles and a return-to-recording routine.

Offer a predictable start, private response and agreed pause. No cold calling or forced personal disclosure. Rejoin at one clearly named next step. Keep teen-appropriate examples and the same learning goal across response modes.

Use sensitive content warnings where relevant. Do not ask for family details or personal experiences.

Assessment

Use questioning and the exit response to decide the next step. Record the learner’s individual contribution and the access support used.

UAS: no approved unit code is supplied. Match the learner’s evidence to the centre-selected unit and exact criteria before submission. This pack makes no automatic award claim.
