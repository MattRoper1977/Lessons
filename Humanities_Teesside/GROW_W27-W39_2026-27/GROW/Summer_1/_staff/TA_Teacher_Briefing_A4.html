The three visible moments

A · hinge after I-do

One question; every pupil responds at once. Choose whiteboard, finger-vote, symbol card, thumbs or point. Look across the whole group.

10-second decision: most right → move on · split → pair check · most wrong → re-teach the I-do step.

B · ladder in We-do

Recall → explain → apply/evaluate. Everyone hears the top rung; scaffold the route, don't lower the rung. Blue way in; green deeper stretch.

Five scaffolding layers

Self-scaffold → prompt → clue → model → correct. Wait and let the pupil use the source or agreed access aid; ask open questions; narrow the clue; show one step then let the pupil try; give one direct fact only if still needed. Move down only when the pupil cannot go on; move back up as soon as they can.

Teacher time at Independent

One adult works with supported pupils while the other holds the group; swap roles across the week. Agree who does which before teaching. C · Exit: four pupil-voice questions

1 What am I learning? · 2 What feedback did I get? · 3 What do I need to improve? · 4 How do I know I've got it?

Illustrations from Job 9 tasks, not assumed pupil responses or feedback. Use the actual goal and work. Pathways may use BUILD with an adult and point, sign or use symbol/AAC.

Pathways

  1. “I’m finding the school here” (points to the displayed goal or symbol with an adult).
  2. “You said check the picture key” (shows or selects the actual cue).
  3. “Next, this one” (points to the next single action).
  4. “The L matches the key” (indicates the checked example).

BUILD

  1. “I’m learning the parts of this plant” (points to the diagram).
  2. “You said I labelled the roots correctly.”
  3. “Next I need to check the stem label.”
  4. “The label points to the right part.”

GROW

  1. “I’m finding a grid square using eastings then northings.”
  2. “You said I swapped the two pairs.”
  3. “Next I’ll read eastings first.”
  4. “The square matches both numbers on the grid.”

LAUNCH

  1. “I’m evaluating two climate responses.”
  2. “You said my benefit needs a source.”
  3. “Next I’ll add evidence and a limitation.”
  4. “I used the same criterion for both choices.”

TA → teacher handoff at Exit

Pass on the deepest prompt layer reached, what the pupil then did independently, response mode/access aid and one remaining content barrier. Teacher chooses one actual NS+; at the next opportunity receive the response and engage before R. Do not infer an EHCP outcome or invent a unit code.
