# Emotion coaching card · staff pocket copy

**Progress Schools staff-facing · print two A5 copies on one A4**

1. **Notice.** See the change in pace, posture, voice or participation; adult steadies voice and pace first.
2. **Name plainly.** Say what you see in a few words; use a body signal if an emotion label does not land.
3. **Validate privately.** Calmly acknowledge that this feels hard, without an audience or a long discussion.
4. **Set a neutral boundary.** State one short safety/task limit and offer the agreed pause signal or structured choice.
5. **Problem-solve when regulated.** Return to the task with one small step; talk through the difficulty later, even next day.
6. **Regulate first, process later.** Rehearse the signal, choice and repair at a calm check-in; return to independence.

**What it is not:** not letting it go · not talking them out of it · not a long chat mid-meltdown.

**Space, not closeness:** Some pupils need their agreed supervised space. Check the existing plan for where, which adult and how to return; do not insist on proximity.

This card accompanies the Job 10 staff briefing. Follow each pupil's current plan and record actual support, not a predicted reaction.
