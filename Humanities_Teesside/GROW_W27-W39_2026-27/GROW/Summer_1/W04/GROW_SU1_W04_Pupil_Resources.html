Physical and human features

Pupil resources | GROW Humanities Summer 1 Week 4

Describe physical and human features of a place.

Authored teaching picture. Use the evidence cards and give your own response in the blank spaces.

Evidence cards

Read the source and limitation before using a detail

D River

In the authored picture, the river is a natural watercourse. Label the watercourse and explain the evidence for a physical feature.

Source: Authored feature picture

Limit: A real river may also be managed. This example specifies a natural watercourse.

H Road

The road in the picture was built for travel. Its surface and planned route are evidence of a human feature.

Source: Authored feature picture

Limit: The drawing does not tell us traffic levels.

O Reservoir

A reservoir holds water behind a dam built by people. Water is a physical part of the environment, while the dam and storage purpose are human changes. Explain why Could be both is useful here.

Source: Authored reservoir example

Limit: Some classifications call the whole reservoir human. State your criterion and justify it.

S Annotation method

Point to a feature. Name it, choose a category, then add a because statement using a visible detail. Check a partner’s label against the picture.

Source: Authored annotation routine

Limit: An attractive label is not enough without a supporting detail.
