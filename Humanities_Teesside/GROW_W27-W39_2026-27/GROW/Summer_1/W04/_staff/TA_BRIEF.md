# GROW_SU1_W04 · TA deployment brief

## EEF five-layer scaffolding key

**Self-scaffold → prompt → clue → model → correct.** Move down only when the pupil can't go on; move back up as soon as they can. Start by waiting, checking the pupil’s own attempt and using the existing visual, communication or reading aid. An access aid is not a content answer.

1. **Self-scaffold — watch for:** Classifies a feature using the stated physical/human rule. Let the pupil check the source, key, diagram or own work before speaking.
2. **Prompt — two open questions:** “What exact feature are we classifying?” “What evidence shows how it formed?” Pause after each; accept point, sign, AAC, speech or writing.
3. **Clue — two narrowed questions:** “Was this named part made or managed by people?” “Which visible detail supports that?” Give one clue at a time and then step back.
4. **Model — one step only:** Show one first step: Ask whether the named feature formed naturally or was built/managed; cite what you see. Then the pupil tries the next matching move with the source in view.
5. **Correct — one direct fact and when:** If the pupil still cannot continue after that model, give only “Physical” for “A river is usually which type of feature?”. Then ask for a fresh application, record the level used and fade back toward self-scaffolding.

**Objective:** Describe physical and human features of a place.

| Stage | Position and role |
|---|---|
| Arrival / Starter | Sit at the side of the group with sight of access needs; quietly support the pupil using the agreed communication or reading aid. Observe the first unprompted attempt. |
| We do / We do 2 | Move beside the pair checking the model or source; alternate turns, point to the evidence and fade cues. Avoid becoming the pupil's answer-giver. |
| Independent | Move to the pupil needing a scribe, reader or accessible pointing aid if assigned; record the pupil's own choice or wording, with prompt level. Teacher retains oversight of the group. |
| Exit | Step back within hearing; offer the existing access route and allow an independent response where possible. Tell the teacher what was actually seen and what support was used. |

**Prompt ladder (stop once the pupil acts):** 1. What exact feature are we classifying? 2. What evidence shows how it formed? 3. How does that evidence fit the agreed rule?

**Job 2 observation link — GROW_SU1_W04:**
- **communication & interaction:** Explains a feature label with a because statement.
- **cognition & learning:** Classifies a feature using the stated physical/human rule.
- **SEMH:** Responds to a different classification by referring to the evidence.

**Record:** In the school's existing note, record the task/source, the observed action, and whether it was independent or prompted (including access support). Do not infer an EHCP outcome or assign a new unit code. Use VF for actual verbal feedback, WS for actual supported work, I for independent work; NS+ needs a specific next step and R only after a pupil response has been received and considered.

**Regulation break:** Offer the pre-agreed help/pause signal without asking for a public explanation. Follow the pupil's existing plan for place, adult and length; retain supervision. On return, point to the last completed step, offer a small re-entry choice, and note access/support factually. Do not create a difficulty to elicit evidence.

## Teacher time and handoff

**TEACHER-TIME:** Independent stage — one adult (teacher OR TA, decided at the start of the lesson) works with the supported pupil(s); the other adult holds the group. Swap roles across the week so the supported pupils get direct teacher time (EEF: supplement, not replace). DEFAULT BY RULING, not from source. The supplied lesson stage evidence does not specify a teacher-with-supported-pupils / TA-with-group exchange; agree this before integration instead of treating a proposed activity as that stage.

**HANDOFF at Exit:** Tell the teacher the deepest prompt level reached (self-scaffold, prompt, clue, model or correct), the independently demonstrated part of “I can classify a specific feature as physical or human and give the evidence for my choice.”, and the next content barrier.
