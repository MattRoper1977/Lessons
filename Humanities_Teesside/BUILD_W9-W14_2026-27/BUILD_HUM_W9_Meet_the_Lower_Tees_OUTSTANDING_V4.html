Meet the Lower Tees

BUILD · Humanities · Teesside Humanities Studio · Week 9 of 14

BUILD teaching route · evidence preparation, not qualification self-certification.

🎯 Learning objective: I can locate the lower Tees, identify an estuary and explain one physical–human connection.
✅ Success criteria
  • ◆ Order river → estuary → North Sea.
  • ▲ Label six features, including at least two physical and two human, and explain two links.
  • ★ Explain one opportunity or limit and one map limitation.
