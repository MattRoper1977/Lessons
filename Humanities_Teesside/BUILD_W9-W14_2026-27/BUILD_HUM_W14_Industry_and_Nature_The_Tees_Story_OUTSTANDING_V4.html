Industry and Nature: The Tees Story

BUILD · Humanities · Teesside Humanities Studio · Week 14 of 14

BUILD teaching route · evidence preparation, not qualification self-certification.

✅ Success criteria
  • ◆ Sort facts into industry, nature or both.
  • ▲ Use six clues, including at least two industry and two nature clues.
  • ★ Make a balanced judgement with a pressure or limitation.

ADAPTED OPERATOR-ACCOUNT CARD

HUM-B-W14-C03 · Recovery involved more than one action

Provenance source: HUM-B-W14-S01

The Canal & River Trust says conservation efforts began before the Tees Barrage opened in 1995 and the river had already begun to recover. Natural England also credits water-quality investment, habitat creation/management and conservation partnerships. The barrage is not presented as the sole cause.

Can establish
Support a cautious multi-action recovery account.
Cannot establish
Measure ecological change independently or assign recovery to one intervention.
Accessible route
Three-stage strip: damage and sewage/industrial pressure → several recovery actions and partnerships → continuing protection and care.
