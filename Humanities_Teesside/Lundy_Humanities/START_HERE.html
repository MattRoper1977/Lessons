The architecture

SPACESafe, informed, accessible routes; source/context available; pass/private/general routes visible.
VOICEA real historical judgement or a genuine choice about route, audience, source focus or provision.
AUDIENCEA named receiver restates the claim, evidence and uncertainty/limit.
INFLUENCEA bounded decision is considered and pupils hear what changed or why it did not.
Formative layer: enquiry → elicit → interpret through the existing five branches → one next step → revisit.
Evidence layer: draft → curriculum-aligned → candidate evidence → centre-verified → submitted → certified. A classroom tool cannot skip stages.

Safeguards

  • Assessed GROW/LAUNCH Week 7 files are protected and receive no content change.
  • No adult initial or receipt mark is added to GROW/LAUNCH closure.
  • No second next step, queue, backlog or participation count is created.
  • Constructed classroom sources are labelled as constructed.
  • Personal/family history is optional, never required.
  • Representative consented work replaces “best work” as the preferred future authoring standard.
