Questions that reveal the disciplinary move

Use one question, interpret the pattern and choose one next step. Do not record branch labels against pupils.

Read the whole-school card first. The interpretation branches used on this page are the humanities specialisation of the whole-school Reading the Response card, which remains the general instrument — open the card. This page layers subject detail on top of it; it does not replace it and is not a second account of the same theory.

ChronologyA source is dated 1850. Which century, and what is the safest claim if the date is approximate?19th century; use a defensible range if precision is uncertain.18th century because the year starts 18; exact date guessed.Shared misconceptionRe-model century rule, then use an unfamiliar date.
SourcesThe source says dock work was plentiful. What does that prove?It proves the source makes the claim; how far it supports the enquiry depends on content, provenance, context and corroboration.It proves dock work was plentiful; or 'biased, so useless'.Shared misconceptionCompare claim in source with evidence for the enquiry.
InferenceA photograph shows crowded housing. What can you infer and what can you not know?Inference tied to visible detail plus explicit limit.Description only or unsupported story.Mixed patternUse show/infer/cannot-know triad and recheck.
CausationIf the trigger had not happened, would migration still have occurred?Counterfactual used to weight conditions, motives and trigger.Nearest event automatically called most important.Shared misconceptionModel condition/motive/trigger with causal mechanism.
SignificanceSignificant to whom, over what timescale and by which criterion?Qualified, question-specific judgement.Famous/liked = significant.Shared misconceptionChange perspective or timescale and compare.
AgencyHow much did one person change, and what conditions enabled or limited them?Balanced agency/structure claim.Hero story or structure erases agency.Mixed patternDual-column agency/context evidence.
ChangeWhat changed, what continued and how far was this a turning point?Periodised judgement with evidence and degree.Any change = total turning point.Shared misconceptionUse before/after/continuity comparison.
AccountWhich paragraph does most work for the thesis, and why?Paragraph role linked to thesis and evidence.Topic list or evidence dump.Mixed patternReorder plan; one paragraph-to-thesis check.
Source utilityUseful for which question, because of what yield, and limited by what bar?Question-specific utility with content and provenance.NOP formula without source content.Shared misconceptionModel yield + bar + enquiry.
MapWhich route is preferable using grid, scale and relief, and what can the map not settle?Measured map evidence, counter-route and limit.Visual preference or unmeasured route.Method/data problemRepair grid/scale method and repeat.
Substantive knowledge gate: a weak causal or source judgement may reflect missing period knowledge. Re-teach the knowledge when the reasoning task assumed facts not yet secure.
