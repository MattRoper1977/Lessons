Influence guide · staff only

Offer only decisions that can genuinely move

Use the existing report-back contract in the Humanities printable packs; do not create a second log.

Adult scaffolds — not pupil words. The sentence frames below are what the adult says at report-back. They record the adult’s decision and its reason; they are not a pupil’s writing and are never displayed or read out as a pupil’s own words.

Usage rule: never show a “could not” line without its Because / boundary attached. A refusal recorded on its own is the opposite of what report-back is for.

Before asking

  1. Name what is genuinely open.
  2. List only viable options.
  3. State what evidence, curriculum, rights, safety or availability constrains the choice.
  4. Name the person who can act.
  5. Set the natural report-back point.

At report-back

Adopted
You recommended __ because __. We changed __.
Adapted
We could not do __, but your evidence led us to __.
Not adopted
We considered __. We could not use it because __. The decision remains open/closed for these reasons.
