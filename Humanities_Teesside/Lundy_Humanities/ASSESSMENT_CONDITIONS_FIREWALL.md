# Assessment conditions firewall

## Applies especially to GROW and LAUNCH Week 7

### Access is allowed

- reader;
- scribe;
- extra time;
- supervised break;
- adapted seating or room;
- enlarged source;
- approved assistive technology;
- other normal access arrangements.

### Content help is not allowed

- deciding the claim;
- selecting evidence;
- explaining which source is most useful;
- supplying a causal link;
- composing a counterargument;
- improving pupil wording;
- directing the conclusion.

### After the assessment

- collect through the normal subject process;
- keep post-assessment Lundy feedback about conditions/future design;
- do not edit or caption the answer in tutor time;
- teacher/assessor gives the one next step after review;
- representative display only after marking and consent.

### Safeguarding

Any disclosure leaves the assessment/evidence workflow and follows the school safeguarding route. No details enter a caption, source register or proposed tutor-time tool.
