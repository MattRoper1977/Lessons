Current strengths

  • The support spectrum makes degree visible.
  • Content, provenance, context and weighting are taught.
  • The current lesson links the unresolved issue to Week 2.

Before the lesson

  1. Check provenance and accessible source versions.
  2. State the viable Week 2 dockets.
  3. Brief staff that the warrant dimension is observed orally only; no new pupil surface.

Formative hinge

Prompt: Overall, how far does the set support the claim? Which detail carries most weight, which discount matters, and what would change your verdict?
Response patternOne teaching move
SecureRun the same source set against a different claim.
MixedLink each cited detail to the degree verdict.
Shared misconceptionRe-model why bias/purpose does not automatically make a source useless.
Access barrierChunk, enlarge, read aloud or transcribe without supplying evaluation.
Method/data problemRepair missing provenance, unclear source identity or unusable extract.

Active Audience

The 'bench' restates enquiry, verdict, strongest evidence and limit before probing cited detail. Use a representative range of responses rather than a 'best verdict' wall.

Bounded Influence and report-back

Pupils choose the Week 2 causation docket from viable curriculum options. Staff report the selected docket and why evidence/availability shaped the final decision.

Evidence boundary

AO3 is a developmental bridge. UAS status depends on exact selected outcomes and authorised centre decisions.

Failure checks

  • Listener rewards fluency rather than evidence.
  • 'Best answer' ranking discourages risk.
  • Warrant prompt is printed or inserted without approval.
  • Ballot includes options that cannot be taught.
  • AO3 language is treated as a grade.
