SECONDARY TEACHING SUMMARY: The National Archives and UK Parliament.

A. In 1948, Empire Windrush brought passengers from the Caribbean to Britain.

B. In 1963, Bristol campaigners opposed a bus company's racial discrimination.

C. Paul Stephenson and other organisers helped lead that campaign.

D. The 1965 Race Relations Act prohibited racial discrimination in some public places.

Beliefs and values

Compare a religious and non-religious reason for peaceful action.

CLASSROOM SOURCE SUMMARIES — paraphrases or explicitly labelled fictional applications; not quotations. Use within the one 40-minute Humanities/RE lesson.

A1. Matthew 5:9 presents Jesus praising peacemakers. This paraphrase is from a Christian sacred text.

B1. Humanists UK explains that empathy and experience can guide decisions. This is a non-religious ethical approach.

U1. The UN Day of Peace is 21 September. A fictional community event asks visitors to solve disagreements without violence.

Your tasks

  1. Order 1948, 1963 and 1965; add a description to each.
  2. Choose one change and justify its place on the timeline.
  3. Write one shared value and explain how each source could support the fictional event’s aim.

Writing stays in this open page only. It is not saved or sent. Copy what you want to keep before leaving.

Teacher guidance

Preparation, access and misconceptions

Print the matching pupil sheets. Provide pencils and coloured pencils; pointing or adult scribing is available.

Think, point, speak, draw or write. You may pass or ask for a quiet start. An adult may scribe your own words.

Watch for: Time gaps do not prove continuity. Shared values do not mean identical beliefs.

Use the matching pupil sheets for any map, table or writing frame named in these tasks.

Teacher key · task answers
  1. 1948 Windrush; 1963 Bristol campaign; 1965 Race Relations Act.
  2. Accept supported campaign/legal change; avoid claiming all racism ended.
  3. Peace is a defensible shared aim in this application. A1 can guide a Christian through Jesus’ teaching; B1 can guide a humanist through concern for experiences and consequences. Do not claim the sources prove every believer attends the Day of Peace.
