Humanities Pathway Tracker

Teesside · Migration & Identity · Autumn 1, 2026–27 · Progress Schools Tees Valley

The ladder runs BUILD → GROW → LAUNCH.

BUILD is the entry pathway. LAUNCH is the TOP pathway — the GCSE bridge, and the highest reading demand in the scheme. A pupil moving up moves towards LAUNCH, never away from it.

Every outcome below is taken from the deployed lesson files, not from a plan.

The three pathways

🧱 BUILD — entry Named frameworks, sorting and placing, a worked example always visible. Evidence is often a photo, a card or a spoken answer.
🌿 GROW — middle The same history, weighed. Provenance, standpoint, counter-points, and written accounts with a thesis.
🚀 LAUNCH — top (GCSE bridge) Exam-shaped enquiry: source utility on a spectrum, ranked causation, unfamiliar problems tackled by method.

Moving a pupil between pathways

Move up when the evidence below is there across two or more weeks — never on one lesson, and never on speed alone. Attendance gaps are not evidence of pathway level.

BUILD → GROW

  • Explains why a source was made, not only what it shows.
  • Holds more than one cause at once and says which carried more weight.
  • Writes a claim with the evidence attached, unprompted.

GROW → LAUNCH

  • Qualifies a judgement without being asked — “although”, “for whom”, “over what span”.
  • Answers a “how far” question with a position on a spectrum rather than yes or no.
  • Meets an unfamiliar source or map and reaches for a method instead of stalling.

When to move a pupil DOWN

Rarely, and never as a consequence. Move down only if the pupil is producing nothing they can point at as their own across several weeks — and check first whether the barrier is access (reading, writing, stamina, attendance) rather than understanding. Access barriers are solved with arrangements, not with a lower pathway.

Assessed weeks — read before Week 7

GROW Week 7 and LAUNCH Week 7 are assessed under centre-approved conditions.

  • Support access, never content. Reading, scribing, timing, seating and breaks are access. Deciding what to say is content.
  • The pupil’s own Week 6 plan travels in with them. Nothing else does.
  • Record every arrangement used, per pupil.
  • Do not print the Week 6 reference zone into an assessed session.
