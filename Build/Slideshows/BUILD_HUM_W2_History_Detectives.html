CHEESE LAYER · ANIMATED EXPLANATION
Not a history specialist? Say this: Say: “A detective separates what a source SHOWS from what they INFER. Point to what you can actually see. Then say what it might suggest — and what it still can’t tell you. ‘It shows ___; I infer ___; I still can’t know ___.’ Doubt is allowed and encouraged.”
