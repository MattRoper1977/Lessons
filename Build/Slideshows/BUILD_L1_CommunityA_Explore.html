I DO · teacher explains

What makes a community work?

🤲 Helping places

Food banks, care homes, charity shops, community centres — run mostly by volunteers.

🚌 Connecting places

Bus stops, stations, paths — how people reach work, shops and each other.

🌳 Sharing places

Parks, pitches, libraries — free places that belong to everyone.

💡 Gaps

Every community has gaps: litter, loneliness, nothing to do. Gaps are where projects live.

Misconception: “Kids can’t change anything round here.” Litter picks, care-home visits, food-bank collections and garden builds in this region are run by school groups exactly like this one, every single term. The question is only which gap we pick.
WE DO · together on the board

Sort the places: what does each one do for the community?

Ask: does it help people in need, belong to everyone, or move people around?

HANDS-ON · the main event

Community stations 🗺️

If we go out (check with your teacher): the short walk follows our trips rules — stay with your named adult, hi-vis on, register carried. If we stay in, the big map does the travelling for us.

Station 1 · BIG MAP

Giant map of our area on the table. Stickers: green = good things we have, red = problems you know about, yellow = empty spaces with potential. Every sticker needs a reason you can say out loud.

Station 2 · NEEDS WALL

Post-its on the wall: WHO around here might need help? (older people, young kids, the park itself, animals, new families…). One need per note. Duplicates prove a pattern — cluster them.

Station 3 · PROJECT PITCH

In pairs: pick ONE need from the wall and pitch a project the class could really do this year. 30-second pitch. Class votes with sticky dots at the end.

Aspire: spot a need nobody else has noticed — the invisible ones matter most.
