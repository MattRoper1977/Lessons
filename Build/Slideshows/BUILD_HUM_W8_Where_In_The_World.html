What a map ADDS, and what it HIDES

➕ ADDS — distance, direction, the route, the sea’s part in it.
➖ HIDES — feelings, reasons, one family’s story.
🤝 PAIR THEM — map + census + letter = the whole journey.

The model sentence: “the map shows the road; the letter shows the reason.”

🌍 And it went BOTH ways

People came here from all over. What they made went back out just as far.

Dorman Long of Middlesbrough built the Sydney Harbour Bridge — Teesside steel, shipped to Australia, opened in 1932 and still standing.

That is this whole term in one line: people arrived, and what they built left.

🛑 WHICH TOOL DO I NEED?

Read what the question is asking for:

A picture or symbol → the KEY 🗝️
A place → the INDEX & GRID 🔢
A distance → the SCALE BAR 📏

If you are still stuck, say the question out loud. The tool is usually in the words.

❓ THE MAP WILL NOT TELL ME WHY THEY LEFT.

It never will — and spotting that is the lesson, not a mistake.

A map can show 300 hard miles. It cannot show hunger, or hope, or a cousin’s letter. For the reason, go back to Week 2’s sources and Week 3’s push, pull and network.

Write both: “the map shows ___, but for the reason I need ___.”
