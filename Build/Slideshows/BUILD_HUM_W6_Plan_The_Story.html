CHEESE LAYER · ANIMATED EXPLANATION
Not a history specialist? Say this: Say: “A pile of facts isn’t a story. Plan the SEQUENCE, choose the EVIDENCE, and add the EXPLANATION that links them. First this, then that, and this is what it meant. A clear order with reasons turns facts into an account.”
