STARTER · talk task

What would make YOU move?

With your partner: imagine your family had to choose a new place to live. What would be the biggest reason to go — a job, being safe, or being near family? Why that one?

Sentence stems:
“I would move if…”
“The biggest reason is ___ because…”
“I would want to be near…”

Stuck? Think about what a family NEEDS: money coming in, feeling safe at night, people who help you. Which one matters most to you?

I DO · teacher explains

Every migration has a cause — and every arrival changes the place

💼 Work

Teesside’s steelworks, docks and railways needed thousands of workers. People came from all over Britain and the world to fill those jobs.

🛟 Safety

Some people move because home has become dangerous — war or persecution. Coming to Britain kept them safe.

👨‍👩‍👧 Family

Some people come to join family who moved first. One person settles — then others follow.

➡️ Because… so…

CAUSE → CONSEQUENCE. They came BECAUSE the steelworks needed workers, SO new streets, shops and communities grew.

Misconception: “People just turn up in a new country for no reason.” Every migration has a cause — work, safety or family. And every arrival has a consequence: Teesside’s towns grew because people came.
WE DO · together on the board

Sort the cards — CAUSE (why they came) or CONSEQUENCE (what changed)?

Ask: is this card a reason to set off (cause), or something that happened AFTER people arrived (consequence)? Causes come first; consequences follow.

HANDS-ON · the main event

Build your arrow ➡️

Ground rule (60 seconds): there are no wrong reasons in this room — every family’s story is different, and nobody has to talk about their own unless they want to.

Station 1 · ARROW STRIPS

Choose a picture reason card (job, safety, family). Build your arrow: “They came because ___” → “so ___ changed.” Stick the cards on your strip.

Station 2 · THEN & NOW

Look at the then-and-now photos of Teesside — steel, docks, railways. Match each THEN photo to its NOW photo and spot one thing that changed.

Station 3 · SAY YOUR ARROW

Say your finished arrow out loud to a partner or the TA: “They came because… so…” Recording it counts too.

Going for Grow: build a second arrow from a DIFFERENT reason and say which cause changed Teesside more.
