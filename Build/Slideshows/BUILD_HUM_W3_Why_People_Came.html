I Do

I Do: Push and Pull

Key Idea: Migration happens when reasons to LEAVE one place meet reasons to CHOOSE another. Push + pull, working together.

📖 Cause Types

Push factor — Drives people away — no work, hunger, danger.

Pull factor — Attracts people — jobs, wages, housing, family.

Network — People follow people — letters, family, friends who 'got set on'.

🧠 Watch me think

An Irish family in the 1850s: hunger at home pushes; iron wages pull; a cousin's letter from Middlesbrough says 'there's work'. Watch — no single cause moves them. All three together do.

I Do

I Do: Causes Combine

Key Idea: Real explanations link causes: a push creates the need, a pull sets the direction, a network makes it possible.

📖 Linking Language

Because — Introduces a cause.

Which meant — Shows one thing leading to another.

Combined with — Joins causes into one explanation.

🧠 Watch me think

Listen to the difference: 'They came for jobs' — thin. 'Hunger pushed them out, iron wages pulled them here, AND a cousin's letter showed the way' — three causes, one journey. That's a BUILD explanation.

Key Facts

  • Middlesbrough: ~40 people in 1829 → 90,000+ by 1901 — Gladstone called it an 'infant Hercules'.
  • Ironstone was found in the Eston Hills in 1850.
  • Irish, Welsh, Scottish, Yorkshire and European families all built the iron towns.
  • Migration to Teesside never stopped — NHS and industry still recruit worldwide.
