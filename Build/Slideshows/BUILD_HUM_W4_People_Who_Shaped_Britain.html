I Do: Mattering vs Fame

Key Idea: A person or community matters when evidence shows they CHANGED something — fame is not required, and one story is never enough.

👀 Look: records follow money and power. Fame is not required — and one story is never enough.

📖 Key Ideas

Impact — A change we can evidence — jobs created, lives improved, ideas spread.

Single story — Reducing a person or group to one label — the trap to avoid.

Attribution — Saying WHO exactly, not 'they all'.

🧠 Watch me think

Bolckow and Vaughan get the statues — evidence: they opened the works. But the census shows thousands of Irish and Welsh names doing the labour. Watch me write it: 'the ironmasters opened the works; migrant labour built the town.' Two truths, precisely attributed.

I Do

I Do: Evidencing Why Someone Matters

Key Idea: Claim + evidence + change: the three-part proof that a person or community shaped Britain.

📖 The Proof Pattern

Before — What was wrong or hard BEFORE they acted? (the cause)

Claim — '___ mattered because…' (what they DID)

Evidence — A source detail — census, record, photo, testimony.

Change — What was different afterwards.

🧠 Watch me think — a worked proof

My person: Bridget from Mayo, one nurse in the 1901 census.

🛠️ Before — fever spread fast in crowded streets and families lost children.

🛠️ Claim — she mattered.

🛠️ Evidence — listed as a fever nurse in the epidemic year.

🛠️ Change — fewer families lost children.

✅ Answer: proved. No statue, no fame — and still solid proof. Fame is not the test; evidenced change is.

📘 Reference Zone — keep this beside your work

The proof chain — four links, in order

⚡ BEFORE (what was wrong) → 🏃 CLAIM (what they DID) → 🔎 EVIDENCE (the source line you can point at) → 💥 CHANGE (what was different after).

🔑 Fame is not the test. Evidenced change is. A nurse in a census can pass; a famous name with no evidence cannot.

The single-story trap

❌ “The Irish were all poor labourers.”   ✅ “Many Irish families worked the docks; some opened shops.”

Say WHO exactly, and say SOME or MANY — never all, never they.

🛑 IS IT THE ACTION OR THE CHANGE?

Ask: did the PERSON do it themselves? → that is the CLAIM / ACTION 🏃

Or did it happen TO the town afterwards? → that is the CHANGE 💥

Nursing through the epidemic = action. Fewer children dying the next year = change.

❓ NO FAMOUS NAME? THAT IS FINE.

You do not need a statue to matter. A census line, a chapel record, a works ledger — any of those can carry a proof. Ordinary people changed this town.
