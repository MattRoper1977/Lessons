STARTER · talk task

What makes you YOU?

With your partner: if someone had to guess things about you from three objects, which three would you choose? Interests, places, colours, textures — anything counts, and you choose how much to share.

Sentence stems:
“People could tell I ___ from…”
“A colour that feels like me is…”
“I’d keep ___ private, and that’s fine.”

Stuck? Start with interests — a ball, headphones, a controller, a lead for the dog. Identity art can be about what you LOVE, not secrets.

I DO · teacher explains

A plan is three choices, made on purpose

💡 The idea

What is your piece ABOUT? One honest idea beats five vague ones — your team, your music, your calm place, your dog.

🧵 The materials

Cardboard, foil, wool, clay, paper, paint, screen — chosen because they FIT the idea. Rough idea? Rough textures. Calm idea? Smooth and cool.

🎨 The technique

ONE technique from your tasters — ideally the one you levelled up in Week 2. A plan that uses a practised skill is a plan that works.

🔣 Symbols

A symbol is a thing that STANDS FOR something — a boot for football, a wave for calm. One good symbol can carry a whole piece.

Misconception: “A plan locks you in — you can’t change it later.” A plan is a springboard, not a cage. Artists change plans constantly — the plan’s only job is to get you STARTED with materials in your hands next week.
WE DO · together on the board

Sort the plan parts — IDEA, MATERIALS or TECHNIQUE?

The idea is what it’s ABOUT. Materials are what you’ll USE. The technique is HOW you’ll work them. About, use, how.

HANDS-ON · the main event

Build your plan 📋

Ground rule (60 seconds): your piece shares exactly as much of you as you choose — interests count fully, and nobody has to put private things in art.

Station 1 · THIS IS ME CARDS

From the card table, pick THREE image, word or texture cards that feel like you. Trust the quick pull — the cards you reach for first usually know.

Station 2 · THE PLAN PAGE

Complete the picture-supported plan: what I’ll make / materials / the textures, patterns and colours that mean something to me. Scribing counts; choices are yours.

Station 3 · TELL ONE THING

Tell the TA one thing your piece will say about you. “It shows I love the beach” is a complete answer.

Going for Grow: add the skill line: “my plan uses my levelled-up skill of ___” — connecting Week 2 to Week 4.
