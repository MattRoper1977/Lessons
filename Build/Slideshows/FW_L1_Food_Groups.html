LEARNING OBJECTIVE

LO: To sort foods into the five food groups and say why balance matters.

✓ I can name the five food groups.
✓ I can sort real foods into the right groups.
✓ I can build a balanced plate and say one reason it is balanced.
Success criteria: By the end you will have sorted at least 6 real foods, built one balanced plate, and captured evidence for your ASDAN FoodWise portfolio (Module 1, Section A).
Aspire: use the word carbohydrate or protein out loud when you explain your plate.

I DO · teacher explains

The five food groups

🍞 Carbohydrates

Bread, pasta, rice, potatoes. Energy.

🍗 Protein

Chicken, eggs, beans, fish. Growth & repair.

🥛 Dairy & alternatives

Milk, cheese, yoghurt. Strong bones.

🥦 Fruit & vegetables

Apples, carrots, peas. Vitamins — aim for 5 a day.

🫒 Oils & spreads

Olive oil, butter. Small amounts only.

Misconception: “Healthy eating means never eating chips or chocolate.” Balance means mostly from the big groups, treats sometimes — no food is banned.
WE DO · together on the board

Sort the foods — tap food, then tap its group

Think: does it give energy (carbs)? Help you grow (protein)? Come from milk (dairy)? Grow in the ground or on a tree (fruit & veg)?

HANDS-ON · the main event

Real food stations 🧤

Hygiene first (60 seconds): wash hands, sleeves up, hair back. We handle food properly in this room — every time.

Station 1 · SORT IT

A basket of real foods and packets. Sort them onto the giant Eatwell mat. Check with a partner before you place tricky ones (beans? pizza?).

Station 2 · BUILD IT

Build a balanced plate on the paper plate: something from each big group. Say your plate out loud: “My plate has ___ for energy…”

Station 3 · TASTE IT

Taste test: try a fruit/veg you have not had this week. Score it /5. No one is forced to taste — smelling and describing counts.

Aspire: find the ONE food on the mat that fits two groups and explain why (cheese & tomato pizza is the classic).
