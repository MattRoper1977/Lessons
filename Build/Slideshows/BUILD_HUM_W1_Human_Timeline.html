I Do

I Do: What Is Chronological Order?

Key Idea: Chronological order means arranging events by WHEN they happened — earliest first. Historians never guess; they use clues.

📖 Key Definitions

Chronology — The order in which events happened in time.

Timeline — A line that shows events in chronological order.

Evidence — A clue that helps us decide when something happened.

🧠 Watch me think

Watch me place 'the blast furnace closes' on our class timeline. I don't know the date by heart — so I look for a clue: my nan remembers it on the news, so it's inside living memory. That narrows it right down.

I Do

I Do: Reading Date Clues

Key Idea: Every object carries clues to its age: materials, style, technology, and what it does NOT show.

📖 Clue Types

Technology — Cars, phones, gas lamps — tech dates a scene fast.

Style — Clothes and buildings change with fashion.

Absence — What's missing is a clue too — no cars can mean pre-1900s.

🧠 Watch me think

This photo of Middlesbrough has trams and gas lamps but no cars. Trams plus gas lamps plus no cars puts me around 1900 — I can defend that with three clues.
