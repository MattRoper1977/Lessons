I Do

I Do: What Is Chronological Order?

Key Idea: Chronological order means putting events in the order they happened, earliest first. On our timeline the years only get BIGGER as you move right — bigger year, closer to today.

👀 Look: chronological order is the order things HAPPENED, not the order we find them.

📖 Key Definitions

Chronology — The order in which events happened in time.

Timeline — A line that shows events in chronological order.

Evidence — A clue that helps us decide when something happened.

Century — A hundred years. The 1800s are the 19th century: take the first two digits and add one.

🧠 Watch me think — years into centuries

My job: say which century each Teesside milestone sits in.

🛠️ Step 1 — look at the first two digits. Step 2 — add one. That is the century.

Guisborough Priory, 1119 → 11 + 1 = 12th century

Captain Cook born, 1728 → 17 + 1 = 18th century

Stockton & Darlington Railway, 1825 → 18 + 1 = 19th century

✅ Answer: 12th, 18th, 19th. Notice 1825 looks like “18” but it is the 19th century — that is the trap.

I Do: Reading Date Clues

Key Idea: Every object carries clues to its age: materials, style, technology, and what it does NOT show.

📖 Clue Types

Technology — Cars, phones, gas lamps — tech dates a scene fast.

Style — Clothes and buildings change with fashion.

Absence — What's missing is a clue too — no cars can mean pre-1900s.

📘 Reference Zone — keep this beside your work

Our timeline runs 1000 CE → today. Earliest on the LEFT. The bigger the year, the closer to today.

🔢 The century rule: take the first two digits of the year and add one. So 1825 → 18 + 1 → the 19th century. The 1800s ARE the 19th century.

🛑 STUCK ON CENTURIES?

1825 starts with “18”, so it feels like the 18th century. It is not.

Count it out: the years 1–100 were the 1st century. 101–200 the 2nd. So the 1800s are the 19th hundred years. First two digits, add one.

❓ DO NOT KNOW THE DATE? YOU ARE NOT STUCK.

Historians rarely know a date by heart — they read a clue. Ask: is it in living memory? Is there a photo, and is it colour? Cars or horses? Steam or diesel?

A defensible range beats a wrong exact year: “about 1900” is a real answer.
