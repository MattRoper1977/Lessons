STARTER · talk task

What makes sharing feel safe?

With your partner: watching someone share their work can be brilliant or awkward — and it’s mostly the AUDIENCE that decides which. What does a good audience actually do?

Sentence stems:
“A good audience…”
“Sharing feels safe when…”
“The worst thing an audience can do is…”

Stuck? Eyes on the work, wait to the end, one kind comment. Three moves — that’s the whole job, and today everyone does both jobs.

I DO · teacher explains

Three stems carry the whole presentation

1️⃣ This is…

Name the piece and what it’s about — one sentence. “This is my identity piece about the beach.” Done.

2️⃣ I did well…

Name your star — the texture, the symbol, the colour pair. You found it in Week 5; today you say it to someone.

3️⃣ Next I’ll…

Your next step, out loud. It goes in the log too — next term starts exactly there.

🎬 Your route

Partner, TA, or camera — ALL count fully for Arts Award. Sharing means someone sees it. It never means performing to a crowd.

Misconception: “Sharing means standing up in front of the whole class.” The Arts Award asks that someone SEES your work and hears your thinking — a partner, the TA, or a video all count completely. Your route, your choice, same credit.
WE DO · together on the board

Sort the moves — the SHARER’s job or the AUDIENCE’s job?

Holding, pointing and using stems = sharer. Eyes, waiting, kind comment, one question = audience. Whose move is it?

HANDS-ON · the main event

Share it 🎤

Ground rule (60 seconds): when someone shares, the room protects them: eyes on the work, wait to the end, one kind comment — every sharer gets the same.

Station 1 · PICK & PRACTISE

Lay out your term’s pieces and pick your favourite. Then practise once, all together, on the demo piece — stems on the board, no pressure.

Station 2 · PRESENT

Share your portfolio by your route: to a partner, to the TA, or on video. Three stems, your work, done. Pointing at parts while you talk is perfect.

Station 3 · NEXT STEP

Your “next I’ll…” goes into the log — and highlight the piece you’ll build on next term.

Going for Grow: invite one question from your audience and answer it — even “I don’t know yet” is an artist’s answer.
