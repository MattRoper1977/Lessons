I Do

I Do: Judging with Criteria

Key Idea: 'Significant' isn't a feeling — it's a judgement made with named criteria: how many, how deep, how long, how remembered.

👀 Look: turn the dials and the judgement changes. That is the argument, not a flaw in it.

📖 The Four Tests

Numbers — How many people were affected?

Depth — How deeply were lives changed?

Duration — How long did the effects last?

Memory — Is it still remembered and used?

🧠 Watch me think — a worked score

The event: ironstone found in the Eston Hills, 1850. Score each test 1–3.

🛠️ Numbers — tens of thousands migrated here → 3

🛠️ Depth — whole lives rebuilt around the works → 3

🛠️ Duration — 165 years of industry followed → 3

🛠️ Memory — it is why this town exists at all → 3

✅ Answer: 12 out of 12. Four tests passed, so 'big deal' is defensible — and I can name WHY, not just say it felt important.

I Do

I Do: 'It Depends' Done Properly

Key Idea: Strong judgements admit perspective and timescale: significant for whom, and over how long?

📖 Qualifying Language

For whom — Significance differs by group — workers, owners, newcomers.

Over what time — A week's crisis vs a century's change.

Although — The word that lets two truths stand together.

🧠 Watch me think

The 2015 closure: by NUMBERS nationally, modest; by DEPTH here, enormous. Watch me hold both: 'Nationally it was one closure among many, ALTHOUGH for Teesside it ended 165 years of iron-making.' Both true. Criteria named.

Saying “it depends” properly

For whom? workers, owners, newcomers — significance differs by group.
Over what time? a week’s crisis or a century’s change.
Although — the word that lets two truths stand together.

Model: “Nationally it was one closure among many, although here the depth was enormous.”

🛑 STUCK ON “WAS IT A BIG DEAL?”

Do not ask “is it interesting?” Ask these two:

1. If this had never happened, would life here be different today?

2. Did it change things for a few weeks, or for decades?

Both point at a test: question 1 is DEPTH and MEMORY, question 2 is DURATION.

❓ SOMEONE RANKED IT DIFFERENTLY. WHO IS RIGHT?

Possibly both. If you used different tests, you will get different answers — and that is how historians actually argue.

Say which test you used. A named test beats a strong opinion every time.
