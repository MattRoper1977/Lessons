Hygiene Routine Cards · FoodWise practicals (print 2 sets, laminate)

1 · HAIR BACK

Tie back or clip hair FIRST.

Why first? Touching hair puts germs on hands — so hands get washed after.

2 · APRON ON

Apron protects the food from your clothes.

Yes — that way round.

3 · WASH HANDS

20 seconds · soap · backs & thumbs.

Last, so hands stay clean for cooking.

4 · WIPE SURFACE

Clean mat, clean board, clean start.

Now we cook.
