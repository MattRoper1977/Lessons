Greet and serve a customer: my service script

Autumn 2 · Week 4Title

Learning goal: I can follow the service routine in order to greet a customer, confirm a drink order or room booking, and handle or refer a complaint.

In this lesson

  • Follow the service routine: greet, listen, write, repeat back, pass on, thank.
  • Write your own four-part service script for greet, drink order, booking and complaint.
  • Rehearse greeting and a drink order calmly at a quiet station with an adult.

Greet and serve a customer: my service script: I do

I do

Follow the service routine in order so every customer gets a calm, polite greet, order, booking or complaint reply.

Step 1 of 5: Greet

Greet every customer in a calm voice: "Hello, welcome. How can I help you?"

Say it: To greet a customer calmly, I say, "Hello, ___. How can I ___ you?"

greet = welcome a customer politely

Step 2 of 5: Drink order

Write the drink order down, then confirm it aloud: "One tea and one water. Is that right?"

Say it: I write the ___ down, then confirm it: "One tea and one ___. Is that ___?"

order = what a customer asks to buyconfirm = repeat back to check it is right

Step 3 of 5: Room booking

For a room booking, check and repeat the booking code, the date, and the number of nights.

Say it: For a room ___, I check the booking ___, the ___, and the number of ___.

booking = a saved room for a guest

Step 4 of 5: Complaint

For a complaint, listen first, say sorry, offer an allowed fix, or refer: "I will ask the manager."

Say it: For a ___, I listen, say ___, offer a fix, or ___ to the manager.

complaint = when a customer says something is wrongrefer = pass a problem to the manager

Step 5 of 5: Put it together

  • Service routine: greet, listen, write it down, repeat it back, pass it on, thank.
  • Orders and bookings: write details down and confirm them aloud so mistakes are caught early.
  • Complaints: listen calmly, say sorry, offer an allowed fix or refer to the manager.

Watch out: never argue with a customer. You may speak, point, sign or use your usual aid; accent, speed and eye contact are never judged.

Rehearse calmly at a quiet station with an adult customer, never in front of the class. Pupils may speak, point, sign, draw, write or use an aid, and may pass. Accent, speed and eye contact are never judged.

Mark with your own school's feedback codes. Give one clear next step; pupils edit in the same colour on the same page, Adult replies are recorded, and Help used: Independent / Supported is noted.

Quick catch-up summary

  • Follow the service routine in order: greet, listen, write, repeat back, pass on, thank.
  • Write drink orders and booking details down, then confirm them back to the customer.
  • For a complaint, listen, say sorry, offer an allowed fix or refer to the manager; never argue.
