Vocational · BUILD · Autumn 2 · Week 2 · VOC_BUILD_A2_W02

I do · 5 min

Wash, rinse, drain and clean the space

Worked model

  1. Wait for the adult safety check.
  2. Wash the item. Rinse it in clean water.
  3. Put it on the rack to drain.

Use a dish cloth for the kit. Use a separate surface cloth. Wash and dry your hands.

  • Check the work area for wet patches.
  • Leave wet kit to air-dry on the rack.
  • If water spills on the floor, stop and tell an adult.
