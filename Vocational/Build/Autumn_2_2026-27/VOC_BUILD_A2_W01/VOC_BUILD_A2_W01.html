Read the three checks. Invite a response and give thinking time. The learner may pass. Use paper cards only. Keep individual dietary, allergy, texture and swallowing needs private. Before later food use, staff check each need through the agreed private procedure. Offer suitable cards. Use this work as formative evidence.

Arrival with regulation check · 4 min

Offer a private regulation check. Use Green · Ready, Amber · Help or Red · Pause. Offer a quiet start, the usual aid or a pass. Quiet pause → usual aid → one short task cue → rejoin at the current step. Use the usual access aids and quiet response route.

Starter · 2 min

Read the scene. Let pupils think and choose a response mode. Check the reason. A plate is kit. Fruit is food.

Teaching script 2

I do · 3 min

Think aloud one step at a time. Use paper cards. Choose a pear and water. Name the kit. A plate holds the pear. A cup holds the water. Pause for the adult check before food is used in a later lesson.

We do · 4 min

Invite a private choice with a reason. Pause before revealing the answer. Add a plate and a cup. Ask an adult to check the food and drink before any later food use. Invite a revision and reset the model for another learner.

I do · 2 min

Model the next steps. 1. Check the plan with an adult. 2. Wash hands and get clean kit. 3. Get the snack ready with an adult in the later activity. 4. Clear up.

Teaching script 3

We do · 3 min

Rehearse with a shared example. Point to the food, drink and kit. Read the four steps in order. Show the adult check first. If a card is missing, choose a suitable card and check the plan again. Invite a reason for a choice before offering a check.
