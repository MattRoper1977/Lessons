I do · 5 minBUILD1/2

Match jobs to useful tasks

Worked model

  1. Find what the worker does.
  2. Choose a job that fits the task.
  3. Show the clue. Choose kit that could help.

The adult’s story is about making soup. Cook could fit. A bowl and spoon could help. The task is the clue.

  • Use what the worker does, not how they look.
  • Some jobs share tasks or kit.
  • A job match does not prove a practical work skill.
