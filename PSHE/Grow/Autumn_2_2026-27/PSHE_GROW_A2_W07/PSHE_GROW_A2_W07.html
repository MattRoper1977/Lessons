Teaching script 1

Arrival · 4 min

Invite a private regulation check. Green · Ready, Amber · Help, Red · Pause. Offer a quiet start, usual aid or pass. Use the cue and accept the pupil’s usual communication mode.

Starter · 2 min

Starter question 1 revisits last week. Take one minute for recall and reveal. What makes a help request clear? Cue: Need · request · reply. Expected: Say what help is needed. Use an agreed route and check the reply. Then ask: Which shows a skill: a blank sheet or actual work? Expected: Actual pupil work, with the help and any gap recorded. Use the made-up task. Begin in your book.

Teaching script 2

I do · 5 min

A safe choice and help route. A made-up message says click now. The person spots pressure and does not click. They ask the class adult for help. The office is a backup. Ask for a quiet pause and a return step. Use made-up cases only. Keep private details out. Record each pupil-owned reason.

We do · 5 min

Shared worked model. Model R is an adult sample. A blank form shows no pupil reply. Keep the pupil’s own work. Then use the decision checkpoint. Is a blank sheet proof of a skill? Ask for a choice and a reason before revealing. No. Keep the pupil’s own choices, reasons and actual help.

Teaching script 3

You do · 17 min

Read or scribe core cases A to C without giving the answer and record each pupil choice, clue and safe action. Keep first and backup adult roles with a practical access route for each. Keep one wellbeing step and a reason, including how to return after a pause. Then offer D, E or F for transfer; Aspire explains why its action fits and compares it with a core case. Review authentic earlier work with a new check rather than automatic reuse. Retain the actual work and support, with missing items marked. Feedback and the pupil’s later reply remain separate. Complete the core cases A, B and C. Show first and backup help routes and ways to ask. Explain a wellbeing step. Review your own work. Supported route. Hear A, B and C. Show your choices. Give a clue with agreed help. Independent route. Explain a clue and safe step for each case. Show help routes and a wellbeing reason. Aspire route. Explain why a new case needs that step. Compare D, E or F with a core case. Choice route. Agree your route and response mode. Keep your own safe steps and reasons.

Teaching script 4

Review · 4 min

Teaching script 1

Arrival · 4 min

Invite a private regulation check. Green · Ready, Amber · Help, Red · Pause. Offer a quiet start, usual aid or pass. Use the cue and accept the pupil’s usual communication mode.

Starter · 2 min

Starter question 1 revisits last week. Take one minute for recall and reveal. What makes a help request clear? Cue: Need · request · reply. Expected: Say what help is needed. Use an agreed route and check the reply. Then ask: Which shows a skill: a blank sheet or actual work? Expected: Actual pupil work, with the help and any gap recorded. Use the made-up task. Begin in your book.

Made by Matt

Teaching script 2

I do · 5 min

A safe choice and help route. A made-up message says click now. The person spots pressure and does not click. They ask the class adult for help. The office is a backup. Ask for a quiet pause and a return step. Use made-up cases only. Keep private details out. Record each pupil-owned reason.

We do · 5 min

Shared worked model. Model R is an adult sample. A blank form shows no pupil reply. Keep the pupil’s own work. Then use the decision checkpoint. Is a blank sheet proof of a skill? Ask for a choice and a reason before revealing. No. Keep the pupil’s own choices, reasons and actual help.

Made by Matt

Teaching script 3

You do · 17 min

Read or scribe core cases A to C without giving the answer and record each pupil choice, clue and safe action. Keep first and backup adult roles with a practical access route for each. Keep one wellbeing step and a reason, including how to return after a pause. Then offer D, E or F for transfer; Aspire explains why its action fits and compares it with a core case. Review authentic earlier work with a new check rather than automatic reuse. Retain the actual work and support, with missing items marked. Feedback and the pupil’s later reply remain separate. Complete the core cases A, B and C. Show first and backup help routes and ways to ask. Explain a wellbeing step. Review your own work. Supported route. Hear A, B and C. Show your choices. Give a clue with agreed help. Independent route. Explain a clue and safe step for each case. Show help routes and a wellbeing reason. Aspire route. Explain why a new case needs that step. Compare D, E or F with a core case. Choice route. Agree your route and response mode. Keep your own safe steps and reasons.

Teaching script 4

Review · 4 min

Check one part. Respond to one helpful next step. Optional. Respond to feedback with an adult. You may pass. Mark R only after the pupil has responded to feedback with an adult. Keep the first response and any change separate. Edit in the same colour, on the same page or usual mode.

Exit · 3 min

Ask the two route checks. What helped you most today, or what would you change next time? Point, speak, sign, draw, write or choose. You may pass. Accept the pupil’s own example or a pass. Record their own words and actual support. At check-out, record E for … against the pupil's target.
