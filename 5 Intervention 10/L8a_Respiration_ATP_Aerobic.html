Starter

Spot the myth 🕵️

Misconception

“Respiration means breathing in and out.”

❌ Breathing moves air. Respiration is a chemical reaction inside every cell that releases energy from glucose.

Quick check: Which part of the cell is called the “powerhouse”?

I Do

What is ATP? 🔋

1. Glucose is the fuel — but cells can’t use it directly for everything.
2. Respiration releases energy from glucose inside the mitochondria.
3. That energy is stored in ATP — think of it as a rechargeable battery 🔋 or the cell’s “energy currency” 💷.
4. When a cell needs energy, it “spends” ATP. Then respiration recharges it.
Exam tip 💡

Energy is released from glucose — never “made” or “created”. Examiners love that word.

I Do

Aerobic respiration 💨

Aerobic = WITH oxygen. This releases the most energy and runs most of the time.

Word equation:
glucose + oxygen → carbon dioxide + water (+ energy / ATP)
Symbol equation:
C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ → 6CO₂ + 6H₂O
In: glucose (fuel) + oxygen.   Out: carbon dioxide + water + energy.
Watch out

“Plants don’t respire.” ❌ All living cells respire, all the time — day and night.
