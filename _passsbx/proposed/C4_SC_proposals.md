## Proposed per-week SC (from each deck's own Independent Work tasks)

| Deck | Proposed "Success looks like" (three criteria) |
|---|---|
| **A2_W1 · Surface Hunt** | *(keep current — already matches the frottage/surface-bank task)* I can take rubbings from different surfaces and label each one · I can repeat a surface and describe what changed · I can choose the surface I will use and explain why |
| **A2_W2 · Arts Inspiration** | I can record an artist and an arts organisation, with one method each · I can place my choices on the decision matrix (what they do / my decision / where it shows) · I can say what I am deliberately not taking, and why |
| **A2_W3 · Stencil Lab** | I can cut a stencil and pull a heavy result and a light result · I can label my results and say which edge works best and why · I can overlay a stencil on an earlier rubbing and judge whether it still reads |
| **A2_W4 · Audience Week** | I can review a work in four parts: noticed, worked, did not, who I would send it to · I can give reasons for what worked and what did not, and share my view with someone · I can say what I would change about how the work was shown, and why |
| **A2_W5 · Layer and Combine** | I can build a piece in layers and record the order · I can name the artist decision I borrowed and show where it appears · I can explain something I covered on purpose and judge whether it was right |
| **A2_W6 · Resolve and Edition** | I can decide a piece is finished and say why I stopped · I can pull an edition of five and place each print (keep one, place four) · I can pick my strongest print and argue whether a variation is a fault or a feature |
| **A2_W7 · Bank It and Plan the Teach** | I can choose a teachable skill and name who I am teaching · I can write three steps a stranger could follow, and rewrite the one that failed when read aloud · I can plan for what could go wrong and choose the piece I will teach from |
