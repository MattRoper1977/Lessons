Stage 6 · Independent studio practice (You do) · SUMMIT Pick your route: Supported · Independent · Aspire

Choose your studio route and create

Supported route: criteria check, swatch test, and final contour/highlight resolution

  1. Check your identity artwork against the four studio criteria on your card and tick your top refinement target.
  2. Test your chosen tool (thick brush contour, shadow glaze, or bright highlight) on the 6 cm swatch box.
  3. Apply the refinement carefully to resolve your main identity artwork.
  4. Complete the 'Before -> Swatch -> After' sentences on your critique sheet.
Success check: Criteria checked, swatch tested, final identity piece resolved, and critique sheet completed.

Independent route: peer criteria audit, live swatch verification, and final resolution

  1. Conduct a structured peer critique using the Four-Criteria matrix, recording specific strengths and one high-leverage refinement target.
  2. Execute a 6 cm live swatch testing your material adjustment (tonal glazing, counterchange sharpening, or opaque highlight lift).
  3. Resolve your final identity piece by applying the verified refinement and mounting it cleanly.
  4. Write a technical evaluation on your critique sheet linking your Week 1 target to today's final resolution.
Success check: Peer audit recorded, live swatch verified, final artwork resolved/mounted, and technical evaluation written.

Aspire route: dual-swatch comparative test, multi-layer resolution, and critical synthesis

  1. Audit your work across all four criteria and formulate two alternative material solutions for your focal transition.
  2. Execute two comparative 6 cm swatches, select the superior solution, and resolve the final artwork to exhibition standard.
  3. Write a rigorous critical synthesis tracing how your Week 1 audit, Week 2 deliberate practice, and Week 3 practitioner analysis converged in the final work.
Success check: Dual comparative swatches, exhibition-grade resolution, and critical synthesis across Weeks 1-4.

Key words and definitions (SUMMIT)

  • criteria-led critique: Evaluating an artwork against specific technical benchmarks rather than personal opinion.
  • high-leverage: A focused change to a key area (like the focal contour or core shadow) that lifts the whole artwork.
  • value contrast: The visual punch created by placing your darkest dark next to your brightest light.
  • resolution: Bringing all parts of an artwork to a purposeful, cohesive finish.
  • synthesis: Combining insights from skill trials, practitioner analysis, and critique into the final piece.
