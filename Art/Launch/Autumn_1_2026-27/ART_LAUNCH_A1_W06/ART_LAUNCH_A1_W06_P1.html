Key words and definitions (SUMMIT)

  • field sketch: A rapid, observant drawing made directly in front of an artwork to capture structure, light, and mark-making.
  • visual moment: A specific focal aspect of an exhibition (how a piece is lit, how two works contrast, or how a joint is made).
  • shorthand mark: Fast, expressive hatching or stippling that records surface texture in seconds.
  • curatorial framing: How plinths, borders, lighting angles, and wall space direct the viewer's response.
  • primary evidence: Your own first-hand sketches and observational notes recorded directly from the artworks.
