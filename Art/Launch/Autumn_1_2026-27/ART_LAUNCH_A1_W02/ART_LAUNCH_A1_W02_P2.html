Starter: spot the visual clues

Visual cues to spot

Examine the four interconnected roles in a Commission studio Ecosystem on screen.

  • 1. Concept artist / Designer: Researches site context, draws elevations, and builds the scale maquette.
  • 2. Structural engineer: Analyses triangulation, joint strength, wind load, and safe mounting.
  • 3. Specialist fabricator: Cuts, laminates, welds, or casts the components and applies surface patina.
  • 4. Exhibition curator / Producer: Manages budget, sightlines, lighting design, and public interpretation.

Stage 6 · Independent studio practice (You do) · SUMMIT Pick your route: Supported · Independent · Aspire

Choose your studio route and create

Supported route: four-role workflow map and mounted corner-joint prototype

  1. Complete the four-role studio map linking Designer, Engineer, Fabricator, and Curator.
  2. Construct a strong corner joint using two laminated card beams, a triangle gusset plate, and a foil rivet strip.
  3. Mount your corner prototype onto the centre of your sheet and label the materials used.
  4. Tick which of the four studio roles matches your strongest skill.
Success check: Workflow map completed, corner-joint prototype mounted, and personal role strength identified.

Independent route: interdisciplinary case study, structural prototype, and fabrication spec

  1. Analyse how a commission moves through Concept design, Structural engineering, Fabrication, and Curation on your A3 sheet.
  2. Engineer and mount an 8 cm structural joint prototype demonstrating 3-level lamination, gusset reinforcement, and metallic patina.
  3. Write a formal Fabrication specification beside the prototype detailing layer order, structural rigidity, and tonal finish.
  4. Reflect on how engineering and curation decisions will shape your Week 3-4 studio build.
Success check: Role analysis, mounted 3-level joint prototype, formal fabrication spec, and reflective link.

Aspire route: comparative joinery prototypes (lap-gusset vs slotted) and pathway audit

  1. Construct two contrasting structural joint prototypes (1: laminated lap joint with foil gusset; 2: interlocking slotted relief joint).
  2. Test both under gentle torsional stress and document a comparative engineering & aesthetic specification.
  3. Map a personal studio pathway linking your technical strengths to two creative industry roles.
Success check: Two comparative joinery prototypes tested, specified, and linked to creative industry pathways.

Key words and definitions (SUMMIT)

  • interdisciplinary: Different specialists (artists, engineers, fabricators, curators) working together on one project.
  • prototype: A working test section built at real or scaled size to prove a joint or surface finish works.
  • specification: A precise technical instruction naming materials, dimensions, and surface treatments.
  • structural load: How weight and force travel through beams, arches, and joints so a piece stands safely.
  • curatorial narrative: The story and layout a curator designs to help the public engage with an exhibition.
