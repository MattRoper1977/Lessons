Stage 6 · Independent studio practice (You do) · SUMMIT Pick your route: Supported · Independent · Aspire

Choose your studio route and create

Supported route: guided workshop delivery, paired wall hang, and ticked evaluation

  1. Use your upgraded 3-tile board to guide a partner or small group through making their sample tile.
  2. Hang your main relief panel on the display row with your museum label and place your group's tiles underneath.
  3. Collect one praise slip from your participant and glue it onto your evaluation sheet.
  4. Complete the final reflection stems on your Studio suite sheet.
Success check: Workshop delivered, relief panel and participant tiles displayed, and evaluation sheet completed.

Independent route: live group facilitation, lit installation curation, and summative evaluation

  1. Facilitate your 10-minute studio workshop using your adapted Leadership board, safety briefing, and pre-prepped blanks.
  2. Collect written participant feedback slips evaluating the clarity of your 3-stage exemplars and coaching.
  3. Collaborate with the curatorial team to align the structural relief panels along the 145 cm sightline, position raking lamps, and mount museum labels.
  4. Write a comprehensive Summative evaluation analysing the technical resolution of your panel and the effectiveness of your workshop leadership.
Success check: Live workshop facilitated, participant feedback collected, panel curated under raking light, and summative evaluation written.

Aspire route: lead exhibition director, interactive masterclass, and critical retrospective

  1. Deliver a differentiated 10-minute masterclass workshop incorporating your stretch exemplar and live troubleshooting cues.
  2. Direct the spatial grouping and raking-light calibration for a full section of the collective architectural installation, authoring the zone's Curatorial statement.
  3. Write an analytical retrospective evaluating how your Weeks 1-7 journey integrated structural engineering, surface patina, curatorial lighting, and studio pedagogy.
Success check: Masterclass delivered, installation zone directed with wall statement, and analytical retrospective written.

Key words and definitions (SUMMIT)

  • facilitation: Guiding participants through a creative process with clear demonstrations, safety checks, and encouraging technical cues.
  • collective installation: Grouping individual relief panels and workshop tiles into a unified architectural display.
  • raking illumination: Setting gallery spotlights at a shallow grazing angle across the hung exhibition wall.
  • curatorial statement: The overarching wall text that explains the shared brief, material constraints, and structural theme.
  • summative evaluation: A final reflective assessment of both your practical installation and your workshop leadership.
