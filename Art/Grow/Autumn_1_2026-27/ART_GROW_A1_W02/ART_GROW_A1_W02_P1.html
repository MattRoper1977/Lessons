Stage 6 · Independent studio practice (You do) · TRACK Pick your route: Supported · Independent · Aspire

Choose your studio route and create

Supported route: three-box tonal ladder and guided reflection

  1. Pick one studio skill (pencil shading or pen hatching) and use the printed form outline in Boxes 1, 2, and 3.
  2. Complete box 1, then tick one adjustment target ('press lighter in the middle' or 'keep lines closer').
  3. Complete box 2 and Box 3 applying your ticked adjustment.
  4. Fill in the log stem: 'From trial 1 to Trial 3, my ___ became more ___.'
Success check: Three progressive trials completed with ticked adjustment and reflection stem.

Independent route: three timed skill trials on an identity form

  1. Draw your chosen identity symbol in all three progression boxes.
  2. Complete trial 1, write a one-line diagnostic target beneath it, and apply that fix in Trial 2.
  3. Refine full tonal range (highlight, mid-tone, core shadow) in Trial 3.
  4. Write a three-sentence studio log entry explaining how you adjusted pressure, angle, or dilution.
Success check: Three progressive trials with intermediate targets and a three-sentence technical log entry.

Aspire route: three-trial progression plus surface-transfer test

  1. Complete all three progressive trials showing five-value tonal control on cartridge paper.
  2. Execute a fourth transfer trial applying the mastered skill onto toned or textured ground.
  3. Write a critical evaluation comparing how paper tooth affected your control.
Success check: Three progression trials, fourth toned-ground trial, and surface evaluation.

Key words and definitions (TRACK)

  • trial: A deliberate practice attempt focused on improving one specific technique.
  • pressure: How firmly or lightly you press the tool onto the paper surface.
  • cross-hatching: Building dark tone by layering intersecting sets of parallel lines.
  • control: Making the tool produce the exact tone and edge you intend.
  • progression: Visible step-by-step improvement across consecutive attempts.
