Visual cues to spot

Stage 6 · Independent studio practice (You do) · TRACK Pick your route: Supported · Independent · Aspire

Choose your studio route and create

Supported route: safety ruler strips and gusset-joined frame

  1. Use the safety ruler and heavy scissors/scorer to cut four straight girder strips along printed guide lines.
  2. Laminate two strips together to build a thick horizontal main deck on your base board.
  3. Add two diagonal braces and secure the corners with pre-cut triangle gusset plates.
  4. Brush a thin dark slate wash over the finished frame.
Success check: Safely cut laminated girder frame with gusset corners and unified dark wash.

Independent route: laminated truss construction and primed relief

  1. Measure and cut parallel girder strips using a cutting mat and safety ruler.
  2. Build a three-level relief truss using laminated primary beams, recessed cross-bracing, and corner gussets.
  3. Prime the assembled relief in dark iron grey and once touch-dry, skim the top edges with graphite or chrome.
  4. Complete the studio safety and technique checklist in your log.
Success check: Three-level laminated truss relief primed, highlighted, and logged.

Aspire route: cantilevered span with slotted joins and rivet array

  1. Measure and cut precision girder strips in three graduated widths.
  2. Engineer an overlapping cantilever or arch span using laminated beams, gusset plates, and raised rivet strips.
  3. Apply a two-stage weathered finish (dark iron base plus dry-brushed oxide and chrome).
  4. Annotate your log explaining how lamination depth changed shadow contrast.
Success check: Graduated three-level relief span with weathered finish and technical annotation.

Key words and definitions (TRACK)

  • score: To press or lightly indent a line along card so it folds or snaps cleanly.
  • laminate: To glue two or more layers of card together for extra thickness and strength.
  • gusset: A small angled corner plate that reinforces where two beams meet.
  • safety ruler: A ruler with a raised finger guard used to guide straight cuts safely.
  • primer: A base coat of paint or wash that unifies mixed card surfaces.

Studio skills and safe workshop practice — studio worksheet

Big question: How do we score, cut, and laminate heavy card safely to build rigid architectural relief?

Key words

  • score: To press or lightly indent a line along card so it folds or snaps cleanly.
  • laminate: To glue two or more layers of card together for extra thickness and strength.
  • gusset: A small angled corner plate that reinforces where two beams meet.
  • safety ruler: A ruler with a raised finger guard used to guide straight cuts safely.
  • primer: A base coat of paint or wash that unifies mixed card surfaces.

Supported, Independent, and Aspire studio tasks

Supported: Use the safety ruler and heavy scissors/scorer to cut four straight girder strips along printed guide lines. Laminate two strips together to build a thick horizontal main deck on your base board. Add two diagonal braces and secure the corners with pre-cut triangle gusset plates. Brush a thin dark slate wash over the finished frame.

Independent: Measure and cut parallel girder strips using a cutting mat and safety ruler. Build a three-level relief truss using laminated primary beams, recessed cross-bracing, and corner gussets.
