Independent surface and structure challenge

Big question: How can we combine collagraph relief, foil embossing, and monochromatic wash in an architectural panel?

Learning goals for today

  • Construct a textured architectural study combining card relief, embossed metal foil, and surface grain.
  • Control a three-value monochromatic ink or acrylic wash to unify and deepen the recessed planes.
  • Evaluate which surface combination creates the strongest illusion of weathered industrial structure.

Studio kit: A4 heavy greyboard panels, corrugated card, craft foil, foam pads, blunt styluses, black/sepia acrylic wash, white/chrome dry-brush pigment

Stage 6 · Independent studio practice (You do) · TRACK Pick your route: Supported · Independent · Aspire

Choose your studio route and create

Supported route: three-texture relief tile with wash and wipe

  1. Glue one corrugated background strip and two smooth girder bars onto your greyboard base.
  2. Emboss a row of rivets on a foil strip, glue it across the centre joint, and press the edges firm.
  3. Brush dark wash over the foil and seams, then dab the top of the foil clean with a paper towel.
  4. Skim chrome dry-brush across the ridged card.
Success check: Three-texture architectural tile completed with dark groove wash and bright ridges.

Independent route: architectural collagraph panel with three-value patina

  1. Construct a balanced architectural relief combining corrugated card, laminated girders, gusset plates, and embossed foil.
  2. Apply a dark monochromatic wash into recessed planes and wipe embossed high points to create aged patina.
  3. Use dry-brush highlights along upper beam flanges to establish a clear light source from above.
  4. Record a technical note in your log evaluating which material held the best tonal contrast.
Success check: Resolved architectural collagraph with three-value monochromatic patina and log evaluation.

Aspire route: multi-depth structural study with directional light and rust patina

  1. Engineer a three-depth structural panel integrating mesh, corrugated fluting, laminated trusses, and detailed foil embossing.
  2. Build a layered patina using dark slate wash in recesses, warm iron-oxide mid-tone, and directional chrome highlights on upper-left edges.
  3. Write a concise studio critique analysing how surface texture and tonal contrast work together.
Success check: Multi-depth relief panel with directional oxide/chrome patina and written critique.

Key words and definitions (TRACK)

  • collagraph: A textured relief board built by gluing varied flat and bumpy materials onto a base.
  • monochromatic: Using dark, mid, and light tones of a single colour plus black and white.
  • recessed: Set further back into the surface so it catches deeper shadow.
  • patina: The weathered surface look that metal or stone develops over time.
  • flange: The projecting rim or edge of a structural steel beam.
