Visual cues to spot

Examine the four-stage professional workflow from commission brief to installed artwork.

  • Stage 1 (Site & Brief): Observing the built environment, history, and audience sightlines.
  • Stage 2 (Maquette & Swatches): Building small card/foil relief models and testing surface patina.
  • Stage 3 (Fabrication): Scaling up with engineers, welders, or master printmakers.
  • Stage 4 (Curation & Install): Lighting, mounting, and writing public interpretation panels.

Key words and definitions (TRACK)

  • practitioner: A skilled creative maker who works with specialist studio methods.
  • maquette: A small-scale 3D test model built to check form and balance before making a big sculpture.
  • fabrication: Building and joining the full-size artwork in a workshop or foundry.
  • commission: A formal brief asking a maker to design an artwork for a specific place.
  • scale model: A miniature version built in exact proportion to the real space.
