Stage 6 · Independent studio practice (You do) · TRACK Pick your route: Supported · Independent · Aspire

Choose your studio route and create

Supported route: numbered portfolio sequence, framed piece, and guided review

  1. Lay your pages in order from Week 1 to Week 6 using the numbered desk map.
  2. Glue your final identity piece onto a backing frame and attach your title slip.
  3. Stick three 'Connection arrows' onto your pages showing your best test, your plan, and your final piece.
  4. Complete the front portfolio review sheet using the sentence stems.
Success check: Pages sequenced 1-5, final piece mounted, and front review sheet completed.

Independent route: curated portfolio layout, technical labels, and synthesis review

  1. Audit and sequence all studio work from Weeks 1-6 into a clear four-stage progression (Media trials, Composition plan, Maker response, Resolved piece).
  2. Mount your final identity piece and check that every study sheet has complete technical annotations.
  3. Complete the Portfolio synthesis Review evaluating how your media control and figure-ground composition developed.
  4. Present your sequenced portfolio to a table partner in a two-minute walkthrough.
Success check: Sequenced and annotated portfolio, mounted final piece, written synthesis review, and peer walkthrough.

Aspire route: exhibition-grade portfolio book with curatorial foreword

  1. Curate and double-mount your key developmental studies alongside your resolved identity piece.
  2. Write a front Curatorial foreword explaining the visual theme, palette rules, and technical progression across the seven weeks.
  3. Include comparative cross-references between your Week 2 skill trials, Week 6 maker response, and final outcome.
Success check: Double-mounted portfolio with curatorial foreword and cross-referenced technical annotations.

Key words and definitions (TRACK)

  • portfolio: A curated collection of studio tests, plans, studies, and finished artwork showing your progression.
  • sequence: The deliberate order of pages that shows how early tests led to the final piece.
  • curation: Selecting and framing your strongest evidence so each page reads clearly.
  • annotation: Short reflective labels that explain the thinking behind your visual choices.
  • synthesis: Bringing skills from media testing, planning, and artist study together in the final piece.

Present and sequence the studio portfolio — studio worksheet

Big question: How do we select, sequence, and annotate our studio studies to tell a clear story of artistic growth?

Key words

  • portfolio: A curated collection of studio tests, plans, studies, and finished artwork showing your progression.
  • sequence: The deliberate order of pages that shows how early tests led to the final piece.
  • curation: Selecting and framing your strongest evidence so each page reads clearly.

  • synthesis: Bringing skills from media testing, planning, and artist study together in the final piece.
