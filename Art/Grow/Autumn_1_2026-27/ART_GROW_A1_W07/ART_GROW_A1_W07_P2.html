Stage 6 · Independent studio practice (You do) · TRACK Pick your route: Supported · Independent · Aspire

Choose your studio route and create

Supported route: partner skill share, spaced panel hang, and ticked review

  1. Show your 3-step demo board to one partner or adult and point to Tile 1, Tile 2, and Tile 3.
  2. Complete your museum label card and place your relief panel in the display row using the spacer block.
  3. Leave one ticked praise slip at a classmate's station.
  4. Complete your final studio review sheet.
Success check: 3-step board shared, panel hung with museum label, and review sheet ticked.

Independent route: Group A/B carousel demo, sightline curation, and evaluation

  1. Host your 90-second skill demonstration during the table carousel using your three-stage exemplar board.
  2. Visit two peer stations, test or inspect their sample tiles, and leave specific technical feedback slips.
  3. Write a full museum label and collaborate with your table team to align your relief panels along the gallery sightline.
  4. Complete your final Studio suite Evaluation reflecting on both your panel resolution and your demonstration.
Success check: Carousel demo delivered, two peer reviews written, panel curated on sightline, and evaluation completed.

Aspire route: lead curator role, live coaching demo, and critical exhibition review

  1. Deliver an interactive 90-second skill share where visitors try a 30-second mark on your mini-kit blank.
  2. Act as zone curator to coordinate panel spacing, tonal grouping, and lighting angle across a four-panel section.
  3. Write a comprehensive end-of-unit evaluation analysing how your architectural relief and leadership skills developed.
Success check: Interactive demo delivered, four-panel zone curated, and critical evaluation written.

Key words and definitions (TRACK)

  • skill share: A short practical demonstration where you teach a studio method to peers.
  • sightline: The horizontal eye-level line that connects a row of hung artworks cleanly.
  • museum label: A formal card beside an exhibited piece listing Title, Medium, Scale, and Maker's Note.
  • cohesive: Looking unified and thoughtfully grouped as one whole exhibition.
  • evaluation: Reviewing what worked well in your demonstration and final artwork.
