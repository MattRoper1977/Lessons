We do · GROW

We do

Team game · pairs

Hidden cue coach

Stay seated. One partner looks at the cue strip. The other looks at their own blank card.

The coach tells or signs the four cues in order. The maker draws them without seeing the strip.

Check the cards. Swap jobs. An adult can guide or draw. You may pass.

Points: 1 team point for all four cues in order. 1 more for a clear start and stop cue. Every team can score. Try again to earn a point. Keep each team’s own score.

  1. Agree the four-part order: start cue, sound pattern, artwork display and stop cue.
  2. Place the cue strip where everyone in the group can see it.
  3. Rehearse the sequence up to the checkpoint.
  4. Ask an observer for one specific comment on cue clarity or timing.
  5. Test that single change while keeping the rest of the sequence constant.
  6. Record the cue arrangement and the observed effect in your book.

Pick Supported, Independent or Aspire below. Your quiet choice counts too.

Independent

Test cues and plan the sequence

In your book

Point, speak, sign, draw, write or choose. You may pass.

  1. Agree your role in the group sequence and mark the start and stop cues.
  2. Rehearse your contribution in order with your group.
  3. Invite one specific piece of feedback on cue visibility or timing.
  4. Refine that single feature and repeat the run to check the effect.
  5. In your book, record your cue plan and refinement for Lesson 2.

I chose ___ because it helps the audience follow ___.

Keep your sequence plan and one tested cue choice in your book.

Aspire

Aspire challenge: compare two cue arrangements

In your book

Point, speak, sign, draw, write or choose. You may pass.

  1. Rehearse your contribution using two different cue placements or pause lengths.
  2. Keep the sequence, roles and stop signal constant across both trials.
  3. Compare how accurately the group follows each trial.
  4. Explain which trial was clearer for the listener using an observed clue.
  5. In your book, record both trials and your reason for Lesson 2.

Arrangement ___ worked better because ___.

Keep both tested arrangements and your justification in your book.

Mark I · S · C beside the task. Record actual support.
