We do

Team game · pairs

Find my clue

Stay seated. One partner can see a real cue sketch or art piece. The other looks at their own book.

Give clear words or signs for one detail. The partner draws or points to that detail in their book.

Show the real work. Check the match, then swap. Keep a missing clue as a gap. You may pass.

Points: 1 team point for finding a real detail. 1 more for linking it to the same good part. Every team can score. Try again to earn a point. Keep each team’s own score.

  1. Find a real cue sketch, art piece or note from the group piece.
  2. Point to one part that went well. Use a quiet role if that was your part.
  3. Say or show what was seen or heard at that moment.
  4. Link the clue to the strength. Check that both refer to the same part.
  5. Compare a clear note with a note that just says it was good.
  6. In your book, keep one real clue and one short strength note.

Independent (You do)

Make it yours · creative

My strength card

Arrange a real work clue and your own strength note on one page. Show how they link.

Pick Supported, Independent or Aspire below. Your quiet choice counts too.

In your book: copy, sketch or mark your own plan and the real change you try.

Points: make and test your own work. No team points in this part.

You may point to the real work, draw a clue, or tell an adult what to record. A quiet review has the same value. You may pass.

Keep the work on the desk. Ask before you move shared work. Keep any short cue test quiet. No solo is needed.

Independent

Explain a strength using a record

In your book

Point, speak, sign, draw, write or choose. You may pass.

  1. Choose an actual cue sketch, artwork or observation from the group piece.
  2. Identify a specific moment that shows a strength.
  3. Explain how the record supports that judgement rather than a general feeling.
  4. Check whether a comment refers to the same moment. Keep any difference clear.
  5. In your book, record the strength, source and explanation. Keep it for Lesson 2.

The observation supports ___ because ___.

In your book, keep a real clue and one review note.

Mark I · S · C beside the task. Record actual support.

Aspire

Evaluate two records

In your book

Point, speak, sign, draw, write or choose. You may pass.

  1. Compare two actual records that appear to describe the same moment.
  2. Identify what each record directly supports and what it leaves uncertain.
  3. Explain a strength using the strongest available observation.
  4. If the records differ, check their context instead of guessing.
  5. Keep both records and a balanced judgement in your book.

The records support ___, while ___ remains uncertain.

In your book, keep a real clue and one review note.

Mark I · S · C beside the task. Record actual support.
