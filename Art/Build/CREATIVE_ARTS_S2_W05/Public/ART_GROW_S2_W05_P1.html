Teacher guidance

What to say

Today is the first slot of our final week and the performance lesson. Confirm who from the class and invited staff is actually present. There are no outside guests, families or public audience. Choose a role and audience route. Agree quiet sound, clear personal space and the open-hand stop cue.

What good looks like

Each pupil confirms a role and access route. The actual allowed audience is identified without assumptions.

Fallback

Offer a seated silent or adult-directed share to one class member or invited staff member. A pupil may pass on the wider group share.

Check and response

A private share within the allowed audience is valid. Record direction as direction. No share means pending; it is never recorded as a completed performance.

Teacher guidance

What to say

Use the real outline about a smaller imagined show space: first wide moves, then a smaller space, then ideas and a small new move, then a clear end. Reuse actual pupil choices, not a claimed memory of absent sessions. If needed, make this four-part outline fresh today and agree one small role.

What good looks like

Pupils know the sequence and their own contribution. Missing prior work starts fresh with today's date.

Fallback

Use four hand cues and let a pupil direct the adult. A two-role version can still show the change and response.

Check and response

The response is a new safe plan after the imagined space changes. Do not physically narrow the working area or recreate distress.

Teacher guidance

What to say

Model one spoken and one silent version from the two fictional cards. Keep movements within a safe personal space. Show a clear pause at the choice and a final held pose. Check after the model: what evidence would show your own contribution, rather than only that the group performed?

What good looks like

Adult models clear performance choices and the need for individual evidence. Pupils identify their own role, chosen cue, line, move or direction.

Fallback

Adult performs under a pupil's chosen cues. Ask for one point to the selected move and confirm the pupil's reason.

Check and response

An individual role note and the pupil's own confirmed choice show contribution. A group photograph alone does not show every learner's decisions.

Teacher guidance

What to say

Use this slot for the actual performance. Share the original smaller-show-space story in a pair, small group or private directed version. Confirm the real audience and one skill to notice. Perform or direct the four parts, then hear one actual feature-based notice if offered. Record each learner's role, choice, reason, actual audience, date and support. A learner who does not share keeps an honest pending entry with the next action; do not invent a stand-in audience or feedback.

What good looks like

A real accessible performance or directed share is made to the class and invited staff only, or an honest pending record is retained. Each learner's contribution is separate from the group result.

Fallback

Pupil directs a seated adult to show the four-part story to one present class member or invited staff member. A silent hand cue can carry the whole role.

Check and response

Example only: I was the cue keeper. I held the pause before our new move. One class member noticed the clear end. Record this only if those events actually occurred.

Teacher guidance

What to say

Ask each maker to confirm their part, choice and any actual notice. No feedback yet is an acceptable entry. Distinguish a pupil's own view from a listener's words. Reserve the final two minutes to clear the space and keep the original plan, trials and actual share note together for Wednesday.

What good looks like

The record contains real contribution and feedback or a clearly stated gap. Own view and audience response are not confused.

Fallback

Adult asks one prompt at a time and reads the note back. A point, sign or sketch can confirm the chosen feature.

Check and response

I think the pause helped is the maker's view; the listener said I saw the new move is feedback. Both may be useful but should be labelled accurately.

Safety

All props and story spaces are imagined. Keep movement within each person's own clear space; no den building, objects to climb on, stage combat, lifting, touching, blindfolds or breath holding. Hold a comfortable pose for about three seconds, breathe normally, then relax. Keep exits clear. Quiet sound stays at talking level or below. Record directed work as direction; do not claim the pupil physically or vocally performed an adult's action.
