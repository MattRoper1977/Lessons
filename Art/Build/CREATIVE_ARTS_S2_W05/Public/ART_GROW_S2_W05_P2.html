In your book or arts log. You can point, speak, sign, draw or ask an adult to write your words.

Teacher guidance

What to say

This second slot closes the review and arts log. Use Monday's real performance evidence where it exists. No one has to reconstruct an absent pupil's performance or invent a listener. If an item is missing, note the gap and a next action. You may work privately and plan in any language.

What good looks like

Pupils gather genuine records and understand the evidence-first purpose without being ranked by attendance or confidence.

Fallback

Adult reads one actual record aloud. A pupil can confirm or correct by sign, point, speech or sketch.

Check and response

Pending is an honest status. The purpose is a reliable review and next action, not a record that every box is complete.

Name one real skill or choice.

Which words are your own view?

Ready

Use a small move, a gesture, words or directing. Pause whenever you need.

Teacher guidance

What to say

Ask each pupil to name one role, cue, pause or move they actually performed or directed. Find a matching item where available. Label the pupil's account as their own recollection if it is not witnessed. Keep their own view separate from a listener's actual words.

What good looks like

Pupil connects a real contribution to evidence and distinguishes own reflection from feedback.

Fallback

Offer two skill words and read a real note. Ask: is this right, or what would you change?

Check and response

My pause helped the scene is a personal view; the listener said the end was clear is feedback only if a real listener said it. Both are labelled honestly.

Teacher guidance

What to say

Show clearly labelled teacher models: a plan to share, a real role note and a blank feedback line. I can say I planned the share, but without an actual share record I cannot claim an audience. Check after the model: can a teacher's good example become a pupil's missing practice note? Explain the honest next action instead.

What good looks like

Adult models a claim, source and gap, and pupils reject fabricated evidence. No example is presented as a pupil record.

Fallback

Use two labels: real item and pending. Pupil directs the adult to place one example under each.

Check and response

No. A teacher model is exploration, not the pupil's own practice. Record the missing item as pending and plan a real opportunity or evidence check.

Which is a useful record of your work?

Teacher guidance

What to say

Use a genuine anonymous class item or an explicit teacher model. A is real participation; B an explored example and own view; C an idea, actual trial and change; D an actual share with date and audience. Hinge: if a group performed, can we tick every pupil's contribution from the group title alone? If uncertain, model a separate role-and-choice note for one learner and ask again.

What good looks like

Pupils use the content of evidence rather than a title or group label. Individual contribution and accurate audience are checked.

Fallback

Read one short entry and ask for a point to what it shows. Adult scribes the pupil's actual judgement and reason.

Check and response

No. Each learner needs a record of their own actual part. A plan or group title does not prove individual attendance, contribution, skill or feedback.

Optional next level stretch

Check your real skill plan and practice. What did you try? What changed? Give your own view and reason. Say what you learned and one next step.

Keep real plans, trials, changes and your own words. Add real feedback or sharing notes if you have them. Mark gaps pending and add the next action.

Teacher guidance

What to say

Use the final evidence list. A: actual role and support. B: actual source explored, notice and own view, with class models labelled as such. C: own idea, real trials, tested change and reason. D: actual share, real date and allowed audience, plus feedback only where given. Add a personal performance reflection supported by available items. For optional next level work, check a real skill goal or plan, actual practice, tested change, own view and reason, genuine learning reflection and next step. Record real feedback or sharing evidence where available. Keep missing items pending with the exact next action; do not fill them with a model or invented performance.

What good looks like

A closing arts-log list locates real evidence and honest gaps. Each pupil gives their own supported reflection. Optional additional evidence is retained only when it exists, and no automatic award completion is claimed.

Fallback

Adult checks one real item at a time, using keep, pending and next action choices. Pupil confirms exact words or gesture meaning. No public sharing, new recording or written paragraph is required.

Check and response

Example only: My role note supports A. My view of the class example supports B. The revised outline supports C. My actual share note supports D. My skill plan is present, but the second practice is pending; next I will practise the cue twice. The learner's list must match their own real items.

Review

Review the performance and close the arts log

5 minutes

Read your evidence list with an adult.

Can you find each real item?

Does each gap have a next action?

Keep your own words and real records together.

Teacher guidance

What to say

Read the record back and invite corrections. Check actual dates, audience and support, and distinguish direction from physical or vocal performance. Confirm the own-view statement belongs to the pupil. Reserve the final two minutes for calm storage. The complete portfolio still needs a separate review; the checklist does not complete the award.

What good looks like

The pupil confirms the record, available items can be found, gaps have actions and no invented evidence or completion claim appears.

Fallback

Use one entry at a time. Let the pupil point, sign or change a word. Adult records the actual support used.

Check and response

An honest list may have pending items in A, B, C or D and in optional next level work. Never turn a missing item into a completed claim.

Type instead

Write in your book first, or use these boxes. Save before closing.

Teacher guidance

What to say

Finish a short own reflection and one practical next step. State which evidence is ready for review and which is pending. Keep the actual work and note together. Thank pupils for their specific contribution without promising an award outcome.

What good looks like

Dated personal reflection, evidence list and next action close the term's work honestly. Award completion remains a separate review.

Fallback

Pupil confirms one view and one action by point, sign or speech. Adult writes the confirmed words and records help.

Check and response

Example only: I kept the pause because it helped our change stand out. My next action is to find my real trial note. Use the pupil's own view and genuine next action.

Arts log closing checklist

Safety

All props and story spaces are imagined. Keep movement within each person's own clear space; no den building, objects to climb on, stage combat, lifting, touching, blindfolds or breath holding. Hold a comfortable pose for about three seconds, breathe normally, then relax. Keep exits clear. Quiet sound stays at talking level or below. Record directed work as direction; do not claim the pupil physically or vocally performed an adult's action.
