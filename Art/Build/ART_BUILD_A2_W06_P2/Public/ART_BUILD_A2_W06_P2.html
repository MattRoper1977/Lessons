Team game · pairs

One-change coach

Stay seated in pairs. The coach sees a real cue plan. The maker looks at their own card.

Adult deployment and responsive feedback

Teacher answers and topic guidance

A gives one small change and one thing to check.

Release the completed review task when pupils choose A and show what will be checked. If they choose B, help them select one cue, one change and one observable result. Repeat the choice and use the parallel check before reducing support.

You have a next-step plan but have not tried it. Can you say it worked?

No. Keep it as a plan. Record a result only after an actual check.

Use actual work or an authentic observation from the group piece or its quiet role. Keep model examples separate from pupil records. Do not infer a public event, attendance or success. If no record exists, offer a short quiet cue test and label it as fresh practice.

Practice evidence only. No completion decision, encounter, public event or completed response is pre-filled.
