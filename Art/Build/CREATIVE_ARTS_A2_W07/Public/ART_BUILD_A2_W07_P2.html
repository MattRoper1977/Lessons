Staff notes

TA brief

Use one route, with Independent as the GROW default. Offer Supported when useful and Aspire as optional stretch. All pupil text uses the BUILD band. The routes change support and depth, not the part requirements. For five learners, use a pair and a trio, with a separate piece and record for each learner. In the trio, rotate maker, observer and materials roles; every learner retains their own choices. Offer private responses, silent directing and a pause. Do not cold call or seek personal or family disclosure. Scribe exact words or confirmed choices, read them back and allow correction. Do not compose pupils’ plans or reviews. Mark I, S or C beside the task in staff records and note the actual prompt. Give one specific success and one useful next step. Use VF / WS / I / NS+ / // / ? / R / E as appropriate. Record R only after Voice, Audience, an NS+ prompt and the pupil’s response in their own colour; capture alone is not closure. Upload the selected individual evidence to the evidence app on the same day.

D page 2 records the actual rehearsal, listener response, pupil’s chosen adjustment or reasoned retained feature, and another try. Audit A–D separately against the existing requirements. Use Evidence_Index pages 1–2 for verified file/page locators, real time totals, file count and gaps. Count the index among the five core PDFs and all separate media within ten. The actual December event must later record enjoyment and achievement, date, audience and receipt at D page 3. Do not prefill a part outcome.

Prompt ladder for adults

Point to the relevant place / instruction.

Ask one short question: "What is it asking you to find?"

Rephrase the instruction in simpler language.

Provide a scaffold or limited choice.

Model the first step only, then return the task to the pupil.

End question: What did your listener understand, and what still needs to be done?
