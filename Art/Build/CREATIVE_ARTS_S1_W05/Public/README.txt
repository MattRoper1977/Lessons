TIMING
Each lesson is 40 minutes. Arrival 3; Starter 3; I do 6; We do 6; Create 14; Review 5; Exit 3. Tidy in the last two Review minutes. Picture-prompt, model, route and optional stretch slides use time within these stages.

BUILD TWO STARS AND A WISH
Use one actual piece or original sequence from Weeks 2 to 4. The eye/ear, numbered stars and pencil/change pictograms have words and do not depend on colour. Find two specific features that work, choose one small wish, test it and make a real revision. A dry print can have a new labelled version on fresh paper; keep the original. Do not count an intended sentence or teacher example as the pupil's improvement.

GROW SELF REVIEW AND REVISION
Compare actual work with the Week 3 plan: intention, resilience idea, features, form, progress and support. Choose one achievable change. Make it, preserve a genuine before and after record and log its actual effect. A change may not work as hoped; record the truth and next step. Missing work or a necessary access change requires an honest new starting point, not invented earlier evidence.

ONE PRIVATE OPTIONAL PEER COMMENT
Use at most one peer comment across both periods, only if both maker and peer choose it. The maker chooses one part to share privately. No class display or compulsory performance. Either pupil can withdraw. Reuse the real first-period comment rather than ask for another in the second. If no peer route is used, record that honestly and use staff feedback or self-review. Keep actual sources distinct and do not write a staff statement as a peer quote. No peer names are needed in the supplied templates.

ACCESS AND GUIDANCE
Every stage has what to say, what good looks like and a materials-free fallback. Pupils can work silently, direct an adult and share privately. Books and existing arts logs come first. Typing is optional and closed under Type instead. Save my responses creates a local text file; it does not submit work. No pupil login or recording device is required. No personal history or public performance is required.

EXPLORE AND STRETCH
Each GROW lesson includes one optional next level task. Explore work stands complete without it. Retain additional reasons, feedback decisions and evaluation separately labelled. This week contributes to the ongoing arts log and does not itself complete an award.

LOGS AND EVIDENCE
Continue actual dated entries in the established five-file pattern if used. Blank templates are optional. Retain the original work or precise starting observation, the review, real revision, observed result, pupil words and actual support. Pictures in the pack are prompts only. No copied artwork, lyrics or quotations are supplied. No visit, live encounter or completed award is claimed.

CHECKS AND LIMITS
CHECKS.json and CHECKS.txt record checks. Print PDFs, document pages and editable slides were rendered and visually reviewed. Interactive code and simulated interactions were checked. Full browser rendering and HTML print pagination were not executed; use supplied PDFs for checked print geometry. READING_FIGURES.json retains exact measured pupil corpora and estimates, not learner assessments. No standalone scripts are included. All resource links are relative.
