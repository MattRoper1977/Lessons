Human beat line

Four volunteers stand in a line. They are beats 1, 2, 3 and 4.

The teacher walks along the line at an even pace. Each person taps once as the teacher passes.

Then swap: beats 2 and 4 become RESTS. They hold up an open hand and stay quiet.

Everyone else is the judge: thumbs up if the gaps stayed even.

We watch a count-in of four.

We play four even taps together.

In your book: A: four even taps.

Conductor's challenge

Work in pairs. One is the Conductor. One is the Player.

The Player looks away or closes their eyes, if they want to. The Conductor gives a quiet count-in on the table: one, two, three, four.

The Player plays TAP REST TAP REST and keeps the rests quiet. The Conductor says "stop" at the end of a bar.

Points: 1 point for a round with even gaps. 1 bonus point for stopping right on the cue. Then swap jobs.

Staff notes · Autumn 2 · Week 2 · Lesson 1

Use Supported as needed, Independent for a pupil ready to take the lead, and Aspire for more depth. The route changes help, not the shared beat goal. Use five individual records within a pair and a trio; rotate beat keeper, part player and watcher. Never require solo performance. Week 2 builds steady pulse from the previous sound exploration. Use four beat positions and original class parts so the task is reproducible with quiet body percussion. Scheme outcome: Keep a steady beat / join a group piece.

Demonstration

0–15 seconds: Point to four numbered spaces. Give four slow, even counts without playing. Say: “This is the count-in. We start on the next one.”

15–30 seconds: Play A: TAP, TAP, TAP, TAP. Move the pointer through spaces 1–4 at the same rate. Say: “One tap in each space. My gaps stay the same.”

30–45 seconds: Play B: TAP, REST, TAP, REST. Keep the pointer moving through all four spaces. Say: “A rest is quiet. Beat two still takes one full space.”

45–60 seconds: Repeat B twice after a fresh four-count. Show the raised-hand stop cue. Say: “I keep the spaces even. I stop at the cue.” Let a pupil show beat two without a sound.

Misconceptions and corrections

The pupil speeds up after a rest: Keep the pointer moving through every numbered space. Say “tap, wait, tap, wait” at one pace. Practise B with a visible nod for every beat, then fade the nod.

The pupil skips a rest and starts the next tap early: Point to beat two and count its full length. Contrast TAP REST TAP REST with TAP TAP. Both rests need a place. Return to four even taps, then put the rests back.

A louder tap is treated as a better beat: Use soft taps or silent movements at the same pace. Praise timing, not loudness. Return to the agreed low sound level and stop cue.

Cfu

After I do, point to beat two in B. Ask: “Tap or quiet?” Each pupil shows quiet, then points to beat three on the next beat. If the pupil skips straight to three, repeat a slow model with a pointer.

Hinge

At We do 2, show B: TAP REST TAP REST. Ask: “Does beat two still take time?” Choose A: no time, or B: one full quiet beat. Correct B. If every pupil shows B and keeps one full quiet beat, move to route practice. If any pupil skips the rest, model four even spaces again and retest that pupil before independent playing.

Parallel hinge

Retest with REST TAP REST TAP. Ask: “What happens on beat one?” Correct: a full quiet beat; play on two. Check with a visible pointer rather than a verbal guess alone.
