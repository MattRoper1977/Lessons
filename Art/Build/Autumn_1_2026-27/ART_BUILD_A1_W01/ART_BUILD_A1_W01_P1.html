Stage 6 · Independent studio practice (You do) · BASE Pick your route: Supported · Independent · Aspire

Choose your studio route and create

Supported route: trace and test four marks

  1. Pick one card shape (star, leaf, wave, or shield).
  2. Trace the shape in all four boxes on your sheet.
  3. Fill box 1 with pencil lines. Box 2 with dark wax.
  4. Fill box 3 with pen dots. Box 4 with a brush stripe.
Success check: All four boxes show your shape with four new marks.

Independent route: draw one sign in four tools

  1. Draw one simple shape in all four boxes on your grid.
  2. Use light. Dark pencil tone in Box 1 and crossed lines in Box 2.
  3. Use neat pen dots in Box 3. Wax with ink wash in Box 4.
  4. Tick the box that has the best dark. Light look.
Success check: Four clear mark styles tested on the same shape.

Aspire route: mix two marks for depth

  1. Draw your shape in all four boxes at the same size.
  2. Test thick. Thin lines in Box 1 and smooth tone in Box 2.
  3. Test dot shade in Box 3. Wax under ink wash in Box 4.
  4. Add a short word under each box to name the tool.
Success check: Four boxes done with a mixed mark test in Box 4.

Key words and definitions (BASE)

  • line: A mark that moves from one spot to the next.
  • tone: How light or dark a mark looks on the page.
  • shape: An flat area with a clear edge around it.
  • bold: Thick, dark, and easy to see from far off.
  • grid: Neat boxes on a page for your art tests.
