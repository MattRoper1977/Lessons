Stage 6 · Independent studio practice (You do) · BASE Pick your route: Supported · Independent · Aspire

Choose your studio route and create

Supported route: taped plate rubbing and ruler bars

  1. Use a board with two bumpy plates taped down.
  2. Rub a flat wax crayon across your page over both plates.
  3. Use a wide ruler. Black pen to draw three straight bars.
  4. Draw two cross lines to make triangles between the bars.
Success check: Two neat rubbings made with bold black bars on top.

Independent route: three rubbings and arch frame

  1. Make three new wax rubbings on one sheet of paper.
  2. Look at a bridge card. Find the main arch and triangles.
  3. Draw the arch. Cross bars over your rubbings in black pen.
  4. Write one word by each rubbing (rough, ridged, or mesh).
Success check: Three rubbings joined with a bold frame drawing. Words.

Aspire route: cut rubbing strips into a frame

  1. Make four neat rubbings using two wax shades.
  2. Cut the rubbings into straight bar strips. Glue them down.
  3. Outline each bar. Add corner bolt dots with a dark pen.
  4. Add white chalk lines along the top edge of each bar.
Success check: Cut rubbing strips built into a strong frame with chalk light.

Key words and definitions (BASE)

  • beam: A long straight bar that holds up a frame.
  • arch: A curved shape that goes across a gap.
  • rough: Bumpy to touch, not flat or smooth.
  • rubbing: Marks made by pressing wax over a bumpy card.
  • edge: The outside line where a shape stops.
