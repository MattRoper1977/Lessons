Stage 6 · Independent studio practice (You do) · BASE Pick your route: Supported · Independent · Aspire

Choose your studio route and create

Supported route: pre-cut card beams and arches

  1. Pick a stiff base board. Four pre-cut card beams.
  2. Arrange your beams into a bridge or tower shape. First gluing.
  3. Spread a thin layer of glue. Press the two main beams down.
  4. Glue two ridged strips across the top to make a raised layer.
Success check: Two layers of card strips glued firmly into a frame.

Independent route: cut and layer three card levels

  1. Cut two wide tall bars. An arch from smooth card.
  2. Glue the first layer firmly onto your stiff base board.
  3. Cut thin cross-bars from bumpy ridged card. Glue on top.
  4. Rub the side of a dark crayon lightly over the top to show the raised edges.
Success check: Three card levels cut, glued cleanly,. Bright lineed with wax.

Aspire route: multi-texture relief with bolt dot details

  1. Plan. Cut a three-layer frame using smooth and ridged card.
  2. Build overlap joins where cross-beams lock over main tall bars.
  3. Add small square card plates. Hole-punch discs as raised bolts.
  4. Sweep dry charcoal. White chalk across the surface to bring out depth.
Success check: Three-layer relief panel with bolt details. Tonal bright line.

Key words and definitions (BASE)

  • relief: Art where raised shapes stick up from a flat base.
  • layer: One piece of card glued on top of another piece.
  • base: The flat stiff board at the bottom of your work.
  • maker: A person who plans and builds artwork by hand.
  • join: Where two pieces of card meet and stick fast.
