Stage 6 · Independent studio practice (You do) · BASE Pick your route: Supported · Independent · Aspire

Choose your studio route and create

Supported route: large template and two-tone fill

  1. Place a large A4 centre cut shape on your heavy card.
  2. Trace firmly around the outside with a thick dark crayon.
  3. Fill one half with dark marks. The other half with light marks.
  4. Check that no white gaps are left along the main edge.
Success check: One large centred sign filled with dark. Light marks.

Independent route: guide dots and chosen art skill

  1. Mark four wide guide dots. Sketch your sign large in the centre.
  2. Use your chosen Week 1 art skill to build tone across the shape.
  3. Add a dark. Light mark set or wash around the outside back space.
  4. Strengthen the main outline so the sign stands out clearly.
Success check: Large freehand sign with clear tonal dark. Light and crisp edge.

Aspire route: layered tone and border frame

  1. Rule a neat border frame. Draw your sign filling the centre.
  2. Layer two marks inside the sign (smooth tone plus texture dots).
  3. Use a cool or warm wash in the outer space to push the sign forward.
  4. Write your title. Tool name cleanly on a back label.
Success check: Centred sign with layered marks, back space wash,. Border.

Key words and definitions (BASE)

  • scale: How big or small a shape is on the card.
  • centre: The middle spot of your page.
  • shade: Dark tone added to one side of a shape.
  • edge: The clear line around the outside of a shape.
  • fill: To put colour or marks right across a space.
