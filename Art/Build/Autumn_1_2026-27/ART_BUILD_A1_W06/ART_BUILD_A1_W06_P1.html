Stage 6 · Independent studio practice (You do) · BASE Pick your route: Supported · Independent · Aspire

Choose your studio route and create

Supported route: pointer arrow and two-word choice card

  1. Stick a small card pointer arrow next to your best mark on your artwork.
  2. Circle one tool word. One mood word on your speaking card.
  3. Show your work to one partner or an adult. Point to the arrow.
  4. Say or read: 'I used [tool] to make it [mood].'
Success check: Pointer arrow placed. Choice shared with a partner or adult.

Independent route: table art show and 'because' short line

  1. Place your pointer arrow on the strongest area of your self piece.
  2. Write your full short line on your share card: 'I chose ___. It works as ___.'
  3. Stand or sit at your table group, hold your artwork steady,. Share your short line.
  4. Give one clear kind note to a table partner about their marks.
Success check: Written 'because' short line shared clearly with table group plus peer feedback.

Aspire route: compare test and final piece for the group

  1. Display both your Week 1/4 test. Your final self piece on the easel.
  2. Point first to the test. Then to the final piece.
  3. Explain what tool you picked, why you chose it,. How you made it bolder.
  4. Answer one question from a friend about your art skill.
Success check: Two-step art show linking test to final piece. Answering a peer question.

Key words and definitions (BASE)

  • share: To show your work to others and talk about it.
  • reason: Why you picked that shape, colour, or mark.
  • pointer: A small card arrow that shows the part you mean.
  • listen: To look quietly at the speaker's artwork. At the same time they talk.
  • proud: Feeling pleased with the care you put into your work.
