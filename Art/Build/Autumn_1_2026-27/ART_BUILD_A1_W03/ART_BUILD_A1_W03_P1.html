Stage 6 · Independent studio practice (You do) · BASE Pick your route: Supported · Independent · Aspire

Choose your studio route and create

Supported route: tick three facts and copy one mark set

  1. Tape your card window over a clear mark set on the artwork print.
  2. Tick one colour word, one mark word,. One mood word on your sheet.
  3. Copy the simple twin shapes inside your window into the big box.
  4. Colour your box using the same two colours as the print.
Success check: Three facts ticked. One close-up mark set coloured neatly.

Independent route: write three facts and draw a close-up study

  1. Move your card card window to find a strong mark set on the print.
  2. Write three short facts on your log page (colours, marks,. Mood).
  3. Draw the close-up mark set with care in your study box.
  4. Match the dark. Light tones with coloured pencils and black pen.
Success check: Three written visual facts. An acset out close-up mark set study.

Aspire route: compare two close-up boxes and create your own twist

  1. Fill in your three visual facts. First close-up mark set study.
  2. In a second box, redraw the same mark set using your own personal sign.
  3. Keep the maker's high-dark. Light colour pair in your new version.
  4. Add a short note explaining what you kept from the artwork.
Success check: Three facts, one study box,. One adapted sign mark set box.

Key words and definitions (BASE)

  • fact: Something true you can see and point to in the work.
  • mood: How the colours and marks make the picture feel.
  • mark set: Shapes or lines that repeat again and again.
  • card window: A small card window that helps you look closely on one bit.
  • copy: To draw a small part with care so you learn how it works.
