Stage 6 · Independent studio practice (You do) · BASE Pick your route: Supported · Independent · Aspire

Choose your studio route and create

Supported route: dry layout check and foil boost

  1. Lay your cut frame strips on your dark base board following your Week 4 plan.
  2. Stand up. Look from two steps back with an adult.
  3. Swap or add one shiny foil strip so the main beam stands out brightly.
  4. Glue layer 1. Layer 2 firmly onto your base board.
Success check: Layout checked from two steps back, foil dark. Light added, and main layers glued.

Independent route: two-pace critique and dark and light change

  1. Cut. Arrange your Layer 1 and Layer 2 card and foil pieces on the base board.
  2. Step back two paces with a partner. Check shape clarity and dark and light.
  3. Make one clear change (widen spacing, add a foil edge, or dry-brush a ridge).
  4. Glue your adjusted layers cleanly. Tick the change you made on your plan.
Success check: Dry layout tested, one change recorded on the plan,. Layers bonded.

Aspire route: strong frame overlap check and tonal edge refinement

  1. Assemble all three relief layers dry on your board. Check from across the room.
  2. Adjust spacing. Overlaps so the focal arch or tower leads the eye cleanly.
  3. Bond layers 1. 2. Then test where chrome and rust dry-brushing will lift the edges.
  4. Write a short note on the back of your plan naming what you changed. Why.
Success check: Three-layer frame tested, refined, bonded, and labelled.

Key words and definitions (BASE)

  • test layout: Placing cut pieces on the board. First gluing to see how they look.
  • adjust: To move or change one piece so the whole picture looks better.
  • dark and light: Big difference between dark base card and bright chrome foil.
  • overlap: When one card strip crosses right over the top of another.
  • strong: Clear to see and firmly built.
