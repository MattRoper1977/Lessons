Stage 6 · Independent studio practice (You do) · BASE Pick your route: Supported · Independent · Aspire

Choose your studio route and create

Supported route: ticked art wall label and spacer placement

  1. Pick a title. Tick the art tools you used on your art wall label card.
  2. Use the card spacer block to place your panel in a straight row on the display table or wall.
  3. Place your label card neatly under the bottom corner.
  4. Put one star praise note next to a friend's panel. Tell them what you liked.
Success check: Label done, panel spaced neatly,. One peer praise note given.

Independent route: written museum label, wall hang, and two reviews

  1. Write a neat art wall label card with your artwork title, three art tools,. One short line on your texture.
  2. Work with your table team to align four panels at eye level with equal spacing.
  3. Walk the art show. Leave two clear praise sticky notes naming texture or dark and light.
Success check: Full art wall label written, team row set outd evenly,. Two peer notes placed.

Aspire route: lead curator role and art show intro sign

  1. Fill in your detailed art wall label card. Help check the flat level straight line of the display.
  2. Write a big art show welcome card explaining how the class built 3-layer relief panels.
  3. Leave three technical praise notes bright lineing strong use of relief depth, foil pressing,. Dry-brushing.
Success check: Art wall label, art show welcome sign,. Three technical peer notes done.

Key words and definitions (BASE)

  • set out: To choose and arrange artworks neatly so people can enjoy them.
  • art show: A wall or table display where finished art is shared.
  • label card: A small card next to an artwork naming the title and tools.
  • eye level: At a comfortable height to look at without bending or stretching.
  • space: Leaving an even gap between each picture on the wall.
