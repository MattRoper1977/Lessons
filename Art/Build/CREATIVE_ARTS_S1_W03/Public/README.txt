TIMING
Each lesson is 40 minutes. Arrival 3; Starter 3; I do 6; We do 6; Create 14; Review 5; Exit 3. Tidy in the last two Review minutes. Artist, model, route and optional stretch slides use time within these stages, not extra minutes.

BUILD ARTIST AND OWN RESPONSE
One long-established public artist, Henri Matisse, is described through line, colour and cut-paper methods. The original classroom description separates real details from personal interpretations of feelings. Pupils try a method and make their own simple response, with their own subject and arrangement. No artwork, lyric, quotation or archive text is reproduced. The original teacher-model sheet is not work by Matisse and is not pupil evidence.

GROW PLANNING
Keep two small thumbnail ideas or equivalent short action plans. Choose one with a reason. Develop a materials list with quantities, ordered actions, time, space and access notes. Check what is actually available and adjust the plan honestly. Keep the chosen plan and arts-log entry for later making. A plan is not a finished artwork. Sound, silent movement or pupil-directed adult actions can have an equivalent sequence plan and no-materials access route.

ACCESS AND GUIDANCE
Every stage has what to say, what good looks like and a materials-free fallback. Pupils can work silently, direct an adult and share privately. Books and existing arts logs come first. Typing is optional and closed under Type instead. Save my responses creates a local text file; it does not submit work. No pupil login or recording device is required. Sound stays below talking volume and stops on a raised hand. No personal history or public performance is required.

EXPLORE AND STRETCH
One optional next level task is included per GROW lesson. Explore work stands complete without it. Retain any extra idea comparison, reasons, constraints, feedback or revisions separately labelled. The week contributes to the ongoing arts log and does not itself complete an award.

LOGS AND EVIDENCE
Continue dated entries in the established five-file pattern if used. Blank templates are optional. Keep artist facts, interpretations, teacher models and pupil work separate. Record actual dates and help. A classroom description does not prove a visit or live encounter; a later event is never prefilled.

CHECKS AND LIMITS
CHECKS.json and CHECKS.txt record checks. Print PDFs, document pages and editable slides were rendered and visually reviewed. Interactive code and simulated interactions were checked. Full browser rendering and HTML print pagination were not executed; use supplied PDFs for checked print geometry. READING_FIGURES.json retains exact measured pupil corpora and estimates, not learner assessments. No standalone scripts are included. All resource links are relative.
