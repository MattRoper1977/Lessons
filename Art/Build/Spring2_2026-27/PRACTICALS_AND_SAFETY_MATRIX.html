Bench cards

Scalpel / craft knife

Cutting mat; cut away from body; non-cutting hand behind line; steel rule; counted out/in; controlled bench only.

Glue gun

Rest and mat; no contact with nozzle/fresh glue. For a burn: cool running water at least 20 minutes, report/escalate, do not peel, no ice.

Wire / cutters

Goggles where required; offcut down into tray; fold/tape/cap cut ends; count tools; carry wire coiled.

Mod Roc

Cut strips before wetting; no dry sanding; bowl not sink; never wrap body parts; apron/sleeves/hands; cover cuts and clear spills.

If a controlled tool is missing, stop work and tell staff. Staff manage movement/exits; pupils are not collectively held. All sculpture is a tabletop maquette — not load-bearing, suspended, climbed on or placed on unstable ledges.
