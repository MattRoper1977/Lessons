We do · BUILD

We do

Team game · pairs

Silent cue band

Stay seated in pairs. Agree the four cues. One partner gives signs with no spoken words.

The other watches, shows the art and stops on the sign. Both can see. Do not cover ears.

Swap jobs. The teacher checks each team as they move round. Use an adult or pass.

Points: 1 team point for showing art on its cue. 1 more for stopping on the sign. Every team can score. Try again to earn a point. Keep each team’s own score.

  1. Bring back your Lesson 1 cue plan and check the four boxes.
  2. Run the group share from start to stop.
  3. Leave a calm gap before showing the art page.
  4. Ask the listener which part was clear and what to try next.
  5. Change one cue or position and run that part again.
  6. In your book, mark the change that helped the group.

We do decision checkpoint

Independent (You do)

Make it yours · creative

My final cue strip

Build the full share from your plan. Test one change in the cue, gap or art place.

Pick Supported, Independent or Aspire below. Your quiet choice counts too.

In your book: copy, sketch or mark your own plan and the real change you try.

Points: make and test your own work. No team points in this part.

You may point, show art, hold a cue card, or tell an adult where to place it. Share with one adult in private if you wish.

Use paper, card, pencils, layered art pieces and the four cue cards. Keep sound low and leave space to pass.

Aspire

Aspire challenge: compare and explain

In your book

Point, speak, sign, draw, write or choose. You may pass.

  1. Run the full group piece twice with one planned change between runs.
  2. Keep the roles and start cue the same in both runs.
  3. Compare how clearly the stop and art cues show in each run.
  4. Explain why one run worked better for the listener.
  5. In your book, record both runs and the listener clue.

I chose ___ because the listener saw ___.

Keep both tries and your reason in your book.

Mark I · S · C beside the task. Record actual support.
