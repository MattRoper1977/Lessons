CREATIVE ARTS SPRING 1 WEEK 1
Theme: Resilience & Change. Year A. Both pathways: Explore.

This folder is a complete portable edition. Keep its assets folder alongside its lesson pages. The two editions contain the same generic classroom content.

START HERE
Read Sequence_and_Evidence_Guide.pdf, then the dated Teacher_and_Cover_Plan.pdf. Open the lesson HTML for interactive teaching or its editable slides for offline teaching. Pupil_Resources.pdf is the checked print pack; Editable_Pack.docx provides editable pupil pages. Each lesson also has separate knowledge organiser, word page, route packs, activity cards, descriptions/comparison, evidence slip, staff answers and TA brief.

Each lesson is 40 minutes. Arrival 3; Starter 3; I do 6; We do 6; Create 14; Review 5; Exit 3. The last two Review minutes include tidy-up. Original model, route and optional stretch slides use time within these stages, not extra minutes.

TEACHING AND ACCESS
Every stage has what to say, what good looks like and a materials-free fallback in the teacher plan and HTML guidance. Pupils may use an invented feeling or abstract idea, direct an adult, work silently and share privately. Painting and quiet original sound are available; movement or original words provide further access. An adult may scribe the pupil's actual words and read them back for confirmation. Sound stays below talking volume and stops on a raised hand.

GROW EXTENSION
One clearly labelled optional next level stretch is supplied in each GROW lesson. The Explore lesson stands complete without it. Retain the additional pupil-owned research/view and skill-practice record if used. Completing Week 1 does not itself complete an award.

ARTS LOG
Use books or the existing arts log first. Optional blank log pages are in Arts_Log_Templates. Add actual dated entries without replacing completed prior work. Retain real work, tests or precise observations plus pupil choices and reasons. No recorded media is required. A description-based discussion does not prove a visit, live encounter or future event.

VISUALS AND CONTENT
The teacher-model sheet is original, attributed to no real artist. Matisse and Thomas are discussed through short original descriptions. No copied artwork, lyrics or quotations are supplied. Feelings and interpretations may differ. The approved mark is unchanged.

CHECKS AND LIMITS
CHECKS.json and CHECKS.txt report executed checks. Print PDFs, document pages and editable slides were rendered and visually reviewed. Interactive code and simulated interactions were checked. Full browser rendering and HTML print pagination were not executed; use the supplied PDFs for checked print geometry. Reading estimates and exact measured pupil corpora are in READING_FIGURES.json. They are estimates, not learner assessments. No standalone scripts are included. All resource links are relative.
