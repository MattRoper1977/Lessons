Staff: evidence and delivery
  • Part A — Take part: at least two arts activities; identify what they learnt
  • Part B — Explore: at least one artist AND at least one arts ORGANISATION, through live or active experience
  • Part C — Create: an artwork, a record of the PROCESS, and the final work
  • Part D — Share: communicate personal enjoyment or achievement, chosen by looking back at A-C

Overall Pass allows ONE Attempted. Any Not Attempted = Below Pass. Only an adviser trained at Explore may assess; delivery is through a validated centre. These are requirements to check, not a claim that arrangements are complete.

Assessment considers art form knowledge and understanding, creativity, communication. Organise evidence by part and give the actual file name plus page, slide or timecode. A cumulative file per part is one possible layout within the 10-file total cap; it is not a required file count.

Explore Part B needs both an artist and an arts organisation through live or active experience. Desk research alone is preparation. Read ORG_SLOT and PRACTITIONER_SLOT. Part A uses two arts activities; Part C is a distinct new creation with process and final work; Part D communicates the pupil’s chosen enjoyment or achievement.

Fourteen forty-minute sessions support the award; they do not supply its whole 35-hour programme. Record actual guided and independent time. Pupils choose their own plans and reviews; adults may accurately scribe their words.
