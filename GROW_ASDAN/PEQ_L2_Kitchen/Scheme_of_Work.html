Kitchen Programme · PEQ Year Map & Scheme of Work

Full year 2026–27 · three level lanes (Entry 3 · Level 1 · Level 2) · one kitchen, one class

The design in one paragraph. The Kitchen Programme is a full 38-week year in which one mixed class cooks, plans, decides, investigates and evaluates its way through the ASDAN Personal Effectiveness Qualifications at three levels at once — the cohort at Entry 3, a small group at Level 1, and a directed few at Level 2. The kitchen is the shared vehicle (spec §9 p16 explicitly permits mixed-level groups with differentiated demand): an E3 pupil, an L1 pupil and an L2 pupil cook in the same session and bank TmWkSkE3, TmWkSk1 and TmWkSk2 respectively — one level per skill per qualification (barred combinations, §6.5), carried by the witness sheet's Level tick and the per-level minima on the evidence sheets. Every surface says working towards: registration and unit/level attribution are centre-coordinator decisions, and nothing on these pages promises a certificate. Food content is cooking skill, budget, teamwork and enjoyment — no diet, calorie, weight or body framing anywhere in this programme.

The hours, stated honestly

The live 12-week deck suite is the knowledge-and-assessment spine, not the hours. Measured, it contributes 8.0 GLH: 12 live decks (LAUNCH_ASDAN/PEQ W1-6 + GROW_ASDAN/PEQ W1-6) x one timetabled 40-minute period each = 480 min = 8.0 GLH. The period length is the estate's timetable unit (the science chassis is named v3_40min; GROW PEQ runs Thursday P4, one period). The decks PROGRAM 53 min of slide time each (sum of data-timer values on every deck's 10 slides) - i.e. they over-program the slot; the GLH claimed is the supervised 40-minute slot, never the programme. Deck-to-unit assignment follows each deck's own banking line. Against that: ComSkE3 alone is 30 GLH, and the smallest qualification (the E3 Award) is 40 GLH. The guided hours therefore come from the planned year — the kitchen practicals, the challenge blocks, supervised tutor-time application — because GLH counts any taught or supervised time toward the unit, including assessment by the centre assessor (spec §9 p16, §5.1 p9).

Weekly PEQ hours, derived. This map is worked at seven timetabled 40-minute periods a week — 4.667 supervised hours, i.e. 4.667 × 38 = 177.3 physical GLH per lane. That figure is two measurements plus one ruling, and it is worth knowing which is which. Measured: the estate's period unit is 40 minutes (fifteen agreeing statements across all three lanes' weekly planners), and BUILD runs six discrete weekly slots at one period each beside its PEQ row — “…which is how LI reaches certification on a one-slot week”, the only cell in the estate that fixes a slot count. Together those bound the lane at 1–7 periods and no further. Ruled (owner, 22 Aug 2026): all six carryable slots — Careers, Vocational/D&T, Living Independently, FoodWise, Community A and Community B — may bank a guided hour to PEQ alongside their own ASDAN short course. That puts the lane at the top of the measured band: 6 + 1 = 7 periods. Full record: _passpq/DERIVATION_YEAR1.md.

What the derived rate changes. The co-delivery claim is withdrawn. At the previous 3.5 h/wk owner input the Entry 3 six-unit ledger (140 unit-GLH) closed only because 7 hours were co-delivered — one supervised session banked to two units — and Level 1 needed 2 hours the same way. At 4.667 h/wk those hours are real supervised time and the ledgers close without the claim, so it is dropped to zero on all three lanes. What is left over is declared QA and consolidation — 37.3 h at Entry 3, 42.3 h at Level 1, 57.3 h at Level 2 — and it is never claimed against a unit. The honest number, not the convenient one.
Every lane now targets the full Certificate, and every milestone is proven in the ledger below rather than asserted: Award by W14 → Extended Award by W26 → Certificate by W38 on all three lanes, with headroom. Had the timetable given five periods instead of seven, the honest answer for Entry 3 and Level 1 would have been the Extended Award, not the Certificate — the table below shows exactly where each threshold falls, so a later change to the timetable can be read off it rather than re-argued.

Centre orchestration — decided, not deferred

Entry is settled. Learners are registered and units are entered. The centre orchestrates units, levels and order freely; no third-party sign-off is outstanding for entry. Recorded 2026-08-21 (owner), recorded PEQ-YEAR-1 s3.
  • Level 1 lane: all six L1 units, 15 credits, all at level (s5.1 permits exceeding 14).
  • Barred combinations: Each pupil banks each of the six skills at ONE level only (spec s6.5); the credit from the highest-level unit counts. The witness sheet's Level tick is the record.
  • The LAUNCH room: The LAUNCH room runs the Entry 3, Level 1 and Level 2 routes CONCURRENTLY - one mixed session, three demands, three levels of evidence. The L2 route is staff-directed and appears in-deck behind the Guidance toggle.
  • Lane targets: SET, on the derived rate of 7 x 40-minute periods (4.667 h/wk). ALL THREE LANES TARGET THE FULL CERTIFICATE: Entry Level Certificate (14 cr) - Level 1 Certificate (14 cr, claimed from the six-unit 15) - Level 2 Certificate (15 cr). Award by W14, Extended Award by W26, Certificate by W38 on every lane, each milestone proven in the ledger rather than asserted. The co-delivery claim is WITHDRAWN on all three lanes (was E3 7 h, L1 2 h): at 4.667 h/wk the hours are real supervised time and the six-unit ledgers close without it, leaving 37.3 h (E3), 42.3 h (L1) and 57.3 h (L2) of declared QA/consolidation.

Thinking vs Critical thinking — the lane split. The E3 and L1 lanes plan ThSkE3 / ThSk1 (Thinking skills); only the L2 lane plans CrThSk2 (Critical thinking skills) — the unit changes name, code and demand at Level 2 (spec pp11–13). The shared summer investigation runs at three demands: E3/L1 pupils work their Thinking plan; the L2 pupil assesses sources for credibility, accuracy and bias across ≥2 sources, primary vs secondary (the blind taste test is the primary source).

Accreditation facts for staff (spec v1.2). Entry 3 sizes (the primary route for this cohort): Award 4 credits (all at level) · Extended Award 9 (min 6 at level) · Certificate 14 (min 11 at level). All six E3 units = exactly 14 credits, every one at level, so a full year homes an E3 Certificate outright with no adjacent-credit accounting at all. Level 1 sizes (the stretch route, for the 2–4 pupils working above Entry 3): Award 4 · Extended Award 9 · Certificate 14 (max 3 adjacent-level credits — in practice only one adjacent unit can ever count); all six L1 units = 15 credits. SMART is introduced but NOT assessed at Entry 3 team working — the SMART minimum sits only at TmWkSk1, so no task may gate the E3 unit on producing SMART targets. Partial achievement still certificates at unit level. The 10-hour plan-use window applies to every skill except Communication. RPL reaches back three years and can include ASDAN Short Course achievement. Assessment records need assessor and learner signatures; IQA before EQA; the first-year EQA sampling visit must be booked during this first delivery year (coordinator action). Unit and level attribution is the centre coordinator’s decision — nothing is promised to a learner.
