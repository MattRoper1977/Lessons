Kitchen Programme · PEQ Year Map & Scheme of Work

Full year 2026–27 · three level lanes (Entry 3 · Level 1 · Level 2) · one kitchen, one class

The design in one paragraph. The Kitchen Programme is a full 38-week year in which one mixed class cooks, plans, decides, investigates and evaluates its way through the ASDAN Personal Effectiveness Qualifications at three levels at once — the cohort at Entry 3, a small group at Level 1, and a directed few at Level 2. The kitchen is the shared vehicle (spec §9 p16 explicitly permits mixed-level groups with differentiated demand): an E3 pupil, an L1 pupil and an L2 pupil cook in the same session and bank TmWkSkE3, TmWkSk1 and TmWkSk2 respectively — one level per skill per qualification (barred combinations, §6.5), carried by the witness sheet's Level tick and the per-level minima on the evidence sheets. Every surface says working towards: registration and unit/level attribution are centre-coordinator decisions, and nothing on these pages promises a certificate. Food content is cooking skill, budget, teamwork and enjoyment — no diet, calorie, weight or body framing anywhere in this programme.

The hours, stated honestly

The live 12-week deck suite is the knowledge-and-assessment spine, not the hours. Measured, it contributes 8.0 GLH: 12 live decks (LAUNCH_ASDAN/PEQ W1-6 + GROW_ASDAN/PEQ W1-6) x one timetabled 40-minute period each = 480 min = 8.0 GLH. The period length is the estate's timetable unit (the science chassis is named v3_40min; GROW PEQ runs Thursday P4, one period). The decks PROGRAM 53 min of slide time each (sum of data-timer values on every deck's 10 slides) - i.e. they over-program the slot; the GLH claimed is the supervised 40-minute slot, never the programme. Deck-to-unit assignment follows each deck's own banking line. Against that: ComSkE3 alone is 30 GLH, and the smallest qualification (the E3 Award) is 40 GLH. The guided hours therefore come from the planned year — the kitchen practicals, the challenge blocks, supervised tutor-time application — because GLH counts any taught or supervised time toward the unit, including assessment by the centre assessor (spec §9 p16, §5.1 p9).

  • Criteria coverage matrix — every AC of all 18 units ↦ week + artefact (zero gaps, proven at build)
  • Plan templates — six skills × three levels, level-correct elements (E3 without review points; L1/L2 with)

  • Assessor criteria checklists — tick-per-AC with the lane's own minima printed
  • Plan-hours tracking grid — the five 10-hour windows, lane column

Registration and claims stay centre-side. Every pupil is working towards these units; the coordinator registers learners before any assessment (spec §14 p20), decides unit and level attribution, and confirms names ≈4 weeks before the EQA sampling date. Accreditation facts for staff (spec v1.2). Entry 3 sizes (the primary route for this cohort): Award 4 credits (all at level) · Extended Award 9 (min 6 at level) · Certificate 14 (min 11 at level). All six E3 units = exactly 14 credits, every one at level, so a full year homes an E3 Certificate outright with no adjacent-credit accounting at all. Level 1 sizes (the stretch route, for the 2–4 pupils working above Entry 3): Award 4 · Extended Award 9 · Certificate 14 (max 3 adjacent-level credits — in practice only one adjacent unit can ever count); all six L1 units = 15 credits. SMART is introduced but NOT assessed at Entry 3 team working — the SMART minimum sits only at TmWkSk1, so no task may gate the E3 unit on producing SMART targets. Partial achievement still certificates at unit level. The 10-hour plan-use window applies to every skill except Communication. RPL reaches back three years and can include ASDAN Short Course achievement. Assessment records need assessor and learner signatures; IQA before EQA; the first-year EQA sampling visit must be booked during this first delivery year (coordinator action). Unit and level attribution is the centre coordinator’s decision — nothing is promised to a learner.
