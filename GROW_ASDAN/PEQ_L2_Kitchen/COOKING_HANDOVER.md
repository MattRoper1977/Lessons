# COOKING_HANDOVER.md — the kitchen year, handed over

**For:** the colleague teaching and resourcing the Kitchen Programme, 2026–27.
**Printable copy:** [`Cooking_Handover.html`](Cooking_Handover.html) — same content, estate-styled, prints clean.

> **This pack contains no recipes, no menus, no dishes and no ingredient lists.** That is
> deliberate, not an omission. You know the kitchen, the budget and the pupils. What is provided
> here is everything that has to line up with the **qualification**, so that whatever you choose
> to cook lands on a criterion.

---

## 1 · What the kitchen year is for

One mixed class, one kitchen, a full 38-week year, running the ASDAN **Personal Effectiveness
Qualifications** at **three levels at once** — the cohort at Entry 3, a small group at Level 1,
a directed few at Level 2. Everyone cooks in the same session. What differs is the **demand**
placed on each pupil and the **level** their evidence banks at:

| Lane | The pupil is asked to… |
|---|---|
| **Entry 3** | state · list · identify |
| **Level 1** | describe · explain |
| **Level 2** | compare · assess · evaluate |

Same bench, same task, three standards. The spec explicitly permits this (§9 p16, mixed-level
groups with differentiated demand), and the witness sheet's **Level tick** is what records which
level a given piece of evidence banked at.

**The qualification is not about food.** It is about six personal skills — communication,
decision making, learning, team working, thinking, and wellbeing in learning. The kitchen is the
*vehicle* that generates honest evidence of them. That is exactly why this pack can hand you the
criteria without handing you a menu: **what** you cook is yours; **what it has to prove** is fixed
by the awarding body.

## 2 · Which block evidences which unit, at which level

The year runs in six blocks, each with a skill in focus. Every block banks the *same skill* in all
three lanes — at three different levels, into three differently-coded units.

| Block | Weeks | Skill in focus | Entry 3 | Level 1 | Level 2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Aut1 | W1–7 | Team working | `TmWkSkE3` | `TmWkSk1` | `TmWkSk2` |
| Aut2 | W8–14 | Decision making | `DecMkSkE3` | `DecMkSk1` | `DecMkSk2` |
| Spr1 | W15–20 | Learning | `LSkE3` | `LSk1` | `LSk2` |
| Spr2 | W21–26 | Communication | `ComSkE3` | `ComSk1` | `ComSk2` |
| Sum1 | W27–32 | Thinking / Critical thinking | `ThSkE3` | `ThSk1` | **`CrThSk2`** |
| Sum2 | W33–38 | Wellbeing in learning | `WellbLeE3` | `WellbLe1` | `WellbLe2` |

At Level 2 the thinking unit is **Critical thinking (`CrThSk2`)**, not `ThSk` — a different unit,
not a harder version of the same one.

The full week-by-week ledger is the [year map](Scheme_of_Work.html). Every individual criterion is
on the [coverage matrix](Criteria_Coverage_Matrix.html), and the same mapping inverted week-by-week
is [criteria by week](Criteria_By_Week.html).

## 4 · What is yours to produce

Everything about the food, and nothing about the qualification:

- **What is cooked each week** — dishes, recipes, menus, ingredient and shopping lists,
  quantities, substitutions.
- **The practical demonstrations** — how techniques are modelled and sequenced.
- **Ordering and the budget** within the band the school office sets each term.
- **Which pupils work at which bench**, and the pairings that work in your room.

> **The one thing to hold on to when you plan a week.** Pick the food first if that is how you
> think — then open that week's shell and check the criteria it has to carry. If a criterion has
> no natural home in what you have chosen, change the *task* slightly rather than the criterion.
> The criteria are fixed by the awarding body; the food is not.

## 5 · The five rules that do not bend

1. **Supervised bench, always.** A trained adult at the bench for any knife or heat work, ratios
   per the school's own policy. The kitchen **risk assessment is the school's document** — this
   programme runs inside it and nothing here replaces it. Allergen and dietary-requirement checks
   are the school's standing process and happen **before** any cooking is planned.
2. **No diet, calorie, weight or body framing.** Anywhere, on any surface a pupil sees. The food
   content of this programme is **cooking skill, budget, teamwork and enjoyment**. This is not a
   style preference; it is a safeguarding rule for this cohort.
3. **Pupil names never enter the repository.** Named registers, photographs showing faces, and
   anything identifying stay on the school network. Repository copies use Pupil A–D.
4. **Evidence is signed and dated by both assessor and learner** (spec §10 p17), with the IQA box
   completed where the piece is sampled. An unsigned sheet is not evidence.
5. **Every surface says "working towards".** A pupil has not achieved a unit until it is assessed,
   internally quality-assured and EQA-sampled. Nothing you write may promise a certificate.

Two safeguarding boxes on the [staff guide](Staff_Kitchen_Guide.html) are **read aloud to pupils
before the activity runs**, in pupil-appropriate words — one when the decision plans open (W9),
one when the wellbeing plans open (W27). They are on the page verbatim; they are not optional.

## 7 · The hours, settled — and what it means for you

The programme is timetabled at **seven 40-minute periods a week** (4.667 supervised hours): the
PEQ session plus the six slots — Careers, Vocational/D&T, Living Independently, FoodWise,
Community A and Community B — which all count, because PEQ evidence is generated *through* those
activities rather than alongside them.

**What that means in practice: all three lanes can finish the full Certificate this year**, with
the Award banked by W14, the Extended Award by W26 and the Certificate by W38. There is real
headroom in the plan — between 37 and 57 hours a lane of consolidation time that is deliberately
*not* claimed against any unit. So a week that overruns, a trip, or a practical that needs a
second go will not cost anyone their qualification.

You do not need to track any of this. It is why the week shells look unhurried: the hours are
there. The one thing worth knowing is that **the criteria on each week's shell are the floor,
not the ceiling** — if a session produces better evidence than the shell asks for, bank it.
