The Model — Solving Problems

Key Idea: Every problem shrinks when you break it.

👀 Look: stuck is a signal, not a stop — back up, think, try another way.

Step 0 of 3

The problem-solving loop: define it, break it, pick an approach, try, adjust.

The loop is define, break, choose, try, adjust — and the order matters. Panic starts at 'try'; method starts at 'define'.

Sorting real responses — what tackles a problem versus what feeds it.

Breaking a problem converts one impossible thing into five possible ones. If a step still feels impossible, it isn't broken enough yet.

Working a live problem as a class, out loud, step by step.

The adjust step is where problem-solvers separate from problem-havers: the first approach failing is information, not verdict.

A tick says it happened. A witness statement says what you did. Before the photo is filed, the adult writes one sentence naming the thing they actually saw you do — and rings whether you did it Supported, Standard or Stretch.

Scaffolding – Supported

Access & sensory notes (staff)
· The supported task today is: Work a picture-problem with the loop mat and an adult; you choose each next step.
· Fine motor: offer the chunky-grip pen, and accept a card sort or a spoken answer scribed by an adult — the judgement is the evidence, not the handwriting. A sorted card layout photographs as well as a written sheet.
· Sensory: Reflection is the loud part inside a pupil, not the room. Offer the write-it-first option before any share-aloud; nobody is put on the spot cold.
· Cognitive load: a completed small piece beats a half-done large one at moderation. Cut the target, never the thinking.
· Ring the level honestly on the witness sheet. Supported with the work finished is a stronger portfolio entry than Standard with it abandoned.
