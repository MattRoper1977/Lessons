Silent captioned opening paragraph model | 24 seconds
Original teaching model, not old footage or quotation.

00–04 seconds
Start with place and time
Montgomery is in Alabama, USA.
The bus boycott took place in 1955–1956.

04–08 seconds
Give the background
Black riders faced unfair bus rules.
They had reasons to seek change.

08–12 seconds
Explain the reason
The unfair rules kept people apart.
They helped cause the protest.

12–16 seconds
Keep the opening in scope
A boycott means not using a service as a protest.
Shared work kept this campaign going.

16–20 seconds
Use the plan before drafting
Background. Causes. Events in date order.
Then effects and why the campaign mattered.

20–24 seconds
Build your own opening
Use place, time and background facts.
Add a reason and keep the card codes.
