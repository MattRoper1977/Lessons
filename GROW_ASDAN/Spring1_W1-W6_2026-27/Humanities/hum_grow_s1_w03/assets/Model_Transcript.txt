Silent captioned causes-and-effects model | 24 seconds
Original teaching model, not historical footage or quotation.

00–04 seconds
A cause built up over time
C: unfair bus rules lasted for years.
They gave people reasons to seek change.

04–08 seconds
A trigger helped set off action
A: Parks was arrested on 1 December 1955.
The boycott began on 5 December.

08–12 seconds
Explain a short-term effect
People stayed off buses, so many
walked or shared rides (E).

12–16 seconds
Keep the legal case in view
P1 worked alongside the protest.
It helped secure the law change (B).

16–20 seconds
Use a separate long-term branch
G: later campaigns could draw
on the boycott as an example.

20–24 seconds
Keep a reason and a limit
Use because to explain a link.
Bus rights changed; racism did not end.
