Illustrative teacher-written teaching model. Attributed to no real person. Silent video with all captions visible. This is a method demonstration, not historical footage, testimony or archive text.

00-04 seconds
A timeline shows order
Five rights and protest events
Keep each date with its event
Read the year first

04-08 seconds
The year comes first
1918 comes before 1955
Compare the years before the months
February and December do not change that order

08-12 seconds
A date and an event are one pair
6 February 1918: votes for some women
5 December 1955: the bus boycott starts
Keep both parts when you move a card

12-16 seconds
The five events in time order
1918: votes for some women
1955: the bus boycott starts
1963: March on Washington
1964: Civil Rights Act
1965: Voting Rights Act

16-20 seconds
The gaps are not all the same
1918 to 1955 is 37 calendar years
1964 to 1965 is one calendar year
Equal boxes on our strip show order

20-24 seconds
Your own strip of five
Put all five dates and events in order
Keep the strip in your book
Give one reason using a date

The model uses selected factual dates from the five event cards. The full date stays on each supplied card. It does not claim laws ended unfair treatment immediately.
