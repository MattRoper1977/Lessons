Original teaching model, not old footage or quotation.

00–04 seconds
Choose one clear test

Use a fact to back your view.

04–08 seconds

08–12 seconds
One possible score

12–16 seconds
Keep a limit in view
These acts show public regard.
They do not tell us what each person knows.

16–20 seconds
Check all four tests
Each row needs a score, card code and reason.
Add a limit. Add the scores out of 12.

20–24 seconds
Write your own judgement
Parks mattered because ____. Yet ____.
Use two facts and weigh a limit.
