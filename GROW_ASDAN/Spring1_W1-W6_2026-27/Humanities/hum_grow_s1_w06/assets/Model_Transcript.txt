Silent captioned source evaluation method | 24 seconds
Original teaching method, not old footage, source text or quotation.

00–04 seconds
Ask what the source shows
Name one clear claim it can help with.
Do not assume it can tell the whole story.

04–08 seconds
Check who made it
Who wrote this new teaching card?
Who made the old record or later history?

08–12 seconds
Keep the context
Look at when and why it was made.
A plan or call to act does not prove the result.

12–16 seconds
Trust for a clear claim
It helps with ____ because ____.
It cannot show ____ on its own.

16–20 seconds
Ask what is still open
One question it leaves is ____.
What other evidence could help?

20–24 seconds
Keep your own first work
Improve the account and check both sources.
Keep your first responses and record any help.
