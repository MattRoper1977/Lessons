Two complete fictional career stories prepared 30 October 2026. A: shop work, role ends after business change, caring hours affect class choice, adviser compares times, office learning uses checking/questions again. B: stock work, health pause, relocation changes travel, interest in repair prompts a course choice, adviser/tutor support and checking/record skills used again. No real person, employer, place, product, provider or named organisation. No live links, current vacancy claims or personal-disclosure prompts. No real work or repair task.

H16 sensitive factors appear across the stories: redundancy, health change, caring, relocation and changing interests. Identify turning points, choices, setbacks, support and transferable skills using stage clues. A stage can fit more than one category with a reason. Sequence alone is not causation. Explain at least two distinct influences and their effects, plus the skill in its old and new tasks. A planned course/application is not a confirmed job. Extract useful strategies without suggesting hardship is deserved, necessary or automatically beneficial. Support and opportunity vary; effort does not control every barrier.

J16 annotated career-journey case study. M16 two factors influencing the journey. Exact G16 also requires all four categories. Independent / Supported / Floor follow K16. Use numbered stages and text labels, never colour alone. Keep word bank, model, short chunks, private rehearsal, normal reader/scribe/typing and processing time. Choices alone are partial until clues and effects are explained. Book first; optional Type instead has the same demand. Plan in any language.

Private help route: a trusted adult or the school’s help route. No requests for personal or family experience of illness, job loss, caring or moves. Allow a pass, pause, neutral task and supported return. Staff use usual procedures if a concern is shared; do not probe or promise secrecy. These stories are not health advice, employment-rights advice or eligibility guidance.
