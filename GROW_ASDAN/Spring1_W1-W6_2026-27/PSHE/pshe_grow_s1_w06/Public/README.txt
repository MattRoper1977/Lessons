Resilience & online-safety task; bank UAS.
Both products required. Invented younger audience only.
Before teaching, agree the discreet pause signal, available adult and familiar supervised quiet route. Staff acknowledge it privately. No public explanation is needed. Offer a neutral task, supported return or private catch-up. Do not send a learner away alone. Pass is not failure.
Any disclosure goes to the safeguarding lead through school procedure. Listen calmly, reassure, do not probe, investigate or promise secrecy. Follow the immediate school route for urgent concerns. Keep real concerns and personal details in the separate safeguarding record, not shared learning evidence. A pupil unable to identify a trusted adult is supported privately, not pressed.
