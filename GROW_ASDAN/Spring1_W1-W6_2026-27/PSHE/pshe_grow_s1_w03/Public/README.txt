Understand mental health and where to get support.
Wednesday 20 January 2027 · P3 · forty minutes.
Before the lesson agree a discreet HELP or PAUSE signal. Staff acknowledge it quietly and use the familiar supervised place and agreed adult support. No public explanation is needed. Offer a neutral task, private catch-up and supported return. Do not send a pupil away alone.
Any disclosure goes to the safeguarding lead through school procedure. Listen calmly, reassure and explain that staff must share a concern with people who can help. Do not probe, investigate or promise secrecy. Use the school's immediate-concern route when needed. Keep real details in the school's safeguarding record, separate from learning evidence. A pupil unable to identify a trusted adult is supported privately, not pressed.
