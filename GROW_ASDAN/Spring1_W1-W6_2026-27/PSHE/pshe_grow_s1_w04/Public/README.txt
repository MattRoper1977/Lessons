Stay safe online; recognise risks and my digital footprint (Safer Internet Day).
Wednesday 27 January 2027 · P3 · Year A.
Before teaching, agree a discreet pause signal and an available adult. Staff acknowledge the signal quietly and support the familiar supervised quiet route. No public explanation is needed. Offer a neutral task, supported return or private catch-up; do not send a learner away alone.
Any disclosure goes to the safeguarding lead through school procedure. Listen calmly, reassure, do not probe, investigate or promise secrecy. Record concerns through the safeguarding route, separately from learning evidence. Follow the immediate school route for urgent concerns. A pupil unable to identify a trusted adult is supported privately, not pressed.
