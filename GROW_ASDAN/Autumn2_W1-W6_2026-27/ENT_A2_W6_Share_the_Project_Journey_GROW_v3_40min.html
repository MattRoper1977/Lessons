Share the Project Journey

An honest project story is stronger than a perfect one: what can the team show, explain and still question?

🎯 Learning objective

I can select evidence, explain the project journey honestly and respond to audience feedback.

✅ Success criteria
  • Explain the need, chosen action, result and improvement in a clear sequence.
  • Use genuine artefacts or observations while stating support, limits and unresolved questions accurately.
  • Receive one relevant feedback point and choose a justified keep, change or test response.
