GROW ASDAN · Autumn 2 W1–W6

Same-day evidence guidance

This page helps staff connect authentic work to learner meaning. It stores, uploads and scores nothing.

Use only the authorised school evidence system. Rehearsal clicks and screenshots are never evidence by themselves. Pair an authentic action/product/source with the learner's own explanation or review, record adult support separately, and leave genuinely missing evidence missing.
🧠 Personal Effectiveness · W1Open lesson →

Jobs and Routes: What Fits Me?

Potential authentic source

Learner-owned job-and-route comparison, personal-fit explanation and next verification question. This is teaching evidence, not a qualification or route decision.

Learner meaning prompt

Explain one route that currently interests you, the evidence behind that interest and what still needs checking.

Adult record

Record preparation, approval, prompt level, observation or witness information separately and only where the current centre process requires it.

Influence

Use the learner’s comparison to select the Autumn 2 Week 2 subject-career examples and route the unanswered question to the appropriate adult or guidance source.

Boundary: This lesson teaches and rehearses personal-effectiveness skills. Registration, selected unit, level, criteria, assessment and any claim remain with the authorised coordinator/assessor.
