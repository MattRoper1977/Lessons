Run the Small Test

Today the idea meets reality—but an honest simulation is stronger than an unauthorised real activity.

🎯 Learning objective

I can follow an approved plan, carry out my role and use an observed result to decide what happens next.

✅ Success criteria
  • Confirm whether the route is an approved real activity or a clearly labelled simulation.
  • Carry out a learner-owned role within the agreed boundaries.
  • Record what happened and choose a justified next action.

Voice → Audience → Influence

VOICE

What did you do, what actually happened, and what should the team do next because of that result?

AUDIENCE

An adult genuinely reads, listens or watches and names back the learner's decision, contribution or change before any tagging.

INFLUENCE

The team’s next action is changed, confirmed or paused using the learner’s result. Any unresolved approval is routed to the responsible adult.
