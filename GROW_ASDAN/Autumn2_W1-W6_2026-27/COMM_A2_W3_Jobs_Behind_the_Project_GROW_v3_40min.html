Jobs Behind the Project

One small community project can hide a whole team of jobs. Can we prove who does what instead of guessing from a title or uniform?

🎯 Learning objective

I can link our project tasks to jobs and justify the connection with evidence.

✅ Success criteria
  • I can identify the real tasks and skills used in our project.
  • I can link those tasks to at least two job roles using an approved source.
  • I can compare one feature of the roles and identify a question that still needs checking.
