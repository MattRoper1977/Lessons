Skill Before Service

Real project work needs more than enthusiasm. Can we perform one needed skill safely, check its quality and improve it after feedback?

🎯 Learning objective

I can practise a project skill safely, use a quality check and improve my next attempt.

✅ Success criteria
  • I can explain the skill's purpose, safe starting conditions and stop/check boundary.
  • I can follow the agreed sequence with the least support I need.
  • I can use feedback or a quality check to make one visible improvement.

Voice → Audience → Influence

VOICE

Name one checkpoint that kept the task safe, one quality sign and the change you made after feedback.

AUDIENCE

An adult genuinely reads, listens or watches and names back the learner's decision, contribution or change before any tagging.

INFLUENCE

The adult uses the learner's check to decide whether to fade a prompt, provide another rehearsal or keep the current supervision, and reports that decision back before the next live task.
