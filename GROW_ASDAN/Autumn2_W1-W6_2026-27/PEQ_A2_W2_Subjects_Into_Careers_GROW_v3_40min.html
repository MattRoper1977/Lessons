Subjects Into Careers

A school subject is more than its name. Can you uncover the skills inside it and find where those skills are used at work?

🎯 Learning objective

I can explain how skills used in my subjects connect to tasks in possible careers.

✅ Success criteria
  • I can identify a skill I use in at least two subjects.
  • I can link each skill to an observable task in a job.
  • I can use the links to choose a useful taster task or development step.
