# GROW ASDAN · Autumn 2 Weeks 1–6 · continuation

This standalone pack contains **18 interactive 40-minute lessons**: one Personal Effectiveness, one Community Project and one Community & Enterprise lesson per week for six weeks.

## Start

Open `START_HERE.html`. Every lesson is a self-contained offline HTML file with inline CSS and JavaScript.

## Before teaching

- Bring forward the approved Autumn 1 project plan, learner-owned artefacts and any genuinely available evidence; do not reconstruct missing work.
- For PEQ Autumn 2 W3, select one bounded, centre-approved careers taster brief and prepare its safe materials.
- For Community Autumn 2 W2, select the vocational skill the live project genuinely needs and confirm equipment, PPE, supervision and stop-or-ask boundaries.
- Authorise every live audience, contact, site, money, food, tool, movement, media or data route through the school's normal process. Where a live route is unavailable, use the lesson's clearly labelled simulation or draft fallback.
- Prepare the school's authorised evidence route and accessible paper/visual response materials; the lesson files themselves store nothing.

## Delivery notes

- The 16-minute transfer is one bounded, pre-staged slice of authentic work. It is not time to set up, approve, run and evidence an entire project. If the real task needs longer, complete the named micro-step or labelled draft in the lesson and schedule the authorised continuation; never compress or invent evidence.
- Privately select Supported, Standard or Optional Reach before the independent phase. Pupils are not expected to scan all three routes. The printable resource book supplies the starter cards, decision lenses, staff pre-stage checklist and recording frame for every lesson.
- In the four-minute exit, use parallel TA reception, one deliberately selected response or a scheduled same-day follow-up so learner meaning is genuinely received rather than rushed.
- Keep the superficially similar SOW weeks distinct: PEQ W5 annotates the individual's potential evidence and meaning; Community W5 organises the project's honest evidence set. Community W6 shares community impact; Enterprise W6 explains the enterprise idea-test-change journey. One approved source set may inform both, but each output keeps its own purpose and learner reflection.

## Why the pack contains 18 lessons

The recovered Autumn 1 GROW ASDAN estate contains 18 taught decks: PEQ 6 + Community Project 6 + Enterprise 6. It therefore establishes three ASDAN sessions per calendar week. Weeks 7–8 are consolidation and portfolio audit. This pack continues at Autumn 2 · Weeks 1–6 and maps them to Autumn 2 Weeks 1–6 in `GROW SOW 2026-27.xlsx`.

## Non-negotiable evidence boundary

- The pages teach and rehearse; they do not select a PEQ/UAS unit, level, code or credit value.
- A click, animation, rehearsal result or screenshot is never qualification evidence by itself.
- Potential evidence requires learner-owned decision/action, an authentic source/task/product and learner meaning/review.
- Adult preparation, prompts, approval, observation and witness information remain separate.
- External contact, risk controls, live enterprise activity and assessment decisions remain with authorised adults.
- Six 40-minute lessons are 240 minutes and must never be represented as satisfying a longer duration requirement.
- Finish by preparing authentic work for the authorised assessor review, never by claiming a unit is signed off.

## Accessibility and SEMH practice

The repeated nine-slide rhythm includes quiet response time, no public comparison, Supported/Standard/Optional reach routes, least-prompt-first staff guidance, Calm mode, keyboard navigation, 44-pixel controls, reduced-motion support and Lundy Space → Voice → Audience → Influence.
