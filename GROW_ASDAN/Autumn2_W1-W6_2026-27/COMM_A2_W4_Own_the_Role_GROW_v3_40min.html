Own the Role

A role is not a badge. It is a promise to notice, act and report. What can you take responsibility for today?

🎯 Learning objective

I can carry out an approved personal project role and report what happened.

✅ Success criteria
  • I can explain my role, intended outcome and stop/check boundary.
  • I can complete the agreed steps with the least support I need.
  • I can check the result and report progress, a problem or a next action honestly.

Voice → Audience → Influence

VOICE

State what you owned, what happened, what support or boundary mattered and what the team needs to know next.

AUDIENCE

An adult genuinely reads, listens or watches and names back the learner's decision, contribution or change before any tagging.

INFLUENCE

The adult updates the role and project boards from the learner's report, adjusts support or responsibility for the next session and makes that change visible to the learner.
