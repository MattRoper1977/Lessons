Unit: Identity & Belonging — Belonging in work
Year A. Autumn 2 Week 4, 23–27 November 2026. Tuesday 24 November 2026, P3. One 40-minute formative lesson for up to five learners. Week 6 retains its mixed source role; Week 7 is assessing. No clock time is supplied.

Source: specialist Careers scheme, GROW Careers physical row 12 / Autumn 2 row 4. SOURCE_MANIFEST.json records exact workbook hash, cell mapping and accepted Week 3 bundle hash. Raw sources not distributed.

Follow source timing: retrieval 5, explicit input 7, guided comparison 10, increasingly independent application 13, reflection 5 minutes. Recall a case clue and gap; neutral fallback available. Model employer type, work status and pattern, then a feature–preference–reason link. Compare fixed on-site work, shifts on site, hybrid work and booked self-employed work. Independently compare at least three case patterns using common headings and labels. Consider security, flexibility and support. Explain a feature that fits a preference and keep a trade-off or question. Retain first thinking, actual prompts and any change.

Four invented teaching cases, prepared 30 October 2026: A small bakery, B hospital ward in the public sector in this example, C large warehouse planning role, D self-employed repair worker. Types/status/patterns can combine differently elsewhere. C’s hybrid arrangement is for a planning role; goods handling remains on site. Public or large employer labels do not prove security. D’s freedom to suggest booking times has customer and work constraints. No actual vacancy, employment term, pay claim, named employer or real place is represented. Nothing is best for everyone.

Independent / Supported / Floor follow the source. Keep three ways and explained fit as the target. Normal reading, scribing, typing, aids, processing time and a regulation break available. Record answer prompts separately and fade when secure. No cold calling, forced public answer, household income or health disclosure. Book first; optional Type instead carries the same demand. No web, login or pupil phone needed. A preference can change. Private own preferences can evidence personal fit. Practice preferences support comparison and hypothetical reasoning; do not claim they show the learner’s own fit.

J12 evidence: Employer/work-pattern comparison. M12 end check: Can the student justify one preferred working pattern or employer type? Also check at least three ways compared for the full outcome. A list of names, two patterns only or a copied model remains partial. The added trade-off/question check is recorded separately from the core outcome. Keep actual work in Careers Portfolio/action plan and offer to the Communication portfolio. Staff confirm any mapping. E only for banked evidence; R after Voice and Audience, NS and learner response in the same colour, on the same page or usual mode.
