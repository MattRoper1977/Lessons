Year A. Autumn 2 Week 3, 16–20 November 2026. Tuesday 17 November 2026, P3. One 40-minute formative lesson for up to five learners. Week 6 retains its mixed source role; Week 7 is assessing. No clock time is supplied.

Source of record: specialist Careers scheme, GROW Careers physical row 11 / Autumn 2 row 3. SOURCE_MANIFEST.json records the workbook hash, cell mapping and accepted clean Week 2 bundle hash. Raw sources are not distributed.

Source timing: retrieval 5 minutes, explicit input 7, guided comparison 10, increasingly independent application 13, reflection 5. Recall source/date/limit, with a neutral fallback. Model label/date/type/purpose/scope and a bounded note. Compare role guides, sample vacancies and worker accounts. Research stockroom assistant and reception assistant from at least two cards per occupation. Record duties, hours, salary range, location/work setting, entry, progression and conditions with source labels, or explicit gaps. Add one supported similarity, an important difference and one unanswered question for each job. Keep actual prompts, first thinking and any revision.

The six prepared cards are inside Support Cards, with three contrasting cards per occupation. A1/B1 give broad guides, A2/B2 sample vacancies, A3/B3 worker accounts. All posts and accounts are invented; each card is dated 30 October 2026. They are distinct teaching sources and perspectives, not independently verified real-world evidence. Generic work settings replace real locations. Different shifts describe different posts. Never generalise the sample pay, hours or entry details to all jobs. Current vacancies and entry rules are not claimed.

Independent / Supported / Floor follow the source. Reader, scribe, typing, normal aids, extra processing and a regulation break remain available. Record answer prompts separately and fade only when secure. No cold calling or compulsory public answer. No household income or fixed career choice required. Book default; Type instead is an equivalent editable option. No web, pupil phone or login needed.

Evidence J11: Two-job research profiles with source record. End check M11: Can the student identify one important difference between the two researched occupations? Check the full outcome separately. One job, one card for a job, or a copied model is partial evidence. Record gaps honestly. Keep actual work in Careers Portfolio/action plan and offer to the Communication portfolio.
