Lesson overview

Write goals we can prove

Hand out one sheet, one pen and one commit card before the pupils sit. Keep the approved card pack face up. Check the signing adult's name is on the group sheet.

GROW ASDAN. Week 2. One 40 minute lesson.

Course: ASDAN PEQ Level 1, the Personal Effectiveness Qualification. Unit 2 Wellbeing, Challenge 1: set and prove a personal challenge goal.

Week 1 covered evidence. Week 2 is goals.

Objective: begin the Wellbeing unit, set one challenge goal from the approved cards, and agree our project goal and success criteria.

Success evidence: a sheet headed with a name, the date and ASDAN Wellbeing, holding a goal, a proof and one criterion; and a group sheet marked AGREED.

Folders: the ASDAN box on the shelf, one folder per pupil.

Resources: approved card pack with Challenge Card 4; two starter cards and three goals on the board; a commit card each; the group sheet; the school camera.

No disclosure. Every card, photo and example comes from the approved school pack. You are never asked about home, family, health or money.

40 minutes. Offline.

Arrival · retrieve

Arrival · retrieve

Sit down. Put your pen on the table. Read the four statements.

Mark each one True, False, or I am not sure. I am not sure is a real answer.

  • Evidence is something another person can see.
  • A goal is finished when you say you tried hard.
  • A photo can be evidence.
  • You may change which row you work on.
Read the statements aloud once. Give thirty seconds of silence. No hands up until all four are marked. Key: 1 True, 2 False, 3 True, 4 True. Correct statement 2 flatly.

Starter · create the need

Starter · create the need

Two goal cards are on the board. Both come from the approved pack.

Card A says: I will get better at cooking.

Card B says: I will make toast on my own in the Food Room on Tuesday and Thursday.

At the end of term, one of these can be proved. The other cannot. Nobody can see better. You cannot take a photo of better.

That is the problem today. A goal needs proof.

  • Notice: does the card say what you will do?
  • Choose: point to the card you could prove.
  • Explain: say what the proof would be.

I Do · model

I Do · model

This unit is called Wellbeing. Here wellbeing means a school activity from the approved cards. You are not asked about home, health or family.

Three words

Goal. One thing you will do.

Evidence. Something another person can see. A photo, a tally, a signature.

Success criteria. The short list we agree that shows the job is done.

Watch me write one

I use Challenge Card 4. It says: help set up the sports store.

First I write what I will do. I carry the cones to the field.

Then I write when. Wednesday, at the start of PE.

Then I write how often. Three Wednesdays.

Now I write the proof. The adult named on the group sheet signs my sheet each Wednesday.

I do not know if the store is open at lunch. I write a question mark. Then I ask an adult.

3 min
We Do · everyone commits

We Do · everyone commits

Three goals are on the board. Only one can be proved.

  • A: I will be kinder in class.
  • B: I will water the planters on Monday and Friday for three weeks.
  • C: I will try my best at everything.

Write A, B or C on your card. Turn it face down. Do not speak yet.

When every card is down, we turn them over. Then you give one reason.

Pointing at your card counts as answering.

Wait for every card before the reveal. If a pupil is stuck, offer two cards: B or C. Take three reasons. Correct the goal, never the pupil.

I Do 2 · connect

I Do 2 · connect

You now have your own challenge goal.

We also need one goal for the whole group. That is our project goal.

The project card says: run the Friday snack stall for four weeks.

That is a proposal, not a decision. You say yes or no to it at the end of the lesson.

A project goal needs success criteria. That is the proof list we agree before we start.

Here are two we could agree. The stall opens at half past twelve. The money is counted and written down.

Another person can see each one.

Your challenge goal goes in your ASDAN folder. The agreed goal and criteria go on the group sheet.

We Do 2 · lab

Now you write success criteria for the snack stall. Pick a row. You may change row at any time.

9 min
Independent · evidence

Independent · evidence

Now you write. You have eight minutes.

Four things go on your sheet.

  • One. Your challenge goal: what you will do, when, and how often.
  • Two. Your proof: the adult named on the group sheet signs it, or an adult photographs the job in school on the school camera.
  • Three. One success criterion for the snack stall. Write it, then add it to the group sheet.
  • Four. Head your sheet: your name, today's date, ASDAN Wellbeing. It goes in your ASDAN folder after the exit.

Work at your own pace. It is better to write one good line than three fast lines.

If you are not sure, write a question mark. Do not guess.

Now we agree together

Put your pens down. I read our project goal aloud. Show a thumb up or a thumb down. A yes or no card counts too.

Then I read each criterion on the group sheet. Show your thumb each time.

I ring the three or four we agree. I write AGREED and the date on the group sheet.

Hand a stuck pupil a card from the pack, not a sentence. Adults take every photo, on the school camera. A photo shows the job or the sheet, never another pupil. Stop the writing at eight minutes.

Exit · Audience and Influence

Exit · Audience and Influence

SPACE: the learner may pause or choose a private response.

VOICE: the evidence statement remains in the learner’s chosen communication mode.

AUDIENCE: a familiar adult receives and names back exactly what was heard or seen.

INFLUENCE: one real next action is recorded: retain, add method detail, or leave open.

Bring your sheet to the front.

Tell me one thing. Your goal, or your success criterion. Pointing at your sheet counts as speaking.

I say it back to you in your own words.

You tell me: that is right, or that is not right. Correcting me helps.

Then we choose one next action. It might be: ask the adult named on the group sheet to sign on Wednesday. Or: ask an adult to photograph the job.

I write that action in the My next action box. You keep the sheet.

Name it back word for word, not tidied up. If the pupil corrects you, change the sheet there and then. One written action each, or the loop has not closed. Sheets go in the named ASDAN folders as pupils leave.
