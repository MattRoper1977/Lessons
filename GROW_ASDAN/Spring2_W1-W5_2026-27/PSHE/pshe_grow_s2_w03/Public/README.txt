Understand peer pressure and assertiveness.
Wednesday 10 March 2027 · P3 · forty minutes · Spring 2.
Before teaching, agree a discreet HELP or PAUSE signal, available adult and familiar supervised place and route. Staff acknowledge it quietly. No public reason is needed. Offer a neutral task, supported return or private catch-up. Do not send a learner away alone. Pass and a pause are not failure.
Any disclosure goes to the safeguarding lead through school procedure. Listen calmly, reassure, do not probe, investigate or promise secrecy. Follow urgent school procedures for immediate concerns. Keep real concerns and identifying details in the separate safeguarding record, not learning evidence. A pupil unable to identify a trusted adult is supported privately, not pressed.
