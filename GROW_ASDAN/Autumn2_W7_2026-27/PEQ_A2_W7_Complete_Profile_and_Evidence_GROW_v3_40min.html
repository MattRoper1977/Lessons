First-item check

Keep the item in its approved original location while you make a temporary note of the source, date and item type. If the location is uncertain, mark NOT SURE YET and ask the responsible adult which approved place to check. Do not copy, rename or move the item merely to make the folder look complete. A visible gap is safer than a confident guess.

Which record is easiest to trust and find again?

Predict first: an undated screenshot, the same photo filed twice, or a dated learner note with its source and adult-support label.

Comparison rehearsal

For each option, ask three checking questions. Can another person find the original? Can they tell which part came from the learner, shared work or adult support? Can they see what is still missing? The dated note currently gives the clearest trail, but the other items are not erased. They remain labelled as unconfirmed, duplicated or awaiting a genuine follow-up.

4 min
I DO - MODEL

Model FIND - LABEL - MAP - GAP

FIND: freeze the inventory before moving files; keep originals in the approved location.
LABEL: record source, date, learner contribution, shared contribution and adult preparation or support.
MAP: connect the item to one profile statement or real action without claiming a unit or criterion.
GAP: mark PRESENT, MISSING or AWAITING AUTHORISED REVIEW and name the next action.
Worked locator model

Begin with a real item already held in the approved evidence store. Record its existing filename or folder locator, the date shown on the source and the kind of record. Add separate labels for learner contribution, shared contribution and adult preparation or support. Connect it only to the profile section it genuinely helps to explain. If the connection or status needs an authorised decision, stop at AWAITING AUTHORISED REVIEW and name the responsible role.

Do not backdate, recreate a quotation, stage a photograph or convert adult wording into the learner's voice.

Hinge explanation check

The middle choice protects the truth trail because it keeps the adult caption labelled as adult observation and keeps learner meaning visibly absent. A genuine later response may add learner meaning if it actually happens. Until then, the record remains incomplete for that purpose. Cross-reference model

One original can have two locators only when each locator points back to the same approved item and each connection is explained separately. Do not create two copies and count them as two experiences. On the second locator, repeat the source and date, name the different profile connection and keep the same authorship or support note. If either connection is weak, record it as a question for authorised review.

Case: one real item has a date and learner contribution, but its folder locator and support note are missing.

Decision rehearsal

FIND protects the approved original. LABEL makes contribution and support visible. MAP + GAP explains the connection and preserves anything still unresolved. Choose the first control needed, then say what must happen second and third. Check that the proposed order never invents a filename, date, quotation, event or decision. The screen is rehearsal; only genuine records in the approved process can be organised.

Independent task

Use only real W1-W6 records. Check every profile section your centre requires. Mark it PRESENT, or log MISSING / AWAITING ACTION. Then work on one section in depth. Add source/date locators. Keep learner, shared and adult contributions separate. Finish with one next action, responsible role and review point.

◆ Supported

Use a prelisted section checklist. Mark every section present or log a gap. Then sort one genuine item, build one locator and point, sign or say why it belongs.

One section at a time: Is it present? If not, what happens next? Then: What is the item? Who did what? Where is it?
▲ Standard

Complete the whole-profile status check. Then build a time-ordered evidence index for one section. Explain links, record support separately and show one gap or review decision.

All sections -> status/action; then source/date -> learner role -> profile link -> evidence status.
★ Optional reach

Check the whole-profile status map and two possible links. Judge cross-reference against duplication, note one limit and choose the next genuine action.

Test the full status map for hidden gaps, then test authenticity, connection, recency and what still needs authorised review. Do not self-sign.
Whole-profile close - organising step, not an assessed criterion

Each required section must be present or handed forward as missing / awaiting action. A visible gap completes this organising step honestly; it does not prove achievement.

1 - Learner ownershipDecision, action, words or explanation.
2 - Truth trailSource, date, original locator and support note.
3 - Honest statusPresent, missing or awaiting authorised review.
Profile-to-evidence rehearsal

Choose one profile section. Point to the genuine item, name the approved original location and mark the section PRESENT, MISSING or AWAITING ACTION. If the link is uncertain, keep the gap visible and name the next authorised check instead of guessing. Before finishing, read the row from left to right: section, item, source and date, learner contribution, shared or adult support, locator, status and next action.

Final cross-check: confirm the profile section label matches the checklist; the original item opens from its approved location; the recorded date comes from the item; the contribution labels remain separate; the profile link is explained; the status is honest; and the next action names a responsible role. Leave every uncertain point visible for authorised review.

Qualification boundary

GROW's live route is PEQ Entry 3 with Level 1 stretch, but teaching routes do not set qualification level. Unit, level and assessment remain coordinator/assessor decisions. For PEQ units other than the four Communication units, current official paperwork includes use of the plan over a minimum of 10 hours. Communication units use their own activity requirements instead. Team working uses a group of at least three. Entry 3 command verbs include State, List and Identify; Level 1 includes Outline, Describe and Give examples. This lesson imports none of those as new criteria.

AQA UAS

Any local AQA UAS title/code considered with this lesson remains an unverified centre record awaiting confirmation from the UAS coordinator's entry record. Verify before delivery or filing. Pupil-facing surfaces make no code or banking claim.
